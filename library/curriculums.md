## Ecclesiastical Latin for the 1962 Roman Missal

This self-paced curriculum has a course guide, a reference companion, 46 prerequisite-ordered learning modules, and four stage-assessment sequences. Each learning module is a separately printable packet designed for two focused sessions: it includes prerequisites, a local reference card, the complete core lesson, an extension for every lesson submodule, two selected worksheets, a mastery record, and matching terminal keys. Omitted worksheet variants remain optional remediation or delayed-recall sources rather than required page completion.

All 52 working snapshots are installed under the maintainer's explicit
exact-snapshot exception of 20 July 2026. That operational authorization does
not complete page-image collation, establish a donor license, supply an
independent rights review, or grant broader reuse of received liturgical or
donor wording; the linked records preserve those limitations.
