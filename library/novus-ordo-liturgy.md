# Novus Ordo: Postconciliar Roman Rite

[Return to the Library](../LIBRARY.md)

This page is for edition-specific studies of the postconciliar Roman Rite, distinct from the 1962 books commonly associated with the Traditional Latin Mass.

“Novus Ordo” can refer to different Latin editions, vernacular translations, territories, and local practices. Each future publication will identify the exact books, language, territory, and date it studies.

There is not yet a dedicated postconciliar liturgy publication.
