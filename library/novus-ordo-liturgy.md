# Novus Ordo: Postconciliar Roman Rite

[Return to the Library](../LIBRARY.md)

This page is for edition-specific studies of the postconciliar Roman Rite, distinct from the 1962 books commonly associated with the Traditional Latin Mass.

“Novus Ordo” can refer to different Latin editions, vernacular translations, territories, and local practices. Each publication identifies the exact books, language, territory, and date it studies.

## The Mass and Its Order

- **[The Order of Mass: A Mystagogy of the Postconciliar Roman Rite](../doc/gpt/liturgy/roman-rite/postconciliar/2008-latin-2011-us-english/ordinary/00-order-of-mass.pdf)** — A movement-by-movement exposition of the complete Order in the 2008 emended third Latin typical edition and, where territorial form matters, its current United States English implementation. It covers the received option tree, all ten Eucharistic Prayers in the Latin Missal, and the three separately authorized U.S. Eucharistic Prayers for Masses with Children.
