## First Nine Sundays after Pentecost in 2026

These Year A preparation companions cover 31 May through 26 July 2026 under the General Roman Calendar for United States dioceses. Their books are the 2008 emended Latin *Missale Romanum*, third typical edition; the U.S. English *Roman Missal, Third Edition*, implemented 2011; and the English *Lectionary for Mass for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America*, second typical edition, Volume I. Corpus Christi is transferred to Sunday; diocesan, parish, and church-proper solemnities are outside this national-calendar resolution.

Dates, Lectionary paths, 2002 Latin formularies, the official 2008 variation list, and official English antiphons are internally collated. The variation list records no substantive change on these formulary pages, but the 2008 reprint images were not directly inspected. Eight of the nine exact current PDF snapshots through 26 July retain exact-snapshot distribution clearance under the later 20 July 2026 “approve all” supplement. The revised PC-S41-A snapshot is in `review`: its earlier authorization remains bound to the historical 12-page PDF, while the current 11-page PDF awaits renewed exact-snapshot authorization, work-specific rights review, liturgical-text permission, named U.S. altar-book collation, and independent proper-guide review. Exact-snapshot clearance does not make any guide profile-final or ecclesiastically approved and supplies neither complete collation of the English orations against an identified U.S. altar-book printing nor independent rights, liturgical, biblical, theological, historical, or ecclesiastical review, imprimatur, nihil obstat, general license, or broader reuse permission.
