This page is for edition-specific studies of the postconciliar Roman Rite, distinct from the 1962 books commonly associated with the Traditional Latin Mass.

“Novus Ordo” can refer to different Latin editions, vernacular translations, territories, and local practices. Each publication identifies the exact books, language, territory, cycle, and dated occurrence it studies.

## First Eight Sundays after Pentecost in 2026

These Year A studies resolve the eight consecutive Sundays from May 31 through July 19, 2026, for the General Roman Calendar as used in the dioceses of the United States. In this territory, Corpus Christi is transferred to Sunday; another territory can produce a different sequence. Unknown diocesan, parish, and church-proper solemnities remain outside this national-calendar resolution.

The guides are preparation companions, not liturgical books or parish enactment records. Their dates, Lectionary paths, 2002 Latin formularies, the official published 2008 variation list, and official English antiphon assignments have been internally collated; that variation list records no substantive change on the formulary pages used here, although the 2008 reprint page images were not directly inspected. Their exact PDF snapshots have release approval, including project-controlled rights and liturgical-distribution clearance. That distribution decision does not make the guides profile-final: complete collation of the English orations against an identified U.S. altar-book printing and independent liturgical, biblical, theological, and historical review remain outstanding.
