§ 1 · The visible claim

A scholarly page earns confidence by making the object, edition, and limits legible before asking the reader to trust its polish.

The primary text should remain primary. Qualification belongs close enough to the sentence it governs that a reader can understand what is known, what is synthesized, and what remains unresolved without opening a dashboard of metadata.

That does not require flattening the record. A quiet identity line can preserve provider and revision; a focused note can name an illustrative source; and a deliberate context surface can disclose the exact artifact, rights basis, derivation, and digest.

The reading axis

A reader should not lose its measure when Contents or Related becomes available. On a narrow viewport the apparatus joins the document order; on a wide viewport it may sit beside the text without moving the text's center or making a second primary plane.

Canonical print: in production, this action opens the installed reviewed PDF. The prototype keeps every destination local and does not imitate a publication binding.
