The propers of the Mass are a separate page with their own address, deliberately: they are a separate task, and the machinery the two share lives in shared/browser-core.js rather than in a mode switch.

Nothing here is pre-rendered. A track assembles each reading in the browser out of shared chapter fragments, fetched once each and cached for the rest of the session, so the number of files grows with the corpus and not with the number of ways of reading it.
