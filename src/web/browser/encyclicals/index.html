✠Papal Encyclicals

Every encyclical this project has catalogued, one row each: sort by pope, year or title, and open the Latin or English reading edition where one is held here, or the Vatican’s own page.

The summaries are AI-written. Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, wrote each one from the encyclical’s own text. They are a finding aid, not papal wording; read the encyclical before relying on one.

No text published after 1930 is offered here. Only Latin printed by 1930, and identified human English translations printed by 1930, are linked. Every other row gives the Vatican’s page and the reason. No modern or AI translation is offered.

Encyclicals, one per row. Press a column heading to order the list by it.

A reading edition is a two-column setting of one identified historical printing — the Latin as printed by 1930, or an English translation printed by 1930 — whose every page was reviewed before it was installed. Each link names the printing it was set from. Fragment marks an edition of a printing that holds only part of the act; damaged printing marks one whose source is defective in places. The edition itself says what is missing.

The papal wording in these editions is historical text, in the public domain in the United States on its identified printing by 1930; its status elsewhere may differ, and it is excluded from this project’s licence. See Third-party material. A Vatican link opens the Holy See’s own site, which this project does not control.
