A catena is a chain of excerpted comment running beside the text. This page shows only commentary this project actually holds: a work believed to comment here but not yet acquired is listed separately, under its own heading, and never beside an excerpt. Fragments are stored at the extent their author addressed and the chapter view is derived from that, so a fragment reaching across a chapter boundary appears under both chapters at its full extent rather than being cut at the seam.
