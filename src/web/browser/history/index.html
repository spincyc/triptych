Every station is an act — a constitution, a decree, an instruction, or a documented exemption — and never a book. A printing is a witness to an act, not a station on the line, because two printings of one prayer differ constantly and no act stands behind those differences. Where the descent runs through an edition this tracer does not represent, the connector says so rather than joining the two ends as though nothing sat between them.
