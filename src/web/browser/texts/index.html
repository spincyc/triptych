A work is one subject; an edition is one model's treatment of it, and the two editions of a work are separate documents that usually carry different titles. So no work is given a single name here: each edition is listed under the title it actually prints, and where a work is issued twice both titles stand. A synthesis edition is the same document set a second time with the appointed texts and the per-element sweep left out, and it is shown as a second issue of its document rather than counted as another one.
