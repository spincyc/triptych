It contains the complete allocution and identifies its delivery at the

The restricted response is identified but not retained. No redistribution
permission, translation, or diplomatic collation against a printed witness
is claimed.

The council study uses the complete Italian delivery as official control for
the bounded chronology that John XXIII publicly proposed an ecumenical
council for the universal Church on 25 January 1959. The act announces the
council together with a Roman diocesan synod and revision of the Code of
Canon Law; it does not supply the finished agenda, document set, or reform
program later produced by the council.
