It contains the complete numbered address, sectional headings, the
11 October 1962 date, and a citation to *Acta Apostolicae Sedis* 54 (1962),
786-796. The restricted response is identified but not retained. No
redistribution permission or diplomatic collation against the Acta witness
is claimed.

The council study uses the complete Latin delivery as official control for
John XXIII's opening of Vatican II and for his distinction between the
deposit of faith and the manner in which the same truths are expressed,
while retaining the qualification that the expression must preserve the
same meaning and judgment. The address establishes an opening program and
pastoral manner; the binding does not treat it as a finished platform for
every later conciliar or postconciliar reform.
