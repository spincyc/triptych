# *Inter Mirifica* and AAS 56 Reuse Review

Reviewed on 2026-07-24.

This record documents the bounded normalization of *Inter mirifica* within
`family.vatican-ii.acts` and its reuse of the volume 56 component of
`family.acta.aas-ass`. The canonical manifests and publication-local binding
remain the machine-enforced records.

## Identity and exact witnesses

The graph distinguishes the Decree from its delivery artifacts and official
gazette container:

- *Inter mirifica* is one conciliar decree, solemnly promulgated on
  4 December 1963 and comprising numbered articles 1--24;
- its dated English and Latin Vatican web states are separate editions and
  exact HTML artifacts;
- the Latin constituent printed in *Acta Apostolicae Sedis* 56 (1964) is a
  separate edition represented as one segment of the existing exact volume
  artifact; and
- AAS volume 56 remains the separately owned official-gazette container.

The English response contains articles 1--24 and two source notes. Its
heading reports solemn promulgation by Paul VI on 4 December 1963, but it
omits the Latin promulgation and subscription apparatus.

The Latin response contains articles 1--24, the promulgation formula and
date, Paul VI's signature, and an incomplete subscription block ending with
Paul Peter Meouchi. The Acta continues through later conciliar signatories,
the continuation notice, and three notarial attestations. The Latin web
response's sole conciliar footnote follows its truncated subscription block.

The Vatican portal's legal notice did not establish repository
redistribution permission. All three artifacts are therefore restricted,
nonretained, and nonindexable. Their exact identities and reusable evidence
boundaries are recorded without importing their bytes into Git.

## Acta constituent and passage boundaries

The complete Acta constituent occupies printed and artifact PDF pp. 145--157.
Printed p. 144 ends Paul VI's *Sacram liturgiam*. Printed p. 145 opens the
Decree and articles 1--2. The numbered body ends with article 24 on printed
p. 151, immediately before the promulgation formula. Printed p. 157 finishes
the conciliar subscriptions, continuation notice, and notarial attestations;
printed p. 158 begins the record of Paul VI's pilgrimage to Palestine.

The boundary pages, numbered-body transition, exact artifact hash, and OCR
text were checked. The OCR is a locating aid, not an independently
authoritative transcription, and no complete word-for-word image collation
is claimed.

## Reuse and consumer limit

The complete English, Latin web, and Latin Acta numbered bodies are centrally
identifiable for future examination. The present council study receives only
a catalog-level work binding for title, decree genre, promulgation date,
second-session chronology, and social-communications subject in its
sixteen-document inventory. It does not claim that the publication inspected
or relied on the complete Decree.

The Decree's exposed scriptural references, institutional prescriptions,
media ethics, and later pastoral-instruction mandate remain leads rather than
inherited publication evidence. Normalizing this work does not verify later
communications dicasteries, *Communio et progressio*, particular media
practices, or claims about the Decree's reception or sufficiency.
