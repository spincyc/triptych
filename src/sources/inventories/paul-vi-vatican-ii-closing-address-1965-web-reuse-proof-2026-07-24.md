`240553e648c4b2f7356d8f52be825892083a8131b1c331e45ae955ebe03c81bc`.
It contains the complete address and its 7 December 1965 date. The
restricted response is identified but not retained. No redistribution
permission or comparison with the Italian original or a printed Acta witness
is claimed.

The council study uses the complete English delivery as official control for
Paul VI's account of Vatican II at its final public session: the council was
concluding with unresolved questions left to later work; it engaged modern
humanism through pastoral charity rather than a new extraordinary dogmatic
pronouncement; and its authoritative teaching and service of humanity
remained ordered to religious and eternal ends. The binding does not convert
the address's retrospective interpretation into the text of a conciliar act.
