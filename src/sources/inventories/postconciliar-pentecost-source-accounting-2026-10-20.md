## Exact grades and join limits

The existing v1 join API has one target-identity grade. It cannot independently
mark a role as present in an older source while certifying nothing about target
correspondence. Accordingly the eleven 1981 Lectionary roles and twelve 2002
Missal appointments retain `target-correspondence-unresolved`, even where a
unique held-role address is recorded. Their source-local presence is not
published as established 2008 target membership.

Only the Antiphonary's five positively scoped appointments have established
target identity. The unique Day Communion and Vigil Entrance/Communion roles
join and classify represented. The two Day Entrance alternatives have no
separate v1 option address; they stay unresolved instead of pretending that one
same-named role proves each source alternative. The two Vigil Collect
alternatives likewise receive no invented option join.

## Wording, reconstruction and rights remain separate

The calendar's only changed field is its bounded Pentecost source note. Its
forms, citations, alternatives, text-status records and all text-bearing fields
are byte-for-byte semantically unchanged. Source references cannot clear a
body's rights or make an unbound English exemplar available.

The current reader's Scripture remains Bible reconstruction, not established
Missal or Lectionary wording. Existing project-created and historical-antecedent
English keeps its own label and relation. Known modern Latin bodies remain
unavailable under their existing text-status dispositions; approved ICEL and
CCD/Lectionary payloads remain outside the public bundle. This patch supplies
no protected words and no plausible replacement text.
