## Psalm: preserve the printed endpoint and the refusal

**Psalm 56:10, 11–12, 13–14** in the calendar's Hebrew addressing. OLM artifact
page 290 / printed page 236 / n. 448 positively prints **Psalm 55:10, 11–12,
13–14**. Endpoint 14 is source evidence; it must not be clipped or replaced by a
chapter-only conversion.

The held Challoner and Clementine Psalm 55 end at verse 13. Their verse 11 joins
clauses which the held King James Psalm 56 divides between verses 10 and 11.
The final deliverance clause is present in both held traditions, but that fact
alone supplies no edition-identified correspondence from the Ordo's numbered
segments to all offered editions. No tracked witness is a Nova Vulgata Psalter.
The sole tracked Psalm concordance accepts equal-length verse runs and cannot
encode this unequal division. It flags Hebrew Psalm 56 as not uniformly offset.

The existing owner therefore refuses the entire citation with `would drop verse
14`; it must return no plausible neighboring passage. This note authorizes no
new Psalm mapping, alias, offset, chapter conversion, or source-endpoint change.
The earlier dated collation counts remain historical, and the old open Psalm
exception remains open. This bounded review closes no whole-source denominator.
