The council study uses the complete English delivery as official control for
Benedict XVI's contrast between a hermeneutic of discontinuity and rupture
and a hermeneutic of reform in the continuity of the Church as one subject.
It also controls the qualification that true reform combines continuity and
discontinuity at different levels, with permanent principles distinguished
from historically contingent practical forms. The binding does not turn the
address into a claim that Vatican II produced no change.
