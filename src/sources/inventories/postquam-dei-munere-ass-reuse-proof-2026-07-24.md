The official Vatican delivery of *Acta Sanctae Sedis* 6 (1870-1871) is a

The complete Latin constituent occupies printed and artifact pages 65-67.
Its title and incipit follow the preceding papal letter on page 65; its
20 October 1870 date and countersignature precede an unrelated decree on
page 67.

The exact restricted volume is identified but not retained. The artifact's
OCR layer was used for navigation and structural inspection, not silently
promoted into a retained or diplomatically corrected text.

The council study uses the complete constituent as official control for the
bounded claim that Pius IX suspended celebration of Vatican I until a more
opportune time to be declared by the Holy See. The act also records the four
public sessions, already promulgated constitutions, unfinished doctrinal and
disciplinary work, the loss of necessary liberty and security at Rome, and
pastors' need to return to their churches.
