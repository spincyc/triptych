Let us pray also for (our) catechumens, that our God and Lord may open the ears of their hearts and the gate of mercy, so that, having received forgiveness of all sins through the washing of rebirth, they too may be found in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Almighty everlasting God, who always make your Church fruitful with new offspring, increase the faith and understanding of (our) catechumens, so that, reborn in the baptismal font, they may be gathered among your adopted children. Through Christ our Lord. R. Amen.
