God of everlasting mercy, at the very recurrence of the paschal feast you kindle the faith of the people consecrated to you. Increase the grace you have given, so that all may understand with worthy insight by what washing they have been cleansed, by what Spirit they have been born anew, and by what blood they have been redeemed. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. R. Amen.
