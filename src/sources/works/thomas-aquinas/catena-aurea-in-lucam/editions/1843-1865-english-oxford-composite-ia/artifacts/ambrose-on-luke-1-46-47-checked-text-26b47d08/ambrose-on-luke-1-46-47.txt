{Lk 1:46-47 Ambrose} Ambrose; The soul of Mary therefore magnifies the Lord, and her spirit rejoiced in God, because with soul and spirit devoted to the Father and the Son, she worships with a pious affection the one God from whom are all things.
{Lk 1:46-47 Ambrose} But let every one have the spirit of Mary, so that he may rejoice in the Lord.
{Lk 1:46-47 Ambrose} If according to the flesh there is one mother of Christ, yet, according to faith, Christ is the fruit of all.
{Lk 1:46-47 Ambrose} For every soul receives the word of God if only he be unspotted and free from sin, and preserves it with unsullied purity.
