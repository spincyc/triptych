{Lk 2:9 Bede} Bede; No where in the whole course of the Old Testament do we find that the Angels who so constantly appear to the Patriarchs, came in the day time.
{Lk 2:9 Bede} This privilege was rightly kept for this time when there arose in the darkness a light to them that were true of heart.
{Lk 2:9 Bede} Hence it follows, and the glory of God shone round about them.
{Lk 2:9 Bede} He is sent forth from the womb, but He shines from heaven.
{Lk 2:9 Bede} He lies in a common inn, but He lives in celestial light.
