{Lk 2:7 Cyril} Cyril; He finds man in his corrupt affections become like the beasts that perish, and therefore He is laid in the manger, in the place of food,
{Lk 2:7 Cyril} that we changing the life of beasts, might be brought to the knowledge that befits man, partaking not of hay, but of the heavenly bread, the lifegiving body.
