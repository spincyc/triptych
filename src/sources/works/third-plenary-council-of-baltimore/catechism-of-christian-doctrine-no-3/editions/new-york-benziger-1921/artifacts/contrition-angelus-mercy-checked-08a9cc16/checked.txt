{p6.contrition.heading} An Act of Contrition.
{p6.contrition} O my God! I am heartily sorry for having offended Thee, and I detest all my sins, because I dread the loss of heaven and the pains of hell; but most of all because they offend Thee, my God, who art all-good and deserving of all my love. I firmly resolve, with the help of Thy grace, to confess my sins, to do penance, and to amend my life.
{q43} Q. 43. What is the Angelus?
{q43.a} A. The Angelus is a prayer giving a brief history of the Incarnation.
{q44} Q. 44. Say the Angelus.
{q44.a} A. The Angel of the Lord declared unto Mary. And she conceived of the Holy Ghost. Hail Mary, &c. Behold the handmaid of the Lord. Be it done unto me according to Thy Word. Hail Mary, &c. And the Word was made flesh. And dwelt among us. Hail Mary, &c. Pray for us, O Holy Mother of God! That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.
{q44.a.let-us-pray} Let us pray:
{q44.a.collect} Pour forth, we beseech Thee, O Lord, Thy grace into our hearts that we to whom the Incarnation of Christ Thy Son was made known by the message of an angel, may, by His Passion and cross, be brought to the glory of His resurrection, through the same. Christ Our Lord. Amen.
{q45} Q. 45. At what time is the Angelus usually said?
{q45.a} A. The Angelus is said in the evening, in memory of the Incarnation; in the morning, in memory of the Resurrection, and at noon in memory of the Passion of Our Lord.

{q108} Q. 108. Give the substance of an Act of Contrition.
{q108.a} A. The substance of an Act of Contrition is: I am sorry for my sins, because they have offended God, and I will never sin again.
{q813} Q. 813. Which are the chief spiritual works of mercy?
{q813.a} A. The chief spiritual works of mercy are seven: To admonish the sinner, to instruct the ignorant, to counsel the doubtful, to comfort the sorrowful, to bear wrongs patiently, to forgive all injuries, and to pray for the living and the dead.
{q819} Q. 819. Which are the chief corporal works of mercy?
{q819.a} A. The chief corporal works of mercy are seven: To feed the hungry, to give drink to the thirsty, to clothe the naked, to ransom the captive, to harbor the harborless, to visit the sick, and to bury the dead.
{q820} Q. 820. How may we briefly state the corporal works of mercy?
{q820.a} A. We may briefly state the corporal works of mercy by saying that we are obliged to help the poor in all their forms of want.
