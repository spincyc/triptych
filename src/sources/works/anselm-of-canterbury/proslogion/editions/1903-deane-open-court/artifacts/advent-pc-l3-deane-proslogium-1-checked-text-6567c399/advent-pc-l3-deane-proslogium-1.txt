{Prosl. 1 p. 3} Up now, slight man! flee, for a little while, thy occupations; hide thyself, for a time, from thy disturbing thoughts.
{Prosl. 1 p. 3} Cast aside, now, thy burdensome cares, and put away thy toilsome business.
{Prosl. 1 p. 3} Yield room for some little time to God; and rest for a little time in him.
{Prosl. 1 p. 3} Enter the inner chamber of thy mind; shut out all thoughts save that of God, and such as can aid thee in seeking him; close thy door and seek him.
{Prosl. 1 p. 3} Speak now, my whole heart! speak now to God, saying, I seek thy face; thy face, Lord, will I seek (Psalms xxvii. 8).
{Prosl. 1 p. 3} And come thou now, O Lord my God, teach my heart where and how it may seek thee, where and how it may find thee.
{Prosl. 1 p. 6} Be it mine to look up to thy light, even from afar, even from the depths.
{Prosl. 1 p. 6} Teach me to seek thee, and reveal thyself to me, when I seek thee, for I cannot seek thee, except thou teach me, nor find thee, except thou reveal thyself.
{Prosl. 1 p. 6} Let me seek thee in longing, let me long for thee in seeking; let me find thee in love, and love thee in finding.
