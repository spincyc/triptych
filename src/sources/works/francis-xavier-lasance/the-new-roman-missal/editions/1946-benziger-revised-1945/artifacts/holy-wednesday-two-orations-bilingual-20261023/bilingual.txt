Receive, we beseech Thee, O almighty God, the gift we offer, and deign to bring it to pass that what we perform in the mystery of the passion of Thy Son, Our Lord we may follow with loving affection. Through the same.
Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine, munus oblatum, et dignanter operare: ut, quod passionis Filii tui Domini nostri mysterio gerimus, piis affectibus consequamur. Per eumdem.
Bestow Thy plenteous gifts on our senses, O almighty God, that through the temporal death of Thy Son, of which the august mysteries give testimony, we may have confidence that thou hast given us life everlasting. Through the same.
Largire sensibus nostris, omnipotens Deus: ut, per temporalem Filii tui mortem, quam mysteria veneranda testantur, vitam te nobis dedisse perpetuam confidamus. Per eumdem.
