{XXXVIII:13} For it is thy heart’s desire that is thy prayer; and if thy desire continues uninterrupted, thy prayer continueth also.
{XXXVIII:13} For not without a meaning did the Apostle say, “Pray without ceasing.”
{XXXVIII:13} Are we to be “without ceasing” bending the knee, prostrating the body, or lifting up our hands, that he says, “Pray without ceasing”?
{XXXVIII:13} Or if it is in this sense that we say that we “pray,” this, I believe, we cannot do “without ceasing.”
{XXXVIII:13} There is another inward kind of prayer without ceasing, which is the desire of the heart.
{XXXVIII:13} Whatever else you are doing, if you do but long for that Sabbath, you do not cease to pray.
{XXXVIII:13} If you would never cease to pray, never cease to long after it.
{XXXVIII:13} The continuance of thy longing is the continuance of thy prayer.
