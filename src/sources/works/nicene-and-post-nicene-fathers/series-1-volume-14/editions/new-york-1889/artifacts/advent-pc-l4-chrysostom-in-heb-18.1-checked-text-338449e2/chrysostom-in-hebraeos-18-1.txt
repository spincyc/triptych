{18.1} Therefore sacrifices are not God's will, but the abolition of sacrifices. Wherefore they sacrifice contrary to His will.
{18.1} What is "To do Thy will"? To give up Myself, He means: This is the will of God. "By which Will we are sanctified."
{18.1} Or he even means something still further, that the sacrifices do not make men clean, but the Will of God. Therefore to offer sacrifice is not the will of God.
