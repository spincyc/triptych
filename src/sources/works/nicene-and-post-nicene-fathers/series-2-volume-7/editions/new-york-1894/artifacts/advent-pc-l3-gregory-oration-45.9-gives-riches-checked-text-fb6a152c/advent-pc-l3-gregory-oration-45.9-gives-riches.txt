{45.9} And He who gives riches becomes poor; for He assumes the poverty of my flesh, that I may assume the riches of His Godhead.
{45.9} He that is full empties Himself; for He empties Himself of His Glory for a short while, that I may have a share in His Fulness.

{45.9} What is this mystery that is around me?
{45.9} I had a share in the Image and I did not keep it; He partakes of my flesh that He may both save the Image and make the flesh immortal.
{45.9} He communicates a Second Communion, far more marvellous than the first, inasmuch as then He imparted the better nature, but now He Himself assumes the worse.
