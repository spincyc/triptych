{29:19} He was born—but He had been begotten: He was born of a woman—but she was a Virgin.
{29:19} The first is human the second Divine.
{29:19} In His Human nature He had no Father, but also in His Divine Nature no Mother.
{29:19} Both these belong to Godhead.
{29:19} He dwelt in the womb—but He was recognized by the Prophet, himself still in the womb, leaping before the Word, for Whose sake He came into being.
{29:19} He was wrapped in swaddling clothes—but He took off the swathing bands of the grave by His rising again.
{29:19} He was laid in a manger—but He was glorified by Angels, and proclaimed by a star, and worshipped by the Magi.
