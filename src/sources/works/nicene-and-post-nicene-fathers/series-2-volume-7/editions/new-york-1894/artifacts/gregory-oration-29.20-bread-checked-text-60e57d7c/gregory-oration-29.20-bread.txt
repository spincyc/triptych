{29:20} He was baptized as Man—but He remitted sins as God—not because He needed purificatory rites Himself, but that He might sanctify the element of water.
{29:20} He was tempted as Man, but He conquered as God; yea, He bids us be of good cheer, for He has overcome the world.
{29:20} He hungered—but He fed thousands; yea, He is the Bread that giveth life, and That is of heaven.
{29:20} He thirsted—but He cried, If any man thirst, let him come unto Me and drink.
{29:20} Yea, He promised that fountains should flow from them that believe.
{29:20} He was wearied, but He is the Rest of them that are weary and heavy laden.
{29:20} He was heavy with sleep, but He walked lightly over the sea.
