{15:1} We preach not one advent only of Christ, but a second also, far more glorious than the former.
{15:1} For the former gave a view of His patience; but the latter brings with it the crown of a divine kingdom.
{15:1} For all things, for the most part, are twofold in our Lord Jesus Christ: a twofold generation; one, of God, before the ages; and one, of a Virgin, at the close of the ages:
{15:1} His descents twofold; one, the unobserved, like rain on a fleece; and a second His open coming, which is to be.
{15:1} In His former advent, He was wrapped in swaddling clothes in the manger; in His second, He covereth Himself with light as with a garment.
{15:1} In His first coming, He endured the Cross, despising shame; in His second, He comes attended by a host of Angels, receiving glory.
{15:1} We rest not then upon His first advent only, but look also for His second.
