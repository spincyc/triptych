{XXI:3} Let us then, dearly beloved, give thanks to God the Father, through His Son, in the Holy Spirit, Who “for His great mercy, wherewith He has loved us,” has had pity on us: and “when we were dead in sins, has quickened us together in Christ,” that we might be in Him a new creation and a new production.
{XXI:3} Let us put off then the old man with his deeds: and having obtained a share in the birth of Christ let us renounce the works of the flesh.
{XXI:3} Christian, acknowledge thy dignity, and becoming a partner in the Divine nature, refuse to return to the old baseness by degenerate conduct.
{XXI:3} Remember the Head and the Body of which thou art a member.
{XXI:3} Recollect that thou wert rescued from the power of darkness and brought out into God’s light and kingdom.
{XXI:3} By the mystery of Baptism thou wert made the temple of the Holy Ghost: do not put such a denizen to flight from thee by base acts, and subject thyself once more to the devil’s thraldom: because thy purchase money is the blood of Christ, because He shall judge thee in truth Who ransomed thee in mercy, who with the Father and the Holy Spirit reigns for ever and ever. Amen.
