{IV:7} Would it not have seemed to you that a herald would have cried, “Go away, make room.”
{IV:7} Instead of the herald’s cry “Go away,” John says “Come.”
{IV:7} The herald makes men stand back from the judge; to the Judge John calls.
{IV:7} Yes, indeed, John calls men to the lowly One, that they may not experience what He will be as the exalted Judge.
