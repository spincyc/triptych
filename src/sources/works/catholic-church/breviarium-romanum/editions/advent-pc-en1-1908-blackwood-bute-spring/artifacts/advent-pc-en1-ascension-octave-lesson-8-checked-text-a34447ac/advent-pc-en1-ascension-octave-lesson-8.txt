{Octave of the Ascension, lesson 8, p. 488} These hills are his lofty and noble achievements.
{Octave of the Ascension, lesson 8, p. 488} “Behold, He cometh! leaping upon the mountains!”
{Octave of the Ascension, lesson 8, p. 488} When He came to redeem us, He came, if I may so say, in leaps.
{Octave of the Ascension, lesson 8, p. 488} My dearly beloved brethren, would you know what His leaps were?
{Octave of the Ascension, lesson 8, pp. 488-489} From heaven he leapt into the womb of the Virgin, from the womb into the manger, from the manger on to the Cross, from the Cross into the grave, and from the grave up to heaven.
{Octave of the Ascension, lesson 8, p. 489} Lo, how the Truth made manifest in the Flesh did leap for our sakes, that He might draw us to run after Him; for this end did He “rejoice, as a strong man to run a race,” that we might passionately say: “Draw us after Thee; we will run after the savour of Thine ointments.”
