{p240 adv3-mattins-l4} Fourth Lesson.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l4} The Lesson is taken from the Sermons of Pope St Leo [the Great,] (Second on the December Fast, and Almsgiving.)
{p240 adv3-mattins-l4} DEARLY beloved brethren, with the care which becometh us as the shepherd of your souls, we urge upon you the rigid observance of this December Fast.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l4} The month of December hath come round again, and with it this devout custom of the Church.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l4} The fruits of the year, which is drawing to a close, are now all gathered in, and we most meetly offer our abstinence to God as a sacrifice of thanksgiving.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l4} And what can be more useful than fasting, that exercise by which we draw nigh to God, make a stand against the devil, and overcome the softer enticements of sin?
