{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} I. From the Vespers of the Saturday before Advent Sunday till those of Candlemas, both inclusive.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} Antiphon. Maiden! Mother of Him Who redeemed us, thou that abidest
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} Heaven’s open gate, and the Star of the Sea, come, succour the fallen!
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} Fallen indeed we are, but fain would rise by thy succour.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} Thou that beyond nature’s course hast borne in time the Eternal;
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} Thou that a Virgin before, and after that childbirth remainest,
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} From the Archangel’s lips the quickening message receiving,
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1} Mother of JESUS and us, turn thine eyes of mercy on sinners.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-advent} Verse. The Angel of the Lord announced unto Mary,
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-advent} Answer. And she conceived of the Holy Ghost.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-advent} Let us pray.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-advent} WE beseech Thee, O Lord, pour Thy grace into our hearts; that, as we have known the Incarnation of Thy Son JESUS Christ by the message of an Angel, so by His Passion and Cross we may be brought unto the glory of His Resurrection.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-advent} Through the same Christ our Lord.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-advent} Answer. Amen.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-christmas} From the First Vespers of Christmas inclusive, the Verse and Answer and Prayer are as follows:
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-christmas} Verse. After thy delivery thou still remainest a Virgin undefiled.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-christmas} Answer. Mother of God, pray for us.
{p210 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-christmas} Let us pray.
{p210-211 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-christmas} O GOD, Who, by the fruitful virginity of the Blessed Mary, hast given unto mankind the rewards of everlasting life; grant, we beseech Thee, that we may continually feel the might of her intercession; through whom we have worthily received the Author of our life, our Lord JESUS Christ Thy Son.
{p211 compline-bvm-antiphon-1-christmas} Answer. Amen.
{p211 footnote-1} Of these four Antiphons, “Maiden Mother” is ascribed to Hermann the Cripple, a monk of Reichenau, who died A.D. 1052.
