{p214 adv1-mattins-l1} FIRST NOCTURN.
{p214 adv1-mattins-l1} First Lesson. (Isa. i. 1.)
{p214 adv1-mattins-l1} THE vision of Isaiah, the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.
{p214 adv1-mattins-l1} Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth, for the LORD hath spoken: I have nourished and brought up children: and they have rebelled against Me.
{p214 adv1-mattins-l1} The ox knoweth his owner, and the ass his master’s crib: but Israël doth not know Me, and My people doth not consider.
