{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} First Lesson.
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} The Lesson is taken from the Holy Gospel according to Luke (i. 26.)
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} AT that time: The Angel Gabriel was sent from God, unto a city of Galilee, named Nazareth, to a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David: and the virgin’s name was Mary.
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} And so on.
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} Homily by St Ambrose, Bishop [of Milan,] (Bk. ii. on Luke.)
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} The mysteries of God are unsearchable, and it is especially declared by a Prophet, that a man can hardly know His counsels. (Wisd. ix. 13.)
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} Nevertheless, some things have been revealed to us, and we may gather from some of the words and works of the Lord our Saviour, that there was a special purpose of God, in the fact that she who was chosen to be the mother of the Lord was espoused to a man.
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} Why did not the power of the Highest overshadow her before she was so espoused?
{p249 ember-wed-mattins-l1} Perhaps it was lest any might blasphemously say that she had conceived in adultery the Holy One.
