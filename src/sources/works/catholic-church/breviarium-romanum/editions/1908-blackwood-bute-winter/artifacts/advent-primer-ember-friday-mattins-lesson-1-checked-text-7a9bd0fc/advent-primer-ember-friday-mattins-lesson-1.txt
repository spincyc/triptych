{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} First Lesson.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} The Lesson is taken from the Holy Gospel according to Luke (i. 39.)
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} AT that time: Mary arose, and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Judah, and entered into the house of Zacharias, and saluted Elizabeth.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} And so on.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} Homily by St Ambrose, Bishop [of Milan,] (Commentary on Luke, Bk. ii. c. i.)
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} When any one asketh another for credence, he is bound to give some reasonable ground.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} And so the Angel, when he announced to Mary the counsel of God, gave, as a proof, the conception of Elizabeth, then aged and barren, that Mary might perceive, by this example, that with God nothing is impossible.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} When the holy Virgin had heard it, she arose and went to visit her cousin.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} She did not go to see if what she had heard was true, because she did not believe God, or because she knew not who the messenger had been, or yet because she doubted the fact adduced in proof.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} She went joyfully as one who hath received a mercy in answer to his vow goeth to pay the same.
{p252 ember-fri-mattins-l1} She went with devotion, as a godly person goeth to execute a religious duty.
{p252-253 ember-fri-mattins-l1} She went into the hill country in joyful haste.
{p253 ember-fri-mattins-l1} And is it not something that she went up into the hills?
{p253 ember-fri-mattins-l1} God was already in her womb, and her feeling bore her continually upward.
{p253 ember-fri-mattins-l1} The grace of the Holy Spirit knoweth no slow working.
