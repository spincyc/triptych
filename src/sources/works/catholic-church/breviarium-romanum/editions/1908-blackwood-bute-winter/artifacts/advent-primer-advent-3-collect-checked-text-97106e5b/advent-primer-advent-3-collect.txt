{p243 adv3-lauds-prayer} Prayer.
{p243 adv3-lauds-prayer} O LORD, we beseech Thee, mercifully incline Thine ears unto our prayers, and lighten the darkness of our minds by the grace of Thy heavenly visitation; Who livest and reignest with God the Father, in the unity of the Holy Ghost, one God, world without end.
{p243 adv3-lauds-prayer} Amen.
