{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} First Responsory.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} I look from afar, and, behold, I see the Power of God coming, and a cloud covering all the land. Go ye out to meet Him, and say: Tell us if Thou art He, That shalt reign over God’s people Israël.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Verse. Both low and high, rich and poor together.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Answer. Go ye out to meet Him, and say.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Verse. Give ear, O Shepherd of Israël, Thou That leadest Joseph like a flock.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Answer. Tell us if Thou art He.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Verse. Lift up your gates, O ye princes; and be ye lift up, ye everlasting doors, and the King of glory shall come in.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Answer. That shalt reign over God’s people Israël.
{p214 adv1-mattins-r1} Verse. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.
