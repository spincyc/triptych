{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} Seventh Lesson.
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} The Lesson is taken from the Holy Gospel according to Matthew (xi. 2.)
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} AT that time: When John had heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples, and said unto Him: Art Thou He That should come, or do we look for another?
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} And so on.
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} Homily by Pope St Gregory [the Great,] (10th Homily on the Gospels.)
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} The sight of so many signs and so many mighty works should have been a source of wonder, and not a stumbling-block.
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} And yet the unfaithful found these very works a rock of offence, when they afterwards saw Him Who had worked so many miracles dying on the Cross.
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} Hence Paul saith: “We preach Christ crucified, unto the Jews a stumbling-block and unto the Gentiles foolishness.” (1 Cor. i. 23.)
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} It is indeed folly in the eyes of men to say that the Author of life died for men: and thus men put as a stumbling-block to hinder them from coming to JESUS, the very thing that doth oblige them the most unto Him.
{p229 adv2-mattins-l7} For the more humbling God hath undergone for man’s sake, the more worthy is He that man should worship Him.
