{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Hymn.
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} CREATOR of the starry pole,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Saviour of all who live,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} And light of every faithful soul,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} JESU, these prayers receive.
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Who sooner than our foe malign
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Should triumph, from above
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Didst come, to be the medicine
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Of a sick world, in love;
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} And the deep wounds to cleanse and cure
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Of a whole race, didst go,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Pure Victim, from a Virgin pure,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} The bitter Cross unto.
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Who hast a Name, and hast a Power,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} The height and depth to sway,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} And Angels bow, and devils cower,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} In transport or dismay;
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Thou too shalt be our Judge at length;
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Lord, in Thy grace bestow
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Thy weapons of celestial strength,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} And snatch us from the foe.
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} Honour and glory, power and praise,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} To Father, and to Son,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} And Holy Ghost, be paid always,
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-hymn} The Eternal Three in One. Amen.
{p213 footnote-1} Another hymn of the Ambrosian school, altered almost beyond recognition; one verse omitted; translation by the late Card. Newman.
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-versicle} Verse. Drop down, ye heavens, from above, and let the skies pour down the Righteous One.
{p213 adv-sat-vespers-versicle} Answer. Let the earth open, and let her bring forth the Saviour.
