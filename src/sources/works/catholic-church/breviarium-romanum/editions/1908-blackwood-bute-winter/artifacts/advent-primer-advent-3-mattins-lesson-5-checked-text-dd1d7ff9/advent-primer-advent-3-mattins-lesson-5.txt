{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} Fifth Lesson.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} FASTING hath ever been the bread of strength.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} From abstinence proceed pure thoughts, reasonable desires, and healthy counsels.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} By voluntary mortifications the flesh dieth to lust, and the soul is renewed in might.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} But since fasting is not the only mean whereby we get health for our souls, let us add to our fasting works of mercy.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} Let us spend in good deeds what we take from indulgence.
{p240 adv3-mattins-l5} Let our fast become the banquet of the poor.
