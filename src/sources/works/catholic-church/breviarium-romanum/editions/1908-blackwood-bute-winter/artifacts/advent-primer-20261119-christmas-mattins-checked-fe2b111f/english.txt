{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} Fourth Lesson.
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} The Lesson is taken from the Sermons of Pope St Leo [the Great,] (1st for Christmas.)
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} DEARLY beloved brethren, “Unto us is born this day a Saviour,” (Luke ii. 11.) Let us rejoice.
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} It would be unlawful to be sad to-day, for to-day is Life’s Birthday: the Birthday of that Life, Which, for us dying creatures, taketh away the sting of death, and bringeth the bright promise of the eternal gladness hereafter.
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} It would be unlawful for any man to refuse to partake in our rejoicing.
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} All men have an equal share in the great cause of our joy, for, since our Lord, Who is the destroyer of sin and of death, findeth that all are bound under the condemnation, He is come to make all free.
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} Rejoice, O thou that art holy, thou drawest nearer to thy crown!
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} Rejoice, O thou that art sinful, thy Saviour offereth thee pardon!
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} Rejoice also, O thou Gentile, God calleth thee to life!
{p274 christmas-mattins-l4} For the Son of God, when the fulness of the time was come, which had been fixed by the unsearchable counsel of God, took upon Him the nature of man, that He might reconcile that nature to Him Who made it, and so the devil, the inventor of death, is met and beaten in that very flesh which hath been the field of his victory.
{p274 christmas-mattins-r4} Fourth Responsory.
{p274 christmas-mattins-r4} How great is this mystery, how wonderful is the teaching of the faith!
{p274 christmas-mattins-r4} The beasts saw the new-born Lord lying in a manger.
{p274 christmas-mattins-r4} Blessed is that Virgin whose womb was made meet to bear our Lord Christ.
{p274 christmas-mattins-r4} Verse. Hail, Mary, full of grace: the Lord is with thee.
{p274 christmas-mattins-r4} Answer. Blessed is that Virgin whose womb was made meet to bear our Lord Christ.
{p274 christmas-mattins-l5} Fifth Lesson.
{p274-275 christmas-mattins-l5} WHEN our Lord entered the field of battle against the devil, He did so with a great and wonderful fairness.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} Being Himself the Almighty, He laid aside His uncreated Majesty to fight with our cruel enemy in our weak flesh.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} He brought against him the very shape, the very nature of our mortality, “yet without sin.” (Heb. iv. 15.)
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} His birth however was not a birth like other births—for no other is born pure, nay, not the little child whose life endureth but a day on the earth.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} To His birth alone the throes of human passion had not contributed, in His alone no consequence of sin had had part.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} For His Mother was chosen a Virgin of the kingly lineage of David, and when she was to grow heavy with the sacred Child, her soul had already conceived Him before her body.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} She knew the counsel of God announced to her by the Angel, lest the unwonted events should alarm her.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l5} The future Mother of God knew what was to be wrought in her by the Holy Ghost, and that her modesty was absolutely safe.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l6} Sixth Lesson.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l6} THEREFORE, dearly beloved brethren, let us give thanks to God the Father, through His Son, in the Holy Ghost: Who, “for His great love wherewith He loved us,” hath had mercy on us: and, “even when we were dead in sins, hath quickened us together with Christ,” (Eph. ii. 4, 5,) that in Him we might be a new creature, and a new workmanship.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l6} Let us then put off the old man with his deeds (Col. iii. 9); and, having obtained a share in the Sonship of Christ, let us renounce the deeds of the flesh.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l6} Learn, O Christian, how great thou art, who hast been made partaker of the Divine nature, (2 Pet. i. 4,) and fall not again by corrupt conversation into the beggarly elements above which thou art lifted.
{p275 christmas-mattins-l6} Remember Whose Body it is Whereof thou art made a member, and Who is its Head, (1 Cor. vi. 15.)
{p275 christmas-mattins-l6} Remember that it is He That hath delivered thee from the power of darkness and hath translated thee into God’s light, and God’s kingdom, (Col. i. 13.)
{p275 christmas-mattins-r6} Sixth Responsory.
{p275 christmas-mattins-r6} O Mary, how holy and how spotless is thy virginity—I am too dull to praise thee!
{p275 christmas-mattins-r6} For thou hast borne in thy breast Him Whom the heavens cannot contain.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} Seventh Lesson.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} The Lesson is taken from the Holy Gospel according to Luke (ii. 1.)
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} AT that time: There went out a decree from Cæsar Augustus that all the world should be enrolled. And so on.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} Homily by Pope St Gregory [the Great,] (8th on the Gospels.)
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} By God’s mercy we are to say three Masses to-day, so that there is not much time left for preaching; but at the same time the occasion of the Lord’s Birth-day itself obliges me to speak a few words.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} I will first ask why, when the Lord was to be born, the world was enrolled?
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} Was it not to herald the appearing of Him by Whom the elect are enrolled in the book of life?
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} Whereas the Prophet saith of the reprobate: “Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.” (Ps. lxviii. 29.)
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} Then, the Lord is born in Bethlehem.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} Now the name Bethlehem signifieth “the House of Bread,” and thus it is the birth-place of Him Who hath said, “I am the Living Bread, Which came down from heaven.” (John vi. 51.)
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} We see then that this name of Bethlehem was prophetically given to the place where Christ was born, because it was there that He was to appear in the flesh by Whom the souls of the faithful are fed unto life eternal.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} He was born, not in His Mother’s house, but away from home.
{p276 christmas-mattins-l7} And this is a mystery, showing that this our mortality into which He was born was not the home of Him Who is begotten of the Father before the worlds.
