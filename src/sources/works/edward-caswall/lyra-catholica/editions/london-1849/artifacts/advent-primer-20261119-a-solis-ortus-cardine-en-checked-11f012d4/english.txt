{p. 49} LAUDS.
{p. 49} A solis ortus cardine.
{p. 49} From the far-blazing gate of morn
{p. 49} To earth’s remotest shore,
{p. 49} Let every tongue confess to Him
{p. 49} Whom holy Mary bore.
{p. 50} Lo! the great Maker of the world,
{p. 50} Lord of eternal years,
{p. 50} To save his creatures, veil’d beneath
{p. 50} A creature’s form appears.
{p. 50} A spotless maiden’s virgin breast
{p. 50} With heav’nly grace He fills;
{p. 50} In her pure womb He is conceiv’d,
{p. 50} And there in secret dwells.
{p. 50} That bosom, Chastity’s sweet home,
{p. 50} Becomes, oh, blest reward!
{p. 50} The shrine of Heav’n’s immortal King,
{p. 50} The temple of the Lord.
{p. 50} And Mary bears the babe, foretold
{p. 50} By an Archangel’s voice;
{p. 50} Whose presence made the Baptist leap,
{p. 50} And in the womb rejoice.
{p. 50} A manger scantly strewn with hay
{p. 50} Becomes th’ Eternal’s bed;
{p. 50} And He, who feeds each smallest bird,
{p. 50} Himself with milk is fed.
{p. 51} Straightway with joy the Heav’ns are fill’d,
{p. 51} The hosts angelic sing;
{p. 51} And shepherds hasten to adore
{p. 51} Their Shepherd and their King.
{p. 51} Praise to the Father! praise to Thee,
{p. 51} Thou Virgin’s holy Son!
{p. 51} Praise to the Spirit Paraclete,
{p. 51} While endless ages run.
