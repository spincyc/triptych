{p. 38} ANTIPHONS OF THE BLESSED VIRGIN.
{p. 38} From the First Sunday in Advent to the Feast of the Purification.
{p. 38} Alma Redemptoris Mater.
{p. 38} Mother of Christ! hear thou thy people’s cry,
{p. 38} Star of the deep, and Portal of the sky!
{p. 38} Mother of Him who thee from nothing made,
{p. 38} Sinking we strive, and call to thee for aid:
{p. 38} Oh, by that joy which Gabriel brought to thee,
{p. 38} Thou Virgin first and last, let us thy mercy see.
