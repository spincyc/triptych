{p. 43} On Sundays and Week-days during Advent.
{p. 43} VESPERS.
{p. 43} Creator alme siderum.
{p. 43} Creator of the starry frame!
{p. 43} Eternal Light of all who live!
{p. 43} Jesu, Redeemer of mankind!
{p. 43} An ear to thy poor suppliants give.
{p. 44} When man was sunk in sin and death,
{p. 44} Lost in the depth of Satan’s snare,
{p. 44} Love brought Thee down to cure our ills,
{p. 44} By taking of those ills a share.
{p. 44} Thou, for the sake of guilty men,
{p. 44} Causing thine own pure blood to flow,
{p. 44} Didst issue from thy Virgin shrine,
{p. 44} And to the Cross a Victim go.
{p. 44} So great the glory of thy might,
{p. 44} If we but chance thy name to sound,
{p. 44} At once all Heaven and Hell unite
{p. 44} In bending low with awe profound.
{p. 44} Great Judge of all! in that last day,
{p. 44} When friends shall fail, and foes combine,
{p. 44} Be present then with us, we pray,
{p. 44} To guard us with thy arm divine.
{p. 44} To God the Father, and the Son,
{p. 44} All praise and power and glory be;
{p. 44} With Thee, O holy Comforter!
{p. 44} Henceforth through all eternity.
{p. 45} [Within the Octave of the Feast of the Conception.]
{p. 45} O Jesu! born of Virgin bright,
{p. 45} Immortal glory be to Thee;
{p. 45} Praise to the Father infinite,
{p. 45} And Holy Ghost eternally.
