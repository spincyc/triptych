{p. 46} LAUDS.
{p. 46} En clara vox redarguit.
{p. 46} Hark! an awful voice is sounding;
{p. 46} “Christ is nigh!” it seems to say;
{p. 46} “Cast away the dreams of darkness,
{p. 46} O ye children of the day!”
{p. 47} Startled at the solemn warning,
{p. 47} Let the earth-bound soul arise;
{p. 47} Christ her Sun, all sloth dispelling,
{p. 47} Shines upon the morning skies.
{p. 47} Lo! the Lamb so long expected,
{p. 47} Comes with pardon down from Heav’n;
{p. 47} Let us haste, with tears of sorrow,
{p. 47} One and all to be forgiven.
{p. 47} So, when next He comes with glory,
{p. 47} Wrapping all the earth in fear,
{p. 47} May He then as our Defender
{p. 47} On the clouds of Heav’n appear.
{p. 47} Honour, glory, virtue, merit,
{p. 47} To the Father and the Son,
{p. 47} With the everlasting Spirit,
{p. 47} While eternal ages run.
