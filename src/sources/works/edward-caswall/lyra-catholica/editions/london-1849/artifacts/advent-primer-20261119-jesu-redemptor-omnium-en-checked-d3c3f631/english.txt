{p. 48} Christmas-Day.
{p. 48} VESPERS AND MATINS.
{p. 48} Jesu Redemptor omnium.
{p. 48} Jesu, Redeemer of the world!
{p. 48} Who, ere the earliest dawn of light,
{p. 48} Wast from eternal ages born,
{p. 48} Immense in glory as in might;
{p. 48} Immortal Hope of all mankind!
{p. 48} In whom the Father’s face we see;
{p. 48} Hear Thou the prayers thy people pour
{p. 48} This day throughout the world to Thee.
{p. 48} Remember, O Creator Lord!
{p. 48} That in the Virgin’s sacred womb
{p. 48} Thou wast conceiv’d, and of her flesh
{p. 48} Didst our mortality assume.
{p. 48} This ever-blest recurring day
{p. 48} Its witness bears, that all alone,
{p. 48} From thy own Father’s bosom forth,
{p. 48} To save the world Thou camest down.
{p. 49} O Day! to which the seas and sky,
{p. 49} And earth and Heav’n, glad welcome sing;
{p. 49} O Day! which heal’d our misery,
{p. 49} And brought on earth salvation’s King.
{p. 49} We too, O Lord, who have been clean’sd
{p. 49} In thy own fount of blood divine,
{p. 49} Offer the tribute of sweet song,
{p. 49} On this blest natal day of thine.
{p. 49} O Jesu! born of Virgin bright,
{p. 49} Immortal glory be to Thee;
{p. 49} Praise to the Father infinite,
{p. 49} And Holy Ghost eternally.
