{p. 45} MATINS.
{p. 45} Verbum supernum prodiens.
{p. 45} O Thou, who thine own Father’s breast
{p. 45} Forsaking, Word sublime!
{p. 45} Didst come to aid a world distress’d
{p. 45} In thy appointed time:
{p. 45} Our hearts enlighten with thy ray,
{p. 45} And kindle with thy love;
{p. 45} That, dead to earthly things, we may
{p. 45} Live but to things above.
{p. 46} So when before the Judgment-seat
{p. 46} The sinner hears his doom,
{p. 46} And when a voice divinely sweet
{p. 46} Shall call the righteous home;
{p. 46} Safe from the black and fiery flood
{p. 46} That sweeps the dread abyss,
{p. 46} May we behold the face of God
{p. 46} In everlasting bliss.
{p. 46} Now to the Father, with the Son,
{p. 46} And Spirit evermore,
{p. 46} Be glory while the ages run,
{p. 46} As in all time before.
