{in-adventu-1.1} To-day, my brethren, we celebrate the beginning of the season of advent.
{in-adventu-1.1} The name of this, as of all our other solemnities, is familiar enough and well known to the entire world.
{in-adventu-1.1} But perhaps the meaning of the name is not so generally understood.
{in-adventu-1.1} For the unhappy children of Adam, taken up as they are with the pursuit of things transitory and perishable, pay but little attention to what is salutary and true.
