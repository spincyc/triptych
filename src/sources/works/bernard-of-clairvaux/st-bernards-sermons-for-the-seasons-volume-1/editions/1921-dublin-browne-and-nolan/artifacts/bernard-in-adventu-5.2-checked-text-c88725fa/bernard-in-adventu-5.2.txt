{in-adventu-5.2} Keep the word of God in the same way in which the food of thy body can be best preserved; for it is a living bread and the food of thy soul.
{in-adventu-5.2} Material bread, whilst it is kept in the cupboard may be stolen by thieves, or nibbled by mice, or may become stale and unfit for use.
{in-adventu-5.2} But once it has been assimilated, we have nothing further to fear from such possibilities.
{in-adventu-5.2} It is thus we should keep the word of God, for “blessed are they who keep it” so.
{in-adventu-5.2} Receive this spiritual bread, therefore, into the stomach of your minds, and having been there properly digested, let it be transmitted thence to the affections and the will.
{in-adventu-5.2} Do not forget (like David) to eat thy bread, lest thy heart (like his) should wither, but nourish thyself therewith that thy soul may be filled “as with marrow and fatness.”
