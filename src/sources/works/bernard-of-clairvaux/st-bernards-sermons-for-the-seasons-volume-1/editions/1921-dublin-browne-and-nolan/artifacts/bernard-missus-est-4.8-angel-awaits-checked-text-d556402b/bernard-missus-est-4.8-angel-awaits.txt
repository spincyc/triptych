{missus-est-4.8} Behold the Angel now awaits thy answer: “it is time that he should return to the Lord Who sent him.”
{missus-est-4.8} We also, O Lady, await from thy lips the sentence of mercy and compassion, we who are so miserably groaning under the sentence of condemnation.
{missus-est-4.8} For lo! the price of our salvation is now offered to thee: if thou wilt only consent, we shall at once be set at liberty.
{missus-est-4.8} We have been created by the eternal Word of God, and behold we die: by thy momentary word we must be renewed and restored to life.
