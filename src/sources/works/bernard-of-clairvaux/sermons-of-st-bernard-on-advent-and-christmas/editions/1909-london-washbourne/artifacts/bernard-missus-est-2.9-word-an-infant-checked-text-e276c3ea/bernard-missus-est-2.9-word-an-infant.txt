{missus-est-2.9} There is seen length abbreviated, width straightened, height lowered, depth filled up.
{missus-est-2.9} There we behold light withholding its rays, the Word an infant, the Living Water athirst, Him Who is the Bread of Heaven suffering hunger.
{missus-est-2.9} Attend and see how Omnipotence is ruled, Wisdom instructed, Power sustained; the God Who rejoices the angels is become a Babe at the breast; He Who consoles the afflicted lies weeping in a manger.
{missus-est-2.9} Attend and see how joy is made sorrowful, strength becomes weakness, life death; but—what is equally wonderful—that sorrow gives joy, that weakness imparts strength, that death restores life.
