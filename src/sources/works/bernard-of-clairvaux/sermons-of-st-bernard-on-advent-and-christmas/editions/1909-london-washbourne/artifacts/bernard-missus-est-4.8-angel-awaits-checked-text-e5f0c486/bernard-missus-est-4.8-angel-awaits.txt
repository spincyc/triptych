{missus-est-4.8} The angel awaits your reply, for it is time that he should return to God, Who sent him.
{missus-est-4.8} We, too, are waiting, O Lady, for a word of mercy—we, who are groaning under the sentence of condemnation.
{missus-est-4.8} See, the price of our salvation is offered to you; if you consent, we shall at once be delivered.
{missus-est-4.8} By the Eternal Word of God we were all created, and behold we die.
{missus-est-4.8} By your short answer we shall be refreshed and recalled to life.
