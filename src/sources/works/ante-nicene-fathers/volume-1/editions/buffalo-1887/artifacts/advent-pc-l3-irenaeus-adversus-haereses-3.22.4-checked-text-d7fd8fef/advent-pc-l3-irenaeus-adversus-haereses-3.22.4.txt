{3.22.4 p. 455} In accordance with this design, Mary the Virgin is found obedient, saying, “Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word.” But Eve was disobedient; for she did not obey when as yet she was a virgin.
{3.22.4 p. 455} And even as she, having indeed a husband, Adam, but being nevertheless as yet a virgin (for in Paradise “they were both naked, and were not ashamed,” inasmuch as they, having been created a short time previously, had no understanding of the procreation of children: for it was necessary that they should first come to adult age, and then multiply from that time onward), having become disobedient, was made the cause of death, both to herself and to the entire human race; so also did Mary, having a man betrothed [to her], and being nevertheless a virgin, by yielding obedience, become the cause of salvation, both to herself and the whole human race.
{3.22.4 p. 455} And thus also it was that the knot of Eve’s disobedience was loosed by the obedience of Mary.
{3.22.4 p. 455} For what the virgin Eve had bound fast through unbelief, this did the virgin Mary set free through faith.
