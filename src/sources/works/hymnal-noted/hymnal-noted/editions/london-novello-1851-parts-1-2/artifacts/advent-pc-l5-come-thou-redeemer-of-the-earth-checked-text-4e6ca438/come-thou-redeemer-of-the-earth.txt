{35 1} Come, Thou Redeemer of the earth, Come, testify Thy Virgin-birth:
{35 1} All lands admire, all times applaud: Such is the birth that fits a God.
{36 4} Proceeding from His Chamber free, The royal hall of chastity,
{36 4} Giant of twofold substance, straight His destin'd way He runs elate.
{36 5} From God the Father He proceeds: To God the Father back He speeds:
{36 5} Proceeds, as far as very hell; Speeds back, to light ineffable.
{36 7} Thy cradle here shall glitter bright, And darkness breathe a newer light;
{36 7} Where endless faith shall shine serene, And twilight never intervene.
