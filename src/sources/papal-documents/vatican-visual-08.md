## Limits and calibration

Visual acceptance concerns the exact frozen bytes and composition only. Source collation, rights clearance, mechanical text fidelity, installation and publication have separate owners. No source text, PDF, generator or manifest was changed in this lane.

Sparse final pages are assessed by content, not blank area alone. *Quod Multum* retains a substantive continuation and complete blessing; *Litteras a Vobis* retains a substantive blessing continuation. Their natural endings pass, with the sparse observations retained above. *Optimae quidem* leaves only one trailing body line before dating/signature; *Iucunda* and *Decessorum* orphan a signature; *Decor Carmeli* leaves dating/signature alone. Those exact hashes require layout correction and fresh review. All terminal notices are visible and legible; the Romanorum notice’s word joins still require correction.
