# Pius X historical source review

Reviewed 26 October 2026. The bounded universe is the 111 Pius X Latin
deliveries in the finalized catalog candidate set: the initial 108 records
and three separately indexed related regulations. Each now has an identified
historical Latin expression and publication basis, including the independently
checked prior Pascendi approval. The source selections and correction below
resolve the initially mixed Sapienti consilio delivery and the Editae saepe
signature error. These are source clearances, not 111 independent papal acts,
installed PDFs, or claims of whole-text image collation.

Every candidate was mechanically extracted from the complete hashed Vatican HTML. Twelve complete official journal PDFs were acquired and hashed. The review inspected Latin opening/body samples, terminal dating/signature passages, and extracted note/annex boundaries in journal OCR. Normalized matching located those passages and screened every substantive extracted block against its cited range; the screen is an identity aid, not verbal collation. Scan OCR and web transcription differences are retained explicitly. Modern bibliographic footers are excluded by exact whole-block matching; their title markers are removed only under the pinned transformation.

Historical source editions and translations retain their own recorded rights, under [the rights position](../inventories/papal-documents-rights-v1.toml) and [source-edition profile](../../../guidance/papal-documents.md). Dates and bibliographic corrections below require catalog/source-note integration before public rendering.
