## Scope and method

Each assigned PNG produced by the repository `pdf-review` run was opened as its own full-size image (1700x2200 source raster) with the Read tool before its observation was recorded; no contact sheet, thumbnail, new rasterization, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was performed. One JSON observation per page was appended to the lane ledger after viewing.

Each page was checked for the displayed title, responsible issuer (the printed attribution, which in this series may include the salutation), the "Latin" label and omission of the unresolved act date against the frozen metadata; headings, two-column flow and gutter, margins, glyphs, clipping and overlap; natural source closing; modern digitizer signatures; raw placeholders; and the coordinate-reconstruction addendum: (a) every printed marginal summary as its own TAB-led block, never fused into a body sentence or hyphen-split word; (b) every printed page-foot note as its own block at the page break, never run into a body, synopsis or protocol paragraph; (c) natural two-column reading order; (d) running heads, folios and signature marks left inline (non-blocking, recorded) and misplaced but separate marginal blocks; (e) fragment presentation.

Mechanical support (read-only, lane-local scripts `mech*.py`, `margs.py`): the source selection of every document was split into paragraphs from the reading-order derivative at the manifest line ranges. It contains **220 TAB-led marginal paragraphs**, and every one matched a standalone `\par`-delimited paragraph of the generated body TeX (220/220). No source paragraph mixes TAB-led and body lines. A scan for note-like lines (note numerals, `Regest`, `Cherub`, `(R. T.)`, `pag. N`, `Mainard`, `Ughell`) inside multi-line paragraphs located exactly the two joins confirmed visually (unit-0095 LF 50339, unit-0131 LF 62476-62478); every other note paragraph begins its own block. No `Missing character` entry occurs in any lane PDF log; no `Google`/`Digitized` string or bare `None` occurs in any lane body TeX. This supports, but does not replace, the visual review.
