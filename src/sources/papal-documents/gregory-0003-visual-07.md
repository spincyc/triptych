## Scope and method

Each assigned page PNG from the shared `pdf-review` raster run was opened on its own with the Read tool at full size (1700×2200 source, shown at 1545×2000). No contact sheet, thumbnail, cache hit or new raster pass replaced that inspection. Pages were recorded in the lane ledger (`observations.jsonl`) in batches of four or five, right after viewing. As a secondary aid only, `pdftotext -layout` text was extracted inside the lane directory and searched for digitizer signatures, `None` placeholders and running-title lines. The search found nothing beyond the issuer line of `line-00045230`. It did not replace looking at any page.

On every page, the title line, the literal responsible-issuer header, the date and the language label were compared with the frozen `metadata` in `assignment.json`. Reviewers also checked headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins and glyphs; the natural source closing (dating clause, sanction, allocution close); and the terminal source and rights apparatus. They looked for modern provider signatures, journal running heads or page furniture inside body sentences, fused marginal summaries or notes, and raw placeholders. None of these were found; the one separately set edition note is a qualification, described below. There was no clipping, overlap, tofu or missing glyph, collapsed table, stranded heading, `Digitized by Google` signature or damaged variant, `None` placeholder, or final page carrying only a colophon. No document in this lane has a `prior_finding`.

Sparse final pages occur where a closing runs over onto a new page. ## Per-document findings

- `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00040790` (1843-08-31, *Ad gravissimas angustias*, 2 pages): **PASS with qualification** (re-issued; first issue CHANGES_REQUIRED). NON-BLOCKING edition-note placement: page 2, left column, middle: the edition's own note '1S, Leo epist. 5, ad Metropolitanos Illyrici.' is set as its own separate paragraph block between 'atque ad salutis semitam revoces, et ad Christum Iesum' and 'reducas. Non dubitamus profecto ...'. It shares no printed line with body words and is not interleaved line by line. Source OCR confirms page-foot placement in source order: LF40958 ends 'et ad Christum Iesum', LF40961 is the note, LF40964 is the omitted running head 'ANNO MDCCCXLIII,' (manifest range gap 40962-40966), and LF40967 resumes 'reducas.'. No folio or running head remains in the body. A second historical note, `1 S. Leo epist. 7, al. 8, ad Episcopos per Italiam.`, is placed after the dating clause on page 2. That placement is acceptable. The dating clause itself is broken over three short paragraphs at former source line breaks. That is nonblocking. Page 1 passes.

The allocution closes with the creation formula and `In nomine Patris + et Filii + et Spiritus + Sancti. Amen.`
- `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00045230` (consistorial allocution, 1844-01-25): the issuer line reads `ACTA GREGORII PP. XVI.`, which matches the frozen metadata.
