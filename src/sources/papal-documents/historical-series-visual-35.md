## Scope and method

Scope is exactly `.scratch/historical_series_visual/lane-35/assignment.json` (lane 35, 62 Latin documents, 68 pages). Each PNG listed in `page-queue.json` from the completed central raster pass was opened as its own full-size image with the Read tool, one image per page; no contact sheet, thumbnail, cache hit or new raster pass was used. Observations were appended to `observations.jsonl` in groups of four, after the pages had been viewed.

Each page was checked for title, responsible issuer (literal OCR form), Latin label and omission of the unresolved date against the frozen `metadata`; headings, two-column flow, gutter, margins and glyphs; natural source closing on the final page; modern provider signatures; inline marginal-summary fusion; and raw project placeholders. No clipping, overlap, tofu/missing glyphs, flattened table, stranded heading, `Rome: None`/`None` placeholder, or project-colophon-only final page was found. Every title line matched its metadata; every document has a natural *Datum Romae …* dating clause.

**Fusion standard applied.** A marginal label (Prooemium, Expositio precum, Tenor concessionis, Decretum irritans, Derogatio contrariorum, Conditio, Deputatio) or recognizable word-like marginal residue printed *inside* a body sentence or a hyphen-split body word is CHANGES_REQUIRED. A label standing as its own block or at a paragraph start, separate historical date-margin blocks, isolated symbol noise (`●`, `*`, `■`, `|`, `j`), and historical running heads (e.g. `Bull. Rom. Tom. XV.`) are recorded but not blocking.
