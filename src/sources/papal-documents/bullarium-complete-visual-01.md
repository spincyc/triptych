## Qualifications

The retained OCR visibly damages wording, names, section/chapter numerals, apparent dates and title/issuer metadata. Running-head fragments, odd punctuation and nonsensical strings remain frequent. Literal U+25A0 black squares were confirmed in generated body inputs for volume 13 unit 0101; volume 15 units 0002 and 0105; volume 18 unit 0103; volume 20 unit 0114; volume 21 unit 0198; and volume 23 unit 0048. These source-input symbols are not diagnosed as missing-font boxes. Visual acceptance does not warrant corrected or reliably readable Latin at damaged loci.

Volume 20 unit 0114 has a title ending at `alterna-`; volume 23 unit 0048 has a title ending at `sancti`. In both, the fuller source heading continues below. The printed title edges are intact; these are visible title-field limitations, not clipped rendering. Volume 17 unit 0050 has an unusually long ten-line title but includes body text on the opening page.

Volume 22 unit 0236 page 2 is sparse, retaining the remaining clause, dating and a historical note before the terminal apparatus. Several other final pages use only the left column. These are nonblocking composition limitations: they retain source content, preserve reading order and do not create an apparatus-only page. Volume 02 unit 0093 retains repeated synopsis-like lines and terminal running-head-like debris after dating; this review records their visible presence without asserting a new source-boundary judgment.

This review establishes visual composition and the distinction between source text and project apparatus. It does not independently establish historical collation, authentication, source identity, complete-act admission, exact printed-page labels, lawful distribution, text fidelity, PDF structure/fonts/metadata, installation or publication approval. The separate source and production records retain those responsibilities.
