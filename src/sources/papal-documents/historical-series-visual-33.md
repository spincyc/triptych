# Historical series visual review 33 — Bullarii Romani Continuatio lane

39 PASS, 1 CHANGES_REQUIRED: 40 assigned PDFs, 68 pages individually opened at full size; zero reused pages. This report covers only lane 33 of batch `continuatio-0016` (tome VI of the *Bullarii Romani Continuatio*, Rome 1843).

## Scope and method

Each queued PNG (1700×2200) was opened as its own full-size image with the Read tool, one image per page. No contact sheet, thumbnail, cache hit, new raster pass, source acquisition, marker scan, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was used or performed. Observations were appended to the inspection ledger after each small group of pages.

The displayed title, responsible-issuer header, omission of the unresolved date (every document in this lane has `date: null`) and the Latin/Multilingual label were compared visually with the frozen metadata in `page-queue.json`. Headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins, visible glyphs, marginal-summary placement, natural closing, historical notes and terminal source/rights apparatus were inspected. No visible clipping, overlap, missing or tofu glyph, materially flattened table, stranded heading, recognizable modern digitizer signature (including damaged "Digitized by"/"Google" footer variants) or raw placeholder such as `Rome: None` was found. Historical marginal summaries appear as separate blocks at column or page breaks, not fused into the middle of body sentences.

Canonical source admission and the successful source-fidelity/font render are upstream evidence, not independent findings of this visual lane. This report does not authorize installation or claim that installation, deployment, historical authentication or publication has occurred.
