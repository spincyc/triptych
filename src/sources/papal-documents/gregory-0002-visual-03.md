## Scope and method

No contact sheet, thumbnail, new rasterization, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was used. A `pdftotext` scan for provider-signature, running-head and placeholder strings was run only as a supplementary locator; every page was still inspected visually.

On each page the displayed title, responsible issuer, act date and language label were compared with the frozen metadata. Headings, two-column flow, gutter, margins and glyphs were checked for clipping, overlap, missing or tofu glyphs, collapsed tables and stranded headings. Each final page was checked for a natural source closing (dating clause, subscriptions or source notes) rather than project-apparatus-only content. Bodies were checked for modern provider signatures, journal or volume running heads interrupting sentences, fused marginal summaries and raw placeholders such as `None`. None was found.
