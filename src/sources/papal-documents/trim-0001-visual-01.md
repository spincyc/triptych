# Trim-0001 visual review 01 — Gregory XVI and Pius IX furniture-omitted readers

PASS: 7 assigned PDFs, 35 pages individually opened at full size; zero blocking findings. Every document carried a `prior_finding`, and each prior defect is confirmed absent, with the surrounding sentence now reading continuously. All inspections are new; zero pages were reused. This report covers only lane 01 of batch `trim-0001` (two Gregory XVI Acta readers and five Pius IX Acta readers).

## Scope and method

Each assigned page PNG, produced by the repository `pdf-review` tool for raster run `.scratch/claude-review/rasters-trim-0001/review-run.json`, was opened individually as a full-size image (1700x2200) with the Read tool. No contact sheet, thumbnail, or new rasterization was used. Observations were appended to the lane ledger page by page as inspection proceeded.

On every page, the title line, responsible issuer, date and language label were compared with the frozen metadata. Headings, two-column flow, gutter, margins, glyphs, natural source closing, separately set historical notes and the terminal source/rights apparatus were also checked. The pages were scanned for folios, running heads, gathering signatures, page-foot marks, modern digitizer signatures, fused marginal summaries and raw placeholders inside the body. In these boundary-reviewed series (Gregory XVI, Pius IX), a folio or running head left in the body would be blocking.

To locate each prior defect precisely, the frozen manifest's `physical_line_ranges` were read against the canonical raw OCR (`text_path`) for every document. The excluded raw lines were checked to be outside the selected ranges, and every other omitted gap line was checked to be standalone furniture (folios, running titles, signatures, digitizer lines, specks, or disclosed provenance/date captions). A mechanical scan of the selected ranges found no standalone numeric or running-title line. A text extraction of each PDF contained no `Digitized`, `Google` or `LjOOQie` string. These checks only located the loci; every conclusion below rests on the opened page images.
