## Scope and method

Each assigned page PNG, produced by the repository `pdf-review` raster run, was opened as its own full-size image with the Read tool (1700x2200 originals). No contact sheet, thumbnail, new rasterization, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was performed. Wording was not judged or repaired.

On every page the title line, responsible issuer, act date and text language were compared with the frozen metadata; headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins and glyphs were inspected for clipping, overlap, missing or tofu glyphs and collapsed tables; the natural source closing (dating clause, subscriptions, historical notes) was checked before the terminal project source and rights notes. Each page was also checked for modern provider signatures, journal or volume running heads, folio numbers and gathering signatures left in the body, marginal summaries or notes fused into body lines, and raw placeholders such as `None`.

Where an isolated number or noise line appeared in a body, the frozen manifest `physical_line_ranges` and the source OCR text were consulted read-only to determine whether it is printed page furniture (folio or signature) or OCR noise. A text extraction of each PDF was searched for `Google`, `Digitized`, `ACTA` and isolated numerals as a supplementary aid only; it does not replace the visual inspection.

No document in this lane carries a `prior_finding`. The source edition, admission and render receipt are upstream evidence and not independent findings of this lane. This report does not authorize or claim installation, deployment, publication or historical authentication.

## Blocking finding

- `pius-ix-acta.volume-01.line-00023322.part-02` (Christianae caritatis, beatification of Peter Claver, 1850-07-16), page 2, column 1, last body line before the Source note: `Loco »K Signi. 42`. The trailing `42` is the gathering signature at the foot of printed p. 649 (raw OCR line 23572, the final line of the last retained range, immediately before folio `650`). The same OCR prints signatures `12` at the foot of p. 169, `13` at p. 185 and `41` at p. 633, which fits 16-page sheets; on that pattern p. 649 begins sheet 42. The source note states that gathering signatures are omitted, and under the standing ruling for these boundary-reviewed Pius IX series, furniture left inside the body is BLOCKING. Correction belongs to the source selection (end the last range before the signature, or a disclosed derivative that drops it). The rest of the document, including the dating clause and the `A. CARD. LAMBRVSCHINI` subscription, is sound.
