# Gregory XVI visual review 07 — batch gregory-0002

PASS: 36 assigned PDFs and 64 pages, each opened individually at full size. No blocking findings. All inspections are new; no pages were reused. This report covers only lane 07 of Gregory XVI Acta batch `gregory-0002`. All documents come from volume 1 of the Rome 1901–1902 Acta.

## Scope and method

Each assigned page PNG from the shared `pdf-review` raster run was opened on its own with the Read tool at full size (1700×2200 source, shown at 1545×2000). No contact sheet, thumbnail, cache hit or new raster pass replaced that inspection. Each page was recorded in the lane ledger right after it was viewed.

On every page, the title line, the literal responsible-issuer header, the known date (or the omission of an unresolved date) and the language label were compared with the frozen `metadata` in `assignment.json`. Reviewers also checked headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins and glyphs; the natural source closing (dating clause, subscriptions, allocution close); and the terminal source and rights apparatus. They looked for modern provider signatures, journal running heads or page furniture inside body sentences, fused marginal summaries, and raw placeholders. None of these were found. There was no clipping, overlap, tofu or missing glyph, collapsed table, stranded heading, `Digitized by Google` signature or damaged variant, `None` placeholder, or final page carrying only a colophon. No document in this lane has a `prior_finding`.

Sparse final pages occur where a dating clause or allocution close runs over onto a new page. In each case the page carries genuine source closing text above the colophon, so it is source-bearing: `line-00068185.part-02`, `line-00070418.part-03`, `line-00071891.part-02`, `line-00072629.part-01`, `line-00073746.part-01` and `line-00022888.part-01`.

No source, PDF, raster, tool, inventory or shared record was changed. This report does not authorize installation. It does not claim that installation, deployment, historical authentication or publication has taken place.
