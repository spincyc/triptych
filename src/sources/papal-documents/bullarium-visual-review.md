# Bullarium PDF visual review

The [machine audit](bullarium-visual-review.json) is the exact-PDF review register. Its current rows cover **1676 PDF versions / 3,855 pages**: **1,673 visual PASS** and **3 CHANGES_REQUIRED**. These are layout judgments; source order, historical wording, attribution and dates have separate evidence and admission decisions.

Current source withdrawals and exact-PDF supersessions are excluded from current approval. Their completed visual reports remain in audit history. A layout PASS never reverses a source-order hold, authenticates an OCR string, or authorizes installation. This review did not install or publish a Bullarium family.

## Limits and source controls

Retained OCR can be severely damaged, with broken words, noisy title/issuer strings, historical running-head fragments and literal square characters. A visual PASS means that the declared mechanical source renders readably in the inspected PDF; it does not establish a critical text or make damaged passages citation-ready. Sparse final pages containing genuine historical dating, signatures or notes are documented as composition limits. Terminal pages containing only running-head or marginal-summary furniture receive CHANGES_REQUIRED.
