The reports pin every PDF by SHA-256. They found readable two-column flow,

against historical images. Generated extent notes also have dense line-reference
spacing; partition 1 records one internal coordination phrase in apparatus
reported for cleanup before installation. No installation or public-release
judgment is made here.
