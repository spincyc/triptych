## Scope and method

Each assigned page PNG, produced by the repository's `pdf-review` tool for this batch, was opened as its own full-size image with the Read tool (1700x2200 originals). No contact sheet, thumbnail, new raster pass, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was performed.

On every page the displayed title, responsible issuer, act date and language label were compared with the frozen metadata in the lane assignment; headings, two-column flow, gutter, margins and glyphs were checked for clipping, overlap, missing or tofu glyphs, collapsed tables and stranded headings; and the natural source closing, modern provider signatures, interrupting running heads or page furniture, fused marginal summaries and raw placeholders such as `None` were looked for. None of these defects was found. No assigned document carried a `prior_finding`.

Canonical source admission, source-fidelity checks and the successful render are upstream evidence, not findings of this visual lane. This report does not authorize or claim installation, deployment, publication or historical authentication.

## Per-document findings and qualifications

- **Identified historical fragment.** `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-02.line-00031836.part-03` (1837-08-08) shows the visible fragment title `Cum ven. frater Maurelius Stabellini — Facultas eligendi coadiutorem vicario apostolico Bombaini (fragmentum)`. Its source apparatus carries an explicit `Fragment:` statement and a `Missing content:` notice: the printing breaks off the dispositive clause after `propositos etc.` and gives `(Sequuntur clausulae etc.)` (LF32096–32098), the remaining faculty and non obstantibus clauses are not printed in this witness, and the dating clause follows. The rendered text matches that notice: the body ends `(Sequuntur clausu- lae etc.).` and is followed by `Datum Romae apud sanctam Mariam Maiorem die octava augusti ... anno septimo.`

XVI. -`, which matches the frozen metadata. Under the standing coordinator ruling this is a recorded non-blocking qualification. - **Sparse final pages with genuine source closing (not colophon-only).** These final pages carry the natural closing or dating clause before the source and rights apparatus: `line-00037414.part-01` p2, `line-00038142` p2, `line-00039870.part-03` p2, `line-00041328` p2, `line-00041478` p2, `line-00043032` p4 and `line-00031091.part-02` p2. - **Disclosed literal `etc.`/`ecc.` abbreviations.** These are disclosed in the source notes and left unexpanded: `line-00036723` (LF36845–36916), `line-00037414.part-01` (LF37531), `line-00039431` (LF39504–39505), `line-00039513` (LF39574–39575), `line-00039685` (LF39768–39769) and the Italian `line-00040993` (`né opporsi ecc.`).
- **Italian motu proprio.** `line-00040993` is labelled Italian (`it`), matching the metadata. Its literal OCR renders some letters as real symbol glyphs: a euro sign for `e` (`di essa € quella`, `conservatori € priori`), `@` (`Quindi @ che`, `Ci @`), `pit`/`pid` for `più`, and stray `|` markers. These are literal OCR substitutions that render visibly (no tofu); they are recorded as limitations and are not repaired.
- **Inserted historical constitution.** `line-00039870.part-02` (1838-08-17) reproduces Benedict XIV's constitution with its own `BENEDICTUS PP. XIV.` protocol and its 1748 dating (`anno nono`) before Gregory XVI's grant and the 1838 dating. This is source content in source order, not a layout defect.
- **Source-numbered sections.** Concessions 1–4 in `line-00041478` and pacta I–VII in `line-00042454` are the source's own numbering; nothing was invented.
- **Unbalanced columns.** `line-00038728.part-01` sets its short text entirely in column 1, and `line-00038142` p1 ends column 2 early before the page break. Both follow normal reading order and are not defects.
- **Literal OCR noise.** Literal OCR noise appears throughout and is retained without attribution. Examples: stray braces (`provincialem}`, `assignamus};`, `haereticam};`), underscores (`_longe`, `_mites`, `ac_`), `nec2ssariis`, `eaqu2?`, `si:sontes`, `°Nont`, `or« dinationibus`, `s1b`, `oNficii`, `Caesa~~`, `Firma~ nus` and line-end `|` marks. No recognizable modern provider signature (for example `Digitized by Google` or damaged variants), interrupting journal running head, fused marginal summary or raw `None` placeholder was seen on any page.
- **Out-of-sequence volume locus.** `line-00031091.part-02` (1841-07-27, `pontificatus Nostri anno undecimo`) displays the date and title from its frozen metadata. Its line locus is earlier in volume 2 than its date order suggests; this lane does not adjudicate segmentation.
