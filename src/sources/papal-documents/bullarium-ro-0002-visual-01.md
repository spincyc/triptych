## Scope and method

Every assigned page PNG produced by the repository `pdf-review` run (1700x2200 rasters) was opened as its own full-size image before its observation was recorded. No contact sheet, thumbnail, rerasterization, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was performed. One JSON observation per page is in the lane ledger `observations.jsonl`.

Each page was checked for title line, responsible issuer, Latin label and omission of the unresolved act date against the frozen metadata; headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins, glyphs, clipping and overlap; natural source closing; modern digitizer signatures; raw placeholders; and, per the series addendum, (a) marginal summaries as separate TAB-led blocks, (b) page-foot notes as separate blocks, (c) natural two-column order, (d) running heads, folios and signature marks left inline, and (e) fragment presentation.

Mechanical support (read-only, lane-local scripts `mech.py`, `fn2.py`): each selection was split into paragraphs from the reading-order derivative at the manifest line ranges. It contains **207 TAB-led marginal paragraphs**; no paragraph mixes TAB-led and body lines, and no generated body-TeX paragraph mixes them either. 22 of the marginal blocks fall between the halves of a hyphen-split body word (separate blocks; qualification under addendum (d)). Printed-note probes found 41 note blocks set as their own paragraphs and exactly one note line inside a body paragraph (unit-0024, vol. VII). No `Missing character` entry occurs in any lane PDF log; no `Google`/`Digitized` string or bare `None` occurs in any lane body TeX. These checks support, but do not replace, the visual review.
