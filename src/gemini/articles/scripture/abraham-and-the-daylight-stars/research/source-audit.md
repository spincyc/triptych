## 2. Excluded Catalog-Only and Unheld Commentaries

The following Genesis 15 witnesses are held only as catalog-level `edition.toml` entries without locally tracked Genesis 15 text (or are absent locally) and were deliberately excluded from body analysis, Appendix A, References, and `source-bindings.toml` rather than summarized without direct primary inspection:
- Aramaic Targums (Onkelos and Pseudo-Jonathan); Philo of Alexandria (*Quis rerum divinarum heres sit*); Babylonian Talmud (*Shabbat* 156a and *Pesachim* 6b as independent tractate texts — *Shabbat* 156a is noted only where explicitly cited inside Abarbanel's verified Hebrew Question 7); *Bereshit Rabbah* 44:12; medieval and nineteenth-century Jewish commentators other than Abarbanel's verified Questions 7 and 16 (Rashi, Abraham Ibn Ezra, Maimonides, Radak, Ramban, Chizkuni, Rabbeinu Bahya, Jacob ben Asher, Malbim, and Netziv); George Leo Haydock's Bible commentary (remote unresolved Loreto facsimile holds no Genesis 15 passage record); and uncollated patristic Genesis commentaries (Chrysostom *Homilies on Genesis* 36–37, Ephrem *Commentary on Genesis*, Theodoret *Questions on Genesis*, Ambrose *De Abraham*, Augustine *Retractationes* II.43).

## 3. Consequential Negative Findings & Second-Pass Checks

1. **Zero attestation for waking daylight stars across every directly inspected source**: Neither the Hebrew text, nor the inspected ancient versions, nor *Jubilees* or Josephus, nor the New Testament, nor the collated Church Fathers and medieval/Counter-Reformation Latin masters, nor Abarbanel's prefatory questions endorse the claim that Abram stood awake under daylight trying to count invisible stars; Abarbanel articulates the waking daytime hypothesis explicitly only to reject it as impossible (*'i 'efshar*).
2. **Second-pass checks performed**:
   - Re-checked Codex Vaticanus's opening lacuna (Gen 1:1–46:28a) to verify that Swete's Genesis 15 text (*ekei* at 15:18) rests on Codex Alexandrinus rather than Vaticanus.
   - Re-checked the morphological accentuation of Hebrew `bāʾāh` at Genesis 15:17 against both the Kittel 1909 passage record and standard Masoretic cantillation (penultimate-stressed Qal perfect vs ultimate-stressed feminine singular active participle).
   - Re-verified Abarbanel's tracked Hebrew artifact (`bereshit-15-she-elah-7.txt`) line by line on 2026-11-26 to restrict all Abarbanel claims strictly to his opening formula and Questions 7 and 16 without uninspected body extrapolation.
