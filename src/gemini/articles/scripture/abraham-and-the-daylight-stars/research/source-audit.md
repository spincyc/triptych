## 3. Consequential Negative Findings & Explicit Absences

1. **Zero ancient or classical attestation for waking daylight stars**: Neither the Hebrew text, nor any ancient version, nor Second Temple Jewish writers, nor the New Testament, nor the collated Church Fathers and medieval Latin masters, nor classical Jewish commentators (including Abarbanel, who calls waking daytime star-counting impossible) endorse the claim that Abram stood awake under daylight trying to count invisible stars.
2. **Uncollated patristic Genesis commentaries**: Chrysostom's *Homilies on Genesis* 36–37, Ephrem's *Commentary on Genesis*, Theodoret's *Questions on Genesis*, Ambrose's *De Abraham*, and Augustine's *Retractationes* II.43 are not tracked locally in `src/sources/works/` and are expressly excluded from verified-silence claims.
3. **Second-pass checks performed**:
   - Re-checked Codex Vaticanus's opening lacuna (Gen 1:1–46:28a) to verify that Swete's Genesis 15 text (`ekei` at 15:18) rests on Codex Alexandrinus rather than Vaticanus.
   - Re-checked the morphological accentuation of Hebrew `bāʾāh` at Genesis 15:17 against both the Kittel 1909 passage record and standard Masoretic cantillation (penultimate-stressed Qal perfect vs ultimate-stressed feminine singular active participle).
