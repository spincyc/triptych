\section{Graded Summary of Catholic Teaching on Judgment and Purgatory}

The table below distinguishes the theological authority of the principal teachings and questions treated in the body. \textit{De fide} denotes truth formally revealed by God and proposed by the Church's solemn or ordinary universal Magisterium as requiring the assent of divine and Catholic faith; \textit{Sententia communis} denotes the steady consensus of the Fathers and Catholic theologians; \textit{Open theological question} denotes a matter on which revered Catholic teachers hold differing views within the bounds of orthodoxy.

\begin{threestable}{Teaching}{Grade}{Principal witnesses}
Christ's second coming in glory to judge the living and the dead according to their works & \textit{De fide} & John 5:22--29; Matt 25:31--46; Acts 10:42; 2 Cor 5:10; Apostles' and Nicene Creeds; Lateran IV (DS 801); \textit{Benedictus Deus} (DS 1002); CCC 1038--1041 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Immediate retribution after death: souls free of stain enter heaven at once; those dying in actual mortal sin descend directly into hell & \textit{De fide} & Luke 16:22--26; 23:43; 2 Cor 5:8; Profession of Faith at Lyons II (DS 857--858); \textit{Benedictus Deus} (DS 1000--1002); Florence, \textit{Laetentur caeli} (DS 1305--1306); CCC 1021--1022 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
The Particular Judgment of each soul immediately upon death & Ordinary and Universal Magisterium / \textit{Sententia fidei proxima} & Heb 9:27; Ecclus 11:28--29; \textit{Roman Catechism} I.7; \textit{Baltimore Catechism No.\ 3} Qq.\ 1372--1378; CCC 1021--1022 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Existence of Purgatory for souls dying in charity who are not yet fully purified & \textit{De fide} & 2 Mach 12:43--46; 1 Cor 3:11--15; Matt 12:32; Profession of Faith at Lyons II (DS 856); Florence, \textit{Laetentur caeli} (DS 1304); Trent, Sess.\ VI can.\ 30 (DS 1580) and Sess.\ XXV (DS 1820); CCC 1030--1031 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Persistence of a debt of temporal punishment after guilt and eternal punishment are remitted through Penance & \textit{De fide} & 2 Kings 12:13--14; Trent, Sess.\ VI can.\ 30 (DS 1580); Aquinas, \textit{ST} I-II, q.\ 87, a.\ 6; III, q.\ 86, a.\ 4; \textit{Roman Catechism} II, Penance (Satisfaction); CCC 1472--1473 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Efficacy of the Church's suffrages---principally the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass, along with prayers, alms, and works of piety---for the relief of the souls in Purgatory & \textit{De fide} & 2 Mach 12:43--46; St.\ Cyril of Jerusalem, \textit{Cat.} XXIII.9--10; St.\ John Chrysostom, \textit{Hom.\ on Phil.} 3; St.\ Augustine, \textit{Conf.} IX.11--13; Profession of Faith at Lyons II (DS 856); Florence (DS 1304); Trent, Sess.\ XXII ch.\ 2, can.\ 3 (DS 1743, 1753) and Sess.\ XXV (DS 1820); CCC 1032 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Christ granted the Church the power to confer indulgences, and their use is salutary to the Christian people & \textit{De fide} & Matt 16:19; 18:18; 2 Cor 2:10; Trent, Sess.\ XXV, \textit{Decree on Indulgences} (DS 1835); Pope St.\ Paul VI, \textit{Indulgentiarum doctrina}; 1983 \textit{Code of Canon Law}, cann.\ 992--997; CCC 1471--1479 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Indulgences remit temporal punishment from the Treasury of the Church and are applied to the dead strictly \textit{per modum suffragii} (by way of suffrage) & Catholic doctrine / Ordinary Magisterium & Pope St.\ Paul VI, \textit{Indulgentiarum doctrina} \S 5 and Norms 1, 3; 1983 \textit{Code of Canon Law}, cann.\ 992, 994; \textit{Baltimore Catechism No.\ 3} Qq.\ 839--845, 853--854; CCC 1471, 1476--1479 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Post-1967 Latin-Church indulgence discipline: abolition of ``days/years'' reckoning for partial indulgences (replaced by a Church-granted remission equal to that gained by the work itself) and usual conditions for a plenary indulgence (confession, Communion, prayer for the Pope's intentions, and complete detachment from all sin) & Mutable ecclesiastical discipline (effective 1967; current Latin law) & Pope St.\ Paul VI, \textit{Indulgentiarum doctrina} (1967), Norms 4--5, 7, 20; 1983 \textit{Code of Canon Law}, cann.\ 992--997; Apostolic Penitentiary, \textit{Enchiridion Indulgentiarum} (4th ed., 1999), Norm 20; cf.\ \textit{Baltimore Catechism No.\ 3} Qq.\ 843--845, 861, 863 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Christ exercises judiciary power not only as God but also in His glorified human nature, displaying the glorious scars of His Passion & Catholic doctrine / \textit{Sententia communis} & John 5:27; Acts 17:31; Apoc 1:7; \textit{Roman Catechism} I.7; Aquinas, \textit{ST} III, q.\ 59, aa.\ 2--3; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 90, aa.\ 1--2 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Souls in Purgatory are confirmed in grace, certain of eternal salvation, incapable of sinning, and unable to merit further for themselves & \textit{Sententia communis} & \textit{Baltimore Catechism No.\ 3} Q.\ 1383; St.\ Catherine of Genoa, \textit{Treatise on Purgatory} II; CCC 1030 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Physical nature of purgatorial ``fire'' (a real cleansing fire versus spiritual affliction or the transforming encounter with Christ Himself) & Open theological question & Deliberately left undefined at Florence (\textit{Laetentur caeli}, DS 1304, defining \textit{poenis purgatoriis}); Latin school (\textit{Supplement}, q.\ 97, a.\ 1, ad 2; \textit{Roman Catechism} I.5) versus Eastern spiritual understanding and Pope Benedict XVI, \textit{Spe salvi} \S 47 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Duration, mode, and degree of purgation, and whether any named departed soul is currently in Purgatory & Open theological question / Undetermined & Council of Trent, Sess.\ XXV, \textit{Decree on Purgatory} (DS 1820, warning against subtle and uncertain curiosities); \textit{Baltimore Catechism No.\ 3} Q.\ 1384 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Whether the inquiry and sentences of the General Judgment will be conducted mentally or vocally, and its exact earthly location (e.g., Valley of Josaphat) & Open theological question & St.\ Augustine, \textit{City of God} XX.14; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 88, aa.\ 2, 4 (preferring instantaneous mental unveiling and noting uncertainty of place) \\
