\subsection*{Liturgical Books, Received Prayers, and Hymns}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini restitutum}, Editio typica (Vatican City, 1962), corroborated in the Roman Canon against the 1604 typical edition. Source for the Roman Canon (\textit{Memento etiam} and \textit{Nobis quoque peccatoribus}) and the First Mass of All Souls' Day (Introit, Collect \textit{Fidelium, Deus}, Gradual, Tract \textit{Absolve, Domine}, and Sequence \textit{Dies irae} Latin strophes 1--8).
\item \textit{The Little Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary and the Office of the Dead}, Latin and English. New York: Benziger Brothers, 1915. Public domain. Source for the full liturgical conclusion and received English translation of \textit{Fidelium, Deus}, closing Requiem versicles, and \textit{De profundis} in the Office of the Dead and Burial Service (pp.\ 278--281, 316, 378).
\item \textit{A Manual of Prayers for the Use of the Catholic Laity}, prepared and enjoined by order of the Third Plenary Council of Baltimore. New York: Catholic Publication Society, 1889. Public domain. Corroborating witness for \textit{Fidelium, Deus} (pp.\ 269, 775).
\item Irons, William Josiah, trans. ``Day of Wrath! O day of mourning!'' (\textit{Dies irae}). In \textit{Hymns Ancient and Modern, Complete Edition}, Hymn 398, p.\ 307, stanzas 1--4. London: Printed for the Proprietors by William Clowes and Sons, Limited, catalogued 1890. Public domain. Received English verse witness for Section 10.
\end{itemize}
