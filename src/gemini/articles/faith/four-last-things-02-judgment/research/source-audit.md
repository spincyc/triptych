## 2. Cataloged Bibliographic Sources Cited in Doctrinal Paraphrase (9 Bindings)

The following nine magisterial, canonical, and patristic works are registered in `src/sources/` as cataloged editions or works (not tracked public-domain plain-text artifacts, or having restricted HTML deliveries that are neither bound nor reproduced). Every reference is bound strictly as `role = "bibliographic"`, `states = ["cataloged"]`, cited by canonical Denzinger-Schönmetzer (DS), canon, norm, or section locus in doctrinal paraphrase only, with zero verbatim quotation and zero claim of local artifact inspection:

1. **Pope Benedict XII, *Benedictus Deus* (29 January 1336, DS 1000–1002)** — `edition.benedict-xii.benedictus-deus.latin-denzinger`. Cited by DS locus in doctrinal paraphrase (immediate beatific vision, purgation, immediate descent into hell, and General Judgment).
2. **Council of Florence, *Laetentur caeli* (6 July 1439, DS 1304–1306)** — `edition.council-of-florence.laetentur-caeli.latin-denzinger`. Cited by DS locus in doctrinal paraphrase (*poenis purgatoriis* without defining material fire, efficacy of suffrages, and immediate retribution).
3. **Council of Trent, Sessions VI, XXII, and XXV (DS 1580, 1743, 1753, 1820, 1835)** — `work.council-of-trent.canones-et-decreta`. Cited by canonical session, chapter, canon, and DS loci (`sessio-6-canon-30`, `sessio-22-decretum-2`, `sessio-22-canon-3`, `sessio-25-decretum-1`, `sessio-25-decretum-5`) in doctrinal paraphrase.
4. ***Catechism of the Catholic Church* (1997)** — `edition.catholic-church.catechism.latin-typical-1997`. Cited by section number (§§ 1021–1022, 1030–1032, 1038–1041, 1471–1479) in doctrinal paraphrase.
5. **Pope Benedict XVI, *Spe salvi* (30 November 2007)** — `work.benedict-xvi.spe-salvi`. Cited by section number (§§ 46–48) in doctrinal paraphrase.
6. **Pope St. Paul VI, *Indulgentiarum doctrina* (1 January 1967)** — `work.papal.paul-vi.hf-p-vi-apc-01011967-indulgentiarum-doctrina`. Cited by section and norm number (§ 5; Norms 1, 3–5, 7, 20) in doctrinal paraphrase for indulgence doctrine and the 1967 disciplinary reform.
7. ***Codex Iuris Canonici* (1983 *Code of Canon Law*)** — `work.catholic-church.codex-iuris-canonici-1983`. Cited by canon number (`canon-992` through `canon-997`) in doctrinal paraphrase for current universal Latin-Church indulgence discipline.
8. **Apostolic Penitentiary, *Enchiridion Indulgentiarum* (4th ed., 16 July 1999)** — `work.apostolic-penitentiary.enchiridion-indulgentiarum`. Cited by norm number (`norma 20`) in doctrinal paraphrase for current plenary indulgence conditions.
9. **St. Gregory the Great, *Dialogues* IV.39** — `work.gregory-the-great.dialogi`. Attested through tracked Aquinas *ST* I-II, q. 87, a. 5, sed contra, and cited in doctrinal paraphrase without verbatim quotation.
