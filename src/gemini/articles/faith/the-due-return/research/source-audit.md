## Bounded Negative Results & Discrepancies Recorded

1. **St. Gregory of Nazianzus, *Oration 14* (*On Love for the Poor*):** A direct search of all oration headings in tracked `artifact.nicene-and-post-nicene-fathers.series-2-volume-7.ccel-web-2026-10-06.ccel-text-56d84cfa` confirmed that NPNF2-7 prints twenty-three selected orations (I, II, III, VII, VIII, XII, XVI, XVIII, XXI, XXVII–XXX, XXXIII–XXXIV, XXXVII–XLIII, XLV) and omits Oration XIV. Rather than citing an unverified text from memory, this study bounds Gregory of Nazianzus to tracked Oration 30.12.
2. **Pope St. John Paul II, *Dies Domini* (1998):** A direct search of `src/sources/works/john-paul-ii/` confirmed that *Dies Domini* is not yet registered in `src/sources/`. It is cited strictly in doctrinal paraphrase and listed in `sections/99-references.tex` without fabricating a `source-bindings.toml` ID.
3. **Psalm 115 Verse Numbering:** Direct collation revealed that Challoner (`pg1581.txt`) numbers Vulgate Psalm 115 as verses 10–19 (115:12 = *Quid retribuam*), whereas Clementine Vulgate (`verse-text-21-psalms-d20e21f8`) and 1899 American Douay-Rheims number it 1–10 (115:3 = *Quid retribuam*). Both correspond to Hebrew Psalm 116:10–19; this discrepancy is explicitly disclosed in Endnote 2 and *Scope, Method, and Qualifications*.
