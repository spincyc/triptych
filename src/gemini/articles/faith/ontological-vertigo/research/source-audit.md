# Source Audit: Ontological Vertigo

## Audit Date and Method
- **Audited:** 2026-11-28 (updated following independent cold review)
- **Method:** Every verbatim quotation in English or Latin was read directly from a locally tracked, public-domain textual artifact or segment under `src/sources/works/` before drafting or revision. Modernized online host revisions (notably New Advent Fathers pages carrying Kevin Knight's modernized text) were detected and bypassed in favor of authentic tracked printings (CCEL ANF-1, NPNF1-1, NPNF2-7, NPNF2-9; IA NPNF2-4; Project Gutenberg Dods 1871 *City of God*). Every inspected witness is bound in `research/source-bindings.toml` (schema = 2) with exact SHA-256 fingerprints and work-valid locus strings.

## Source-Hygiene Detections, Substitutions, and OCR Normalizations
1. **New Advent modernization avoided (Gregory of Nazianzus, *Oration* 38):** Inspection of `artifact.gregory-of-nazianzus.oration-38.1894-browne-swallow-english-web-2026-09-29.complete-oration-text-635aae9d` revealed a New Advent web extraction carrying modernized English (`guidance/sources.md`). All quotations from *Oration* 38 were taken instead from the unrevised 1894 Browne & Swallow printing tracked in CCEL NPNF2-7 (`ccel-text-56d84cfa`).
2. **New Advent modernization avoided (Augustine, *City of God* XI–XII):** Inspection of `book-11-text-adefed6a` and `book-12-text-e39b35b2` revealed New Advent web extractions. All quotations from Books XI–XII were taken instead from the unrevised 1871 Marcus Dods printing tracked in `pg45304-body-lf`.
3. **New Advent Book-II-only edition replaced (John of Damascus, *De Fide Orthodoxa* I.9):** Cold review detected that `edition.john-of-damascus.de-fide-orthodoxa.1899-salmond-english-web-2026-09-29` contains only Book II on New Advent. Replaced with direct inspection of the unrevised 1898 Edinburgh NPNF2-9 CCEL segment (`segment.john-of-damascus.de-fide-orthodoxa.1898-edinburgh-npnf-ccel.complete-text`, lines 29380–29384, Book I, ch. 9). Both Aquinas's Latin-mediated wording (*ST* I, q. 13, a. 11: `"an infinite and indeterminate sea of substance"`) and Salmond's direct English rendering (`"a sea of essence infinite and unseen"`) are distinguished accurately.
4. **OCR and transcription normalization record:**
   - `ia-djvu-text-ae54c8ba` (Athanasius, *Contra Gentes* §§41–42): line-break hyphens rejoined, section numeral `3.` and footnote markers omitted, and DjVu OCR artifacts (`grudge nothings :` -> `grudge nothing:`, stray glyph `■♦` after `existence`) normalized to the underlying 1892 print wording.
   - `complete-english-ocr` (Vatican I, *Dei Filius*, Manning 1912): line-break hyphens rejoined and footnote numerals omitted.
   - `segment.augustine-of-hippo.confessiones.ccel-web-2026-10-13.complete-work` (*Confessions* VII.10.16): supplied the missing opening single quotation mark in ``'I Am that I Am'`` where the CCEL file prints `I Am that I Am.'`.
5. **Confessions Latin limitation:** No locally tracked Latin artifact of Augustine's *Confessions* is present (only remote links exist). Accordingly, *Confessions* III.6.11 and VII.10–17 are quoted exclusively from the tracked public-domain Pilkington English text without unverified Latin phrases.
6. **Direct Latin translations vs. published English quotations:** Latin clauses from Aquinas's *De Ente et Essentia* (ch. 4–5), Augustine's *De Genesi ad litteram* (IV.12.22–23), and Fourth Lateran's *Firmiter credimus* (DS 800) are rendered in unquoted prose paired with the verified Latin text and explicit endnote attribution, never placed in misleading double quotation marks alongside published translations.
7. **Scriptural secondary citations distinguished:** `Wis. 8:1` is quoted solely as embedded in Vatican I's *Dei Filius* ch. 1 (Manning 1912), and `1 Cor. 2:12` is quoted solely inside Aquinas's *ST* II-II, q. 161, a. 3; neither is claimed as a direct Douay-Rheims quotation in `source-bindings.toml` or References.
8. **Restricted and untracked works:** The *Catechism of the Catholic Church* (nn. 300–301) is bound solely at the cataloged edition level (`edition.catholic-church.catechism.latin-typical-1997`, `role = "bibliographic"`, `states = ["cataloged"]`) and summarized doctrinally without verbatim quotation. Plato, Aristotle (beyond Aquinas's direct quotation of *Metaph.* II, text 4 inside *ST* I, q. 44, a. 1), Henry of Ghent, Duns Scotus, Suárez, Hume, and Russell are analyzed strictly in original prose without unverified verbatim quotations.
9. **Misattributed aphorism excluded:** The popular maxim on humility frequently misattributed to C. S. Lewis is omitted; the exposition relies directly on Aquinas (*ST* II-II, q. 129, a. 3, ad 4; q. 161, aa. 1–3).
