## 2. Cataloged Bibliographic Magisterial Records (Doctrinal Paraphrase Only)

The following magisterial records are held in `src/sources/` strictly as catalog-level bibliographic entries without tracked local text payloads. In `source-bindings.toml` they are bound strictly as `role = "bibliographic"`, `states = ["cataloged"]`. They are cited solely by canonical DS or section locus in original doctrinal paraphrase, with zero verbatim quotation and zero claim of local artifact inspection or web verification:

- **Council of Vienne**, Constitution *Ad nostrum qui* (6 May 1312), DS 895 (`edition.council-of-vienne.ad-nostrum-qui.latin-denzinger`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for condemning the denial of the necessity of the *lumen gloriae*.
- **Pope Benedict XII**, Apostolic Constitution *Benedictus Deus* (29 January 1336), DS 1000–1001 (`edition.benedict-xii.benedictus-deus.latin-denzinger`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for immediate entry into the Beatific Vision, cessation of faith and hope, and indefeasibility of beatitude.
- **Council of Florence**, Bull of Union with the Greeks *Laetentur caeli* (6 July 1439), DS 1305 (`edition.council-of-florence.laetentur-caeli.latin-denzinger`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for clear vision of the Triune God and inequality of glory according to merits.
- **Council of Trent**, Session VI, Canon 32 (13 January 1547), DS 1582 (`edition.council-of-trent.canones-et-decreta.english-waterworth`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for the true merit of increase of grace, eternal life, and increase of glory.
- **Pope Pius XII**, Apostolic Constitution *Munificentissimus Deus* (1 November 1950), § 44, DS 3903 (`edition.pius-xii.munificentissimus-deus.english-vatican-web-2026-07-25`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for the dogmatic definition of the bodily Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary.
- **Pope Pius XII**, Encyclical Letter *Ad Caeli Reginam* (11 October 1954) (`work.pius-xii.ad-caeli-reginam`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for the royal dignity of the Blessed Virgin Mary as Queen of Heaven.
- **Catechism of the Catholic Church**, 2nd ed. (1997), §§ 1023–1029, 1042, 1048 (`edition.catholic-church.catechism.latin-typical-1997`): cited in doctrinal paraphrase for heaven, the Beatific Vision, the communion of saints, and the renewal of creation (citing *Lumen gentium* 48 and *Gaudium et spes* 39).

- **Bounded *Supplement* coverage**: Because the tracked 1922 English Dominican *Supplement* volume covers Questions 87–99, Section 6 grounds the four endowments of the glorified body strictly in directly inspected tracked witnesses (1 Cor 15:42–44, Matt 13:43, Phil 3:21, *Roman Catechism* Creed Art. - **Haddan / Kevin Knight *De Trinitate* notice**: Per `guidance/sources.md`, the tracked New Advent English text of St. Augustine's *De Trinitate* is Arthur West Haddan's 1887 translation revised by Kevin Knight; this modern web revision is credited explicitly in Section 3 endnote 1, Appendix B, and References.
- **Typographical and OCR resolutions**: Noted explicitly in endnotes and Appendix B where historical printing or OCR artifacts occur: Donovan 1829 Creed Art. XI bracketed `[acquire]` for printed `"require"` (Latin *acquiret*); Vatican I *Dei Filius* tracked HTML split `in comprehensibilem`; Gutenberg Prima Pars `"1 John 2:2"` slip for 1 John 3:2 in *ST* I q. 12 a. 5; and nineteenth-century DjVu OCR character fixes in Section 10 (*Te Deum* and St. Alphonsus prayer).
