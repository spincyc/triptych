\subsection*{Liturgical Books and Received Prayers}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini restitutum}, Editio typica (Vatican City, 1962), collated in the Feast of All Saints (1 November) with the 1922 Tours Mame \textit{editio quarta iuxta typicam} and in the Roman Canon with the 1604 Vatican typical edition. Source for the All Saints Latin propers (including the received Latin Collect in Section 10) and Roman Canon.
\item Lasance, Rev.\ F.\ X., ed. \textit{The New Roman Missal in Latin and English}. Revised ed. New York: Benziger Brothers, 1945. Consulted for English All Saints scriptural propers.
\item Cummiskey, Eugene, pub. \textit{The Roman Missal, Translated into the English Language for the Use of the Laity}. Philadelphia: Eugene Cummiskey, 1861. Public domain. Source for the English rendering of the Roman Canon in Section 10.
\item \textit{The Roman Breviary: Reformed by Order of the Holy Oecumenical Council of Trent}. Summer (Autumn) Volume. Trans.\ John, Marquess of Bute, K.T. Edinburgh and London: William Blackwood and Sons, 1879. Public domain. Source for the pre-1955 Second Day within the Octave of All Saints (2 November) Matins Lessons IV--V (from Venerable Bede), p.\ 1335.
\item \textit{Manual of Prayers for the Use of the Catholic Laity}, Prepared and Published by Order of the Third Plenary Council of Baltimore. New York: Catholic Publication Society, 1889. Public domain. Source for the Ordinary of the Mass (pp.\ 135--137), received Latin and English \textit{Te Deum laudamus} (pp.\ 160--162), and received English Collect of All Saints (p.\ 773).
\end{itemize}
