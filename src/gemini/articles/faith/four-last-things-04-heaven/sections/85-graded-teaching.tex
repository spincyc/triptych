\section{Graded Summary of Catholic Teaching on Heaven}

The table below distinguishes the theological authority of the principal teachings and questions treated in the body. \textit{De fide} denotes truth formally revealed by God and proposed by the Church's solemn or ordinary universal Magisterium as requiring the assent of divine and Catholic faith; \textit{Ordinary Magisterium} denotes authentic teaching of the Roman Pontiffs and episcopate; \textit{Sententia communis} denotes the steady consensus of the Fathers and Catholic theologians; \textit{Open theological question} denotes a matter on which revered Catholic teachers hold differing views within the bounds of orthodoxy.

\begin{threestable}{Teaching}{Grade}{Principal witnesses}
Existence and eternity of heaven as the everlasting kingdom prepared for the just & \textit{De fide} & Matt 25:34, 46; John 14:2--3; 2 Cor 5:1; Lateran IV (DS 801); \textit{Benedictus Deus} (DS 1000--1001); CCC 1023--1024 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Immediate entry of purified souls into heaven and the Beatific Vision after death, before the General Resurrection & \textit{De fide} & Luke 23:43; 2 Cor 5:8; Phil 1:23; \textit{Benedictus Deus} (DS 1000); Florence, \textit{Laetentur caeli} (DS 1305); CCC 1023 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Direct, intuitive, face-to-face vision of the Divine Essence, Three and One, with no creature mediating as object seen & \textit{De fide} & 1 Cor 13:12; 1 John 3:2; \textit{Benedictus Deus} (DS 1000); Florence, \textit{Laetentur caeli} (DS 1305); CCC 1023, 1028 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Necessity of the supernatural light of glory (\textit{lumen gloriae}) elevating the created intellect to behold the Divine Essence & \textit{De fide} & Ps 35:10; Council of Vienne, \textit{Ad nostrum} (1312, DS 895); St.\ Thomas, \textit{ST} I, q.\ 12, aa.\ 4--5; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 92, a.\ 1; \textit{Roman Catechism} I.12; CCC 1028 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Incomprehensibility of the infinite God by any created intellect, even in the Beatific Vision & \textit{De fide} & 1 Tim 6:16; Fourth Lateran Council, \textit{Firmiter credimus} (1215, DS 800); First Vatican Council, \textit{Dei Filius} (1870, ch.\ 1, DS 3001); St.\ Thomas, \textit{ST} I, q.\ 12, a.\ 7 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Cessation of theological faith and hope in heaven, replaced by clear vision and possession, while charity abides forever & \textit{De fide} & 1 Cor 13:8--13; Rom 8:24; \textit{Benedictus Deus} (DS 1001) \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]

\addlinespace[0.35em]
Inequality of heavenly glory proportioned to each saint's merits (\textit{pro meritorum diversitate}) & \textit{De fide} & John 14:2; 1 Cor 3:8; 15:41; Florence, \textit{Laetentur caeli} (DS 1305); Trent, Sess.\ VI, can.\ 32 (DS 1582) \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Resurrection of the just in their own numerically identical bodies, united once more to their souls & \textit{De fide} & Job 19:25--27; Dan 12:2; 1 Cor 15:20--54; Lateran IV, \textit{Firmiter credimus} (DS 801); St.\ Cyril of Jerusalem, \textit{Cat.} 18.3, 18; St.\ John of Damascus, \textit{De fide orth.} IV.27 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Assumption of the Immaculate Mother of God, body and soul, into heavenly glory & \textit{De fide} & Pope Pius XII, \textit{Munificentissimus Deus} (1950), \S 44 (DS 3903); Apoc 12:1 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Royal dignity of the Blessed Virgin Mary as Queen of Heaven over angels and saints & Ordinary Magisterium & Pope Pius XII, \textit{Ad Caeli Reginam} (1954); Apoc 12:1; Roman Canon (\textit{Communicantes}) \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]

\addlinespace[0.35em]
Charity as the proximate measure of heavenly capacity, and the complete absence of envy among the blessed & \textit{Sententia communis} & St.\ Augustine, \textit{Tractates on John} 67.2; \textit{City of God} XXII.30; St.\ Thomas, \textit{ST} I, q.\ 12, a.\ 6; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 93, aa.\ 2--3 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Four endowments (\textit{dotes}) of the glorified body: impassibility, clarity, agility, and subtlety & \textit{Sententia communis} & Matt 13:43; 1 Cor 15:42--44; Phil 3:21; \textit{Roman Catechism} I.11; St.\ Thomas, \textit{ST} III, q.\ 54, aa.\ 1--2; St.\ Cyril of Jerusalem, \textit{Cat.} 18.18; St.\ Augustine, \textit{City of God} XXII.21, 29--30; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 91, a.\ 1 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Accidental beatitude of heavenly fellowship with the angels and saints, and the distinct \textit{aureoles} of martyrs, virgins, and doctors & \textit{Sententia communis} & Heb 12:22--24; Apoc 7:9--12; St.\ Gregory the Great, \textit{Hom.\ on the Gospels} 34.6--10; St.\ Thomas, \textit{ST} I, q.\ 108, aa.\ 1, 5; I-II, q.\ 4, a.\ 8; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 94, a.\ 1; q.\ 96, aa.\ 1, 5--7 \\

\addlinespace[0.35em]
Exact physical mode of cosmic purification and renewal & Open theological question & St.\ Augustine, \textit{City of God} XX.16; \textit{Supplement}, q.\ 91, a.\ 3; CCC 1048 \\
\addlinespace[0.35em]
Visions of heavenly glory and divine truth reported by St.\ Teresa of Avila (\textit{Life}, chs.\ 38, 40) & Approved private testimony & Ecclesiastical recognition of authenticity and spiritual fruit; adds nothing to public Revelation and does not bind the assent of divine faith \\
