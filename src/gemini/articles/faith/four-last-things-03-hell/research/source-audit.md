## 3. Substitutions, Corrections, and Open Limits

- **Translator correction (Alphonsus)**: Initial draft endnote tentatively named R. A. Coffin for the 1843 Duffy *Preparation for Death*; inspection of `edition.toml` established the translator is an unnamed Catholic clergyman (Dublin: James Duffy, 1843). Corrected before finalization.
- **Catholic vs. Anglican witness (Francis de Sales)**: Inspection of both tracked English editions revealed that the 1876 Rivingtons edition (H. L. Sidney Lear) omitted the closing *Hail Mary* from Part I, Chapter 15; the Roman Catholic Pustet edition was adopted instead.
- **Newman excluded from body**: Although St. John Henry Newman's *Parochial and Plain Sermons* Vol. 1, Sermon 1 ("Holiness Necessary for Future Blessedness") was inspected in `src/sources/`, it belongs to his pre-1845 Anglican period and contains a passing anti-merit remark (p. 9). Omitted so that every spiritual witness in the essay is unambiguously Catholic.
- **Quotations from remote papal texts**: Where web-search quota throttled a follow-up exact-string check on John Paul II's 28 July 1999 General Audience and *Spe salvi* 45, unconfirmed phrases were removed from quotation marks and restricted strictly to verified excerpts or faithful paraphrase.
