## 3. Substitutions, Corrections, and Editorial Controls

- **Translator attribution (Alphonsus 1843)**: Verified against the Duffy 1843 edition record as translated by "a Catholic clergyman" (matching Part III's audit).
- **Vienne constitution distinction**: Confirmed that `work.council-of-vienne.ad-nostrum-qui` is the decree against the Beghards/Beguines, not *Fidei catholicae* (DS 902); accordingly bound DS 902 and DS 1440 via Denzinger rather than misbinding `ad-nostrum-qui`.
- **Zero verbatim reproduction from catalog-only or restricted texts**: Confirmed that zero verbatim quotations appear from *Gaudium et spes* § 18, the *Catechism of the Catholic Church*, Council of Trent Sessions V, XIV, XXII, or XXV, Council of Vienne, Fifth Lateran Council, *Benedictus Deus*, *Laetentur caeli*, *Divino afflatu*, *Laudis canticum*, or *Sacram unctionem infirmorum*; removed an uninspected Waterworth page citation in Section 1.
- **Historical dating of liturgical discipline**: Explicitly dated the pre-1972 / 1962 *Rituale Romanum* rites (Anointing of the Sick, Viaticum, and Commendation) alongside *Sacram unctionem infirmorum* (1972) and *Pastoral Care of the Sick*, and explicitly dated the pre-1911 Roman Breviary four-psalm Compline alongside *Divino afflatu* (1911) / 1962 Breviary and *Laudis canticum* (1970) / 1971 *Liturgia Horarum*; marked the omitted *Preces* in the Section 10 Compline excerpt with `[\ldots]` and an explanatory endnote.
- **Direct witness parity**: Directly read and bound *ST* I, q. 89, aa. 3--5 and *Roman Catechism* I.7 (Donovan, pp. 61--63), and harmonized Appendix A's grading of immediate retribution (*de fide*) and Particular Judgment (*Ordinary and Universal Magisterium / Sententia fidei proxima*) with Part II.
