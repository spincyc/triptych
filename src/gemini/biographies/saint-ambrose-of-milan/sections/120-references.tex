Ancient works are cited by conventional title, book, chapter, and section matching the tracked editions below. All source inspections were completed on 27~November 2026 against tracked public-domain artifacts in the source library.

\subsection*{Primary sources: Saint Ambrose of Milan}

\begin{itemize}
\item Ambrose of Milan, \work{Select Works and Letters}, trans.\ H.~de Romestin, E.~de Romestin, and H.~T.~F. Duckworth, NPNF Series~2, Vol.~10, ed.\ P.~Schaff and H.~Wace (Edinburgh/New York, 1896; CCEL unrevised printing). Controls Ambrose's principal treatises, Symmachus's \work{Relatio} III, \work{Sermon against Auxentius}, \work{Epistles} XVII--XVIII, XX--XXII, XL--XLI, LI, LVII, LXI--LXIII, and H.~de Romestin's Prolegomena and notes. Public domain.
\item Ambrose of Milan, \work{Two Books on the Decease of His Brother Satyrus} (\work{De Excessu Fratris Satyri}), constituent segment within NPNF Series~2, Vol.~10 (CCEL unrevised printing). Public domain.
\item Ambrose of Milan, \work{Hexameron}, Latin text after J.-P. Migne, \textit{Patrologia Latina}, vol.~14 (Paris, 1845), Books~I.6.20, V.24.88--25.89, VI.2.7--8. Public domain.
\item Ambrose of Milan, \work{Expositio Evangelii secundum Lucam}, ed.\ K.~Schenkl, CSEL~32/4 (Vienna: Tempsky, 1902), passages II.19, II.26, II.41. Public domain.
\item Ambrose of Milan, excerpts from \work{Expositio Evangelii secundum Lucam} in Thomas Aquinas, \work{Catena Aurea}, Vol.~III, St.~Luke (Oxford: John Henry Parker, 1843), II.26 and II.41. Public domain.
\item Ambrose of Milan (or late-fourth-century Milanese witness), \work{De Sacramentis} IV.5.21--23, IV.6.27, in Migne, \textit{Patrologia Latina}, vol.~16 (Paris, 1880), cols.~462--464, with Maurist notes. Public domain.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Liturgical and official ecclesial reception}

\begin{itemize}
\item \work{Manuale Ambrosianum ex codice saec.\ XI olim in usum Canonicae Vallis Travaliae: Pars Altera}, ed.\ M.~Magistretti (Milan: Ulricus Hoepli, 1904). Inspected at \latin{Die VII Men.\ Decembris: Ordinatio Sancti Ambrosii}, pp.~17--22 (Vespers hymn \latin{Miraculum laudabile}, p.~18; morning antiphon \latin{Ant.\ xxiii}, pp.~21--22). Public domain.
\item \work{Missale Ambrosianum}, promulgated by Cesare Cardinal Monti, Archbishop of Milan (Milan: Io.\ Ambrosius Sirturus, 1640). Inspected at the Archbishop's prefatory letter to the clergy of Milan on Myrocles, Ambrose, Simplicianus, and Eusebius. Public domain.
\item \work{Martyrologium Romanum}, editio novissima under Leo~XIII (Rome: Ex typographia polyglotta S.~C.\ de Propaganda Fide, 1902). Inspected at 4~April (deposition of Ambrose), 19~June (Gervasius and Protasius), 17~September (Satyrus), and 7~December (ordination of Ambrose, Bishop and Doctor of the Church). Public domain.
\item \work{Missale Romanum}, editio typica (Vatican City, 1962), 7~December proper (\latin{S.~Ambrosii}, Collect \latin{Deus, qui populo tuo}, p.~435, no.~1863); collated for public-domain Latin and English with F.~X. Lasance and F.~A. Walsh, \work{The New Roman Missal}, rev.\ ed.\ (New York: Benziger, 1945), pp.~845--846, and the postconciliar General Roman Calendar (7~December Obligatory Memorial).
\end{itemize}
