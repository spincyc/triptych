# Saint Ambrose of Milan — chronology record

Provider: Google Gemini. Inspection cutoff: 2026-11-27. Evidence classes: A / N / E / L / M / K / S.

Dates are historical claims with stated evidentiary bases. Ambrose rarely inserts consular dates into his sermons and treatises; absolute anchors come from dated imperial reigns (Valentinian I, Gratian, Maximus, Valentinian II, Theodosius I, Eugenius), synodal acts (Aquileia 381), fixed liturgical feasts (7 December ordination; Holy Saturday 4 April 397 death), and cross-references with St. Basil and St. Augustine.

## Dated and approximate life events

1. **c. 339–340 (older alternative c. 333) — Birth in Gaul (probably Trier).**
   - Basis: H. de Romestin, NPNF II.10 Prolegomena §§III, V & n. 17 (K), noting that the choice between 333 and 340 depends on whether Ambrose's age allusion amid "barbaric commotions and the storms of war" in *Epistle* 59 (not tracked in full locally) refers to Maximus's invasion (387) or Eugenius's (393–394). Father Ambrosius was praetorian prefect of the Gauls (seat Trier).
   - Confidence: Probable range; exact year and city unresolved.

2. **c. 353–354 — Move to Rome; Marcellina receives the veil from Pope Liberius.**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *De Virginibus* III.1.1–4 (A, checked verbatim in NPNF II.10) confirms Liberius's Christmas address at St. Peter's; Prolegomena §III dates the family's return to Rome after the father's death to c. 353–354 (before Liberius's 355 exile) (K).
   - Confidence: Event secure; exact year approximate (353 or 354).

3. **c. 365–370 — Legal advocacy and appointment as *consularis* of Aemilia and Liguria.**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *De Excessu Fratris Satyri* I.25, 49, 58 (A, checked) on the brothers' legal careers and reluctant acceptance of public office; *De Officiis* I.1.4 (A, checked) on being carried off from the judgment seat and consular insignia (*infulis*); Paulinus via Prolegomena §V (E/K) assigns appointment under Praetorian Prefect Probus to c. 370.
   - Confidence: Office secure; start year approximate.

4. **7 December 374 (modern alternative: 7 December 373) — Election, baptism, and consecration as bishop of Milan.**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *Letter* XXI.7 (A, checked: Milanese people demanded him of Valentinian I); *De Officiis* I.1.2–4 (A, checked: unbaptized magistrate taken straight from tribunal); St. Basil, *Letter* CXCVII.1 (N, checked, placed in 375); *Martyrologium Romanum* 7 December (M, checked); Prolegomena §§III, V (K, giving 7 December 374, eight days after baptism).
   - Competing chronology: Modern scholarship debates 373 vs. 374 because 7 December fell on a Sunday in 374 (the customary day for episcopal consecration), whereas 7 December 373 was a Saturday, though some prosopographical readings of Auxentius's death favor late 373. Both dates are preserved; 374 is adopted as primary anchor.
   - Confidence: Event and day (7 December) secure; year 374 probable, 373 preserved as alternative.

5. **374–375 — Recovery of St. Dionysius's relics from Cappadocia.**
   - Basis: St. Basil, *Letter* CXCVII.2 (N, checked verbatim in NPNF II.8, placed in 375).
   - Confidence: Secure.

6. **c. 377 — *De Virginibus* and *De Viduis*.**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *De Virginibus* I–III (A, checked); Prolegomena §§III, VI (K) dates *De Virginibus* to 377 (opening on St. Agnes's feast, 21 January), followed shortly by *De Viduis*.
   - Confidence: Approximate.

7. **378 — *De Fide* I–II for Gratian; Battle of Adrianople (9 August); melting of sacred vessels for captives.**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *De Fide* I.prol.1–4 (A, checked: Gratian setting out to war); *De Officiis* II.28.136–140 (A, checked: breaking up sacred vessels to ransom captives from barbarian slavery); Prolegomena §III (K).
   - Confidence: Secure.

8. **c. 378–379 — Death of Satyrus and *De Excessu Fratris Satyri* I–II.**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *De Excessu* I.1, 31 (A, checked: dread of imminent barbarian invasion across the Alps, fitting post-Adrianople 378/379); *Martyrologium Romanum* (M, checked) commemorates Satyrus's deposition on 17 September; de Romestin's introduction (lines 17110–17111, K) gives 17 October 379.
   - Competing chronology: 378 vs. 379 and 17 September vs. 17 October are both recorded without forced harmonization.
   - Confidence: Event secure; exact year and month unresolved.

9. **379–381 — *De Fide* III–V (379–380), Sirmium (c. 380–381), *De Spiritu Sancto* (381), and Council of Aquileia (September 381).**
   - Basis: Ambrose, *De Fide* III–V and *De Spiritu Sancto* I–III (A, checked); Prolegomena §§III, V, VI summarizing *Epistles* 9–12 on the condemnation of Palladius and Secundianus (K).
   - Confidence: Secure (Sirmium year c. 380–381 approximate).

10. **383–384 — Murder of Gratian (25 August 383); first embassy to Maximus at Trier.**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Epistle* XX.23 and *Letter* XXI.20 n. 3465 (A/K, checked); Prolegomena §III (K).
    - Confidence: Secure.

11. **384 — Altar of Victory debate (*Relatio* III, *Epistles* XVII–XVIII); *De Paenitentia*; Augustine arrives in Milan.**
    - Basis: Symmachus, *Relatio* III (N, checked); Ambrose, *Epistles* XVII–XVIII and *Letter* LVII.2–3 (A, checked); *De Paenitentia* I–II (A, checked; Maurist dating c. 384 via Prolegomena §VI); Augustine, *Confessions* V.13.23 (N, checked).
    - Confidence: Secure (*De Paenitentia* date approximate).

12. **Holy Week 385 — First Basilica Crisis (*Epistle* XX).**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Epistle* XX (A, checked verbatim; Palm Sunday through Holy Thursday of 385).
    - Confidence: Secure.

13. **January–June 386 — Homoian assembly law; *Letter* XXI; *Sermon against Auxentius*; introduction of hymns; discovery of Sts. Gervasius and Protasius (June).**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Letter* XXI, *Sermon against Auxentius*, *Letter* XXII (A, checked); Augustine, *Confessions* IX.7.15–16 and *City of God* XXII.8 (N, checked); *Martyrologium Romanum* 19 June (M, checked).
    - Confidence: Secure.

14. **c. 386–387 — *Expositio Evangelii secundum Lucam*; *De Mysteriis*; Easter 387 baptism of Augustine; second embassy to Maximus.**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Expositio Lucae* (CSEL verified passages) and *De Mysteriis* (A, checked); Augustine, *Confessions* IX.5.13–6.14 (N, checked); Prolegomena §§III, VI (K, under 387 noting *Ep.* 24 and Ambrose's expulsion from Trier for refusing communion with Gratian's murderer). Note: *Confessions* IX.6.14 places Augustine's baptism at Easter 387 without specifying the calendar day; by historical Easter computation, Easter Sunday in 387 fell on 25 April (placing the Easter Vigil on the night of 24–25 April 387).
    - Confidence: Baptism and embassy secure; treatise dates approximate.

15. **Late 388 — Callinicum crisis (*Letters* XL–XLI).**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Letters* XL and XLI (A, checked verbatim; written after Theodosius's 388 defeat of Maximus mentioned in XL.23).
    - Confidence: Secure.

16. **c. 389–391 — *Hexameron* (c. 389) and *De Officiis* (c. 391).**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Hexameron* (PL 14, A, checked) and *De Officiis* (NPNF II.10, A, checked); Prolegomena §§III, VI (K).
    - Confidence: Approximate.

17. **390 — Massacre at Thessalonica and *Letter* LI.**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Letter* LI (A, checked verbatim); Prolegomena §§III, V (K); Theodoret V.18 at reported ceiling (E).
    - Confidence: Secure (year and penance); doorway staging later.

18. **392–395 — Death of Valentinian II (15 May 392); Eugenius usurpation and Ambrose's withdrawal (*Letter* LVII, 393); Battle of the Frigidus (6 September 394) and clemency pleas (*Letters* LXI–LXII); death of Theodosius I (January 395).**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Letters* LVII, LXI, LXII (A, checked); Prolegomena §III (K).
    - Confidence: Secure.

19. **396 — *Epistle* LXIII to Vercellae.**
    - Basis: Ambrose, *Epistle* LXIII.1–9, 68–70 (A, checked: Eusebius of Vercellae, Sarmatio & Barbatianus, and Dionysius of Milan's exile); Prolegomena §III under 396 (K, line 456: visit to Vercellae).
    - Confidence: Secure.

20. **Holy Saturday, 4 April 397 — Death at Milan; Easter Sunday, 5 April 397 burial beside Sts. Gervasius and Protasius.**
    - Basis: Paulinus via Prolegomena §§III, V (E/K); *Martyrologium Romanum* 4 April (M, checked); Ambrose's burial intention in *Letter* XXII.13 (A, checked); Simplicianus's succession corroborated in Augustine *Confessions* VIII.2.3 n. 608 (N/K).
    - Confidence: Secure.

## Posthumous milestones

21. **835 — Archbishop Angilbert II's porphyry sarcophagus at Basilica Ambrosiana.** Reported via de Romestin Prolegomena §II (K/L), with §II's misnaming of Gervasius and Protasius as Cosmas and Damian explicitly corrected.
22. **11th century — *Manuale Ambrosianum* (ed. Magistretti 1904).** Direct witness (L) to the medieval Milanese 7 December office of Ambrose's ordination.
23. **1298 — Pope Boniface VIII confirms the solemn feasts of the four original Latin Doctors of the Church.** Reported reference consensus (M/K); decretal text not tracked locally.
24. **1640 — Archbishop Cesare Cardinal Monti's *Missale Ambrosianum*.** Direct witness (M) to official Milanese historical framing of the Ambrosian Rite's cumulative growth.
25. **1864 — Basilica Ambrosiana excavations.** Reported via de Romestin Prolegomena §II (K); no local primary archaeological report.
26. **1902 — Leonine *Martyrologium Romanum* (Propaganda Fide).** Direct witness (M) to 4 April and 7 December entries.
