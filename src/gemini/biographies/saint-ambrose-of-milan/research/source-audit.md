## 2. Source families searched and consequential negative results / reporting ceilings

Offline sandbox environment without external network access. Searches covered all tracked directories under `src/sources/works/` (`ambrose/`, `augustine-of-hippo/`, `jerome/`, `migne/`, `nicene-and-post-nicene-fathers/`, `catholic-church/`, `archdiocese-of-milan/`) and `src/sources/calendars/`.

1. **Paulinus of Milan, *Vita Sancti Ambrosii* (c. 422):** Not tracked as a standalone text in `src/sources/works/`. Consequence: episodes unique to Paulinus (Probus's "act as a bishop" charge, the child's shout "Ambrose, bishop!", judicial torture and flight expedients, Euthymius's carriage in ch. 12, Stilicho's embassy, deathbed sayings, and Honoratus's Viaticum) are cited strictly via H. de Romestin's 1896 NPNF II.10 Prolegomena and notes; the infant bees story (*Vita* ch. 3), absent even from de Romestin's summary, is flagged at reporting ceiling in the tradition audit.
2. **Socrates, Sozomen, Rufinus, and Theodoret:** `src/sources/works/nicene-and-post-nicene-fathers/series-2-volume-2` contains only two image leaves and passage records for Socrates 1.11 and Sozomen 1.23 (not the Ambrose chapters); `series-2-volume-3` (Theodoret, Jerome, Rufinus) holds metadata only without a tracked text payload. Consequence: Theodoret V.18 (cathedral-door encounter with Theodosius; sanctuary exclusion noted in NPNF II.8 line 1700) and Sozomen/Socrates references are cited strictly at secondary reporting ceiling.
3. **Jerome, *De Viris Illustribus* ch. 124:** Tracked `src/sources/works/jerome/de-viris-illustribus/` holds only ch. 2 (Gospel of the Hebrews), not ch. 124 on Ambrose. Jerome's sharp criticism of *De Spiritu Sancto* (*"ex Graecis bonis Latina non bona"*) and praise of Ambrose as "a pillar of the Church" (*Adv. Rufin.* I.2) are therefore cited strictly via Blomfield Jackson's NPNF II.8 note (quoting Swete) and de Romestin's NPNF II.10 Prolegomena.
4. **Ambrose's funeral orations (*De Obitu Valentiniani*, *De Obitu Theodosii*) and *Letter* 59:** Not included in NPNF II.10 (de Romestin expressly laments their omission in the Translator's Preface, lines 92–94) and not tracked elsewhere locally. Used strictly via de Romestin's Prolegomena summaries.
5. **Augustine's mention in Ambrose's corpus (bounded negative):** Within all tracked Ambrose texts in NPNF II.10, `ambrose/hexameron/`, and checked `expositio-evangelii-secundum-lucam/` passages, Augustine is never named; the asymmetric evidence horizon rests on Augustine's own works.
6. **Boniface VIII's 1298 decretal (*Gloriosus Deus*) & modern Basilica Ambrosiana archaeological / *Te Deum* hymnological reports:** Not tracked locally in `src/sources/works/`. Retained at explicit reporting ceiling without claiming first-hand text inspection.
7. **Catechism of the Catholic Church (`src/sources/works/catholic-church/catechism/`):** Searched all tracked Catechism passage manifests for "Ambrose"; zero hits in the currently registered passage subset (bounded negative; does not claim Ambrose is absent from the full Catechism).
