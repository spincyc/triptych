## 3. Dated Occurrence Resolution: Sunday, 29 November 2026

### 3.1 Calendar computation (finding aid)

Resolved under `guidance/liturgy/calendar-computation.md` and `tools/tpt calendar-days day --calendar roman-1962 --date 2026-11-29`:

- **Liturgical-year anchors (2026–2027):** In civil year 2026, Easter Sunday fell on 5 April 2026, Pentecost on 24 May 2026, and the Twenty-fourth and Last Sunday after Pentecost on 22 November 2026 (`E = 3`, `P = 26`). Christmas Day (25 December 2026) falls on Friday; the fourth Sunday before 25 December (equivalently the Sunday falling on or closest to 30 November) is **Sunday, 29 November 2026**, which opens the **2026–2027 liturgical year** (running from 29 November 2026 through Saturday, 27 November 2027).
- **Universal calendar candidates on 29 November 2026:**
  1. `advent-1` — *Dominica I Adventus* (First Sunday of Advent), 1st-class Sunday (`RG 11`; `RG 91` row 6).
  2. `comm-s-saturnini-martyris` — *Comm. S. Saturnini Martyris* (Commemoration of St. Saturninus, Martyr; fixed date 29 November, rank `Comm.`, *Proprium Sanctorum* opening unnumbered folio, marginal nn. 1809–1811, PDF p. 509; `passage.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-1962.phase2-comm-s-saturnini-martyris-orations-recheck-2026-10-22`).
- **Occurrence, precedence, and commemoration result (`src/sources/calendars/roman-1962/rubrics.yaml`):**
  - **Slot / nominal formulary:** First Sunday of Advent (`advent-1`).
  - **Winner:** *Dominica I Adventus* (`RG 91` row 6, class I).
  - **Displaced / impeded item:** *Comm. S. Saturnini Martyris* is an **ordinary commemoration** (`RG 109` closing: *Omnes aliae commemorationes sunt commemorationes ordinariae*). Under `RG 111 a` and `RGMR 434`, a 1st-class liturgical day admits at most one commemoration and **only a privileged commemoration** (`RG 109 a–f`: Sunday, 1st-class day, days within the Nativity octave, September Ember Days, ferias of Advent/Lent/Passiontide, or Greater Litanies in Mass). St. Saturninus is therefore **omitted** in Mass (both Low and Sung) on Sunday, 29 November 2026; fixed commemorations do not transfer (`RG 94`).
  - **Abolished pre-1955 seasonal orations:** Under the 1960 *Variationes* (cap. II n. 13; cap. IV n. 18; *AAS* 52 [1960], pp. 708–709), the pre-1955 seasonal second and third orations (*orationes pro diversitate temporum*: *De S. Maria: Deus, qui de beatae Mariae Virginis utero...* and *Contra persecutores Ecclesiae* / *Pro Papa*) printed beneath the Collect in older missals such as Pustet 1862 and Lasance 1945 were abolished and are excluded from the 1962 celebration.
  - **Imposed and votive prayers:** Under `RGMR 454` and `457 d`, an ordinary *oratio imperata* is barred on 1st-class days (and in sung Masses); votive prayers (`RGMR 461–464`) belong only to 4th-class days. Under the universal 1962 calendar, Mass on Sunday, 29 November 2026 therefore has **one Collect, one Secret, and one Postcommunion** (`RGMR 480–481, 505`).
