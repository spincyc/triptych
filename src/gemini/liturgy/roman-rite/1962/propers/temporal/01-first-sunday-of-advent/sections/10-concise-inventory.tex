\subsection*{Appointed Propers and Governed Ordinary}

\begin{concisemaptable}
\textbf{Introit} & \latin{Ad te levavi}; Ps.~24:1--3, 4b (Heb.~25:1--3a, 4) & ``To thee have I lifted up my soul'': trusts God (\latin{qui te exspectant}, ``who wait on thee''), asks His ways and paths; keeps \latin{Gloria Patri}.\\
\textbf{Collect} & \latin{Excita, quaesumus}; echoes Ps.~79:3 (Heb.~80:3) & ``Bestir, O Lord, Thy might'': begs Christ (\latin{Qui vivis}, ``Who livest'') for rescue from sin's perils. Sole Collect (St.~Saturninus omitted 29~Nov.~2026).\\
\textbf{Epistle} & \latin{Scientes quia hora est}; Rom.~13:11--14a & ``Knowing that it is now the hour'': wakes believers to shed works of darkness and put on armour of light and Christ (no v.~14b).\\
\textbf{Gradual} & \latin{Universi, qui te exspectant}; Ps.~24:3b, 4b (Heb.~25:3a, 4) & ``All who wait on thee'': repeats unashamed trust; asks God's ways and paths (\latin{notas fac mihi}, ``make known to me'').\\
\textbf{Alleluia} & \latin{Ostende nobis}; Ps.~84:8 (Heb.~85:8; Eng.~85:7) & ``Shew us, O Lord, thy mercy'': Sunday only (omitted on resumed weekdays); begs mercy and salvation (\latin{salutare tuum}).\\
\textbf{Gospel} & \latin{Erunt signa in sole}; Luke~21:25--33 & ``There shall be signs in the sun'': cosmic signs, Son of Man in a cloud, fig tree and all trees budding, unfailing words.\\
\textbf{Offertory} & \latin{Ad te levavi}; Ps.~24:1--3 (Heb.~25:1--3a) & ``To thee have I lifted up my soul'': repeats the Introit antiphon verbatim as gifts reach the altar.\\
\textbf{Secret} & \latin{Haec sacra nos}; composed prayer & ``Cleansing us by their mighty power'': prays these Mysteries bring us purer to their divine Source (\latin{principium}).\\
\textbf{Communion} & \latin{Dominus dabit benignitatem}; Ps.~84:13 & ``The Lord will give goodness'' (Heb.~85:13; Eng.~85:12): proclaims God's goodness (\latin{benignitatem}) and our earth yielding her fruit.\\
\textbf{Postcommunion} & \latin{Suscipiamus, Domine}; echoes Ps.~47:10 & ``May we receive Thy mercy'' (Heb.~48:10; Eng.~48:9): asks temple mercy to prepare with fitting honour for the coming feast of reparation.\\
\textbf{Ordinary} & Violet; no \latin{Gloria}; \latin{Credo} \& Trinity Preface & Confesses Incarnation and Judgment; angelic Trinity praise; dismissal (\latin{Ite, missa est}); John~1:1--14. Weekdays: no \latin{Alleluia}/\latin{Credo}, Common Preface.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
