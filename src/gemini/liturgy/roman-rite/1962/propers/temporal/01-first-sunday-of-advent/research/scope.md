# Research Scope and Evidence Audit: First Sunday of Advent (1962 *Missale Romanum*)

## 1. Document Scope, Publication Mode, and Dated Occurrence

- **Canonical identity:** `liturgy/roman-rite/1962/propers/temporal/01-first-sunday-of-advent` (`roman-1962` calendar key `advent-1`, registry `01`).
- **Liturgical edition:** *Missale Romanum ex decreto SS. Concilii Tridentini restitutum, Summorum Pontificum cura recognitum, editio typica* (Vatican City: Typis Polyglottis Vaticanis, 1962; *Novo rubricarum corpore*, 23 June 1962). Strict 1962 liturgical isolation is observed throughout: no postconciliar calendar, Sunday cycle (A/B/C), Lectionary appointment, or postconciliar profile is consulted or imported.
- **Publication mode:** Full-text (`appointed_text_completeness = "complete"`, canonical print label `Full PDF`) under the three-document contract (`guidance/liturgy/propers-three-documents.md`) and 1962 profile (`guidance/liturgy/roman-1962-propers.md`).
- **Dated occurrence:** Sunday, **29 November 2026**, opening the 2026–2027 liturgical year (`RG 20`, `RG 71`, verified verbatim in *AAS* 52 [1960] checked texts). *Dominica prima Adventus* is a **Sunday of the 1st class** (*I classis* printed on p. 1; `RG 11`, `RG 15`, Table of Precedence `RG 91` row 6); its weekday ferias through 16 December are 3rd-class ferias (`RG 24–25`, resuming the Sunday Mass under `RGMR 299`).
  - **Displaced commemoration:** *Comm. S. Saturnini Martyris* (29 November; ordinary commemoration per `RG 109`) is omitted in Mass under `RG 111 a` (*AAS* 52) and `RGMR 434` (`src/sources/calendars/roman-1962/rubrics.yaml`).
  - **Abolished pre-1955 seasonal orations:** Under the 1960 *Variationes* (cap. II n. 13; cap. IV n. 18; *AAS* 52 [1960], pp. 708–709), the pre-1955 seasonal second and third orations (*De S. Maria* and *Contra persecutores Ecclesiae* / *Pro Papa*) printed in older missals (Pustet 1862, Tournai 1889, Lasance 1945) and expounded in Dom Prosper Guéranger are excluded. Mass on Sunday, 29 November 2026 has one Collect, one Secret, and one Postcommunion (`RGMR 434, 454, 457 d, 505` in `src/sources/calendars/roman-1962/rubrics.yaml`; `RGMR 480–481` in `rubricae-generales-missalis.toml`).
  - **Governed variable Ordinary elements verified:** Omission of *Gloria in excelsis* (visually collated printed rubric p. 1 R; `RGMR 431–432`); *Credo* required on Sundays (visually collated printed rubric p. 2 R; `RGMR 475–476`); **Preface of the Most Holy Trinity** (*Praefatio de sanctissima Trinitate*, n. 1082, visually collated rubric p. 2 R; `RGMR 494 b`; Common Preface on resumed Advent ferias, `RGMR 482, 498`); *Ite, missa est* at dismissal (**verified verbatim** in *AAS* 52 [1960], pp. 680–681, `artifact.holy-see.acta-apostolicae-sedis.volume-52-1960.rubricae-generales-missalis-n-507-checked-text-990c303a` — Mass ends with *Ite, missa est* [*Deo gratias*], while *Benedicamus Domino* applies only to the Holy Thursday evening Mass followed by solemn reposition of the Most Blessed Sacrament and to other Masses followed by a procession [*aliqua processio*], with double *Alleluia* in Easter octave Masses *de Tempore* and *Requiescant in pace* in Requiem Masses; no exception applies to Advent or Masses omitting *Gloria*); Ordinary Last Gospel John 1:1–14 (`RGMR 509–510`); dalmatic and tunicle retained, folded chasubles and broad stole abolished (**verified verbatim** in *AAS* 52 checked text `artifact.holy-see.acta-apostolicae-sedis.volume-52-1960.rubricae-generales-n-137-checked-text-3e16a97d`); rose permitted on Advent III and Lent IV (**verified verbatim** in *AAS* 52 checked text `artifact.holy-see.acta-apostolicae-sedis.volume-52-1960.rubricae-generales-n-131-checked-text-469cab21`).
  - **Dated 2026 institutional Ordos & rubric bounds:** Two dated 2026 institutional 1962 Ordo artifacts (`artifact.fssp-france.ordo-du-mois.2026-11-22-web.ordo-2404c50f` and `artifact.icrsp-france.ordo.2026-11-22-web.ordo-973580cb`) are registered in `src/sources/` with `storage = "restricted"`; in this run their registered metadata and SHA-256 hashes were audited without re-opening their restricted HTML bytes. Likewise, no checked *AAS* 52 artifact for the violet-color clause (`RG 128`) is tracked locally; violet is attested via the registered FSSP 2026 Ordo metadata (`ordo-2404c50f`) without asserting unverified `RG 128` Latin wording.
