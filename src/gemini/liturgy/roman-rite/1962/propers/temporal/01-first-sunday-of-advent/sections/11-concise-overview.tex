\subsection*{Four-Sense Overview}

\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & Trusting God and seeking His ways (Ps.~24); pleading for mercy and fruitful earth (Ps.~84); waking to put on Christ (Rom.~13:11--14a); cosmic signs, Son of Man, and budding trees (Luke~21:25--33); prayers at St.~Mary Major for rescue, cleansing, and Christmas.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Angelic judges and world's ruin as fruit (Gregory); armour of light (Chrysostom); Mary's ``light cloud'' and blossoming nations (Ambrose); the Spirit's goodness in Mary yielding Christ (Bellarmine, Sicard); Christ as Fruit of Ps.~84:13 (Bernard); the whole Christ (\latin{totus Christus}, Augustine); Christ coming within souls (Ambrose), His Middle Coming (Bernard), and purifying Mysteries (Schuster).\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Detachment from an aging world (Gregory) and earthly briers (Bellarmine); Advent fasting and alms (Leo); guarding baptismal dew (Ambrose); humility, confession ploughing hearts for delight, and breaking carnal habit (Augustine); commandments (\latin{viae}) to counsels (\latin{semitae}, Aquinas).\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & The public Parousia of the Son of Man, bodily resurrection into incorruption (Cyril), heaven and earth cleansed by fire in outward form (\latin{per imaginem}, Bede) and renewed by transmutation, not destroyed (Augustine), the summer harvest of nations, and purified worshippers approaching their divine \latin{principium} in Trinitarian praise.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
