| key | topic | verification finding | resolution applied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| catch-001 | Advent II Collect conclusion | The 1962 *Missale Romanum* (`propers.yaml`) ends `Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate.` (addressed to the Father regarding His Son). | catch-004 | Luke 2:7 vs. Luke 23:53 verbs | Checked in SBLGNT v1.2 and Robinson-Pierpont 2018: zero shared Greek verbs (`esparganosen`/`aneklinen` vs. `enetylixen`/`etheken`); Vulgate shares only `involvit`. | Framed affirmatively in Ch. 12 and App. C alongside St. Gregory Nazianzen *Oration* 29.19. |
| catch-005 | 1962 Advent dismissal & ferial Alleluia | Verified in 1960 *Codex Rubricarum* RGMR n. 507 (`Ite, missa est` at all Masses except Requiem/procession) and RGMR n. 299 (ferias omit Alleluia with no Tract). | Stated accurately in Ch. 6 and App. A. |
| catch-006 | 24 Dec Vigil on Sun IV & 8 Dec on Sun II | Verified in RG nn. 15, 30 a, 91, 109 a: 24 Dec Vigil replaces Sun IV with no commemoration; 8 Dec wins over Sun II with one privileged commemoration of Sun II. | Reflected across Ch. 7, Ch. 11, App. A, and `research/calendar-verification.md`. |
| catch-007 | Upstream `latVUC` typo in Latin *Benedictus* (Luke 1:70) | Tracked `eBible.org latVUC` (`49-luke.tsv`, Luke 1:70) carries the ungrammatical digitization error `sicut locutum est` instead of deponent `sicut locutus est`. | Collated against Migne PL 142 (Bruno of Wurzburg) and Blunt (1866), restored `sicut locutus est` in App. B, and documented the defect in App. C and App. D. |
