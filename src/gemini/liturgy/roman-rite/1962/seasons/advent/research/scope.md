- **Provider leaf:** `gemini/liturgy/roman-rite/1962/seasons/advent`

- **Kind:** `seasonal-handbook-1962` (`liturgy-reference-v1`)

- **Liturgical horizon:** The universal 1962 Roman Rite (*Missale Romanum* and *Breviarium Romanum* under Pope John XXIII's motu proprio *Rubricarum instructum* of 25 July 1960 and the Sacred Congregation of Rites' *Codex Rubricarum* of 26 July 1960, effective 1 January 1961; typical Missal decree 23 June 1962; and the Sacred Congregation of Rites' instruction *De musica sacra et sacra liturgia* of 3 September 1958).
- **Seasonal boundaries:** From First Vespers of the First Sunday of Advent (the Sunday falling on or nearest 30 November, i.e., 27 November through 3 December) through None inclusive of the Vigil of the Nativity of the Lord (24 December), ending at the threshold of Christmas First Vespers and Midnight Mass (RG nn. 20, 71).
- **Legal and territorial horizon:** Historical discipline (Gallican councils, Trent Sess. XXIV c. 10, 1889 Baltimore *Manual of Prayers*), 1962 universal Latin discipline (1917 *Codex Iuris Canonici* cc. 1108 §§1–3, 1252 §§1, 2, 4; Sacred Congregation of the Council decree of 3 December 1959 on Christmas Vigil fast anticipation), and current universal Latin law (1983 *Codex Iuris Canonici* cc. 1246–1247, 1249–1253; St. Paul VI, *Paenitemini*, 17 February 1966) alongside United States particular law (NCCB *Pastoral Statement on Penance and Abstinence*, 18 November 1966, and the 1983/1984 USCCB Complementary Norm to cc. 1252–1253), checked as of 2026-11-25.

## Explicit Exclusions and Source Limits

- **No 1961/1962 *Breviarium Romanum* held:** Breviary texts are quoted from checked pre-1911 witnesses (Pustet Ratisbon 1888 Latin; Marquess of Bute 1908 English) read strictly under the structural rubrics of the 1960 *Codex Rubricarum* (AAS 52).
- **Unverified or absent items excluded from claims:** No *Veni, veni Emmanuel*, no pre-1632 *Conditor alme siderum* verbatim, no medieval commentary attribution for *ERO CRAS*, no Advent wreath blessing ritual, no candlelit dawn Rorate custom, no St. Nicholas or St. Lucy folk customs, no St. Mary Major crib-relic or Holy Cross relic claims, no Eastern primary Nativity-fast source, no 1917 CIC c. 1254 age bounds, and no 1991/1992 USCCB c. 1246 §2 holy-day abrogation decree text.
- **Unverified station pages noted honestly:** Advent I, Vigil of Christmas, and the three Christmas Masses are page-image verified on the 1962 typical edition and 1862 Pustet; Advent II, III, IV, and Ember Days rest on tracked OCR layers of Pustet 1862 and Lasance 1945.
- **Standalone proper guides:** Full Mass formularies, complete component inventories, and standalone Sunday homilies belong to the Proper of Time guides rather than this seasonal handbook.
