\section{References}\label{app:references}

All editions listed below were consulted directly through the repository's
primary-source holdings. Public-domain works remain public domain; restricted
contemporary texts of the Holy See and the United States Conference of Catholic
Bishops are cited for verified norms and summarized without bulk reproduction.

\subsection{Sacred Scripture}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{The Holy Bible, Douay-Rheims, Complete} (Challoner revision;
Project Gutenberg eBook 1581; public domain). Primary English Scripture text
for all biblical quotations in the body and daily course.
\item \textit{The Holy Bible, Douay-Rheims Version, American Edition} (1899;
eBible.org \texttt{engDRA}; public domain). Used for the Gospel canticles in
Appendix~\ref{app:prayers} and liturgical chant comparisons.
\item \textit{Biblia Sacra Vulgata Clementina} (1598; eBible.org
\texttt{latVUC}; public domain). Latin Scripture text; for the
\textit{Benedictus} (Luke 1:70), collated against Bruno of W\"urzburg,
\textit{Expositio Psalmorum et Canticorum} (Migne PL 142, Paris, 1853) and
J.~H.~Blunt, \textit{The Annotated Book of Common Prayer} (London, 1866), to
restore the liturgical reading \textit{sicut locutus est}.
\item \textit{The Greek New Testament: SBL Edition}, ed.\ Michael W.\ Holmes
(v1.2; CC BY 4.0) and \textit{The New Testament in the Original Greek:
Byzantine Textform 2018}, ed.\ Maurice A.\ Robinson and William G.\ Pierpont
(public domain). Checked for Luke 2:7 and Luke 23:53.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Liturgical Books, Sacramentaries, and Chant Sources}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini
restitutum, Summorum Pontificum cura recognitum, editio typica} (Vatican City:
Typis Polyglottis Vaticanis, 1962). Page-image verification control for the
1962 Latin propers, ferial Alleluia rubrics, and stations of Advent~I, the
Vigil, and the three Christmas Masses.
\item \textit{Missale Romanum} (Ratisbon: Fridericus Pustet, 1862; public
domain). Historical Latin base text collated against the 1962 typical edition.
\item Lasance, Francis Xavier, and Francis Augustine Walsh, \textit{The New
Roman Missal in Latin and English}, revised edition (New York: Benziger
Brothers, 1945; public domain). English translations of the Collects of
Advent~I (p.~103), Advent~III (p.~110), Ember Wednesday first Collect (p.~113),
Ember Friday (p.~118), Ember Saturday (p.~121), Advent~IV (p.~130), the Vigil
of the Nativity (pp.~133--135), the three Masses of Christmas (pp.~137,
140--141, 143--144), and the Immaculate Conception (p.~851).
\item Lefebvre, Dom Gaspar, O.S.B., \textit{Daily Missal with Vespers} (Bruges:
Abbey of St.~Andr\'e; St.~Paul: E.~M.~Lohmann, 1925; public domain). English
translations of the Collect of Advent~II (p.~339) and the second Collect of
Ember Wednesday (\textit{Festina, quaesumus}, pp.~352--353).
\item \textit{Breviarium Romanum, editio prima post typicam, pars hiemalis}
(Ratisbon, New York, and Cincinnati: Fridericus Pustet, 1888; public domain).
Latin text for the Isaiah Matins lesson headings (pp.~133--173), First
Vespers of Advent~I (pp.~132--133), \textit{Alma Redemptoris Mater}
(pp.~130--131), \textit{Creator alme siderum} (p.~132), Great O Antiphons
(pp.~156--157), Vigil Matins headings (pp.~174--175), \textit{O magnum
mysterium} (p.~183), and \textit{O admirabile commercium} (p.~231).
\item \textit{The Roman Breviary}, translated out of Latin into English by John,
Marquess of Bute, K.T., new edition for use in England, Vol.~I: Winter
(Edinburgh and London: William Blackwood and Sons, 1908; public domain).
English text for \textit{Alma Redemptoris Mater} (pp.~210--211), St.~John
Henry Newman's translation of \textit{Creator alme siderum} (p.~213), the
\textit{Rorate} versicle (p.~213), Advent~I--IV and Ember Matins lessons
(pp.~214, 228, 240--241, 249, 252--254, 259), the Great O Antiphons
(pp.~243--244), the Christmas Eve \textit{Kalenda} (p.~269), \textit{O magnum
mysterium} (p.~274), St.~Gregory's Homily~8 (p.~276), and \textit{O admirabile
commercium} and \textit{Quando natus es} (p.~315).
\item \textit{Pal\'eographie musicale, deuxi\`eme s\'erie, I: Antiphonale du
B.~Hartker} (Solesmes: Imprimerie Saint-Pierre par Sabl\'e, 1900; Cod.\ Sang.\
390, late 10th c., MS pp.~40--41 / PDF pp.~94--95). Medieval source for the
seven Roman Great O Antiphons in order.
\item \textit{Martyrologium Romanum} (Rome: Propaganda Fide, 1902, p.~193;
public domain). Pre-1913 Latin text for the Christmas Eve \textit{Kalenda}.
\item Abbey of Solesmes, \textit{A Manual of Gregorian Chant} (Rome and
Tournai: Descl\'ee, Lefebvre \& Co., 1903, pp.~313--315; public domain). Latin
text of the four-verse \textit{Rorate caeli} prose.
\item Gu\'eranger, Dom Prosper, O.S.B., \textit{The Liturgical Year: Advent},
2nd ed., trans.\ Dom Laurence Shepherd, O.S.B.\ (Dublin: James Duffy, 1870,
pp.~24--28, 155--156; public domain). English text of the \textit{Rorate caeli}
prose and historical remarks.
\item \textit{The Raccolta}, trans.\ Ambrose St.~John (London: Burns \& Oates;
New York: Benziger Brothers, 1910, no.~182, pp.~151--152; public domain). Latin
and English text of the \textit{Angelus}.
\item \textit{The Gelasian Sacramentary: Liber Sacramentorum Romanae
Ecclesiae}, ed.\ H.~A.~Wilson (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1894, Book~II,
LXXX--LXXXIV; public domain). Five Advent Masses in Vat.\ Reg.\ lat.\ 316.
\item \textit{The Gregorian Sacramentary under Charles the Great}, ed.\
H.~A.~Wilson, Henry Bradshaw Society Vol.~49 (London, 1915, pp.~113--116;
public domain). Four Sundays of Advent in the \textit{Hadrianum}.
\item \textit{Manuale Ambrosianum ex codice saec.~XI}, ed.\ Marco Magistretti
(Milan: Hoepli, 1904; public domain). Six-Sunday Ambrosian Advent.
\item \textit{Le Liber Mozarabicus Sacramentorum}, ed.\ Dom Marius F\'erotin
(Paris: Firmin-Didot, 1912; public domain). Five- and six-Sunday Mozarabic
Advent manuscripts and editions.
\end{itemize}
