\section{An Evergreen Daily Course for Advent}\label{app:daily-course}

This daily course is built to serve any civil year without rewriting. Throughout
the tables below, what the 1962 Roman Missal and Breviary appoint at Mass and
Matins is marked separately from the devotional reading and prayer chosen for
the home.

\subsection{How to Find Today in Any Year}

Under the 1960 \textit{Codex Rubricarum} (RG nn.~20, 71), Advent opens at First
Vespers of the Sunday falling on or nearest 30 November (27 November through
3 December) and closes at None inclusive of 24 December, lasting between
twenty-two and twenty-eight calendar days. Every year follows two tables:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{From the First Sunday of Advent through 16 December:} Follow
Table~1 by recurring week and weekday (Week~I Sunday through Saturday, Week~II
Sunday through Saturday, and Week~III Sunday through Saturday as far as 16
December). Because Week~I always ends by 9 December and Week~II always ends by
16 December, every day of Weeks~I and II occurs in every year.
\item \textbf{From 17 December through 24 December:} On 17 December, every year
switches cleanly to Table~2 (the seven Great O Antiphons and the Vigil of the
Nativity). Any weekday slots of Week~III not yet reached before 17 December are
simply omitted, matching the Breviary's omission of impeded Scriptural lessons
(RGBR n.~213).
\item \textbf{Movable Sundays, Ember Days, and Fixed Feasts:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Third or Fourth Sunday inside 17--24 December:} The Fourth Sunday
of Advent always falls between 18 and 24 December; when Advent~I falls on
3 December, the Third Sunday itself falls on 17 December. On any Sunday from
17 to 23 December, take the Sunday's Mass readings and Collect alongside that
calendar date's Great O Antiphon at evening prayer. When 24 December falls on a
Sunday, the Vigil of the Nativity replaces the Fourth Sunday outright with no
commemoration (RG n.~30~a).
\item \textit{Winter Ember Days (Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday after the Third
Sunday):} Ember Wednesday falls 14--20 December, Ember Friday 16--22 December,
and Ember Saturday 17--23 December (always inside Table~2). On those three
days, use the Ember Day rows in the movable overlay table below, keeping the
date's Great O Antiphon at dusk on 17--23 December.
\item \textit{Fixed Feasts:} On \textbf{8 December (Immaculate Conception)},
use the Immaculate Conception row below; when 8 December falls on the Second
Sunday (Advent~I = 1 December), the Immaculate Conception takes precedence as a
first-class feast and the Second Sunday receives a privileged commemoration
(RG nn.~15, 109~a). On \textbf{30 November (St.~Andrew)} and \textbf{13
December (St.~Lucy)}, a Sunday takes precedence if they meet. On \textbf{21
December (St.~Thomas, Apostle)}, the second-class feast wins over an Advent
feria or Ember Friday/Saturday (which receives a privileged commemoration, RG
n.~109~e), but yields to the Fourth Sunday when Advent~I falls on 30 November.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
