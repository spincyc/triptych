\section{An Evergreen Daily Course for Christmastide}\label{app:daily-course}

This daily course serves any civil year without rewriting. Throughout both
tables below, the Scripture appointed by the 1962 Roman Missal and Breviary at
Mass and Matins is marked separately from the devotional reading and prayer
chosen for the home.

\subsection{How to Find Today in Any Year}

Under the 1960 \textit{Codex Rubricarum} (General Rubric~72~a--b), Christmastide
(\textit{Tempus Nativitatis}) opens at First Vespers of Christmas on the evening
of 24~December and closes at None inclusive of 5~January, spanning twelve
calendar days in every year (25~December through 5~January). Because the twelve
days never cross 29~February, the weekday of 25~December uniquely determines the
entire calendar shape of the season. Three simple steps locate today in any
year:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Follow Table~1 by civil date (25~December through 5~January):}
Most days stand fixed on their calendar dates---Christmas Day (25~December),
St.~Stephen (26~December), St.~John (27~December), the Holy Innocents
(28~December), Days~5--7 within the Octave (29--31~December), the Octave Day of
the Nativity (1~January), and the ferias of 2--5~January.
\item \textbf{Apply the Sunday switch inside 26--31~December:} Whenever a
\textbf{Sunday} falls from 26 through 31~December (which happens in six of the
seven calendar shapes; never when 25~December is itself a Sunday), turn to the
\textbf{Sunday within the Octave} row in Table~2. On Sunday 26, 27, or
28~December, Low Mass adds an ordinary commemoration of St.~Stephen, St.~John,
or the Holy Innocents; on Sunday 29 or 31~December, St.~Thomas of Canterbury and
St.~Sylvester~I are omitted altogether.
\item \textbf{Apply the Most Holy Name and Saturday switches inside
2--5~January:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Feast of the Most Holy Name of Jesus:} On whichever \textbf{Sunday}
falls between 2 and 5~January---or on \textbf{2~January} when no Sunday falls
between 1 and 6~January (that is, whenever 25~December falls on Sunday, Monday,
or Tuesday)---turn to the \textbf{Most Holy Name of Jesus} row in Table~2. When
this feast falls on Sunday 5~January, St.~Telesphorus is omitted even in Low
Mass.
\item \textit{Our Lady on Saturday (2--5~January):} Whenever a \textbf{Saturday}
falls on 2, 3, 4, or 5~January (which happens whenever 25~December falls on
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday), turn to the \textbf{Our Lady on
Saturday} row in Table~2; if that Saturday is 5~January, Low Mass adds the
ordinary commemoration of St.~Telesphorus.
\item \textit{Monday--Friday ferias (2--5~January):} On every remaining weekday
from 2 through 5~January, follow Table~1; Mass repeats the 1~January Mass
with the \textit{Gloria} and Nativity Preface, without the Creed or proper
\textit{Communicantes} (adding St.~Telesphorus in Low Mass on 5~January). At
dusk on 5~January, First Vespers of the Epiphany opens \textit{Tempus
Epiphaniae}.
\end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
