\section{References}\label{app:references}

All editions listed below were consulted directly through primary-source
holdings. Public-domain works remain public domain; restricted contemporary
texts of the Holy See and the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops are
cited for verified norms and summarized without bulk reproduction.

\subsection{Sacred Scripture}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{The Holy Bible, Douay-Rheims, Complete} (Challoner revision;
Project Gutenberg eBook~1581; public domain). Primary English Scripture text for
biblical quotations throughout the chapters and daily course.
\item \textit{The Holy Bible, Douay-Rheims Version, American Edition} (1899;
eBible.org \texttt{engDRA}; public domain). English text for the Gospel
canticles in Appendix~\ref{app:prayers}.
\item \textit{Biblia Sacra Vulgata Clementina} (1598; eBible.org
\texttt{latVUC}; public domain). Latin Scripture text; for the
\textit{Benedictus} (Luke~1:70), collated against Bruno of W\"urzburg,
\textit{Expositio Psalmorum et Canticorum} (Migne PL~142, Paris, 1853) and
J.~H.~Blunt, \textit{The Annotated Book of Common Prayer} (London, 1866), to
restore the liturgical reading \textit{sicut locutus est}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Liturgical Books, Sacramentaries, and Historical Surveys}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini
restitutum, Summorum Pontificum cura recognitum, editio typica} (Vatican City:
Typis Polyglottis Vaticanis, 1962). Page-image verification control for the
1962 Latin propers, stations, Nativity Preface, proper \textit{Communicantes},
and Saturday Our Lady rubric.
\item \textit{Missale Romanum}, editio quarta iuxta typicam (Tours: Alfred Mame,
1922; public domain). Latin Ordinary control for the Preface of the Nativity
(p.~329) and the proper \textit{Communicantes} (p.~329).
\item \textit{Missale Romanum} (Ratisbon: Fridericus Pustet, 1862; public
domain). Historical Latin base text for the Christmas stations (pp.~16--19) and
the pre-1913 placement of the Most Holy Name of Jesus (pp.~378--379).
\item Lasance, Francis Xavier, and Francis Augustine Walsh, \textit{The New
Roman Missal in Latin and English}, revised edition (New York: Benziger
Brothers, 1945; public domain). English Collects of Midnight Mass (p.~137), Dawn
Mass (pp.~140--141), St.~Anastasia (p.~141), Day Mass (pp.~143--144), St.~John
(p.~150), the Holy Innocents (p.~154), St.~Thomas of Canterbury (pp.~157--158),
the Sunday within the Octave (pp.~160--161), and the Common of Supreme Pontiffs
(p.~1302); collated against the 1962 Latin for St.~Stephen, 1~January, the Dawn
Postcommunion, and the Nativity Preface.
\item Lefebvre, Dom Gaspar, O.S.B., \textit{Daily Missal with Vespers} (Bruges:
Abbey of St.~Andr\'e; St.~Paul: E.~M.~Lohmann, 1925; public domain). English
Collect of St.~Stephen (p.~399) and Nativity \textit{Communicantes} (pp.~59--60).
\item \textit{Breviarium Romanum, editio prima post typicam, pars hiemalis}
(Ratisbon, New York, and Cincinnati: Fridericus Pustet, 1888; public domain).
Latin text for \textit{Alma Redemptoris Mater} (pp.~130--131), Christmas First
Vespers, Matins, Lauds, and Second Vespers (pp.~177--191), \textit{Jesu
Redemptor omnium}, \textit{A solis ortus cardine}, \textit{Salvete flores
Martyrum} (p.~207), 1~January antiphons and responsories (pp.~229--232), and the
Most Holy Name Office and hymns (pp.~412--418).
\item \textit{The Roman Breviary}, translated out of Latin into English by John,
Marquess of Bute, K.T., new edition for use in England, Vol.~I: Winter
(Edinburgh and London: William Blackwood and Sons, 1908; public domain). English
text for \textit{Alma Redemptoris Mater} (pp.~210--211), the Christmas Eve
\textit{Kalenda} (p.~269), the Christmas and Octave Offices, 1~January
Martyrology (p.~309), 1~January Collect (p.~315), and Matins patristic lessons
(pp.~271--315), the hymns translated by R.~F.~Littledale (pp.~278--279),
J.~M.~Neale (pp.~296--297), and Edward Caswall (pp.~365--372), and the Most Holy
Name Collect and Office (pp.~365--373).
\item \textit{Martyrologium Romanum} (Rome: Propaganda Fide, 1902; public
domain). Latin text for the Christmas Proclamation (25~December), St.~Almachius
(1~January), and St.~Telesphorus (5~January).
\item Chronograph of 354 / \textit{Depositio Martyrum}, ed.\ Theodor Mommsen,
\textit{Chronica Minora}, Vol.~I, MGH Auctores Antiquissimi Vol.~IX (Berlin:
Weidmann, 1892, p.~71; public domain). Earliest Roman attestation of Christmas
on 25~December.
\item \textit{Sacramentarium Leonianum} [\textit{Veronense}], ed.\ Charles
Lett Feltoe (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1896; public domain).
Fifth- and sixth-century Roman Nativity \textit{libelli}.
\item \textit{The Gelasian Sacramentary: Liber Sacramentorum Romanae Ecclesiae},
ed.\ H.~A.~Wilson (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1894, Book~I, IX--X; public domain).
Early Roman Nativity, \textit{Comites Christi}, 1~January Octave, and
\textit{Prohibendum ab Idolis}.
\item \textit{The Gregorian Sacramentary under Charles the Great}, ed.\
H.~A.~Wilson, Henry Bradshaw Society Vol.~49 (London, 1915; public domain).
Three Roman Christmas stations and the 1~January Pantheon Octave.
\item Schuster, Bl.~Ildefonso, O.S.B., \textit{The Sacramentary (Liber
Sacramentorum): Historical \& Liturgical Notes on the Roman Missal}, Vol.~I,
trans.\ Arthur Levelis-Marke (London: Burns Oates \& Washbourne, 1924; public
domain). Roman stational archaeology for 24~December through 5~January.
\item \textit{The Catholic Encyclopedia}, Vol.~III (New York: Robert Appleton
Co., 1908; public domain via New Advent normalized text): C.~C.~Martindale,
S.J., ``Christmas,'' and John Tierney, ``Feast of the Circumcision.''
\end{itemize}
