## 1. Critical Textual and Liturgical Verification Catches

| key | topic | verification finding | resolution applied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| catch-001 | Holy Innocents (28 Dec) 1962 vestments and chants | Pre-1960 books (e.g., Lasance 1945 p. 153) kept weekday Holy Innocents in mourning violet without *Gloria*, *Te Deum*, or *Alleluia*, using folded chasubles and *Benedicamus Domino*. Under the 1960 *Codex Rubricarum* (RG 124 e, RG 137, RGMR 431 a, 507) and 1962 Missal pp. 27–28 (unit 186), red, *Gloria*, *Te Deum*, *Alleluia* (*Laudate, pueri*), and *Ite, missa est* apply unconditionally, and folded chasubles are abolished. | Stated accurately in Ch. 3, Ch. 7, App. A, and App. C. |
| catch-002 | Non-Holy-Name Saturdays in 2–5 January | On Saturdays in 2–5 Jan (57/101 seasons, whenever 25 Dec is Tue–Fri), obligatory IV cl. Office and Mass of Our Lady on Saturday (`missa-de-s-maria-in-sabbato-2`, *Vultum tuum*, row 27, 1962 pp. [44]–[45]) outranks the IV cl. feria (row 28) and retains its proper BVM Preface (*Et te in veneratione*) because 2–5 Jan lacks the Octave override of 26–31 Dec (1962 p. 284). | Verified 0/98 discrepancies in `tools/calendar-rubrics`; documented in Ch. 12, App. A, App. C, and `calendar-verification.md`. |
| catch-003 | St. Stephen (26 Dec) Collect omission in Lasance | 1962 Missal (p. 23, no. 152) ends `Dominum nostrum Jesum Christum, Filium tuum: Qui tecum...`. | catch-004 | 1 January Collect (*Deus, qui salutis aeternae*) Lasance typo | Lasance (1945, pp. 167 and 1403) inserts an erratic semicolon (`"Our Lord Jesus Christ; Thy Son."`). | Used Marquess of Bute (1908, p. 211) in Ch. 10 and App. B; noted defect in App. C. |
| catch-005 | St. Sylvester Collect Lasance p. 164 vs. Common p. 1302 | Lasance p. 164 inserts *"Thy"* before *"Supreme Pontiff"* (absent from 1962 Latin). | catch-008 | Upstream `latVUC` typo in Latin *Benedictus* (Luke 1:70) | Tracked `eBible.org latVUC` (`49-luke.tsv`) prints `sicut locutum est` instead of deponent `sicut locutus est`. | Collated against Migne PL 142 (Bruno of Würzburg) and Blunt (1866), restored `sicut locutus est` in App. B, and noted in App. C & D. |
| catch-009 | *Benedicta et venerabilis es* & Durandus locus | Verified in Pustet 1888 p. 229 that *Benedicta et venerabilis es* is 1 January R6 (not Christmas Day); verified Durandus's *Comites Christi* sits in Book VII Ch. 42 (1612 Lyon) and *Dum medium silentium* in Book VI (1568). | Cited accurately across Ch. 3, Ch. 7, Ch. 8, Ch. 10, and App. C. |
