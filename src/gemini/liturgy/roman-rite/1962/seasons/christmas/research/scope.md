# Scope and Plan: Keeping Christmas (1962 Roman Rite)

## Identity and Boundaries

- **Provider leaf:** `gemini/liturgy/roman-rite/1962/seasons/christmas`
- **Title:** *Keeping Christmas: A Companion to the 1962 Roman Rite*
- **Kind:** `seasonal-handbook-1962` (`liturgy-reference-v1`)
- **Governing profiles:** `guidance/editorial.md`, `guidance/liturgy/roman-1962-seasonal-handbooks.md`, `guidance/liturgy/calendar-computation.md`, `guidance/document-schemas.md`, `guidance/web-editions.md`
- **Liturgical horizon:** The universal 1962 Roman Rite (*Missale Romanum* issued by decree of the Sacred Congregation of Rites on 23 June 1962, and *Breviarium Romanum*, governed by Pope St. John XXIII's motu proprio *Rubricarum instructum* of 25 July 1960 and the *Codex Rubricarum* of 26 July 1960, effective 1 January 1961).
- **Seasonal boundaries:** Under General Rubric 72 a–b, *Tempus Natalicium* runs from First Vespers of the Nativity (24 December evening) through 13 January, subdivided into **Christmastide (*Tempus Nativitatis*: First Vespers of 24 December through None of 5 January inclusive)** and Epiphanytide (*Tempus Epiphaniae*: First Vespers of 5 January through 13 January). This handbook covers the complete twelve-day arc of Christmastide and hands off cleanly at dusk on 5 January to the separate Epiphany seasonal handbook.
- **Legal and territorial horizon:** Earlier historical discipline (Council of Trent Sess. XXIV c. 10; Third Plenary Council of Baltimore 1884 / *Manual of Prayers* 1889), 1962 universal Latin discipline (1917 *Codex Iuris Canonici* cc. 1108 §§1–3, 1252 §§1, 2, 4; Sacred Congregation of the Council decree of 3 December 1959 on Christmas Vigil fast anticipation), and current universal Latin law (1983 *Codex Iuris Canonici* cc. 1246–1247, 1249–1253; Pope St. Paul VI, *Paenitemini*, 17 February 1966) alongside United States particular law (NCCB *Pastoral Statement on Penance and Abstinence*, 18 November 1966; USCCB Complementary Norm to cc. 1252–1253, 1983/1984; 2026 USCCB *Liturgical Calendar* miscellaneous notes), checked as of 2026-11-27.

## Explicit Exclusions and Source Limits

- **No 1961/1962 *Breviarium Romanum* held:** Breviary texts rest on tracked pre-1911 public-domain witnesses (Pustet Ratisbon 1888 Latin; Marquess of Bute 1908 English) read strictly under the 1960 *Codex Rubricarum* structural rubrics (AAS 52).
- **Absent primary works omitted rather than fabricated:** St. Ephrem, St. Bonaventure (*Legenda maior*; Greccio cited via Martindale 1908), St. Gertrude, St. Mechtild, St. Catherine of Siena, St. Bernardine of Siena, Innocent XIII's 1721 Holy Name decree, Guéranger's Christmas volume, Newman's *Parochial and Plain Sermons* Vol. 2, Chrysostom's *In diem natalem*, 1917 CIC cc. 806 §1 and 1247, 1983 CIC c. 1248, 1991/1992 USCCB c. 1246 §2 decree text, and Pope John XXIII's Nov 13, 1962 St. Joseph Canon decree text (AAS 54 p. 873).
- **Restricted contemporary texts summarized without bulk quotation:** 1983 CIC, *Paenitemini*, *Mysterii Paschalis*, USCCB 1966/1983/2026 norms, Benedict XVI (*Roman Curia* 2005, *Sacramentum Caritatis*), and Pope Francis (*Admirabile signum*, Dec 30, 2020 Audience).
- **Epiphanytide belongs to its own handbook:** First Vespers of Epiphany (5 January evening) through 13 January belongs to the separate Epiphany seasonal handbook.
