## Occurrence and Rights Verification Notes

1. **Calendar occurrence:** Both the USCCB 2026 and 2027 *Liturgical Calendar* front-matter tables (`principal-celebrations-and-cycles`) confirm that the First Sunday of Advent opening Liturgical Year 2027 falls on **29 November 2026**, inaugurating **Sunday Cycle B** and **Weekday Cycle I** (corroborated against `tools/tpt calendar-days`). Because the full restricted PDFs (`2026cal.pdf`, `2027cal.pdf`) and USCCB daily readings web page (`112926.cfm`) are not tracked in Git and this research ran offline, the 29 November day-entry page of `2027cal.pdf` and live HTML of `112926.cfm` remain explicit uninspected offline boundaries; the Sunday cycle and OLM appointments rest on the verified front-matter cycle tables and the 200-dpi OLM 1981 collation recorded in `src/sources/calendars/postconciliar/propers.yaml` (`advent-1`, Year B).
2. **Rights posture:** Under `rights-limited`, protected ICEL 2011 English Missal texts, modern Latin Missal oration/Preface/blessing bodies, and copyrighted CCD/NABRE U.S. Lectionary translations and refrains are never reproduced or closely paraphrased. Study Scripture texts use tracked public-domain Clementine Vulgate Latin and Douay–Rheims (Challoner) study English, explicitly distinguished from the approved proclaimed U.S. Lectionary.
