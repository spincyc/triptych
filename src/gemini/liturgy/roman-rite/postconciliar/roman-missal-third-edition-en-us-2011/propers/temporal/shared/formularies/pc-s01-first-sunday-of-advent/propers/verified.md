## 2. Missal-Owned Formulary Elements, Boundaries, and Rights Dispositions

Under `liturgical-english-rights-v1.toml` and `postconciliar-latin-rights-v1.toml`, modern Missal oration bodies and ICEL 2011 English proper texts are rights-restricted (`appointed_text_completeness = "rights-limited"`). Only lawfully distributable public-domain texts, short Latin identifying incipits, verified structural metadata, and independent historical witnesses are recorded here.

### 2.1 Entrance Antiphon (`entrance-antiphon`)
- **Identifying Latin incipit:** *Ad te levavi*
- **Cataloged Scripture reference:** `Psalm 25:1-3` (Hebrew / U.S. numbering) = `Psalm 24:1-3` (Clementine Vulgate / Douay–Rheims whole-verse carrier; `Ps 24:1b–3b` excluding superscription).
- **Collation status & explicit bound:** Per `src/sources/calendars/postconciliar/propers.yaml` (`advent-1`) and `src/sources/inventories/postconciliar-seasonal-collation-v1.toml`, the Entrance Antiphon of *Dominica I Adventus* was **not** directly read from the 2002/2008 Latin Missal or the 2010 ICEL Antiphonary during the 1981 *Ordo lectionum Missae* pass; `Psalm 25:1-3` remains an uncollated catalog lead. Whether the Missal cue reads `Cf. Ps 24(25):1-3` or `Ps 25:1-3`, and the exact editorial truncation of the antiphon text relative to verses 1–3, remain unverified in this offline session.
- **Rights disposition:** Protected ICEL English antiphon omitted; public-domain study Scripture (Clementine Vulgate and Douay–Rheims Challoner `Ps 24:1–3`) is used strictly as study Scripture, never presented as the approved proclaimed antiphon.
- **Rubrical alternatives (GIRM 48):** Four United States options for singing — (1) Missal antiphon or *Graduale Romanum* antiphon with its psalm, (2) *Graduale Simplex* antiphon and psalm, (3) a chant from another approved collection of psalms and antiphons, or (4) another approved suitable liturgical chant — and recitation of the Missal Entrance Antiphon where there is no singing.

### 2.2 Collect (`collect`)
- **Identifying Latin incipit:** *Da, quaesumus, omnipotens Deus*
- **Rights disposition:** `rights-withheld` (`edition.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-tertia-2002`; recorded in `postconciliar/propers.yaml` and `postconciliar-proper-translations-v1.toml` under `[[untranslated]]`). Neither the modern Latin body nor the ICEL 2011 English translation is reproduced or closely paraphrased.
- **Historical antecedent (not a modern substitute):** The *Sacramentarium Gelasianum Vetus* II.lxxxiv (*Item alia missa de Adventu*, *Postcommunio* 683, Wilson 1894, p. 218; tracked raw uncollated OCR locating witness `historical-book-ocr-e4500b54`, lines 20493–20499; section heading verified on p. 218 in `advent-missae-a3-checked-text-d4a8d64a`) transmits the ancient Roman formula:
  > *Da, quaesumus, omnipotens Deus, cunctae familiae tuae [OCR-corrupted word: `^^^`] voluntatem in Christo Filio tuo, Domino nostro venienti in operibus iustis aptos occurrere : et eius dexterae sociati regnum mereantur possidere caeleste. Per.*
  (Note: this Wilson 1894 OCR is an uncollated locating witness (`inspected`, not facsimile-verified); the single word preceding *voluntatem* on line 20495 is OCR-corrupted as `^^^`, and the printed p. 218 image is not tracked in this checkout, so the garbled token is preserved honestly rather than silently emended.) This historical prayer is documented strictly as a patristic-era Roman antecedent asking God for the will to go forth to meet the coming Christ in righteous deeds so as to be joined to His right hand and possess the heavenly kingdom. Its exact lexical alignment against the 2002/2008 printed Missal was not directly collated offline and is not claimed to be identical word-for-word to the modern Collect.
- **Rubric:** Preceded by the Penitential Act (or optional sprinkling of water, GIRM 51) without the *Gloria* (GIRM 53 / IGMR 53); no competing feast or commemoration is admitted on Sundays of Advent (NUALC 59–60).

### 2.3 Prayer over the Offerings (`prayer-over-the-offerings`)
- **Identifying Latin incipit:** *Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine*
- **Rights disposition:** `rights-withheld` (`edition.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-tertia-2002`). Protected modern Latin and ICEL English bodies are omitted.
- **Verified bounded comparison against historical antecedent:** `passage.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-tertia-2002.english-completion-20261029-postconciliar-phase3-temporal-advent-1-main-prayer-over-the-offerings-all-1-comparison` (secondary 2002 digital PDF pp. 82–83, verified `2026-10-29`) directly compared the complete prayer against `passage.catholic-church.sacramentarium-veronense.feltoe-1896.english-completion-20261029-postconciliar-phase3-temporal-advent-1-main-prayer-over-the-offerings-all-1-source` (*Sacramentarium Veronense* XXVIIII, *Item alia*, marginal 372, Feltoe 1896, printed p. 74 / artifact PDF p. 98, SHA-256 `3b991b133889bbd4803831455bb4f49de30ecb4d8386534cee1f80f79541967a`):
  > *Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine, munera quae de tuis offerimus collata beneficiis: et quod nostrae devotioni concedis effici temporalis, tuae nobis fiat praemium redemptionis aeternae. per.*
  The verified comparison establishes that the entire ancient petition agrees with the secondary 2002 reproduction except for one inflectional change (ancient *temporalis* vs. secondary-2002 *temporali*), editorial punctuation/capitalization, and the short ending cue (`per.` vs. `Per Christum.`). Printed-2002 and printed-2008 altar-book correspondence remain unverified.
- **Offertory chant rubric (GIRM 74):** The Offertory chant accompanies the procession with the gifts under the same norms as the Entrance chant (GIRM 48), and singing may always accompany the rite even without a procession. No proper Offertory antiphon is printed in the Missal itself; this remains a distinct chant-layer option.

### 2.4 Seasonal Preface (`preface`) and Eucharistic Prayer Compatibility
- **Verified structural scope and offline boundary:** `passage.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-tertia-2002.ordo-missae-praefationes` (artifact PDF pp. 311–346) verifies that the *Ordo Missae, Praefationes* section contains **two Prefaces of Advent** (*"two of Advent"*), and `GIRM 364` states that the Missal's many Prefaces bring out more fully the motives for thanksgiving and the facets of the mystery of salvation. Because the restricted 2002/2008 Missal PDF and U.S. eMissal were not reopened offline, the exact Latin title, specific Advent calendar date-range rubric distinguishing the two Advent Prefaces, and modern Latin/ICEL English wording of the Advent Preface appointed for *Dominica I Adventus* were **not** directly inspected and remain an explicit fail-closed offline boundary.
- **Rights disposition:** Protected modern Latin and ICEL 2011 English Preface texts are omitted (`rights-limited`) and are neither reproduced nor paraphrased. Theological discussion of Christ's twofold Advent in the study rests transparently on directly verified patristic witnesses (St. Cyril of Jerusalem, *Catechesis 15* §§1–2, 4, 26; St. Augustine, *Enarratio in Psalmum 24* §10 and *De gratia et libero arbitrio* ch. 45; St. Bernard of Clairvaux, *Sermones in Adventu* 3.4 & 5.1), never on recalled modern Preface wording.
- **Eucharistic Prayer compatibility (GIRM 79, 365a–d; *Ordo Missae*):**
  - **Permitted:** Eucharistic Prayer I (Roman Canon, GIRM 365a — always available and especially suitable on Sundays; `ordo-missae-prex-eucharistica-i` confirms proper *Communicantes* only for Christmas, Epiphany, Easter, Ascension, and Pentecost, and paschal *Hanc igitur*, so no proper Advent insert occurs in the Roman Canon), Eucharistic Prayer II (GIRM 365b — permitted with qualifying other Prefaces), and Eucharistic Prayer III (GIRM 365c — used with a Preface and preferred on Sundays and festive days).
  - **Omitted / excluded:** Eucharistic Prayer IV (GIRM 365d), because it has an invariable Preface and is available only when a Mass has no Preface of its own and on Sundays in Ordinary Time.
  - **Fail-closed boundary (Appendix options):** The Eucharistic Prayers for Reconciliation and the Eucharistic Prayers for Masses for Various Needs and Occasions were not directly collated from the 2008 Missal Appendix in this checkout; their seasonal compatibility on a Sunday of Advent is left explicitly unresolved rather than asserted.

### 2.7 Concluding Blessing (`concluding-blessing`)
- **Verified structural scope (GIRM 90; *Ordo Missae*; 2008 *Notitiae*):** `GIRM 90` establishes the elements of the Concluding Rites: brief announcements if necessary; the Priest's greeting and blessing, *"on certain days and occasions enriched by a solemn blessing or prayer over the people"*; the dismissal of the people by the Deacon or Priest; and veneration of the altar. `passage.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-tertia-2002.ordo-missae-ritus-communionis-et-conclusionis` (`Ordo Missae nn. 124–146`, PDF pp. 366–370) confirms that the blessing precedes the dismissal *Ite, missa est*. `passage.holy-see.variationes-et-additiones-missalis-romani-2008.latin-notitiae-503-504.ordo-missae` verifies two 2008 Order-of-Mass updates: at `n. 144` (Missal p. 605), three alternative dismissal formulas are added after *Ite, missa est*, and at Missal p. 616, the solemn-blessing rubric reads `dicit invitationem: Inclinate vos ad benedictionem.` (also cataloged in `src/sources/inventories/postconciliar-ordo-missae-v1.toml`, lines 2150–2170).

## 3. Seasonal Ordinary-of-Mass Rubrics for *Dominica I Adventus*

1. **Omission of the *Gloria*:** The *Gloria in excelsis* is sung or said on Sundays *outside Advent and Lent* and is therefore **omitted** on all Sundays of Advent (verified in both Latin IGMR 53 and U.S. English GIRM 53).
2. **Profession of Faith (*Credo*):** Sung or said by the Priest with the people on Sundays and Solemnities (verified in Latin IGMR 68 and U.S. English GIRM 68). The *Ordo Missae* (`passage.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-tertia-2002.ordo-missae-ritus-initiales-et-liturgia-verbi`) permits the Apostles' Creed in place of the Nicene Creed. (Any seasonal rubric recommending the Apostles' Creed for particular seasons is not recorded in `68.toml` and is not claimed from GIRM 68.)
3. **Vestment color and altar/music moderation (offline boundary):** GIRM 346 (liturgical color) and GIRM 305/313 (floral and instrumental moderation in Advent) are not extracted as tracked passages in `src/sources/` and remain outside this offline verification boundary.
4. **Precedence:** Sundays of Advent hold place 2 in Part I of the Table of Liturgical Days (NUALC 59); no memorial, feast, or solemnity supersedes this formulary, and no commemoration is admitted (NUALC 60).
