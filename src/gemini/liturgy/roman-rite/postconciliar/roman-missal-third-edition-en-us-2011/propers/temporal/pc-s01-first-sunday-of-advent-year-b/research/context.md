   - Under NUALC n. 40, Advent begins with Evening Prayer I of the Sunday falling on or closest to 30 November; in 2026, 30 November is Monday, so Sunday, **29 November 2026** is the First Sunday of Advent.

### Seasonal vs. Ordinary Time Structural Note

Because Advent is a strong liturgical season rather than Ordinary Time, the Ordinary Time semi-continuous-readings premise does **not** apply (`proper-study/contract.md`; English OLM *Praenotanda* n. 74 reserves Isaiah to Advent). Nevertheless, the Mass formulary remains a **liturgical compilation** (`guidance/liturgy/propers-three-documents.md`, "The formulary is a compilation"): the shared Missal orations and antiphons serve all three cycles (A, B, and C) and were not composed for Year B's readings specifically.
