\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & Israel laments wandering, stained deeds, and God's hidden face before appealing to him as Father and Potter (Isa.~63--64), while the psalmist asks the Shepherd to visit his ruined vineyard (Ps.~80[79]); Paul thanks God for enriching a quarrelsome Corinthian church (1~Cor.~1:3--9); Jesus on the Mount of Olives charges his disciples to keep watch for an unannounced return (Mark~13:33--37). \\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & The Fathers read these texts of Christ and the Church across both comings: God's descent (Jerome on Isa.~64:3b~Vg., beside Aquinas's literal Exodus and Arnon exposition), the vineyard perfected on the Son of man (Augustine and Bellarmine on Ps.~80[79]:15--18), the earth bearing Christ from the Virgin (Bellarmine on Ps.~85[84]:13), and the householder ascending bodily while leaving pastors and faithful endowed with the Spirit's gifts (Bede on Mark~13:34, drawing on Gregory, Hom.~9; Chrysostom on 1~Cor.~1:7). \\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Because divine patience can feel like absence (Jerome on Isa.~63:17) and righteous deeds are soiled by mortal sin, negligence, perverse intention, or pride (Aquinas on Isa.~64:6~Vg.), believers resist torpor through humble confession, pliability under grace (Chrysostom and Lapide on 1~Cor.~1:9; Augustine on Ps.~85[84]:13), watchful prayer, practicing faith in deeds (Gregory, Hom.~13, via Bede), and Advent temperance and almsgiving (Leo, \work{Sermon}~19). \\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & Present waiting moves toward the \emph{apokalupsis} (``unveiling'') of an already-present Lord (Chrysostom on 1~Cor.~1:7), when Christ appears openly in glory and judgment (Cyril of Jerusalem, \work{Catechesis}~15; Augustine on Ps.~25[24]:10) and each believer meets the Judge in the state fixed at personal death (Augustine, \work{Ep.}~199 §3, via Aquinas's \work{Catena Aurea}), confirmed blameless into the Son's fellowship (1~Cor.~1:8--9). \\
\end{conciseoverview}
