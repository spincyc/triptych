\section{Appendix: Liturgical Resolution}

\subsection{Celebration, Editions, and Occurrence}
The First Sunday of Advent, Year~B (\texttt{PC-S01-B}, U.S.\ Lectionary No.~2; \work{Ordo lectionum Missae} n.~2), opens Liturgical Year~2027 in the Dioceses of the United States of America on Sunday, November~29, 2026 (Sunday Cycle~B, Weekday Cycle~I), verified from the front-matter cycle tables of the USCCB \work{Liturgical Calendar} for 2026 and 2027 and the \work{Ordo lectionum Missae} \work{Praenotanda} (nn.~65--66, 69). Governing liturgical editions are the \work{Missale Romanum}, \work{editio typica tertia} (2002) and \work{reimpressio emendata} (2008), \work{The Roman Missal}, Third Typical Edition (U.S.\ English, 2011, with the February 2021 USCCB Collect conclusion emendation), the \work{Ordo lectionum Missae}, \work{editio typica altera} (1981), and the \work{Lectionary for Mass for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America}, Second Typical Edition (1998/2002).

\subsection{Rank, Precedence, and Shared Missal Ownership}
Under the \work{Normae universales de anno liturgico et de calendario} (NUALC~59), Sundays of Advent hold place~2 in Part~I of the Table of Liturgical Days, outranking every solemnity on the General Roman Calendar; any competing solemnity transfers to the following Monday (NUALC~60), and no commemoration is admitted. The seven Missal-level parts of \work{Dominica I Adventus} (Entrance Antiphon, Collect, Prayer over the Offerings, Seasonal Preface, Communion Antiphon, Prayer after Communion, and Concluding Blessing) are shared identically across Years~A, B, and~C and are owned canonically by the shared First Sunday of Advent Missal record (\texttt{PC-S01}); the five Year~B Lectionary parts (First Reading, Responsorial Psalm, Second Reading, Gospel Acclamation, and Gospel) belong specifically to Year~B. The \work{Gloria in excelsis} is omitted (GIRM~53 / IGMR~53), no Sequence applies (GIRM~64), and the Profession of Faith (\work{Credo}) is required (GIRM~68 / IGMR~68). Liturgical vestment color (GIRM~346) and Advent floral or instrumental moderation (GIRM~305, 313) were not extracted in the held source passages and remain unverified offline.
