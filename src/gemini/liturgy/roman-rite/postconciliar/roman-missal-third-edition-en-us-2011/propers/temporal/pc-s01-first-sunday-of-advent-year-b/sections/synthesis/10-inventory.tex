\begin{properinventory}{Textual unit}{Text / citation}
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Introductory Rites}} \\
Entrance Antiphon & \emph{Ad te levavi} (Ps.~25[24]:1--3); four U.S.\ sung options (GIRM~48), or Missal antiphon when unsung. \work{Gloria in excelsis} omitted on Advent Sundays (GIRM~53). \\
Collect & \emph{Da, quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} (shared across Years~A, B, and~C; modern text withheld). \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Liturgy of the Word}} \\
First Reading & Isa.~63:16b--17, 19b; 64:2b--7 (Vg.~63:16b--17; 64:1, 3b--8; no shorter form). \\
Responsorial Psalm & Ps.~80(79):2ac, 3b, 15--16, 18--19; R.~v.~4 (or U.S.\ Advent Common Ps.~25[24] or Ps.~85[84] when sung, Lectionary No.~174; GIRM~61). \\
Second Reading & 1~Cor.~1:3--9 (continuous; no shorter form). \\
Gospel Acclamation & \emph{Alleluia} with \emph{Ostende nobis, Domine} (Ps.~85[84]:8; GIRM~62; no Sequence, GIRM~64). \\
Gospel & Mark~13:33--37 (continuous; omits 13:32; Nicene Creed required, or Apostles' Creed permitted, GIRM~68). \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Liturgy of the Eucharist}} \\
Prayer over the Offerings & \emph{Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine} (shared across Years~A, B, and~C; modern text withheld; Offertory chant locally selected, GIRM~74). \\
Seasonal Preface & Advent Preface (two Advent Prefaces in the Order of Mass [\work{Ordo Missae}]; paired with Eucharistic Prayer~I [no proper insert], II, or~III; IV excluded, GIRM~364--365; modern text withheld). \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textit{Communion and Concluding Rites}} \\
Communion Antiphon & \emph{Dominus dabit benignitatem} (Ps.~85[84]:13); four U.S.\ sung options (GIRM~87), or Missal antiphon when unsung. \\
Prayer after Communion & \emph{Prosint nobis} (shared across Years~A, B, and~C; modern text withheld). \\
Concluding Blessing & Ordinary blessing, or optional solemn blessing or prayer over the people before dismissal (GIRM~90; modern solemn-blessing texts withheld). \\
