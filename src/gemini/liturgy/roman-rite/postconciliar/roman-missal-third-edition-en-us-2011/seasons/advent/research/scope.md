## Scope Boundaries and Independence

- **Independent Gemini publication:** Authored from primary sources tracked in `src/sources/` and postconciliar calendar/lectionary data in `src/sources/calendars/postconciliar/`, without reusing prose, structure, or editorial claims from any other provider's edition.
- **Distinct from the 1962 Advent handbook:** Organized around the postconciliar two-phase structure (First Sunday through Dec 16 vs. Dec 17–24 privileged ferias), three-year Sunday Lectionary (Cycles A, B, C), 17 week-keyed + 8 date-keyed weekday Mass courses, postconciliar *Liturgy of the Hours*, removal of universal Ember Days, and United States Marian celebrations (Dec 8 Immaculate Conception and Dec 12 Our Lady of Guadalupe).
- **Copyright compliance (`guidance/liturgical-text-publication-policy.md`):** Zero copyrighted ICEL 2010/2011 English Missal or Liturgy of the Hours prayers, antiphons, or hymns and zero copyrighted NABRE/USCCB Lectionary texts are reproduced. All verbatim English Scripture quotations come from the tracked public-domain Douay-Rheims Bible; all vocal prayers, hymns, antiphons, and proclamations printed in Appendix B come verbatim from tracked pre-1928 public-domain Catholic editions.
- **Not a parish ordo or diocesan decree:** Readers are directed to their diocese and parish for local feast ranks, Mass schedules, and local pastoral ordinances.
