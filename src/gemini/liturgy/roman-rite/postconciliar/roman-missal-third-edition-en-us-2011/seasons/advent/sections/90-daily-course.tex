\section{Evergreen Daily Course for Advent}\label{app:daily-course}

Every Sunday across Lectionary Years~A, B, and~C, every weekday of Weeks~I--III (Monday through Saturday in Weeks~I and~II; Monday through Friday in Week~III), every privileged weekday from December~17 through December~24, and the principal Marian and sanctoral days of Advent in the United States appear below. Each entry supplies the appointed postconciliar Scripture readings, a located patristic or spiritual reading, and a public-domain prayer, psalm, or canticle for home recitation (printed in full in Appendix~B or read directly from Scripture).

\subsection{How to Route Yourself in Any Year}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Find the Sunday cycle.} Take the civil year in which the liturgical year \textit{ends} (that is, the coming January's year) and divide by~3: remainder~1 is \textbf{Year~A} (e.g., Advent~2025, 2028, 2031), remainder~2 is \textbf{Year~B} (e.g., Advent~2026, 2029, 2032), and remainder~0 is \textbf{Year~C} (e.g., Advent~2024, 2027, 2030).
  \item \textbf{Follow Weeks~I--III through December~16.} On ordinary weekdays through December~16, use the Weeks~I--III weekday table below. Notice that Week~III has at most five weekdays (Monday--Friday); as soon as December~17 arrives, stop that table and move immediately to the December~17--24 table below (in years opening December~2 or~3, no Week~III weekday occurs at Mass).
  \item \textbf{Handle United States Marian overlaps.} On \textbf{December~8} (Immaculate Conception), use the Marian and sanctoral table below unless December~8 is the Second Sunday of Advent (as in 2024 and 2030), in which case keep the Second Sunday on December~8 and transfer the Solemnity to Monday, December~9. On \textbf{December~12} (Our Lady of Guadalupe), use the Marian and sanctoral table below unless December~12 is the Third Sunday of Advent (as in 2027 and 2032), in which case keep the Third Sunday and omit the Feast that year.
  \item \textbf{Follow December~17--24.} Use the December~17--24 table below by calendar date, stepping aside once for the Fourth Sunday of Advent (which always falls inside December~18--24). On the evening of December~24, Advent closes at Evening Prayer~I of Christmas.
\end{enumerate}
