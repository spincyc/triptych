## Controlling Theological Synthesis

Christ alone is the eternal Word, the New Covenant, and the definitive Presence of God in person. The Church calls Mary the living Ark of the New Covenant because the Son of God truly took flesh within her womb and was borne by her into the world. Her dignity is wholly created, graced, maternal, and subordinate to her Son's unique mediation (*Lumen gentium* 60--62; CCC 970). The four Marian dogmas unfold that single Christological truth in ordered sequence: Divine Motherhood confesses whom she bears; Perpetual Virginity confesses the Spirit-wrought, undivided consecration of her motherhood; the Immaculate Conception confesses Christ's prevenient redemptive preparation of his dwelling; and the Bodily Assumption confesses the incorruptible ark's sharing in her Son's Paschal victory.
