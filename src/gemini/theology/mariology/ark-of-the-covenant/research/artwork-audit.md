# Artwork and Cartographic Audit — Gemini Ark of the Covenant Monograph

This record audits the six monochrome graphite illustrations authored for `src/gemini/theology/mariology/ark-of-the-covenant/`, recording their technical properties, exact SHA-256 digests, scriptural bounds, visual inspection passes, and web-edition textual equivalents.

## Visual and Editorial Contract

1. **Medium and ground:** Pure 8-bit monochrome grayscale (`mode=L`) PNGs on pure flat white (`#FFFFFF`, corner pixels verified `(255, 255, 255, 255)`). No color, sepia, yellow tint, drop shadows, paper-grain rectangles, or baked-in bounding frames.
2. **Text separation:** Zero embedded text, letters, numerals, arrows, or labels inside any raster PNG. All typographic callouts, station names, legends, and cartographic routes are typeset live in TeX/TikZ overlays in the print edition.
3. **Evidentiary boundary:** Generated artwork supplies visual orientation and disciplined architectural imagination; it supplies no archaeological, historical, or doctrinal proof beyond what Sacred Scripture and historical witnesses warrant.
4. **Web-edition accessibility:** Every plate and TikZ overlay is enclosed in `\ifdefined\TriptychPrintEdition ... \else ... \fi` and paired with a complete, authored textual equivalent (structural description or full station itinerary table) so the reflowed web edition omits no substantive content.

## Coordinate Calibration and Post-Build Visual Verification

Every plate was checked against a temporary 0.1-unit normalized coordinate grid in `.scratch/lanes/lane-1/checks/` prior to authoring TikZ overlays, and every rendered PDF page containing a plate (as well as title, contents, and representative prose/table pages) is rendered via `pdftoppm -png -r 150` and inspected directly after `pdflatex` compilation to confirm zero overlapping labels, zero clipping, zero box seams, and legible print contrast.
