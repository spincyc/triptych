\section{References}

Works listed below are grouped by source function. Verbatim quotations in the body are drawn strictly from the tracked public-domain and licensed primary witnesses recorded in \texttt{research/source-audit.md} and \texttt{research/source-bindings.toml}; cataloged and copyright-restricted modern magisterial works are cited by official act, date, and paragraph without manufactured quotation.

\subsection{Primary Scriptural Witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Douay-Rheims Bible (Challoner revision).} English College of Douay / Bishop Richard Challoner (1749--1752), Project Gutenberg eBook 1581 verse-text artifacts and commentary notes, checked against the 1899 American Douay-Rheims edition (\texttt{engDRA}). Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Clementine Vulgate.} \textit{Biblia Sacra Vulgatae Editionis} (1598), eBible.org \texttt{latVUC} verse-text artifacts (Gallican Psalter). Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Brenton Septuagint.} Sir Lancelot Charles Lee Brenton, \textit{The Septuagint Version of the Old Testament, with an English Translation} (London, 1851), eBible.org \texttt{eng-Brenton} English verse-text artifacts. Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Swete Greek Old Testament.} Henry Barclay Swete, \textit{The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint}, First1KGreek digitisation (Psalms and 3--4 Kingdoms). Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  \item \textbf{SBL Greek New Testament (SBLGNT v1.2).} Michael W. Holmes, ed., Society of Biblical Literature / Faithlife snapshot (Luke and Revelation surface text). Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  \item \textbf{MorphGNT-SBLGNT (v6.12).} James Tauber, one-lemma-per-line projection across all 27 New Testament books (137,554 tokens). Licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  \item \textbf{Byzantine Textform Greek New Testament (RP2018).} Maurice A. Robinson and William G. Pierpont (2018), Unicode CSV files for Matthew, Mark, Luke, Acts, and Revelation. Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Masoretic-Base English Controls.} World English Bible, Catholic Edition (\texttt{eng-web-c}, 2020, public domain); Revised Version with Apocrypha (1895, US public domain); King James Version with Apocrypha (1769, US public domain).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Conciliar, Papal, and Magisterial Witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Second Council of Constantinople (A.D. 553), Canons VI and XIV} (reaffirming Ephesus 431 and Chalcedon 451). Tracked verbatim in Karl Joseph von Hefele, \textit{A History of the Councils of the Church}, Vol. 4, trans. W. R. Clark (Edinburgh: T. \& T. Clark, 1895). Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Lateran Council (A.D. 649), Canon 3.} Referenced historically under Pope St. Martin I (cataloged in Denzinger; no local verbatim text tracked).
  \item \textbf{Council of Trent (1546--1547), Session V (\S 6) and Session VI (Canon XXIII).} Tracked verbatim in \textit{Dogmatic Canons and Decrees} (New York: Devin-Adair, 1912). Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Pope Pius IX, \textit{Ineffabilis Deus} (8 December 1854).} Tracked verbatim in \textit{Pii IX Pontificis Maximi Acta}, Pars I, Vol. 1 (Rome, 1854), and in bilingual Latin--English in \textit{Official Documents Connected with the Definition of the Dogma of the Immaculate Conception} (Baltimore: John Murphy \& Co., 1855, with approbation of Abp. Francis Patrick Kenrick). Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Pope Leo XIII, \textit{Quamquam pluries} (15 August 1889).} Tracked verbatim in Latin from \textit{Acta Sanctae Sedis} 22 (1889--1890), pp. 65--69. Public domain.
  \item \textbf{Pope Pius XII, \textit{Munificentissimus Deus} (1 November 1950).} \textit{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 42 (1950), pp. 753--771, \S 44. Cataloged metadata record (restricted Vatican text; summarized without verbatim quotation).
  \item \textbf{Second Vatican Council.} Dogmatic Constitution on the Church \textit{Lumen gentium} (21 November 1964), ch. VIII (\S\S 52--69, esp. \S\S 53, 56, 60--62); Dogmatic Constitution on Divine Revelation \textit{Dei Verbum} (18 November 1965), \S\S 11--12. Cataloged metadata records (restricted Vatican text; summarized without verbatim quotation).
  \item \textbf{Catechism of the Catholic Church (1992/1997).} \S\S 65--67, 499, 963--975 (esp. \S 970). Cataloged metadata records (restricted LEV/USCCB text; summarized without verbatim quotation).
  \item \textbf{Recent Papal and Dicasterial Acts (Cataloged Metadata Records).} Pope Paul VI, \textit{Marialis Cultus} (2 February 1974); Pope St. John Paul II, \textit{Redemptoris Custos} (15 August 1989); Pope Francis, \textit{Patris Corde} (8 December 2020); Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, \textit{Mater Populi Fidelis: Doctrinal Note on Some Marian Titles Regarding Mary's Cooperation in the Work of Salvation} (approved by Pope Leo XIV on 7 October 2025; published 4 November 2025).
\end{itemize}

Public domain.
