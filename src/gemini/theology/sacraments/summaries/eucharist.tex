\sacramentSummaryStart{Eucharist}{Third Sacrament of Christian Initiation --- Source and Summit}{Unbloody sacrifice of Calvary and Christ's Body and Blood under bread and wine}

\begin{summaryidentity}
Definition & Sacrament and sacrifice containing Christ---Body, Blood, soul, divinity---truly, really, substantially under bread and wine (Trent~XIII; \emph{ST}~III, q.~65).\\
Institution & Instituted at Last Supper (Matt~26:26--28; Luke~22:19--20; 1~Cor~11:23--26), fulfilling John~6:48--59 DR [6:48--58 Gk].\\
Sacramental structure & \latin{Sacramentum tantum}: species. \latin{Res et sacramentum}: Christ's Body and Blood substantially present. \latin{Res tantum}: Mystical Body unity, charity, glory.\\
Order of ends & \textbf{Primary:} nourishment and sacrificial communion. \textbf{Secondary:} ecclesial unity, worship, propitiation. \textbf{Contingent:} Viaticum, zeal. \textbf{Ultimate:} eternal banquet.\\
\end{summaryidentity}

\begin{summaryconstitution}
Matter & Wheaten bread, grape wine, drop of water (\emph{ST}~III, q.~74; Florence). & Latin/Armenian/Maronite: unleavened (CIC~926). East: leavened (CCEO~707). & Non-wheat/non-grape invalid; both wheat types valid.\\
Form & Christ's words \latin{in persona Christi} in Eucharistic Prayer (\emph{ST}~III, q.~78). & Latin highlights institution words; East honors unified Anaphora with epiclesis. & Words are instrumental form, distinct from conversion.\\
Minister & Validly ordained bishop or priest alone consecrates (Lateran~IV; Trent~XXII). & Consecrator: bishop/priest (CIC~900; CCEO~699). Distribution: clergy. & Deacons/laity never consecrate; extraordinary ministers assist.\\
Subject & Validly baptized person not barred by law (CIC~912). & Adults need grace (c.~916) and fast; Latin discretion, East infants (CCEO~710). & Mortal sin needs Confession; whole Christ under either kind.\\
Change effected & \textbf{Substantial conversion} (\emph{transubstantiation}): bread/wine substance becomes Body/Blood. & Accidents (\latin{species}) remain unaltered without former substance (\emph{ST}~III, qq.~75--77). & Unique among seven; no character; repeatable; abides intact.\\
\end{summaryconstitution}

\begin{summaryeconomy}
Salvation history & Gen~14:18; Exod~12, 24:8; Mal~1:11. & Last Supper; Calvary re-presented unbloodily; same Victim/Priest. & Summit of sacraments (\emph{ST}~III, q.~73); one Body. & Resurrection pledge (John~6:55 DR [6:54 Gk]).\\
Ecclesial order & Clean oblation (Irenaeus~IV.18). & Sacrifice and banquet one; propitiatory for living/dead (CCC~1382). & Honors Latin/Eastern anaphoras; grounds adoration. & Veils fall before the Lamb (Apoc~21:22).\\
\end{summaryeconomy}

\begin{summarywitnesses}
Scripture & Gen~14:18; Exod~12; Mal~1:11; Matt~26:26--28; John~6:48--59 DR [48--58 Gk]; 1~Cor~10--11 & Real Flesh/Blood, institution, memorial, discerning Body.\\
Fathers & Ignatius; Justin~66; Irenaeus~IV.18; Cyril~IV--V; Ambrose~IX; Chrysostom; Augustine; Damascus & Medicine of immortality, pure sacrifice, nature changed.\\
Scholastics & Aquinas (\emph{ST}~III, qq.~73--83) & Transubstantiation, accidents on quantity, \latin{ex vi verborum}/concomitance.\\
Magisterium & Lateran~IV; Florence; Trent~XIII, XXI--XXII; \emph{SC}~47--48; \emph{Mysterium Fidei}; \emph{Ecclesia de Euch.} & Substantial presence, transubstantiation, Calvary identity.\\
Canon law & CIC~1983, cc.~897--958; CCEO~1990, cc.~698--717 & Priest consecrator, matter, grace, fast, adoration.\\
\end{summarywitnesses}

\begin{studybox}{Distinctions to preserve}
\textbf{Transubstantiation vs.\ words/accidents:} conversion of the \emph{entire substance} of bread and wine into Christ's Body and Blood, distinct from consecratory words (instrumental form, not ``transignification'') and unaltered accidents (\emph{ST}~III, qq.~75--77; \emph{Mysterium Fidei}). \textbf{\latin{Ex vi verborum} vs.\ concomitance:} Body under bread, Blood under wine by words; whole Christ under each kind (CCC~1390). \textbf{Re-presentation vs.\ repetition:} unbloody Calvary (Trent~XXII).
\end{studybox}
