\sacramentSummaryStart{Baptism}{First Sacrament of Christian Initiation --- Gate of Life in Christ}{Spiritual rebirth from water and the Spirit into Christ's death and Resurrection}

\begin{summaryidentity}
Definition & Washing with natural water in the Triune Name freeing from sin, regenerating in grace, imprinting character, and uniting to the Church (\emph{ST}~III, q.~66).\\
Institution & Instituted by Christ: Jordan (Matt~3:13--17), Nicodemus (John~3:5), pierced side (John~19:34), Great Commission (Matt~28:18--20).\\
Sacramental structure & \latin{Sacramentum tantum}: water washing and Triune words. \latin{Res et sacramentum}: indelible character. \latin{Res tantum}: sin/punishment remitted, grace, adoption.\\
Order of ends & \textbf{Primary:} rebirth and purification. \textbf{Secondary:} membership, sacramental capacity, unity (\emph{UR}~22). \textbf{Contingent:} parish joy, convert witness. \textbf{Ultimate:} beatific glory.\\
\end{summaryidentity}

\begin{summaryconstitution}
Matter & Natural water applied by real washing flowing on skin (\emph{ST}~III, q.~66). & Blessed water; immersion, infusion, or flowing aspersion (CIC~854); triple immersion (CCEO~675). & Natural water valid in emergency; non-water invalid.\\
Form & Trinitarian invocation joined to washing (CCC~1240). & Latin: ``N., I baptize you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.'' East: passive form. & Altering Triune names or ``We baptize'' invalidates (CDF~2020).\\
Minister & Christ acts principally; Church's intention required. & Latin: bishop, priest, deacon (CIC~861~\S1). East: priest (CCEO~677~\S1). & In danger of death, anyone---even unbaptized---baptizes validly (Lateran~IV).\\
Subject & Living unbaptized person (CIC~864; CCEO~679). & Infants promptly in Church's faith (CIC~867); adults with faith/repentance. & Doubtful: conditional (CIC~869); insincerity blocks grace until penance.\\
Change effected & Accidental formal elevation by grace/character plus ecclesial bond. & Human substance and creaturehood remain intact (\emph{ST}~III, q.~69). & No substantial change; character forbids repetition (Trent~VII, can.~9).\\
\end{summaryconstitution}

\begin{summaryeconomy}
Salvation history & Gen~1:2; Ark (1~Pet~3:20); Red Sea (1~Cor~10:2); Ezech~36:25. & Jordan; pierced side (John~19:34); buried with Christ (Rom~6:3--5). & Gate to all other sacraments; common priesthood (\emph{LG}~10). & Pledge of life (Titus~3:7); character abides.\\
Ecclesial order & Replaces carnal ablutions with rebirth in Christ. & One share in Christ's death (Damascus~IV.9). & Latin: infant B, then E/C (adult B--C--E). East: unified B--C--E. & Ecumenical bond ordered to full unity (\emph{UR}~22).\\
\end{summaryeconomy}

\begin{summarywitnesses}
Scripture & Gen~1:2; Matt~28:19; John~3:5, 19:34; Acts~2:38; Rom~6:3--5; Eph~4:5; Titus~3:5 & Birth of water/Spirit, Triune mandate, burial in Christ, one Body.\\
Fathers & Justin~61; Cyril, \emph{Myst.}~II.4; Ambrose~III--IV; Augustine, \emph{In Ioann.}~Tr.~80.3, 120.2 & Illumination, grave and mother; word with water cleanses infants.\\
Scholastics & Aquinas (\emph{ST}~III, qq.~66--71); Lombard (IV, d.~3) & Water matter, Triune form, necessity, water/blood/desire, character.\\
Magisterium & Lateran~IV; Florence; Trent~V--VII; \emph{UR}~22; CCC~1213--1284; CDF~2020 & Emergency minister, infant baptism, character, singular ``I'' form.\\
Canon law & CIC~1983, cc.~849--878; CCEO~1990, cc.~675--691 & Ordinary ministers, emergency rule, infant promptness, sponsors.\\
\end{summarywitnesses}

\begin{studybox}{Distinctions to preserve}
\textbf{Character vs.\ grace:} valid Baptism imprints character even if insincerity blocks grace; penance revives grace. \textbf{Concupiscence vs.\ sin:} all sin and punishment are remitted; concupiscence remains for combat, not as sin (Trent~V, can.~5). \textbf{Necessity vs.\ God's freedom:} martyrdom (blood) and supernatural desire confer grace without character (\emph{ST}~III, q.~66, a.~11).
\end{studybox}
