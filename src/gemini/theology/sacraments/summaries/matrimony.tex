\sacramentSummaryStart{Matrimony}{Second Sacrament at the Service of Communion --- Spousal Covenant and Domestic Church}{Natural covenant elevated into a living sign of Christ and His Church}

\begin{summaryidentity}
Definition & Lifelong covenant of one baptized man and woman elevated by Christ to a sacrament (Trent~XXIV; c.~1055; CCEO~c.~776).\\
Institution & Inscribed in creation (Gen~1:27--28; 2:24), blessed at Cana (John~2:1--11), restored (Matt~19:4--9) as great mystery (Eph~5:25--32).\\
Sacramental structure & \latin{Sacramentum tantum}: manifested consent (East: with priestly blessing). \latin{Res et sacramentum}: perpetual \latin{vinculum}. \latin{Res tantum}: sanctifying/spousal grace.\\
Order of ends & \textbf{Primary:} spousal good and procreation/education of offspring. \textbf{Secondary:} mutual aid, remedy, domestic church. \textbf{Contingent:} hospitality, service. \textbf{Ultimate:} Lamb's Supper.\\
\end{summaryidentity}

\begin{summaryconstitution}
Matter & Mutual self-giving and acceptance of baptized man and woman. & Analogical; no physical element (irreplaceable consent, c.~1057). & Incapacity, simulation, or fear invalidates (cc.~1095, 1101, 1103).\\
Form & Legitimately manifested present marital consent. & Latin: official + two witnesses (c.~1108); East: priestly blessing (CCEO~c.~828). & Extraordinary form: two witnesses in death-danger/1-month absence (c.~1116).\\
Minister & Latin: spouses confer; East: priest/bishop blesses (CCC~1623). & Latin official receives vows; East confers sacred Crowning. & Only a priest validly assists an Eastern party (c.~1108~\S3).\\
Subject & Eligible baptized man and woman free of impediments. & Natural/divine impediments undispensable; equal rights (c.~1135). & Impotence invalidates; sterility does not (c.~1084).\\
Change effected & Permanent relational bond (\latin{vinculum}); \textbf{no character}. & \latin{Ratum et consummatum} dissolves only by death (c.~1141; 1~Cor~7:39). & Nullity declares no bond arose; privileges touch only non-consummated/natural unions.\\
\end{summaryconstitution}

\begin{summaryeconomy}
Salvation history & Edenic blessing (Gen~1--2; Tob~8; Mal~2). & Cana; images Christ and Church (Eph~5:25--32). & Builds domestic church (\emph{LG}~11) for the altar. & Anticipates Lamb's Supper (Apoc~19:7--9).\\
Ecclesial order & Contract--sacrament inseparable (\emph{Arcanum}). & Christ's fidelity grounds bond; separation only for grave cause. & East--West ministerial complementarity (\emph{De concordia}). & Earthly sign yields to heaven; virginity stands higher (Trent~XXIV, can.~10).\\
\end{summaryeconomy}

\begin{summarywitnesses}
Scripture & Gen~1--2; Tob~8; Matt~19; John~2; 1~Cor~7; Eph~5; Apoc~19 & Lifelong monogamy elevated into a great mystery of Christ and Church.\\
Fathers & Augustine (\emph{Civ.~Dei}~XIV--XV; \emph{Gen.\ ad litt.}~IX.7); Chrysostom (\emph{Eph.}~XX) & Three goods (\latin{fides, proles, sacramentum}); Edenic blessing; domestic church.\\
Scholastics & Aquinas (\emph{ST}~III, q.~65; \emph{Suppl.}~41--68); Lombard (IV, dd.~26--42) & Consent as efficient cause; natural office, social bond, and sacrament.\\
Magisterium & Florence~(\S16); Trent~XXIV; \emph{Arcanum}; \emph{Casti Connubii}; \emph{GS}~47--52; \emph{FC} & Grace, indissolubility, inseparability, openness to life, virginity's excellence.\\
Canon law & CIC~cc.~1055--1165; CCEO~cc.~776--866; \emph{Mitis Iudex}; \emph{De concordia} & Ends, consent, form, \latin{ratum et consummatum}, Pauline/Petrine privileges.\\
\end{summarywitnesses}

\begin{studybox}{Distinctions to preserve}
\textbf{Minister:} valid marriage of baptized persons is always a sacrament; Latin spouses minister, Eastern priests bless (CCC~1623). \textbf{Bond:} \latin{ratum et consummatum} ends only by death; nullity declares no bond arose; privileges never touch it. \textbf{Ends:} spousal good and offspring co-inhere; virginity stands higher (Trent~XXIV, can.~10).
\end{studybox}
