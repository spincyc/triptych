\sacramentSummaryStart{Holy Orders}{First Sacrament at the Service of Communion --- Apostolic Hierarchy and Ministry}{Continuation of Christ's eternal priesthood and diaconal service}

\begin{summaryidentity}
Definition & Sacrament continuing Christ's apostolic mission in the episcopate, presbyterate, and diaconate (CCC~1536; c.~1008; CCEO~c.~743).\\
Institution & Instituted at Last Supper (Luke~22:19) and Easter (John~20:21--23); conferred by apostolic hand-imposition (Acts~6:6; 14:22~[Gk~14:23]; 1--2~Tim).\\
Sacramental structure & \latin{Sacramentum tantum}: hand-imposition and prayer. \latin{Res et sacramentum}: indelible character by degree. \latin{Res tantum}: sanctifying grace and grace of office.\\
Order of ends & \textbf{Primary:} Eucharist, sacraments, Word, governance, \latin{diakonia}. \textbf{Secondary:} minister's holiness, unity. \textbf{Contingent:} pastoral structures. \textbf{Ultimate:} Body's glory.\\
\end{summaryidentity}

\begin{summaryconstitution}
Matter & Silent imposition of hands on head (\emph{Sacramentum Ordinis}). & Bishop imposes hands in all degrees; anointing/vessels explanatory. & Omitting imposition invalidates; explanatory rites do not.\\
Form & Essential consecratory prayer for that degree. & Approved Latin (\emph{Pontificalis Romani}) and Eastern (\latin{cheirotonia}) prayers. & Must signify Order and power (\emph{Apostolicae Curae}).\\
Minister & Validly consecrated bishop alone (Trent~XXIII, can.~7). & Bishop ordains all degrees; two co-consecrators for bishops. & Consecration without mandate is valid yet incurs excommunication (c.~1387).\\
Subject & Validly baptized male alone (c.~1024; CCEO~c.~754). & Freely called, tested, formed, free of impediments. & Male subject is divine law (\emph{Ordinatio Sacerdotalis}); celibacy varies.\\
Change effected & Accidental formal elevation imprinting indelible character (\emph{ST}~III, q.~63, a.~5). & Priestly \latin{in persona Christi Capitis}; diaconal \latin{diakonia} (c.~1009~\S3). & Never repeated; character endures despite loss of clerical state (c.~290).\\
\end{summaryconstitution}

\begin{summaryeconomy}
Salvation history & Fulfills Aaron (Exod~28--29) and Melchisedech (Gen~14:18). & Shares Christ's eternal priesthood (Heb~5, 7) and service. & Ordered to the Eucharist and Body (\emph{ST}~III, q.~65). & Character abides in heaven (Apoc~5:9--10).\\
Ecclesial order & Threefold hierarchy constitutive of Church (Ignatius, \emph{Trall.}~3). & Common vs.\ ministerial priesthood differ in essence (\emph{LG}~10). & Celibate in Latin, Syro-Malabar/Malankara, Alexandrian Churches; married East priests. & Bishops celibate; no marriage after Orders (c.~1087).\\
\end{summaryeconomy}

\begin{summarywitnesses}
Scripture & Heb~5, 7; Luke~22:19; John~20:21--23; Acts~6, 14; 1--2~Tim & Imposition of hands and prayer confer abiding ministerial grace.\\
Fathers & Ignatius (\emph{Trall.}~2--3; \emph{Smyrn.}~8); Chrysostom (\emph{De sac.}; \emph{1--2~Tim}); Dionysius & Threefold hierarchy constitutive of Church; episcopal fullness.\\
Scholastics & Aquinas (\emph{ST}~III, qq.~63, 65; \emph{Suppl.}~34--40); Lombard (IV, d.~24) & Indelible priestly character; every degree ordered to the Eucharist.\\
Magisterium & Florence~(\S15); Trent~XXIII; \emph{Apostolicae Curae}; \emph{Sacr.\ Ordinis}; \emph{LG}~10, 21, 29; \emph{Ord.\ Sac.}; \emph{Omnium in Mentem} & Sole essential rite; male subject; priestly vs.\ diaconal acts; lay ministries distinct.\\
Canon law & CIC~cc.~230, 277, 290, 1008--1054; CCEO~cc.~180, 373, 743--775; \emph{Pont.\ Praec.} & Latin and Eastern validity, celibacy, and diaspora married clergy.\\
\end{summarywitnesses}

\begin{studybox}{Distinctions to preserve}
\textbf{Priesthood vs.\ diaconate:} common and ministerial differ in essence (\emph{LG}~10); bishops and presbyters act \latin{in persona Christi Capitis}, deacons in non-priestly \latin{diakonia} (c.~1009~\S3). \textbf{Character:} valid ordination never becomes invalid (c.~290). \textbf{Discipline:} male subject is divine law; celibacy is ecclesiastical law.
\end{studybox}
