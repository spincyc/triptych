\sacramentSummaryStart{Confirmation}{Second Sacrament of Christian Initiation --- Seal of the Holy Spirit}{Pentecostal strengthening by consecrated chrism and hand-imposition for witness}

\begin{summaryidentity}
Definition & Sacrament (Eastern Chrismation) giving the baptized a special outpouring of the Spirit, character, deeper ecclesial bond, and witness (\emph{LG}~11; \emph{ST}~III, q.~72).\\
Institution & Instituted by Christ as Pentecost's continuation (John~7:37--39; Acts~1:8), given by apostolic hand-imposition after Baptism (Acts~8:14--17; 19:6).\\
Sacramental structure & \latin{Sacramentum tantum}: chrism, hand-imposition, words. \latin{Res et sacramentum}: confirmation character. \latin{Res tantum}: Spirit's outpouring, grace, sevenfold gifts.\\
Order of ends & \textbf{Primary:} spiritual maturity and witness. \textbf{Secondary:} closer ecclesial/episcopal bond. \textbf{Contingent:} vow renewal, catechesis. \textbf{Ultimate:} glory in the Spirit.\\
\end{summaryidentity}

\begin{summaryconstitution}
Matter & Chrism / \latin{myron} (oil, balsam) blessed by bishop/Patriarch, with hand-imposition. & Latin: forehead with hand-imposition (CIC~880). East: forehead/senses (CCEO~692). & Unconsecrated oil invalid; Latin joins chrism and hand.\\
Form & Sealing words naming the Gift of the Spirit (cf.~CCC~1300). & Latin: ``Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit.'' East: ``The seal of the gift of the Holy Spirit'' (Vatican web: ``gift that is the Holy Spirit''). & 1971 Latin form keeps East--West continuity.\\
Minister & Apostolic episcopal authority seals the baptized (\emph{ST}~III, q.~72, a.~11). & Latin: bishop (CIC~882). East: bishop/presbyter with episcopal \latin{myron} (\emph{OE}~13--14). & Latin presbyter for adults/danger of death (c.~883) or delegation (c.~884).\\
Subject & Validly baptized, unconfirmed living person (CIC~889; CCEO~692). & Latin: discretion/conference age, grace (c.~891); adults with Baptism. East: with Baptism. & Unbaptized invalid; Latin infants in danger of death (c.~883).\\
Change effected & Accidental formal strengthening of soul plus deeper ecclesial bond. & Increases grace, sevenfold gifts (Is~11:2--3), imprints character (\emph{ST}~III, q.~72). & No substantial change; never repeated (Trent~VII, can.~9).\\
\end{summaryconstitution}

\begin{summaryeconomy}
Salvation history & Prophetic anointing; sevenfold Spirit (Is~11:1--3, 61:1); Joel~2:28. & Jordan (Luke~4:18); Pentecost (Acts~2). & Perfects baptismal life toward Eucharist. & Seal and pledge of glory (2~Cor~1:22; Eph~1:14).\\
Ecclesial order & Extends messianic unction to all in Christ. & Apostolic hand-imposition (Acts~8, 19). & West keeps episcopal bond via bishop; East via \latin{myron} (\emph{OE}~13--14). & Indelible character abides in glory (\emph{ST}~III, q.~63).\\
\end{summaryeconomy}

\begin{summarywitnesses}
Scripture & Is~11:1--3, 61:1; Joel~2:28; Acts~1:8, 8:14--17, 19:6; 2~Cor~1:21--22; Eph~1:13--14; Heb~6:2 & Sevenfold Spirit (fear of Lord in Is~11:3), hand-imposition, seal.\\
Fathers & Cyril (or John~II), \emph{Myst.}~III.1, 3--5; Ambrose, \emph{De myst.}~VII.42 & Chrism verifies ``Christian'' and imparts the sevenfold seal.\\
Scholastics & Aquinas (\emph{ST}~III, q.~72) & Maturity, oil/balsam, combat character, bishop minister.\\
Magisterium & Florence; Trent~VII; \emph{LG}~11; \emph{OE}~12--14; \emph{Divinae Consortium Naturae}; CCC~1285--1321 & True sacrament, 1971 matter/form, Eastern Chrismation.\\
Canon law & CIC~1983, cc.~879--896; CCEO~1990, cc.~692--697 & Latin bishop minister/faculties; Eastern conjoint Chrismation.\\
\end{summarywitnesses}

\begin{studybox}{Distinctions to preserve}
\textbf{Joined to Baptism:} Baptism already gives the Spirit; Confirmation brings newborn life to maturity for witness (\emph{ST}~III, q.~72, a.~1). \textbf{Latin vs.\ East:} both keep episcopal unity---West via bishop, East via episcopal \latin{myron} (\emph{OE}~13--14). \textbf{Seal vs.\ graduation:} divine sealing, not coming of age; valid in infancy.
\end{studybox}
