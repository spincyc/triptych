\textbf{Governing law and census.} The twenty-four Churches \latin{sui iuris} comprise the Latin Church (CIC~1983, c.~1) and twenty-three Eastern Catholic Churches across the five traditions of CCEO~c.~28~\S2 (six Patriarchal, four Major Archiepiscopal, five Metropolitan \latin{sui iuris}, and eight Other Churches \latin{sui iuris}). \emph{Orientalium Ecclesiarum} 12--14 confirms ancient Eastern discipline and valid presbyteral Chrismation with patriarchal or episcopal myron. CCEO~cc.~694--697 and~710 mandate conjoint Baptism and Chrismation outside necessity and direct Eucharistic admission as soon as possible according to each Church's liturgical books and particular law; the 1996 Eastern Liturgical \emph{Instruction} (\S\S42--51) urges recovery of ancestral B--C--E unity. Adult Latin initiation follows B--C--E (CIC~866); valid Baptism is never repeated. Particular synodal and eparchial law governs current local timetables.}
