\textbf{Sacrament of the New Law.} A sacrament is an efficacious sensible sign of invisible grace, instituted by Jesus Christ and entrusted to the Church, by which divine life is dispensed to us. Each rite commemorates Christ's Passion, causes and manifests present sanctifying grace in a capable and unobstructed recipient, and pledges future glory (Augustine, \emph{De civitate Dei}~X.5; \emph{In Ioann. Evang.}~Tr.~80.3; \emph{ST}~III, q.~60, aa.~2--3; q.~62, aa.~1, 5; Trent, Sess.~VII; CCC~1131).
\end{minipage}}

Baptism\newline\emph{Initiation} & Natural water applied by real washing (immersion, infusion, or flowing aspersion) & Latin: ``N., I baptize you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.'' East: passive Trinitarian formula (CCC~1240) & Any living unbaptized person; adults with faith, repentance, intention & Latin: bishop, priest, deacon. East: priest ordinarily. Confirmation / Chrismation\newline\emph{Initiation} & Anointing on forehead (East: principal senses) with episcopally consecrated chrism / myron, with imposition of hand & Latin: ``Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit.'' East: ``The seal of the gift of the Holy Spirit'' (cf.\ CCC~1300; Vatican web: ``gift that is the Holy Spirit'') & Any validly baptized, unconfirmed person & Latin: bishop (presbyter by law or delegation). East: presbyter ordinarily with patriarchal / episcopal myron (\emph{OE}~13--14) & Confirmation character empowering public witness; accidental strengthening of baptized soul in the Spirit & Special outpouring of the Holy Spirit as at Pentecost, increase of grace, sevenfold gifts, apostolic courage & \textbf{Primary:} spiritual maturity and witness. \textbf{Secondary:} deeper ecclesial bond. \textbf{Contingent:} vow renewal. \textbf{Ultimate:} glory in the Spirit & Indelible character; never repeated\\

Eucharist\newline\emph{Initiation} & Wheaten bread (unleavened in Latin, Armenian, and Maronite rites; leavened in Byzantine and other Eastern rites) and natural grape wine (with a drop of water) & Christ's words of institution spoken \latin{in persona Christi} within the Eucharistic Prayer (East invokes Spirit in epiclesis within unified anaphora) & Validly baptized person in state of grace (age of discretion in Latin rite; infants in unified Eastern rites) & Validly ordained bishop or priest alone consecrates; deacon and mandated extraordinary ministers may distribute & True Body and Blood of Christ under the species; \textbf{substantial change} (\emph{transubstantiation}), distinct from words and leaving accidents unaltered & Union with Christ, increase of charity, unity of the Mystical Body, forgiveness of venial sin, preservation from mortal sin, pledge of resurrection & \textbf{Primary:} nourishment and sacrificial communion. \textbf{Secondary:} unity, worship, propitiation. \textbf{Contingent:} Viaticum, zeal. \textbf{Ultimate:} eternal banquet & No character; repeatable daily (consecrated species abide while intact)\\

\addlinespace[0.20em]

Holy Orders\newline\emph{Communion} & Consecrating bishop's silent imposition of hands upon the ordinand's head (\emph{Sacramentum Ordinis}) & Essential prayer of consecration specifying episcopate, presbyterate, or diaconate and invoking the Holy Spirit & Validly baptized male (CIC~1024; CCEO~754), freely called and suitably tested & Validly consecrated bishop alone; law ordinarily requires co-consecrators for episcopal ordination & Hierarchical character of deacon, presbyter, or bishop; permanent formal deputation to Christ the Head and Servant & Special sacramental grace of office for sanctifying, teaching, and governing (priestly degrees) or liturgical, word, and charitable \latin{diakonia} (diaconate) & \textbf{Primary:} apostolic ministry and altar. \textbf{Secondary:} minister's holiness, unity. \textbf{Contingent:} pastoral structures. \textbf{Ultimate:} Body's glory & Indelible character in each degree; no degree repeated\\

East: priest / bishop ministers the Crowning (CCC~1623) & Perpetual, exclusive marital bond (\latin{vinculum}), image of Christ's union with the Church; permanent relational change & Grace perfecting spousal love, indissoluble fidelity, and Christian nurture of children; holiness of the domestic church & \textbf{Primary:} spousal good and offspring. \textbf{Secondary:} mutual aid, remedy. \textbf{Contingent:} hospitality, service. \textbf{Ultimate:} Lamb's Supper & No character; ratified and consummated bond dissolves only by death\\

\textbf{Validity, liceity, and fruitfulness.} Christ is the principal cause of every sacrament; human ministers act as His intentional instruments (\emph{ST}~III, q.~64, aa.~1--10). Validity requires the essential matter and form, a valid minister with at least the intention to do what the Church does, and a capable subject; fruitful grace in a responsible adult also requires faith and freedom from mortal sin (\latin{obex}). Latin scholastic categories illuminate common Catholic faith without flattening Eastern liturgical theology.}
