\section{The Nine Angelic Orders and Comparative Orderings}
\label{app:orders}

The Catholic tradition gathers nine angelic names from Sacred Scripture (Is~6:2; Gen~3:24; Ezech~1 and 10; Col~1:16; Eph~1:21; 1~Thess~4:15 [Vulgate/Greek~4:16]; Jude~1:9; Ps~103:4 [Heb~104:4]). Before Pseudo-Dionysius organized them into three concentric triads around the year 500, several Fathers enumerated partial lists or arranged the nine names in distinct sequences; after him, St.~Gregory the Great proposed two alternative arrangements in his \emph{Homilies on the Gospels} and his \emph{Moralia in Job}, and St.~Thomas Aquinas reconciled the Dionysian and Gregorian orderings in \ST{I, q.~108, aa.~5--6}. Every table row below reproduces the verified primary sequence of each witness.
