\section{The Demons' Powers and Limits, and Positions on the First Sin}
\label{app:demons-first-sin}

\subsection{The Demons' Powers and Limits Under Divine Providence}

Catholic theology teaches that the fallen angels retained their natural angelic intelligence and active capacity while losing supernatural grace, and that every demonic operation remains strictly bounded by divine permission and creaturely nature. Matters of liturgical exorcism, canonical discipline, and pastoral discernment belong to the separate historical study of Catholic exorcism.

\subsection{Positions on the First Angelic Sin}

Catholic theologians agree on the defined core: the devil and his angels were created good by nature, sinned freely without coercion, and fell irrevocably (Lateran~IV, DS~800--801). Within that settled confession, Fathers and Doctors have proposed distinct explanations of the exact object, timing, and occasion of Lucifer's first sin.
