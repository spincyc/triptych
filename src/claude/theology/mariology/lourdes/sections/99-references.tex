\subsection*{The 1862 judgment and its witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Bertrand-S\'ev\`ere Laurence, Bishop of Tarbes,
\work{Mandement de Mgr l'\'Ev\^eque de Tarbes, portant jugement
sur l'Apparition qui a eu lieu \`a la Grotte de Lourdes}, 18
January 1862. Role: the operative approval act, quoted exactly in
\S1 and analyzed in \S4. Witness: the reproduction in Lasserre
(next entry), pp.~397--409, with the operative pages collated
against page images; collated further against the Sanctuary's
French transcription of art.~1
(\url{https://www.lourdes-france.com/1862-2022-160-ans-de-la-reconnaissance-des-apparitions-de-lourdes/})
and English presentation
(\url{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/the-decree/}). Ceilings
in \S4.3 and Appendix~A. The 1862 text is public domain.
\item *Henri Lasserre, \work{Notre-Dame de Lourdes} (Paris:
Victor Palm\'e, 1870), read in the Internet Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/notredamedelou00lass}; OCR
text and IIIF page images, leaves collated for printed
pp.~397--409 and the pp.~73--74 footnote). Role: period
narrative witness and documentary carrier of the mandement, the
officials' identities, the refused-access footnote, and the
Pius IX brief. Public domain (Lasserre d.~1900); apologetic
narrative, used at that ceiling.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Holy See acts and documents}

\begin{itemize}
