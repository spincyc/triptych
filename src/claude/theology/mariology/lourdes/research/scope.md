## Reader and governing question

A serious Catholic reader who wants devotion, history, source
criticism, medical honesty, and ecclesial obedience held together.
Governing question: what was reported at Lourdes in 1858 on the
nearest genuinely accessible record; what exactly Bishop Laurence
judged on 18 January 1862 (act, wording, object, ceilings); how the
self-identification of 25 March is transmitted and how it relates to
the 1854 dogma; how reported cures are and have been examined and
recognized (Bureau 1883, CMIL 1947/1954, criteria, the list of 72);
how Bernadette, the liturgy, and the popes received the event; and
what the judgment does and does not oblige, including its standing
under the 2024 DDF norms.

## Thesis

The 1862 mandement judged the eighteen 1858 apparitions real,
declared the faithful justified in believing them certain, and
authorized diocesan cult — no more. Everything later (cure
recognitions, feast, canonization, encyclical, visits, the World Day
of the Sick) has its own object, date, and authority. Approved
private revelation invites prudent human assent (fides humana) and
adds nothing to public Revelation; "medically inexplicable" is a
bounded, dated negative finding, never a scientific proof of divine
action; the water is ordinary; Bernadette was canonized for her
response to grace, not to re-judge the apparitions; and the record's
ceilings (lost and later critically recovered police records;
custodial harmonizations; institutional self-description) are stated
rather than papered over.

## Material discrepancies and stalenesses preserved

- **Gascon orthography:** the Sanctuary's French page prints "Que
  soy era immaculada councepciou"; its English page "Que soy era
  Immaculada Concepciou." Preserved as found; no autograph spelling
  asserted.
- **Chapel clause date:** the mandement's motivation joins the
  chapel request to the 25 March sentence; the Sanctuary's
  chronology puts the request on 2 March. Reported as the bishop's
  summary style; not harmonized.
- **Traynor cure year:** Sanctuary chronology (cure 25 July 1923;
  Bureau recognition 2 October 1926) controls against press
  accounts dating the cure 1926.
- **Sanctuary staleness:** the general Miracles page still names Dr
  de Franciscis as currently responsible after the dated Monami
  announcement; the older statistics page still counts 70 cures;
  the Medical Bureau history page still names Mgr Brouwet as CMIL
  episcopal co-president after the 2022 appointment of Bp Micas
  (confirmed current at source on the papal-visit page). Dated
  specific sources control.
- **Canonization formula OCR:** AAS 26 scan reads "exarationem" for
  "exaltationem"; standard reading used, variant recorded.
- **1907 decree year clause:** OCR numerals garbled; clause elided,
  not reconstructed.
