# Lourdes — corpus and status record (controlling inventory)

Provider: Anthropic Claude. This record is the controlling inventory
required by the apparition-judgment addendum of
`guidance/theology/mariology.md` for `theology/mariology/lourdes`. It
is updated before any narrative status change in the publication.

## Corpus definition

Single-event study. The included event corpus is exactly one dossier:
the eighteen reported apparitions of the Blessed Virgin Mary to
Bernadette Soubirous at the grotto of Massabielle, Lourdes, diocese
of Tarbes, on 11, 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25, 27, 28 February;
1, 2, 3, 4, 25 March; 7 April; and 16 July 1858 (dates per the
Sanctuary's chronology; the judged corpus is the mandement's own "le
11 février 1858 et jours suivants, au nombre de dix-huit fois"). No
"all approved apparitions" claim is made anywhere; no master registry
is asserted. Related phenomena and materials — the personal prayer
and secrets (received, undisclosed), the candle episode of 7 April as
a distinct prodigy, the reported cures (each its own recognition
act), later narratives and devotional literature — are inside the
narrative scope but **outside** the 1862 operative articles except
where those articles name them, and the publication says so wherever
they appear.

## Status terms used

- *jugeons ... a réellement apparu* / *fondés à la croire certaine* —
  the 1862 act's own formulas; legacy positive diocesan judgment; not
  relabeled under the 2024 taxonomy.
- *authorized cult* — the 1862 act's art. 2 (diocesan; devotional
  publications reserved to written approval).
- *recognized (cure)* — the competent diocesan bishop's declaration
  that a cure is miraculous; 72 such acts 1862–2025 per the
  Sanctuary's list.
- *unexplained in the present state of medical knowledge* — the
  Bureau/CMIL medical ceiling; never converted into a religious or
  scientific claim.
- *fides humana* — the assent invited by approved private revelation.
- *reported* — default register for all words and phenomena
  attributed to the apparition.
- *announced* — the 27 September 2026 papal visit (future event,
  announcement only).

## Event dossier (as of 2026-07-25)

- **Stable identity:** Our Lady of Lourdes (the apparition self-named
  "Que soy era immaculada councepciou," 25 March 1858); grotto of
  Massabielle, Lourdes; eighteen reported apparitions 11 February –
  16 July 1858.
- **Jurisdiction:** Diocese of Tarbes (now Tarbes and Lourdes), Latin
  Church, France. Current ordinary: Jean-Marc Micas (appointed 30
  March 2022; confirmed at source on the Sanctuary's 2026 papal-visit
  page).
- **Claimed type:** visions of the Blessed Virgin with a small
  reported verbal corpus (penance, prayer for sinners, procession and
  chapel, the fortnight promise, the 25 March self-identification);
  two empty days within the fortnight (22, 26 February) per the
  mandement and the Sanctuary chronology.
- **Recipient:** Bernadette Soubirous (1844–1879; Sister
  Marie-Bernard; canonized 8 December 1933).
- **Witness corpus:** Bernadette's oral accounts of 1858 surviving
  inside other documents; her later autograph accounts (from 1861;
  not accessible to this project); the 1858 administrative and
  judicial paper (existence documented; contents not accessible; the
  critical edition Laurentin–Billet, *Documents authentiques*,
  identified but not consulted); the 1858–1862 commission record as
  summarized in the mandement; period narrative (Lasserre 1870, used
  with collated page images); the Sanctuary's institutional
  chronology and pages (custodial harmonization).
- **Authority / act / date / language / locus / source:** Bishop
  Bertrand-Sévère Laurence; *Mandement portant jugement sur
  l'Apparition qui a eu lieu à la Grotte de Lourdes*; 18 January
  1862; French; operative articles ("Art. 1er. Nous jugeons que
  l'Immaculée Marie, Mère de Dieu, a réellement apparu à Bernadette
  Soubirous, le 11 février 1858 et jours suivants, au nombre de
  dix-huit fois, dans la Grotte de Massabielle, près de la ville de
  Lourdes ; que cette Apparition revêt tous les caractères de la
  vérité, et que les fidèles sont fondés à la croire certaine. /
  Nous soumettons humblement notre jugement au jugement du
  Souverain-Pontife... / Art. 2. Nous autorisons dans notre diocèse
  le culte de Notre-Dame de la Grotte de Lourdes; mais nous
  défendons de publier aucune formule particulière de prières...
  sans notre approbation donnée par écrit."); witness: Lasserre 1870
  reproduction pp. 397–409 (pages collated as images) with the
  Sanctuary's transcriptions in agreement; diocesan original and
  1862 imprint not consulted.
- **Procedural regime and operative formula:** pre-1978
  diocesan-ordinary discernment following Benedict XIV's rules;
  commission ordinance 28 July 1858; consulted physicians (Vergez)
  and scientists; operative formula = judgment of real apparition +
  warrant of certainty for the faithful + diocesan cult
  authorization + submission to the Roman Pontiff.
- **Exact object judged:** the eighteen 1858 apparitions and the
  diocesan cult; seven named cures recognized in connection with the
  same act. Not judged: verbal inerrancy of reported sentences; the
  candle episode as a distinct prodigy; the private prayer and
  secrets; later narratives, images, books, devotions (expressly
  reserved by art. 2); physical mechanism of the spring or cures;
  any obligation of divine faith.
- **Later acts and their objects:** 65 further episcopal cure
  recognitions through 16 April 2025 (total 72; list at the
  Sanctuary); Bureau des Constatations 1883 with Leo XIII's assent
  related 1886 and Holy See confirmation of procedures 1905 (per the
  Sanctuary's history; documents not located); CMIL 1947/1954;
  proper Office and Mass approved 11 July 1890 (Leo XIII); universal
  feast 13 November 1907 (Pius X; 11 February, double major, from
  1908); cause acts 1913/1923/1925; beatification 14 June 1925;
  canonization 8 December 1933 (universal memory 16 April); Pius
  XII, *Le pèlerinage de Lourdes*, 2 July 1957 (centenary); World
  Day of the Sick instituted 13 May 1992 (first kept 11 February
  1993); papal visits: John Paul II 1983 and 14–15 August 2004;
  Benedict XVI 12–15 September 2008; announced: Leo XIV, 27
  September 2026.
- **Current controlling status:** positive legacy diocesan judgment
  of 1862 in force in its historical formula; no superseding,
  restricting, or reversing act located; no DDF act under the 2024
  norms concerns Lourdes (DDF documents index checked 2026-07-25);
  the 2024 norms govern new discernments, not this completed one.
  Not established by any act: obligation of divine faith;
  supernatural-origin certainty as a scientific claim; curative
  property of the water; authentication of every narrative or
  publication.
- **Reported-message themes checked:** penance and prayer for
  sinners (24–25 February, Sanctuary chronology); procession and
  chapel (2 March); the fortnight promise (18 February); the
  self-identification (25 March; textual history in section 3 of
  the publication). Cautions: no date-setting, no political content,
  no new doctrine in the received corpus; the rosebush did not bloom
  (Sanctuary's own negative); crowd figures are received round
  numbers.
- **Source / translation / rights / disagreement / gap / review
  status:** witnesses and rights in `source-audit.md` and the
  publication's References; translations editorial and marked;
  disagreements and stalenesses preserved (Gascon orthography
  between the Sanctuary's own pages; mandement vs. chronology on
  the chapel clause's date; Traynor cure-year press confusion;
  stale Sanctuary pages on the Bureau physician, the 70-cure count,
  and the CMIL's episcopal co-president); gaps recorded (diocesan
  archive, Documents authentiques, autographs, police records, 1905
  act, privilege acts, typical editions); review = internal only,
  no independent or ecclesiastical review.
