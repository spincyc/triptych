\subsection*{The controlling act and the procedural law}

\begin{itemize}
\item *David L. Ricken, Bishop of Green Bay, \work{Decree on the
Authenticity of the Apparitions of 1859 at the Shrine of Our Lady of
Good Help, Diocese of Green Bay}, 8 December 2010, four pages. Role:
the operative approval act, quoted at its operative clause in \S1 and
analyzed in \S7. Witness: the signed PDF published by the National
Shrine,
\url{https://championshrine.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Shrine-of-Our-Lady-of-Good-Help-Declaration.pdf}
(SHA-256 of the file as retrieved
\texttt{b10ad96f0821fef8072164d6f83d3f33d9e008e1aa510d1c5706bbe53bbd664e};
pp.~1--3 have a text layer, p.~4 is an image and was read at 150~dpi).
Rights status unresolved; quoted only so far as identifying the act's
object and effect requires.
\item *Sacred Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, \work{Norms
regarding the manner of proceeding in the discernment of presumed
apparitions or revelations}, 25 February 1978. Role: the procedural
regime in force when the 2010 decree issued; its positive criteria and
three-step path are collated with the decree in \S7.2. Witness: the
Holy See's English text,
\url{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19780225_norme-apparizioni_en.html}.
\item *Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, \work{Norms for
Proceeding in the Discernment of Alleged Supernatural Phenomena}, 17
May 2024, with the Prefect's presentation. Role: current procedural
law; the six conclusions, nn.~11--13 and 23, arts.~1--2, 22 and 27, and
the presentation's account of pre-2024 practice, used in \S\S7--8.
Witness: the Holy See's English text,
\url{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240517_norme-fenomeni-soprannaturali_en.html}.
\item *\work{Codex Iuris Canonici} (1983), cann.~1230--1234. Role: the
legal category of shrine and national shrine, used in \S8.1. Witness:
the Holy See's English text,
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann1205-1243_en.html}.
\item *Second Vatican Council, \work{Lumen gentium}, 21 November 1964,
nn.~60--62. Role: the doctrinal boundary within which the reported
message is read in \S9.4. Witness: the Holy See's English text,
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The nineteenth-century published narratives}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Eliza Allen Starr, \work{Patron Saints} (Baltimore: John B. Piet
\& Co., 1883), chapter ``St.~Lioba,'' pp.~297--301; copyright entered
1871. Role: the earliest published narrative of the events located by
this study; quoted at length in \S3 and used as the base text of the
stratigraphy in \S5. Witness: the Internet Archive scan of the New York
Public Library copy, \url{https://archive.org/details/patronsaints00star}
(optical-character-recognition text for reading and searching; front
matter and pp.~298--301 read at 150~dpi). Collated against the Chicago
1896 printing of the first series,
\url{https://archive.org/details/PatronSaints1st2ndSeries}, which
reproduces the same setting at the same page numbers. Public domain
(author died 1901).
\item *Peter Pernin, \work{The Finger of God Is There! or, Thrilling
Episode of a Strange Event Related by an Eye-Witness} (Montreal:
printed by John Lovell, 1874), Appendix, pp.~94--102. Role: the second
earliest published narrative and the sole early witness to the
preservation of 8 October 1871; quoted in \S\S4 and 6. Witness: the
Canadian Institute for Historical Microreproductions scan,
\url{https://archive.org/details/cihm_04805} (recognition text for
reading and searching; printed pp.~95 and 98--100 read at 200~dpi and
collated against the quotations). Public domain (author died 1909). Published with the approbation of the Bishop of Montreal
and for the benefit of a church at Marinette, Wisconsin; used at the
ceiling of a devotional fundraising narrative that declares its own
abridgement.
\item *Peter Pernin, \work{Le doigt de Dieu est l\`a! ou \'Episode
\'emouvant d'un \'ev\`enement \'etrange racont\'e par un t\'emoin
oculaire} (Montreal: Eus\`ebe S\'en\'ecal, 1874), Appendice,
pp.~91--101. Role: textual control on the English printing; collated
clause by clause in \S\S4 and 6. Witness:
\url{https://archive.org/details/cihm_23982}. Public domain.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The 1871 fires}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Frank Tilton, \work{Sketch of the Great Fires in Wisconsin at
Peshtigo, the Sugar Bush, Menekaune, Williamsonville, and Generally on
the Shores of Green Bay} (Green Bay: Robinson \& Kustermann, 1871),
Preface and ch.~XIV, with the \work{Map of the Burnt District of
Wisconsin 1871}. Role: the contemporary compilation against which the
Champion preservation tradition is tested in \S6.1; it names
Robinsonville among the settlements destroyed and records no exception
there. Witness: \emph{a modern reset reproduction} digitized on the
Internet Archive,
\url{https://archive.org/details/sketch-of-the-great-fires-in-wisconsin},
read at 150~dpi; the 1871 imprint was not reached, and that copy prints
``October 3d'' where the volume's own preface and map give 8 October
1871. Public domain in text.
\item \work{History of Northern Wisconsin} (Chicago: Western Historical
Company, 1881). Role: negative-search corpus only, for the absence of
any mention of Adele Brise or the chapel; the single occurrence of
``Robinsonville'' is in an unrelated biographical sketch. Witness: the
Internet Archive optical-character-recognition text,
\url{https://archive.org/details/wi-northern-wi-1881-western-historical-co}.
Public domain. Searched literally and line by line; the limits of that
search are stated in \S2.3 and Appendix~A.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Custodial and diocesan presentations}

\begin{itemize}
\item *National Shrine of Our Lady of Champion, ``Our Story''
(\url{https://championshrine.org/our-story/}), ``Shrine Historical
Timeline'' (\url{https://championshrine.org/timeline/}), and
``Solemnity of Our Lady of Champion''
(\url{https://championshrine.org/oloc-solemnity/}), all read
2026-07-25. Role: the current custodial narrative of the events, the
fire, the dated frame, the national-shrine designation, the 2023 change
of title and solemnity, and the paragraph attributed to Pernin that
\S6.3 shows is not his text. Used at institutional-self-description
ceilings throughout. \copyright{} the publisher; quoted briefly with
citation.
\item Diocese of Green Bay, ``Past Bishops''
(\url{https://www.gbdioc.org/about-us/past-bishops/}), read 2026-07-25.
Role: the establishment of the diocese in 1868 and the succession
making Bishop Ricken the twelfth. \copyright{} the publisher.
\item \url{https://adelebrice.org/adele-brice-path-to-sainthood/},
read 2026-07-25. Role: the existence and presentation of the cause of
canonization; it supplied no dated act, and none is asserted.
\copyright{} the publisher.
\end{itemize}
