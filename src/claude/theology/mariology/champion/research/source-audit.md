Provider: Anthropic Claude. All web loci were retrieved and read on
2026-07-25 unless another date is stated. This record names, for each

## 1. The controlling act

**Decree on the Authenticity of the Apparitions of 1859 at the Shrine
of Our Lady of Good Help, Diocese of Green Bay**, David L. Ricken,
Bishop of Green Bay, 8 December 2010, four pages.

- Witness: PDF published by the National Shrine at
  `https://championshrine.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Shrine-of-Our-Lady-of-Good-Help-Declaration.pdf`
- SHA-256 of the file as retrieved:
  `b10ad96f0821fef8072164d6f83d3f33d9e008e1aa510d1c5706bbe53bbd664e`
  (476,793 bytes, PDF 1.6, four letter pages).
- Pages 1–3 carry an extractable text layer and were read that way and
  spot-checked against 130 dpi renderings. **Page 4 has no text layer**
  and was read entirely from a 150 dpi rendering; it carries the
  operative clause, the date clause, Bishop Ricken's signature, the
  countersignature of Chancellor John F. Doerfler, and the diocesan
  seal with *Concordat cum Originali* / *Cancellarius* / *Dioecesis
  Sinus Viridis*.
- Claims resting on it: the operative clause and its four features
  (§1); the "documents are not abundant" recital and the expert's
  coherence sentence (§2.2, §7.1); the message quotation and its
  ellipses (§7.4); the fire recital (§6.4); the "none of these favors
  have been officially declared a miracle" sentence (§6.4, §7.7); the
  predecessor-bishops recital (§7.6); the boundary table (§7.7).
- **Ceilings.** The diocesan original, protocol number, and register
  entry were not consulted. The three Marian experts are unnamed in the
  decree and their reports were not reached. The instrument
  constituting the 2009 commission was not located. The file's internal
  creation timestamp is 5 December 2010, three days before the decree's
  date; nothing is inferred from that and it is not mentioned in the
  publication. Rights status of the decree text was not resolved;
  quotation is limited to what identifying the act's object and effect
  requires.

## 2. The nineteenth-century published narratives

### 2.1 Eliza Allen Starr

*Patron Saints* (Baltimore: John B. Piet & Co., 1883), chapter "St.
Lioba," pp. 297–301.

- Witness: Internet Archive scan of the New York Public Library copy,
  identifier `patronsaints00star`. Optical-character-recognition text
  used for reading and searching; the front matter and pp. 298–301 were
  read at 150 dpi from the scan's PDF.
- Page images established: the imprimatur, printed "MARTINUS JOANNES,
  ARCHIEPISCOPUS BALTIMORENSIS"; the copyright line, "Entered,
  according to Act of Congress, in the year 1871, by ELIZA ALLEN STARR,
  in the Office of the Librarian of Congress, at Washington"; and the
  dedication, dated "St. Joseph's Cottage, Chicago. Feast of All
  Saints, MDCCCLXIX." The 1871 reading of the copyright year was
  checked on the image because the OCR garbles it.
- Collation: against the Chicago 1896 printing of the first series,
  identifier `PatronSaints1st2ndSeries` (Google scan), whose front
  matter reads "PATRON SAINTS FIRST SERIES," copyright 1881, imprimatur
  in the name of the Archbishop of Chicago. The Adele passage appears
  at the same page numbers from what is evidently the same setting; the
  two OCR texts were compared line by line and no substantive variant
  was located.
- Claims resting on it: the whole of §3; the base text of the
  stratigraphy in §5.3; the 15 August commemoration in §5.4; the free
  school and the Chicago begging journey in §2.5.
- **Ceilings.** The 1871 first-series imprint was not located in any
  digital library reached. Whether the Adele passage was composed
  before or after the fire of 8 October 1871 cannot be determined from
  the volume; no argument depends on it, and the publication says so.
  The priest who wrote the letter of introduction is "Rev. Father P.
  ——" in the printed text and is not identified.

### 2.2 Peter Pernin

*The Finger of God Is There!* (Montreal: printed by John Lovell, 1874),
Appendix, pp. 94–102, and *Le doigt de Dieu est là!* (Montreal: Eusèbe
Sénécal, imprimeur, 1874), Appendice, pp. 91–101.

- Witnesses: Canadian Institute for Historical Microreproductions scans
  on the Internet Archive, identifiers `cihm_04805` (English) and
  `cihm_23982` (French). Read in OCR text throughout, with the
  consequential English pages also read as page images (below); both
  title pages and the English approbation were read in the OCR and are
  consistent with each other.
- Both title pages carry: publication with the approbation of the
  Bishop of Montreal, and publication for the benefit of the Church of
  Our Lady of Lourdes, Marinette, Wisconsin.
- **Collation performed:** the entire appendix was compared clause by
  clause between the two languages. The texts agree substantively
  throughout. Divergences recorded: "seven miles and a-half" renders
  *deux lieues et demi*; "the tree sanctified by her apparent touch"
  renders *l'arbre sur lequel s'est faite l'apparition*; "about six
  acres" renders *six arpents*.
- **Page-image collation.** Printed pp. 95 and 98–100 of the English
  printing were rendered at 200 dpi and the quoted passages were read
  directly from those images. This established three things the OCR
  obscured: the seer's name is set with a grave accent ("Adèle Brisse"),
  so the acute in the recognition text is a scanner artifact; the
  sentence "If the Blessed Virgin still has need of us, she will protect
  our lives, if not, we must succumb to the fiery death awaiting us" is
  legible on the page and is not a reconstruction; and the printing
  carries a compositor's error, "twice very year" for "twice every
  year," at printed p. 99, where the French has *chaque année, à deux
  époques fixes*. Elsewhere, only plain within-word scanner misreads
  were corrected (e.g. "Brisge" → "Brisse"); no wording was supplied.
- Claims resting on it: the whole of §4; the preservation account and
  Pernin's two refusals in §6.2; the institutional detail in §2.5; the
  east-shore/west-shore distinction in §6.1; the collation columns in
  §5.2; the "Go, and fear nothing" stratum in §5.3.
- **Ceiling.** Pernin's implied date, "ten or twelve years" before
  1874, is incompatible with October 1859 and is left unreconciled.
  His declared abridgement ("I will repeat only what is necessary to
  the elucidation of my subject") means his silences prove much less
  than Starr's, and the publication says so.

## 3. The 1871 fires

### 3.1 Frank Tilton

*Sketch of the Great Fires in Wisconsin* (Green Bay: Robinson &
Kustermann, 1871), Preface, chapter XIV, map of the burnt district.

- Witness: **a modern reset reproduction**, digitized and uploaded to
  the Internet Archive in 2022, identifier
  `sketch-of-the-great-fires-in-wisconsin`. The item's metadata gives
  the date 1871 and reproduces the 1871 title page typographically, but
  the body is modern typesetting, not a facsimile. Pages cited were
  read at 150 dpi.
- Readings established at image resolution: the preface ("the night of
  October 8th, 1871"; "more than a thousand men, women and children";
  "about four thousand people homeless"); the east-shore chapter,
  including the list of "the largest settlements destroyed in this
  track of fire" with Robinsonville among them; the distinctness of the
  east-shore and west-shore firestorms; "If a building escaped it was
  an exception to the general rule"; and the Door County figures. The
  map shows Robinsonville within the shaded burnt district on the east
  shore.
- **Textual crux.** The reset text prints "On the night of October 3d"
  for the east-shore firestorm, contradicting the same volume's preface
  and map. The reading was verified on the page image. Whether the
  error is the 1871 compositor's or the modern setter's cannot be
  determined from this artifact; the publication records both
  possibilities and relies on the preface, map, and Pernin for 8
  October.
- Claims resting on it: §6.1 in full, and the negative result in §2.3.
- **Ceiling.** The 1871 Green Bay imprint was not reached. The
  negative result — that Tilton records no preservation at
  Robinsonville — is stated in the publication as a bounded argument
  from silence with its weaknesses named.

### 3.2 *History of Northern Wisconsin* (1881)

- Witness: Internet Archive OCR text, identifier
  `wi-northern-wi-1881-western-historical-co` (about 10.4 MB of text).
- **Method.** Literal, case-insensitive search of that exact text, one
  LF-delimited physical line at a time, for `Brise`, `Brisse`, `Good
  Help`, `Bon Secours`, `Robinsonville`. The first four returned no
  hit. `Robinsonville` returned exactly one hit, in a Fort Howard
  biographical sketch (Silas L. Doyne), referring to a school "under
  the care of the Winnebago Presbytery." The hit was read in context in
  the OCR text; the corresponding page images were **not** checked, and
  the sketch is reported only for what it excludes.
- **Ceiling.** A literal line-bounded search cannot exclude hyphenation
  across lines, variant spellings, damaged recognition, or a passage
  the scanner mangled. The negative result is bounded and correctable
  and is stated that way.

## 4. Custodial and diocesan presentations

- National Shrine of Our Lady of Champion: "Our Story"
  (`https://championshrine.org/our-story/`, page stamp 2026-04-13),
  "Shrine Historical Timeline"
  (`https://championshrine.org/timeline/`, page stamp 2026-01-07), and
  "Solemnity of Our Lady of Champion"
  (`https://championshrine.org/oloc-solemnity/`, page stamp
  2026-07-21). Read 2026-07-25.
- **The misattribution finding.** The "Our Story" page introduces a
  paragraph with "The following is the account of Father Peter Pernin"
  and attributes it to "Fr. Peter Pernin." That paragraph was collated
  in full against both 1874 printings. It is not Pernin's text in
  either language. The only genuine correspondences are the emerald
  isle in a sea of ashes and the charred palings. The downpour, the
  extinguishing of the fire, the answered prayer, and the convent are
  absent from Pernin. The source of the paragraph was **not**
  identified; the finding is confined to non-identity with the 1874
  text, and the publication states that limit.
- **Internal custodial inconsistencies recorded rather than
  harmonized:** the timeline's 1871 entry says "five acres" where
  Pernin says six in both languages, and places the beginning of "15th
  of August" devotions in 1871 although Starr, in a volume
  copyright-entered that year, describes the August procession as
  already annual.
- Diocese of Green Bay, "Past Bishops"
  (`https://www.gbdioc.org/about-us/past-bishops/`). Retrieved via a
  fetching tool after direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. Establishes
  the 1868 establishment of the diocese and the succession making
  Ricken the twelfth bishop. The prior jurisdiction is not stated on
  the page and is not asserted anywhere in the publication.
- `https://adelebrice.org/adele-brice-path-to-sainthood/`. Establishes
  the existence and presentation of the cause; supplied **no** dated
  act, and the publication asserts no date for the cause.

## 8. Production review — 2026-07-25

A settled two-pass build through `make PROVIDER=claude
DOC=theology/mariology/champion doc` produced a 32-page US-letter PDF,
SHA-256 `94d9b44bcfd7fe4d61aa12da4e5840f825f37207f8db5e9726a02c301c067929`.
The final log contained no fatal error, undefined reference, overfull or
underfull box, or unresolved rerun or layout warning. Generation
metadata validated; PDF title and Letter page size were checked; every
font is embedded, subsetted, and Unicode-mapped; text extraction is
non-empty. Review rasters were generated through
`scripts/pdf-review` and every rendered page was visually inspected for
clipping, overlap, accidental blanks, bad breaks, table and box
integrity, footer and metadata placement, and legibility. Four layout
faults were found and corrected: a table of contents that spilled one
line onto an otherwise empty second page; three line-break hyphens that
rendered as a stray hyphen followed by a space; a doubled dash in the
Starr endnote; and paragraph breaks inside the judgment blocks that the
frame had suppressed. After correction, the
final-page rights colophon is readable, unclipped, non-overlapping,
sits with terminal content, and creates no rights-only spill page. The
leaf declares `eligible` in `web-edition.toml`, `make
check-web-editions PROVIDER=claude` passes, and the web conversion runs
clean. `python3 scripts/source-library validate` passes. The PDF was
**not** installed to `doc/`, and no catalogue entry, release binding, or
commit was made: those steps were outside the authorization for this
work.
