Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every check below was performed 2026-07-25
against the named online witness. "Checked" means the passage or fact
was retrieved and read at the stated URL; it does not assert collation
against a critical print edition (an outstanding review item) except

1889 Manual: see `prayer-text-audit.md`). No search, source, or
verification event is recorded here that did not occur.

established the magisterial, historical, and prayer-text base. The
second (**deepening revision, 2026-07-25**) added the per-mystery
scriptural, magisterial, patristic, and devotional evidence, the

liturgical-discipline material, and the second prayer-text witness.
Entries added in the second campaign are marked *(deepening)*.

## Negative results and checked absences

- No Raccolta or other prayer-text work record existed in
  `src/sources/works/` before this publication (directory enumerated
  2026-07-25); the Baltimore Catechism witness was therefore newly
  registered. *(deepening)* Nine further work/edition records were
  registered for this revision (Salutaris ille; Vi è ben noto; the 1889
  Manual of Prayers; the 2020 CDW circular letter; NPNF1 vol. 7, NPNF2
  vols. 7 and 12; Cyril of Alexandria's Commentary on Luke; John of
  Damascus's Dormition homilies). ANF vol. 1 already existed.
- newadvent.org/cathen/13185a–13188a were probed for the feast article
  and are not it (13188a is "Breviary Hymns of the Rosary"); the feast
  article is 13189a.
- The Enchiridion's concession 17 § 1 defines the Rosary with
  "quindecim … decades" (fifteen); it contains no mention of the 2002
  twenty-mystery cycle. This absence is reported, not interpreted as a
  judgment against the Luminous Mysteries.
- drbo.org chapter URLs redirect from http to https (301); https URLs
  recorded.
- *(deepening)* **The count of "twelve" Leonine Rosary encyclicals could
  not be verified.** Neither the Vatican indices, nor RVM, nor the
  Directory on Popular Piety states any count. Eleven encyclicals carry a
  Rosary subject line on vatican.va; tallies reaching twelve add
  documents that are not encyclicals on the Rosary (Salutaris ille,
  Parta humano generi) or an encyclical on another subject (Quamquam
  pluries). The publication reports eleven and discloses the
  circulating figure without asserting it.
- *(deepening)* **The transfer of the feast to 7 October was not
  located.** Thurston records only the first Sunday of October and gives
  no later date; his article never mentions 7 October or Pius X.
- *(deepening)* **No Holy See page consulted publishes the General Roman
  Calendar table.** Mysterii Paschalis (14 Feb 1969) approves the
  calendar in general terms and is on vatican.va and in AAS 61 (1969)
  222–226, but neither prints the table; vatican.va liturgy directories
  returned HTTP 403. The rank and title of the 7 October memorial are
  therefore reported from a territorial calendar, with that limit stated
  in the publication.
- *(deepening)* **The acts adding *Mater Ecclesiae* (1980) and *Regina
  familiae* (1995) to the Litany of Loreto could not be located at a Holy
  See source.** AAS 72 (1980), 73 (1981), 87 (1995), and 88 (1996) were
  downloaded and searched without result; both additions are reported to
  have been promulgated in *Notitiae*, which the Holy See does not
  publish online. *Mater Ecclesiae* is at least presupposed as present by
  the 2020 CDW letter. The publication names the two additions as widely
  reported and records the negative result rather than filling it.
- *(deepening)* **No verifiable public-domain patristic reading was found
  for the Proclamation of the Kingdom or the Crowning with Thorns.** For
  the Proclamation the difficulty is structural: the mystery's object is
  a phase of the ministry rather than a single pericope. Ambrose's
  *Exposition of Luke* and Origen's *Homilies on Luke*, the classic
  commentaries for the Visitation and the Presentation, have no
  public-domain English online (the current translations are in
  copyright); the NPNF Augustine sermon collection was searched and
  contains no Visitation, Simeon, or Gethsemane material. Cyril of
  Alexandria and John Damascene were used instead, from outside the
  ANF/NPNF corpora.
- *(deepening)* Bernard of Clairvaux's homilies *Missus est* are not in
  the ANF/NPNF corpora and so not at New Advent; a public-domain 1909
  English exists at the Internet Archive but was not needed and was not
  used.
- *(deepening)* The 1889 Manual of Prayers prints, in the second
  sorrowful mystery, a traditional figure for the number of stripes
  ("about five thousand") with its own qualifier "it is said." The figure
  is reported in the publication as a feature of the witness and
  expressly not as history.

## Claim-to-source rule

Every quotation in the publication appears above. Historical claims rest
on the named CE articles or on the papal documents' own texts; where a
papal text repeats the received Dominic tradition, the publication
reports the repetition as reception, not as verification. Editorial
paraphrases of Latin (the Enchiridion definition and concession, and the
Salutaris ille decree) are labeled as paraphrase, summary, or
identification rendering in the publication and are not translations
offered for recitation or legal reliance. Patristic passages are used
only as readings of the scriptural events named beside them.
