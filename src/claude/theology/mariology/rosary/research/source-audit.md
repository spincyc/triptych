## Claim-to-source rule

Every quotation in the publication appears above. Historical claims
rest on the named CE articles or on the papal documents' own texts;
where a papal text repeats the received Dominic tradition, the
publication reports the repetition as reception, not as verification.
Editorial paraphrases of Latin (the Enchiridion definition and
concession) are labeled as paraphrase/summary in the publication and
are not translations offered for recitation or legal reliance.
