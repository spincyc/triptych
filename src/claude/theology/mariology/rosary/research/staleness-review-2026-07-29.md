## Three-way verdict

The modified candidate and old edition agree in substance. The rewritten
candidate is necessarily narrower because the changed inputs cannot sustain a
Rosary reference. Its omissions do not contradict claims supported by
unchanged Rosary sources. The doctrinal passages strengthen existing
safeguards; the Sacristy/Sedilia records are unrelated; Cyril Sermon 120 is
theologically useful but does not satisfy the edition's mystery-specific
patristic criterion. No changed input removes, weakens, or contradicts a
consequential claim.

**Verdict: no material change.** This review does not rebaseline the edition.
