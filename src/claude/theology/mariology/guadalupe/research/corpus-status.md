# Guadalupe — corpus and status record (controlling inventory)

Provider: Anthropic Claude. This record is the controlling inventory
required by the apparition-judgment addendum of
`guidance/theology/mariology.md` for `theology/mariology/guadalupe`.
It is updated before any narrative status change in the publication.

## Corpus definition

Single-event study. The included event corpus is exactly one dossier:
the reported appearances of the Blessed Virgin Mary to the Nahua
commoner Juan Diego Cuauhtlatoatzin at the hill of Tepeyac, on the
northern edge of Mexico City, in December 1531, together with the
reported imprinting of an image on his *tilma* and the reported cure
of his uncle Juan Bernardino. Jurisdiction: Archdiocese of Mexico.

No "all approved apparitions" claim is made anywhere; no master
registry is asserted. Related matters — the cult at Tepeyac from the
1550s, the physical image, the cause of Juan Diego, the liturgical and
papal reception — are inside the narrative scope but are separate
objects with separate acts, and the publication says so wherever they
appear.

**As-of date: 2026-07-25.**

## Status terms used, and what each does not establish

- *reported* — the default register for the events of 1531 and for
  everything attributed to the apparition. Used because no competent
  act determines them.
- *received tradition* — the narrative as transmitted by the printed
  witnesses of 1648–1649 and their descendants.
- *antiqua et constans traditio docet* — the formula substituted in
  the proper Office in 1894 for the earlier *fertur*. A statement
  about a tradition; not a determination of fact. Not relabelled under
  the 2024 taxonomy.
- *sin ser un dogma de fe … una tradición antigua, constante y
  universal* — the Fifth Mexican Provincial Council's own formula
  (1896). A provincial-council edict; not a modern determination.
- *equipollent beatification by decree of immemorial cult* — the 1990
  act's own juridical route. Its object is the cult and the reputation
  of sanctity.
- *canonization* — the 2002 act. Proposes sanctity and public cult; it
  does not determine biography and, by the principle now stated at
  DDF *Norms* (2024) n. 13, does not authenticate associated
  phenomena.
- *gradu festi* — the rank assigned by the 1999 CDW decree for the
  calendars of the Americas. No rank is asserted for the General
  Roman Calendar.
- *fides humana* — the prudent human assent invited by approved
  private revelation.

## Current controlling status

**No determination of the supernatural origin or historical
occurrence of the 1531 events by any competent authority was
located.** The Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith's documents
index was retrieved on 2026-07-25 and searched case-insensitively for
`guadalupe`, `tepeyac`, `juan diego` and `mexic`: zero matches, while
searches for `apparition` and `supernatural` returned the 2024 *Norms*,
the 1978 *Normae*, and determinations concerning Dozulé, Amsterdam,
Gisella Cardia, the Madonna della Guardia and Mount S. Onofrio — which
establishes that the index does carry acts of this kind.

The bounded negative is not a finding that the events were judged not
to have occurred. It is a finding about what the record contains.

## Witness corpus and evidentiary ceilings

- **1649 imprint (JCB copy):** headings, the opening sentence, the
  self-identification at fol. 2r, and the fourfold consolation at
  fol. 5r collated against page images; the *Nican motecpana* death
  notice read at OCR level only. Second witness for the Nahuatl
  passages: de la Rosa 1887.
- **1648 Sánchez; Becerra Tanco; Florencia; the 1666 *Informaciones*;
  the *Información* of 1556:** not consulted directly. Reported at the
  level of the 1785 compilation, of O'Gorman, and of García
  Icazbalceta.
- **Shrine dossier:** reproduced at the shrine's own level of
  assertion; no manuscript inspected; no dating adopted.
- **Códice Escalada:** disputed object; no primary publication of any
  examination reached.
- **Image examinations:** 1666, 1751 and 1787 at Cuadriello's level;
  1982 at Cuadriello's level from the 2005 printing of the Sol Rosales
  and Guadarrama reports; Callahan 1981 identified but not read.
- **1990s objections:** reported from press and reference accounts;
  no letter read; two retrievals returned HTTP 403.

## Message and caution check

The reported message content used in the publication is limited to
what the 1649 imprint carries and to what the Holy See's own acts
narrate. The narrative's self-identification subordinates the
apparition to *huelnelli Teotl Dios*; no reported message in the
corpus asserts an independent Marian source of grace, a future
chronology, a political programme, or a claim against pastoral,
medical or civil action. The publication states the LG 60–62 / CCC 970
boundary explicitly and flags the devotional risk of the detached
"Am I not here, I who am your mother?" slogan.

## Disagreement, gaps, and review state

Disagreement is preserved rather than resolved: the historicity
dispute (§4), the date and authorship of the *Nican mopohua*, the
*Códice Escalada*, and the material questions about the image. Gaps
are recorded in the publication's Appendix A and in `source-audit.md`.
Review state: internal only; no independent or ecclesiastical review.
