## Evidence states used

- **collated** — quoted wording checked against a page image (or two
  independent witnesses) of the identified edition;
- **read at first hand** — the identified page located and read in the
  source's own scan or delivery state by the author of this study;
- **reported** — taken at the level of a named intermediate witness,
  which is cited;
- **assisted verification** — retrieved and read by an assisting
  research agent of the same AI contributor and not independently
  re-read; marked in place in the publication;
- **not traced** — searched for and not reached.

## D. Consequential negative results

1. **DDF documents index** — retrieved and searched
   case-insensitively for `guadalupe`, `tepeyac`, `juan diego`,
   `mexic`: zero matches. `apparition`/`supernatural` return the 2024
   *Norms*, the 1978 *Normae*, and determinations for other phenomena.
   Bounded by language, index coverage and tool.
2. **Latin text of the 1754 brief** — not reached. The Mechelen
   *Bullarium* volume covering 1754 is not digitized at the Internet
   Archive; the accessible volume whose index lists the incipit *Non
   est equidem* contains constitutions of 1756 and no Mexican material
   (full-text search for `Guadalup`, `Mexic`, `Tepe` returned
   nothing).
3. **Leo XIII 1887 brief; SRC decree of 6 March 1894; contemporary
   notice of the 1895 coronation** — not found in *ASS* 19, 20, 26,
   27.
4. **Pius X's declaration of the patronage of Latin America** — not
   found in *AAS* 1910 or 1911.
5. **Any Guadalupan act of Pius XII in 1946** — *AAS* 38 (1946)
   contains none.
6. **Latin canonization formula for Juan Diego** — not found in *AAS*
   94 (2002).
7. **Image claims** — seven popular claims searched and recorded as
   "not traced" at §6.3: NASA; Richard Kuhn 1936; the cloth's
   inexplicable survival (no modern materials-science study located);
   the eye studies; the star map; the *coatlaxopeuh* etymology; and,
   separately, the primary publication of the Callahan study and of
   any *Códice Escalada* examination.
8. **1990s objection letters** — no original or published text
   reached; National Catholic Register and *La Jornada* retrievals
   returned HTTP 403.
9. **León-Portilla, *Tonantzin Guadalupe* (2000)** — bibliographic
   identity confirmed through a Library of Congress record by assisted
   verification, but the work was not read and no position is
   attributed to him in the publication.

not reproduced.
