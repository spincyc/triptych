\subsection*{Primary witnesses read in the original}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Luis Laso de la Vega, \work{Huei tlamahui\c{c}oltica omonexiti
in ilhuicac tlat\'oca \c{C}ihuapilli Santa Maria Totla\c{c}\`onantzin
Guadalupe in nican huei altepenahuac Mexico itocayocan tepeyacac}
(Mexico: Juan Ruiz, 1649). Role: the controlling witness of the
\work{Nican mopohua} and \work{Nican motecpana}; quoted in \S2.1 and
\S7 and collated at the page-image level. Copy used: John Carter Brown
Library, digitized at
\url{https://archive.org/details/hueitlamahuiolti00lass}. The 1649 text
is public domain; the JCB states that its digital-collection images
are licensed CC~BY~4.0 with the credit line ``Courtesy of the John
Carter Brown Library.'' A second digitized copy, from the Center for
the Study of the History of Mexico CARSO, is at the Library of
Congress, \url{https://www.loc.gov/item/2021666123/}, whose rights
statement reads: ``The Library of Congress is unaware of any copyright
or other restrictions in the World Digital Library Collection.''
Note: the JCB volume is a composite binding and contains a later
bound-with sermon which is not part of the 1649 tract (\S2.1).
\item *Agust\'in de la Rosa, \work{Dissertatio historico-theologica de
apparitione B.~M.~V.\ de Guadalupe} (Guadalajara, 1887). Role:
independent nineteenth-century printing of the Nahuatl
\work{Nican mopohua}, used to collate the passages quoted in \S7.
Copy used: New York Public Library, digitized by Google,
\url{https://archive.org/details/dissertatiohist00rosagoog}; Internet
Archive metadata records \texttt{possible-copyright-status:
NOT\_IN\_COPYRIGHT}.
\item *\work{Colecci\'on de obras y opusculos pertenecientes a la
milagrosa aparicion de la bellisima imagen de Nuestra Se\~nora de
Guadalupe} (Madrid [in fact Mexico], 1785). Role: eighteenth-century
apparitionist compilation; used for the sequence of the printed
relations, its own admission that nothing was printed in the first
century, the 1666 petition to Alexander~VII, the 1754 brief's incipit,
and Cabrera's account of the 1751 examination. Copy used: John Carter
Brown Library,
\url{https://archive.org/details/coleccindeobrasy00unkn}. Public
domain.
\item *Joaqu\'in Garc\'ia Icazbalceta, \work{Carta acerca del origen
de la imagen de Nuestra Se\~nora de Guadalupe de M\'exico} (written
October 1883; first printed 1896). Role: the classical statement of
the argument from silence, quoted in \S3.1 and used in \S3.2, \S6.3
and \S8.2. Quoted from the Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes
transcription
(\url{https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra-visor/carta-acerca-del-origen-de-la-imagen-de-nuestra-senora-de-guadalupe-de-mexico--0/html/b28296be-844c-4ebe-a169-2732231ce572_2.html}),
collated against the 1896 imprint in the Harvard copy digitized by
Google, \url{https://archive.org/details/cartaacercadelo00icazgoog}
(\texttt{NOT\_IN\_COPYRIGHT}). Public domain; author d.~1894.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Scholarship used at its own level}

\begin{itemize}

de la imagen y culto de Nuestra Se\~nora de Guadalupe del Tepeyac},
2nd ed. (Mexico: UNAM, Instituto de Investigaciones Hist\'oricas,
Serie Historia Novohispana 36; digital edition 2019). Chapters and
appendices read: part~II ch.~3 (pp.~81--92); Appendix~I; Appendix~IV

\url{http://www.historicas.unam.mx/publicaciones/publicadigital/libros/222c/destierro_sombras.html};

authorized on the conditions observed at \S10 (Rights). Role: \S3.2,
\S3.4, \S4.3--4.4, \S6.2, \S8.3. Strongly anti-apparitionist; used at
that level, with its own conclusions attributed to it.
\item *Jaime Cuadriello, ``Cifra, signo y artilugio: el `ocho' de
Guadalupe,'' \work{Anales del Instituto de Investigaciones
Est\'eticas} 39, no.~110 (2017): 155--206,
\url{https://doi.org/10.22201/iie.18703062e.2017.1.2593}; open-access
full text at
\url{https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0185-12762017000100155}.
Role: the documented examinations of the image (\S6.2), including its
use of Jos\'e Sol Rosales, ``Dictamen de conservaci\'on de la imagen
de la Virgen de Guadalupe,'' and Jorge Guadarrama Guevara, ``Informe
sobre aspectos t\'ecnicos de conservaci\'on,'' in Fernando Ojeda
Llanes, \work{La tilma guadalupana revela sus secretos} (Mexico:
Miguel \'Angel Porr\'ua, 2005), pp.~212--222 and 230--238, neither of
which was consulted directly.
\item Fidel Gonz\'alez Fern\'andez, ``Juan Diego Cuauhtlatoatzin:
procesos de beatificaci\'on y de canonizaci\'on,'' \work{Diccionario
de Historia Cultural de la Iglesia en Am\'erica Latina},
\url{https://www.dhial.org/}. Role: procedural chronology of the cause

\end{itemize}
