## Reader and governing question

A serious Catholic reader who wants devotion, history, source
criticism, and ecclesial obedience held together. Governing question:
what occurred and was reported at Fátima in 1917 on the
contemporaneous record; what exactly the competent authorities judged
(acts, dates, wording, objects); how the documentary corpus (memoirs;
three-part secret) developed; how the Holy See interpreted and
received it through 2000–2024; and what the judgments do and do not
oblige.

## Thesis

The 1930 Leiria judgment declared the children's 1917 Cova da Iria
visions worthy of belief and permitted the cult — no more. Everything
later (memoirs, secret, consecrations, canonizations, the 2000
interpretation) has its own object, date, and authority. Approved
private revelation invites prudent human assent (fides humana), adds
nothing to public Revelation, and its evangelical center is
conversion, penance, the Rosary, reparation, and the Immaculate
Heart. Material disagreements in the record (above all the October
war statement) are preserved, not harmonized.

## Included

- The six 1917 apparitions and the solar phenomenon, bounded to
  contemporaneous witnesses (parish-priest interrogations from c. 27
  May 1917; Formigão interrogations; O Século 15 Oct 1917 and
  Ilustração Portuguesa 29 Oct 1917 by Avelino de Almeida), via the
  Sanctuary's critical *Seleção de documentos (1917–1930)*;
- the diocesan process: provisão of 3 May 1922, sworn interrogation
  of Lucia 8 July 1924, commission report 13 April 1930, and the
  Carta Pastoral of 13 October 1930 with its exact operative clauses
  in Portuguese;
- memoirs' late composition (1935, 1937, 1941×2) and the secret's
  textual history: parts 1–2 written 31 Aug 1941 (annotated 8 Dec
  1941), circulated publicly from the early 1940s; part 3 written 3
  Jan 1944 at Tuy, custody 1944–2000, published 26 June 2000 in the
  CDF's *Message of Fatima* (read in full), with the 1960-date answer
  ("It was not Our Lady...") and the Fourth Memoir "etc." annotation;
- the 2000 interpretation: Sodano announcement, Ratzinger commentary
  at exact wording, Lucia's 12 May 1982 letter;
- the consecration question: 1917/1925/1929 reported requests; acts
  of 31 Oct 1942, 7 Jul 1952 (Sacro vergente anno), 7 Jun 1981, 13
  May 1982, 25 Mar 1984; Lucia's 8 Nov 1989 letter and the CDF's
  closure sentence; the 25 Mar 2022 act of Francis (Russia and
  Ukraine named);
- reception: papal pilgrimages (1967, 1982, 1991, 2000, 2010, 2017),
  beatification 13 May 2000, canonization 13 May 2017, Lucia declared
  Venerable 22 June 2023; the 21 May 2016 communiqué on the Dollinger
  claims quoted exactly;
- current standing under the 2024 DDF norms: six conclusions, n. 23
  rule, legacy decisions retained in their historical formulas;
  bounded negative search of the DDF documents index (no Fátima act
  after 2000).

## Excluded or bounded

- No certification of a physical mechanism for the solar phenomenon;
  no adjudication of individual witnesses; no scientific claims;
- 1915 reports, 1916 angel cycle, 1921 experience, Pontevedra
  1925–1926, Tuy 1929 treated as reported and received, never placed
  inside the 1930 operative clause;
- no integral reproduction of memoirs, prayers, or modern
  translations; no prayer text printed for recitation;
- "fourth secret," date-setting, and automatic-salvation theories
  only insofar as controlling public sources answer them (2000
  dossier; 2016 communiqué);
- no declaration about the "conversion of Russia," no period-of-peace
  calculation, no political application;
- archival claims of the 1980s consecration controversy not examined;
  the position of record is reported as such.

## Material discrepancies preserved

- **October war statement:** parish-priest interrogation of 16 Oct
  1917 records "a guerra acaba ainda hoje"; Formigão's 19 Oct
  interrogation presses the failure and records Lucia's "Não sei...
  Talvez não entendesse bem a Senhora"; the 1941 memoir gives "the
  war is going to end." Published with the 2000 commentary's
  interior-vision frame as the controlling category; no silent
  harmonization.
- **Secret chronology:** 1917 records prove a secret existed and a
  miracle was promised three months ahead; the detailed wording dates
  from 1941/1944. The study refuses to read the 1941 text as a 1917
  stenograph.
- **1981 entrustment date:** the 2000 introduction prints both "7
  June" and "7 May" for the 1981 act; reproduced as found, Pentecost
  was 7 June 1981.
- **Lucia's birth registration:** 28 March 1907 born, registered 22
  March; per her Fifth Memoir as cited by the Sanctuary.
- **19 August (Valinhos) day** rests on the memoir stratum; the fact
  of the August apparition is contemporaneous (27 Aug 1917
  interrogation).
