# Fátima — corpus and status record (controlling inventory)

Provider: Anthropic Claude. This record is the controlling inventory
required by the apparition-judgment addendum of
`guidance/theology/mariology.md` for `theology/mariology/fatima`. It
is updated before any narrative status change in the publication.

## Corpus definition

Single-event study. The included event corpus is exactly one dossier:
the reported apparitions at the Cova da Iria, parish of Fátima,
diocese of Leiria, 13 May – 13 October 1917 (with the 19 August
Valinhos encounter), including the reported solar phenomenon of 13
October 1917. No "all approved apparitions" claim is made anywhere;
no master registry is asserted. Related phenomena (1915 reports, 1916
angel cycle, 1921 experience, Pontevedra 1925–1926, Tuy 1929) are
inside the narrative scope but **outside** the judged corpus of the
1930 act, and the publication says so wherever they appear.

## Status terms used

- *worthy of belief* (dignas de crédito) — the 1930 act's own
  formula; legacy positive diocesan judgment; not relabeled under the
  2024 taxonomy.
- *permitted cult* — the 1930 act's second clause.
- *received without fact-judgment* — Pontevedra, Tuy, angel cycle,
  memoirs, secret texts: published, interpreted, and built upon by
  the Holy See without a formal supernaturality judgment.
- *fides humana* — the assent invited by approved private revelation.
- *reported message* — default register for all attributed words.
- *project synthesis* — the study's own judgment, confined to labeled
  blocks in the publication, unreviewed, carrying no authority beyond
  its argument. It is never a status term and never changes the
  controlling status recorded below.

## Event dossier (as of 2026-07-25)

- **Stable identity:** Our Lady of Fátima; Cova da Iria, parish of
  Fátima; six reported apparitions, 13 May, 13 June, 13 July, 19
  August (after the 13 August detention), 13 September, 13 October
  1917.
- **Jurisdiction:** Diocese of Leiria (restored 1918; now
  Leiria-Fátima), Latin Church, Portugal.
- **Claimed type:** visions of Our Lady with reported words (Lucia
  the interlocutor throughout the 1917 interrogations); public solar
  phenomenon on 13 October. The received detail that Francisco saw
  but did not hear was not separately re-verified and the publication
  does not assert it.
- **Recipients:** Lucia dos Santos (1907–2005), Francisco Marto
  (1908–1919), Jacinta Marto (1910–1920).
- **Witness corpus:** parish-priest interrogations from c. 27 May
  1917; Formigão interrogations Sept–Oct 1917; contemporary press
  (O Século 23 Jul and 15 Oct 1917; Ilustração Portuguesa 29 Oct
  1917); 1922–1930 process records; retrospective memoirs 1935–1944.
  Critical witness: DCF *Seleção de documentos (1917–1930)*, Fátima
  2013 (docs. 1, 3, 4, 13, 14, 18, 21, 68, 120, 133, 134 used).
- **Authority / act / date / language / locus / source:** Bishop José
  Alves Correia da Silva; Carta Pastoral sobre o culto de Nossa
  Senhora de Fátima; 13 October 1930; Portuguese; closing clauses
  ("Havemos por bem 1º declarar como dignas de crédito as visões das
  crianças na Cova da Iria, freguesia de Fátima, desta Diocese, nos
  dias 13 de maio a outubro de 1917; 2º permitir oficialmente o culto
  de Nossa Senhora de Fátima"); DCF Seleção, Doc. 133 (= DCF II, Doc.
  11), printed pp. 550–551.
- **Procedural regime and operative formula:** pre-1978
  diocesan-ordinary discernment; provisão 3 May 1922 (commission,
  canonical process); sworn interrogation of Lucia 8 July 1924
  (Porto); commission report 13 April 1930; operative formula =
  worthy-of-belief declaration + official permission of cult.
- **Exact object judged:** the children's visions at the Cova da
  Iria, 13 May–October 1917, and the public cult. Not judged: solar
  mechanism; verbal inerrancy of reported words; 1916 angel cycle;
  1915 reports; 1921 experience; Pontevedra/Tuy; memoirs; secret
  texts; later promises/devotional accretions; translations.
- **Later acts and their objects:** custody of part 3 (Holy Office
  from 4 Apr 1957); readings by John XXIII (1959) and Paul VI (1965)
  without publication; CDF publication + interpretation 26 June 2000
  (Message of Fatima); consecrations 31 Oct 1942, 7 Jul 1952, 7 Jun
  1981 / 13 May 1982 / 25 Mar 1984, 25 Mar 2022; Lucia's 8 Nov 1989
  confirmation letter; beatification 13 May 2000; canonization 13 May
  2017; Lucia Venerable 22 Jun 2023; Press Office communiqué 21 May
  2016 (publication complete).
- **Current controlling status:** positive legacy diocesan judgment
  of 1930 in force; CDF 2000 interpretation controlling for the
  secret; no DDF act under the 2024 norms concerns Fátima (DDF
  documents index checked 2026-07-25, current at that date to 2 July
  2026, carrying exactly one Fátima entry — the 26 June 2000
  dossier); no superseding, restricting, or reversing act located.
  **No act restrains discussion of the Fátima secret**, and the
  competent dicastery published it with a commentary that invites
  reflection; the study interprets on that footing. Not established
  by any act: obligation of faith; supernatural-origin certainty;
  event-level authentication of every text or claim; and no
  determination of the vision's literary genre, of the identity of
  the Bishop dressed in White with any particular pope, or of the
  meaning of the third part beyond the 2000 commentary's own
  self-described "attempt."
- **Reported-message themes checked:** Rosary at every 1917
  apparition (contemp. records); conversion/penance; reparation;
  Immaculate Heart (memoir stratum + 2000 dossier); conditional
  Russia prophecy (1941 text); hell vision (1941 text); war-end
  statement (contemp. records; discrepancy preserved). Cautions: no
  date-setting; no fatalism (2000 commentary, quoted).
- **Source / translation / rights / disagreement / gap / review
  status:** witnesses and rights in `source-audit.md` and the
  publication's References; translations editorial and marked;
  disagreements preserved (war statement; 7 May/June 1981 slip; birth
  registration); gaps recorded (1930 imprint, 1942 imprints, archive
  access, full DCF series); review = internal only, no independent or
  ecclesiastical review.
