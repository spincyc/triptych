Witnesses are listed with role and rights notes; access date for all

\subsection*{Primary documentary corpus, 1917--1930}

\begin{itemize}
\item *\work{Documenta\c{c}\~ao Cr\'itica de F\'atima: Sele\c{c}\~ao
de documentos (1917--1930)} (F\'atima: Santu\'ario de F\'atima, 2013;
ISBN 978-972-8213-91-6; \copyright~Santu\'ario de F\'atima; scientific
sponsorship of the Centro de Estudos de Hist\'oria Religiosa,
Universidade Cat\'olica Portuguesa), distributed without charge by
the Sanctuary as a 653-page PDF:
\url{https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/fontes/F001_DCF_selecao.pdf}
(documentation page
\url{https://www.fatima.pt/en/documentacao/f001-documentacao-critica-de-fatima-selecao-de-documentos-1917-1930}).
Role: critical witness for the 1917 interrogations (Docs.~1, 3, 14,
18), the press dossier (Docs.~4, 13, 21), the process acts (Docs.~68,
120), and the 1930 pastoral letter (Doc.~133) with its promulgation
account (Doc.~134). The underlying 1917--1930 documents are public
domain; the edition's transcription and apparatus remain the
Sanctuary's, quoted briefly with citation.
\item *Jos\'e Alves Correia da Silva, Bishop of Leiria, \work{Carta
Pastoral sobre o culto de Nossa Senhora de F\'atima}, 13 October
1930, as printed in the above selection, Doc.~133 (published earlier
as DCF~II, Doc.~11). Role: the operative approval act, quoted exactly
in \S1. Ceilings recorded in \S3.3.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Holy See acts and documents}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, \work{The Message
of Fatima} (26 June 2000), English text, read in full:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20000626_message-fatima_en.html}.
Role: controlling dossier and interpretation---Bertone introduction
(custody history, 1984 act, 1989 letter), the secret's three parts in
translation with facsimiles, John Paul II's letter of 19 April 2000,
the 27 April 2000 conversation record (the 1960-date answer), the
Sodano announcement of 13 May 2000, and Cardinal Ratzinger's
theological commentary, quoted at its exact wording.
\item *Holy See Press Office, ``Communiqu\'e on articles regarding
the Third Secret of Fatima,'' 21 May 2016:
\url{https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2016/05/21/160521b.html}.
Role: Benedict XVI's denial of the Dollinger claims; completeness of
the 2000 publication.
\item *Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, \work{Norms for
Proceeding in the Discernment of Alleged Supernatural Phenomena}
(given 17 May 2024; approved 4 May 2024; effective 19 May 2024):
\url{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240517_norme-fenomeni-soprannaturali_en.html}.
Role: current procedural law; six conclusions; n.~23 rule; its
presentation's citation of the F\'atima 2000 explanation of
approval. Checked negative: the DDF documents index
(\url{https://www.doctrinafidei.va/en/documenti.html}) lists no
F\'atima act after 2000.
\item *Francis, Homily, Holy Mass with the rite of canonization of
Blesseds Francisco Marto and Jacinta Marto, Shrine of Our Lady of
F\'atima, 13 May 2017:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/homilies/2017/documents/papa-francesco_20170513_omelia-pellegrinaggio-fatima.html}.
Role: canonization record and its homiletic framing.
\item *Francis, \work{Act of Consecration to the Immaculate Heart of
Mary}, 25 March 2022:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/prayers/documents/20220325-atto-consacrazione-cuoredimaria.html};
with the Letter to the Bishops, 21 March 2022:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/letters/2022/documents/20220321-lettera-consacrazione-cuoredimaria.html}.
Role: the 2022 act's text (Russia and Ukraine named) and its
self-description.
\item Pius XII, Radio message to Portugal with the prayer of
consecration to the Immaculate Heart, 31 October 1942 (Italian):
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/it/speeches/1942/documents/hf_p-xii_spe_19421031_immaculata.html};
\work{Sacro vergente anno}, 7 July 1952 (Italian):
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/it/apost_letters/documents/hf_p-xii_apl_19520707_sacro-vergente-anno.html}.
Role: the 1942 and 1952 consecration texts.
\item John Paul II, Homily at the beatification of Francisco and
Jacinta, F\'atima, 13 May 2000:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/homilies/2000/documents/hf_jp-ii_hom_20000513_beatification-fatima.html}.
Role: beatification record (identity confirmed; not quoted).
\item Benedict XVI, Homily at the Esplanade of the Shrine, F\'atima,
13 May 2010:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/homilies/2010/documents/hf_ben-xvi_hom_20100513_fatima.html}.
Role: the ``prophetic mission'' sentence, quoted exactly.
\item Paul VI, \work{Signum magnum}, 13 May 1967, Vatican web text
(cited for date and fact of the anniversary act; not quoted).
\item Dicastery for the Causes of Saints, Decree on the heroic
virtues of the Servant of God Maria Lucia of Jesus and of the
Immaculate Heart, 22 June 2023, Portuguese text distributed by the
Sanctuary:
\url{https://www.fatima.pt/files/upload/documentos_do_magisterio/20023-06-22decretosobreasvirtudesheroicasdairmaluciadejesusedoimaculadocoracao.pdf}.
Role: competent summary of Lucia's life, Pontevedra and Tuy, the
memoirs' dating, and the 1984 fulfillment sentence.
\end{itemize}
