\section{References}

Witnesses are listed with role and rights notes; access date for all web
loci is 2026-07-25 unless stated. Stable source identities for the

\subsection*{Defining and formulating acts}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Council of Ephesus (431), extracts from the acts with Cyril's
second letter to Nestorius, the third letter with the twelve anathemas
and Nestorius's counter-anathemas, and the decree of deposition; the
Formula of Reunion in Cyril's letter to John of Antioch (433) among the
Chalcedonian documents; Chalcedon (451) and Constantinople II (553), in
\work{The Seven Ecumenical Councils}, tr. Henry R. Percival, NPNF series
2, vol.~14 (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1900; public domain), New
Advent transcriptions: \url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3810.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3811.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3812.htm}. Role: definition-text
and reception witness (divine motherhood; \term{aeiparthenos}), with the
editor's bracketed note on the lacuna at the anathemas disclosed in the
Notes.
\item *Lateran Council of 649, canons 1--4 (DS 500--504, with canon 3 =
DS 503 / DB 256), Latin with dual numbering at
\url{http://patristica.net/denzinger/enchiridion-symbolorum.html};
English clauses from Roy J. Deferrari, \work{The Sources of Catholic
Dogma} (St. Louis: Herder, 1957; rights unresolved, quoted in short
attributed clauses), \url{http://patristica.net/denzinger/}. Role:
formulation witness (perpetual virginity) and its Monothelite context
(the same witness's DS 496--498). The underlying seventh-century Latin is
public domain; the online Latin witness does not identify its print
edition and its query mark at DS 503 is disclosed in the Notes.
\item *Denzinger, \work{Enchiridion symbolorum}, further loci in the same
Latin witness: DS 291 (Leo's Tome, virginity preserved in conceiving and
bearing), 852 (Lyons II, 1274), 1337 (Florence, 1442), 1400 and
1425--1426 (Sixtus IV, \work{Cum praeexcelsa} and \work{Grave nimis}),
1516 (Trent, Session V, can.~6), 1880 (Paul IV, \work{Cum quorumdam
hominum}, 1555), 2015--2017 (Alexander VII, \work{Sollicitudo omnium
ecclesiarum}, 8 December 1661), 2803--2804 (the 1854 definition and
sanction). Role: Latin textual control. The witness's date for
\work{Cum praeexcelsa} (27 February 1477, ``1476 st.~cur.'') and its
running heading ``Pie IV'' at DS 1880 are recorded as found.
\item *Pius IX, \work{Ineffabilis Deus} (8 December 1854; DS
2803--2804), English (translator unidentified on the witness) at
\url{https://www.papalencyclicals.net/pius09/p9ineff.htm}. Role:
definition text; the constitution's own argument structure (liturgical
argument, Roman doctrine, papal sanctions, Trent, testimonies of
Tradition, interpreters of Scripture, the Annunciation, Eve, biblical
figures, the mind of the bishops, the definition); and its historical
survey (Trent Sess.~V, Sixtus IV, Alexander VII 1661, \work{Ubi primum}
of 2 February 1849), with its Latin footnotes. Checked negative: the
Vatican web English page
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-ix/en/documents/18541208-costituzione-apostolica-ineffabilis-deus.html})
delivers a title shell without body text; the Latin original is public
domain.
\item *Pius XII, \work{Munificentissimus Deus} (1 November 1950; AAS 42
[1950] 753--771; DS 3903--3904), Vatican English (48 numbered
paragraphs) and Latin web texts:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/en/apost_constitutions/documents/hf_p-xii_apc_19501101_munificentissimus-deus.html}
and the parallel \texttt{/la/} page. Role: definition text (\S44),
sanction (\S45), the bond with 1854 (\S\S4--5), consultation record
(\S\S11--12), liturgical argument (\S\S17--20), Damascene (\S21),
scholastic survey (\S\S24--37), scriptural foundation (\S\S38--39), and
definition rationale (\S\S40--42).
\item *Pius XII, \work{Deiparae Virginis Mariae} (1 May 1946), Vatican
English:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-xii_enc_01051946_deiparae-virginis-mariae.html}.
Role: the consultation document itself (\S\S2, 4) and, in the page's own
editorial note, its reserved circulation and later publication in AAS.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Patristic, scholastic, and medieval witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Ignatius of Antioch, \work{Epistle to the Ephesians} 7, 18--19,
and Irenaeus, \work{Against Heresies} III.22.4 and V.19.1, in the
Ante-Nicene Fathers (Roberts--Donaldson--Coxe; public domain), New
Advent: \url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0104.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103322.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103519.htm}. Role: second-century
substance of the divine motherhood and the Eve--Mary parallel. The
Ignatian page presents the shorter recension.
\item Jerome, \work{The Perpetual Virginity of Blessed Mary} (against
Helvidius, 383), NPNF 2/6 translation at
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3007.htm}. Role: the
fourth-century arguments quoted in Chapter 4 (\S\S6, 12, 15--17, 19,
21--23), including the treatise's own invective on marriage, reported and
not endorsed.
\item Vincent of L\'erins, \work{Commonitory}, chs.~XII, XV, XXIII, and
Edgar C. S. Gibson's introduction to John Cassian, both in NPNF series
2, vol.~11 (public domain), Christian Classics Ethereal Library:
\url{https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf211}. Role: Latin reception of
\term{Theotokos} (c.~434); the rule of development restated in 1854; and
the reported occasion and date of Cassian's seven books against
Nestorius.
\item Bernard of Clairvaux, letter to the canons of Lyons on the
Conception of S. Mary (c.~1140; commonly cited as Ep.~174), in Samuel J.
Eales, \work{Some Letters of Saint Bernard, Abbot of Clairvaux} (London:
John Hodges, 1904; public domain), where it is numbered XLV:
\url{https://ccel.org/ccel/bernard/letters}. Role: the classic medieval
objection to the feast, with its closing submission to the See of Rome.
\item Thomas Aquinas, \work{Summa theologiae} III, qq.~27 and 28
(Dominican Fathers translation, 1920; public domain), New Advent:
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4027.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4028.htm}. Role: the Thomistic limit
on the Immaculate Conception (q.~27, aa.~1--2), his report of the Roman
practice on the feast (q.~27, a.~2 ad~3), and his four reasons against
Helvidius (q.~28, a.~3); ST III, q.~35 cited at generality only.
\item John Duns Scotus, \work{Ordinatio} III, d.~3, q.~1: standard
scholarly reference for preservative redemption; no online witness
checked, and no quotation made.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Apocrypha, lists, and dating scholarship}

\begin{itemize}
\item ``Apocryphal Works on the Assumption of Mary'' (the
\work{Transitus} literature, including the account ascribed to John the
Theologian), Ante-Nicene Fathers translation at
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0832.htm}. Role: reception
evidence only; no historical value claimed.
\item \work{Decretum Gelasianum de libris recipiendis et non
recipiendis}, part V, English translation of Ernst von Dobsch\"utz's
edition (Texte und Untersuchungen 38.4, 1912) at
\url{https://www.tertullian.org/decretum_eng.htm}. Role: the Roman list
placing ``the book which is called the Assumption of holy Mary'' among
the apocrypha. Date and authorship disputed; the Denzinger witness (DS
350--354) abbreviates the apocrypha list.
\item Piotr Towarek, ``Prayer `Sub Tuum praesidium': Time of Origin,
Place in Liturgy and Reception in Musical Culture. Outline of the
Issues,'' \work{Vox Patrum} 80 (2021), pp.~239--268, DOI
10.31743/vp.12929:
\url{https://czasopisma.kul.pl/index.php/vp/article/view/12929}
(abstract read). Role: the palaeographic dating dispute over P.\,Ryl. III
470.
\item \work{Protevangelium of James}: cited as apocryphal reception
evidence for the Epiphanian solution and for virginity in birth; no
witness quoted.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Documents of other communions (cited as their own acts)}

\begin{itemize}
\item Patriarchal and Synodal Encyclical of Constantinople (Anthimus
VII, 1895), art.~XIII, English text at
\url{http://orthodoxinfo.com/ecumenism/encyc_1895.aspx}. Role: the
Orthodox objection to the 1854 definition. Private site; translator and
Greek edition unidentified; uncollated.
\item \work{The Smalcald Articles} (Luther, 1537), Part I, art.~IV, tr.
F. Bente and W. H. T. Dau, \work{Triglot Concordia} (St. Louis:
Concordia, 1921; public domain):
\url{https://www.gutenberg.org/files/273/273-h/273-h.htm}. Role: the
Lutheran confessional retention of the divine motherhood and perpetual
virginity; the edition's bracket convention is noted at the point of use.
\item Anglican--Roman Catholic International Commission, \work{Mary:
Grace and Hope in Christ} (the Seattle Statement, agreed 2004, published
2005), \S\S9, 58--63, on the Holy See's Christian-unity site:
\url{https://www.christianunity.va/content/unitacristiani/en/dialoghi/sezione-occidentale/comunione-anglicana/dialogo/arcic-ii/en.html}.
Role: the most developed ecumenical treatment of the two modern dogmas,
and the witness for the \work{Sub tuum praesidium} text quoted in
Chapter 3. Status: an agreed statement of a bilateral dialogue
commission, not an act of either communion's magisterium.
\end{itemize}
