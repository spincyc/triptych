\section{References}

Witnesses are listed with role and rights notes; access date for all web
loci is 2026-07-25 unless stated. Stable source identities for the
starred entries are registered in the repository source library; this
publication's bindings are in \texttt{research/source-bindings.toml}.

\subsection*{Defining and formulating acts}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Council of Ephesus (431), extracts from the acts with Cyril's
second letter to Nestorius, and Chalcedon (451) and Constantinople II
(553) documents, in \work{The Seven Ecumenical Councils}, tr. Henry R.
Percival, NPNF series 2, vol.~14 (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons,
1900; public domain), New Advent transcriptions:
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3810.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3811.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3812.htm}. Role: definition-text
and reception witness (divine motherhood; \term{aeiparthenos}).
\item *Lateran Council of 649, canon 3, Latin with dual numbering (DS
503 / DB 256) at
\url{http://patristica.net/denzinger/enchiridion-symbolorum.html};
English clauses from Roy J. Deferrari, \work{The Sources of Catholic
Dogma} (St. Louis: Herder, 1957; rights unresolved, quoted in short
attributed clauses), \url{http://patristica.net/denzinger/}. Role:
formulation witness (perpetual virginity). The underlying seventh-century
Latin is public domain; the online Latin witness does not identify its
print edition and its query mark at DS 503 is disclosed in the Notes.
\item *Pius IX, \work{Ineffabilis Deus} (8 December 1854; DS
2803--2804), English (translator unidentified on the witness) at
\url{https://www.papalencyclicals.net/pius09/p9ineff.htm}. Role:
definition text and the constitution's own historical survey (Trent
Sess.~V, Sixtus IV, Alexander VII 1661, the 1849 consultation). Checked
negative: the Vatican web English page
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-ix/en/documents/18541208-costituzione-apostolica-ineffabilis-deus.html})
delivers a title shell without body text; the Latin original is public
domain.
\item *Pius XII, \work{Munificentissimus Deus} (1 November 1950; AAS 42
[1950] 753--771; DS 3903--3904), Vatican English (48 numbered
paragraphs) and Latin web texts:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/en/apost_constitutions/documents/hf_p-xii_apc_19501101_munificentissimus-deus.html}
and the parallel \texttt{/la/} page. Role: definition text (\S44),
sanction (\S45), consultation record (\S\S11--12), and historical survey
(\S\S17--41).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Current teaching and framing}

\begin{itemize}
\item *\work{Catechism of the Catholic Church}, English, Holy See web
(IntraText) pages for \S\S484--511 and 963--975:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P1K.HTM},
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P2C.HTM}. Role: current
catechetical framing; its apparatus supplied the checked DS loci (251,
427 chain, 503, 2803, 3903).
\item *Second Vatican Council, \work{Lumen gentium} (21 November 1964),
ch.~8 (\S\S52--69), Vatican English web text:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html}.
Role: conciliar synthesis and cooperation-language control (LG 53,
57, 59--62, 66).
\item *Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, \work{Mater Populi
Fidelis} (approved 7 October 2025; published 4 November 2025), Vatican
English web text:
\url{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20251104_mater-populi-fidelis_en.html}.
Role: current controlling judgment on Co-redemptrix (\S22), mediation
language (\S27), and Mediatrix of All Graces (\S\S67--69).
\item First Vatican Council, \work{Dei Filius} (1870), ch.~3 (DS 3011),
English presentation at
\url{https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum20.htm} (translation
identity not stated on the witness; paraphrased, not quoted). Role:
criterion of dogma.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Scripture, patristic, and scholastic witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item Douay-Rheims Bible (Challoner revision; public domain), drbo.org:
\url{http://drbo.org/chapter/49001.htm} (Lk 1),
\url{http://drbo.org/chapter/01003.htm} (Gen 3, with its annotation on
\term{ipsa conteret}). Role: English Scripture quotations.
\item Thomas Aquinas, \work{Summa theologiae} III, q.~27 (Dominican
Fathers translation, 1920; public domain), New Advent:
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4027.htm}. Role: the Thomistic
limit on the Immaculate Conception, quoted exactly; ST III, qq.~28 and
35 cited at generality only.
\item Jerome, \work{The Perpetual Virginity of Blessed Mary} (against
Helvidius, 383), NPNF 2/6 translation at
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3007.htm}. Role: identity
witness for the fourth-century controversy; not quoted.
\item John Duns Scotus, \work{Ordinatio} III, d.~3, q.~1: standard
scholarly reference for preservative redemption; no online witness
checked, and no quotation made.
\end{itemize}
