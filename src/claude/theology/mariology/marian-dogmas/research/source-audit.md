## Scripture, patristic, and scholastic witnesses — checked

- Douay-Rheims (Challoner): Lk 1:28 ("Hail, full of grace…"), Lk 1:34
  ("How shall this be done, because I know not man?"), Lk 1:43 ("the
  mother of my Lord"), Gen 3:15 ("I will put enmities…") with the site's
  own annotation on "she shall crush" (*ipsa*); checked at
  drbo.org/chapter/49001.htm and drbo.org/chapter/01003.htm. Gal 4:4 and
  Jn 1:14 cited from the same version without block quotation.
- Thomas Aquinas, ST III q.27 aa.1–2 (Dominican Fathers tr., New
  Advent, newadvent.org/summa/4027.htm): quotations "she would not have
  needed redemption and salvation which is by Christ" (a.2 corpus
  region) and "the Blessed Virgin did indeed contract original sin, but
  was cleansed therefrom before her birth from the womb" (a.2). ST III
  q.28 and q.35 cited at generality only (not fetched).
- Jerome, Perpetual Virginity of Blessed Mary (against Helvidius):
  identity confirmed at newadvent.org/fathers/3007.htm (title page
  only); not quoted.
- Scotus, Ordinatio III d.3 q.1: standard reference, no witness checked,
  no quotation; *potuit, decuit, fecit* labeled a later formula.
- Protevangelium of James: cited as apocryphal reception evidence only,
  no witness quoted.

## Claim-to-source rule

Every quotation in the publication appears in the tables or lists above.
Historical claims inside the two papal constitutions are labeled in the
prose and notes as reported on the constitutions' authority. DS numbers
in the publication rest on the CCC's own apparatus (nn. 135, 145, 154,
506) plus the dual-numbered Latin Denzinger witness; the anathema-level
concordance for Constantinople II is bounded as stated above. The
letter-level range DS 250–251 for Cyril's second letter is standard
reference usage; the online Latin transcription's marginal-number
placement around 250/251 is loose (the Theotokos sentence sits between
its "250" and "251" marks) and was not relied on — the sentence-level
assignment DS 251 follows CCC 495 n.145.
