\subsection*{The 1851 judgment and its witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Philibert de Bruillard, Bishop of Grenoble, doctrinal
\term{mandement} on the event of La Salette, 19 September 1851.
Role: the operative approval act, quoted at \S1 and analyzed at
\S4. No French printing of the integral act was located. Witnesses
used: the Sanctuary's French transcription of article~1 (below); the
Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith's 2024 citation of the
operative clause (below); the English of the whole article and of
the preamble in the 1852 and 1854 volumes (below); and the
eight-article structure in the 1854 Italian translation of the
pilgrim's manual (below). The 1851 text is public domain.
\item *\work{A Pilgrimage to La Salette; or, a Critical Examination
of All the Facts Connected with the Alleged Apparition} (London,
1852), read in the Internet Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/apilgrimagetola00nortgoog}).
Role: documentary carrier of the mandement's preamble and narrative
in English translation (Appendix, pp.~69--71), of the July 1851
writing and 18 July delivery of the secrets (pp.~28--29), and of the
mandement of 1 May 1852 (pp.~30--32). Anonymous English Catholic
apologetic review article; public domain; used at that ceiling.
\item *William Bernard Ullathorne, \work{The Holy Mountain of La
Salette: A Pilgrimage of the Year 1854} (London: Richardson, 1854),
read in the Internet Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/holymountainlas00ullagoog}).
Role: the operative articles in English (pp.~120--121); the
children's recital in parallel French, \term{patois}, and English
(pp.~35--43); the interrogation answers on the secret (pp.~44--46);
the depositions of 27 November 1847 (pp.~30--31); the superioress's
1847 opinion (pp.~32--33); the Ars episode (pp.~136--140). Public
domain (Ullathorne d.~1889); pro-La Salette pilgrimage narrative
reproducing diocesan documents in translation, used at that ceiling.
\item *Pierre-Joseph Rousselot, \work{Manuale del pellegrino a
Nostra Signora di La Salette} (Italian translation of the
\work{Manuel du pèlerin}, 1854), read in the Internet Archive scan
of the Bavarian State Library copy
(\url{https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_ipMnF-iMgtcC}). Role: the
mandement's four considerations and eight articles in Italian
translation (pp.~42--47). Public domain; a translation of a
diocesan pilgrim's manual, used at that ceiling.
\item *\work{Vie de M.~Rousselot, professeur de théologie au grand
séminaire de Grenoble} (Grenoble, 1866), read in the Internet
Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/viedemrousselot00abbgoog}). Role:
the inventory of Roman grants of 1852 with their dates
(pp.~138--139); Ginoulhiac's acts of 1854 and the circular of 1857
(pp.~145--146); the operative French clause of the judgment inside
the biographer's own sentence (p.~415). Public domain; partisan
diocesan biography, used at that ceiling.
\item \work{Le miracle de La Salette} (1855), read in the Internet
Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_AbQAAAAAcAAJ}). Role: the
existence of the 1855 civil litigation at Grenoble (pp.~86--87)
only. Public domain; openly hostile volume, used solely for that
fact and for nothing evaluative.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Holy See acts read in the official gazettes}

\begin{itemize}
\item *Sacred Congregation of Rites, rescript \term{Portus Aloisii},
12 May 1877: \work{Acta Sanctae Sedis} 11 (1878), pp.~512--514, read
in the Holy See's archive PDF
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ass/documents/ASS-11-1878-ocr.pdf}).
Role: the \term{fide solum humana} answer naming La Salette (\S8.3,
\S10.2); the limits on feast rank and on litanies of the apparition.
\item *Sacred Congregation of Rites, rescript \term{Maurianen.}, 7

(\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ass/documents/ASS-13-1880-ocr.pdf}).
Role: confraternities, altars, and oratories under the name of Our
Lady of La Salette, subject to the earlier decree's condition.
\item *Sacred Congregation for Religious, \term{Decretum quo
approbantur ad decennium constitutiones Congregationis
Missionariorum ``de la Salette''}, 29 January 1909, confirmed by
Pius~X 1 February 1909: \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 1 (1909),

\work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 7 (1915), p.~594
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-07-1915-ocr.pdf}).

saving clause.
\item *Supreme Sacred Congregation of the Holy Office,
\term{Declaratio circa opus quoddam}, 12 April 1916, and Sacred
Congregation of the Index, decree of 5 June 1916: \work{Acta

\item *Supreme Sacred Congregation of the Holy Office, decree
\term{Damnatur opusculum}, 9 May 1923, approved by Pius~XI the same

\item *Sacred Congregation for Religious, definitive approval of the
constitutions of the Missionaries ``de la Salette,'' 7 June 1926:
\work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 18 (1926), p.~314
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-18-1926-ocr.pdf}).

\work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 38 (1946), pp.~155--156

(\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-53-1961-ocr.pdf}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The secret literature}

\begin{itemize}
\item *\work{Le secret complet de la Salette, publié par Mélanie de
la Salette, Sœur Marie de la Croix, précédé d'une préface, suivi des
dernières lettres de Mélanie et d'une lettre de Mgr Zola, évêque de
Lecce} (Louvain: Imprimerie de J.~Lefever, 1881), read in the
Internet Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/LeSecretCompletDeLaSaletteMelanieDeLaSalette}),
whose page images were collated line by line against every French
sentence quoted from it. Role: the principal text of this study's
\S6---the narrative of 1878 and the secret it prints, published and
analyzed there---and, documentarily, the subscription dated
Castellammare 21 November 1878, the \term{Nihil obstat: imprimatur}
of 15 November 1879, Mélanie's letters of 25 March and 9 May 1880,
the anonymous \term{avant-propos}, and Bishop Zola's letter of 24
May 1880 supplying the custody chronology of \S5.4. Reprint of the
1879 Lecce booklet, which was not reached; publication of a text
approved by no competent authority and condemned in its 1922
reprint, quoted here neither as true nor as revealed (\S5.1, \S6.2,
Appendix~A). The 1881 imprint is public domain; the scan is a
user-contributed copy of an identified imprint whose searchable text
layer is poor and was used only for navigation.
\item *I.~Roubaud, \work{La vérité sur le secret de Mélanie
ex-bergère de La Salette}, 2nd~edition (Louvain: Imprimerie de
J.~Lefever, 1882), read in the Internet Archive scan
(\url{https://archive.org/details/LeVrittSurLeSecretDeMLanieExBergereDeLaSaletteAbbIROUBAUDLouvain1882})
and collated against its page images. Role: a partisan defence of
the secret, used only for what it concedes against its own
case---the 1882 press controversy, the Congregation of the Index's
refusal to condemn, the Holy Office's taking up of the matter, and
the letter of Cardinal Prospero Caterini of 14 August 1880 (\S5.6,
\S6.8, \S6.11)---and never for its arguments. Public domain; carries
no \term{imprimatur}, only the author's own submission formula.
\end{itemize}
