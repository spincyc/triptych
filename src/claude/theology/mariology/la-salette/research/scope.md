## Reader and governing question

A serious Catholic reader who wants devotion, source criticism,
juridical precision, and ecclesial obedience held together.
Governing question: what was reported at La Salette on 19 September
1846 on the nearest reachable record; how the public discourse is
transmitted and where its strata differ; what exactly Bishop
Philibert de Bruillard judged on 19 September 1851, in which words
and with what object; what happened to the secrets, from the two
manuscripts written for Pius IX in July 1851 through Mélanie
Calvat's expanded publications to the Holy Office acts of 1915,
1916, and 1923 and the juridic question they leave open; how the
shrine, the Missionaries, the liturgy, and the popes received the
event; and what all of that obliges and leaves open, including the
standing of the dossier under the 2024 DDF norms.

## Thesis

The 1851 mandement judged one apparition on one day to two children
to bear all the marks of truth, declared the faithful *fondés à la
croire indubitable et certaine*, authorized cult, reserved
devotional publications to written episcopal approval, and forbade
public contest in the diocese — no more. The Holy See's own
description of its position, in a Rites rescript of 12 May 1877 that
names La Salette, was neither approval nor condemnation but
permission, with assent *fide solum humana*. The secrets are a
separate stratum throughout: their existence removed an objection in
1851, their content was never judged, the expanded 1879 text is on
its own promoter's testimony a different and larger document from
the 1851 manuscript, and the only universal Roman acts on the
subject (1915, 1923) were restrictive of the literature and
expressly protective of the devotion. Whether the 1915 precept still
binds is unresolved by any located act, and this study does not
resolve it. **Posture change of 2026-07-25:** on the express
direction of the project's maintainer, this edition now publishes
and analyzes the 1879 text rather than refusing exposition, under a
prominent disclosure (section 5.1) that quotes the 1915 command,
penalties and saving clause, records the acts of 1916 and 1923,
reports the decree's present force as unresolved in both directions,
and states that nothing in the study is an ecclesiastical
interpretation, dispensation, or canonical opinion. The judgment
reached, labelled project synthesis, is that the 1879 text is not
the 1851 text.

## Material discrepancies and limits preserved

- **la main / le bras:** the 1854 witness prints «laisser aller *la
  main* de mon fils. Elle est si forte, si pesante»; the received
  custodial text «laisser aller *le bras* de mon Fils. Il est si
  fort et si pesant». Preserved as a variant; not decided.
- **conduisent / mènent** in the carters' sentence: preserved.
- **Imprimatur date — corrected 2026-07-25.** An earlier state of
  this record preserved a discrepancy between the printed formula
  (15 November 1879) and Zola's letter, whose OCR reads 18 November
  1879. Page-image collation of both pages shows the letter reads
  *le 15 novembre 1879*; the witnesses agree and the discrepancy was
  an artifact of the scan's text layer. Recorded as a correction, not
  removed.
- **1881 volume, unresolved items:** an ambiguous two-digit number at
  p. 9 ("pendant plus de 33/35 ans"), not quoted anywhere; the title
  page's "depuis 1867" for Zola's direction against his own "de 1868
  jusqu'en 1873" at p. 31.
- **Language of Mélanie's secret:** the 1847 interrogation has it in
  *patois*; the 1879 text has it *en français*. Not harmonized; the
  contradiction is the load-bearing internal datum of section 6.6–6.7
  and the 1847 answer is preferred, with reasons stated.
- **Gazette transcription defects:** AAS 7 (1915) 594 prints
  "Beconciliatricis" for *Reconciliatricis* and "cum" for the second
  *tum*; AAS 15 (1923) 288 prints the pamphlet year "1845" where the
  day-name *samedi* requires 1846; the 1854 witness prints "1840"
  for 1846 in Pra's deposition. Each recorded, bracketed, or noted;
  no page images exist in the Holy See archive files for collation.
- **AAS sweep gaps:** volume 75 (1983) is not served at the archive
  route; volumes from 99 (2007) are not published there as annual
  files. Recorded as the search boundary.
- **1966 Index notification:** its AAS 58 (1966) 445 locus is taken
  from the repository's existing source record, not re-verified here.
- **Sanctuary page availability:** the message and seers pages are no
  longer on the live site and are cited from dated Internet Archive
  captures.
