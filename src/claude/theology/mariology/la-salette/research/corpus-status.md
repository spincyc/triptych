# La Salette — corpus and status record (controlling inventory)

Provider: Anthropic Claude. This record is the controlling inventory
required by the apparition-judgment addendum of
`guidance/theology/mariology.md` for `theology/mariology/la-salette`.
It is updated before any narrative status change in the publication.

## Corpus definition

Single-event study. The included event corpus is exactly one dossier:
one reported apparition of the Blessed Virgin Mary to Maximin Giraud
and Mélanie Calvat on a mountain above the village of La Salette,
parish of La Salette, archpresbytery of Corps, diocese of Grenoble,
on 19 September 1846. There is no series, no fortnight, and no later
appearance forming part of the judged event. No "all approved
apparitions" claim is made anywhere; no master registry is asserted.

Related matters are inside the narrative scope but **outside** the
1851 operative articles except where those articles name them, and
the publication says so wherever they appear: the two secrets given
separately to the children; the two manuscripts written for Pius IX
in July 1851; Mélanie Calvat's later expanded publications; the
reported prodigies and cures named collectively in article 2; the
spring; and every later locution, writing, or claim of either seer.

Since 2026-07-25 the publication also **publishes and analyzes** the
principal text of that expanded corpus — the booklet printed at
Lecce in 1879, read in the Louvain reprint of 1881 — under the
disclosure at its section 5.1. That text is inside the study's
analytical scope and remains outside every act of approval in this
dossier: it is judged by no competent authority, was condemned in
its 1922 reprint, and is asserted by this publication neither as
true nor as revealed.

## Status terms used

- *porte en elle-même tous les caractères de la vérité* / *les
  fidèles sont fondés à la croire indubitable et certaine* — the
  1851 act's own formulas; legacy positive diocesan judgment; not
  relabelled under the 2024 taxonomy.
- *authorized cult* — the 1851 act's art. 3 (with art. 4 reserving
  devotional publications to written episcopal approval and art. 5
  forbidding public contest in the diocese).
- *neque approbatas neque reprobatas ... sed tantum permissas
  tamquam pie credendas fide solum humana* — the Sacred
  Congregation of Rites' own characterization, 12 May 1877, naming
  La Salette; the controlling Roman description of the See's
  posture.
- *fides humana* — the assent invited by permitted private
  revelation.
- *condemned / proscribed / withdrawn* — the 1916 and 1923 book
  actions, each attaching to a named imprint.
- *precept of the Holy Office* — the 1915 command to all the
  faithful; present force unresolved, and asserted neither to have
  lapsed nor to bind.
- *analyzed text* — the 1879 Lecce booklet as the 1881 Louvain
  reprint prints it: published, quoted, and assessed by this
  publication, asserted neither as true nor as revealed, and
  approved by no authority.
- *project synthesis* — this publication's own labelled judgment,
  including the judgment of section 6.9 that the 1879 text is not
  the 1851 text; never received teaching.
- *reported* — default register for all words and phenomena
  attributed to the apparition, as in the papal texts of 1946 and
  1996 themselves.
- *optional memorial* — the 19 September celebration in the Proper
  of France (decree of 18 March 2016, reported at institutional
  ceiling).

## Event dossier (as of 2026-07-25)

- **Stable identity:** Notre-Dame de La Salette, invoked as
  *Réconciliatrice des pécheurs*; mountain above La Salette, Isère;
  one reported apparition, 19 September 1846.
- **Jurisdiction:** Diocese of Grenoble in 1846–1855; today Grenoble–
  Vienne, Latin Church, France. Current ordinary: Jean-Marc
  Eychenne (diocesan site, checked 2026-07-25). Shrine served by the
  Missionaries of Our Lady of La Salette.
- **Claimed type:** one vision of a weeping "Belle Dame" with an
  extended verbal discourse in French and in the local *patois*,
  plus a separate secret given to each child (Mélanie's in *patois*,
  Maximin's in French, on the children's own testimony).
- **Recipients:** Maximin Giraud (b. Corps, 26 August 1835;
  d. Corps, 1 March 1875) and Mélanie Calvat, called Mathieu
  (b. 7 November 1831; d. Altamura, 14 December 1904 per the
  Sanctuary's page).
- **Witness corpus:** same-day oral transmission to employers, the
  parish priest, and the mayor; early adult records and the
  interrogations associated with Abbé Lagier (not reached); the
  depositions of Pierre Selme and Jean-Baptiste Pra signed 27
  November 1847 and the superioress of Corps's opinion, read in the
  1854 printing; the commission of 1847 as the mandement describes
  it; the *rapporteur*'s printed report of 1848 and its 1850
  supplement (identified, not consulted); 1850s apologetic
  literature (1852 and 1854 English volumes; 1854 Italian
  translation of the pilgrim's manual; 1866 Grenoble biography);
  the present custodial text of the Sanctuary and the Missionaries.
- **Authority / act / date / language / locus / source:** Philibert
  de Bruillard, Bishop of Grenoble; doctrinal *mandement*; 19
  September 1851; French; operative clause of article 1
  («L'apparition de la Sainte Vierge à deux bergers sur la montagne
  de La Salette […] porte en elle-même tous les caractères de la
  vérité et que les fidèles sont fondés à la croire indubitable et
  certaine»). Witnesses: the Sanctuary's French transcription with
  its own ellipsis; the DDF's 2024 citation of the operative clause;
  the whole article in the English of Ullathorne 1854, pp. 120–121;
  the eight-article structure in the Italian of the 1854 pilgrim's
  manual, pp. 42–47. **No French printing of the integral act was
  located.**
- **Procedural regime and operative formula:** pre-1978 diocesan
  discernment — consultation of chapter and seminary; commission of
  grave, pious, and learned men sitting in the bishop's presence,
  concluded 13 December 1847; children interrogated separately and
  simultaneously; printed report of a vicar-general; the secrets
  written under obedience in July 1851 and delivered to Pius IX on
  18 July 1851, removing "the last objection"; judgment three and a
  half years after the commission's conclusion. Operative formula =
  marks of truth + grounds for believing indubitable and certain +
  corroboration from concourse and prodigies + cult authorization +
  publication reservation + prohibition of public contest +
  building programme.
- **Exact object judged:** one apparition on 19 September 1846 and
  the cult of Our Lady of La Salette. Not judged: any secret in any
  form; any prediction; the verbal inerrancy of any reported
  sentence; any individual cure or prodigy; any property of the
  spring; the seers' later lives or writings; any obligation of
  divine faith.
- **Later acts and their objects:** mandement of 1 May 1852
  instituting the Missionaries; foundation stone 25 May 1852; Roman
  grants of cult 24 August – 2 December 1852 (privileged altar,
  votive Mass, confraternity and archconfraternity indulgences,
  visit indulgence, mission indulgences, faculties for objects and
  the scapular, and the 19 September indult); Ginoulhiac's
  disciplinary mandements of 30 September and 4 November 1854 and
  circular of 19 September 1857; his 1855 sentence closing the
  seers' role; Rites rescripts of 12 May and 7 July 1877;
  constitutions of the Missionaries approved *ad decennium* 29
  January 1909 (confirmed by Pius X 1 February 1909) and
  definitively 7 June 1926; reported letter of the Secretary of the
  Holy Office, 14 August 1880 (not reached in any text; one partisan
  witness of 1882); Holy Office decree of 21 December 1915;
  Holy Office declaration of 12 April 1916 and Index decree of 5
  June 1916; Holy Office decree of 9 May 1923; Pius XII's letter of
  8 October 1945; John XXIII's patronage act of 16 June 1961; John
  Paul II's letters of 6 May and 9 July 1996; optional memorial in
  the Proper of France by decree of 18 March 2016.
- **Current controlling status:** positive legacy diocesan judgment
  of 1851 in force in its historical formula; the Holy See's own
  1877 characterization of its posture as permission with assent
  *fide solum humana* stands unrepealed by any located act; no
  superseding, restricting, or reversing act on the event located;
  no determination under the 2024 norms concerns La Salette (DDF
  document index checked 2026-07-25, zero occurrences); the
  restrictive acts of 1880–1923 concern the secret literature and
  touch neither the judgment nor the cult; the present force of the
  1915 precept is **unresolved** by any located act and this
  publication does not resolve it, asserting neither that it has
  lapsed nor that it binds; and the 1879 text, published and
  analyzed at section 6 on the maintainer's express direction, is
  judged by this publication — as labelled project synthesis, not as
  received teaching — not to be the text written for Pius IX in July
  1851.
- **Reported-message themes checked:** submission and the Son's hand
  or arm; unceasing maternal intercession; the six days and the
  seventh; blasphemy of the carters; spoilt harvest and potatoes,
  repeated in *patois*; corn, famine, children under seven,
  walnuts, grapes; conversion reversing the sign; morning and
  evening prayer with at least an Our Father and a Hail Mary;
  neglected Mass, Sunday work, and Lent; the Coin wheat episode;
  the twofold charge to make it pass to all her people. Cautions:
  the discourse contains no date, no political programme, no
  chronology of the last things, no promise attached to a devotion,
  and no claim about any bishop, priest, or order; its dearth
  prophecy is conditional and is not treated here as fulfilled or
  unfulfilled; the saying about children under seven is reported
  because it is in the earliest reachable recital and is not applied
  to anyone.
- **Source / translation / rights / disagreement / gap / review
  status:** witnesses and rights in `source-audit.md` and the
  publication's References; translations editorial and marked;
  disagreements preserved (*la main* / *le bras*; *conduisent* /
  *mènent*; Mélanie's secret *in patois* in 1847 against *en
  français* in 1879, not harmonized; the 1881 volume's ambiguous
  "33/35 ans" and its "depuis 1867" against Zola's "de 1868 jusqu'en
  1873"; gazette
  transcription defects at AAS 7 (1915) 594 and AAS 15 (1923) 288
  and in the 1854 printing of Pra's deposition; and one *corrected*
  item, the imprimatur date formerly recorded as 15 vs 18 November
  1879, shown by page-image collation to be 15 in both witnesses);
  gaps recorded (no
  French printing of the integral mandement; the 1851 manuscripts;
  any identified text of Maximin's secret; any text of the letter of
  14 August 1880; the 1879 Lecce imprint; the Grenoble diocesan
  archives; the
  *rapporteur*'s report; the critical documentary edition; the 2016
  CDW decree; AAS volume 75 and volumes from 99); review = internal
  only, no independent or ecclesiastical review.
