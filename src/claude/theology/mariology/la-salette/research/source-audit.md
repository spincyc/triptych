## The 1851 mandement — checked, with a stated gap

**Gap of record: no French printing of the integral mandement was
located by this project.** Searches for one included the Sanctuary's
current and archived pages, the Missionaries' French and
international sites, the French episcopal conference's Nominis
service, and Internet Archive metadata queries for nineteenth-century
La Salette titles; Gallica's SRU endpoint refused automated queries
(HTTP 403) and the Google Books API returned a daily-quota error
(HTTP 429) throughout the session. The consequence is stated in the
publication at section 1 and in Appendix A: the French is quoted only
as far as two independent witnesses print it, and no French
reconstruction is offered.

## Claim-to-source rule

Every quotation in the publication appears in the tables or lists
above. Words attributed to the apparition are always tied to their
stratum (recital stratum in the 1854 printing; received custodial
text; the act's own summary). Editorial translations from French,
Latin, Italian, and *patois* are marked as such in the prose or
notes. Gazette loci give the volumes' printed page numbers as
observed in the Holy See's archive text; nineteenth-century loci give
the printed page numbers of the named imprint.

## The 1879-versus-1851 comparison (section 6.6) — checked

Performed 2026-07-25, line by line, between Ullathorne 1854,
pp. 35–46 (French, *patois*, and English columns; the interrogation
answers on the secret), and the 1881 Louvain reprint, pp. 7–15, the
latter read in page images. Results as published: the 1879 text
carries every element of the discourse in the same order and
substance; it differs verbally in the opening address (*annoncer* /
*conter*), in the Son's hand (*si lourde… la retenir* / *si forte, si
pesante… la maintenir*, both against the received custodial *le
bras*), in the children's answers, and in printing no *patois* at
all; and it states that Mélanie's secret was given *en français*
where her own 1847 answer says *patois*. The publication draws the
methodological consequence at section 6.6 and the evidentiary
consequence at section 6.7.

and now publishes and analyzes the 1879 text (new section 6), under
