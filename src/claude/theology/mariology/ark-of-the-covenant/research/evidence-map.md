## Evidence layers and confidence vocabulary

Layers per the historical-accounts profile, with two auxiliary classes:

- **W** — ancient narrative witness (the canonical text in its versions; other
  ancient literary witnesses, including ancient translations, Josephus, the
  pseudepigrapha).
- **C** — contemporary or near-contemporary document or object (site archaeology,
  inscriptions; first-century eyewitness-horizon description of the Second Temple).
- **R** — later institutional memory and reception (rabbinic tradition, patristic
  exegesis, liturgy, the Douay tradition's own notes, Ethiopian and Islamic
  reception).
- **M** — modern critical reconstruction (scholarship reported as a school with
  premises).
- **N** — bounded negative result.
- **J** — project synthesis, labeled as such in print.

Controlling profile fact, stated once: **no contemporary document or object attests
the ark itself.** The earliest controlled evidence for the object and its journey is
the canonical literary corpus (layer W); layer C enters only for sites on the
itinerary and for the Second-Temple empty sanctuary. Every narrative row below
therefore rests on W unless marked, and "High" confidence means high confidence in
what the witness says and that the section reports it exactly — not independent
corroboration of the underlying event.

Quotation authority: English Scripture is printed only where a Douay-Rheims
(Challoner) verification event exists in `w2-rights.md` §1 (drbo.org chapter reads,
2026-08-15); loci verified in wave 1 only at RSV level are paraphrased with the
locus, never quoted. The w2-rights honesty caveat carries over: the drbo reads were
fetch-mediated, and `research/source-audit.md` owes each printed quotation a
character-by-character comparison before release.
