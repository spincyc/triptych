# The Ark of the Covenant — source audit

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every check below was performed 2026-08-15
against the named online witness by the research campaign whose dossiers
are preserved in the production records (`build/ark-research/`).
"Checked" means the passage or fact was retrieved and read at the stated
URL; it does not assert collation against a critical print edition
except where page-image reading is expressly recorded (Field's Hexapla;
the Patrologia scans; the 1942 Breviarium). No search, source, or
verification event is recorded here that did not occur.

Two campaigns are recorded, both of 2026-08-15. The first (**wave 1**,
thirteen lanes) established the Scripture corpus, the philology, the
patristic florilegium, the liturgical-magisterial base, and the
journey/fate history, and was followed by two adversarial verification
passes (philology; florilegium) whose corrections are incorporated
below. The second (**wave 2**, ten gap-closure lanes, files `w2-*`)
re-anchored all English Scripture to the Douay-Rheims (Challoner) base
text, settled the quotation-rights classes, and closed the named gaps
(text criticism, fate cognates, ark-function scholarship, the modern
Catholic school, the 1587–1950 chain, the medieval West, the Apocalypse
commentators, Munificentissimus Deus §26). Entries below carry the
dossiers' own evidence states.

**Collation pass, performed 2026-08-15:** every Douay-Rheims quotation
printed in the body (180 quotations across the thirteen body sections,
inventoried section by section) was compared character-by-character with
the text delivered by the cited drbo.org chapter page, in a four-agent
collation pass run after drafting. Six corrections were applied (two
verse-initial capitals; ``house of the Lord'' restored at 3 Kings 8:11;
the colon ending Apocalypse 12:1; a lowercase ``ark of his testament'';
an unmarked elision at Luke 1:39 now marked with an ellipsis), and two
non-Douay renderings inside quotation marks were reworded or re-anchored
to the Douay wording (the Judges 20:27 temporal marker; the Ezechiel
11:23 phrase, Douay ``the mount that is on the east side of the city''
read at drbo chapter 31011). The Latin, Greek, and translated patristic
quotations stand on the page-image and full-text verification events of
the research dossiers recorded below; a further independent collation of
those classes against print editions remains listed in the document's
outstanding-review appendix.
