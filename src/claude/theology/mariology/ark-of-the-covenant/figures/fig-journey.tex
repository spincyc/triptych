\sketchcaption{The journey, drawn small: (1) up from Sinai east of the Salt
Sea, in by Jordan and Jericho (Josue 3--6); (2) Ebal, then the long
residence at Silo --- the tent marks it; (3) out to battle and taken at
Aphec (1 Kings 4); (4) seven months among the Philistine cities (1 Kings
5--6); (5) home by Bethsames, then the parked decades at Cariathiarim;
(6) David's two processions to Jerusalem (2 Kings 6). A sketch map, not to
scale, with sites at their traditional identifications and the Douay's
names; where an identification or an event's archaeology is disputed, the
text says so at the place. Nebo carries a tradition, not a finding.}
