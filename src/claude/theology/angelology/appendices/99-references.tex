\section{References}\label{app:references}

The editions and translations read for this treatise, grouped by kind and
listed once each. An entry gives the work, its editor or translator, the
place and date of the edition as recorded at the reading, and the copy
that was read, named by its host. Appendix~\ref{app:witnesses} names the
witnesses and where each is treated.

Abbreviations: ANF, \emph{The Ante-Nicene Fathers}, American edition;
NPNF1 and NPNF2, \emph{A Select Library of the Nicene and Post-Nicene
Fathers}, first and second series (Schaff edition), both read in the plain
text of the Christian Classics Ethereal Library (ccel.org); PG and PL,
J.-P. Migne, \emph{Patrologia Graeca} and \emph{Patrologia Latina}, read
in the OCR text or page images of scans at archive.org and, for several PL
volumes, in the page images at \mbox{documentacatholicaomnia.eu}; CSEL,
\emph{Corpus Scriptorum Ecclesiasticorum Latinorum}; GCS, \emph{Die
griechischen christlichen Schriftsteller}; MGH, \emph{Monumenta Germaniae
Historica}; DS, Denzinger's numbering.
