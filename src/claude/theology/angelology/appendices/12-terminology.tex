\section{Terminology}\label{app:terminology}

The English terms this reference prefers, with the Latin and Greek
of the sources and the loci that fix the usage. English \emph{Summa}
wording follows the English Dominican translation; the Latin follows
the Leonine text; Greek is transliterated.
