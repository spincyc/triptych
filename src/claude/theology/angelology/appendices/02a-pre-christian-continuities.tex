\section{Continuities with the Writings before Christ}\label{app:continuities}

Each teaching on the angels or the spirits that a pre-Christian writing
holds is set here beside the Fathers who received, corrected, or rejected
it and the place where Aquinas or the Church takes the matter up
(\S\ref{sec:before-christ}). A parallel without a naming or quotation
by a Father is marked as a parallel.
