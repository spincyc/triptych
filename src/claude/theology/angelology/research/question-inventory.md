# Question Inventory

Every article of ST I qq. 50-64 and 106-114, with the other *Summa*
articles the publication treats, exactly once. Determinations are
source-grounded synthesis of the English Dominican translation checked
against the Corpus Thomisticum Latin; grades follow the profile's rule and
their basis is the act or witness named. The article census appendix
projects this table.
