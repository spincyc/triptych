# Question Inventory

Every article of ST I qq. 50-64 and 106-114, with the other *Summa*
articles the publication treats, exactly once. Determinations are
source-grounded synthesis of the English Dominican translation checked
against the Corpus Thomisticum Latin; grades follow the profile's rule and
their basis is the act or witness named. The article census appendix
projects this table.

| q052-a1 | I q. 52 a. 1 | Whether an angel is in a place? | Yes, equivocally: by application of its power, containing rather than contained. | Collect of Compline | Common teaching (fact); Thomist position (the account) | sec:q52 | Objections from Boethius and Aristotle |

| q052-a3 | I q. 52 a. 3 | Whether several angels can be at the same time in the same place? | No. Two complete causes cannot be immediate causes of one effect. | Analogy: not two souls in one body | Thomist position | sec:q52 | None named |
| q053-a1 | I q. 53 a. 1 | Whether an angel can be moved locally? | Yes, by successive virtual contacts, continuous or not. | Christ's soul descended into hell (article of faith) | Thomist position (premise defined, DS 801) | sec:q53 | Aristotle's argument that the indivisible is not moved |
| q053-a2 | I q. 53 a. 2 | Whether an angel passes through intermediate space? | If its motion is continuous, yes; if not, it can pass without the middle, as it wills. | Argument that change is preceded by motion | Thomist position | sec:q53 | None named |
