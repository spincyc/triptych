# Saint Jerome — source audit

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every check below was performed 2026-07-24
against the named online witness. "Checked" means the passage was retrieved
and its wording read at the stated URL; it does not assert collation against
a critical edition (an outstanding review item). No search, source, or
verification event is recorded here that did not occur.

## Translation basis

Jerome's letters and most treatises are quoted from NPNF series 2, vol. 6
(New York, 1893; trans. W. H. Fremantle, G. Lewis, W. G. Martley), read in
the New Advent transcriptions, with the Preface to Samuel and Kings and the
prolegomena read at CCEL; De viris illustribus and the two Apologies from
NPNF series 2, vol. 3; the Augustine–Jerome correspondence from NPNF series
1, vol. 1; Augustine's On the Proceedings of Pelagius from NPNF series 1,
vol. 5; Sulpicius Severus from NPNF series 2, vol. 11; the Tobit and Judith
prologues from the public-domain translations at tertullian.org (Edgecomb;
Jacobs). All public domain. A catalog-level source-library record exists for
NPNF2-6 (`src/sources/works/nicene-and-post-nicene-fathers/series-2-volume-6/`)
and is bound in `source-bindings.toml`; the other volumes are identified
here and in the References without central records, following the sibling
editions' practice.
