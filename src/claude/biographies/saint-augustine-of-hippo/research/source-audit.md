# Saint Augustine of Hippo — source audit

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every check below was performed 2026-07-24/25
against the named witness. "Checked" means the passage was retrieved and its
wording read at the stated URL or registered artifact; it does not assert
collation against a critical edition (an outstanding review item). No
search, source, or verification event is recorded here that did not occur.

## Translation basis

The Confessions are quoted from NPNF series 1, vol. 1 (New York, 1886;
trans. J. G. Pilkington), read in the New Advent transcriptions
(newadvent.org/fathers/1101NN.htm); the letters from the same volume's
Cunningham translation (newadvent.org/fathers/1102NNN.htm); On the Gift of
Perseverance from NPNF series 1, vol. 5 (newadvent.org/fathers/15122.htm).
The City of God is quoted from the Dods 1871 translation as retained in the
repository source library (exact tracked artifacts of Project Gutenberg
eBooks 45304/45305; see source-bindings.toml for search and inspection
receipts). Possidius is quoted from Weiskotten's 1919 public-domain
translation at tertullian.org; Paul the Deacon from Foulke 1907 at
elfinspell.com; the Golden Legend from Caxton's version at Fordham's
Internet Medieval Sourcebook. All public domain. Catalog-level
source-library records exist for NPNF1-1
(`src/sources/works/nicene-and-post-nicene-fathers/series-1-volume-1/`,
created for this publication) and Possidius/Weiskotten
(`src/sources/works/possidius/vita-augustini/`, created for this
publication) and are bound in `source-bindings.toml`; the other web
witnesses are identified here and in the References without central
records, following the sibling editions' practice.

## Source families searched and negative results (bounded)

- The Confessions (Pilkington, books 1–10) and Possidius's Vita as
  transcribed were searched for: the concubine's name (absent), the
  seashore-child story (absent), the Te Deum legend (absent), relic
  movements (absent from Possidius beyond burial).
- The registered Dods 1871 City of God artifacts were searched by the
  repository's replayable raw-line tool for the quoted passages (receipts
  in source-bindings.toml); those searches establish presence of the
  quoted lines, not absence claims.
- These are literal searches of English transcriptions on 2026-07-24/25
  and cannot exclude unsearched works, other languages, or unindexed
  witnesses.
