# Saint Augustine of Hippo — chronology record

Provider: Anthropic Claude. All web checks 2026-07-24/25. Evidence classes as
in the publication appendix (A/N/E/L/M/K/S).

Dates are claims. Augustine's own narrative gives ages and sequence, almost
never absolute years; the absolute skeleton rests on the birth and death
dates carried by official and critical tradition (M/K) and on a handful of
dated documents (Ep. 21, Ep. 93, Ep. 133, Ep. 213). For each event this
record states the basis actually checked, competing views, and the
confidence used in the publication.

## Anchors (checked at the source)

1. **13 November 354 — birth at Thagaste.** Benedict XVI, General Audience
   9 Jan 2008 (checked at vatican.va): "Augustine was born in Tagaste in
   the Roman Province of Numidia, Africa, on 13 November 354 to Patricius,
   a pagan who later became a catechumen, and Monica, a fervent Christian"
   (M). Ages in the Confessions cohere with 354. Status: secure.
2. **c. 365 — boyhood illness, baptism deferred.** Conf. 1.11.17 (checked
   at newadvent.org/fathers/110101.htm): signed with the cross and salted;
   "So my cleansing was deferred" (A). No year stated. Status: episode
   secure, date editorial.
3. **c. 370 — sixteenth year, idleness, pear theft.** Conf. 2.3.6 ("in
   that sixteenth year of my age") and 2.4.9 (checked) (A). Status: age
   stated by subject.
4. **c. 371 — Carthage; Patricius dies.** Conf. 3.1.1 ("To Carthage I
   came") and 3.4.7 chapter context (father "having died two years
   before" the Hortensius year) (A, checked). Status: approximate.
5. **c. 372 — concubine; Adeodatus born.** Conf. 4.2.2 (one woman, "yet
   one only, remaining faithful even to her"); Adeodatus "barely fifteen"
   at the 387 baptism (Conf. 9.6.14, checked) ⇒ born c. 372/373 (S,
   inference from stated ages). Status: inference.
6. **373 — nineteenth year: Hortensius; Manichaean adhesion.** Conf. 3.4.7
   (checked): "This book, in truth, changed my affections"; Conf. 4.1.1
   (checked): "from my nineteenth to my eight and twentieth year" nine
   years a hearer (A). Status: secure relative anchor.
7. **373–382 — teaching at Thagaste and Carthage; friend's death; De
   pulchro et apto.** Conf. 4 (checked): 4.1.1 span; 4.6.11 "Thou half of
   my soul"; chapter heading "books on the fair and fit" (A). Status:
   secure as span.
8. **383 — twenty-ninth year: Faustus; Rome.** Conf. 5.3.3 (checked):
   "that twenty-ninth year of my age" and Faustus "a great snare of the
   devil"; 5.7.13 (checked): endeavours "came completely to an end";
   5.8.15 (checked): "And I lied to my mother — and such a mother! — and
   got away" (A). Status: secure.
9. **384 — Milan chair.** Conf. 5.13.23 (checked): Symmachus "having
   proved me by proposing a subject, would send me"; "And to Milan I came,
   unto Ambrose the bishop" (A). Year editorial (received chronology).
   Status: secure.
10. **385 — Monica arrives; betrothal; concubine dismissed.** Conf. 6.1.1;
    6.13.23 ("a maiden sued who wanted two years of the marriageable
    age"); 6.15.25 (all checked) (A). Status: approximate (sequence
    within the Milan years).
11. **386 — Platonist books; Ponticianus; garden; Cassiciacum.** Conf.
    7.9.13–14 (checked); 8.6.14–8.12.30 (checked; scene quoted in full);
    9.2.2–4 (checked; vintage vacation); SEP "Saint Augustine" (checked at
    plato.stanford.edu): conversion "summer 386" (K). O'Donnell comm9
    (checked): book 9 covers "August 386 - late 387" (K). Status:
    resolution secure; scene single-witness (see tradition audit §1).
12. **Easter 387 — baptism at Milan.** Conf. 9.6.14 (checked); Possidius,
    Vita 1 (checked at tertullian.org): "with the coming of the holy days
    of Easter he received the water of baptism" (N); Augustinum
    Hipponensem (checked at vatican.va): "baptized on the night of Holy
    Saturday—April 23-24—by Ambrose" (M). Status: year secure; the
    official day statement is reported as such (calendar reconstructions
    of Easter 387 vary by a day and are not adjudicated).
13. **387, after 25 April — Ostia; Monica's death at 56.** Conf.
    9.10.23–9.11.28 (checked): "the fifty-sixth year of her age, and the
    thirty-third of mine"; O'Donnell comm9 (checked): "M. must have died
    between 25 April and 13 November 387" (K). Status: secure; day
    unknown.
14. **388 — return to Africa; Thagaste community.** Possidius, Vita 2–3
    (checked): "settled for almost three years"; O'Donnell comm9
    (checked): return "late in the sailing season of 388" after Maximus's
    fall (K). Status: secure.
15. **c. 389/390 — death of Adeodatus.** Conf. 9.6.14 (checked): "Quickly
    did You remove his life from the earth"; no date in any source.
    Status: unrecoverable; publication says "within a few years."
16. **391 — ordination as presbyter at Hippo.** Possidius, Vita 4
    (checked): seizure scene, "while he wept freely"; Ep. 21 (checked at
    newadvent.org/fathers/1102021.htm, headed A.D. 391): request for study
    leave (A/N). CE "Life of St. Augustine" (checked): "was ordained in
    391" (K). Status: secure.
17. **392 — Fortunatus disputation.** Possidius, Vita 6 (checked):
    Fortunatus "left the city of Hippo soon after and returned to it no
    more"; New Advent's editorial dating of the Acta contra Fortunatum
    (392) (K). Status: secure.
18. **395/396 — coadjutor consecration.** Possidius, Vita 8 (checked):
    Megalius of Calama, primate of Numidia; Augustine's refusal "contrary
    to the custom of the Church"; later regret citing "the prohibition of
    the Ecumenical Council"; Ep. 213 (checked): "which I did not know to
    have been forbidden by the Council of Nicaea" (A/N). Year: Benedict
    XVI 9 Jan 2008 (checked): "Bishop from 395 to his death in 430" (M);
    CE (checked): "He was then forty two" ⇒ 396 (K). Status: event secure;
    year unresolved (395/396 printed).
19. **c. 397–400 — Confessions.** SEP (checked): "ca. 396–400" (K);
    Benedict XVI 20 Feb 2008 audience context (M). Status: approximate.
20. **399–c. 420 — De Trinitate.** Benedict XVI, 20 Feb 2008 (checked):
    "the first 12 books between 399 and 412, published without the
    knowledge of Augustine, who in about 420 completed and revised the
    entire work"; Ep. 169.1.1 quoted there (M). Status: approximate.
21. **405 — laws applied to Donatists.** CE "Donatists" (checked):
    Honorius's measures of 405 (K). Status: reported.
22. **408 — Ep. 93.** New Advent heading "(A.D. 408)" (checked); the
    coercion-change passage 93.5.17 and "compel them to come in" 93.2.5
    (checked verbatim) (A). Status: secure.
23. **410–411 — sack of Rome; Pelagius in Africa; conference of
    Carthage.** Benedict XVI 20 Feb 2008 (checked): CoG occasioned by the
    410 sack (M); CE "Pelagianism" (checked): Pelagius and Caelestius
    landed near Hippo in 411, Augustine absent, later saw Pelagius in
    Carthage (K); Possidius, Vita 13 (checked): conference under
    Marcellinus by command of Honorius (N). Status: secure.
24. **412 — Ep. 133.** New Advent heading "(A.D. 412)" (checked); the
    Restitutus/Innocentius clemency plea (checked verbatim) (A). Status:
    secure.
25. **413–426 — City of God.** Benedict XVI 20 Feb 2008 (checked):
    "written between 413 and 426 in 22 books" (M); opening line and 14.28,
    15.22, 19.13, 22.30 verified against registered Dods artifacts
    (search/inspection receipts in source-bindings.toml). Status: secure
    as span.
26. **415 — Diospolis.** CE "Pelagianism" (checked): synod of fourteen
    bishops at Diospolis, December 415; Augustine's De gestis Pelagii as
    the source, cited at reported level (K). Status: secure.
27. **418 — Council of Carthage; Tractoria.** Possidius, Vita 18
    (checked): African councils, Innocent and Zosimus, imperial decrees
    (N); CE "Pelagianism" (checked): canons that "afterwards came to be
    articles of faith binding on the universal Church"; Zosimus's
    "Epistola tractoria" (418) (K). Status: secure.
28. **26 September 426 — Eraclius designated.** Ep. 213 (checked):
    notaries recording; "He shall be as he now is, a presbyter" (A);
    Benedict XVI 16 Jan 2008 (checked): 26 September 426 (M). Status:
    secure.
29. **c. 427 — Retractationes.** Benedict XVI 20 Feb 2008 (checked):
    "composed in two books in about 427 A.D."; Possidius, Vita 28
    (checked): "Shortly before the time of his death he revised the
    books... Thus he wrote two volumes whose title is On the Revision of
    Books" (M/N). Status: approximate.
30. **429–430 — Vandal invasion; siege of Hippo.** Possidius, Vita 28
    (checked): Vandals and Alans from Spain; Benedict XVI 16 Jan 2008
    (checked): crossing 429, siege of Hippo May/June 430 (M/N). Status:
    secure.
31. **28 August 430 — death; burial at Hippo.** Possidius, Vita 29
    (checked): "in the third month of the siege he succumbed to fever";
    Vita 31 (checked): "he lived seventy-six years, almost forty of which
    he spent as a priest or bishop" (inclusive count from 354); "he was
    buried"; Benedict XVI 16 Jan 2008 (checked): died 28 August 430 (M/N).
    Status: secure.
32. **c. 432+ — Possidius writes the Vita.** Weiskotten's introduction
    (checked at tertullian.org): after July 431; "the probabilities favor
    432 or soon after" (K). Status: approximate.

## Posthumous (checked)

33. **Relics to Sardinia (undated; later tradition c. 500).** Paul the
    Deacon, Hist. Lang. 6.48 (checked at elfinspell.com): bones "had been
    formerly carried [to Sardinia] on account of the devastation of the
    barbarians" (E; no date, no agent named). Later attribution to bishops
    exiled by the Vandal kings is tradition (L), not asserted. Status:
    unresolved.
34. **By c. 720s — Liutprand's translation to Pavia.** Paul the Deacon
    6.48 (checked, quoted verbatim): ransom from Saracen-raided Sardinia,
    burial at Ticinum/Pavia (E; Liutprand reigned 712–744). Golden Legend
    (checked at sourcebooks.fordham.edu): elaborated narrative, "two
    hundred and eighty years" in Sardinia, Genoa meeting, San Pietro
    "Cieldore" (L). Benedict XVI, Pavia homily 22 Apr 2007 (checked):
    "the King of the Longobards acquired Augustine's remains for the City
    of Pavia" (M, current usage). Status: secure as 8th-century report.
35. **1298 — Boniface VIII's decretal on the four Latin doctors.** CE 1912
    "Doctors of the Church" (checked) (M/K). Status: secure.
36. **1430 — Monica's relics to Rome.** CE "St. Monica" (checked): "In
    1430 Martin V ordered the relics to be brought to Rome"; d'Estouteville
    church (K). Status: reported.
37. **1483 — Caxton's seashore-child addition.** Golden Legend, Life of
    S. Austin (checked): Caxton's note that he found it "not in the
    legend, mine exemplar, neither in English, French, ne in Latin" but
    painted at Antwerp (L). Status: secure as textual fact.
38. **1986–2008 — modern papal reception.** Augustinum Hipponensem
    (checked); Pavia homily 2007 (checked); five audiences Jan–Feb 2008
    (checked) (M). Status: secure.

## Competing chronologies preserved

- Consecration 395 (Benedict XVI; most modern accounts) vs 396 (CE's age
  42): both printed; no resolution.
- Baptism vigil "23–24 April 387" (Augustinum Hipponensem) vs calendar
  reconstructions placing Easter 387 on 25 April: the official statement is
  reported as such; the publication prints "Easter 387" with the official
  day in apposition.
- Possidius's "seventy-six years" (inclusive) vs the 354–430 count of 75
  completed years: noted, not harmonized.
- Golden Legend's "two hundred and eighty years" in Sardinia: legendary
  arithmetic, recorded in the tradition audit only.
