  asserted in the publication.

## Disambiguation guard

Leo XIII's encyclical Aeterni Patris (4 August 1879) is a different work
from Pius IX's bull Aeterni Patris (1868) convoking the First Vatican
Council. The repository source library contains a family for the latter
(`src/sources/works/pius-ix/aeterni-patris-1868`); it was examined for
identity and deliberately NOT bound or cited in this publication. All 1879
claims rest on the vatican.va English page of Leo XIII's encyclical.

## Translation and quotation basis

English wording of Thomas follows the public-domain English Dominican
translation (1920) as transcribed at New Advent where quoted; Latin
follows the Leonine text as centrally verified (I.1.1) or as presented in
the Corpus Thomisticum web edition (SCG I.2, brief). Papal and conciliar
texts are quoted briefly from the vatican.va pages named above, otherwise
paraphrased. The 1912 Catholic Encyclopedia and the 24-theses translation
hosted at franciscan-archive are quoted as identified. SEP is quoted in
brief attributed snippets under fair quotation.
