## Translation basis

Scripture: Challoner Douay–Rheims at drbo.org (public domain; translates
the Vulgate), with the Clementine Vulgate of John 21 read at the same site,
and the Greek read directly in the Robinson–Pierpont Byzantine Textform
(RP2018) at the loci listed below. Apostolic Fathers/Irenaeus: ANF as transcribed at New Advent
(1 Clement attributed there to John Keith, ANF 9; Ignatius Romans, middle
["shorter"] recension, Roberts–Donaldson, ANF 1; Irenaeus, Roberts–Rambaut,
ANF 1). Tertullian: ANF 3 (Holmes; Thelwall). Eusebius: McGiffert, NPNF2-1.
Jerome: Richardson, NPNF2-3. Tacitus: Church–Brodribb 1876 (Wikisource).
Acts of Peter: M. R. James 1924 (earlychristianwritings.com transcription).
All public domain. Origen and 1 Clement: ANF 9 (Patrick; Keith). Cyprian: ANF 5 (Wallis).
Augustine's letters and Jerome's Letter 112: NPNF1-1 (Cunningham), with the
parallel presentation of Jerome's own numbering at NPNF2-6. Augustine's
Tractates on John: NPNF1-7. Calvin: Beveridge 1845 (CCEL). Josephus:
Whiston (Project Gutenberg 2848). Muratorian Fragment: Metzger's English
translation as transcribed at Early Christian Writings, quoted in one clause
under scholarly fair use. All others public domain.

Source-library records: catalog-level for ANF 1, 3, 5, 9; NPNF1-1, NPNF1-7,
NPNF2-1, NPNF2-3, NPNF2-6; Catholic Encyclopedia 3 and 4; Calvin's
Institutes (Beveridge); Josephus (Whiston); byte-verified for Pastor
aeternus; and acquired-and-inspected, with pinned fingerprints, for the
eight Robinson–Pierpont artifacts newly registered under
`src/sources/works/robinson-pierpont/`. All are bound in
`source-bindings.toml`. Records created for this revision: ANF volume 5,
ANF volume 9, NPNF series 2 volume 3, Catholic Encyclopedia volumes 3 and 4,

work, edition, and eight tracked artifacts.

## Source families searched and negative results (bounded)

Searches were literal, over the English transcriptions named above, on
2026-07-25:

- New Testament (DR text of the fetched chapters): no statement that Peter
  went to Rome; no narrative of his death.
- 1 Clement 5–6: no place, manner, or date of death.
- Checked early witnesses through Origen: no explicit crucifixion
  statement earlier than Tertullian (c. 200); no head-down detail earlier
  than the Acts of Peter/Origen stratum; no 29 June death-date claim.
- CE 1912: 25-year episcopate origin "unable to trace."
- Romans 16 and the greeting lists of Colossians, Philemon, and 2 Timothy:
  no mention of Peter.
- Among the witnesses checked: no first- or early second-century text that
  names a successor to Peter in a distinctive Petrine function, and none that
  applies the rock-and-keys language of Mt 16 to the apostolic body without
  reference to Simon.
- Jerome, Letter 120 (to Hedibia): NPNF2-6 prints only an abstract, so the
  remark about Peter using different interpreters could not be verified and
  is not used.
- Augustine's *Retractations* 1.21, in which he is reported to have set out
  both of his readings of "this rock" and left the choice to the reader,
  could not be reached in a verifiable edition; the publication says so and
  quotes only the Tractate.
- These results cannot exclude unsearched works, other languages, or
  unindexed witnesses.
