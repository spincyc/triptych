# Saint Peter — chronology

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every date is a claim with a stated basis. No
event in Peter's life carries a documented absolute date before 64; all
Gospel-era dating is relative to a ministry conventionally placed
c. 28–30/33, and the 30/33 alternative for the crucifixion of Jesus
propagates through every dependent entry. This file and the publication's
terminal chronology table state the same claims.

Rejected or non-chronological items (see tradition audit): arrival at Rome
in the second year of Claudius (42); the 25-year Roman episcopate; 29 June
as death day; same-day martyrdom with Paul as calendar fact.

Unresolved dating questions carried in the publication: whether the Antioch
incident of Gal 2:11–14 precedes or follows the Jerusalem council (Paul's
narrative order suggests after; Augustine, Letter 82.11, thought before);
whether the earth grave beneath the Vatican aedicula is first-century at all
(von Gerkan against the excavators; Prandi's judgment that no Campo P burial
is first-century, from which he declined to draw the conclusion; Magi's 1957
find removing the assumption that Nero's ground admitted no burials).
