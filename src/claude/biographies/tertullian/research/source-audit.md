# Tertullian — source audit

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every check below was performed 2026-07-24 against
the named online witness. "Checked" means the passage was retrieved and its
wording read at the stated URL; it does not assert collation against a
critical Latin edition (an outstanding review item). No search, source, or
verification event is recorded here that did not occur.

## Translation basis

Quotations of Tertullian follow the Ante-Nicene Fathers translation, vols.
3–4 (Buffalo: Christian Literature Publishing Co., 1885), read in the
transcriptions at newadvent.org and ccel.org. Volume identity (titles, 1885
printing, ten-volume American series) checked at ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf03
and /anf04. Catalog-level source-library records for both volumes were created
at `src/sources/works/ante-nicene-fathers/volume-{3,4}/` (no exact artifact
registered; bindings are cataloged-level). Eusebius, Jerome, and Vincent
follow NPNF series 2; Lactantius follows ANF vol. 7; all public domain.

## Ecclesial reception (M) — checked

- CCC 2761 with footnote "Tertullian, De orat. 1: PL 1, 1155": checked at
  vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P9W.HTM. Only this paragraph was verified;
  the publication claims no other Catechism locus. Vatican English
  paraphrased except the six-word phrase "the summary of the whole Gospel,"
  which renders Tertullian's ancient Latin.
- Benedict XVI, General Audience 30 May 2007: checked at
  vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/audiences/2007/documents/hf_ben-xvi_aud_20070530.html;
  paraphrased throughout.
- Chapman, "Tertullian," Catholic Encyclopedia 14 (1912): checked at
  newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm; quoted for "seems to have definitively
  separated from the Church about 211" as reception history.

## Modern scholarship (K) — checked, with honest ceilings

- **Barnes 1971** (*Tertullian*, Clarendon). Not read in full. Conclusions
  cited as reported in W.H.C. Frend's review, Classical Review 24 (1974)
  72–76, read at tertullian.org/articles/frend_on_barnes.htm (quotes: "was
  born a member of the Carthaginian literary class…", "Tertullian's father
  was not a centurion; there never was a centurio proconsularis"), and in the
  survey pages of the Tertullian Project (readfirst.htm). Frend's caution
  quoted from the same review. The publication attributes accordingly
  ("as reported").
- **Powell 1975**, "Tertullianists and Cataphrygians," Vigiliae Christianae
  29: 33–54. Full text read at
  tertullian.org/articles/powell_tertullianists.htm (reproduced there by
  permission of Brill). Key sentence quoted verbatim.
- **Rankin 1995**, *Tertullian and the Church* (CUP). Not read in full;
  thesis paraphrased from Cambridge University Press book and chapter
  descriptions (cambridge.org/core listing checked). No page-level claim
  made.
- **Tabbernee / survey of positions**: tertullian.org/tertullianistae/
  tertullianistae.htm (checked); the "memory-circle" suggestion attributed as
  a suggestion, not a page-cited argument.
- **Tertullian Project** (Roger Pearse, ed.): used as a specialist finding
  aid and host of permitted reproductions; not treated as a critical edition
  or institutional catalog.
