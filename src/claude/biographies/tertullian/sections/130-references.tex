\section{References}

All web sources were accessed and checked on 2026-07-24 or 2026-07-25.
Ancient-work citations in the text use conventional English titles with the
chapter numbering of the translations listed here.

\subsection*{Primary sources: Tertullian}

\begin{itemize}
\item Tertullian, works in English translation: \textit{The Ante-Nicene
Fathers}, vols.~3--4, ed.\ A.~Roberts, J.~Donaldson, A.~C.~Coxe (Buffalo:
Christian Literature Publishing Co., 1885). Quotations follow the
transcriptions at New Advent (\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/}) and
CCEL (\url{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf03.html} and
\url{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf04.html}). Works quoted:
\work{Apology} 2, 17, 18, 30, 37, 39, 40, 50; \work{To the Nations} 1.1, 1.7,
1.8;
\work{The Testimony of the Soul} 1, 2, 5, 6; \work{To the Martyrs} 1, 2, 3;
\work{To Scapula} 2, 3, 4, 5; \work{An Answer to the Jews} 1, 7; \work{The
Chaplet} 1, 3, 11; \work{On Baptism} 1, 4, 7, 8, 15, 17, 18; \work{On
Prayer} 1, 25, 29; \work{On Repentance} 4, 7, 9; \work{Of Patience} 1; \work{To His Wife}
1.1, 2.8; \work{Prescription Against Heretics} 7, 13, 36; \work{Against
Marcion}
1.1, 1.15, 3.24, 4.2; \work{Against Hermogenes} 1, 2; \work{Against the
Valentinians} 1, 3; \work{On the Flesh of Christ} 1, 5; \work{Against
Praxeas} 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, 25, 27; \work{Scorpiace} 1; \work{A Treatise on
the Soul} 9, 20, 55, 58; \work{On the Resurrection of the Flesh} 1, 8; \work{On the
Veiling of Virgins} 1, 17; \work{On Exhortation to Chastity} 7; \work{On
Monogamy} 1, 12; \work{On Fasting} 1; \work{On Modesty} 1, 21; \work{On the
Apparel of Women} 1.1, 2.13; \work{On Idolatry} 1, 14, 19; \work{The Shows}
4, 25, 30; \work{On Flight in Persecution} 1, 9, 14; \work{On the Pallium} 1, 6.
The translation is public domain; underlying Latin is ancient and public
domain.
\item Latin control texts for the tags quoted in Latin: \work{Adversus
Praxean} 2 and 27 at the Tertullian Project
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/latin/adversus_praxean.htm}); \work{De carne
Christi} 5 and \work{Apologeticum} 17 at The Latin Library
(\url{https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/tertullian/tertullian.carne.shtml};
\url{https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/tertullian/tertullian.apol.shtml});
\work{De resurrectione carnis} 8 at the Tertullian Project
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/latin/de_resurrectione_carnis.htm}). The
Latin of \textit{semen est sanguis Christianorum} is quoted as the famous
transmitted tag; the ANF English at \work{Apology} 50 was verified.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Ecclesial reception}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 2761, English text, Vatican
website archive (\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM});
cited for its quotation of Tertullian, \work{De oratione} 1.
\item Benedict XVI, General Audience of 30 May 2007 (catechesis on
Tertullian), Vatican website
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/audiences/2007/documents/hf_ben-xvi_aud_20070530.html});
paraphrased.
\item J.~Chapman, ``Tertullian,'' \textit{The Catholic Encyclopedia}, vol.~14
(New York, 1912), at New Advent
(\url{https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/14520c.htm}); cited as reception
history and quoted briefly on the Latin Bible, the theological idiom, and the
debt to Irenaeus.
\end{itemize}
