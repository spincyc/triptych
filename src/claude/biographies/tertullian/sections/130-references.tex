\section{References}

All web sources were accessed and checked on 2026-07-24. Ancient-work
citations in the text use conventional English titles with the chapter
numbering of the translations listed here.

\subsection*{Primary sources: Tertullian}

\begin{itemize}
\item Tertullian, works in English translation: \textit{The Ante-Nicene
Fathers}, vols.~3--4, ed.\ A.~Roberts, J.~Donaldson, A.~C.~Coxe (Buffalo:
Christian Literature Publishing Co., 1885). Quotations follow the
transcriptions at New Advent (\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/}) and
CCEL (\url{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf03.html} and
\url{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf04.html}). Works quoted: \work{Apology} 17, 37, 39, 50; \work{To
Scapula} 2, 3, 5; \work{The Chaplet} 1, 3; \work{On Baptism} 1, 15, 17;
\work{On Repentance} 4, 7, 9; \work{Of Patience} 1; \work{To His Wife} 1.1,
2.8; \work{Prescription Against Heretics} 7, 36; \work{Against Marcion} 1.1,
1.15; \work{Against Praxeas} 1, 2, 27; \work{On the Soul} 9, 55; \work{On the
Resurrection of the Flesh} 8; \work{On the Veiling of Virgins} 1, 17; \work{On
Exhortation to Chastity} 7; \work{On Monogamy} 1, 12; \work{On Fasting} 1;
\work{On Modesty} 1; \work{On the Apparel of Women} 1.1; \work{The Shows} 30.
The translation is public domain; underlying Latin is ancient and public
domain.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Primary sources: other ancient witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item \work{Acts of the Scillitan Martyrs}, public-domain English translation
at Early Christian Writings
(\url{https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/scillitan.html}).
\item Eusebius of Caesarea, \work{Church History} 2.2.4; 5.5; 5.16.7, NPNF
series 2 translation at New Advent
(\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2501.htm}).
\item Lactantius, \work{Divine Institutes} 5.1, ANF vol.~7 translation at New
Advent (\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/07015.htm}).
\item Jerome, \work{On Illustrious Men} 53, NPNF series 2 vol.~3 translation
at New Advent (\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2708.htm}). ``Give me
the master'' renders Jerome's public-domain Latin \textit{Da magistrum} as
given in this translation.
\item Augustine, \work{On Heresies} 86, paraphrased from the Latin text and
the copyrighted translation of L.~G.~M\"uller (1956) as presented at the
Tertullian Project
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/tertullianistae/de_haeresibus.htm}); no
protected wording is reproduced.
\item \work{Praedestinatus} 86, paraphrased from the presentation at the
Tertullian Project
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/tertullianistae/praedestinatus.htm}).
\item Vincent of Lérins, \work{Commonitorium} 18, NPNF series 2 vol.~11
translation at New Advent (\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3506.htm}).
\item \work{Decretum Gelasianum} (pseudo-Gelasian, 6th c.), English
translation at the Tertullian Project
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/decretum_eng.htm}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Modern scholarship}

\begin{itemize}
\item T.~D.~Barnes, \textit{Tertullian: A Historical and Literary Study}
(Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1971; reissued with postscript 1985). Consulted
through the contemporary reviews reproduced at the Tertullian Project,
principally W.~H.~C.~Frend, \textit{Classical Review} 24 (1974): 72--76
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/articles/frend_on_barnes.htm}); Barnes's
conclusions are cited as reported there.
\item D.~Powell, ``Tertullianists and Cataphrygians,'' \textit{Vigiliae
Christianae} 29 (1975): 33--54; full text reproduced by permission of Brill
at the Tertullian Project
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/articles/powell_tertullianists.htm}).
\item D.~Rankin, \textit{Tertullian and the Church} (Cambridge: Cambridge
University Press, 1995); thesis paraphrased from the publisher's book and
chapter descriptions at Cambridge Core:
\url{https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/tertullian-and-the-church/B47AD762216F1E525349DF6D639F6DBB}
\item W.~Tabbernee's suggestion on the character of the
\textit{Tertullianistae} and the survey of positions at the Tertullian
Project, ``The Tertullianistae''
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/tertullianistae/tertullianistae.htm}).
\item The Tertullian Project (ed.\ R.~Pearse), orientation pages
(\url{https://www.tertullian.org/}); used as a specialist finding aid, not as
a critical edition.
\end{itemize}
