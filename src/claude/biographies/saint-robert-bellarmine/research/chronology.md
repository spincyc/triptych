Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every date is a claim with a stated basis.

at day level; the soft places are the middle-year edges of formation, the
count of the controversies-chair years, one day-level conflict (the Capua
consecration), and every date inside the cause's contested history, which
rests on the process literature at reported level. This file and the
