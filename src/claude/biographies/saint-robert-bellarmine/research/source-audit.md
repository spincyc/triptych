## Source families searched and consequential negative results

Bounded literal searches over the retrieved plain texts (see limits in
the terminal appendix): no naming of Bellarmine's individual Bruno acts
in CE 1908 or in any witness read; no early witness for the
"opponents' arguments better" quip in the texts searched; no competent
patronage act for catechists/catechumens/canonists in AAS 22, 23, 60 or
the calendar record; no incorruption claim in Lux illa, Providentissimus
Deus, Roma altera, or CE 1907; the 26 Feb 1616 minute carries no
signatures (checked in Favaro's diplomatic text); Bellarmine's name
absent from the qualifiers' signature list (checked) and from the 5 Mar
1616 decree (checked). These are bounded, correctable raw-text results,
not semantic absence proofs.
