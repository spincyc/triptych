## Translation basis

Origen's own works are quoted from the Ante-Nicene Fathers, vol. 4 (Buffalo,
1885; trans. F. Crombie), read in the New Advent transcriptions; Rufinus's
translator's preface from NPNF series 2, vol. 3; Eusebius from NPNF2-1
(McGiffert); Jerome from NPNF2-3 (De viris illustribus) and NPNF2-6/the
New Advent letters series; Gregory Thaumaturgus from ANF vol. 6 (Salmond);
Vincent from NPNF2-11; conciliar texts from Percival, NPNF2-14; Photius from
Freese (SPCK 1920) at tertullian.org. All public domain. Catalog-level
source-library records exist for ANF vols. 3, 4 (pre-existing) and 6
(created 2026-07-24 at `src/sources/works/ante-nicene-fathers/volume-6/`);
bindings are catalog-level (see `source-bindings.toml`).

## Source families searched, and negative results

- Subject-authored corpus: loci above; the Commentary on Matthew 15 and On
  Prayer were deliberately not quoted (translation rights / not in ANF), and
  their evidentiary role is carried by named scholarship or the CCC's own
  citations.
- Acts of the Alexandrian proceedings: none extant; no acts, charges, or
  signatures located in any witness consulted — bounded negative central to
  the rupture section.
- Liturgical/cult family: searches for a feast, cult, canonization, or
  commemoration of Origen (general web, 2026-07-24) returned none in any
  rite or period; bounded negative, stated in the publication.
- Death/burial: no eyewitness account, no surviving identifiable tomb;
  medieval Tyre tomb tradition located (Prat) and classed L.
- Conciliar acts: the fifteen anathemas confirmed absent from the 553 acts
  as printed in the consulted editions (Percival's presentation; Tanner's
  exclusion note) — this is the documentary basis of the disputed-status
  discussion, not a new finding of this study.
- Not searched (out of scope, recorded as limits): manuscript catalogs
  beyond the BSB identification, papyrological literature, ACO critical
  text, Justinian's Liber adversus Origenem in full, Epiphanius's Panarion,
  the Philocalia's text, catena fragments.
