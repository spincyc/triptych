\subsection*{Primary sources: Origen}

\begin{itemize}
\item Origen, works in English translation: \textit{The Ante-Nicene
Fathers}, vol.~4, ed.\ A.~Roberts, J.~Donaldson, A.~C.~Coxe (Buffalo:
Christian Literature Publishing Co., 1885), trans.\ F.~Crombie. Quotations
follow the transcriptions at New Advent: \work{On First Principles},
preface and book 1
(\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04120.htm},
\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04121.htm}), with Rufinus's
translator's preface as printed in NPNF series 2, vol.~3
(\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2712.htm}); \work{Against Celsus},
preface and book 1 (\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04161.htm});
\work{Letter to Gregory}
(\url{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0415.htm}). The translations are
public domain; the underlying Greek and Latin are ancient and public
domain.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Modern scholarship}

\begin{itemize}
\item M.~J.~Edwards, ``Origen,'' \textit{The Stanford Encyclopedia of
Philosophy} (entry as revised 21 July 2026)
(\url{https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/origen/}); consulted for the
current critical consensus on the self-mutilation report (with its
references to C.~Markschies and to Epiphanius, \work{Panarion} 64.2), the
death, and the fate of the corpus; quoted in brief attributed snippets.
\item F.~Diekamp, \textit{Die origenistischen Streitigkeiten im sechsten
Jahrhundert und das f\"unfte allgemeine Concil} (M\"unster, 1899); position
cited as reported in Prat 1911 (above). Not independently inspected.
\item R.~Price, \textit{The Acts of the Council of Constantinople of 553}
(Liverpool, 2009); position cited as reported in the survey literature on
the council. Not independently inspected; no page-level claim is made.
\item J.~A.~McGuckin (ed.), \textit{The Westminster Handbook to Origen}
(Louisville, 2004); skeptical judgment on the self-castration report cited
as reported in the survey literature. Not independently inspected.
\item H.~Crouzel, \textit{Orig\`ene} (Paris, 1985; English trans.\
A.~S.~Worrall, \textit{Origen}, Edinburgh, 1989); H.~de Lubac,
\textit{Histoire et esprit: l'intelligence de l'\'Ecriture d'apr\`es
Orig\`ene} (Paris, 1950); J.~Dani\'elou, \textit{Orig\`ene} (Paris, 1948);
H.~U.~von Balthasar, \textit{Origenes: Geist und Feuer} (Salzburg, 1938).
Cited for the shape of the twentieth-century rehabilitation at the level
of publication facts and widely reported theses; none is quoted, and no
page-level claim is made.
\item M.~Molin Pradel and L.~Perrone on Munich, Bayerische
Staatsbibliothek, Cod.\ graec.\ 314: identification announced 2012;
critical edition L.~Perrone et al., \textit{Origenes: Die neuen
Psalmenhomilien} (GCS N.F.~19, Berlin, 2015); cited at the level of the
published identification and edition record.
\end{itemize}
