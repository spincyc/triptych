# Saint Paul — source audit

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Every check below was performed 2026-07-25
against the named online witness. "Checked" means the passage was retrieved

## Translation basis

Scripture: Challoner Douay–Rheims at drbo.org (public domain; translates
the Vulgate; its versification occasionally shifts against modern
numbering and its Vulgate readings are flagged in place — goad in Acts
9:5; "Lystra" for Myra in Acts 27:5). Apostolic Fathers: ANF as
transcribed at New Advent (1 Clement attributed there to John Keith,
ANF 9; Ignatius middle recension and Polycarp, Roberts–Donaldson, ANF 1).
Irenaeus: Roberts–Rambaut, ANF 1. Tertullian: Holmes, ANF 3. Muratorian
fragment: ANF 5 translation at earlychristianwritings.com. Eusebius:
McGiffert, NPNF2-1. Jerome: Richardson, NPNF2-3. Chrysostom: NPNF1-11.
Tacitus: Church–Brodribb 1876 (Wikisource). Suetonius: Rolfe 1914
(Wikisource). Acts of Paul: M. R. James 1924
(earlychristianwritings.com). Deissmann: Strachan translation 1912
(archive.org scan, read in full for Appendix I). All public domain.
Catalog-level source-library records exist and are bound for ANF 1,
NPNF2-1 (created for this study), and CE vol. 11 (created for this
study); see `source-bindings.toml`.

## Modern scholarship access limits

No modern critical monograph, commentary, or journal article was
inspected; modern-critical claims in the publication are limited to
(a) the Deissmann study read in full, (b) positions reported in the
checked Benedict XVI catecheses and CE 1911, and (c) commonplaces

the 2009 sarcophagus testing (terms: "san paolo sarcophagus carbon 14

and located announcement-level coverage only; recorded as a bounded
negative result.

## Source families searched and negative results (bounded)

Literal searches over the English transcriptions named above, 2026-07-25:

- New Testament (DR text of the fetched chapters): no narrative of Paul's
  death; no mention of Roman citizenship in the letters; no mention of
  Paul's letters in Acts.
- 1 Clement 5: no city, date, or manner of death stated; "extreme limit
  of the west" ambiguous.
- Checked witnesses before c. 160: no physical description; no execution
  scene; no beheading statement earlier than the Acts of Paul /
  Tertullian stratum.
- Checked witnesses: no ancient death-year except Nero-regnal variants;
  no ancient evidence for 29 June as death day.
- These results cannot exclude unsearched works, other languages, or
  unindexed witnesses.
