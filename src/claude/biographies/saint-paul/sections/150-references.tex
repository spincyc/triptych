\subsection*{Apocrypha and later tradition}

\begin{itemize}
\item \work{The Acts of Paul} (apocryphal; per the translator's headnote,
composed by a presbyter of Asia and datable c.~A.D.\ 160 from
Tertullian's notice), including the \work{Acts of Paul and Thecla} and
the \work{Martyrdom}, in M.~R. James, \work{The Apocryphal New
Testament} (Oxford: Clarendon, 1924), as transcribed at Early Christian
Writings (\url{https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/actspaul.html}).
\item Ferdinand Prat, ``St.~Paul,'' \textit{The Catholic Encyclopedia},
vol.~11 (New York, 1911), at New Advent
(\url{https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11567b.htm}); source for the
Aquae Salviae/Tre Fontane execution tradition, the Nero-regnal death-year
variants (Epiphanius, Euthalius, Jerome), the \textit{kata ton auton
kairon} judgment, and the feast history (29 June; 30 June commemoration;
25 January conversion feast). A catalog-level source-library record of CE
vol.~11 was created for this study and is bound in
\texttt{research/source-bindings.toml}.
\item Pseudo-Marcellus, \work{Acts of Peter and Paul} (fifth century),
and Pseudo-Abdias, \work{Passion of Paul} (fourth century): cited only at
the reported level from Benedict XVI's General Audience of 4 February
2009 (below); not independently inspected.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{The Gallio anchor}

\begin{itemize}
\item Adolf Deissmann, \work{St.~Paul: A Study in Social and Religious
History}, trans.\ L.~R.~M. Strachan (London: Hodder and Stoughton, 1912),
Appendix~I, ``The Proconsulate of L.~Junius Gallio,'' pp.~235--260, with
phototype facsimile of the Delphi inscription; read in full in the
public scan at the Internet Archive
(\url{https://archive.org/details/stpaulstudyinsoc00deis}, plain text at
\texttt{stpaulstudyinsoc00deis\_djvu.txt}). Basis for the dating of
Claudius's 26th imperatorial acclamation (with Dessau's collation) and of
Gallio's proconsulship from the summer of 51.
\item Benedict XVI, General Audience of 27 August 2008 (English),
Vatican website
(\url{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/audiences/2008/documents/hf_ben-xvi_aud_20080827.html});
the modern official statement of the anchor (``Gallio was Proconsul in
Corinth between the years 51 and 53'') and of the c.~AD~8 birth
convention.
\end{itemize}
