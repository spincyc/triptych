## Territory, language, and date

Rite: Roman. Form: the Order of Mass of the postconciliar Roman Missal. Language of the
controlling text: Latin. Vernacular in view: English. Territory: the dioceses of the United
States of America. As-of date for all territorial and disciplinary statements: 25 July 2026.

## Editions deliberately not used

The 1970 and 1975 typical editions (cited only for their existence from previously registered
decrees), the *Ordo lectionum Missae* (identified as the Instruction's own authority but not
examined, and its text nowhere reproduced), the *Graduale Romanum* and *Graduale Simplex* (named
by the Instruction, not examined), any publisher-specific altar book, and the 1962 Benziger
*editio iuxta typicam*.
