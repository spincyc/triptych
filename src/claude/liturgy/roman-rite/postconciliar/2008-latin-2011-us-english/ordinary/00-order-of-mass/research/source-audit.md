# Source audit

Every consequential claim in the publication rests on a source reached and read for this study,
at the loci below. Availability, acquisition, inspection, and verification are distinguished
throughout; the ceilings recorded here are the ones the publication asserts.

### 1.8 Ambrose / *De sacramentis*, PL 16

New records: work `work.migne.patrologia-latina-16`, edition `…paris-1880`, artifacts
`leaf-235-image` and `leaf-236-image` (storage `remote`, public domain, US), passages `coll-462`

The Internet Archive OCR of the volume was used only to locate the passage. The quoted Latin of

## 2. Consequential negative results

1. The 2008 altar book itself was not located as a distributable or authenticatable artifact.
   Every 2008 claim in the publication is mediated by the published variation list, and the
   Scope appendix says so.
2. USCCB General Instruction chapters III and IV were not obtainable (see 1.5). The publication
   declares the resulting gap in place rather than filling it by inference.
3. No edition of the anaphora of St Basil was obtained. The claim about Prayer IV is therefore
   made about form only, and the note says so explicitly.
4. No official act was found identifying the sources of Prayers II, III, and IV by name. The
   dependence of Prayer II on the Verona text is demonstrated from the texts themselves, not
   asserted from an official statement; the composition of III and IV is described from their own
   structure. Drafting attributions circulating in the literature are not repeated.

## 3. Boundary between quoted evidence and protected text

The Latin *editio typica* is quoted as evidence. The approved 2011 English of the Order of Mass
is not reproduced anywhere in the publication; four short phrases are quoted where an argument
turns on exact wording ("and with your spirit," "consubstantial with the Father," "for many,"
"under my roof"), each inside a discussion of the documented change that produced it. No
approved English is paraphrased closely enough to reconstruct it. The consequences are declared
at the affected elements — Collect, Prayer over the Offerings, Prayer after Communion, the four
dismissals — and section 11.5 states what the constraint left undone: no 1973/2011 side-by-side,
no assessment of the 2011 Roman Canon, no judgement on singability. The Lectionary text is never
reproduced. No scriptural passage required English quotation; loci are cited instead, so the
repository's Douay–Rheims records were not needed and are not bound.

Short phrases from *Liturgiam authenticam* and from the 2006 circular letter are quoted because
those instruments' own wording is the evidence for what was required; the General Instruction is
quoted in Latin and described in English.
