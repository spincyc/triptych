# Sequence inventory

One row per received boundary of the Order of Mass, in the Missal's own order and numbering.
Columns: the Missal number; the Latin incipit or received name that bounds the unit; the
speaker or agent and the addressee where the rite prescribes one; status (fixed, conditional,
optional, territorial); and the coverage actually given in the publication. Coverage codes:
**L** literal ritual function, **T** theological or doctrinal reading, **H** history or
development, **W** relation to the whole action, **E** treatment of the 2011 English.

These are coverage controls. They are not visible fields in the publication, and the depth given
to each unit is proportionate to its significance rather than uniform.

## Proportionality check

The units receiving the fullest treatment are the Eucharistic Prayer's common grammar, the Roman
Canon, Prayers II–IV, the Penitential Act, the Creed, the Universal Prayer, the Preparation of
the Gifts, and the Communion Rite's invitation and Communion — that is, the units where the rite
carries doctrine, where the reform made its deepest changes, or where the 2011 English changed a
text the people say. Units treated briefly (nn. 3, 12, 21, 27, 135–137, 140, 143) are those
whose ritual function is plain and whose history adds nothing the whole-order account does not
already carry. No unit was omitted from the inventory; three units (nn. 135, and the postures
and manner of reception governed by *IGMR* 160 and 281–287) are marked as declared source gaps
rather than covered.
