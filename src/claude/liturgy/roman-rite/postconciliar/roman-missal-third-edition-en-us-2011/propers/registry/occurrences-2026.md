## Adjacent ferial state

The Sunday's Year A readings, Gloria, Creed and Sunday Preface are not carried into the following weekday. The national calendar assigns the ferias of that week the independent Cycle II course: Monday 27 July, no. 401; Tuesday 28 July, no. 402; Wednesday 29 July is the Memorial of Saints Martha, Mary and Lazarus with no. 403 and the proper Gospel from no. 607; Thursday 30 July, no. 404, whose Gospel is Matthew 13:47–53. Any ferial use of the Week XVII Missal formulary would be a recorded local selection under the Ordinary Time rubrics and GIRM 363, not a continuation of the Sunday celebration.

The same rule governs Week XVI: the ferias following 19 July 2026 take the independent Cycle II course (Monday 20 July, no. 395, through Saturday 25 July, the Feast of Saint James, no. 605), and any ferial use of the Week XVI Missal formulary would likewise be a recorded local selection rather than a continuation of the Sunday.

For Week XXV the days following 20 September 2026 likewise take the independent Cycle II course, except where a feast has its own readings: Monday 21 September is the Feast of Saint Matthew with its proper readings, no. 643; Tuesday 22 September, no. 450; Wednesday 23 September is the Memorial of Saint Pius of Pietrelcina with no. 451; Thursday 24 through Saturday 26 September, nos. 452–454. Any ferial use of the Week XXV Missal formulary would be a recorded local selection and not a continuation of the Sunday.
