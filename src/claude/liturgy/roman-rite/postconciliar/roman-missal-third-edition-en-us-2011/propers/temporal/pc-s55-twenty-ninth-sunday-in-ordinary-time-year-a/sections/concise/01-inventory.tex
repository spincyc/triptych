\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Entrance Antiphon & \latin{Ego clamavi, quoniam exaudisti me, Deus}; cf.\ Ps
17 (16):6, 8, adapted & One who calls on God because God hears asks to be kept
as the apple of the eye, under the shadow of his wings.\\
Collect & \latin{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, fac nos tibi semper} & That the
assembly may always hold its will devoted to God and serve his majesty
\latin{sincero corde}, with an undivided heart.\\
First Reading & Is 45:1, 4--6 (vv.~2--3 not read) & The Lord grasps the right
hand of ``my anointed Cyrus'', who has ``not known'' him, that all may know
``there is none besides me''.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Ps 96 (95):1, 3, 4--5, 7--8, 9--10a, 10c; response from
v.~7b & The nations are to bring the Lord glory; their gods are nothing; he
reigns and will judge the peoples with justice.\\
Second Reading & 1~Thes 1:1--5b & To the church ``in God the Father'', to whom
the gospel came ``not \ldots\ in word only, but in power also''.\\
Alleluia verse & Phil 2:15d, 16a & They shine as lights in the world, holding
forth the word of life.\\
Gospel & Mt 22:15--21 & Asked whether tribute is lawful, Jesus calls the
flatterers hypocrites, has them name the coin's image, and says, ``Render
therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar's; and to God, the things that
are God's.''\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \latin{Tribue nos, Domine, quaesumus, donis tuis}
& That those who serve God's own gifts may do so \latin{libera mente} and be
cleansed by the mysteries they serve.\\
Communion Antiphon, first option & \latin{Ecce oculi Domini super timentes
eum}; cf.\ Ps 33 (32):18--19 & The eyes of the Lord rest on those who fear him
and hope in his mercy, to rescue them from death and feed them in famine.\\
Communion Antiphon, second option & \latin{Filius hominis venit}; Mk 10:45,
adapted & The Son of Man came to give his life as a ransom for many.\\
Prayer after Communion & \latin{Fac nos, quaesumus, Domine, caelestium rerum}
& That sharing in heavenly things may make the assembly advance, aided by
temporal benefits and instructed by eternal ones.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
