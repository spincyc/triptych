Rubric 6 of \incipit{Tempus per annum} directs that, of the two Communion
antiphons, preference be given to the one that accords with the Gospel of the
Mass. Neither Week XVI antiphon is drawn from Matthew 13, so that preference
supplies no resolution here; the two remain open alternatives under the general
norm.
