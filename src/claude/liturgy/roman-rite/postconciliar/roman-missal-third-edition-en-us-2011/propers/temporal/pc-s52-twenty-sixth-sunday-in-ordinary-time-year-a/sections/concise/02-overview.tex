\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:start}
\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & Ezekiel answers exiles who
call the Lord's way unfair: the just man who turns to iniquity dies, the wicked
man who turns lives. Jesus asks the chief priests and ancients which of two
sons did the father's will, and says that the publicans and harlots who
believed John go before them. Paul asks for one mind and humility after the
mind of Christ, who in the longer form emptied himself and obeyed unto the
cross.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & The father is God,
who calls by an inward inspiration, and his ``to day'' is the whole of a life
(Aquinas, on Matthew). The sons are the nations, who refused and then
believed, and the people who promised at Sinai and did not go (Jerome,
Chrysostom, Aquinas); Hilary reverses them. In the longer form, Christ is
the Son who obeyed and so honoured the Father (Chrysostom), the lowly God who
found proud man (Augustine).\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Let no one in vice despair, and
no one in virtue slumber (Chrysostom); no one has promised you tomorrow
(Augustine); the worst is he who does not repent of what he has done
(Aquinas), who applies the second son to clerics and religious. Count others
better than yourself, by the divine image in them if by nothing else
(Aquinas); where the second Communion antiphon is chosen, begin to lay down
your life by giving to a brother in need (Augustine, on 1~John 3).\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & Each will be judged in
that in which he is found (Jerome, on Ezekiel), so the penitent must persevere
(Aquinas), and merits meet for the last judgment will be required (Augustine,
on the psalm). The shepherd chooses the simple from each nation for eternal
life (Gregory, on John 10:16). Exaltation follows humility (Hilary, on the
servant's lowliness, where the first Communion antiphon is chosen; Aquinas, on
Philippians, where the longer form is read), and in that form every knee bows
at the name of Jesus; the communicants ask to be fellow heirs of his glory.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
