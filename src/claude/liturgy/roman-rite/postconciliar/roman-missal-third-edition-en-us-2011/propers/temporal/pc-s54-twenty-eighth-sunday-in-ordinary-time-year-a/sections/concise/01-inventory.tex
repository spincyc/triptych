% both Communion antiphons shown as the alternatives they are. The four

\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Entrance Antiphon & \latin{Si iniquitates observaveris}; Ps 130 (129):3--4,
adapted & If the Lord kept account of iniquities, no one could stand; with him
there is propitiation. It closes by naming the God of Israel.\\
Collect & \latin{Tua nos, quaesumus, Domine, gratia} & That the Lord's grace
always go before and follow the assembly, and keep it constantly intent on
good works.\\
First Reading & Is 25:6--10a & On this mountain the Lord makes a feast for all
peoples, casts death down for ever and wipes away tears from every face.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Ps 23 (22):1--3a, 3b--4, 5, 6; response from v.~6cd &
The Lord shepherds the singer, who wants nothing; a table prepared, the head
anointed, the cup; mercy following to the house of the Lord.\\
Second Reading & Phil 4:12--14, 19--20; vv.~15--18 not read & Paul knows how
to be full and hungry and can do all things in him who strengthens him; God
supplies their want; a doxology.\\
Alleluia verse & cf.\ Eph 1:17--18, recast in the first person plural & That
the Father enlighten the eyes of our heart, to know the hope of our calling.\\
Gospel & Longer form: Mt 22:1--14 & The invited refuse the king's wedding; the
hall is filled with bad and good; a guest without a wedding garment is cast
out; many are called, few chosen.\\
Gospel & Shorter form: Mt 22:1--10 & The same parable as far as the filling of
the hall, without the guest and the closing saying.\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \latin{Suscipe, Domine, fidelium preces} & The
faithful's prayers are offered with the gifts, that through these acts of
devout worship they may pass over to heavenly glory.\\
Communion Antiphon & First option: \latin{Divites eguerunt et esurierunt};
cf.\ Ps 34 (33):11 & The rich have known want and hunger; those who seek the
Lord will lack no good thing.\\
Communion Antiphon & Second option: \latin{Cum apparuerit Dominus}; 1~Jn 3:2,
adapted & When the Lord appears, his own will be like him, because they will
see him as he is.\\
Prayer after Communion & \latin{Maiestatem tuam, Domine, suppliciter
deprecamur} & As the Lord feeds the assembly with the Body and Blood of
Christ, so may he make them partakers of the divine nature.\\
\end{concisemaptable}

\vspace{0.2em}
{\footnotesize\noindent The two forms of the Gospel are alternatives, and so
are the two Communion antiphons (the second under \latin{Vel}); every other
element is required. Scripture is quoted in the Douay--Rheims (Challoner), a
public-domain study translation, not the Lectionary's approved English
proclaimed at Mass; psalms are numbered as in the Lectionary, the Vulgate's
number in parentheses.\par}
