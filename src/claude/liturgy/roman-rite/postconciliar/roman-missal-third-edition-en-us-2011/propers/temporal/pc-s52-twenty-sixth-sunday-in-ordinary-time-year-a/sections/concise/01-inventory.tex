\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Entrance Antiphon & \latin{Omnia, quae fecisti nobis}; Dan 3:29--31, 42--43,
adapted & Azarias's prayer: all the Lord did to his people was true judgment,
for they sinned; may he glorify his name and show mercy.\\
Collect & \latin{Deus, qui omnipotentiam tuam} & God's almighty power is shown
above all in sparing and mercy; those who run toward his promises ask to share
the goods of heaven.\\
First Reading & Ezek 18:25--28 & To the complaint that the Lord's way is not
right: the just man who turns to iniquity dies; the wicked man who turns to
justice lives.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Ps 25 (24):4--5, 6--7, 8--9; response v.~6a & A prayer to
be shown God's ways and to be remembered in compassion, not for the sins of
youth; he gives a law to sinners and teaches the meek.\\
Second Reading & Longer form: Phil 2:1--11 & One mind and humility, each
esteeming others better; the mind of Christ Jesus, who emptied himself, obeyed
unto the cross and was exalted.\\
Second Reading & Shorter form: Phil 2:1--5 & The same appeal, ending with the
call to have the mind of Christ Jesus.\\
Alleluia verse & Jn 10:27, speaker formula added & ``My sheep hear my voice.
And I know them: and they follow me.''\\
Gospel & Mt 21:28--32 & The son who refused and then went and the son who
promised and did not; the publicans and harlots who believed John go first.\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \latin{Concede nobis, misericors Deus} & That the
offering be accepted and open the fountain of every blessing.\\
Communion Antiphon & First option: \latin{Memento verbi tui}; cf.\ Ps
119 (118):49--50 & Adapted: the servant asks God to remember the word in which
he gave him hope, his comfort in lowliness.\\
Communion Antiphon & Second option: \latin{In hoc cognovimus}; 1~Jn 3:16 & God's charity is known in that he laid down his life for us; so we ought
to lay down our lives for the brethren.\\
Prayer after Communion & \latin{Sit nobis, Domine, reparatio mentis et
corporis} & Repair of mind and body by the heavenly mystery; a share in
Christ's glory as fellow heirs for those who share his sufferings.\\
\end{concisemaptable}

{\footnotesize\noindent The two forms of the second reading are alternatives,
and so are the two Communion antiphons (the second under \latin{Vel}); every
other element is required. Scripture is quoted in the Douay--Rheims
(Challoner), a public-domain study translation, not the Lectionary's approved
English proclaimed at Mass; psalms are numbered as in the Lectionary, the
Vulgate's number in parentheses.\par}
