## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified content of the record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | Psalm 54:6, 8 (printed as *Ps 53, 6.8*) | God as helper and upholder of the soul; willing sacrifice and praise of the name. |
| Collect | *Propitiare, Domine, famulis tuis, et clementer gratiae tuae super eos dona multiplica, ut, spe, fide et caritate ferventes, semper in mandatis tuis vigili custodia perseverent* | Petition for multiplication of gifts of grace already given, so that the three theological virtues may burn and perseverance in the commandments be kept under a watchful guard. | Prayer over the Offerings | *Deus, qui legalium differentiam hostiarum unius sacrificii perfectione sanxisti … et pari benedictione, sicut munera Abel, sanctifica … ut, quod singuli obtulerunt … cunctis proficiat ad salutem* | The diversity of the sacrifices of the Law ratified in the perfection of one sacrifice; blessing asked equal to that of Abel's gifts; each one's offering ordered to the salvation of all. |

| Prayer after Communion | *Populo tuo, quaesumus, Domine, adesto propitius, et, quem mysteriis caelestibus imbuisti, fac ad novitatem vitae de vetustate transire* | Presence asked for the people steeped in the heavenly mysteries, and passage from oldness into newness of life. |

No Offertory antiphon, no proper Preface, and no Eucharistic Prayer insert belong to this formulary.

## Governing rubrics read in the same edition

- *Tempus per annum* rubric 3(b): on ferias any one of the thirty-four Ordinary Time Masses may be used, according to pastoral usefulness. Chronological proximity does not make one of them appointed.
- Rubric 4: the Gloria and the Creed are said on Sundays and omitted on ferias.
- Rubric 5: unless a Eucharistic Prayer with its own Preface is used, a Preface of the Sundays in Ordinary Time is said on Sundays (the edition points to its pp. 537–544) and the common Preface on ferias.
- Rubric 6: two Communion antiphons are given, the first from the psalms and the second usually from the Gospel; either may be chosen, preferring the one that accords with the Gospel of the Mass. **In this formulary the second antiphon is from the Apocalypse and neither antiphon is drawn from any Gospel**, so the rubric's preference supplies no resolution here.
- General Instruction of the Roman Missal 363 separately permits, on Ordinary Time weekdays, orations from the preceding or from another Sunday in Ordinary Time.

## Rights and disposition

Only citations, short Latin incipits and argument-bearing Latin phrases, and original English summaries are retained. No complete oration is reproduced in either language, and no protected English translation is reproduced at all. The Latin third typical edition is not freely reproducible in bulk; the registered reproduction is restricted and its bytes are not held in this repository.
