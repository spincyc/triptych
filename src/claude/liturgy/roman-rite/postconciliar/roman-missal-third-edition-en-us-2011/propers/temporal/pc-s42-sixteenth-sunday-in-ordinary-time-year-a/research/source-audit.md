## Source classes and rights

- **Public domain:** Douay–Rheims (Challoner); ANF and NPNF translations; the Dods 1871 *City of God*; the English Dominican *Summa*; the CSEL 40 Latin; Priestley (1771); *Doctrine and Covenants* 86 in its nineteenth-century printings; Wodehouse (1907, United States); Easton (1897); Chambers's (1908).
- **Protected, not reproduced:** the United States Lectionary; the ICEL English Roman Missal. No wording from either appears in the publication.
- **Protected, quoted briefly with attribution:** the General Instruction, the General Introduction to the Lectionary, the Universal Norms, and *Dignitatis humanae*; the Latin *Missale Romanum* (short incipits and argument-bearing phrases only).
- **Restricted:** the registered digital reproduction of the 2002 Latin Missal. Its bytes are not held in this repository; only identity, hash, and review metadata are tracked centrally.
- **Corpus Thomisticum:** used as a checked Latin witness by link; no bulk text incorporated.

## Consequential negative results

1. The live USCCB readings page was unreachable; an archived capture supplied the Lectionary structure, and this substitution is disclosed in the publication.
2. No direct patristic exposition of Revelation 3:20 was read for this guide; the publication therefore supplies literary context instead of a patristic witness at that antiphon and says so.
3. No continuous patristic commentary on the discontinuous Wisdom unit was located in the checked corpus; the retained illumination is the Missal's own Week XXVI Collect.
4. *Summa theologiae* II-II q. 157 contains no citation of Wisdom 12:18.
5. Origen's extant *Commentary on Matthew* does not cover Matthew 13:24-35, so no Origenian witness supports the shorter Gospel form.
6. The interpretive-proposal searches returned no located precedent for five of the six proposals and a near analogue for the fourth; for the sixth, a checked witness (Aquinas on the birds of the mustard tree) runs against the proposal and is recorded as its controlling limit. The classifications and their boundaries are in `scope.md`.
