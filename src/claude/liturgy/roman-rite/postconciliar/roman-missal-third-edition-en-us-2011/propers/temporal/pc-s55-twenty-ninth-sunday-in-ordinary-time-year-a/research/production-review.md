### What the study contains

An opening on the tribute question, with the standing notice on the study
English where Scripture first appears, the *Ordo*'s three titles and an
eleven-row map of the appointed elements, both Communion antiphons shown as
alternatives; the appointed Scripture in the Douay–Rheims (Challoner), with the
words the Lectionary does not appoint set in brackets (Isaiah 45:2–3, the psalm
title within Psalm 95:1, vv. 2 and 6 and the middle clause of v. 10, the rest
of 1 Thessalonians 1:5, the words of Philippians 2:15–16 outside the verse, and
the parts of Psalm 16:6–8 and Mark 10:45 that the antiphons leave out); the
Entrance Antiphon, both Communion antiphons and the Alleluia verse by Latin
incipit or *Ordo* Latin and description with their departures stated and their
source verses printed as sources, not as their words; the three orations by
incipit, description and short Latin phrases; an element-by-element section
giving each text its literary, textual and liturgical setting, including the
Latin of Isaiah 45:1 and 45:4, the psalm response and v. 5, the acclamation's
mood, the Gospel's two-horned question, and the early witnesses of the three
orations; three interpretations of the whole formulary, each carrying all
eleven element keys, naming its difficulties and closing with its own four
senses; a comparison; a terminal Scriptural Date and Location appendix that
prints the twelve generated orientation rows once with their place phrases and
gives each passage its fuller evidence beneath, the reviewed home of the
chronology the concise page 2 will reprint; the Liturgical Resolution appendix
with the twelve branch IDs of the manifest; the Scope and Qualifications
appendix; and References. One `\label{proper-<element-key>}` stands at each of
the eleven appointed texts, immediately after a heading naming only that
element.

### Bounds observed

- RES-015 (advisory): the third interpretation anchors the difficulty in
  Matthew 22:18, "knowing their wickedness" (*Cognita autem Iesus nequitia
  eorum*), the lemma under which Jerome and Aquinas write, and says only that
  Matthew does not say whether anyone else knew the questioners' purpose
  beforehand. Jerome is not cited for the Godhead, the consultation is not
  called open, and Chrysostom is not credited with an argument from the crowd.
- RES-014 (advisory): the Psalms composition row carries only "Place of
  composition unknown", and the scope appendix says the Psalter bound was read
  in a current public delivery of the introduction, not in the registered
  state. The record inconsistency the advisory names is in `research/scope.md`
  and `research/source-bindings.toml`, which this stage does not own; it is
  left for the research owner.
- The image reading is presented as the Fathers' spiritual reading of the coin,
  never put in Jesus' mouth, with Jerome's literal answer beside it; Hilary's
  premise (origin) and Augustine's (image) are kept distinct; Ambrose is cited
  as commenting on Luke.
- The *assimilavi* typology is reported as Jerome's on the Vulgate's word, with
  the Lectionary's English and Aquinas against it; Cyrus is used as instrument,
  not type. The "Christ my Lord" variant is told and not built on.
- The two grounds of Caesar's claim (possession: Hilary, Ambrose; justice:
  Chrysostom, Augustine) are stated as a disagreement and not merged.
- On the counter-question the witnesses are presented as converging; none is
  set against another.
- Under `research/scope.md` section 3.10 and *Nostra aetate* 4, none of the
  polemical sentences named there is used, Jerome's report on the Pharisees'
  view of tribute is not used, the whited wall is used without its
  anti-Donatist application, Augustine's "Reign by the Cross" sentence is not
  used, and the agents are kept to those the Gospel names.
- Communion antiphon B's contrast of service is credited to the antiphon's
  context, not its words, and every such clause is marked as holding only where
  that antiphon is sung.
- No claim rests on the second noun of the psalm response or on the mood of the
  acclamation's English verb. "From the wood" is presented as Augustine's Old
  Latin text, not the appointed verse. No imperial echo of *maiestas* is
  asserted.
- Schuster and Fromage are not cited in the readings; their co-occurring
  Gospel and Psalm 16:6 are named in one clause of the scope appendix only,
  since their pages were read in OCR only (scope lead 9). Honorius is not used.
- No other Mass of the present Missal (the antiphons' other uses, the Year B
  acclamation) is named. The sacramentary witnesses of the orations are given
  in the element section only, without a claim about their route or purpose.
- No mission observance is asserted for the date; the
  `mass-for-evangelization-of-peoples` branch is listed as conditional and not
  supplied.
- No date figure is printed outside the generated Date cells; the place
  phrases are those planned in `research/scope.md` section 8.3, in places
  shortened, with nothing added except the clause that David's reign dates
  David and not the psalm.

### Repairs

- STU-001: the body no longer narrates its own evidence discipline. The
  recurrences paragraph, the psalm-title sentence, the Collect, Prayer over the
  Offerings and Prayer after Communion settings, the opening's crediting
  sentence and the three lane introductions now state the texts and the
  readings without disclaiming design or intention; the negative results on the
  orations and the statement that no witness joins Isaiah 45 to Matthew 22 stand
  only in the scope appendix, which already carried them. The "the join is the
  editor's", "the interpretation does not ..." and "the studies say so"
  sentences were rewritten so that each Father is credited only with what he
  says and each connection is stated as the reading's ("Read beside the
  Gospel", "On this reading", "Carried through the whole Mass"). Each "Where
  this interpretation strains" section now states its reading's real difficulty
  once. Kept beside their claims: dependence on Communion antiphon B and the
  contrast with the rulers as its unsung context; Ambrose on Luke's form;
  Augustine's "from the wood" as his Old Latin text; *assimilavi* as the
  Clementine's word, absent from the Lectionary's English; Jerome's "Prima
  virtus" as a rule for any answerer and a sign of no one's Godhead; "sincere"
  outside the acclamation's words; Acts 17 and Romans 13 as not read at this
  Mass; the agents as the Gospel names them.
- STU-002: the clause "whose psalter read 'worship and honour'" is removed;
  the study makes no claim about Augustine's reading at Psalm 95 (96):7. At
  this stage Augustine's Latin of *En. in Ps.* 95.8 was read in the tracked
  Migne text, which has *afferte Domino gloriam et honorem*; "worship and
  honour" is the NPNF English. For the research owner (STU-004):
  `research/interpretations.md`, Reading 1, element-contributions table,
  responsorial psalm row, carries the same mistaken parenthesis ("his v. 7
  reads 'worship and honour'") and should be corrected when that record is
  next reopened; this stage did not edit it.
- STU-003: the comparison's "Principal inference" row now credits each witness
  only with his own clause in all three columns; in the second column the join
  of Isaiah 45 to Matthew 22 stands uncredited as the reading's, under the
  Lectionary's pairing. The row formerly headed "Disagreement kept" is headed
  "Disagreement among the witnesses".
- Advisories: Matthew 21:45–46 is placed after the parable of the husbandmen,
