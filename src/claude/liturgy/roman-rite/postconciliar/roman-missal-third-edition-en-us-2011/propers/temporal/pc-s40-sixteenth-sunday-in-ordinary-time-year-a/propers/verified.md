## Relationship classification

- **Officially correlated:** Wisdom 12:13, 16–19 with the Gospel. The General Introduction to the Lectionary 66–67 establishes that in Ordinary Time the Old Testament reading is harmonized with the Gospel.
- **Responsorial:** Psalm 86 to the first reading, with the refrain drawn from 86:5a.
- **Acclamatory:** the adapted Matthew 11:25 verse before the Gospel.
- **Semi-continuous:** Romans 8:26–27, an independent strand (General Introduction 67, 107). No thematic dependence on the Gospel is asserted.
- **Seasonal or ritual:** the three orations and both Communion antiphons, shared with Years B and C.
- **Textual observation:** the Collect's *vigili custodia* beside Matthew 13:25's *cum dormirent homines*; the Prayer over the Offerings' *sicut munera Abel* beside the Roman Canon's *munera pueri tui iusti Abel*; the removal of the adversary from both appointed psalm selections.
- **Documented reception:** the patristic, Thomistic, and magisterial loci recorded in `research/scope.md`.
- **Source-grounded synthesis:** the *singuli*/*cunctis* reading of the offering prayer against the servants' proposal; the observation that the Week XXVI Collect states Wisdom 12's theology in the same book.
- **Editorial or AI proposal:** the five items in the guide's interpretive section, labelled there and audited in `research/scope.md`.

## Rights, discrepancies, and disposition

The United States Lectionary, the ICEL Missal translation, the psalm response, and the official antiphon translations are protected and are not reproduced. This record retains citations, boundaries, Latin incipits, and original summaries only. Scriptural wording quoted in the publication is from the public-domain Douay–Rheims (Challoner) version, named at each use.

No discrepancy was found between the official calendar entry and the archived readings page in the Lectionary number, the reading citations, or the two Gospel forms; the calendar does not print the psalm, which the readings page supplies. No United States altar book was collated, and the 2008 emended reprint's own pages were not inspected. Independent theological review is outstanding. The leaf is source-audited at the layers stated here and is not publication-final.
