% appointed elements in the order of the Mass, the two Communion antiphons
% shown as the alternatives they are. The four overview rows follow

\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Entrance Antiphon & \latin{Salus populi ego sum}, a composed text; identified
basis Ps 37 (36):39--40 & The Lord
is his people's salvation, will hear them in any tribulation, and is their
Lord for ever.\\
Collect & \latin{Deus, qui sacrae legis omnia constituta} & The whole law rests
on love of God and neighbour; may those who keep it reach eternal life.\\
First Reading & Isa 55:6--9 & Seek the Lord while he may be found; he is
bountiful to forgive; his thoughts are not ours.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Ps 145 (144):2--3, 8--9, 17--18; response from v.~18a &
The Lord gracious, merciful and patient, just in all his ways, near to all who
call on him in truth.\\
Second Reading & Phil 1:20c--24, 27a & To live is Christ, to die is gain; Paul
stays for his people's sake.\\
Alleluia verse & \latin{Aperi, Domine, cor nostrum}; cf.\ Acts 16:14b & An
adaptation: that the Lord open our heart to heed his Son's words, as he opened
Lydia's.\\
Gospel & Mt 20:1--16a & Labourers hired at five hours and paid alike; the
murmur; the householder's reply; the last first.\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \latin{Munera, quaesumus, Domine, tuae plebis} &
May what the people profess in faith be laid hold of through the sacraments.\\
Communion Antiphon & First option: \latin{Tu mandasti mandata tua custodiri
nimis}; Ps 119 (118):4--5 & God commands that his commandments be kept most diligently; a
wish that one's ways be directed to keep them.\\
Communion Antiphon & Second option: \latin{Ego sum pastor bonus}; Jn 10:14 &
``I am the good shepherd: and I know mine, and mine know me.''\\
Prayer after Communion & \latin{Quos tuis, Domine, reficis sacramentis} & May
those restored by the sacraments possess the effect of redemption in the
mysteries and in conduct.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:end}

\vspace{0.2em}
{\footnotesize\noindent The Communion antiphons are alternatives (the second
stands under \latin{Vel}); every other element is required. Scripture is
quoted in the Douay--Rheims (Challoner), a public-domain study translation,
not the Lectionary's approved English proclaimed at Mass; psalm numbers are
the Lectionary's, with the Vulgate's in parentheses.\par}
