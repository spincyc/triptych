\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Entrance Antiphon & \latin{Ad te levavi animam meam}; cf.\ Ps 25 (24):1--3,
adapted & The speaker lifts up his soul to God in trust; none who wait for God
(\latin{qui te exspectant}) will be put to shame.\\
Collect & \latin{Da, quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} & That God give his faithful
the will to go out with just works to meet his Christ as he comes, and be set
at his right hand.\\
First Reading & Is 63:16b--17, 19b; 64:2b--7 (Douay 63:16b--17; 64:1, 3--8)
& The people ask why God let them stray; they cry, ``O that thou wouldst rend
the heavens, and wouldst come down''; they confess their sin and say, ``we are
clay: and thou art our maker''.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Ps 80 (79):2a and 2c, 3b, 15--16, 18--19; response from
v.~4 & The Shepherd of Israel is asked to come, visit the vine and lay his hand
on the son of man. Response: ``Convert us, O God: and shew us thy face, and we
shall be saved.''\\
Second Reading & 1~Cor 1:3--9 & Paul thanks God for a church that lacks no
grace as it waits for Christ's revelation; Christ will confirm it blameless;
``God is faithful.''\\
Alleluia verse & Ps 85 (84):8 & ``Shew us, O Lord, thy mercy; and grant us thy
salvation.''\\
Gospel & Mk 13:33--37 & The time is unknown; a man going abroad commands his
doorkeeper to watch; ``what I say to you, I say to all: Watch.''\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \latin{Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine, munera} & That
God accept gifts offered from his own gifts, and make the devotion of this
passing time the reward of eternal redemption.\\
Preface & Preface I of Advent, \latin{De duobus adventibus Christi} & Thanks
for Christ's first coming in lowly flesh and his second in glory, when those
who now keep watch will receive openly what was promised.\\
Communion Antiphon & \latin{Dominus dabit benignitatem}; Ps 85 (84):13 &
``The Lord will give goodness: and our earth shall yield her fruit.''\\
Prayer after Communion & \latin{Prosint nobis, quaesumus, Domine} & That the
mysteries may profit us, for by them God already teaches us, among passing
things, to love the heavenly and hold to what lasts.\\
Solemn blessing (optional) & \latin{Omnipotens et misericors Deus} & For those
who believe the Son has come and await his return: holiness; faith, hope and
charity; eternal life when he comes in majesty.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:end}

\vspace{0.1em}
{\footnotesize\noindent Violet; no Gloria; Creed. The Preface is appointed by
the Missal's rubric; the solemn blessing is optional. An approved chant may
replace either antiphon, the psalm or the Alleluia verse. Scripture is quoted in
the public-domain Douay--Rheims (Challoner), a study translation, not the
proclaimed English; the prayers are described, not translated.\par}
