## Reception witnesses

All were read at the exact loci cited in the guide's references. Translations and public Latin transcriptions were used; no critical edition was collated and no new translation was made.

- **Read in standard English translations:** Ambrose, *De officiis* II.8; Cassian, *Conferences* II; Chrysostom on Matthew 47 and on Romans 15–16; Origen, *Comm. in Matt.* X; Augustine, *Enarrationes* 68, 103 and 118/119, *De doctrina christiana* I, *De sermone Domini in monte* I, *Sermon* 74, *De civitate Dei* XVII and XX, *De praedestinatione sanctorum*, *De dono perseverantiae*; Gregory the Great, *Regula pastoralis*; Aquinas, *Summa theologiae* I q. 23, II-II qq. 45, 47, 66, 83, I-II q. 68.
- **Read in public Latin transcriptions of Migne:** Jerome and Hilary on Matthew 13; Gregory the Great, *Hom. in Evang.* I.11; Augustine's *Enarratio in Ps. CXVIII* prologue; Ambrose, *Expositio Psalmi CXVIII*, Sermones VIII and IX. **Limit:** these were read in Internet Archive OCR of nineteenth-century printings. Column numbers are not cited where they could not be confirmed, and the Ambrose material should be checked against CSEL 62, which was not reachable.
- **Read in Latin at Corpus Thomisticum:** *Super Matthaeum* XIII lect. 4; *Super Romanos* 8 lect. 6; the complete four parts of the *Summa* for the search that produced the Matthew 13:45–46 negative. **Limit:** Marietti paragraph numbers for *Super Romanos* beyond n. 702 could not be confirmed and are not cited.
- **Could not be opened at all:** Augustine's *Ad Simplicianum* I.2 and his early *Expositio quarundam propositionum ex epistula ad Romanos*. Both are used only through Augustine's own verbatim self-quotation in *De praedestinatione sanctorum* 7–8, and the guide says so.
- **Period reference work:** the 1908 *Catholic Encyclopedia* article on the *Congregatio de auxiliis*, used only for the terms of Paul V's decree of 5 September 1607, and explicitly not as an arbiter between the schools. A primary text of the decree was not located.

## Rights disposition

No protected liturgical text is reproduced in the guide or in any record of this leaf: not the English Missal, not the United States Lectionary, not the psalm response, and not the ICEL antiphons. The Latin typical edition is quoted only in short incipits and five short phrases on which stated arguments turn. Two restricted artifacts — the 2002 Missal reproduction and the *Notitiae* issue — were acquired for inspection into a session-local working directory outside the repository and are **not** tracked here; only their identity, hashes and page loci are recorded. Public-domain scriptural and literary texts are quoted freely and named as public domain at the point of use.
