\section*{References}

Only sources actually used are listed. Where an online witness was read, the delivery route is named; a link is not a claim about rights, and third-party pages retain their own status.

\subsection*{Liturgical books, instructions and calendars}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Missale Romanum}, editio typica tertia (Typis Vaticanis, 2002): \emph{Dominica XVII ``per annum''} at artifact pp.~281--282, and the Order of Mass, First Sunday of Advent, Thursday of the Fourth Week of Lent, and the Ritual Mass \emph{In dedicatione ecclesi\ae}. A digitally typeset secondary reproduction, not a page-image facsimile.
\item \emph{Missale Romanum}, editio typica tertia, \emph{reimpressio emendata} (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2008), the Latin base of the United States Missal. Not inspected; identified through the official notices below.
\item \emph{Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America}, English, implemented 27 November 2011. Not collated; no wording reproduced.
\item \emph{Lectionary for Mass for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America}, Second Typical Edition, Volume~I, no.~109. Boundaries read from the official calendar and occurrence page; no wording reproduced.
\item \emph{General Instruction of the Roman Missal}, United States English edition (2011) with the 2021 emendations, nos. 48, 87, 363, 365(b) and 365(d). \sourceurl{https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/the-mass/general-instruction-of-the-roman-missal}{usccb.org}.
\item \emph{Normae universales de anno liturgico et de calendario}, nn. 4--6, and the \emph{Calendarium Romanum generale}, entry for 26 July, both read in the 2002 \emph{Missale Romanum} artifact.
\item ``Reimpressio emendata \emph{Missalis Romani}'' and ``Variationes et additiones in \emph{Missali Romano},'' \emph{Notitiae} 44 (2008), nos. 503--504, pp. 367--387; \emph{Tempus per annum} subsection at printed p.~371. Read in the official Dicastery issue PDF, \sourceurl{https://www.cultodivino.va/}{cultodivino.va}.
\item Bishops' Conference of England and Wales, \emph{Antiphonary: Excerpted from the Roman Missal} (ICEL, 2010), p.~75. \sourceurl{https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Missal/Music/Antiphonary.pdf}{liturgyoffice.org.uk}.
\item USCCB Secretariat of Divine Worship, \emph{Liturgical Calendar for the Dioceses of the United States of America}, 2026, entry for 26 July and the following week. \sourceurl{https://www.usccb.org/resources/2026cal.pdf}{usccb.org}.
\item Official occurrence page for 26 July 2026, giving Lectionary no.~109 and the psalm response citation. \sourceurl{https://bible.usccb.org/}{bible.usccb.org}.
\item \emph{Missale Romanum} (1962), \emph{Dominica III} and \emph{Dominica XI post Pentecosten}. Consulted in the Divinum Officium project's community transcription, a secondary witness and not a collated printing. \sourceurl{https://www.divinumofficium.com/}{divinumofficium.com}.
\end{itemize}
