# Edition Manifest

Every entry names the exact witness through which the text was read for this leaf, and the state in
which it was left. `read` means the identified passage was rendered and examined; `image-collated`
means it was additionally read in a rendered page image; `reported` means the act is known only
through another source named here.

## Amendments through the cutoff

- No amendment to the Table of Liturgical Days after 1969 was located. Bounded negative result over
  the sources consulted.
- Universal additions and rank changes made to the General Roman Calendar after the printed 2002
  base are visible in the territorial calendars, and one of them - the Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother
  of the Church, on the Monday after Pentecost - is used in the publication as an example of a
  movable memorial. The individual decrees were not reached; no claim is made about their dates,
  protocol numbers or complete enumeration.

## Non-controlling aids

- The Gregorian computus, applied to compute Easter and every date derived from it. It is a
  computation, not an authority, and every result it produced was checked against an official
  witness before publication.
- No commercial, devotional or aggregated calendar was used at any point.
