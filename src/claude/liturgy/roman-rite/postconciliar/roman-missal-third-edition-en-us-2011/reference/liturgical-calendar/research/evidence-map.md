# Evidence Map

Each row names a principal published claim, the witness that carries it, the evidentiary state, and
the qualification that travels with it. States follow the universal standard: *verified source text*
(wording read in the identified artifact at the recorded locus), *checked paraphrase* (the published
statement checked at an exact locus), *source-grounded synthesis* (an editorial conclusion resting on
named loci), and *project judgment* (labelled argument beyond the sources).
