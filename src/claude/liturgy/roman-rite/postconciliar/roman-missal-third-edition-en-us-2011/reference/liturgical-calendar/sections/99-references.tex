\subsection{Controlling books}

\begin{itemize}
\item \latin{Missale Romanum, ex decreto Sacrosancti Oecumenici Concilii Vaticani II instauratum,
auctoritate Pauli PP.\ VI promulgatum, Ioannis Pauli PP.\ II cura recognitum}, \latin{editio typica
tertia}. Civitas Vaticana: Typis Vaticanis, 2002. Containing, and cited here as:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \latin{Institutio generalis Missalis Romani}, nn.~53, 68, 353--355, 357--358, 363, 372,
  374--378, cited as \loc{igmr};
  \item \latin{Normae universales de anno liturgico et de calendario}, nn.~1--61 with the
  \latin{Tabula dierum liturgicorum secundum ordinem praecedentiae disposita}, cited as \loc{nualc};
  \item \latin{Calendarium Romanum Generale}, including the movable entries appended to December and
  June and the convention that an entry without a stated grade is an optional memorial;
  \item the rubrics \latin{Tempus ``per annum''} 1--6, and the formularies of
  \latin{Hebdomada I} and \latin{Hebdomada XXXIV ``per annum''};
  \item the rubric \latin{In Baptismate Domini}; the heading \latin{In feriis temporis Nativitatis};
  the rubric on the announcement of movable feasts at the Epiphany;
  \item \latin{Tabella temporaria praecipuarum celebrationum anni liturgici}, civil years 2000--2023.
  \end{itemize}
\item \latin{Ordo lectionum Missae}, \latin{editio typica altera}. Civitas Vaticana: Libreria
Editrice Vaticana, 1981. \latin{Praenotanda} nn.~65, 66 with n.~102 of the notes, 69, 103, 104 with
nn.~115--116 of the notes, cited as \loc{olm}; with the decree of promulgation of 25 May 1969
(prot.\ 106/69) and the decree \latin{De editione typica altera} of 21 January 1981
(prot.\ CD 240/81) in the front matter.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Promulgating and governing acts}

\begin{itemize}
\item Second Vatican Council, Constitution \emph{Sacrosanctum Concilium}, 4 December 1963,
arts.\ 102--111 and the appended \latin{declaratio de calendario recognoscendo}. Acta Apostolicae

\item Paul VI, \latin{Litterae Apostolicae motu proprio datae} \emph{Mysterii Paschalis},

Chapter~III n.~36.
\item \latin{Codex Iuris Canonici}, 25 January 1983, can.\ 1246 \S\S1--2.
\item Congregation for Divine Worship and the Discipline of the Sacraments, notification Prot.\
n.\ 2671/98/L, 8 December 1998, \emph{De occurrentia memoriae obligatoriae Immaculati Cordis
beatae Mariae Virginis una cum altera memoria eiusdem gradus}. Official Latin web delivery:

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Territorial witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item United States Conference of Catholic Bishops, Secretariat of Divine Worship, \emph{Liturgical
Calendar for the Dioceses of the United States of America}, editions for 2026, 2027 and 2028.
Washington, DC: USCCB. Used as the competent territorial witness to the resolution of those three
liturgical years, to the provincial distribution of the Ascension, to the Hawaii holy-day decree of
23 March 1992, to the application of the complementary norm to canon 1246~\S2 in 2026, to the 1998
notification on the Immaculate Heart, and to the identity of the current Missal and Lectionary
editions in use. All rights in those documents are reserved by their publisher; they are cited, not
reproduced.
\end{itemize}

of the 2018 decree inscribing the Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of the Church in the General Roman

tried, and both were initially reported from the territorial calendar. An independent subsequent

canon 1246~\S2 returned an access refusal to this session on 25 July 2026. Its effect is reported
