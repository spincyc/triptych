# Advent primer: source audit

Collated on 2026-11-18 (UTC) by lane C1 from the phase 2 dossiers (R1-R4), the phase 3 acquisition notes (A1-A4) and the library records under `src/sources/`. Every row names one library record; its evidence state is the record's, read by script from the record and its checked transcription on 2026-11-18. The bound records, their states and fingerprints are in `source-bindings.toml` beside this file.

Evidence states: **verified source text** (the record is verified and the wording stands in its checked transcription or verbatim extract); **checked quotation** (the wording was checked against an exact witness short of verification: a web text only inspected, a dossier reading of the 1962 Missal pages, or a page image read by this lane for a record not yet inspected); **source-grounded synthesis** (a statement inferred or computed from the named witness).

Standing qualifications that apply to every row that cites them:

- **AAS 52 (1960 rubrics).** The records say "read on the microfilm page image". Lane A3 found that the vatican.va "-ocr.pdf" pages are ABBYY re-typesetting, not facsimiles; lane F1 registered a microfilm facsimile for pp. 589-745. Which rendering each AAS 52 record was read on is not settled here. Treat the wording as verified against the registered microfilm only once that is confirmed.
- **Latin of Gregory's homilies.** PL 76 was not acquired (TLS failure). No Latin Gregory is verified; English rests on Bute and on the Catena.
- **Catena Aurea.** Its excerpts join or condense passages. Attribute such text "as the Catena gives it".
- **Bute 1908.** It prints the pre-1960 nine-lesson Office. Its homily numbering is unreliable: it heads Hom. 6 as "10th". Cite Gregory by the standard numbering.
- **The 1962 Office.** No printed 1961-62 Breviary was read. The one-nocturn form is stated from the rubrics (nn. 162 a, 220, 222 a). The Isaiah course is that of the 1888 Breviary, which R2 found matches the 1942 Breviary day for day.

## Law statements

Each discipline the primer states, with its body of law, territory and date. "As of" means checked against the held text on 2026-11-18 for the primer's as-of date, 29 November 2026. Cells and passage ids are in `law-table.md` in the writer's pack.

- **Universal Latin law, 1918-1983 (1917 Code).** Territory: Latin Church, worldwide. Date: in force 19 May 1918 to 26 Nov 1983. can. 1252 par. 2: fast and abstinence on the Ember days and the Vigil of the Nativity; par. 4: lapses on Sunday, vigils not anticipated. can. 1108 par. 2: solemn nuptial blessing forbidden Advent I to Christmas inclusive.
- **S. C. Concilii decree, 3 Dec 1959 (AAS 51, 918).** Territory: Latin Church, worldwide. Date: from 3 Dec 1959. Faculty to anticipate the Vigil fast and abstinence to 23 December.
- **1960 Code of Rubrics (AAS 52).** Territory: Roman Rite. Date: from 1 Jan 1961; the 1962 books. Advent bounds (n. 71), ranks (nn. 11, 24-25), precedence (nn. 15, 30), colours (n. 131), Matins (nn. 162 a, 169, 213, 220, 221 a, 222 a), commemorations (n. 109), votive Masses (nn. 384, 387), Ite missa est (n. 507).
- **US particular discipline, 1889 (Baltimore Manual).** Territory: United States; some dioceses excepted (Province of New Orleans). Date: 1889; survival after 1918 unresolved. Fridays of Advent fast days; Ember days; Vigil of Christmas.
- **Paenitemini, 17 Feb 1966.** Territory: Universal Church (norms for the Latin Church and rites). Date: from 1966; ages superseded. Penitential days: Fridays and Lent (Ash Wednesday); no Advent day named; conferences may substitute.
- **NCCB pastoral statement, 18 Nov 1966.** Territory: United States. Date: effective 27 Nov 1966; in force as of 2026-11-29. Advent: meditation and liturgical participation (¶7); vigils and Ember days no longer oblige (¶17); Friday remains penitential (¶¶22-25).
- **1983 Code, cc. 1246-1247.** Territory: Latin Church, worldwide. Date: from 27 Nov 1983; as of 2026-11-29. Sunday and the holy days of obligation, the Immaculate Conception among them unless a conference has suppressed it or moved it to a Sunday (c. 1246 par. 2); Mass on those days (c. 1247). No conference's calendar of holy days is stated.
- **1983 Code, cc. 1250-1253.** Territory: Latin Church, worldwide. Date: from 27 Nov 1983; as of 2026-11-29. Penitential days: Fridays and Lent; no Advent day named.
- **USCCB complementary norm to cc. 1252-1253 (1983; amended 1984).** Territory: United States. Date: from 1983/1984; as of 2026-11-29. Keeps the 1966 norms in force; fasting "from the completion of the eighteenth year to the beginning of the sixtieth" (1984 amendment).
- **S. C. Rites, instruction on sacred music, 1958 (AAS 50, p. 654).** Territory: Roman Rite. Date: 1958; the 1962 books. Instruments silent in Advent except at eucharistic Benediction (n. 81 a); exceptions in n. 83 a-c.
