Date: 27 July 2026

This targeted pass follows the independent exact-snapshot review. It checks
the five residual claims against the already admitted 1962 Missal and general
rubrics, the registered 1925 Ritual historical control, the 1861 hand missal,
and the second-pass historical records. No new source family was needed to
state the resulting evidence ceilings.

| Claim | Evidence ceiling and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| n. 503 is recent rather than ancient, in a rite otherwise unchanged since 1570 | The 1962 Missal proves a rule promulgated in the 1960 code; the 1925 Ritual proves an earlier procedure. Neither proves the age of every procedural element or an unchanged remainder since 1570. Replaced with the exact bounded change. |
| The people hear only selected phrases; the rite chose those phrases for a purpose | General rubric n. 511 and the local rubrics establish voice classifications and a somewhat raised voice. | The two Communion prayers are personal unlike anything else in the Mass | Their first-person singular grammar is direct evidence; uniqueness is contradicted by other first-person material in the same study. The unique comparison was removed. |
| Every Eastern anaphora is stratified | No representative Eastern-anaphora corpus was admitted. The universal comparison was removed without altering the narrower argument about the Roman text. |

`inaudible`, `only heard`, `meant to hear`, `the rite chose`, `never used`,
