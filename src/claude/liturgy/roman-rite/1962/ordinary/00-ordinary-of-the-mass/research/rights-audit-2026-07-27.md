# Rights audit: 1962 Latin Ordinary

Date: 27 July 2026
Jurisdictions examined: United States and Vatican City/source-country law
Question: what, if anything, permits redistribution of the Latin liturgical
wording used in this study?

This is a research record, not legal advice. It does not determine rights in
any jurisdiction not examined, identify every possible rightsholder, or grant
permission.

## Result

No affirmative basis was established for redistributing the complete 1962
Latin Ordinary, the 1962 typical edition's complete selection and arrangement,
or the CMAA-hosted facsimile. The publication may retain focused Latin
quotations needed for criticism, comment, scholarship, and source verification
under its existing publication authorization and a case-specific United States
fair-use assessment. That assessment does not license a complete recitation
text, a substitute missal, bulk OCR, page images, typography, artwork, or the
facsimile bytes.

The current source-library disposition is therefore correct: the exact
facsimile remains `remote` with rights `unresolved`; no payload is retained.
The underlying text, the 1962 edition, and the 2007 digitization remain separate
rights questions.

## Objects that must not be conflated

| Object | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Ancient and received prayers | Much wording long predates modern copyright. That age is relevant to the individual inherited prayers, but it does not alone determine rights in a later official edition, its new matter, or its compilation-level selection, coordination, and arrangement. No prayer-by-prayer first-publication and authorship audit was attempted here. |
| 1962 Vatican typical edition | A foreign official liturgical book published in Vatican City in 1962. Its status cannot be inferred from age, online availability, or its ecclesiastical authority. United States Copyright Office guidance treats original selection, coordination, and arrangement as potentially protectable compilation authorship even when components are independently old or public domain. |
| Rubrics and edition-specific editorial matter | These are not treated as interchangeable with the ancient prayer texts. The 1962 rubrical system, locators, selection, sequence, typography, music, and other editorial matter require their own analysis. This audit found no blanket redistribution permission. |
| Saint Joseph insertion | The seven-word clause added to the `Communicantes` by the Sacred Congregation of Rites in 1962 is new official matter in that year, although it entered an ancient prayer. Vatican Copyright Law No. XII of 12 January 1960 expressly applies copyright protection to laws and official acts published by the Holy See and Vatican City State. This audit does not decide whether the short clause alone is copyrightable in the United States, whether its protection has expired in the source country, or who now owns any applicable rights. ## United States controls

1. A 1962 publication is not public domain in the United States merely because
   it is more than sixty years old. Copyright Office Circular 15A explains that
   works already under statutory protection before 1978 may receive a maximum
   term of ninety-five years. Foreign works require a separate analysis.
2. Section 104A may restore eligible foreign works that had entered the United
   States public domain for specified reasons, but only if the work was not in
   the public domain in its source country through expiration of term on the
   applicable restoration date. The Holy See has belonged to the Berne Union
   since 12 September 1935. Those facts make an unsupported inference from
   missing United States notice or renewal unsafe; they do not prove that this
   edition qualified for restoration.
3. Section 107 identifies criticism, comment, scholarship, and research as
   purposes that may qualify as fair use and requires weighing purpose,
   character, nature, amount and substantiality, and market effect. The present
   study uses selected passages analytically, attributes and locates them, and
   does not distribute the source artifact. Fair use remains fact-specific; the
   nonprofit or scholarly character does not decide the question by itself.
4. Copyright Office guidance recognizes possible protection in original
   selection, coordination, and arrangement of preexisting material. Therefore
   an argument that individual received prayers are old cannot establish a
   right to reproduce the 1962 edition as an assembled whole.

## Vatican/source-country controls and unresolved questions

WIPO's reproduced English text of Vatican Copyright Law No. XII (12 January
1960) says that Vatican City observes the Italian copyright legislation and
regulations in force when that law entered into force, subject to stated
Vatican qualifications; article 2 expressly extends protection to texts of
laws and official acts published by the Holy See and Vatican City State.
WIPO records the Holy See's accession to the Rome Act of the Berne Convention
on 19 July 1935, effective 12 September 1935.

This pass did not establish:

- the controlling Vatican term, ownership, and expiration rules for a 1962
  typical liturgical book as distinct from a discrete official act;
- whether the Missal is treated as an official act, a collective work, another
  juridical-person work, or several differently classified components;
- the first-publication and source-country status of every inherited prayer,
  chant, rubric, image, and editorial element;
- whether any permission, contract, or later rights disposition covers the
  Vatican Polyglot Press edition or the CMAA/ABBYY artifact; or
- the status of the material outside the United States and Vatican City.

Because source-country subsistence is a predicate in the United States
restoration analysis, these are material gaps rather than merely historical
details. The responsible conclusion is `unresolved`, not public domain.

## Operational boundary

- Keep the facsimile remote and do not track or redistribute its bytes, OCR,
  page images, artwork, or music.
- Keep Latin quotations no longer than the analysis actually requires and
  preserve source locators and commentary around them.
- Do not market the study as a missal, complete Ordinary, ritual handout, or
  recitation substitute.
- Do not infer permission from a host's public download, the book's official
  status, or the age of component prayers.
- Obtain written permission or a qualified legal opinion before publishing
  complete 1962 formularies or a source-substituting edition.
- Reassess this record if a rights statement from the Holy See, Vatican
  publisher, digitizer, or host is located.

## Applied disposition

The publication source formerly contained 42 panels transcribing appointed
liturgical units. All 42 bodies were replaced on 27 July 2026 with
non-substituting source locators. The exposition retains only incipits and
focused Latin phrases needed for the adjacent analysis. No facsimile bytes,
OCR, page image, musical notation, or complete formulary is incorporated.
This is a forward correction of the publication source; it does not assert
that the earlier draft had a bulk-redistribution basis.
