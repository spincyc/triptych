# Second research pass — 27 July 2026

## Purpose and method

This pass re-examined every material claim narrowed or removed during
independent review. It searched the expected source families before accepting
an evidentiary gap: the controlling 1962 Missal and rubrics; primary patristic
and papal texts; official conciliar and catechetical teaching; an identified
Roman Ritual; and specialist liturgical histories. Search results were treated
as leads until they matched an already registered exact passage. No conclusion
below raises a source above its recorded evidence ceiling.

## Bounded negative results

- No checked printer, publisher or editor statement was found explaining the
  1962 volume's typography, pagination or physical division as theological
  argument.
- No exact checked modern critical passage was available in this pass to prove
  the former historical superlatives for the preface dialogue or the complete
  old-public/new-private chronology.
- The registered 1925 *Rituale Romanum* supports a bounded historical
  comparison. No registered exact 1952 witness supports a continuity claim.
- No source found permits a full textual collation claim between the 1861 hand
  missal and the amended 1962 facsimile; their Saint Joseph comparison remains
  bounded to that clause.

## Leads preserved for later normalization

  uses of `Kyrie eleison`.
- *Rituale Romanum* (1952 typical edition), title IV, chapter II; an exact
  artifact remains unregistered and is needed only for edition-specific
  continuity claims.
- Enrico Mazza, *The Eucharistic Prayers of the Roman Rite* and *The Origins of
  the Eucharistic Prayer*, identified specialist critical histories whose
  exact relevant passages were not available for verification.
- Ivan Dujčev, “Evolutio Canonis Missae in Liturgia Romana” (1932), a
  specialist lead on the relation between *De sacramentis* and the Roman Canon.

These leads are not publication evidence until normalized and checked under the
repository source standard.
