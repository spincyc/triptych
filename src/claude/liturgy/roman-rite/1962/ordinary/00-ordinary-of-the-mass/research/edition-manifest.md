### Internal disagreement recorded, not resolved

The 1962 promulgating decree gives the date of *Rubricarum instructum* as 23
July 1960. The Congregation's own general decree, printed later in the same
volume and dated 26 July 1960, gives 25 July, which alone is consistent with
that decree's *postero die*. Both readings are recorded on the source records.
