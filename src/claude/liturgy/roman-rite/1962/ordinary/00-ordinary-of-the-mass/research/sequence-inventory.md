# Sequence inventory

One row per received boundary of the 1962 Ordinary, in the Missal's own order
and under its own marginal numbers. Columns: the marginal number; the Latin
incipit or received name that bounds the unit; the speaker and, where the rite
prescribes one, the addressee, with the prescribed gesture or voice; status
(fixed, conditional, proper, rank-dependent); and the coverage actually given
in the publication.

Coverage codes: **L** literal ritual function; **T** theological or scriptural
reading; **H** history or development; **W** relation to the whole action.
These are coverage controls, not visible fields, and the depth given to each
unit is proportionate to its significance rather than uniform.
