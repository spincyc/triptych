\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Dignus est Agnus}; Apoc 5:12; 1:6; verse Ps 71:1 (72:1) &
``The Lamb that was slain is worthy to receive power and divinity''; the
verse, ``Give to the king thy judgment, O God.''\\
Collect & \latin{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, qui in dilecto Filio tuo} & God
willed to restore all things in his Son, King of all; that the nations,
scattered by sin, be subjected to his most gentle rule.\\
Epistle & Col 1:12--20 & The Father has ``translated us into the kingdom of
the Son of his love,'' who makes ``peace through the blood of his cross.''\\
Gradual & \latin{Dominabitur a mari usque ad mare}; Ps 71:8, 11 (72:8, 11) &
``he shall rule from sea to sea''; ``all kings of the earth shall adore him:
all nations shall serve him.''\\
Alleluia & \latin{Potestas eius, potestas aeterna}; Dan 7:14 & ``His power is
an everlasting power that shall not be taken away: and his kingdom that shall
not be destroyed.''\\
Gospel & Jn 18:33--37 & Bound before Pilate, Jesus says, ``My kingdom is not
of this world,'' and that he was born to give testimony to the truth.\\
Offertory & \latin{Postula a me}; Ps 2:8 & ``Ask of me, and I will give thee
the Gentiles for thy inheritance, and the utmost parts of the earth for thy
possession.''\\
Secret & \latin{Hostiam tibi, Domine, humanae reconciliationis} & The victim
of mankind's reconciliation is offered; that the Son, immolated in it, may
give all nations unity and peace.\\
Preface & \latin{Qui unigenitum Filium tuum}; Missal p.~291 & The Son,
anointed eternal Priest and King, offers himself on the cross and hands over
to the Father an eternal and universal kingdom.\\
Communion & \latin{Sedebit Dominus Rex in aeternum}; Ps 28:10--11 (29:10--11)
& ``The Lord shall sit king for ever \dots\ the Lord will bless his people
with peace.''\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Immortalitatis alimoniam consecuti} & Fed with the food
of immortality, those who fight under Christ the King's standards ask to reign
with him for ever.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
