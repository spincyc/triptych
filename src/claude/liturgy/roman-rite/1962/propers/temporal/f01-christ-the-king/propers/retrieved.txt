RETRIEVED -- D. N. Iesu Christi Regis (Dominica ultima octobris), 1962 Missale Romanum
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Three machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording: every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images of the controlling facsimile rendered at 200 and
400 dpi, and the 1925 first publication was read on page images of its registered microfilm
scan. The pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what each text layer gives and where
it fails. Retrieved and recorded on 2026-10-19 in the research stage of proper-study run
7397c12187517ff2.
