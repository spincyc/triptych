\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Ad te levávi}; Ps 24:1--3 (25:1--3); verse Ps 24:4 (25:4) &
``To thee have I lifted up my soul. In thee, O my God, I put my trust \dots\
none of them that wait on thee shall be confounded''; the verse asks God to
show his ways.\\
Collect & \latin{Excita, quǽsumus}; to the Son & ``Bestir, O Lord, Thy might,
we pray Thee and come,'' to rescue us ``from approaching dangers brought on
by our sins.''\\
Epistle & Rom 13:11--14a & ``It is now the hour for us to rise from sleep. For
now our salvation is nearer than when we believed''; ``put ye on the Lord
Jesus Christ.''\\
Gradual & \latin{Univérsi}; Ps 24:3--4 (25:3--4) & ``None of them that wait on
thee shall be confounded''; the verse asks God to make his ways known.\\
Alleluia & \latin{Osténde nobis}; Ps 84:8 (85:8) & ``Shew us, O Lord, thy
mercy; and grant us thy salvation.''\\
Gospel & \latin{Erunt signa}; Lk 21:25--33 & Signs in sun, moon and stars; the
Son of man ``coming in a cloud, with great power and majesty''; ``lift up your
heads, because your redemption is at hand''; the fig tree; ``my words shall
not pass away.''\\
Offertory & \latin{Ad te levávi}; Ps 24:1--3 (25:1--3) & The Introit's
antiphon sung again, without its verse.\\
Secret & \latin{Hæc sacra} & That ``these Holy Mysteries,'' cleansing us,
make us come purer \latin{ad suum princípium}, to their own source.\\
Communion & \latin{Dóminus dabit}; Ps 84:13 (85:13) & ``The Lord will give
goodness: and our earth shall yield her fruit.''\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Suscipiámus} & ``May we receive Thy mercy, O Lord, in
the midst of Thy temple,'' and go before the coming feast
\latin{reparatiónis nostræ}, of our restoration.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
