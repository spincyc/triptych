2026*). Read and recorded on 25 November 2026 in the research stage
(iteration 0) of proper-study run `77c1ca91ef2d5e93`. The study is in the
ordinary appointed-text mode, not reference-only.

## The publication basis of the Latin

The repository's holding
(`src/sources/inventories/missale-romanum-1962-facsimile-rights-v1.toml`) is
that the 1962 printing is a collation witness and that 17 U.S.C. 103(b) frees
only preexisting material: an element's Latin may be published where a
public-domain printing carries the same words. Orthography, pointing,
capitalisation, accents and the length of a conclusion cue are transformations
and do not change a verdict.

Pustet 1862, *Dominica I. Adventus*, printed pp. 1-3, read on its page images,
prints every element of this formulary written out under its own heading:

- p. 1, left column: Introit (*Introitus. Ps. 24.*; verse *Psalm. ib.*),
  *Gloria Patri*, the repetition rubric; right column: Collect (*Orémus.
  Oratio.*) and the pre-1960 rubrics and orations *De S. Maria*;
- p. 2, left column: the pre-1960 orations *Contra persecutores Eccles.* and
  *pro Papa*, rubrics, the Epistle (*Léctio Epístolæ beáti Pauli Apóstoli ad
  Romános. c. 13.*); right column: the end of the Epistle, Gradual (*Graduale.
  Ps. 24.*), Alleluia (*℣. Ps. 84.*), the ferial rubric, and the Gospel
  (*Sequéntia sancti Evangélii secúndum Lucam. c. 21.*) to *cum prodúcunt jam
  ex se*;
- p. 3, left column: the end of the Gospel, *Et dicitur Credo.*, Offertory
  (*Offertorium. Ps. 24.*), Secret, and the pre-1960 Secrets; right column:
  Communion (*Communio. Ps. 84.*; the text layer's *Ps. 81* is an optical
  misreading), Postcommunion, and the pre-1960 Postcommunions.

The pre-1960 orations and the rubrics that add them belong to the older
rubrics (research/context.md) and are no part of the 1962 formulary.

The differences between the 1862 and 1962 printings of the ten elements are:

- **orthography**: *Jesus*, *Jesum*, *majestáte*, *jam* (1862) for *Iesus*,
  *Iesum*, *maiestáte*, *iam* (1962); *Abjiciámus* for *Abiciámus*;
  *expéctant* for *exspéctant* (Introit, Gradual, Offertory); *Cœlum*,
  *cœlórum* for *Cælum*, *cælórum*;
- **capitals and pointing**: *Spíritus sancti* for *Spíritus Sancti*;
  *movebúntur: et tunc* for *movebúntur. Et tunc*; minor comma differences;
- **conclusion cues**: the 1862 Secret ends *Per Dóminum.*, which the 1962
  prints in full; the Collect's full conclusion and the Postcommunion's cue
  are printed in both;
- **citation form**: 1862 *c. 13.*, *c. 21.*, *Ps. 24.*, *Ps. 84.*; 1962
  *Rom. 13, 11-14*, *Luc. 21, 25-33*, *Ps. 24, 1-3*, *Ps. 84, 8*, *Ps. 84, 13*.

No word differs in any element. On that reading the Latin of all ten elements
can be published, the six with no ledger row (Introit, Gradual, Alleluia,
Gospel, Offertory, Communion) included; the ledger's missing rows are recorded
in research/scope.md, section 11. The Latin provenance ledger's rows for the
Collect, Secret, Postcommunion and Epistle (research/context.md, *Lawful
study-text routes*) were not re-derived here; this stage's own collation
agrees with them.

## Appointed text, as printed in 1962

### 1. Introit (`introit`) — no. 1, printed p. 1, left column

*Ant. ad Introitum*, citation *Ps. 24, 1-3*

> Ad te levávi ánimam meam: Deus meus, in te confído, non erubéscam: neque
> irrídeant me inimíci mei: étenim univérsi, qui te exspéctant, non
> confundéntur. *Ps. ibid., 4* Vias tuas, Dómine, demónstra mihi: et sémitas
> tuas édoce me. ℣. Glória Patri, et Fílio, et Spirítui Sancto. Sicut erat in
> princípio, et nunc, et semper, et in sǽcula sæculórum. Amen.

*Quo finito, repetitur Ad te levávi usque ad psalmum.* ¶ *Hic modus
repetendi antiphonam ad Introitum servatur per totum annum.*

### 2. Collect (`collect`) — no. 2, printed p. 1, right column

Preceded by ¶ *Non dicitur Glória in excélsis in Missis de Tempore ab hac
dominica usque ad vigiliam Nativitatis Domini inclusive.* *Orémus.* *Oratio*

> Excita, quǽsumus, Dómine, poténtiam tuam, et veni: ut ab imminéntibus
> peccatórum nostrórum perículis, te mereámur protegénte éripi, te liberánte
> salvári: Qui vivis et regnas cum Deo Patre in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus:
> per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen.

### 3. Epistle (`epistle`) — no. 3, printed p. 1, right column, to p. 2, left column

*Léctio Epístolæ beáti Pauli Apóstoli ad Romános.* — *Rom. 13, 11-14*

> Fratres: Sciéntes, quia hora est iam nos de somno súrgere. Nunc enim
> própior est nostra salus, quam cum credídimus. Nox præcéssit, dies autem
> appropinquávit. Abiciámus ergo ópera tenebrárum, et induámur arma lucis.
> Sicut in die honéste ambulémus: non in comessatiónibus, et ebrietátibus, non
> in cubílibus, et impudicítiis, non in contentióne, et æmulatióne: sed
> induímini Dóminum Iesum Christum.

### 4. Gradual (`gradual`) — no. 4, printed p. 2, left column

*Graduale*, citation *Ps. 24, 3 et 4*

> Univérsi, qui te exspéctant, non confundéntur, Dómine. ℣. Vias tuas,
> Dómine, notas fac mihi: et sémitas tuas édoce me.

¶ *In feriis Adventus, quando per hebdomadam adhibetur Missa de dominica, non
dicitur Allelúia, nec versus sequens, sed tantum graduale.*

### 5. Alleluia (`alleluia`) — no. 5, printed p. 2, left column

> Allelúia, allelúia. ℣. *Ps. 84, 8* Osténde nobis, Dómine, misericórdiam
> tuam: et salutáre tuum da nobis. Allelúia.

### 6. Gospel (`gospel`) — no. 6, printed p. 2, left column, to right column

*Sequéntia sancti Evangélii secúndum Lucam.* — *Luc. 21, 25-33*

> In illo témpore: Dixit Iesus discípulis suis: Erunt signa in sole, et luna,
> et stellis, et in terris pressúra géntium præ confusióne sónitus maris, et
> flúctuum: arescéntibus homínibus præ timóre et exspectatióne, quæ
> supervénient univérso orbi: nam virtútes cælórum movebúntur. Et tunc
> vidébunt Fílium hóminis veniéntem in nube cum potestáte magna, et maiestáte.
> His autem fíeri incipiéntibus, respícite, et leváte cápita vestra: quóniam
> appropínquat redémptio vestra. Et dixit illis similitúdinem: Vidéte
> ficúlneam, et omnes árbores: cum prodúcunt iam ex se fructum, scitis
> quóniam prope est æstas. Ita et vos cum vidéritis hæc fíeri, scitóte
> quóniam prope est regnum Dei. Amen dico vobis, quia non præteríbit
> generátio hæc, donec ómnia fiant. Cælum et terra transíbunt: verba autem
> mea non transíbunt.

¶ *Credo, quod dicitur in qualibet dominica, etsi eius Officium alicui festo
locum cedat, vel Missa votiva II classis celebretur; non dicitur autem in
feriis, quando adhibetur Missa dominicæ præcedentis.*

### 7. Offertory (`offertory`) — no. 7, printed p. 2, right column

*Ant. ad Offertorium*, citation *Ps. 24, 1-3*

> Ad te levávi ánimam meam: Deus meus, in te confído, non erubéscam: neque
> irrídeant me inimíci mei: étenim univérsi, qui te exspéctant, non
> confundéntur.

### 8. Secret (`secret`) — no. 8, printed p. 2, right column

*Secreta*

> Hæc sacra nos, Dómine, poténti virtúte mundátos, ad suum fáciant purióres
> veníre princípium. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui
> tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus: per ómnia sǽcula
> sæculórum. ℟. Amen.

A small mark above *suum* on the 400-dpi crop is a printing speck, not an
accent.
¶ *Præfatio de sanctissima Trinitate: quæ dicitur tamquam de Tempore in
dominicis Adventus et in omnibus dominicis II classis, extra tempus
natalicium et paschale; non vero in feriis, quando adhibetur Missa harum
dominicarum, sed tunc dicitur præfatio communis.*

### 9. Communion (`communion`) — no. 9, printed p. 2, right column

*Ant. ad Communionem*, citation *Ps. 84, 13*

> Dóminus dabit benignitátem: et terra nostra dabit fructum suum.

### 10. Postcommunion (`postcommunion`) — no. 10, printed p. 2, right column

*Orémus.* *Postcommunio*

> Suscipiámus, Dómine, misericórdiam tuam in médio templi tui: ut
> reparatiónis nostræ ventúra solémnia cóngruis honóribus præcedámus. Per
> Dóminum nostrum.

Two rubrics follow (p. 2 foot to p. 3 head): ¶ *Per totum annum, si dicenda
sit infra hebdomadam Missa de feria, adhibetur Missa dominicæ præcedentis,
nisi propria Missa assignetur; et similiter sumuntur orationes de dominica,
quando de feria, quæ Missam propriam non habeat, facienda sit commemoratio
iuxta rubricas.* ¶ *De feriis Adventus fit commemoratio in omnibus Missis, tam
in cantu quam lectis.* Neither alters the Sunday.

### Recommended display

Following `guidance/liturgy/roman-1962-propers.md`, *English*: scriptural
elements from the Douay-Rheims (Challoner); orations from Lasance, cited by
formulary (*First Sunday of Advent*). Nothing is composed.

- **Epistle (Rom 13:11-14a) and Gospel (Lk 21:25-33).** The Douay for the
  body of each lesson. The Missal's liturgical openings are disclosed as Missal
  forms, not blended into the Douay: the Epistle's *Fratres: Sciéntes* stands
  where the Vulgate and the Douay open "And that, knowing the season"; the
  Gospel's *In illo témpore: Dixit Iesus discípulis suis: Erunt signa* stands
  for *Et erunt signa*, "And there shall be signs". The Epistle ends at v. 14a,
  "But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ", and the display stops there. Lasance's
  openings ("BRETHREN"; "AT THAT time, Jesus said to His disciples", small
  capitals as printed) may be
  given as Lasance's rendering of the Missal's words, attributed.
- **Alleluia (Ps 84:8) and Introit verse (Ps 24:4b).** The Douay: the
  Missal's words are the Vulgate's.
- **Introit and Offertory antiphon (Ps 24:1-3).** The Douay can carry the
  sense but not the form: it has "O Lord", which the Missal's *Ad te levávi*
  lacks, and its "wait on thee" renders *sustinent te*, where the Missal reads
  *te exspéctant*. Lasance has the same words. Give the Douay, attributed, and
  say that the Missal lacks the vocative and reads *exspéctant*.
- **Gradual (Ps 24:3b-4).** Neither English carries *notas fac mihi*: the
  Douay and Lasance both give "Shew (Show), O Lord, thy ways to me", which
  renders *demonstra*. Give the Latin, say that the Gradual's verse asks God
  to "make known" (*notas fac*) where the Introit verse has *demónstra*, and
  give the Douay of Ps 24:4b beside it as the Vulgate's wording. The respond's
  closing *Dómine* is in neither English; say so.
- **Communion (Ps 84:13).** The Douay's "For the Lord will give goodness"
  renders *Etenim*, which the Missal omits. Lasance prints the Douay without
  "For": "The Lord will give goodness: and our earth shall yield her fruit."
  Give Lasance's line, attributed, with the Douay verse beside it.
- **Orations.** Lasance, as above.

## Unresolved discrepancies

None in the Latin: every word of every element was read on the 1962 images,
and the 1862 printing agrees in every word. Two observations remain for the
author, neither a discrepancy of the text: the 1862 text layer's Communion
citation *Ps. 81* is an optical misreading of *Ps. 84* (image read), and
Lasance's English of the Introit, Gradual and Communion follows the Douay
with the adjustments recorded above rather than translating the Missal's
chant forms afresh.
