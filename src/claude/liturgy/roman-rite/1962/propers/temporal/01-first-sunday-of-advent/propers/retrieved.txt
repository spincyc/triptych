RETRIEVED -- Dominica prima Adventus (First Sunday of Advent), 1962 Missale Romanum,
with its public-domain antecedent in the Pustet 1862 Missal and the 1945 Lasance hand
missal that prints the formulary in English
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Three machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording. Every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images: the 1962 facsimile at 200 dpi whole (PDF
pp. 82-84) and in 400-dpi crops of each column section of PDF pp. 82-83; the Pustet 1862
facsimile on 130-dpi page images rendered from the whole registered PDF (PDF pp. 87-89);
the Lasance pages on 110-dpi page images rendered from the whole registered PDF (PDF
pp. 104-107). The pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what each text layer gives
and where it fails. Retrieved and recorded on 2026-11-25 in the research stage (iteration
0) of proper-study run 77c1ca91ef2d5e93.

Each pull is reproduced byte for byte between its BEGIN and END lines, form feeds included.
Where an extract does not end in a line feed, one line feed is added before the END line;
each stated extract SHA-256 is of the bytes as the tool emitted them.
