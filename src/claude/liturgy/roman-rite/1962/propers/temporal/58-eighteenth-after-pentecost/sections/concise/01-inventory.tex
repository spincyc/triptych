\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Da pacem}; Ecclus 36:18, adapted; Ps 121:1 & Peace for
those who wait on the Lord, that his prophets be found faithful. The verse: ``We
shall go into the house of the Lord.''\\
Collect & \latin{Dirigat corda nostra} & That the working of God's mercy may
direct our hearts, \latin{quia tibi sine te placere non possumus}.\\
Epistle & 1 Cor 1:4--8 & Thanks for the grace given the Corinthians in
Christ, rich in utterance and knowledge, as they wait for the Lord who will
confirm them.\\
Gradual & \latin{Laetatus sum}; Ps 121:1, 7 & The Introit's verse sung again,
and peace in the city's strength, abundance in its towers.\\
Alleluia & \latin{Timebunt gentes}; Ps 101:16 & ``The Gentiles shall fear thy
name, O Lord, and all the kings of the earth thy glory.''\\
Gospel & Mt 9:1--8 & In his own city Jesus forgives a paralytic whom others
brought, and bids him rise, to show that the Son of man has power on earth to
forgive; the crowds glorify God.\\
Offertory & \latin{Sanctificavit}; Ex 24:4--5, compiled & Moses hallows an
altar and offers sacrifice in the sight of the children of Israel.\\
Secret & \latin{Deus, qui nos} & Through this sacrifice's venerable exchanges
God makes us sharers in his Godhead; may we attain by our lives the truth we
know.\\
Communion & \latin{Tollite hostias}; Ps 95:8--9 & ``Bring up sacrifices, and
come into his courts: Adore ye the Lord in his holy court.''\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Gratias tibi referimus} & Thanks for the sacred gift
that has quickened us, and a plea that God's mercy make us worthy of our share
in it.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
