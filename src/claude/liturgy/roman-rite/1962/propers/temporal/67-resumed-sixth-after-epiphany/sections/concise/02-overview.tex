\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:start}
\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & The least of seeds becomes a
tree, and a little leaven leavens three measures of meal; God promises the
exiles peace, a hearing and return; one psalm gives thanks for a deliverance,
another cries from the depths; Paul thanks God for a church that received the
word in much tribulation and waits for the Son from heaven.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & The seed is the Lord,
put to death and buried (Hilary), and for Ambrose also faith in him; the
leaven is love (Augustine, Bede) or the teaching of Scripture (Jerome) and of
the Church (Ambrose), and the measures are the whole person (the same
four); the captivity is sin (Augustine), and the exiles return to the Church
(Jerome).\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Faith ground by adversity pours
out its strength (Ambrose); love hidden in the mind must grow until it changes
the whole mind (Bede), and to love genuinely is great labour (Chrysostom); God
urges his people to pray for what he promised, so that they may deserve to
receive it (Jerome).\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & The nations fly to the
tree's branches in hope of life (Hilary); the love begun here is perfected
where God is all in all (Bede), and the whole is leavened ``when Christ
comes'' (Chrysologus); after the confusion of this world God brings his people
back to their place (Jerome).\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
