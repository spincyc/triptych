Epiphany), 1962 Missale Romanum, with its public-domain antecedent in the Pustet 1862 Missal
and the 1945 Lasance hand missal that prints the resumed formulary in English
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Four machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording. Every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images: the 1962 facsimile at 200 dpi whole and in
400-dpi crops of each column section, the Pustet 1862 facsimile on page images rendered
from the whole registered PDF, and the Lasance pages on 250-dpi images. The pulls are kept so
that a later reader can see what each text layer gives and where it fails. Retrieved and
recorded on 2026-11-11 in the research stage (iteration 0) of proper-study run
decbd44ee5bcce3b.

Each pull is reproduced byte for byte between its BEGIN and END lines, form feeds included.
Where an extract does not end in a line feed, one line feed is added before the END line;
