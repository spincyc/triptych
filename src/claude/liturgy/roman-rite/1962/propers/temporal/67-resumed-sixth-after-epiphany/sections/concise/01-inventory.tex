\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Dicit Dominus}; Jer 29:11--12, 14; verse Ps 84:2 &
God to the exiles: ``I think thoughts of peace, and not of affliction; you
shall call upon Me and I will hear you; and I will bring back your captivity
from all places''; the verse: ``Thou hast turned away the captivity of
Jacob.''\\
Collect & \latin{Praesta, quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} & That, ``ever fixing
our thoughts on reasonable things, we may both in word and in deed do what is
pleasing to Thee.''\\
Epistle & 1~Thess 1:2--10 & Paul remembers ``the work of your faith and
labour and charity''; they received the word ``in much tribulation, with joy
of the Holy Ghost,'' and from them it spread ``in every place''; they wait
``for his Son from heaven \dots\ Jesus, who hath delivered us from the wrath
to come.''\\
Gradual & \latin{Liberasti nos}; Ps 43:8--9 & ``Thou hast delivered
us, O Lord, from them that afflict us: and hast put them to shame that hate
us. In God we will glory all the day.''\\
Alleluia & \latin{De profundis}; Ps 129:1--2 & ``From the depths I
have cried to Thee, O Lord: Lord, hear my prayer.''\\
Gospel & Mt 13:31--35 & The kingdom is like a mustard seed, ``the least indeed
of all seeds,'' that ``becometh a tree,'' and like leaven a woman ``hid in
three measures of meal, until the whole was leavened''; Jesus speaks in
parables, ``things hidden from the foundation of the world.''\\
Offertory & \latin{De profundis}; Ps 129:1--2 & The Alleluia's cry
sung again, its first line repeated.\\
Secret & \latin{Haec nos oblatio} & ``May this oblation, O God, cleanse, renew,
govern, and protect us.''\\
Communion & \latin{Amen dico vobis}; Mk 11:24 & ``Whatsoever you ask when you
pray, believe that you shall receive, and it shall be done to you.''\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Caelestibus, Domine, pasti deliciis} & ``Being fed with
celestial delights,'' that ``we may ever hunger after those things by which we
truly live.''\\
\end{concisemaptable}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:end}

English (1945), lessons in the Douay--Rheims (Challoner). Psalms in the
