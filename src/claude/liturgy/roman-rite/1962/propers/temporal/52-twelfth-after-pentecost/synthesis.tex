mechanical \textit{synthesis edition}. It keeps the map, the dossier sheet,
the two-page thematic movement, one integrated commentary, the complete
source-grounded and exploratory syntheses, the quotation gallery, and the
terminal apparatus, and it omits the complete appointed texts and the
element-by-element reception sweep. It adds no research of its own. The
canonical \textit{full edition}, published at the same identity without the
\texttt{-synthesis} suffix, prints the complete appointed formulary and the
full sweep from the same source leaf; the two editions rest on one body of
research and share one set of source records.}
