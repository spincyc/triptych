RETRIEVED -- In Festo Omnium Sanctorum (1 novembris), 1962 Missale Romanum
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Two machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording: every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images of the controlling facsimile rendered at 200
and 400 dpi, and the public-domain witnesses (Mame 1922, Pustet 1862) stand in their own
registered artifacts. The pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what each text
layer gives and where it fails. Retrieved and recorded on 2026-10-26 in the research
stage of proper-study run 427e2f58bb897a16.

Boundaries shown    : printed p. 702 opens with the running head FESTA NOVEMBRIS and
                      a full-width engraved headpiece (the throne, the Lamb, the four

                      text layer for it), then the rubric Die 1 novembris and the
                      heading IN FESTO / OMNIUM SANCTORUM / I classis; nos. 3974-3975
                      follow in the left column and no. 3976 opens in the right. Printed p. 703 (running head Festa
                      novembris (1)) carries the end of the Lesson, nos. 3977-3983, and

                      outside these two pages; research/context.md records both
                      boundaries as read on page images.
