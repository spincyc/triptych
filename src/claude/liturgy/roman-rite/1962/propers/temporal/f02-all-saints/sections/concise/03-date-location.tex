{\footnotesize\noindent In the Passage column, Revelation is the Apocalypse; the Missal's Gospel
ends at Mt 5:12a, and the Gradual sings Ps 33:10 and only the second half of
33:11. David's
reign is the reference point for the psalms' traditional attribution, and
A.M.~2944 is Ussher's year, as Haydock reports it, for the flight to Achis that
the title of Ps 33 names; the composition bound covers the whole Psalter. The
dates of Wisdom, Matthew and the Apocalypse are dates of the books. The
sources and evidence for every date are set out in the expansive study's
Scriptural Date and Location.\par}
