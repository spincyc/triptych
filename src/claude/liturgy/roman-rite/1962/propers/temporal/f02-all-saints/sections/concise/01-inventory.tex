\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Gaudeamus omnes in Domino}; verse Ps 32:1 (33:1) & An
antiphon of the Church: all rejoice on the feast of all the saints, at which
the angels rejoice and praise the Son of God; ``Rejoice in the Lord, O ye
just.''\\
Collect & \latin{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, qui nos} & The merits of all the
saints venerated in one celebration; mercy asked through their many
intercessors.\\
Commemoration Collect & \latin{Absolve, quaesumus, Domine} & That God free his
peoples from the bonds of the sins their frailty has contracted.\\
Lesson & Apoc 7:2--12 & The servants of God sealed, ``an hundred forty-four
thousand''; then ``a great multitude, which no man could number, of all
nations,'' before the throne.\\
Gradual & \latin{Timete Dominum}; Ps 33:10, 11b (34:10, 11b) & ``Fear the
Lord, all ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him''; those
who seek him lack no good.\\
Alleluia & \latin{Venite ad me}; Mt 11:28 & ``Come to me all you that labor
and are burdened, and I will refresh you.''\\
Gospel & Mt 5:1--12a & The eight beatitudes, and to the persecuted disciples,
``your reward is very great in heaven.''\\
Offertory & \latin{Iustorum animae}; Wis 3:1--3, adapted & The souls of the
just are in God's hand, untouched by the torment of malice; they seemed to
die, but are in peace.\\
Secret & \latin{Munera tibi, Domine} & The gifts offered for the honour of all
the just and for our salvation.\\
Commemoration Secret & \latin{Pro nostrae servitutis augmento} & The sacrifice
of praise; that God complete what he gave to the undeserving.\\
Preface & Trinity (no.~1082) or common (no.~1086) & None of the feast's own;
the general rubrics can be read for either.\\
Communion & \latin{Beati mundo corde}; Mt 5:8--10 & The clean of heart, the
peacemakers and the persecuted: the Gospel's last three beatitudes.\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Da, quaesumus, Domine} & That the faithful peoples
rejoice in venerating the saints and be defended by their unceasing prayer.\\
Commemoration Postcommunion & \latin{Quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} & That those
who share divine things be not left subject to human dangers.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
