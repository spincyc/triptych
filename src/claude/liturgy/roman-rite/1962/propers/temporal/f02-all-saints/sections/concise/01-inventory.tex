\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Gaudeamus omnes in Domino}; verse Ps 32:1 (33:1) &
All rejoice in the Lord on the feast of all the saints, whose solemnity the
angels keep; the verse bids the just rejoice in the Lord.\\
Collect & \latin{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus} &
God gave us to venerate the merits of all his saints in one celebration;
that, the intercessors multiplied, he grant the abundance of his mercy.\\
Commemoration Collect & \latin{Absolve, quaesumus, Domine} (the Sunday's) &
That God loose his peoples' sins from the bonds their frailty contracted.\\
Lesson & Apoc 7:2--12 &
The servants of God sealed, twelve thousand from each tribe; then a
multitude no man could number.\\
Gradual & \latin{Timete Dominum}; Ps 33:10--11b (34:10--11b) &
Fear the Lord, all his saints; those who fear him and seek him shall lack
no good.\\
Alleluia & \latin{Venite ad me omnes}; Mt 11:28 &
The Lord calls all who labour and are burdened, and will refresh them.\\
Gospel & Mt 5:1--12a &
On the mountain Jesus teaches the eight beatitudes; the persecuted's reward
is great in heaven.\\
Offertory & \latin{Iustorum animae}; Wis 3:1--3, adapted &
The souls of the just are in God's hand; they seemed to die, but are in
peace; alleluia.\\
Secret & \latin{Munera tibi, Domine} &
The gifts of our devotion, for the honour of all the just and our
salvation.\\
Commemoration Secret & \latin{Pro nostrae servitutis augmento} (the
Sunday's) &
The sacrifice of praise for our service's increase; that God complete his
gifts to the undeserving.\\
Preface & Unsettled; two readings &
Trinity (p.~293, no.~1082) or common (p.~297, no.~1086); the feast has no
Preface of its own.\\
Communion & \latin{Beati mundo corde}; Mt 5:8--10 &
The clean of heart shall see God; the peacemakers shall be called children
of God; the persecuted possess the kingdom of heaven.\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Da, quaesumus, Domine} &
That the faithful peoples ever rejoice in venerating all the saints,
defended by their prayer.\\
Commemoration Postcommunion & \latin{Quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} (the
Sunday's) &
That those given to rejoice in divine things be not left subject to human
dangers.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
