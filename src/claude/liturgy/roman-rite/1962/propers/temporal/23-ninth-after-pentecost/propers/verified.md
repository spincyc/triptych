# Ninth Sunday after Pentecost — Verified Propers

Facsimile-collated appointed text in liturgical order. This record is the text
control for the guide. Reader-facing prose quotes only what an argument needs;
this file carries the complete collated element.

Recorded, not harmonised. Every Vatican typical reading below is what this guide

## 1. Introit — marginal no. 1522

**Printed heading and reference:** `Ant. ad Introitum` · `Ps. 53, 6-7`
(Vulgate numbering; modern Ps. 54:6–7). The psalm verse is cited
`Ps. ibid., 3` (modern 54:3).

> Ecce Deus ádiuvat me, et Dóminus suscéptor est ánimæ meæ: avérte mala inimícis
> meis, et in veritáte tua dispérde illos, protéctor meus, Dómine. *Ps. ibid., 3*
> Deus, in nómine tuo salvum me fac: et in virtúte tua líbera me. ℣. Glória
> Patri.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 388, left column, below the
formulary heading; PDF p. 469. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Note on the antiphon's construction.** The antiphon takes vv. 6–7 and the
psalm verse takes v. 3, so the appointed order inverts the psalm's own order:
the confession of help is sung first and the opening cry for rescue second.
That is a feature of the printed formulary, verified as printed, not an
editorial rearrangement.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved wording, accent, punctuation,
or reference issue.

## 2. Collect — marginal no. 1523

**Printed heading:** `Oratio`.

> Páteant aures misericórdiæ tuæ, Dómine, précibus supplicántium: et, ut
> peténtibus desideráta concédas; fac eos, quæ tibi sunt plácita, postuláre. Per
> Dóminum nostrum.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 388, top of the right column;
PDF p. 469. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Note on punctuation.** The colon after `supplicántium`, the comma after `et`,
and the semicolon after `concédas` are as printed and are load-bearing: the
semicolon separates the purpose clause (`ut … concédas`) from the imperative it
depends on (`fac eos … postuláre`), so that the granting is subordinated to the
forming of the petition.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.

## 3. Epistle — marginal no. 1524

**Printed heading and reference:** `Léctio Epístolæ beáti Pauli Apóstoli ad
Corínthios.` · `1 Cor. 10, 6-13`.

> Fratres: Non simus concupiscéntes malórum, sicut et illi concupiérunt. Neque
> idolólatræ efficiámini, sicut quidam ex ipsis: quemádmodum scriptum est: Sedit
> pópulus manducáre et bíbere, et surrexérunt lúdere. Neque fornicémur, sicut
> quidam ex ipsis fornicáti sunt, et cecidérunt una die vigínti tria mília.
> Neque tentémus Christum, sicut quidam eórum tentavérunt, et a serpéntibus
> periérunt. Neque murmuravéritis, sicut quidam eórum murmuravérunt, et
> periérunt ab exterminatóre. Hæc autem ómnia in figúra contingébant illis:
> scripta sunt autem ad correptiónem nostram, in quos fines sæculórum
> devenérunt. Itaque qui se exístimat stare, vídeat ne cadat. Tentátio vos non
> apprehéndat, nisi humána: fidélis autem Deus est, qui non patiétur vos tentári
> supra id quod potéstis, sed fáciet étiam cum tentatióne provéntum, ut possítis
> sustinére.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 388, right column; PDF p. 469.
**Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Notes.** The liturgical incipit `Fratres:` is the missal's, not Paul's; the
pericope begins mid-sentence at v. 6 and ends at v. 13, so the canonical
`Propter quod… fugite ab idolorum cultura` of v. 14 is *outside* the appointed
lesson. `mília` is the typical edition's spelling (Benziger: `míllia`). The
OCR finding aid's `raciet` is a recognition failure for the printed `fáciet`.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue in the Vatican typical
witness.

## 4. Gradual — marginal no. 1525

**Printed heading and reference:** `Graduale` · `Ps. 8, 2`.

> Dómine Dóminus noster, quam admirábile est nomen tuum in univérsa terra! ℣.
> Quóniam eleváta est magnificéntia tua super cælos.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 388, right column, below the
Epistle; PDF p. 469. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Note.** The exclamation mark after `terra` is printed. The verse takes the
second half of the same psalm verse, so the whole Gradual is one biblical verse
divided at its own caesura.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.

## 5. Alleluia — marginal no. 1526

**Printed form and reference:** no separate heading; the reference `Ps. 58, 2`
(modern 59:2) is printed inline after the versicle sign.

> Allelúia, allelúia. ℣. *Ps. 58, 2* Eripe me de inimícis meis, Deus meus: et ab
> insurgéntibus in me líbera me. Allelúia.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 388, foot of the right column;
PDF p. 469. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.

## 6. Gospel — marginal no. 1527

**Printed heading and reference:** `✠ Sequéntia sancti Evangélii secúndum
Lucam.` · `Luc. 19, 41-47`.

> In illo témpore: Cum appropinquáret Iesus Ierúsalem, videns civitátem, flevit
> super illam, dicens: Quia si cognovísses et tu, et quidem in hac die tua, quæ
> ad pacem tibi, nunc autem abscóndita sunt ab óculis tuis. Quia vénient dies in
> te: et circúmdabunt te inimíci tui vallo, et circúmdabunt te: et coangustábunt
> te úndique: et ad terram prostérnent te, et fílios tuos, qui in te sunt, et non
> relínquent in te lápidem super lápidem: eo quod non cognóveris tempus
> visitatiónis tuæ. Et ingréssus in templum, cœpit eícere vendéntes in illo, et
> eméntes, dicens illis: Scriptum est: Quia domus mea domus oratiónis est. Vos
> autem fecístis illam spelúncam latrónum. Et erat docens cotídie in templo.

**Rubric immediately following:** `Credo.` (printed on its own line).

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 389, left column; PDF p. 470.
**Checked:** 2026-07-25 at 600 dpi for `cœpit eícere` and `spelúncam`.

**Notes.** The pericope stops at v. 47a. Luke 19:47b–48 — the chief priests and
scribes seeking to destroy him, and the people hanging on his words — is
*outside* the appointed Gospel, which therefore ends on the note of continued
daily teaching rather than on the plot against him. The aposiopesis at
`quæ ad pacem tibi` (with no verb) is printed exactly so; it is the Vulgate's
broken sentence, not an omission here. `cœpit eícere` uses the `œ` ligature in
`cœpit` and the contracted `eícere`.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue in the Vatican typical
witness. See the discrepancy table for `eícere` / `eiícere`.

## 7. Offertory — marginal no. 1528

**Printed heading and reference:** `Antiphona ad Offertorium` ·
`Ps. 18, 9, 10, 11 et 12` (modern Ps. 19:9–12). The reference is printed at the
head of the text line, not on a separate line.

> Iustítiæ Dómini rectæ, lætificántes corda, et iudícia eius dulcióra super mel
> et favum: nam et servus tuus custódit ea.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 389, left column, below the
`Credo` rubric; PDF p. 470. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Note.** The antiphon is a centonisation: it takes the first half of v. 9,
skips to `iudicia eius` in v. 10, the honey comparison in v. 11, and the
servant's keeping in v. 12, which is why the missal prints four verse numbers
rather than a range. Verified as printed.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.

## 8. Secret — marginal no. 1529

**Printed heading:** `Secreta`.

> Concéde nobis, quæsumus, Dómine, hæc digne frequentáre mystéria: quia,
> quóties huius hóstiæ commemorátio celebrátur, opus nostræ redemptiónis
> exercétur. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et
> regnat in unitáte.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 389, left column; PDF p. 470.
**Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Notes.** The typical edition prints the long conclusion, breaking off at
`in unitáte` (the celebrant supplies the rest from the Ordinary). Benziger
prints only `Per Dóminum.` The OCR finding aid's `Cnc6de` is a recognition
failure for `Concéde`. `digne` is printed and is not present in every earlier
witness of this prayer; that is a matter for historical commentary, not for the
1962 text, which is as above.

**Rubric immediately following:** `Præfatio de Ssma Trinitate.` — the Preface of
the Most Holy Trinity, printed with `Ssma` under a tilde abbreviation for
`Sanctissima`. Expanded here from the same book's own abbreviation practice.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.

## 9. Communion — marginal no. 1530

**Printed heading and reference:** `Ant. ad Communionem` · `Io. 6, 57`
(the Clementine Vulgate's verse numbering; the same clause is 6:56 in the
modern critical numbering used by most current editions).

> Qui mandúcat meam carnem, et bibit meum sánguinem, in me manet, et ego in eo,
> dicit Dóminus.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 389, left column, foot; PDF
p. 470. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Note.** `dicit Dóminus` is the antiphon's own liturgical attribution formula,
added to make the first-person clause singable as address; it is not part of the
Johannine verse. Verified as printed and preserved as printed.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.

## 10. Postcommunion — marginal no. 1531

**Printed heading:** `Postcommunio`.

> Tui nobis, quæsumus, Dómine, commúnio sacraménti, et purificatiónem cónferat,
> et tríbuat unitátem. Per Dóminum nostrum.

**Witness:** Vatican typical edition, printed p. 389, top of the right column;
PDF p. 470. **Checked:** 2026-07-25.

**Note.** `Tui … sacraménti` is a single genitive phrase split around `nobis,
quæsumus, Dómine`; the subject of both verbs is `commúnio`. Verified as
printed.

**Verification result:** collated; no unresolved issue.
