\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:start}
\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & The Lord promises to hear his
people from any tribulation. Paul bids the Ephesians put on the new man in
truth, anger ended before sunset, and work that can give. In the temple Jesus
tells of a king whose invited guests refuse his son's wedding, whose hall is
filled from the highways with bad and good, and who casts out a guest without a
wedding garment. The psalmist lifts his hands at evening, walks in tribulation,
and wishes his ways directed to keep God's justifications.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & The wedding is the
Son's union with the Church (Gregory; Augustine), gathered from Jews and
Gentiles (Jerome) by the one God who called by the prophets and calls by the
apostles (Irenaeus). The garment is charity (Gregory; Augustine), which the
Bridegroom himself wore (Gregory), or the new man's garment, made of the Lord's precepts
kept, and the new man is Christ (Jerome). The evening sacrifice is the Lord's
Passion (Augustine), or the works of mercy of the last age (Hilary).\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Not to make light of the call for
farm or trade (Gregory; Hilary). To put on charity, woven on the love of God
and of neighbour and proved toward the enemy (Gregory), in truth, anger settled
the same day, and work that gives (the Epistle, with Chrysostom and Jerome). To
lift hands in works of mercy (Hilary) and without anger (Chrysostom), and to ask
of God what he commands (Augustine; Hilary).\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & The king's coming in is
the judgement (Jerome). We know that we are called, not whether we are chosen
(Gregory); the few are rare among the chosen, not few among the invited
(Hilary). The feast that now is, received worthily, leads to the other, to
which the evil do not come (Augustine), and the glory of the heavenly kingdom is
announced as a wedding (Hilary).\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
