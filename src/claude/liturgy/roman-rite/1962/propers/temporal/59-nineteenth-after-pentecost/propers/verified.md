# Nineteenth Sunday after Pentecost — Verified Propers

Facsimile-collated appointed text in liturgical order. This record is the text
control for the canonical leaf
`liturgy/roman-rite/1962/propers/temporal/59-nineteenth-after-pentecost` and for
every output derived from it. It carries the Latin of the controlling edition as
printed, the collation of every element against the public-domain 1862
antecedent, each element's relation to the Clementine Vulgate, and the lawful
English route for each element. It contains no study prose.

## The appointed texts

Latin as printed in the 1962 typical edition, with its accents, ligatures and
pointing. `℣.` renders the printed versicle sign. The drop capital of each
element is joined to its word.

### 1. Introit (`introit`) — no. 1679, printed p. 411

*Antiphona ad Introitum*

> Salus pópuli ego sum, dicit Dóminus: de quacúmque tribulatióne clamáverint ad
> me, exáudiam eos: et ero illórum Dóminus in perpétuum. *Ps. 77, 1* Atténdite,
> pópule meus, legem meam: inclináte aurem vestram in verba oris mei. ℣. Glória
> Patri.

The Missal prints no citation for the antiphon; `Ps. 77, 1` stands before the
verse only. The antiphon is not a continuous Vulgate text; see
`research/scope.md` § 3.1.

### 2. Collect (`collect`) — no. 1680, printed p. 411

*Oratio*

> Omnípotens et miséricors Deus, univérsa nobis adversántia propitiátus exclúde:
> ut mente et córpore páriter expedíti, quæ tua sunt, líberis méntibus
> exsequámur. Per Dóminum.

### 3. Epistle (`epistle`) — no. 1681, printed p. 411

*Léctio Epístolæ beáti Pauli Apóstoli ad Ephésios.* — *Ephes. 4, 23-28*

> Fratres: Renovámini spíritu mentis vestræ, et indúite novum hóminem, qui
> secúndum Deum creátus est in iustítia et sanctitáte veritátis. Propter quod
> deponéntes mendácium, loquímini veritátem unusquísque cum próximo suo: quóniam
> sumus ínvicem membra. Irascímini, et nolíte peccáre: sol non óccidat super
> iracúndiam vestram. Nolíte locum dare diábolo: qui furabátur, iam non furétur;
> magis autem labóret, operándo mánibus suis, quod bonum est, ut hábeat unde
> tríbuat necessitátem patiénti.

### 4. Gradual (`gradual`) — no. 1682, printed p. 411

*Graduale* — *Ps. 140, 2*

> Dirigátur orátio mea sicut incénsum in conspéctu tuo, Dómine. ℣. Elevátio
> mánuum meárum sacrifícium vespertínum.

### 5. Alleluia (`alleluia`) — no. 1683, printed p. 411

> Allelúia, allelúia. ℣. *Ps. 104, 1* Confitémini Dómino, et invocáte nomen eius:
> annuntiáte inter gentes ópera eius. Allelúia.

### 6. Gospel (`gospel`) — no. 1684, printed pp. 411–412

✠ *Sequéntia sancti Evangélii secúndum Matthǽum.* — *Mt. 22, 1-14*

> In illo témpore: Loquebátur Iesus princípibus sacerdótum et pharisǽis in
> parábolis, dicens: Símile factum est regnum cælórum hómini regi, qui fecit
> núptias fílio suo. Et misit servos suos vocáre invitátos ad núptias, et
> nolébant veníre. Iterum misit álios servos, dicens: Dícite invitátis: Ecce
> prándium meum parávi, tauri mei et altília occísa sunt, et ómnia paráta: veníte
> ad núptias. Illi autem neglexérunt: et abiérunt, álius in villam suam, álius
> vero ad negotiatiónem suam: réliqui vero tenuérunt servos eius, et contuméliis
> afféctos occidérunt. Rex autem cum audísset, irátus est: et missis exercítibus
> suis, pérdidit homicídas illos, et civitátem illórum succéndit. Tunc ait servis
> suis: Núptiæ quidem parátæ sunt, sed qui invitáti erant, non fuérunt digni. Ite
> ergo ad éxitus viárum, et quoscúmque invenéritis, vocáte ad núptias. Et egréssi
> servi eius in vias, congregavérunt omnes, quos invenérunt, malos et bonos: et
> implétæ sunt núptiæ discumbéntium. Intrávit autem rex, ut vidéret
> discumbéntes, et vidit ibi hóminem non vestítum veste nuptiáli. Et ait illi:
> Amíce, quómodo huc intrásti non habens vestem nuptiálem? At ille obmútuit. Tunc
> dixit rex minístris: Ligátis mánibus et pédibus eius, míttite eum in ténebras
> exterióres: ibi erit fletus et stridor déntium. Multi enim sunt vocáti, pauci
> vero elécti.

The printed cue *Credo.* follows on the same line as the last words.

### 7. Offertory (`offertory`) — no. 1685, printed p. 412

*Ant. ad Offertorium* — *Ps. 137, 7*

> Si ambulávero in médio tribulatiónis, vivificábis me, Dómine: et super iram
> inimicórum meórum exténdes manum tuam, et salvum me fáciet déxtera tua.

### 8. Secret (`secret`) — no. 1686, printed p. 412

*Secreta*

> Hæc múnera, quǽsumus, Dómine, quæ óculis tuæ maiestátis offérimus, salutária
> nobis esse concéde. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum
> vivit et regnat in unitáte.

Followed by the printed direction *Præfatio de Ssm̃a Trinitate.* (the
abbreviation is set with a tilde over the m).

### 9. Communion (`communion`) — no. 1687, printed p. 412

*Ant. ad Communionem* — *Ps. 118, 4-5*

> Tu mandásti mandáta tua custodíri nimis: útinam dirigántur viæ meæ, ad
> custodiéndas iustificatiónes tuas.

### 10. Postcommunion (`postcommunion`) — no. 1688, printed p. 412

*Postcommunio*

> Tua nos, Dómine, medicinális operátio, et a nostris perversitátibus cleménter
> expédiat, et tuis semper fáciat inhærére mandátis. Per Dóminum.

## Collation against the 1862 antecedent

Every element of this formulary stands in the 1862 printing in the same words.
The differences found on the 1862 page images are of five kinds only, and none
changes a word:

| Kind | 1862 reading (page image) | 1962 reading |
|---|---|---|
| Orthography | *exequámur* (Collect); *jam* (Epistle); *Jesus*, *ejus* (Gospel); *majestátis* (Secret); *justítia*, *justificatiónes* | *exsequámur*; *iam*; *Iesus*, *eius*; *maiestátis*; *iustítia*, *iustificatiónes* |
| Capitals | *Princípibus*, *Pharisǽis* (Gospel) | *princípibus*, *pharisǽis* |
| Pointing | *Atténdite pópule meus legem meam* (no commas); *Fratres.*; *in justítia, et sanctitáte*; *orátio mea, sicut incénsum in conspéctu tuo Dómine.*; *tauri mei, et altília*; *neglexérunt et abiérunt*; *malos et bonos, et implétæ*; *intrásti, non habens*; *fletus, et stridor*; *vivificábis me Dómine:*; *quæsumus Dómine,* | *Atténdite, pópule meus, legem meam*; *Fratres:*; *in iustítia et sanctitáte*; *orátio mea sicut incénsum in conspéctu tuo, Dómine.*; *tauri mei et altília*; *neglexérunt: et abiérunt*; *malos et bonos: et implétæ*; *intrásti non habens*; *fletus et stridor*; *vivificábis me, Dómine:*; *quǽsumus, Dómine,* |
| Citation form | *Ps. 77.*, *c. 4.*, *Ps. 140.*, *Ps. 104.*, *cap. 22.*, *Ps. 137.*, *Ps. 118.* | verse-level citations as printed above |
| Conclusion length | *Per Dóminum.* on the Secret | the longer form printed above |

The 1862 prints the Introit under *Introitus.*, the chants as *Graduale.*,
*Offertorium.*, *Communio.*, and adds the pre-1960 *Secunda Oratio. A cunctis nos.
fol. 315.*, *Alia Secreta. Exaúdi nos Deus. fol. 316.*, *Alia Postcommunio. Mundet
et múniat. fol. 316.* and *Tertia ad libitum.* after the three orations. These
belong to the rubrics before 1960 and are not part of the 1962 formulary.

The 1862 Postcommunion prints *inhærére mandátis* whole on the page image; the
1862 text layer damages the word (`propers/retrieved.txt`).

## Relation to the Clementine Vulgate

Textual observations about wording and boundary only; no origin is asserted by
this table (see `research/scope.md` § 3 for what was searched).

| Element | Missal against Clementine |
|---|---|
| Introit antiphon | No continuous Clementine text; the Missal cites none. |
| Introit verse, Ps 77:1 | Omits the Clementine's title *Intellectus Asaph*, which the Clementine counts inside v. 1. Otherwise agrees. |
| Epistle, Eph 4:23–28 | Prefixes the address *Fratres:* and omits *autem* (*Renovamini autem spiritu*). Otherwise agrees word for word. |
| Gradual, Ps 140:2 | Adds *Domine* after *in conspectu tuo*. Otherwise agrees. |
| Alleluia, Ps 104:1 | Omits the Clementine's *Alleluja*, counted inside v. 1 as the psalm's title. Otherwise agrees. |
| Gospel, Mt 22:1–14 | The liturgical opening *In illo tempore: Loquebatur Iesus principibus sacerdotum et pharisaeis in parabolis, dicens* stands for *Et respondens Jesus, dixit iterum in parabolis eis, dicens*; the addressees are those Mt 21:45 names. At v. 13 the Missal reads *Tunc dixit rex*, the Clementine *Tunc dicit rex*. Every other word agrees. |
| Offertory, Ps 137:7 | Adds *Domine* after *vivificabis me*; reads the futures *extendes* and *faciet* where the Clementine reads *extendisti* and *fecit*. |
| Communion, Ps 118:4–5 | Agrees word for word. |

Every one of these divergences already stands in the 1862 printing.
