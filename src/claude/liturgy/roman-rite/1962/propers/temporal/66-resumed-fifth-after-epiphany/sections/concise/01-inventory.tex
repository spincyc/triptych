\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Dicit Dominus}; Jer 29:11--12, 14; verse Ps 84:2 (Heb.\ 85) &
God to the exiles: ``I think thoughts of peace, and not of affliction''; they
shall call and he will hear, and he will bring back their captivity; the
verse thanks him for turning it away.\\
Collect & \latin{Familiam tuam} & That God keep his family with continual
mercy, so that, leaning on the hope of heavenly grace alone, it may always be
defended by his protection.\\
Epistle & Col 3:12--17 & Put on mercy, kindness, humility, modesty, patience;
bear with and forgive one another as the Lord forgave you; above all have
charity; let Christ's peace rejoice and his word dwell in you; sing to God in
your hearts.\\
Gradual & \latin{Liberasti nos}; Ps 43:8--9 (Heb.\ 44) & ``Thou hast delivered
us, O Lord, from them that afflict us: and hast put them to shame that hate
us''; in God we will glory all the day.\\
Alleluia & \latin{De profundis}; Ps 129:1--2 (Heb.\ 130) & ``From the depths I
have cried to Thee, O Lord: Lord, hear my prayer.''\\
Gospel & Mt 13:24--30 & While men sleep an enemy sows cockle among the wheat;
the householder will not let his servants gather it, lest they root up the
wheat with it, and leaves both to grow until the harvest.\\
Offertory & \latin{De profundis}; Ps 129:1--2 (Heb.\ 130) & The Alleluia's cry
sung again, its first line repeated.\\
Secret & \latin{Hostias tibi} & Offerings of propitiation; that God
mercifully absolve our sins and direct our wavering hearts.\\
Communion & \latin{Amen dico vobis}; Mk 11:24 & ``Whatsoever you ask when you
pray, believe that you shall receive, and it shall be done unto you.''\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} & That we may receive the
effect of the salvation whose pledge we have received in these mysteries.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
