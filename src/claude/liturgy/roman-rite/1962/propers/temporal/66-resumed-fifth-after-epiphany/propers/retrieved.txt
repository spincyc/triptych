RETRIEVED -- Dominica quinta quae superfuit post Epiphaniam (resumed Fifth Sunday after the
Epiphany), 1962 Missale Romanum, with its public-domain antecedent in the Pustet 1862 Missal
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Three machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording. Every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images: the 1962 facsimile at 200 dpi whole and in
400-dpi crops of each column section, and the Pustet 1862 facsimile on page images rendered
from the whole registered PDF. The pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what each
text layer gives and where it fails. Retrieved and recorded on 2026-11-05 in the research
stage (iteration 0) of proper-study run eb621aedbeee828e.

Each pull is reproduced byte for byte between its BEGIN and END lines, form feeds included.
Where an extract does not end in a line feed, one line feed is added before the END line;
each stated extract SHA-256 is of the bytes as the tool emitted them.
