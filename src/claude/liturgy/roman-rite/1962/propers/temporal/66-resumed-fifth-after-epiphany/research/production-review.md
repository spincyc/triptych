## Author-study, iteration 0 (5 November 2026)

**Scope.** The expansive study (`main.tex`, generated by `tools/tpt
document-types refresh` from the `proper-study-v1` declaration in
`publication.toml`) of the resumed Fifth Sunday after the Epiphany as said on
Sunday 8 November 2026, the twenty-fourth Sunday after Pentecost, in the
universal 1962 calendar: the opening with the occurrence, the three questions
and an element map; the appointed Latin of the ten elements (nos. 1749-1758)
with public-domain English; the element-by-element settings; three
interpretations of the whole formulary (`patience-of-the-field`,
`cry-from-captivity`, `word-in-the-heart`), each with its own four senses; a
comparison; the generated Scriptural Date and Location appendix; the scope
appendix and References. The schema-2 `proper-components.toml` declares the
future concise and homily components and the `interpretive-pagination-v1`
presentation roles; `web-edition.toml` (eligible) and
`generation-metadata.tex` were written. No leaf-local `format.tex` is used; the
running head is a `[document]` field.

**Appointed-text mode.** `complete`, as the research records establish: no
reference-only authorization exists for this identity, and every word of the
ten texts stands in the Pustet 1862 printing read on its page images. Labels
`proper-<key>` stand after the ten element headings of the appointed-text
section and nowhere else.

**English.** Douay-Rheims (Challoner) for the Epistle and Gospel and for each
chant's canonical verses; Lasance 1945, *Supplementary Mass No. 2*, for the five
chants as the Missal sings them, following the recommendation of
`research/scope.md` section 2.4; Lasance 1945 for the Collect (p. 196), Secret
(p. 746) and Postcommunion (p. 198, the rendering that carries *in these
mysteries*). Latin of the Fathers without a public-domain English edition is
given with an English rendering made for the study, as the scope appendix
states.

**Extent.** 30 physical pages in the author proof (letter, Latin Modern,
11 pt). Approximate word counts by component, LaTeX markup excluded: opening
1,540; appointed texts 2,300 (the set Latin and English included); each element
