\section*{The Propers: Map}

\begingroup
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.0}
\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit \cue{Int.}}{\latin{Deus, in adiutórium meum inténde} \properrefs{Ps. 69:2--3, v.~4}}{The emergency cry}{The verse with which the Office opens every hour: help begged before anything else is said.}
\properrow{Collect \cue{Coll.}}{\latin{Omnípotens et miséricors Deus} \properrefs{composed}}{Service as gift}{Concedes what the Epistle will argue: worthy service comes \latin{de cuius múnere}, of God's own gift.}
\properrow{Epistle \cue{Ep.}}{\latin{Fidúciam talem habémus} \properrefs{2 Cor. 3:4--9}}{Letter and spirit}{Not sufficient of ourselves to think anything; the letter kills, the spirit gives life; two ministrations compared.}
\properrow{Gradual \cue{Grad.}}{\latin{Benedícam Dóminum} \properrefs{Ps. 33:2--3}}{Praise without pause}{The receiver's posture: blessing at all times, with the meek --- not the strong --- bidden hear and rejoice.}
\properrow{Alleluia \cue{All.}}{\latin{Dómine, Deus salútis meæ} \properrefs{Ps. 87:2}}{The dark psalm's first verse}{One cry, day and night, from the psalter's one lament that never brightens.}
\properrow{Gospel \cue{Gosp.}}{\latin{Beáti óculi \dots\ Homo quidam} \properrefs{Lk. 10:23--37; Credo}}{Mercy proves the neighbour}{Blessed eyes; the lawyer's test; priest and Levite passing; the Samaritan's oil, wine and inn; \latin{Vade, et tu fac simíliter}.}
\properrow{Offertory \cue{Off.}}{\latin{Precátus est Móyses} \properrefs{Ex. 32:11, 13, 14}}{Intercession, sung}{A rare story-chant: Moses pleads, recalls God's own oath, and the Lord is appeased --- as the gifts go up.}
\properrow{Secret \cue{Sec.}}{\latin{Hóstias, quǽsumus, Dómine} \properrefs{composed}}{Pardon as glory}{By lavishing pardon on us, the offerings give honour to God's name: one transaction, not two.}
\properrow{Communion \cue{Comm.}}{\latin{De fructu óperum tuórum} \properrefs{Ps. 103:13--15}}{Bread, wine, oil}{The creation psalm's three staples, named at the moment the faithful eat.}
\properrow{Postcommunion \cue{Postcomm.}}{\latin{Vivíficet nos, quǽsumus} \properrefs{composed}}{Life, cleansing, defence}{Asks one participation to give life and bestow, \latin{páriter}, expiation for the past and a guard for what comes.}
\end{properstable}
\endgroup

\subsection*{The four senses}

\begingroup
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & Christ blesses the eyes that see, and answers a self-justifying lawyer with a Samaritan whose mercy proved him neighbour \cue{Gosp.}; Paul's sufficiency as minister is from God \cue{Ep.}; Moses pleads and the Lord is appeased \cue{Off.}.\\
Allegorical & From Irenaeus to Bede the Samaritan is Christ --- the name received as \latin{custos}, guardian --- binding wounded humanity and carrying it to the inn of the Church \cue{Gosp.}; the Communion sets that bread, wine and oil on the communicant's lips \cue{Comm.}.\\
Moral & \latin{Vade, et tu fac simíliter} \cue{Gosp.}: mercy, not pedigree or office, makes the neighbour; the letter alone kills \cue{Ep.}, and the survivor's posture is unbroken praise among the meek \cue{Grad.}.\\
Anagogical & The Collect asks that we run \latin{sine offensióne} to promises not yet held \cue{Coll.}; the ministration of justice shall abound in glory \cue{Ep.}; the oath names a land flowing with milk and honey \cue{Off.}.\\
\end{sensestable}
\endgroup
