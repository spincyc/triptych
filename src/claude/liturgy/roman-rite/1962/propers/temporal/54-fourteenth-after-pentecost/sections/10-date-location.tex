\section*{Scriptural Date and Location}

\begin{dossiertable}
Offertory & Ps. 33:8--9a (modern 34:7--8a) & Geth in the superscription; David before kingship & \chronodate{offertory}{\chronology{israel.monarchy.david-at-geth}{superscription-setting}{A. M. 2944, A. C. 1060}}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{The psalm's title names Achimelech, while 1~Kings~21 names Achis as the king before whom David changed his behavior. The 2014 Loreto/Feeney Haydock edition places that chapter within its continuous marginal frame A.~M.~2944, A.~C.~1060; its note at Ps.~70:1 identifies the reckoning as Ussher-attributed. This dates the received superscription setting, not the psalm's composition. Moving from deliverance to instruction and redemption, the psalm fixes neither its first audience nor its place or date of composition.}
\midrule
Introit & Ps. 83:10--11a; 2--3a (modern 84) & Korahite pilgrimage toward Sion and the temple & \chronodate{introit}{\chronology{composition.psalms-of-the-sons-of-korah}{composition}{between the days of Isaias and the return from exile}}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{The sons of Korah sing the courts of the Lord, ascent, the anointed, and one better day. Drum places the Korahite collection between the days of Isaias and the return from exile. Augustine and Cassiodorus read that day eschatologically; Theodoret relates the psalm to displaced Israel, and Bellarmine to heavenly pilgrimage.}
\midrule
Alleluia & Ps. 94:1 (modern 95:1) & Israel summoned before the Lord, creator and shepherd & \chronodate{alleluia}{Undated in the received chronology.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Praise leads to the warning against a hardened heart and exclusion from rest, received by Hebrews as God's word for ``today.'' Augustine and Cassiodorus hear jubilation beyond speech, while Theodoret proposes a Josian setting in his exposition. Drum's traditional account assigns no date, and the psalm's composition date remains undetermined.}
\midrule
Gradual & Ps. 117:8--9 (modern 118:8--9) & Israel's thanksgiving procession toward the altar & \chronodate{gradual}{Undated in the received chronology.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Confidence in the Lord stands amid deliverance, gates of justice, the rejected stone, the Lord-made day, and approach to the altar. The text names neither writer nor first audience. Augustine and Cassiodorus include angelic princes, Theodoret earthly rulers, and Bellarmine subordinate creaturely aid; none dates the psalm.}
\midrule
Gospel, Communion & Mt. 6:24--33; v.~33 adapted & Galilee; a mountain near Capharnaum & \chronodate{gospel,communion}{\chronology{composition.gospel-of-matthew}{composition}{about A.D. 38-45}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-matthew}{composition}{about the year 40-42}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-matthew}{composition}{the years 40-45}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-matthew}{composition}{about the year 60-68}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-matthew}{composition}{about the years 64-67}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-matthew}{composition}{about the year 50}}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierevent{Event: Christ teaches within the Sermon on the Mount, amid the Galilean ministry and hearers from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond Jordan. Event date undetermined in the received chronology.}
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Treasure, undivided sight, and rival masters lead to birds, lilies, and the Father's knowledge of bodily need. Jacquier reports about A.D.~38--45 from Eusebius, Theophylact, Euthymius, and Nicephorus; about 40--42 or 60--68 from rival datings of the apostolic dispersal; about 64--67 from Irenaeus; and 40--45 as the prevailing view among Catholic critics. Durand places the lost Aramaic original about A.D.~50 but leaves its Greek translation undated. The Communion is a liturgical adaptation of v.~33, not a second composition.}
\midrule
Epistle & Gal. 5:16--24 & Galatian churches; place of composition disputed & \chronodate{epistle}{\chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{A.D. 49-50}; \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{about A.D. 53, 54}; \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{(1 and 2 Corinthians; Galatians), 56}; \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{A.D. 57 or 58}}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Paul writes to evangelized communities under pressure to impose Mosaic observances on Gentile converts. Aherne reports three disputed datings: Ramsay and Weber at Antioch before the council in A.D.~49--50, Cornel and most southern-Galatian interpreters about A.D.~53--54, and northern-Galatian interpreters in A.D.~57 or 58. Prat places Galatians with the Corinthian letters in A.D.~56. A Lapide reports the Roman tradition of the end-subscriptions, Jerome, Athanasius, and Theodoret, then the Ephesian or other eastern placement of Chrysostom and Baronius, and concludes that time and place remain uncertain. The USCCB/NABRE introduction favors a northern audience near Ancyra and composition at Ephesus about A.D.~54--55; it treats a southern audience in Pisidia, Lycaonia, and Pamphylia at perhaps A.D.~48--50 as less likely and assigns that hypothesis no definite place of composition.}
\end{dossiertable}
