\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Dicit Dominus}; Jer 29:11--12, 14; verse Ps 84:2 &
God to the exiles: ``I think thoughts of peace, and not of affliction; you
shall call upon Me and I will hear you; and I will bring back your captivity
from all places''; the verse: ``Thou hast turned away the captivity of
Jacob.''\\
Collect & \latin{Excita, quaesumus, Domine} & That God ``arouse \dots\ the
wills of Thy faithful,'' that they ``may the more abundantly partake of Thy
mercies.''\\
Epistle & Col 1:9--14 & Paul prays that the Colossians ``walk worthy of
God'' in patience, and thanks the Father, ``Who hath delivered us from the
power of darkness and hath translated us into the kingdom of the Son of his
love.''\\
Gradual & \latin{Liberasti nos}; Ps 43:8--9 & ``Thou hast delivered us, O
Lord, from them that afflict us: and hast put them to shame that hate us. In
God we will glory all the day.''\\
Alleluia & \latin{De profundis}; Ps 129:1--2 & ``From the depths I have cried
to Thee, O Lord: Lord, hear my prayer.''\\
Gospel & Mt 24:15--35 & The abomination and the flight, with no going down
from the housetop; false christs; the coming like lightning; the body and the
eagles; the elect gathered from the four winds; the fig tree; ``Heaven and
earth shall pass: but my words shall not pass.''\\
Offertory & \latin{De profundis}; Ps 129:1--2 & The Alleluia's cry sung again,
its first line repeated.\\
Secret & \latin{Propitius esto, Domine} & That God ``turn all our hearts
unto Thee, that, being delivered from earthly desires, we may pass on to the
enjoyments of heaven.''\\
Communion & \latin{Amen dico vobis}; Mk 11:24 & ``Whatsoever you ask when you
pray, believe that you shall receive, and it shall be done unto you.''\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Concede nobis, quaesumus, Domine} & That, ``through
this sacrament which we have received, whatever is evil in our hearts may be
restored by its gift of healing.''\\
\end{concisemaptable}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:end}

\vspace{0.1em}
{\footnotesize\noindent Second class; green; Gloria, Credo, Trinity
Preface. Said on the twenty-sixth Sunday after Pentecost by count, in the last
place, after the resumed Fifth and Sixth Sundays after the Epiphany
(RG~18~b); St Cecilia is not commemorated (RG~111~b). Chants (those of the
Twenty-third Sunday) and orations in the Lasance English (1945), lessons in
the Douay--Rheims (Challoner); psalms in the Vulgate numbering, the Hebrew one
higher.\par}
