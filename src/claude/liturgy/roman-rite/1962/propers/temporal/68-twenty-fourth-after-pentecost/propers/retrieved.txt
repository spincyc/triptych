Sunday after Pentecost), 1962 Missale Romanum, with its public-domain antecedent in the
Pustet 1862 Missal and the 1945 Lasance hand missal that prints the formulary in English
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Three machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording. Every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images: the 1962 facsimile at 200 dpi whole and in
400-dpi crops of each column section, the Pustet 1862 facsimile on 200-dpi page images
rendered from the whole registered PDF, and the Lasance pages on 220-dpi page images. The
pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what each text layer gives and where it fails.
Retrieved and recorded on 2026-11-16 in the research stage (iteration 0) of proper-study run
11c5af05fe374203.

Each pull is reproduced byte for byte between its BEGIN and END lines, form feeds included.
Where an extract does not end in a line feed, one line feed is added before the END line;
