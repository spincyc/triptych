RETRIEVED -- Twentieth Sunday after Pentecost, 1962 Missale Romanum
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Three machine pulls follow. None controls any published wording: every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images of the controlling facsimile rendered at 200 and
400 dpi, and the 1862 antecedent was read on the page images of its tracked extractions at
their native 300 ppi. The pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what a text layer gives
and where it fails.
