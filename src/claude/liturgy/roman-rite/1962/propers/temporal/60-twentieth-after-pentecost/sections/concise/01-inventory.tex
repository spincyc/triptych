\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Omnia, quae fecisti nobis}; Dan 3:31, 29 and 35, as printed;
verse Ps 118:1 (Heb.\ 119) & Azarias's confession in the furnace: all that God
has done ``thou hast done by a just judgment: for we have sinned and disobeyed
thy commandments''; then the plea for mercy (1861 English). The verse blesses
``the undefiled in the way''.\\
Collect & \latin{Largire, quaesumus, Domine} & Pardon and peace, that the
faithful, cleansed of every offence, ``may serve thee with secure minds'' (1861
English).\\
Epistle & Eph 5:15--21 & Walk as wise, ``redeeming the time, because the days
are evil''; be filled with the Holy Spirit, singing in your hearts and giving
thanks always for all things.\\
Gradual & \latin{Oculi omnium}; Ps 144:15--16 (Heb.\ 145) & ``The eyes of all
hope in thee, O Lord: and thou givest them meat in due season.''\\
Alleluia & \latin{Paratum cor meum}; Ps 107:2 (Heb.\ 108) & ``My heart is
ready, O God, my heart is ready''; the Missal's form names God the singer's
glory.\\
Gospel & Jn 4:46--53 & A ruler asks Jesus to come down to his dying son and is
rebuked for a faith that waits on signs; ``Go thy way. Thy son liveth''; he believes the word,
goes, learns the hour, and believes with his whole house.\\
Offertory & \latin{Super flumina Babylonis}; Ps 136:1 (Heb.\ 137) & Captives
weep by the rivers of Babylon, remembering Sion, addressed as ``thee''.\\
Secret & \latin{Caelestem nobis praebeant} & That these mysteries ``procure us
a heavenly remedy, and cleanse away the vices of our hearts'' (1861 English).\\
Communion & \latin{Memento verbi tui}; Ps 118:49--50 (Heb.\ 119) & God is
asked to remember his word, ``in which thou hast given me hope''.\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Ut sacris, Domine} & ``That we may be worthy of thy
sacred gifts \dots\ we may always obey thy commandments'' (1861 English).\\
\end{concisemaptable}
