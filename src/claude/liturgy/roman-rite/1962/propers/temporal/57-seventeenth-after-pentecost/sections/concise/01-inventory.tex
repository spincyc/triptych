\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:start}
\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \latin{Iustus es, Domine}; Ps 118:137, 124; verse 1 & God is just and
his judgment right; deal with thy servant according to thy mercy.\\
Collect & \latin{Da, quaesumus, Domine, populo tuo} & To shun the devil's
contagion and follow the one God with a pure mind.\\
Epistle & Eph 4:1--6 & Walk worthily of the calling in humility and patience,
keeping the unity of the Spirit: one body, one Spirit, one hope, one Lord, one
faith, one baptism, one God and Father of all.\\
Gradual & \latin{Beata gens}; Ps 32:12, 6 & Blessed the nation whose God is the
Lord, the people he chose for his inheritance; the heavens made firm by his word
and the spirit of his mouth.\\
Alleluia & \latin{Domine, exaudi}; Ps 101:2 & ``Hear, O Lord, my prayer: and
let my cry come to thee.''\\
Gospel & Mt 22:34--46 & The great commandment and the second like it; whose son
the Christ is, whom David calls Lord; the questioners' silence.\\
Offertory & \latin{Oravi Deum meum ego Daniel}; Dan 9:17--19 & Daniel prays God
to hear his servant, shine his face upon his sanctuary, and favour the people
called by his name.\\
Secret & \latin{Maiestatem tuam, Domine} & That the holy things we do strip us
of past and future sins.\\
Communion & \latin{Vovete et reddite}; Ps 75:12--13 & Vow and pay to the Lord,
all you round about him who bring gifts, to the God who takes away the spirit
of princes.\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Sanctificationibus tuis} & That our vices be cured by
God's sanctifying gifts, and eternal remedies come to us.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:end}

\vspace{0.4em}
{\footnotesize\noindent The Mass is of the second class, in green with Gloria
and Credo; it says one set of orations and admits no commemoration. Its
Preface, of the Most Holy Trinity, is appointed by rubric and is not proper to
the day.\par}
