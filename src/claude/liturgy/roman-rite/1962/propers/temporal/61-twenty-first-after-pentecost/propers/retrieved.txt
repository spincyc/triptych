RETRIEVED -- Twenty-first Sunday after Pentecost, 1962 Missale Romanum
Unedited finding aid. NOT publication text. Optical-reading errors are preserved deliberately.

Two machine pulls follow. Neither controls any published wording: every Latin form in
propers/verified.md was read on page images of the controlling facsimile rendered at 200 and
400 dpi, and the 1862 antecedent was read on page images of its registered complete scan.
The pulls are kept so that a later reader can see what each text layer gives and where it fails.
