# Edition Manifest

Every source actually used, with its exact identity, the role it plays, and what was read.

## Controlling Missal

**Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini restitutum, Summorum Pontificum
cura recognitum, editio typica.** Vatican City: Typis Polyglottis Vaticanis, 1962.

Read in the Church Music Association of America facsimile, a 1,088-page PDF,
SHA-256 `648fdb8fe830ed65a08aa4a95de6f94424c533ddf2398c8fc26b18735fd3518a`, registered as
`artifact.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-1962.cmaa-facsimile-pdf`.

The artifact-page to printed-page offset is not constant, and both values are recorded here
because they are easy to get wrong. In the roman-numbered front matter it is **+2**: artifact
page 13 carries printed page XV, artifact page 34 carries printed page XXXVI, established by
reading the running heads on artifact pages 12–18 and 34 as images. In the arabic-numbered
body it is **+81**: artifact page 203 carries printed page 122 (*Feria IV post dominicam I
Passionis*) and artifact page 364 carries printed page 283 (*Praefatio defunctorum*).

| Constituent | Artifact pages | Printed pages | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Rubricae generales*, nn. 1–137 | 10–18 | XII–XX | In full; nn. 1–20, 91–114, 115–137 as 260 dpi page images |
| *Rubricae generales Breviarii Romani* (nn. 138–268) | 18 | XX | The Missal's own two-word substitute, `Hic omittuntur`, read as a page image |
| *Rubricae generales Missalis Romani*, nn. 269–530 | 19–34 | XXI–XXXVI | In full; nn. 301–334, 384–411, 424–443, 460–479, 480–503, 504–514 as 260 dpi page images |

| Proper of the Season, Proper of Saints, Commons, votives, Requiems | as cited per case | — | Only the formularies the worked cases use |

The 1962 Missal prints the precedence table of RG 91. It does **not** print the *Tabella
occurrentiae*, the *Tabella concurrentiae* or the *Notanda* that follow the code in the
*Acta*. It also does not print part two of the code. Both omissions are stated in the
publication.

The code as printed in the Missal ends at **n. 530**, not at a higher number; the last rubric
is `Usus accendendi cereum, prope altare, a Consecratione ad Communionem, ubi viget,
servetur.` The *Acta* text ends at the same number. This was checked in both witnesses.

## Promulgating acts and the promulgated code

**Acta Apostolicae Sedis 52 (1960).** Read in the Holy See's own scanned volume,
SHA-256 `c734079d1a9393f09595bd95dd5900ad30e9c5a2bc6939a8bfd84e048f414702`, registered as
`artifact.holy-see.acta-apostolicae-sedis.volume-52-1960.vatican-ocr-pdf-c734079d`. Artifact
page numbering equals printed page numbering in this volume.

| Constituent | Printed pages | Role | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| John XXIII, *Rubricarum instructum*, 25 July 1960 | 593–595 | Approving act; three-part division; effective 1 January 1961 | In full, optical text layer |
| S.R.C., *Decretum generale quo novus rubricarum Breviarii ac Missalis Romani codex promulgatur*, 26 July 1960 | 596 | Promulgating decree | In full, optical text layer |
| *Rubricae Breviarii et Missalis Romani* | 597–705 | Textual control; source of RGBR 237–238 | Pars prima and pars tertia compared against the Missal at the rules used; RGBR 237–239 read as a page image |
| *Variationes in Breviario et Missali Romano* | 706–740 | What the reform abolished | Cap. II n. 13 and cap. IV n. 18 read as 200 dpi page images; the rest not read line by line |
| S. Congregatio Concilii, *Novus index statuitur dierum festorum, quibus inest obligatio litandi Sacrum pro populo*, 3 December 1960 | 985–986 | The contemporaneous *festa de praecepto* list, cited once | In full, optical text layer |

**Difference between the two witnesses of the code.** The *Acta* prints the code under the
title *Rubricae Breviarii et Missalis Romani* divided into *Pars prima* and numbered
*Caput* headings; the Missal reprints the same numbered rules under running divisions
(`I - Normae generales`, `II - De die liturgico in genere`, …). The rule numbers, wording and
order agree at every rule this publication uses. No variant reading affects any result here.

## Identified English witnesses

- **The Roman Missal translated into the English language for the use of the laity**, first
  revised edition. Philadelphia: Eugene Cummiskey, 1861. Used through the project transcription
  `artifact.eugene-cummiskey.roman-missal-english-laity.philadelphia-1861.temporal-orations-en`
  (tracked TSV, public domain), row `epiph-05 / collect`. A pre-1955 witness; its rubrics,
  seasonal second and third orations and Holy Week are not those of 1962 and are not used.
- **Douay-Rheims Bible, Challoner revision**, in the tracked Project Gutenberg electronic
  edition, book file `50-john`. Used for John 1, 1–3 and 14.

## Non-controlling aid

- **Missale Romanum … editio iuxta typicam.** New York: Benziger Brothers, 1962. Its Internet
  Archive optical transcription, SHA-256
  `2a2da44d4f2054ffd9fa5a0fa87ec427fe2a99410f438112282eed82254585ce`, was downloaded, hashed
  against the registered artifact, and used only to locate the front-matter rubrics before the
  Vatican facsimile was read. It controls no wording in the publication.

## Deliberately not consulted

No rubrical manual, commentary, handbook, Ordo or authorised-response collection was
consulted or cited. The publication states this limitation in its References and identifies it
as the reason some questions in Section 10 remain open here that a competent commentator would
close.
