\section{Rubrical concordance}

The code's three parts are numbered continuously from 1 to 530, and each part
has its own shorthand here. \rg{n} is \work{Rubricae generales}, the first
part, printed at nn.~1--137 in the front matter of the 1962 \work{Missale
Romanum}. \rgm{n} is \work{Rubricae generales Missalis Romani}, the third
part, nn.~269--530 in the same place. \rgb{n} is \work{Rubricae generales
Breviarii Romani}, the second part, nn.~138--268, which the Missal omits and
which is cited from the promulgated text in the \work{Acta}; only
\rgb{237}--\rgb{238} are used here.

This table lists the decisions this book teaches and the exact rubric that
answers each. Claim class: \textbf{D} a direct rule; \textbf{I} a necessary
inference from two or more direct rules; \textbf{C} contested, with both
readings given at the place shown.
