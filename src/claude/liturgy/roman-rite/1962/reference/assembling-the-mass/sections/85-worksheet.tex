\section{A worksheet}

Copy the twelve lines below onto a card and fill them in for the date in
question. Each line names the rubric that answers it, so a disputed answer can
be checked against its own locus rather than against a memory.

\begin{tablerule}{Line}{Fill in}{Locus}
1. Calendar & Which calendar binds this Mass in this church? Universal,
diocesan or religious, plus the proper of this church. Note any feast of
\rgm{278} that must be said whatever else. & \rgm{274}--\rgm{284}\\
2. Competitors & Every liturgical day falling today, in every column of that
calendar: Sunday or feria (with its class), vigil, day within an octave,
feast, Our Lady on Saturday. & \rg{6}, \rg{78}\\
3. Row numbers & The precedence-table row of each competitor. Write the
numbers, not the names. & \rg{91}\\
4. Winner & The highest row. This is the Office of the day. & \rg{7},
\rg{93}\\
5. Losers & For each: omitted, commemorated, transferred, or repositioned? Was
the occurrence accidental or perpetual? Only first-class feasts transfer; only
perpetual collisions reposition. & \rg{95}--\rg{102}\\
6. Commemoration budget & How many commemorations does today admit, and is any
of them privileged? \emph{Is this Mass sung and non-conventual?} If so the
budget is one privileged commemoration only. & \rg{108}--\rg{114},
\rgm{434}\\
7. Mass category & Mass of the Office, or a festive Mass in the broad sense, a
votive of class I--IV, a Mass of the dead of class I--IV, a ritual Mass, or an
external solemnity? Check the category's own conditions --- one-Mass church,
conventual burden, exercises actually held, who is celebrating and for whom.
& \rgm{270}, \rgm{301}--\rgm{423}\\
8. Formulary & Proper of the Season, Proper of Saints, or Common --- and if the
Proper of Saints refers to a Common, which Common and which formulary within
it. Note any seasonal substitution the Missal prints at the place. &
\rgm{298}--\rgm{299}, \rgm{305}, \rgm{314}--\rgm{315}\\
9. Colour & From \rg{119}--\rg{132}, or \rgm{323} for a fourth-class read
votive. Then look at line 10 again, because violet suppresses the Gloria. &
\rg{117}--\rg{132}\\
10. Ordinary conditionals & Psalm \latin{Iudica me}; \latin{Gloria Patri} at
the Introit; Gloria; Creed; sequence; \latin{Oratio super populum}; dismissal;
blessing; Last Gospel. & \rgm{424}--\rgm{432}, \rgm{470}, \rgm{475}--\rgm{476},
\rgm{506}--\rgm{510}\\
11. Orations & List them in order with their conclusions: Mass's own; anything
under a single conclusion with it; commemorations, season first; imposed
prayer; votive prayer. Count. Three is the absolute ceiling. Then copy the
same number and order to the secrets and postcommunions. & \rgm{433}--\rgm{446},
\rgm{480}, \rgm{505}\\
12. Preface & Proper of this Mass? If not, is one assigned \latin{de Tempore}
for today's date? If not, Common. Remember that no commemoration brings a
Preface with it. & \rgm{482}--\rgm{499}\\
\end{tablerule}

\subsection{Six checks that catch most errors}

\begin{steps}
\item \textbf{Did you compare row numbers, or classes?} Two third-class items
can meet, and the table --- not the class --- decides. \rg{25}'s two clauses are
the standard trap: a Lenten feria beats a third-class feast, an Advent feria
before 17 December loses to one.

\item \textbf{Is this Mass sung and not the conventual Mass?} If so, every
ordinary commemoration disappears, and the imposed prayer is barred outright
whether conventual or not.

\item \textbf{Is the Mass \latin{de Tempore}?} \rgm{425}\,a, \rgm{428} and
\rgm{506} all turn on that phrase, and a saint's Mass on a Lenten weekday is
not \latin{de Tempore} even though it is said in Lent.

\item \textbf{Did the Preface come from the Mass or from the date?} Step two of
\rgm{482} is a calendar question. Establish it before you choose the formulary
and it cannot be got wrong.

\item \textbf{Is something following the Mass?} A procession removes
\latin{Ite, missa est}, the blessing and the Last Gospel together; an absolution
at the tomb removes the Last Gospel.

\item \textbf{Did a particular calendar enter the answer?} If so, name the
approved proper you read. A patronage, title, dedication or indult asserted
from memory is not a source.
\end{steps}
