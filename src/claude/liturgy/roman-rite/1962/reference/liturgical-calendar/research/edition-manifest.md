# Edition Manifest

Every entry names the exact witness through which the text was read for this leaf and the state in
which it was left. `read` means the identified passage was rendered and examined as text;
`image-collated` means it was additionally read in a rendered page image at the stated resolution;
`reported` means the act is known here only because another source named below names it.

The 1942/44 Benziger witness was treated in an earlier revision of this leaf as of wholly unresolved
identity, on the strength of the hosting item's empty metadata. Reading the scan's own title page,
copyright line, and approval leaf corrected that. The correction is recorded in the edition record
under `src/sources/works/catholic-church/missale-romanum/editions/unidentified-pre-1955-witness-ia-1947/`,
whose directory slug is deliberately left unchanged so that existing bindings do not break; the
printing year of the scanned copy is still not established, and the hosting item's "1947" is
uncorroborated.

## Not read

- The Breviary rubrics, *pars secunda*, nn. 138-268.
- The Acta's *Tabella occurrentiae* and *Tabella concurrentiae* in detail.
- Any Ordo, for any year, under the 1960 rubrics.
- Any pre-1955 Breviary, and therefore the pre-1955 tables of occurrence and concurrence.
- Any second exemplar of either controlling printing.
