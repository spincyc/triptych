# Evidence Map

Each row names a principal published claim, the witness carrying it, the evidentiary state, and the
qualification that travels with it. States follow the universal standard: *verified source text*
(wording read in the identified witness at the recorded locus), *checked paraphrase* (the published
statement checked at an exact locus), *source-grounded synthesis* (an editorial conclusion resting
on named loci), and *project judgment* (labelled argument beyond the sources). Unqualified loci are
numbers of the 1960 code; "Missal rubrics" marks part three.
