# Two Missals, One Sacrifice — Sequence Inventory

The ordinary-expositions profile requires a record of every movement's
boundary, incipit, locator, speaker, action and status. Because this is a
cross-edition study, each row carries both witnesses and the classification the
publication assigns. Classifications are: **R** retained with the same Latin
words; **RA** retained with altered words; **RR** retained but reassigned in
speaker, audibility or position; **D** dropped; **N** newly composed or newly
prescribed.

Locators: 1962 by marginal number where the Ordo Missae supplies one, otherwise
by printed page and artifact page; 2002 by *Ordo Missae* article number and
artifact page.

## Coverage note

Every movement printed in either Order of Mass is accounted for above. The
publication treats in detail those movements where the two books differ
materially or where a common claim is at stake; it does not give equal length
to movements that are identical in both. Rubrical matter outside the Order of
Mass — the *Ritus servandus*, *De defectibus*, the general rubrics, the
*Institutio generalis* — is inventoried in the edition manifest and cited by
article, not by movement.
