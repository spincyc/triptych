# Two Missals, One Sacrifice — Edition Manifest

This record identifies every liturgical or legal witness the publication uses,
the exact artifact through which it was reached, and the boundary of what was
inspected. It is the ordinary-expositions profile's edition manifest for a
cross-edition study; each witness keeps its own source trail.

- **Work:** `work.catholic-church.missale-romanum`
- **Edition:** `edition.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-1962` —
  *Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini restitutum,
  Summorum Pontificum cura recognitum*, editio typica (Vatican City: Typis
  Polyglottis Vaticanis, 1962).
- **Artifact:**
  `artifact.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-1962.cmaa-facsimile-pdf`
  — Church Music Association of America facsimile, 1,088 pages, SHA-256
  `648fdb8f…3518a`. Downloaded publication-locally 2026-07-25; the digest
  matched the registered record exactly. Storage `remote`; no payload retained.
- **Language, rite, territory:** Latin; Roman Rite; universal.
- **Governing rubrics:** the *Rubricae generales Missalis romani* printed in the
  book, which are the Missal's part of the *Codex rubricarum* approved by John

- **Not this witness:** the Benziger *editio iuxta typicam* of 1962, which is a
  separate registered edition and is not used here; any earlier Roman typical
  edition; any hand missal.

### Internal discrepancy recorded, not resolved

The book states two different dates for *Rubricarum instructum*:

| Locus | Date given |
| --- | --- |
| *Decretum* of the Sacred Congregation of Rites, 23 June 1962 (artifact p. 2) | `diei 23 iulii anno 1960`, with the code promulgated `postero die` |
| Sacred Congregation of Rites, general decree promulgating the code, 26 July 1960 (artifact p. 9) | `die 25 iulii huius anni` |

Only 25 July is consistent with the 1962 decree's own *postero die* relative to
the general decree of 26 July. Both pages were read; the earlier one visually at
a rendered page image. The discrepancy is internal to this edition. No claim in
the publication depends on which date is right, and it is left unresolved.

## Terminology fixed for this study

- **``the 1962 Missal''** means witness A, and nothing else.
- **``the postconciliar Missal''** means witness B unless the sentence names the
  1969 *Ordo Missae*, the 1970 or 1975 typical edition, or the 2008 reprint.
- **``the Roman Canon''** is used for the same prayer in both books; where a
  distinction is needed the study says *Canon Missae* of 1962 or *Prex
  eucharistica I* of 2002.
- ``Novus Ordo'', ``Tridentine Mass'' and ``TLM'' are not used in analytical
  prose; where a popular label matters it is named as a label.
