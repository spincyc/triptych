# The Vulgate — evidence map

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Each principal claim of the publication is
mapped to its evidence layer, exact witness, confidence, and the
qualification that must travel with it. Layers: D = document/artifact;
A = contemporary participant document; T = later tradition/reception;
M = official ecclesial act; K = named modern reconstruction or dated
survey; S = project synthesis. Witness URLs and access dates (all
2026-07-24, re-verified 2026-07-25 as recorded there) are in
`source-audit.md`.
