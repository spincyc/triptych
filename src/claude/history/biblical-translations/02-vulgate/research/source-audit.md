## Official ecclesial acts (M) — checked

- Trent, Session IV (8 Apr 1546), both decrees, Waterworth English at
  papalencyclicals.net/councils/trent/fourth-session.htm: canon list
  "entire with all their parts... as they are contained in the old Latin
  vulgate edition" under anathema; *Insuper*: "the said old and vulgate
  edition... in public lectures, disputations, sermons and expositions,
  held as authentic"; printing "in the most correct manner possible."
- Pius XII, Divino afflante Spiritu 21 (1943), vatican.va EN: Trent
  authenticity "applies only to the Latin Church and to the public use";
  does not diminish the original texts; "not specified primarily as
  critical, but rather as juridical"; free from error in faith and
  morals.
- Dei Verbum 22 (1965), vatican.va EN: honor to the Septuagint, Eastern
  versions, "and the Latin translation known as the vulgate";
  translations "especially from the original texts."
- Scripturarum thesaurus (25 Apr 1979), vatican.va EN
  (.../hf_jp-ii_apc_19790425_scripturarum-thesaurus.html; note the URL
  uses date 19790425): declares the Nova Vulgata "typical" and
  promulgates it "to be used especially in the sacred Liturgy";
  commission 29 Nov 1965; volumes 1969–1977; "certain books of the Old
  Testament which Saint Jerome did not touch"; recites Inter praecipuas
  (15 June 1933) founding the Abbey of St. Jerome.
- Nova Vulgata, Praefatio ad Lectorem (editio typica altera), vatican.va
  LA: Pius XII and the Pian Psalter (1945); commission under Bea (1965)
  then Schick (1971); Psalter 10 Aug 1969; NT 1970–71 (3 vols); OT
  1976–77 (4 vols); final volume presented to Paul VI 26 Oct 1977;
  working norms 1–9; second-edition retouches; Clementine book order
  retained; Masoretic psalm numbering with Vulgate numbers in
  parentheses.
- Liturgiam authenticam (28 Mar 2001) 24, 37, 41, 43, vatican.va EN:
  translation from original languages; Nova Vulgata "normally to be
  consulted as an auxiliary tool"; canonical-text/manuscript-tradition
  reference; guidance on traditional readings and terms.
- CCC 131 (and bounded check of 101–141), vatican.va EN Catechism site:
  "access to Sacred Scripture ought to be open wide to the Christian
  faithful"; no named treatment of the Vulgate in the article on
  Scripture.

The authoring context was lost before final review, so on 2026-07-25

two exact-bytes events were replayed locally: the registered Dods 1871
literal search reproduced its recorded single hit and fingerprint, and
the tracked Hetzenauer scan page 0875 was re-inspected visually
(hederam/hedera at Jon 4:6-10; marginal "et propter ricinum arefactum
irascens"; fingerprints match the bindings).

The 2026-07-24 record proved accurate except for four wording slips,
corrected 2026-07-25 in the rendered text (the original claim that these
passages were checked stands; the slips were transcription-level):

- De doctrina 2.15.22: Shaw prints "the Italian (Itala) is to be
  preferred"; the text had quoted "the Itala is to be preferred".
- Letter 27: the witness reads "that I had endeavored to correct
  passages in the gospels..."; the text had recast it as "endeavoring
  to correct...". Reworded to quote the witness's form.
- Prologus galeatus: the witness reads "whatever is outside of these is
  set aside among the apocrypha. Therefore, Wisdom, ... the First Book
  of the Maccabees (is) Hebrew"; the text had "is to be set aside",
  "Therefore Wisdom", and "is" without the translator's parentheses.
- Augustine, Letter 71.5: the witness reads "a very different rendering
  from that which had been of old familiar..."; the text had rearranged
  the span to "gave a rendering 'very different from that which...'".

Also normalized to the translators' conventions: "(but)" restored in the
Solomon-preface quotation and "(instead)" in the Daniel-preface
quotation (Edgecomb marks supplied words with parentheses). One witness
defect noted, not reproduced: the tertullian.org Solomon-preface
transcription reads "among the the canonical Scriptures" (doubled
article, an evident transcription slip); the quotation prints the single
article. A source-audit row and References entry for Jerome's Letter 71
(to Lucinius) were added 2026-07-25: the locus was cited in the text and
evidence map from the start but had no audit row. Houghton 2016 chapter
claims were not re-verified against the LOC PDF on 2026-07-25; they
stand as the 2026-07-24 record reports them, attributed as class K
throughout.
