# The Septuagint — evidence map

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Each principal claim of the publication is
mapped to its evidence layer, exact witness, confidence, and the
qualification that must travel with it. Layers: D = document/artifact;
A = ancient literary witness; T = later tradition/reception; M = official
ecclesial act; K = named modern reconstruction; S = project synthesis.
Witness URLs and access dates (all 2026-07-24) are in `source-audit.md`.
