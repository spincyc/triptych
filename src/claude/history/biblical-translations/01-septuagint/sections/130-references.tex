\section{References}

All web sources were accessed and checked on 2026-07-24. Citations in the
text use conventional English titles with the numbering of the
translations listed here. Sources are grouped by function.

\subsection*{Primary narrative and documentary witnesses}

\begin{itemize}
\item \work{Letter of Aristeas}, English translation from R.~H. Charles
(ed.), \textit{The Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament},

(\url{https://www.attalus.org/translate/aristeas1.html} and

28--51, 301--322 checked. Public domain.
\item Prologue to Sirach (Ecclesiasticus), in L.~C.~L. Brenton's English
translation of the Septuagint (1851), at ebible.org
(\url{https://ebible.org/eng-Brenton/SIR01.htm}); also Psalm 39:7 and Amos
9:11--12 (\url{https://ebible.org/eng-Brenton/PSA039.htm},
\url{https://ebible.org/eng-Brenton/AMO09.htm}). Public domain.
\item Philo, \work{On the Life of Moses} 2.25--44, C.~D. Yonge
translation (Bohn, 1855), at Early Christian Writings
(\url{https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/yonge/book25.html}). Public
domain.
\item Josephus, \work{Antiquities of the Jews} 12.2, William Whiston
translation (1737), at the University of Chicago Penelope transcription
(\url{https://penelope.uchicago.edu/josephus/ant-12.html}). Public
domain.
