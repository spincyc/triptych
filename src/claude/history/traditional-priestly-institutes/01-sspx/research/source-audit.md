## Source families searched, and consequential negative results

- **Founding and suppression documents.** No decree of erection (1970),
  decree of suppression (1975), letter of the Cardinals' Commission
  (1975), or decree of suspension (1976) was located in *Acta
  Apostolicae Sedis*, on vatican.va, or in the Society's own published
  documents. The 1974 and 1975 *Acta* volumes contain no occurrence of
  the founder's name.
- **1988 documents.** Neither the protocol of 5 May 1988, nor the
  information note of 16 June 1988 (cited in *Ecclesia Dei* fn. 1 as
  published in *L'Osservatore Romano* of 17 June 1988, pp. 1–2), nor the
  declaratory decree *Dominus Marcellus Lefebvre* of 1 July 1988 was
  located in any digital witness. All three are attested by other
  official acts.
- **Gagnon report (1987–1988).** Not published; not located. All claims
  about its findings are treated as unevidenced.
- **Doctrinal Preamble (2011).** Not published; not located. Both
  parties' characterizations are reported as characterizations.
- **February 2026.** The Society's communiqué of 19 February refers to
  "the statement published by the Holy See on 12 February"; no item
  concerning the Society appears in the Holy See Press Office bulletins
  for February 2026 (all listings for the month checked).
- **After the decree.** No response by the Holy See to the recourse of
  11 July 2026 appears in the bulletins for 2–25 July 2026.
- **Not searched (recorded as limits):** diocesan archives of Lausanne,
  Geneva and Fribourg and of Sion; the Apostolic Signatura's files;
  *L'Osservatore Romano* back issues; *Acta Synodalia* of Vatican II;
  the Spiritan generalate's records; the Society's printed periodicals
  and books; French and Italian scholarly monographs on the conflict.

## Claim-to-source rule

Every quotation in the publication appears in the tables above. Claims
resting on party sources say so in the text itself. Claims resting on a
bounded negative say so and name the corpus and date. No claim in the
publication rests on an unpublished document, and no unpublished
document's contents are characterized.
