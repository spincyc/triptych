# The Society of Saint Pius X — evidence map

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Each principal claim of the publication is
mapped to its evidence class, exact witness, confidence, and the
qualification that must travel with it. Classes: **A** = act printed in
*Acta Apostolicae Sedis*; **H** = official Holy See web text or press
bulletin; **L** = canonical text; **P** = document published by the
Society (interested party); **N** = bounded negative result; **S** =
project synthesis. Loci and access facts are in `source-audit.md`;
exact-bytes events are in `source-bindings.toml`.
