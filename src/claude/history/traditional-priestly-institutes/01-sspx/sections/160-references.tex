\section{References}

All sources were read on 2026-07-25. \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis}
volumes and fascicles were read in the Holy See's own scanned archive at
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/index_sp.htm}; individual volume
files follow the pattern
\texttt{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-\textit{vol}-\textit{year}-ocr.pdf},
and the 2009--2017 fascicles the pattern
\texttt{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/\textit{year}/}.
Sources are grouped by function.

\subsection*{Consequential negative results}

\begin{itemize}
\item No decree of erection (1970), decree of suppression (1975),
Cardinals' Commission letter (1975), decree of suspension (1976),
protocol (1988), information note of 16 June 1988, decree \work{Dominus
Marcellus Lefebvre} (1988), Gagnon report, or Doctrinal Preamble (2011)
was located in \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis}, on vatican.va, or in the
Holy See Press Office bulletin.
\item Literal searches of the text layer of the scanned \work{Acta}
volumes for 1971, 1974 and 1975 return no occurrence of Archbishop
Lefebvre's name; the 1976 volume contains only the consistory allocution;
the 1988 volume only the papal letter of 8 April and \work{Ecclesia Dei}.
\item No item concerning the Society appears in the Holy See Press Office
bulletins for February 2026, although the Society's communiqué of
19 February refers to a Holy See statement of 12 February.
\item No Holy See response to the recourse of 11 July 2026 appears in the
bulletins through 25 July 2026.
\end{itemize}
