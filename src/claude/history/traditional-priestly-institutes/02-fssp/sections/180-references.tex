\section{References}

All sources were read on 2026-07-25; the exact bytes registered in this
repository's source library for the documents published by the Fraternity
and for the Roman acts of 1999--2000 were re-fetched and hashed on
2026-07-26. \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} was
read in the Holy See's own scanned archive at
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/index_sp.htm}; the volume used
here is
\url{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-82-1990-ocr.pdf}.
Sources are grouped by function.

\subsection*{Acts printed in \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis}}

\begin{itemize}
\item Pontifical Commission \work{Ecclesia Dei}, \latin{Rescriptum ex
audientia Ss.mi quo Cardinali Praesidi Pontificiae Commissionis
``Ecclesia Dei'' speciales tribuuntur facultates, foras datur}, incipit
\latin{Quia peculiare munus}, audience of 18 October 1988, AAS 82 (1990)
533--534 (fascicle 5, 3 May 1990); Prot.\ N.~283/88; signed Augustinus
card.\ Mayer, president. Read in the scanned volume.
\item Gazette loci taken from the canonical literature and not
independently checked in the scanned volumes for this account:
\work{Ecclesia Dei}, AAS 80 (1988) 1495--1498; \work{Summorum
Pontificum}, AAS 99 (2007) 777--781; \work{Universae Ecclesiae}, AAS 103
(2011) 413--420; \work{Traditionis custodes}, AAS 113 (2021) 793--796. The
Holy See's whole-volume archive does not carry the 2011 or 2021 volumes,
and the 1988 and 2007 loci are established in the companion volume of this
series.
\item A lead not pursued: the canonical literature gives the decree
\latin{Dominus Marcellus Lefebvre} of 1 July 1988, which was never printed
in the gazette, a locus in the canonical periodical \work{Apollinaris} 61,
550--551. That periodical was not reached, and this account asserts
nothing about the decree.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Consequential negative results}

\begin{itemize}
\item No text of the protocol of 5 May 1988, of the decree of 10
September 1988, of the decree of erection of 18 October 1988, of the
constitutions of 1988 or 2003 beyond the published excerpt, of the
rescript of 24 March 1996, of the responses of 3 July 1999, of the
Commission's letter of 13 July 1999, of the letter of 29 June 2000, of the
decree of 12 October 2000, or of the papal decree of 11 February 2022 was
located in \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis}, on vatican.va, or in the Holy
See Press Office bulletin.
\item \work{Notitiae} 35 (1999) 307--309, the printed locus of the 1999
responses, and \work{Archiv für katholisches Kirchenrecht} 157, the
printed locus of the erection decree's Latin, were not obtained; both are
used only as cited by the canonical literature, which quotes the Latin of
the second.
\item John Paul II's address of 26 October 1998 was not located on
vatican.va or in \work{Acta Apostolicae Sedis}; it is used only as quoted
in French by the canonical literature from \work{L'Osservatore Romano}.
\item No report, decree or communiqué concluding the apostolic visitation
opened in September 2024 was located through 25 July 2026, in either the
Holy See's channels or the Fraternity's.
\item The Fraternity's English, French and German news feeds carry no
item later than 16 February 2026, and therefore no communiqué on the
episcopal consecrations of 1 July 2026 or on the acts of 2 July 2026.
\item Not searched, and recorded as limits: the diocesan archives of
Augsburg, Scranton and Lincoln; the archives of the Dicastery for
Institutes of Consecrated Life and Societies of Apostolic Life; the
Fraternity's \work{Vademecum}, \work{Ordo administrativus} and internal
directories; \work{Notitiae}, \work{Communicationes},
\work{Apollinaris} and \work{Archiv für katholisches Kirchenrecht} in
print; \work{La Documentation Catholique} 85 (1988); Philippe Levillain,
\work{Rome n'est plus dans Rome} (2010); and the French, German and
Italian monographic literature on the \work{Ecclesia Dei} communities.
\end{itemize}
