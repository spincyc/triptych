## Source families searched, and consequential negative results

- **The founding acts.** No text of the protocol of 5 May 1988, of the
  decree of 10 September 1988, of the decree of erection of 18 October
  1988, or of the constitutions of 1988 was located in *Acta Apostolicae
  Sedis*, on vatican.va, or in the Holy See Press Office bulletin. All
  are available only from the beneficiary, and their originals only as
  quoted in the canonical literature.
- **The 1999–2000 acts.** Neither the responses of 3 July 1999, nor the
  Commission's letter of 13 July 1999, nor the letter of 29 June 2000,
  nor the decree of 12 October 2000 was located in any Holy See channel.
- **The 2022 decree.** No publication by the Holy See was located in
  *Acta Apostolicae Sedis*, on vatican.va, or in the press bulletin.
- **The 1998 papal address.** Not located on vatican.va; the Holy See's
  French index of John Paul II's October 1998 speeches was checked and
  the address does not appear at the address this account tried. It is
  used only as quoted in French by the canonical literature from
  *L'Osservatore Romano* (Italian edition), n. 248, p. 8.
- **The visitations.** No Roman act concerning the 2014 visitation was
  located. The Dicastery's statement of 30 September 2024 was not located
  in the press bulletin or on a Holy See site. No report, decree or
  communiqué concluding the visitation opened in 2024 was located through
  25 July 2026.
- **After February 2026.** The Fraternity's English, French and German
  news feeds carry no item later than 16 February 2026; the general house
  published no communiqué on the episcopal consecrations of 1 July 2026
  or on the acts of 2 July 2026. Silence in a published feed is not
  silence *tout court*, and the account says so.
- **A reproducibility limit.** The pages on fssp.org emit a fresh random
  element identifier in the site's search widget on every request, so the
  SHA-256 digests recorded for those six artifacts identify the exact
  responses acquired on 2026-07-26 and will not be reproduced by a later
  fetch. The document text they carry is stable, and the pages on
  fssp.com, unavox.it and amdg.asso.fr were fetched twice and were
  byte-identical.
- **Not searched, recorded as limits:** the diocesan archives of Augsburg,
  Scranton and Lincoln; the archives of the Dicastery for Institutes of
  Consecrated Life and Societies of Apostolic Life; the Fraternity's
  *Vademecum*, *Ordo administrativus* and directories; *Notitiae*,
  *Communicationes*, *Apollinaris* and *Archiv für katholisches
  Kirchenrecht* in print; *La Documentation Catholique* 85 (1988);
  Levillain, *Rome n'est plus dans Rome* (2010); and the monographic
  literature in French, German and Italian.

## Claim-to-source rule

Every quotation in the publication appears in the tables above. Claims
resting on party sources say so in the text itself. Claims resting on a
bounded negative say so and name the corpus and date. No claim in the
publication rests on an unpublished document whose contents are unknown,
and no unpublished document's contents are characterized.
