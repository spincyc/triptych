# The Priestly Fraternity of Saint Peter — evidence map

Provider: Anthropic Claude. Each principal claim of the publication is
mapped to its evidence class, exact witness, confidence, and the
qualification that must travel with it. Classes: **A** = act printed in
*Acta Apostolicae Sedis*; **H** = official Holy See web text or press
bulletin; **L** = canonical text; **P** = document published by the
Priestly Fraternity of Saint Peter or one of its members; **Q** = Roman
act read only in an unofficial rendering hosted by an interested third
party; **S** = peer-reviewed canonical scholarship; **N** = bounded
negative result; **J** = project synthesis. Loci and access facts are in
`source-audit.md`; exact-bytes events are in `source-bindings.toml`.
