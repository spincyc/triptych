## Bounded negative results

1. **No authentic interpretation of cc. 277 or 288.** The dicastery's collection
   page as delivered on 2026-07-25 carries interpretations of cc. 87, 119, 127,
   230, 299, 455, 502, 509, 684, 700, 767, 830, 910, 917, 951, 964, 1103, 1263,
   1367, 1398, 1673, 1686, 1737, plus a separately posted interpretation of
   c. 1041 nn. 4–5. Method: canon-number extraction from the exact fetched
   response. Ceiling: one page, one date; it cannot exclude an act promulgated
   elsewhere or later. No source-library search receipt exists because the
   artifact is restricted and unretained.
2. **The silence of *Querida Amazonia*.** Case-insensitive literal searches over
   the text extracted from the exact fetched official English delivery returned
   zero occurrences of "celibacy", "celibate", "viri probati", "married men",
   "ordain", and "ordination"; the only cognate is "ordained ministers" at
   n. 93. Ceiling: one language of one delivery; correctable.
3. **No current official online enumeration of the Churches *sui iuris*.** The
   Dicastery for the Eastern Churches' pages, its own site, and its S.I.C.O.
   review (last online edition 2013) were checked; none enumerates the Churches
   or gives a total. The official repertories — *Oriente cattolico* and the
   *Annuario Pontificio* — are printed works. The article's count of twenty-four
   is a reconstruction from the 2010 Press Office sheet plus two verified 2015
   acts and is marked as such.
4. **Paul VI's letter of 10 October 1965.** Not published in *AAS* 57 (1965),
   whose whole volume was searched; not on vatican.va; the Holy See's index of
   his 1965 letters lists only *Laeto animo* of 9 November 1965 to the same
   addressee. Attested only in the note to *Sacerdotalis caelibatus* n. 2, which
   fixes the date at 10 October and the reading in the general congregation at
   the following day. No wording is quoted.
5. **CDW circular letter Prot. n. 26397 of 6 June 1997.** Not located at any
   Holy See delivery. Its content is reported only as the 1998 Basic Norms
   (note 44) and Directory (note 226) state it.
