## Independent material audit discovery

Section 40 calls Christ's oblation “the one offering adequate to the
creature's debt,” says it was made “from the creature's side,” and describes
revelation as supplying “an offering worthy of the case.” The scope record
echoes this as sacrifice's “adequate offering.”

Augustine, *City of God* X.6, and CCC 2100 establish Christ's unique saving
oblation and the Church's dependent participation in it. They do not establish
that the Cross pays a creaturely creation-debt or that it is an offering made
“from the creature's side.” The current wording also creates avoidable
person/nature ambiguity by foregrounding a created human will and life without
naming the divine acting subject in the main sentence.

The ignored modified candidate instead states that the incarnate Son freely
offered his human life to the Father for salvation and that creatures are
included in his prior oblation. If the correction is separately authorized,
the matching language in `research/scope.md`, the audit and binding context,
and the terminal scope summary requires coherent review before rebuild and
