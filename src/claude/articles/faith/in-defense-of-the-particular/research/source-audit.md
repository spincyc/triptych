# Source Audit — In Defense of the Particular (Claude edition)

Checks below were performed on 2026-07-24 (first edition) and 2026-07-25 (deepened edition) against the identified online witnesses. Public English translations are working study witnesses; consequential claims are governed by the identified work and exact locus, not by any claim that an online transcription is critical. The endnotes carry loci, witnesses, and claim-local qualifications; the main argument remains complete without them.

The 2026-07-25 deepening added: the objection genealogy at depth (Contra Celsum IV.3–7, 14, 99; V.25–26, 41–42; VI.78–79; Spinoza's TTP ch. 3 read whole; the Lessing occasion and structure, first at a secondary ceiling and, after the second verification pass, from the Lachmann–Muncker critical edition; a new Kant subsection from Semple's 1838 translation), the individuation and person material (ST I.8.1; I.29.1, 3; I.50.4; De ente et essentia on materia signata; Scotus reported via SEP), the expanded scriptural economy (Gen 17, 25, 28; Deut 7 in context; Deut 10:14–19; Isa 49:1–7; Amos 9:7–8; Matt 1; Luke 3; Eph 4:4–6; Mark 6:3–5), the expanded sacramental section (ST III.60.2, 6; III.61.1; III.75.1; III.76.3; CCC 1145–1152), the new contemporary-pluralism section (Hick reported via named SEP witness; Dominus Iesus quoted exactly), and the objections synopsis table.

## Consequential negative results and verification limits

- **Lessing 1777 first printing not directly inspected; secondary ceiling since lifted to a critical edition.** Attempts on 2026-07-24: zeno.org unreachable (connection refused); projekt-gutenberg.org page for the essay not found; de.wikisource page not found; Deutsches Textarchiv full-text search for "garstige breite Graben" returned zero hits (the essay is not in that corpus); an MDZ (digitale-sammlungen.de) search errored server-side; the USC Scalar Fragmentenstreit record confirmed the 1777 Braunschweig printing's identity but reproduces no text. Renewed attempts on 2026-07-25 (first pass): zeno.org still unreachable; de.wikisource and projekt-gutenberg.org URLs 404; textlog.de returned its index shell without the essay; gutenberg.spiegel.de returned a page not containing the essay text; the DTA search again returned zero hits. Re-tested on 2026-07-25 in the second verification pass: zeno.org 000, de.wikisource 404, projekt-gutenberg.org 404, textlog.de 200 but serving a 1.6 KB index shell carrying none of the essay's wording. **Result of the second pass:** an archive.org advanced-search query for Lessing's collected works surfaced the Lachmann–Muncker *Sämtliche Schriften*, 3rd ed., vol. 13 (Stuttgart, 1897), item `11817266bsb`, whose full OCR text carries the essay at pp. 5–8 together with Muncker's editorial note. The article now quotes that edition, and the German Wikipedia witness has been removed from the article entirely. The residual limit is only that the 1777 Brunswick first printing — the edition's own stated basis for textual criticism — was not inspected. No public-domain English translation was identified; Chadwick's 1956 translation was deliberately not used or reproduced. A sibling item (`smtlicheschrif13lessuoft`) was checked and found not to carry the essay; it is not relied on.
- **Kant's German not collated.** The deepening quotes Semple's 1838 public-domain translation from the Internet Archive scan (item religionwithin00kantuoft; title page and imprint read in the scan's front matter). No German edition was collated; the ceiling is stated in the Notes and appendix, and no wording-critical claim about Kant finer than the quoted argumentative structure is made. Kant's comparative characterizations of other religions in book IV were read, deliberately not reproduced, and expressly disavowed in a Note.
- **Hick and Scotus engaged only at named secondary witnesses.** No Hick text and no Scotus text was collated; both SEP entries' authorship and revision blocks were read and recorded; every claim about either position is bounded by what the named entry supports, and the bounds are stated in the Notes.
- **De ente et essentia English.** No public-domain English translation was identified; the two clauses used are given in the article's own labeled working translations against the Corpus Thomisticum Latin, and the chapter-numbering divergence between the common division and the Corpus Thomisticum presentation is recorded in the Note.
- **ST I.50.4 lies outside the registered Leonine vol. 4 coverage** (qq. 1–49); it was checked at the English Dominican translation only and is identified here rather than bound.
- **No claim of full-work examination** is made for any patristic or Thomistic source; loci were checked individually as listed.
- The New Advent transcription of De incarnatione truncated (in the fetched text) before §41; sections 41–43 were verified instead in the full-text NPNF copy identified above. Wording between the two witnesses was consistent for the sections both carried (8, 17). **Amended 2026-07-25 (second verification pass):** the New Advent page was retrieved whole (153 KB of extracted text) and does carry §§41–43; all five quoted clauses from those sections were matched verbatim there. The earlier truncation was an artifact of the fetching tool, not of the transcription. Both witnesses now stand behind the quotation, and the reference list retains both.
- **Quotation-mechanics conventions applied throughout, confirmed at re-verification.** Where a source sentence is spliced into one of the article's own sentences, a sentence-initial capital is lowered (CCC 1147 "Light and darkness" → "light and darkness"; Dominus Iesus 10 "It is likewise" → "it is likewise"; Dominus Iesus 6 "The truth about God" → "the truth about God"; Deut 7:8 "But because" → "but because"); Douay–Rheims house capitals are normalized as already recorded; Vatican footnote markers inside quoted sentences are dropped (Gaudium et spes 22, "acted by human choice(23) and loved").
