## Canon 2335 verification

The exact Latin of canon 2335 was verified visually against the retained page
image of leaf 693 (printed page 635) of the 1918 Vatican Polyglot printing
(Internet Archive item `codexiuriscanoni00cath`, University of Illinois
exemplar), fetched through the archive's IIIF service and hashed. The same
page carries Gasparri's fontes footnote 4 to the canon, from which the essay
takes the dates: In eminenti, 28 apr. 1738; Providas, 18 mart. 1751; Ecclesiam,
13 sept. 1821; Quo graviora, 13 mart. 1825; Qui pluribus (9 nov., year on the
following page, not relied on). The OCR discovery text agrees with the visual
reading. Ceiling: one printing was examined; no collation against AAS 9 (1917)
was performed. The 1917 Code's promulgation (Providentissima Mater Ecclesia,
27 May 1917) and force date (19 May 1918) are carried as common documentary
record consistent with the printing's title matter.
