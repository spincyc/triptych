# Heaven — Register Revision, 26 October 2026

## Result and boundaries

The maintainer asked that the register guidance of 21 October 2026
(`guidance/editorial.md`, "Say what the texts say, plainly", with "Plain is
not flat") and the rule "Speaking from within the tradition" be applied to
this essay, which was rewritten on 16 October before that guidance existed.
Only the essay's own prose in the body sections `00`–`70` was revised;
`80-in-paradisum.tex` holds only the received antiphon and is unchanged.

Four kinds of defect were removed.

- **Paragraph-closing maxims, paradoxes and restatements.** Examples: "We do
  not contain heaven. Heaven contains us." after Aquinas had just said so;
  "The saints do not leave the vision to help us. They help us from inside
  it." after Thérèse's own words; "What Mary is, the Church will be."; "The
  family and the saints are one company."; "Whatever else eternity is, it is
  not a completed inspection." Each was deleted where the paragraph already
  said what it needed to.
- **Asserted significance.** "That reading holds, in two sentences, nearly
  everything the Church knows about heaven" became "In it John says what
  Christians are now, and why they cannot yet know what they will be";
  "Everything else in the vision is said for the sake of that line" became
  "Several of the subtractions are explained by that presence", followed by
  the reasons the Apocalypse itself gives; Augustine's opening sentence is no
  longer one "the Church has made her own, because every Christian finds his
  own heart described in it".
- **Antitheses against positions nobody holds, and staged objections.** "The
  restlessness is not a defect in us"; "is not a cosmetic laid on from
  outside"; "Heaven is not the flowering of a power we were born with. It is
  grace brought to its end"; "The last of the last things, then, is not a
  thing"; "The Church does not explain heaven over a Christian's body". Each
  now states its positive claim. The one antithesis kept on purpose is
  Thomas's own: merit is "not credit laid up to be exchanged at the end for a
  better seat", because his explanation of merit by charity answers that
  misunderstanding.
- **Questions answered in the next breath, emphatic fragments, stress words.**
  "What, then, measures the light? The Council's word is merit" became "The
  Council names merit as the measure"; "We are the bat" became "The created
  mind stands to God as the bat to the sun"; "They see." and "a wonder" went;
  the four-fragment gloss on Augustine's "rest and see, see and love, love and
  praise" became sentences that follow the order of his verbs.

The argument was kept moving by replacing each cut connector with the actual
reason one text bears on the next. The Apocalypse's subtractions now lead to
Alphonsus because speaking by subtraction keeps the reader from furnishing
heaven with what he already knows. Newman's sermon is introduced as the reason
for the condition stated by Matthew, Hebrews and the Apocalypse. The
"Seeing without exhausting" subsection opens on what Thomas adds. Where the
essay draws its own inference it says so in ordinary words ("On his
argument", "By this account", "On either view"), without moving the inference
onto a Father. The concrete scenes (Moses in the rock, Ostia, the horse and
the hay, the thimble and the tumbler, Philip Neri's cap, the bier), the
teaching on the light of glory, the degrees of glory, the body, the communion
of saints and the resolution, and the closing movement to *In paradisum* are
unchanged in substance.

## Repairs after cold review

An independent cold review found no fidelity defect. It asked for four
register repairs, and all four were made.

- **Restatement closers (blocking).** In §00 the paraphrase of 1 John 3:2
  ("What we shall be depends on what we shall see") was cut, and the two
  sentences about why the likeness cannot be described became one. In §40 the
  paragraph after Thomas's block quotation was cut, so the section moves
  straight from his conclusion to Bernard. In §50 the closing "made perfect
  and made permanent" sentence was replaced by the step it skipped: since
  Thomas teaches that the causes of virtuous love remain in the blessed, a
  friendship ordered to God now is one that heaven will keep. The essay's
  last paragraph (§70) no longer re-lists the antiphon's petitions, no longer
  says that it "describes nothing of heaven", and no longer ends on "what
  they too were made for". It now concludes that the holy city the Church
  asks for her dead is the one in which, the Apocalypse says, the blessed
  shall see the face of God, and that those who follow the bier will one day
  be carried along the same road under the same prayer.
- **Repeated connectives (advisory).** In §10 the run of "Thomas then asks",
  "Thomas adds" and "Thomas adds" was replaced by the content of each step:
  if the seeing must be given, something must change on the creature's side;
  and the blessed never comprehend God, for the two reasons Thomas gives
  together after the quotation: God is infinitely knowable, and the created
  light of glory is finite.
- **Unconnected quotation (advisory).** In §20 Ecclus 24:29 is now introduced
  as the hunger that goes on while it is fed, which both saints describe and
  which Scripture already has. The link is the essay's own.
- **Idle credential (advisory).** In §50 "a bishop and a martyr" was cut from
  the Cyprian sentence.
