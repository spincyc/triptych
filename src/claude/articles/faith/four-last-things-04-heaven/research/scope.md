## Governing purpose

Part IV, the closing volume, of the Claude series *The Four Last Things*,
rewritten in place under the maintainer's decisions of 16 October 2026
(`four-last-things-shared/cold-review-2026-10-16.md`, "Decisions taken"). It is Catholic spiritual reading for the close of the
liturgical year and the annual preparation for death: the hope of the vision of
God, carried by Scripture, the liturgy, the Fathers, the Doctors and the saints,
moving from teaching to desire, resolution and received prayer. It is written
from inside the faith to a Catholic reader (`guidance/editorial.md`, "Speaking
from within the tradition"; `guidance/articles.md`, "Faith and theology").

Governing frame: "Heaven is a Person, seen" (Apoc 22:4; 1 John 3:2), opened in
§1 and closed in §8, before the closing antiphon *In paradisum*.

## Included

- The vision of God: Ex 33:20 and 1 Tim 6:16 against Matt 5:8 and 1 Cor 13:12;
  Irenaeus IV.20.5–7; *Benedictus Deus* read phrase by phrase in brief (vision
  clauses only); Vienne, *Ad nostrum* (1312), DS 895, condemning the denial that the soul needs the light of glory; Florence DS 1305 (*sicuti est*; *pro meritorum diversitate*);
  Aquinas I q.12 aa.1, 4–7 (bat and sun, light of glory as *medium quo*,
  non-comprehension); CCC 1023, 1028.
- The restless heart: *Confessions* I.1, IX.10.23–25; Aquinas *In Symbolum*
  a.12 (Latin); II-II q.28 a.3; Gregory of Nyssa's enlarging vessel; Bernard
  XI; Alphonsus XXIX; *Spe salvi* 12; *De civ.* XXII.30.
- Degrees of glory: 1 Cor 15:41; Florence (defined measure: merits, V7); Trent
  VI can. 32; Aquinas I q.12 a.6 (charity as his explanation); Augustine
  XXII.30; Thérèse's thimble; Dante *Paradiso* III; Gregory *Dialogues* IV.33.
- The glorified body: Lateran IV DS 801; 1 Cor 15; Phil 3:21; Assumption
  (MD 44; *Credo* 15); I-II q.4 a.5; Bernard XI; the four gifts as the schools'
  teaching (III q.28 a.2 ad 3; Supplement qq.82–85); Augustine's caution once
  (XXII.30).
- Communion and those we love: CCC 1024, 1045, 1053; Cyprian *De mort.* 26;
  Gregory *Dial.* IV.33; Aquinas *In Symb.* a.12 and II-II q.26 a.13;
  Alphonsus; Thérèse; the saints' prayer for us as defined by Trent session XXV
  (DS 1821) and the Tridentine Profession (DS 1867).
- Holiness for heaven: Heb 12:14; Apoc 21:27; CCC 1054; Newman PPS I.1; de
  Sales I.16; Alphonsus XXIX with his affection.
- Season: All Saints (both Missals), Last Sunday after Pentecost and Advent I
  (1962), Year A 33rd Sunday and Christ the King (Postconciliar, labelled).
- Close: *In paradisum*, Rituale 1872 Latin with Lasance 1945 English; it is
  described as sung while the body is carried to the grave (Rituale pp. 141–143).
- Attribution: the saying *toti gaudentes intrabunt in gaudium*, which Aquinas
  (*In Symbolum* a.12) gives as Augustine's, is identified as Anselm's
  (*Proslogion* 26).

## Authority boundary

Each teaching is stated at its degree in one clause; Appendix A grades them
(defined; Church teaching; common teaching of the Fathers and Doctors;
theological opinion; pious tradition). One open question is named once with
its positions (Nyssa's unending enlargement; Aquinas's appointed term). The
reunion with those we love is affirmed on its patristic warrant (Cyprian,
Gregory, Aquinas) and graded as common teaching.
