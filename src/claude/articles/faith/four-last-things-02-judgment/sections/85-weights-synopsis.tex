\section{Synopsis of Claims by Weight}

The article classifies its consequential claims by the weight of the authority behind them: definitive teaching; authoritative but non-definitive teaching; Scripture and its patristic reading; common or probable school theology; and project synthesis. The table gathers that classification in one place so that a reader can see at a glance which sentences carry the Church's authority, which carry a school's, and which carry only this essay's. Nothing here adds to the argument; where the table and the body differ, the body and its notes govern. The fourth column names what the cited witness leaves open, not what the article doubts.
