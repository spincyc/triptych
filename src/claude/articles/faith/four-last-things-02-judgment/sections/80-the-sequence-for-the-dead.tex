\section{The Sequence of the Mass for the Dead}\label{sec:sequence}

The strophes of the \latin{Dies irae} in which the singer prays to the Judge, in the Latin of the Roman Missal and the English of W.~J. Irons.\endnote{Latin: \emph{Missale Romanum} (Tournai: Desclée, Lefebvre et Soc., 1889), \emph{Missae pro Defunctis}, Sequence, p.~92*, the seventh strophe to the end; stress accents omitted. English: W.~J. Irons, ``Day of Wrath! O day of mourning,'' stanzas 4--10, \emph{Hymns Ancient and Modern}, complete edition (London: printed for the Proprietors by William Clowes and Sons, n.d., catalogued 1890), hymn 398, pp.~307--309. Both texts are in the public domain.}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Quid sum miser tunc dicturus?\\
\quad Quem patronum rogaturus,\\
\quad Cum vix justus sit securus?\\
Rex tremendae majestatis,\\
\quad Qui salvandos salvas gratis,\\
\quad Salva me, fons pietatis.}

\smallskip
What shall I, frail man, be pleading,\\
Who for me be interceding,\\
When the just are mercy needing?\\
King of Majesty tremendous,\\
Who dost free salvation send us,\\
Fount of pity, then befriend us!
\end{quote}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Recordare Jesu pie,\\
\quad Quod sum causa tuae viae,\\
\quad Ne me perdas illa die.\\
Quaerens me, sedisti lassus:\\
\quad Redemisti, crucem passus:\\
\quad Tantus labor non sit cassus.}

\smallskip
Think, good Jesu, my salvation\\
Caused Thy wondrous Incarnation;\\
Leave me not to reprobation.\\
Faint and weary Thou hast sought me,\\
On the Cross of suffering bought me;\\
Shall such grace be vainly brought me?
\end{quote}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Juste judex ultionis,\\
\quad Donum fac remissionis\\
\quad Ante diem rationis.\\
Ingemisco tamquam reus:\\
\quad Culpa rubet vultus meus:\\
\quad Supplicanti parce Deus.}

\smallskip
Righteous Judge! for sin's pollution\\
Grant Thy gift of absolution,\\
Ere that day of retribution.\\
Guilty, now I pour my moaning,\\
All my shame with anguish owning;\\
Spare, O God, Thy suppliant groaning.
\end{quote}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Qui Mariam absolvisti,\\
\quad Et latronem exaudisti,\\
\quad Mihi quoque spem dedisti.\\
Preces meae non sunt dignae:\\
\quad Sed tu bonus fac benigne,\\
\quad Ne perenni cremer igne.}

\smallskip
Thou the sinful woman savedst;\\
Thou the dying thief forgavest;\\
And to me a hope vouchsafest.\\
Worthless are my prayers and sighing;\\
Yet, good Lord, in grace complying,\\
Rescue me from fires undying.
\end{quote}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Inter oves locum praesta,\\
\quad Et ab haedis me sequestra,\\
\quad Statuens in parte dextra.\\
Confutatis maledictis,\\
\quad Flammis acribus addictis,\\
\quad Voca me cum benedictis.}

\smallskip
With Thy favour'd sheep O place me,\\
Nor among the goats abase me,\\
But to Thy right hand upraise me.\\
While the wicked are confounded,\\
Doom'd to flames of woe unbounded,\\
Call me with Thy Saints surrounded.
\end{quote}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Oro supplex et acclinis,\\
\quad Cor contritum quasi cinis:\\
\quad Gere curam mei finis.}

\smallskip
Low I kneel, with heart submission,\\
See, like ashes, my contrition;\\
Help me in my last condition.
\end{quote}

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\begin{quote}
\latin{Lacrimosa dies illa,\\
\quad Qua resurget ex favilla\\
\quad Judicandus homo reus.\\
Huic ergo parce Deus:\\
\quad Pie Jesu Domine,\\
\quad Dona eis requiem. Amen.}

\smallskip
Ah! that day of tears and mourning!\\
From the dust of earth returning\\
Man for judgment must prepare him:\\
Spare, O God, in mercy spare him!\\
Lord, all-pitying, Jesu Blest,\\
Grant them Thine eternal rest.\\
Amen.
\end{quote}
