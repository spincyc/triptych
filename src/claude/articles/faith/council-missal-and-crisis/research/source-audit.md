(registered artifact `secondary-digital-pdf-0b458944`) was re-downloaded from

constitution's Latin text, following the existing pattern of the 2000 decree

not the printed altar book; claims are bounded to the inspected pages.

The 2008 reimpressio emendata is characterized only from the registered
Notitiae records (notice p. 367; variationes pp. 368–387), whose central

essay relies on that checked record and did not re-inspect the Notitiae scan.
