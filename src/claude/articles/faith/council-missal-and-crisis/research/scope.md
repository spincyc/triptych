- **Intended reader:** a serious general reader, Catholic or not, able to follow documentary argument.
- **Governing question:** how are the Second Vatican Council, the liturgical reform culminating in the Missal of Paul VI, and the postconciliar crisis narrative historically, juridically, and theologically related — and where must they be kept distinct?
- **Thesis (argued in epistemic order, not announced):** the Council mandated real but bounded liturgical reform under elastic standards whose application it assigned to the Apostolic See; the postconciliar Missal is the Apostolic See's legitimate determination of that mandate, structurally faithful to its letter and debatable in manner and accompaniments where the mandate was elastic; the measured crisis is real and severe in the West but will not carry a monocausal liturgical explanation in either direction; Benedict XVI's 2005 hermeneutical fork disciplined rather than ended the argument; the traditionalist critique is textually serious, partially conceded, and self-refuting only in its absolutized schismatic form; and the 1984–2026 discipline records a prudential disagreement between papal acts that the Church has governed without adjudicating, which the essay also declines to adjudicate.

## Argument order

1. The coincidence: the two undisputed facts, the three incompatible causal readings, and the resolve to read texts and numbers exactly (CARA attendance and sisters series quoted with ceilings).
2. What the Council was called to do: the 1959 announcement; Gaudet Mater Ecclesia's three load-bearing sentences (prophets of misfortune; deposit vs. mode with eodem sensu; medicine of mercy), each quoted in Latin with labeled glosses and its own context preserved; the authority question answered by the 6 March 1964 Doctrinal Commission declaration, Paul VI's closing address, and the 12 January 1966 audience (supreme ordinary magisterium).
3. What the constitution says: SC 14/48 (participation), 21 (changeable elements), 22 (authority), 23 (elastic limits), 4 (equal dignity), the two-sided particulars (36, 54, 116 vs. 34, 50, 51, 55, 57), and two bounded negative claims (no versus populum, no Communion in the hand, no all-vernacular directive; elastic clauses require a judge).
4. From constitution to Missal: Consilium chronology as background; Missale Romanum (1969) quoted exactly (three added Canons, common words of consecration, lectionary, one-prayer-in-many-languages, effective date); the 1970 promulgation decree in Latin; edition history 1970/1971/1975/2002/2008 from the registered witnesses; the mandated/authorized/added sorting and its consequences.
5. The crisis measured: CARA U.S. and worldwide series with exact values and explicit ceilings (definitions, instruments, 1970 start, pandemic column, two population versions); Pew 2019 vs CARA/McGrath 2022 as an instrument lesson; the causal obstacles (no counterfactual, confounding, internal timing) and the bounded conclusion that no monocausal claim survives.
6. The hermeneutical fork: the 2005 address quoted at length; the textual precision that its term is "hermeneutic of reform" with "combination of continuity and discontinuity at different levels" (bounded negative claim on "hermeneutic of continuity"); its charter in the 1962/1965 addresses; its liturgical application in the 2007 letter ("no rupture"; "held as sacred"); its authority level and its reception in Francis's 2021 letter; what the fork does and does not settle.
7. The critique and its limits: the critique at full documentary strength; authority's concessions (2007 letter; "never abrogated"; 1984/1988; Francis on abuses); three limits (textual ranking; Ecclesia Dei's diagnosis of an incomplete and contradictory notion of Tradition; the empirical failure of the causal story); the reform-versus-manner resting point; the SSPX arc 1970–1988–2009–2026 ending in the DDF decree and note of 2 July 2026.
8. The discipline of memory: the five stations (1984 indult; Ecclesia Dei; Summorum Pontificum; Traditionis custodes with its letter; Responsa 2021 and rescript 2023) with exact operative quotations; the dated currentness check (no later universal act as of 2026-07-25; Leo XIV practice reports treated as leads only); the located contradiction between the 2007 and 2021 frameworks as prudential, not doctrinal; the closing three-sentence synthesis.
