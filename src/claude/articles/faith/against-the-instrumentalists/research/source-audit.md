## Consequential negative results and boundaries

- No claim that the collapse of the verifiability criterion establishes the truth or meaningfulness of any metaphysical assertion.
- No demonstration of God's existence is executed or claimed; the five ways are named and their objections quoted as evidence of method only.
- No gaps-based argument is used; the effects section expressly distinguishes contingency reasoning from appeals to unexplained phenomena.
- No genuinely readable public witness of Hempel 1950/1965 was found on either research day (Internet Archive holdings are access-restricted; journal hosting is paywalled); the Hempel material therefore remains at the reported ceiling, which is stated precisely in the Notes: structure and conclusion of the survey only, via the named SEP witness, corroborated by Ayer's printed concessions; no finer Hempel claims are made.
- Popper's *Logic of Scientific Discovery* full text was found only in access-restricted lending scans; the Popper subsection therefore quotes the 1963 essay at a transcription ceiling and keeps *Logik der Forschung* bibliographic.
- Ayer's later interview remarks disparaging the book were deliberately not used; only his printed 1946 concessions are relied on.
- Berlin 1939, Church 1949, and Carnap's *Logical Syntax* are cited bibliographically and labeled not collated.
- The Guardian's report of Hawking's 2011 Google Zeitgeist remarks was not reachable from the research environment and is not used; the Grand Design sentence rests on the named study-page witness and the book's public reception, with the book itself uncollated and the ceiling stated.
- The Atlantic's 2012 Krauss interview was not reachable; only Krauss's own Scientific American text is quoted.
- Rosenberg's "On the Human" essay (2009) returned 404 at the original host and its archive copy was not reachable; the Rosenberg exhibit rests on the named review witness, ceiling stated.
- No claim is made that any contemporary exhibit author defends Ayer's semantic criterion; the residue section grades the exhibits and engages the strongest reading of each.
- The Dei Filius canon and ch. 4 English, and all manifesto English, are labeled working glosses; the Latin and German govern.
- Romans 1 is not presented as Paul doing philosophical method; the culpability context and received-reading status are stated.
- Wisdom's deuterocanonical status is noted for non-Catholic readers.
- No claim of ecclesiastical approval, independent review, or immunity of Church history from criticism is made.
