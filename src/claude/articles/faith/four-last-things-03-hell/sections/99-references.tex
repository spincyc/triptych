{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]

\item Irenaeus of Lyons, \emph{Adversus haereses} V.27.2 (separation from God is death; the loss of eternal goods is eternal; the blindness image); trans.\ Alexander Roberts and William H. The OCR is a locator aid, not textual control.
\item Origen, \emph{De principiis} I.6.1--2 (the end like the beginning; all recalled to one end; the author's own disclaimer of genre) and II.10.4--6 (the sinner kindles his own fire; the medicinal reading); trans.\ Frederick Crombie, \emph{Ante-Nicene Fathers}, vol.~4 (Buffalo, 1885); public domain; read 2026-07-25 at \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04121.htm}{New Advent (book I)} and \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/04122.htm}{New Advent (book II)}. The treatise survives complete only in Rufinus's Latin.
\item Gregory of Nyssa, \emph{Oratio catechetica magna} (\emph{The Great Catechism}) XXVI (the restoration of those now lying in sin; the healing of the introducer of evil); trans.\ William Moore and Henry Austin Wilson, \emph{Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers}, 2nd series, vol.~5 (New York, 1893); public domain; read 2026-07-25 at \sourceurl{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf205.xi.ii.xxviii.html}{ccel.org}. \item John Chrysostom, \emph{Homilies on the Gospel of Saint Matthew} 23, section 9 (two punishments in one judgment; ten thousand hells); trans.\ George Prevost, revised M.~B. Riddle, \emph{Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers}, 1st series, vol.~10 (New York, 1888); public domain; read 2026-07-25 at \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200123.htm}{New Advent}. Section divisions are editorial; the Greek was not consulted.
\item Augustine of Hippo, \emph{De civitate Dei} XXI.9 (the worm and the fire; ``let each man choose which he will''), XXI.11 (duration of penalty not proportioned to duration of offence), XXI.12 (the greater part left under punishment), XXI.17 (the tender-hearted Christians; Origen more indulgent), XXI.23 (the correlativity of Matthew 25:46); trans.\ Marcus Dods, \emph{The City of God}, vol.~II (Edinburgh: T.\,\&\,T.
