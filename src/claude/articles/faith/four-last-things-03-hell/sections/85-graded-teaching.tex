\section{Teaching and Its Grades}

\begin{threestable}{Teaching}{Grade}{Principal witnesses}
Hell exists and is entered at death & Defined & Lyons II, DS 858; \latin{Benedictus Deus}, DS 1002; Florence, DS 1306; CCC 1033, 1035\\
Its punishment is eternal & Defined & \latin{Quicumque}, DS 76; anathemas against Origen (543), can.~9, DS 411; Lateran IV, DS 801; Paul VI, \emph{Credo} 12; CCC 1035\\
The punishments are unequal & Defined & Lyons II, DS 858; Florence, DS 1306; Gregory, \emph{Dialogues} IV.43\\
The damned rise with their own bodies & Defined & \latin{Quicumque}; Lateran IV, DS 801; Cyprian, \emph{To Demetrianus} 24; Cyril, \emph{Catecheses} 18.19\\
No one is predestined to hell & Defined & Orange II, DS 397; Trent VI, can.~17, DS 1567; CCC 1037\\
Attrition from fear of hell is a gift of God & Defined & Trent XIV, ch.~4 and can.~5, DS 1678, 1705\\
The chief pain is the loss of God & Church teaching & CCC 1035; John Paul II (1999); Irenaeus V.27.2; Chrysostom, \emph{On Matthew} 23.9; Augustine, \emph{Enchiridion} 112; Aquinas, I-II q.~87 a.~4\\
There is a pain of sense, called fire & Church teaching & Matt 25:41; Mark 9:42--47; \latin{Quicumque}; CCC 1034--1035\\
No repentance after death & Church teaching & CCC 1033, 1861; 2 Clement 8; Cyprian, \emph{To Demetrianus} 25; Gregory, \emph{Dialogues} IV.44; Aquinas, I-II q.~87 a.~3\\
Judgment of persons belongs to God & Church teaching & CCC 1861; John Paul II (1999), n.~4\\
The fire is a true fire & Common teaching of the Fathers and Doctors & Augustine, \emph{City of God} XXI.10; Gregory, \emph{Dialogues} IV.29; Supplement q.~97 aa.~5--6 (of one species with ours, with other properties). Other positions: Damascene, cited in Supplement q.~97 a.~5, ``not material fire, such as that which we have''; John Paul II (1999), the images of Scripture ``must be correctly interpreted''\\
The worm is remorse of conscience & Common teaching of the Fathers and Doctors & Supplement q.~97 a.~2; Gregory Nazianzen, \emph{Oration} 16.9; Bernard, \emph{On Consideration} V.12; Catherine, \emph{Dialogue}. Augustine, \emph{City of God} XXI.9, leaves the worm to the reader's choice\\
The saved are fewer than the lost & Theological opinion & The common opinion of the Fathers and Doctors: Matt 7:13--14; 22:14; Chrysostom, \emph{On Matthew} 23.7, \emph{On Acts} 24; Augustine, \emph{City of God} XXI.12; Gregory, \emph{Hom.\ in Evang.} 19.5; Aquinas, I q.~23 a.~7 ad 3; Alphonsus XVII (``the most common opinion''). The number is known to God alone (Missal; Aquinas, I q.~23 a.~7), and the Church has proposed none. Other position: Benedict XVI, \emph{Spe salvi} 46, supposes in ``the great majority of people'' ``an ultimate interior openness to truth, to love, to God''\\
Judas was lost & Common teaching of the Fathers and Doctors & Matt 26:24; John 17:12; collect of Holy Thursday; Augustine, \emph{City of God} I.17; Alphonsus XXVII. Other position: Benedict XVI, audience of 18 October 2006, speaks of ``the mystery around his eternal fate''\\
The mode of the darkness & Theological opinion & Matt 8:12; 22:13; 25:30; Supplement q.~97 a.~4; Gregory, \emph{Morals} IX.66\\
The place of hell & Theological opinion & Gregory, \emph{Dialogues} IV.42 (``I dare not rashly define anything''); John Paul II (1999): ``Rather than a place, hell indicates the state''\\
Teresa's vision of hell & Approved private revelation & Teresa, \emph{Life} 32; canonized 1622; Doctor of the Church 1970; no decree on this vision as such. Approved in the sense Benedict XIV gives: owed an assent of human faith, as ``probable and credible to piety'' (cited in \emph{The Message of Fatima}, 2000)\\
The vision of 13 July 1917 at Fatima & Approved private revelation & Bishop of Leiria, 13 October 1930; Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, \emph{The Message of Fatima} (2000)\\
\end{threestable}
