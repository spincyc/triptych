\subsection*{Definitive magisterium}

{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]
\item Council of Vienne (1311--1312), constitution \latin{Fidei catholicae}, DS 902 (the rational soul the form of the human body \latin{per se et essentialiter}).
\item Fifth Lateran Council, session VIII (19 December 1513), bull \latin{Apostolici regiminis}, DS 1440--1441 (the intellective soul immortal and individually multiplied; no ``double truth'').
\item John XXII, bull \latin{Ne super his} (3 December 1334), DS 990--991 (the declaration on the vision of the purified souls, with its qualifier).
\item Benedict XII, apostolic constitution \latin{Benedictus Deus} (29 January 1336), DS 1000--1002 (the intuitive and facial vision immediately after death and before the resumption of bodies and the general judgment; the immediate descent of those dying in actual mortal sin). The central definition's English is quoted as given in CCC 1023.
\item These four are quoted in Latin from the dated web presentation of the Latin \emph{Enchiridion symbolorum} registered in the source library, \sourceurl{http://patristica.net/denzinger/enchiridion-symbolorum.html}{patristica.net}, read 2026-07-26. The presentation does not identify the printed edition it transcribes and is used as a finding aid and convenient Latin witness to acts that are themselves the cited authorities; English renderings are labeled working glosses.
\item Council of Trent, session V (17 June 1546), decree concerning original sin, canons 1--2; session XIII (11 October 1551), ch.~VI and canon 7 (reserving the Eucharist and carrying it to the sick); session XIV (25 November 1551), doctrine on the sacrament of extreme unction, chs.~I--III, and canons 1--4. Latin from \emph{Canones et decreta sacrosancti oecumenici Concilii Tridentini}, editio stereotypa undecima (Leipzig: Tauchnitz, 1887); English from the public-domain translation of J.~Waterworth (London: Burns and Oates; reprint of the London 1848 translation). Both read 2026-07-26 in the Internet Archive digitizations of the printings registered for those editions in the source library.
\end{itemize}}
