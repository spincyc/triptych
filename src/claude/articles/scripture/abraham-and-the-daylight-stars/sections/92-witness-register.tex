\section{Witness register}

Every witness on which a claim in this study rests, with the locus at which it
was read, the state of the evidence, and what it contributes. Witnesses reached
only through another author's citation are marked as such; witnesses not reached
at all are listed in the following appendix among the limitations.

\subsection{Scriptural text and versions}

\begin{witnessstable}
\witnessrow{Masoretic Hebrew, Genesis 15}{Consonantal and pointed text of
15:1--21 read at the Sefaria digital text; not collated against a printed
Masoretic edition or a manuscript facsimile}{The verbal chain, the directional
endings at 15:5, the imminence/completion contrast between 15:12 and 15:17, and
\textit{bayyom hahu} at 15:18}
\witnessrow{Septuagint, Swete's edition, volume I (Cambridge, 4th ed.\ 1909)}{Genesis
15:1--21 read in the printed text and foot apparatus by way of the archive.org
text layer of that volume, checked against an independently compiled database of
the same edition}{\textit{anablepson} at 15:5; \textit{sunekathisen} at 15:11;
\textit{ekstasis} at 15:12; the absence of any darkness at 15:17;
\textit{ekei} at 15:18; no apparatus variant at 5, 12, 17, or 18}
\witnessrow{Clementine Vulgate, Hetzenauer 1914}{Genesis 15:1--21 read from a 200
dpi page image of numbered scan page 0012, retained as a tracked artifact with a
checked transcription}{\textit{per visionem}; \textit{eduxitque eum foras};
\textit{suspice caelum}; the \textit{occumberet}/\textit{occubuisset} tense
contrast; \textit{caligo tenebrosa}; \textit{in illo die}; the marginal summary
and the foot cross-references}
\witnessrow{Douay--Rheims, Challoner revision}{Genesis 15 and every other English
scriptural quotation taken from the registered public-domain witness in its
Vulgate numbering; the divergence table for the 1899 American edition was checked
and lists no verse used here}{All English scripture in this study; the chapter
argument naming the chapter a vision}
\witnessrow{Douay Old Testament, 1609, volume 1}{Genesis 15 with its marginal
notes and the annotations following the chapter, read in the archive.org text
layer of the Princeton copy}{The pre-Challoner English wording of 15:5; the
absence of any note at verse 5; the citation of Theodoret at verse 9 and of
Hilary and Ambrose at verse 6}
\witnessrow{Targum Onkelos, Genesis 15:5, 12, 17}{Aramaic read at the Sefaria
digital text}{Close rendering of the three markers with no interval inserted}
\witnessrow{Targum Pseudo-Jonathan, Genesis 15:5, 12, 17}{Aramaic and
Etheridge's public-domain English of 1862}{Extensive expansion at 15:12 and
15:17 with no temporal interval inserted anywhere in the chapter}
\end{witnessstable}

\subsection{Ancient Jewish and Christian witnesses}

\begin{witnessstable}
\witnessrow{Philo, \textit{Quis rerum divinarum heres sit}, chs.\ XVI--XVII, XX,
LI--LIII}{Yonge's public-domain English (Bohn, 1854), vol.\ II; Greek not
collated}{The going-out read as the soul's exit from body and senses; the
Chaldean star-fatalism reading at 15:7; the four kinds of trance; the sunset
allegorized as the setting of the human mind}
\witnessrow{Josephus, \textit{Antiquitates Judaicae} I.7.2 and I.8.2}{Whiston's
public-domain English, read in the Penelope transcription; Greek not
collated}{A paraphrase of Genesis 15 that deletes the command to look and every
temporal marker in the chapter; and, separately, the claim that Abram taught
arithmetic and astronomy to the Egyptians}
\witnessrow{b.~Shabbat 156a}{Vocalized Aramaic and facing English of the William
Davidson edition (CC BY-NC)}{\textit{tse me'itstagninut shellekha}: the
derivation of ``Israel has no constellation'' from \textit{vayyotse oto
hahutsah}}
\witnessrow{b.~Pesachim 6b}{Vocalized Aramaic and facing English of the William
Davidson edition (CC BY-NC)}{\textit{ein muqdam ume'uhar batorah} and Rav Pappa's
limitation of it to two matters}
\witnessrow{Genesis Rabbah 44:10 and 44:12}{The Sefaria Midrash Rabbah (2022, CC
BY) with the Torat Emet Hebrew}{Three readings of ``outside'' side by side ---
the streets of heaven, above the vault, prophet-not-astrologer --- and Abram's
complaint that his constellation presses him}
\witnessrow{Augustine, \textit{De civitate Dei} XVI.23--24}{Hoffmann's CSEL 40
Latin and Dods' 1871 English, both registered in this repository}{The
countability argument with Aratus and Eudoxus; the allegorized sunset;
\textit{Haec omnia in uisu facta}; an Old Latin text agreeing with the Greek at
15:11 and 15:17 and with the Hebrew at 15:18}
\end{witnessstable}

\subsection{Medieval and later Jewish commentators}

\begin{witnessstable}
\witnessrow{Rashi on Genesis 15:1, 5, 11, 12}{Rosenbaum--Silbermann Hebrew and
English, 1929--1934, public domain}{\textit{ahar} as immediately after; three
senses of ``outside'' with the plain sense first; the above-the-stars reading}
\witnessrow{Ibn Ezra on Genesis 15:1, 12, 17}{Piotrkow 1907--1911 Hebrew (public
domain) for 15:1 and 15:12; Strickman--Silver English (CC BY-NC) for 15:17,
where the Piotrkow text consulted ends earlier}{The intervening-day reading; the
explicit contrast between ``about to go down'' at 15:12 and ``went down'' at
15:17}
\witnessrow{Ramban (Nahmanides) on Genesis 15:1, 5, 12, 17}{Vocalized Hebrew and
Chavel's English (CC BY)}{The \textit{mahazeh} as a \emph{daytime} vision; the
plain sense of ``outside'' as the tent; the objection from Ishmael to the
astrological reading}
\witnessrow{Radak on Genesis 15:5}{Hebrew and Munk's English (CC BY)}{The
bringing-out as internal to the vision, with the Ezekiel 8:3 parallel; both
comparisons as hyperbole}
\witnessrow{Chizkuni on Genesis 15:5}{Public-domain Hebrew with Munk's English
(CC BY)}{The bringing-out as a dream appearance}
\witnessrow{Rabbeinu Bahya on Genesis 15:5}{Warsaw 1878 Hebrew (public domain)
with Munk's English (CC BY)}{The argument from \textit{habbet} to the
above-the-firmament reading; the general/individual distinction on
\textit{mazzal}}
\witnessrow{Tur HaArokh on Genesis 15:5}{Hanover 1838 Hebrew (public
domain)}{Dust and stars as Esau and Jacob}
\witnessrow{Malbim on Genesis 15:5}{Hebrew Wikisource text (public
domain)}{The regimes of the stars; the enlarged-sight reading; the telescope
aside}
\witnessrow{Netziv, \textit{Haamek Davar}, on Genesis 15:5}{Vilna 1879 Hebrew
(public domain)}{That the comparison is to eminence rather than number, since
dust had already supplied the number --- consulted and found not to bear on the
hour}
\witnessrow{Abarbanel on Genesis 15, prefatory questions 7 and 16, and the
comment on 15:5}{Warsaw 1862 Hebrew, read at Sefaria and independently collated
against the Hebrew Wikisource transcription}{The day/night dilemma stated
explicitly; the observation that Genesis fixes the hour nowhere else in
Abraham's prophecies; the three constructions of ``outside'', the third with a
waking interval between two prophecies; the Ran's answer, reported and rejected}
\witnessrow{Maimonides, \textit{Guide of the Perplexed} II.46}{Friedl\"ander's
public-domain English, 1903}{The whole chapter as vision; the rule that internal
temporal coherence does not establish waking sequence}
\end{witnessstable}

\subsection{Later Catholic commentators}

\begin{witnessstable}

Genesis 15:1, 5, 10, 11, 12, 17}{Latin read in the archive.org text layer of the

rejection of the Septuagint addition at verse 13}
\end{witnessstable}

\subsection{Reported only, not read}

The following are named in this study solely as another author reports them, and
no claim rests on their own wording: Ambrose, \textit{De Abraham} (reported by a
Lapide and by the 1609 Douay annotator); Theodoret, \textit{Quaestiones in
Genesim} (reported by the 1609 Douay margin); Rabbi Nissim of Gerona, the Ran
(reported by Abarbanel); Alonso Tostado, Benedictus Pererius, and Oleaster
(reported by a Lapide); the verses attributed to Orpheus in Clement's
\textit{Stromateis} (reported by a Lapide); Nicholas Abram and Tournemine
(reported by Haydock).
