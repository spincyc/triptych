\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{The grammatical placeholder}
    {A printed placeholder inherits its case from its local construction, not from the saint's citation form.}
    {For three placeholder contexts already described in the lesson, write the required case question, derive the form from a supplied nominative/genitive pair, and audit adjacent titles.}
    {Why is a nominative name unsafe as a universal substitution?}
    {Each model form answers the local governor or function and retains the name's declension. A nominative is correct only where that context demands nominative.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{A Common Gospel command}
    {Speaker, addressee, reflexive, and object may shift through a command sequence.}
    {Make a participant grid for the lesson's Common Gospel example and assign every personal or reflexive form to a participant with morphological evidence.}
    {State the difference between reflexive reference and simple person agreement.}
    {The grid uses clause subject and discourse participant to resolve reflexives; finite person alone does not identify every pronoun's antecedent.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Wisdom on the lips}
    {A verbless or compressed clause still requires heads, modifiers, and predicative relations to be distinguished.}
    {On the lesson phrase, mark head noun, dependent genitives or adjectives, and any interpretive copula in brackets.}
    {Identify which supplied element is not printed.}
    {The model preserves the nominal syntax and brackets an English or Latin copular expansion as interpretive. It does not claim a finite form appears in the source.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{The Marian Common}
    {Agreement can exclude a tempting nearby antecedent and identify the relative's referent.}
    {Trace the lesson's Marian relative through gender, number, internal case, and finite verb; list and reject the nearest incompatible antecedent.}
    {Which feature excludes that antecedent first?}
    {The model uses gender and number to test referents, then internal case for function. The rejected antecedent fails the printed agreement before thematic sense is considered.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Name and agreement ledger}
    {A reusable ledger must record forms actually required by a feast rather than a generic list of saintly titles.}
    {Create a blank-to-filled audit with heading, source location, name citation forms, inserted form, title, pronoun, and agreement controller.}
    {Mark one form that still requires checking in the physical Missal.}
    {A complete ledger distinguishes observed wording from proposed insertion and preserves an unresolved status where the page or direction has not been checked.}
