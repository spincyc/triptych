\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{Repetition as theological precision}
    {Repeated roots can bind clauses and propositions even when polished English would vary the vocabulary.}
    {On the lesson's repeated-word example, underline every recurrence, give each form's morphology and function, and state what remains constant or develops.}
    {Name one reason not to replace all repetitions with English synonyms during analysis.}
    {The model preserves the repeated Latin lemma while distinguishing each form's clause role. Variation in English may be stylistic, but it must not conceal the source's lexical linkage.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Impersonal frame and result}
    {A narrative frame and the event it introduces are separate grammatical layers.}
    {Bracket the lesson's impersonal frame, then indent the event or result clause and identify the tense and mood of each verbal center.}
    {State which element carries the story event's propositional content.}
    {The frame remains outside the subordinate event clause; the latter carries the event content. A model that translates the frame as the event without mapping the clause fails.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Participles and a moving subject}
    {A participant ledger prevents a participle from being attached to the most recent noun by proximity.}
    {For Worksheet III.22, make one row per participant and place every participle and finite verb under its controlled subject.}
    {For one participle, give case, gender, number, voice, and relative time.}
    {Every verbal form belongs to the row supported by agreement and narrative reference. The participle parse must match its controller in case, gender, and number and state time relative to its finite verb.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{The first day of the week}
    {Time expressions and narrative sequence must be parsed before a chronology is paraphrased.}
    {Using the lesson's printed resurrection narrative, label every time expression, motion phrase, completed action, and reported observation.}
    {Distinguish time-when from duration or motion in one sentence.}
    {The model cites the case or preposition governing each expression and orders events from finite forms and connectors. A devotional recollection without those forms is not sufficient.}
