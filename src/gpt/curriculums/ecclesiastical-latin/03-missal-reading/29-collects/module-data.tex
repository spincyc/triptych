\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{Imperatives, infinitives, and ablative absolutes}
    {Compression is manageable when each non-finite form is attached before the sentence is rendered.}
    {On the guided Collect, make a finite-spine line and hang every infinitive and ablative absolute beneath its governor with relative time and voice.}
    {Name the subject of each non-finite construction, stated or supplied.}
    {The model identifies the main petition first, treats complementary infinitives as dependent, and gives each absolute its own ablative subject. A translation without those attachments is incomplete.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Correlative relatives}
    {A repeated demonstrative or relative can bind distant parts of the prayer without creating a new independent clause.}
    {Trace the lesson's correlative pair with arrows, then replace the nouns in a project-composed parallel while preserving gender, number, and internal case.}
    {State which features come from the antecedent and which from the relative's own clause.}
    {The acceptable model preserves the pair's referent and grammatical roles; gender and number follow the antecedent, while case follows the relative's function.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Gerunds as divine accompaniment}
    {A gerund is a verbal noun whose case and governor must be explained before an English participle is chosen.}
    {For each gerund in the lesson, record case, governor, verbal complement, and the relation it adds to the petition.}
    {Distinguish a gerund from a gerundive in one deciding sentence.}
    {A passing record gives the governing preposition or case use and any object. A gerund is substantival; a gerundive is adjectival and agrees with a noun.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{A collect lexicon}
    {A useful lexicon predicts constructions as well as meanings.}
    {Choose four recurrent Collect verbs from the lesson and give principal parts, expected complement, one printed form, and one plausible wrong parse.}
    {Retrieve the complement pattern without supplying an English translation first.}
    {Each entry must identify a real construction---for example infinitive, content clause, dative, or object---and reject the wrong parse from morphology or government.}
