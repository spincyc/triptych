\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{The complete-Mass protocol}
    {A complete read is a sequence of auditable decisions, not a long translation assignment.}
    {Before Worksheet III.45, make a one-page process record with genre inventory, cold-map checkpoint, lookup release, direct-summary checkpoint, and final audit.}
    {State the evidence required before the lookup checkpoint opens.}
    {The model requires every finite frame, participant or antecedent uncertainty, and proposed lemma to be written before lookup. Missing one genre returns the learner to the inventory.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Dictionary thresholds}
    {A lookup ceiling measures morphological independence only when repeated and false lookups remain visible.}
    {For ten sample entries from the completed capstone, mark known, inferred, justified new lookup, avoidable lookup, or repeated ledger failure.}
    {Compute the content-word rate using the packet's stated denominator.}
    {The audit counts only new content words as permitted lookups, but records avoidable and repeated failures separately. A rate without the word-count denominator or contemporaneous ledger is not accepted.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Assigned capstones}
    {A second formulary tests transfer only when category, heading, and prior exposure are documented.}
    {Complete the assignment block for temporal Worksheet III.45 now and for Common Worksheet III.47 only after delay: exact heading, category, source trail, prior exposure, and assessor choice.}
    {Give one reason a familiar teaching Mass cannot serve as blind transfer.}
    {A valid record identifies the physical Missal heading and honestly discloses exposure. Reused teaching material may diagnose fluency but cannot prove unfamiliar transfer.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Measurable final gate}
    {Each domain protects a different failure mode, so strength in translation cannot erase weak morphology or reference.}
    {Score the completed temporal work in five rows and prescribe one exact remediation action for every row below threshold.}
    {Explain why averaging the rows is prohibited.}
    {The model records earned/available evidence or the stated operational threshold for every domain. Averaging would allow a strong domain to conceal a structural failure that still prevents independent reading.}
