\newcommand{\ModuleAssessmentDetails}{%
  \begingroup\small
  \textbf{Worksheet III.45, temporal core.} Complete the formulary inventory
  and pre-lookup cold map; identify the Collect's subject/content; cite six or
  more steps in the Epistle's argument; answer all six Gospel agent/action
  questions in accurate simple Latin; and score at least 36 of 40 sampled
  forms/functions, with at most ten new content-word lookups per five hundred
  words. Propose a lemma, form, and function before each lookup.

  \par\smallskip\noindent
  \textbf{Worksheet III.47, delayed Common.} Document the Proper/Common trail
  and every supplied name form; classify only printed vocabulary; give lossless
  prose-order reconstructions of the three orations and a chant verse; answer
  at least 9 of 10 questions with at least 85 percent of the Latin clauses
  grammatically defensible; score at least 36 of 40 sampled forms with at most
  five new content-word lookups per five hundred words; and count a repeated
  ledger word twice.
  \par\endgroup}

\newcommand{\ModulePractice}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{The complete-Mass protocol}
    {A complete read is a sequence of auditable decisions, not a long translation assignment.}
    {Before Worksheet III.45, make a one-page process record with genre inventory, cold-map checkpoint, lookup release, direct-summary checkpoint, and final audit.}
    {State the evidence required before the lookup checkpoint opens.}
    {The model requires every finite frame, participant or antecedent uncertainty, and proposed lemma to be written before lookup. Missing one genre returns the learner to the inventory.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Dictionary thresholds}
    {A lookup ceiling measures morphological independence only when repeated and false lookups remain visible.}
    {For ten sample entries from the completed capstone, mark known, inferred, justified new lookup, avoidable lookup, or repeated ledger failure.}
    {Compute the content-word rate using the packet's stated denominator.}
    {The audit counts only new content words as permitted lookups, but records avoidable and repeated failures separately. A rate without the word-count denominator or contemporaneous ledger is not accepted.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Assigned capstones}
    {A second formulary tests transfer only when category, heading, and prior exposure are documented.}
    {Complete the assignment block for temporal Worksheet III.45 now and for Common Worksheet III.47 only after delay: exact heading, category, source trail, prior exposure, and assessor choice.}
    {Give one reason a familiar teaching Mass cannot serve as blind transfer.}
    {A valid record identifies the physical Missal heading and honestly discloses exposure. Reused teaching material may diagnose fluency but cannot prove unfamiliar transfer.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Worksheet-specific mastery decision}
    {The temporal and Common worksheets ask for different evidence, so each form must be judged by its own printed tasks and thresholds.}
    {Prepare separate decision checklists for III.45 and delayed III.47. Copy every requested task and numeric floor from its worksheet; after completing the form, mark each requirement met or not met and prescribe one exact remediation action for every failure.}
    {State the form-sample and lookup limits for both worksheets and explain why passing III.45 cannot certify III.47 or EL-GATE-III.}
    {III.45 requires its inventory, cold map, Collect analysis, six-step argument map, six Gospel answers, at least 36/40 sampled forms/functions, and no more than ten new lookups per five hundred words. III.47 requires its assignment trail, supplied forms, evidence-bounded vocabulary, four reconstructions, at least 9/10 comprehension answers with 85 percent grammatically defensible clauses, at least 36/40 sampled forms, and no more than five new lookups per five hundred words. A failed requirement directs remediation and a fresh attempt; neither worksheet replaces EL-GATE-III.}
