\newcommand{\ModulePractice}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{Responses and legitimate ellipsis}
    {A response ledger separates printed words from a conventional expansion and from an invented completion.}
    {For the lesson's opening dialogue, make three columns: printed response, bracketed pedagogical expansion, and evidence for the expansion's force.}
    {Explain why an indicative copula cannot simply replace an optative construal.}
    {The printed response remains a prepositional phrase; a bracketed optative expansion may expose the conventional wish. The model rejects an indicative merely because it supplies a verb: it would alter discourse force.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Agreement across distance}
    {Familiar word order can conceal agreement and apposition that endings alone establish.}
    {Choose one line already printed in the selected Gloria or Creed worksheet and draw every agreement, apposition, and antecedent link over the text.}
    {For each link, state separately what gives gender/number and what gives case.}
    {A passing map names the controlling noun or antecedent, the linked form, and the form's local function. Proximity or capitalization without morphological evidence earns no credit.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Purpose clauses and prayer diction}
    {A familiar \Latin{ut} must be classified from its governor, negation, mood, and discourse role.}
    {Take the selected lesson's purpose-clause example and write an evidence table before translating it. Then invent a project-composed result clause with a clear degree signal for contrast.}
    {Give the ordinary negative marker for purpose and for result.}
    {The purpose analysis cites its governing petition or action and would negate with \Latin{ne}. A valid project-composed example such as \Latin{Tanta lux est ut omnes videant} is result and would negate with \Latin{ut non}.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Vocabulary families of the fixed Order}
    {Families become useful when each member retains its own construction.}
    {Build a five-member family from one repeated fixed-Order root and record lemma, form, governor or complement, and local sense for each occurrence.}
    {State why five English synonyms do not constitute a Latin vocabulary family.}
    {A checkable ledger groups related Latin lemmas, cites their actual forms, and preserves distinct government. English-only grouping lacks morphological and construction evidence.}
