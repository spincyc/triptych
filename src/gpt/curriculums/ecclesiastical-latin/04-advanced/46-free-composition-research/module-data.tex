\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{The composition portfolio}
    {A portfolio is evidence of decisions over time, not a clean final text detached from its process.}
    {Assemble proposal, proposition plan, first draft, correction layer, source ledger, revised draft, rubric, and unresolved list in that order.}
    {Identify which artifact proves that a polished sentence was revised rather than copied.}
    {The marked first draft and dated correction layer show revision; the source ledger distinguishes borrowed wording and modeled construction. A clean final alone cannot prove process.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{The independent reading dossier}
    {Reading evidence must remain independent from the composition's chosen source material.}
    {Design the later Worksheet IV.31 dossier now: blind heading selection, exposure declaration, cold map, lookup ledger, direct summary, difficult-form notes, and residual uncertainties. Do not complete it in this sitting.}
    {State why a text mined for the composition cannot serve as blind reading evidence.}
    {The design keeps the dossier's selection and cold record separate from composition research. Prior mining changes exposure and must be disclosed; it may support study but not blind transfer.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Final standard}
    {Graduation is a defensible judgment across reading, research, composition, and delayed revision, not the completion date of one packet.}
    {Score the first revised draft provisionally, marking each category as demonstrated, not yet demonstrated, or outside this packet. Prescribe one next evidence event for every incomplete category.}
    {Write a one-sentence claim that accurately describes the current milestone without claiming independence.}
    {A valid claim is limited to a bounded proposal and supported first revised draft. Independent reading, delayed retention, final portfolio audit, and defense remain explicitly outstanding.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Worked proposal-to-draft process}
    {A strong free project narrows choices before prose expands.}
    {Create a project-composed model packet: one-sentence aim, six propositions, declared register, three checked construction models, 150--250-word draft, and three-pass change log.}
    {For each model, distinguish borrowed phrase from abstract construction.}
    {The model labels all new Latin project-composed, cites every borrowed phrase, describes abstract syntax without claiming ownership of wording, and records at least one substantive revision in skeleton, concord, and idiom.}
