\newcommand{\ModulePractice}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{From proposal to supported draft}
    {A bounded artifact still needs an evidence trail that distinguishes planning, borrowing, correction, and revision.}
    {Prepare an assembly checklist in this order: proposal, proposition plan, first draft, correction layer, source and idiom ledger, revised draft, rubric, and unresolved list. Use it while completing Worksheets IV.29 and IV.30 and the later draft.}
    {Identify which artifacts prove that a polished sentence was revised rather than copied.}
    {The marked first draft and correction layer show revision; the source and idiom ledger distinguishes borrowed wording and modeled construction. A clean final alone cannot prove process.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Planning a later independent reading dossier}
    {Reading evidence must remain independent from the composition's chosen source material.}
    {Prepare the empty Worksheet IV.31 dossier plan now: blind heading selection, exposure declaration, cold map, lookup ledger, direct summary, difficult-form notes, and residual uncertainties. Do not select or read its Mass in this module.}
    {State why a text mined for the composition cannot serve as blind reading evidence.}
    {The design keeps the dossier's selection and cold record separate from composition research. Prior mining changes exposure and must be disclosed; it may support study but not blind transfer.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Local module boundary}
    {A local milestone must name the evidence actually produced without inflating it into course completion.}
    {Draft a one-sentence claim limited to the proposal, ledger, and supported first revision, then list the later evidence reserved to EL-GATE-IV.}
    {Name the three local deliverables and two course-level claims this packet cannot establish.}
    {The local deliverables are the bounded proposal, source and idiom ledger, and supported first revision. A mature portfolio, unfamiliar-formulary dossier, delayed retention, final audit, and defense remain outstanding; the packet establishes neither independence nor graduation.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Worked proposal-to-draft process}
    {A strong free project narrows choices before prose expands.}
    {Create a project-composed model packet: one-sentence aim, six propositions, declared register, three checked construction models, 150--250-word draft, and three-pass change log.}
    {For each model, distinguish borrowed phrase from abstract construction.}
    {The model labels all new Latin project-composed, cites every borrowed phrase, describes abstract syntax without claiming ownership of wording, and records at least one substantive revision in skeleton, concord, and idiom.}
