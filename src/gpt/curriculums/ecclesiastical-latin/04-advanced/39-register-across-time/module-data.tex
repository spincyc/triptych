\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{Four questions for a marked feature}
    {A reproducible register note separates the page's feature, the comparison norm, the checked parallel, and the remaining inference.}
    {For the lesson's worked passage, make four columns: observed form, ordinary grammar, stated baseline, and claim still requiring a dated witness.}
    {Classify each row as observation, comparison, inference, or unresolved.}
    {The model treats morphology and coordination as observations, any classical comparison as baseline-dependent, and historical cause or period as unresolved unless a dated cited parallel is supplied.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Contextual sense without etymological fallacy}
    {Construction and immediate context choose among lexical senses before word history is considered.}
    {Take one specialized lemma already discussed in the lesson and record construction, local participants, defensible sense, tempting wrong sense, and the evidence excluding it.}
    {Explain why origin cannot compel the same sense in every period or genre.}
    {A passing note derives the local sense from governor, objects, modifiers, and context. Etymology may motivate a question but cannot override those controls.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Composition laboratory}
    {Register-controlled composition requires declared models and an audit trail, not a cloud of period-sounding vocabulary.}
    {Write three project-composed Latin sentences expressing one proposition in plain narrative, prayer-shaped, and rubric-style registers; label every deliberate structural difference.}
    {Identify one morphological feature shared by all three.}
    {Models vary, but each sentence must be grammatical, preserve the proposition, declare its instructional status, and defend register from syntax and lexicon. Ordinary case and agreement should remain shared rather than falsely treated as register markers.}
