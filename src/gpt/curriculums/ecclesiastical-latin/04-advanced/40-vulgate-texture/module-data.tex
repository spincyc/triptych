\newcommand{\ModuleEnrichment}{%
  \SubmoduleExtension{Recurring textures}
    {Texture emerges from repeated observable choices, not from one unfamiliar-looking construction.}
    {For three examples already printed in the lesson, record the repeated signal, full grammatical parse, local discourse relation, and what broader claim remains unproved.}
    {Distinguish grammaticality from discourse frequency.}
    {Each model row parses ordinary Latin first, then records recurrence. Frequency can characterize a corpus even when the construction is grammatical elsewhere; one occurrence cannot prove source influence.}
  \SubmoduleExtension{Controlled imitation}
    {Imitation tests whether the learner can preserve a visible pattern while changing the proposition and labeling the result as project composition.}
    {Choose one lesson pattern, state its invariant frame, and write two project-composed Latin sentences with new content; then audit every changed case, person, and agreement.}
    {Name the invariant before comparing vocabulary.}
    {A passing model explicitly labels the Latin project-composed, preserves the declared syntax or discourse frame, changes the proposition, and accounts for every morphological cascade. Mere synonym substitution does not pass.}
