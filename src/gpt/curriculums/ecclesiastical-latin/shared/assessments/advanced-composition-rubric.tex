% Authoritative advanced-composition rubric. Consumers supply the heading.

Score each category from 0 to 4. A piece is ready for the portfolio at 3 or 4
in every category; a high total cannot conceal a structural failure.

A \Term{rubric} is a scoring instrument that defines separate dimensions of
performance and describes evidence at each level. Here \Term{morphology}
scores the forms produced; \Term{syntax} scores their dependencies;
\Term{lexicon} scores contextual word choice; \Term{cohesion} scores relations
across clauses and sentences; \Term{register} scores consistency with the
chosen language model; \Term{rhetoric} scores arrangement in service of
thought; \Term{documentation} scores traceability of evidence; and
\Term{revision} scores principled improvement across drafts. The categories
overlap in actual prose, but each diagnoses a different kind of strength or
failure.

\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{@{}P{1.45in}P{1.45in}P{1.45in}Y@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Category} & \textbf{4: independent} & \textbf{3: sound} &
  \textbf{2 or below: revise} \\
  \midrule\endhead
  Morphology & accurate throughout; any rare form defensible & isolated slip,
       no repeated pattern & repeated or meaning-changing form errors \\
  Syntax & relations clear, varied, idiomatic, controlled & sound core with
       limited awkwardness & English calques, broken government/sequence, or
       ambiguous spine \\
  Lexicon & precise contextual senses and attested constructions & generally
       apt with minor repetition & first-gloss substitutions or anachronistic
       sense \\
  Cohesion & explicit logical movement and stable reference & readable sequence
       with occasional rough join & sentence pile without controlled relation \\
  Register & consistent chosen ecclesiastical model & mostly consistent &
       accidental mixture or unsupported imitation \\
  Rhetoric & arrangement serves thought without display & some effective
       balance/repetition & labels or ornament disconnected from meaning \\
  Documentation & all borrowed language and disputed choices traceable & small
       citation gaps & patchwriting or unverifiable claims \\
  Revision & drafts show principled structural and stylistic decisions & errors
       corrected with explanation & clean copy only or unexplained replacement \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}
