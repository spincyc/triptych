\begin{courseunit}{M07}
\chapter{Reader: comparison, number, and reference}

\textbf{Text class: P --- project-composed exposition.}

\Latin{Duae viae ad urbem ducunt. Via montana brevior est quam via flumini
proxima, sed multo difficilior. Haec hieme saepe clauditur; illa per totum
annum patet. Tres viatores viam breviorem elegerunt, quattuor autem longiorem.
Primi ante meridiem ad portam venerunt, sed fessi erant; alteri serius
advenerunt, tamen sarcinas integras portaverunt. Neutra via semper melior est:
consilium ex tempore, caelo, et viribus capiendum est.}

\textbf{Gloss.} \Latin{montanus}, mountain; \Latin{flumini proximus}, near the
river; \Latin{brevior}, shorter; \Latin{difficilior}, more difficult;
\Latin{hiems}, winter; \Latin{clauditur}, is closed; \Latin{patet}, lies open;
\Latin{eligo}, choose; \Latin{fessus}, tired; \Latin{integer}, intact;
\Latin{neuter}, neither; \Latin{vires}, strength; \Latin{capiendum est}, must
be formed.

Resolve \Latin{haec}, \Latin{illa}, \Latin{primi}, and \Latin{alteri} before
translating.  Rewrite the comparison three ways: positive with \Latin{tam
\ldots quam}, comparative with \Latin{quam}, and comparative with an ablative.
Then compose a seven-sentence comparison of two real routes, tools, or methods;
include cardinal, ordinal, and distributive numerical ideas.

\ifsolutions
\section{Models and points to check}
\textbf{Project translation.} Two roads lead to the town. The mountain road is
shorter than the road near the river, but much more difficult. The former is
often closed in winter; the latter lies open all year. Three travelers chose
the shorter road, while four chose the longer. The first arrived at the gate
before midday, but were tired; the others arrived later, yet carried their
packs intact. Neither road is always better: a plan must be formed from the
time, weather, and available strength.

The pronouns refer to roads until \Latin{primi/alteri}, whose masculine plural
forms refer to travelers.  Agreement, not mere proximity, controls the shift.
\else
\section{Before continuing}
You should reasonably be able to maintain reference through a comparison,
recover comparative constructions and numerical expressions, and write a
coherent exposition rather than a list of isolated examples.
\fi
