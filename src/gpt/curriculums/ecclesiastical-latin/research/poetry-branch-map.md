# Advanced poetry branch map

## Place in the curriculum

Poetry is introduced in the common trunk at M16 because poetic order,
parallelism, ellipsis, source boundaries, and line-aware syntax are necessary
for reading the Missal. Formal prosody becomes its own advanced branch, P1–P8.
The branch is neither a detour around prose grammar nor a condition for reading
later prose packets. It begins after M22, once the person can recover syntax
without trusting word order and can maintain a source ledger.

The branch teaches textual quantity and meter; it does not teach or prescribe
pronunciation. Work proceeds at the pace needed for accurate recall, scansion,
construal, commentary, and revision.

The Henderson page reads Trojae and Lavinaque. The course normalizes j/i but
retains Lavinaque and records the edition rather than silently importing a
different critical reading. Phaedrus and the Missal are normalized only under
the declared u/v and omitted-accent convention. The P6/P7 three-member display
of the sequence is analytical lineation, not received Missal typography or a
pronunciation claim. Every English rendering is a new project translation.

## Exercise and composition contract

Every packet includes or is paired with four kinds of work:

1. memory reconstruction of quantity rules, metrical schemes, and relevant
   vocabulary;
2. scansion or pattern marking with a reason for every consequential mark;
3. syntax and direct reading that cross line boundaries;
4. bounded commentary or composition with source, quantity, syntax, and
   revision ledgers.

Models remain closed during a fresh attempt, but no work is described as a
test that grants permission. When uncertainty persists, the useful response is
to name the exact quantity, form, clause, line, or source boundary and revisit
it after other Latin.

## Expansion slots

Prudentius, Sedulius, Fortunatus, securely attributed Ambrosian hymnody, and
additional classical lyric are desirable later strata. Their work/locus labels
remain U or L until an exact public-domain edition and page image are checked.
No wording from those slots enters the received course corpus merely from
memory or a modern web transcription. When supplied, each addition must state
authorship confidence, work boundary, edition, page, normalization, rights
status, and which existing packet it materially improves.
