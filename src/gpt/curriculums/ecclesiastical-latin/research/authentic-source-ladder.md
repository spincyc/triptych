# Authentic source ladder

## Purpose and boundary

This record governs the connected-reader layer in shared/content/readers.
The Missal remains the course destination, but competence is built through
ordinary project Latin, classical prose, patristic prose, medieval argument,
biblical and liturgical genres, and composition. A source is not printed merely
because its wording is familiar. The exact edition and the relevant scan page
must be identified and checked first.

Pronunciation is outside this edition. Pacing is self-directed. The transition
notes state what a person should reasonably be able to do before continuing;
they are not grades, permissions, or claims that every difficulty has vanished.

## Evidence states and text classes

- **U — unverified lead:** a possible work, locus, edition, or attribution;
  it may guide research but supplies no course quotation.
- **L — located:** an edition and candidate locus have been found.
- **T — transcribed:** wording has been transcribed from a page image and its
  normalization recorded.
- **V — visually verified:** the transcription and locator have been checked
  against the identified page image.
- **P — project-composed:** original instructional Latin, never attributed to
  a received author.
- **R — received:** wording drawn from an identified witness or edition.

U/L/T/V record verification state; P/R record textual authorship. They are not
interchangeable.

## Normalization and audit rules

1. Record edition spelling before normalization. Caesar's partis tris and the
   Henderson Virgil's Lavinaque therefore remain visible where used.
2. Regularize u/v, j/i, and display capitalization only when the reader says so.
3. A prose-order reconstruction is P analysis, never a corrected R text.
4. Every project translation is labeled. No old facing translation is silently
   revised or reused.
5. A source, allusion, or attribution claim needs an adequate context window.
   Shared common vocabulary alone supports at most a parallel.
6. A candidate that has not reached V remains outside the printed received
   corpus. Prudentius, Sedulius, Fortunatus, and additional Ambrosian hymn
   witnesses remain expansion slots until their exact pages are checked.
