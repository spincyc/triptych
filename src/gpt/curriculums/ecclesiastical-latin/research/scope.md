# Ecclesiastical Latin — Research Scope

## Identity

- **Provider:** OpenAI GPT, represented by the structured
  `generation-metadata.tex` record in each publication leaf.
- **Collection / genre:** *Triptych*; self-paced language curriculum and
  edition-specific Roman liturgical reference work.
- **Canonical owner:** `curriculums/ecclesiastical-latin`, a non-publishable
  source and audit root. Its publishable children are two course companions,
  46 prerequisite-ordered learning modules, and four stage-assessment
  sequences.
- **Rite, edition, and principal language:** Roman Rite as printed in the 1962
  typical edition of the *Missale Romanum*; English instruction with Latin
  source text, examples, answers, and composition.
- **Jurisdictional scope:** the universal 1962 typical-edition corpus. Local
  propers may be used by a learner only after identifying the governing
  edition; none controls the course.
- **As-of date:** 2026-07-20 for online witnesses, integration, and internal
  audit. The date does not answer present authorization to celebrate with the
  1962 books.

## Learner, entry assumptions, and exit standard

The intended learner reads fluent English, knows formal English grammar, and
knows the structure of Mass, but begins with no Latin grammar. Pronunciation is
not an objective. A learner who lacks either named prerequisite should acquire
it before beginning.

The observable terminal standard is direct reading of complete unfamiliar
1962 formularies without a facing translation; defensible morphological,
syntactic, lexical, and source analysis; controlled and guided composition;
and a short original composition meeting the advanced rubric. It does not mean
memorizing every page, possessing a credential, or acquiring authority to make
liturgical, theological, textual, or canonical judgments.

## Included material

- a separately printable course guide, mastery sequence, study loop, error
  ledger, and reference-grammar companion;
- twelve foundation, fourteen core-grammar, twelve genre-centered
  Missal-reading, and eight advanced reading/composition modules, each with
  its own prerequisite, two-session core, local reference card, enriched
  submodules, selected practice, mastery decision, and terminal solutions;
- 184 authoritative worksheet variants at the owner root, of which each
  learning module prints two deliberately contrasting variants and their
  matching keys while identifying the other two as remediation or delayed
  work;
- reusable handwritten forms, designated-formulary diagnostics, an
  unfamiliar-formulary transfer gate, and advanced portfolio defenses in four
  assessment-sequence companions;
- terminal solutions rendered from the same authoritative exercise and
  enrichment sources as their prompts;
- received excerpts from the 1962 Missal and Clementine Vulgate, together with
  visibly instructional examples, transformations, translations, and models;
  and
- source, scope, passage, curriculum, answer, provenance, and production audit
  records.

## Exclusions

- pronunciation, phonetics, chant, singing, and accent training;
- ceremonial instruction, selection of a proper, calendar computation, and a
  present-law judgment about use of the 1962 books;
- an official Latin text, critical edition, official translation, approved
  seminary curriculum, credential, or replacement for the Missal or Vulgate;
- comprehensive patristic, medieval, humanist, or canonical-Latin reading;
- a claim that school grammars alone establish every biblical, late,
  ecclesiastical, or liturgical development; and
- redistribution of donor PDFs, full source scans, OCR corpora, dictionary
  databases, or modern Missal translations.

## Text, orthography, and translation boundary

Received wording follows the identified 1962 Missal or the named 1914
Hetzenauer Clementine Vulgate witness at the locus recorded in
`passage-inventory.md`. The course normally preserves ligature spelling
`æ/œ`, consonantal `i` rather than `j`, and the Missal's ordinary capitalization
and punctuation, but does not reproduce printed accents consistently. Such
typographic normalization is not represented as a textual variant. A change
that affects form, parsing, meaning, or source identity must be recorded
locally and in the passage inventory.

English explanations, structural translations, natural renderings, prompts,
and model compositions are project-created teaching material unless explicitly
attributed. They are not official translations. Uncited short drills are
project-composed; a received worksheet reading carries a stable printed
passage ID. Open compositions are evaluated by a rubric, not by treating the
printed model as uniquely correct.

## Source hierarchy

1. page images of the 1962 typical *Missale Romanum* for Missal wording,
   boundaries, headings, and printed scriptural citations;
2. page images of the 1914 Hetzenauer Clementine Vulgate for biblical
   comparison, with its electronic transcription used only for finding;
3. the exact printed grammar or lexicon edition named in
   `edition-manifest.md` for classical morphology, syntax, and lexical range;
4. an identified primary ecclesiastical or historical text for later usage;
5. searchable presentations, OCR, and dictionary interfaces as finding aids;
   and
6. internal cross-consistency and automated checks, which can reveal an error
   but cannot establish a received text's source by themselves.

## Completeness, review, and rights state

The complete donor teaching corpus has been integrated and partitioned without
duplicating its shared lessons or answer sources; no donor PDF is installed or
treated as an input. The curriculum map accounts for all 46 units and the six
companions. The internal Latin and answer audit is recorded by family in
`answer-key-audit.md` and `source-audit.md`. Exact received-text coverage and
unresolved loci are recorded without concealment in `passage-inventory.md`.

The work has no imprimatur, nihil obstat, ecclesiastical approval, or
independent Latin, liturgical, pedagogical, theological, textual, canonical,
or rights review. Its donor provenance, incorporated liturgical wording, and
page-image collation remain incomplete; current production verification is
recorded separately. The 52 rebuilt PDFs remain uninstalled review candidates.
The prior installed snapshots and their exact-byte authorization are unchanged;
that authorization does not record completion of any missing review, establish
an open license, authorize extracted material, or authorize later replacement
bytes. Installation and changed-byte release authorization remain separate.
