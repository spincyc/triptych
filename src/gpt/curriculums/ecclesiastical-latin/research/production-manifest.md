contents geometry. The complete audit also retains checks for source selection,

resolution; cache hashes for the complete set matched the final build. Every
rendered page was inspected through the contact sheets, with full-resolution
checks of contents pages and identified high-risk pages. The complete exact set
showed no clipping, overflow, blank-or-rule-only pages, or remaining structure
or pagination defect.
