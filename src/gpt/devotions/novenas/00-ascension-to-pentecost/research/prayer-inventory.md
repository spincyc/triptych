## Inventory controls

- No prayer originating solely in a modern vernacular source is included.
- No proprietary current English Missal or Liturgy of the Hours prayer is reproduced.
- The two ancient hymns are complete rather than selectively paraphrased; their project translations preserve every stanza and do not silently add devotional promises.
- The daily Latin collects intentionally imitate the grammar of a Roman collect without claiming liturgical composition, ecclesiastical approval, or idiomatic finality.
- The spontaneous statement of the reader's intention is an instruction, not a printed fixed prayer; no English-only vocal formula is assigned.
- Before any ecclesiastical submission, public parish adoption, or claim of linguistic maturity, every project Latin prayer requires independent line-by-line review.
