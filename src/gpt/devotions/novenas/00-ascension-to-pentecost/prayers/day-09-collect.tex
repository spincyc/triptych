\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 9}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui in die Pentecóstes Spíritum Sanctum super Apóstolos effudísti et Ecclésiam ad omnes gentes misísti: renóva in nobis grátiam Baptísmatis et Confirmatiónis, purga nos igne caritátis, vivífica nos hálitu tuo, et fac nos, verbo et ópere, mirabília tua annuntiáre. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who on the day of Pentecost didst pour out the Holy Spirit upon the apostles and send the Church unto every nation: renew in us the grace of Baptism and Confirmation; cleanse us by the fire of charity; quicken us by thy breath; and make us proclaim thy mighty works in word and deed. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
