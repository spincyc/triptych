Historical source approval does not extend to this novena.}{%
Illo nos igne, qu\'{\ae}sumus, Dómine, Spíritus Sanctus inflámmet: quem Dóminus noster Iesus Christus misit in terram, et vóluit veheménter accéndi: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte eiúsdem Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
May the Holy Spirit, we beseech Thee, O Lord, inflame us with that fire which our Lord Jesus Christ sent down upon earth, and earnestly desired might be enkindled: Who with Thee and the same Holy Spirit liveth and reigneth one God. For ever and ever. Amen.}
