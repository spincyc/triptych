\bilingualprayer{For the fire of the Holy Spirit}{Received collect from the shorter form of Pentecost Ember Saturday in the 1962 \textit{Missale Romanum}; reproduced here devotionally with its full conventional conclusion and a project English translation. Its approval for liturgical use belongs to that historical Missal context, not to this unapproved novena.}{%
Illo nos igne, qu\'{\ae}sumus, Dómine, Spíritus Sanctus inflámmet: quem Dóminus noster Iesus Christus misit in terram, et vóluit veheménter accéndi: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte eiúsdem Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
May the Holy Spirit inflame us with that fire, we beseech thee, O Lord: the fire which our Lord Jesus Christ sent upon the earth and willed to be kindled mightily: who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the same Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
