\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 5}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui divérsas géntium línguas in confessióne mirabílium tuórum congregáre dignátus es: custódi nos in una fide, ab omni supérbia et contémptu líbera, et da nobis ita dona populórum recípere, ut veritáti Evangélii serviámus et omnes in caritáte ædificémus. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who wast pleased to gather the diverse tongues of the nations in confession of thy mighty works: keep us in the one faith; free us from every pride and contempt; and grant us so to receive the gifts of peoples that we may serve the truth of the Gospel and build up all in charity. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
