\bilingualprayer{The unity of the baptized}{Received Thursday within the octave of Easter collect from the 1962 \textit{Missale Romanum}; reproduced here devotionally with its full conventional conclusion and a project English translation. Its approval for liturgical use belongs to that historical Missal context, not to this unapproved novena.}{%
Deus, qui diversitátem géntium in confessióne tui nóminis adunásti: da, ut renátis fonte baptísmatis una sit fides méntium, et píetas actiónum. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who hast united the diversity of nations in the confession of thy name: grant that those reborn from the font of Baptism may have one faith in their minds and one devotion in their deeds. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
