\bilingualprayer{For a devoted will}{Received 1962 Latin collect, with its abbreviated conclusion mechanically completed from \textit{Rubricae generales} 115(a), p.~XIX; received historical English from Lefebvre's 1925 \textit{Daily Missal}, p.~948, with its abbreviated conclusion mechanically completed from pp.~8--9. Historical source approval does not extend to this novena.}{%
Omnípotens sempitérne Deus: fac nos tibi semper et devótam gérere voluntátem; et maiestáti tuæ sincéro corde servíre. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O almighty and eternal God, grant us ever to have a will devoted to Thee, and to serve Thy majesty with a sincere heart. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Thy Son, Who liveth and reigneth with Thee in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
