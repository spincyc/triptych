\bilingualprayer{For a devoted will}{Received Sunday after Ascension collect from the 1962 \textit{Missale Romanum}; reproduced here devotionally with its full conventional conclusion and a project English translation. Its approval for liturgical use belongs to that historical Missal context, not to this unapproved novena.}{%
Omnípotens sempitérne Deus: fac nos tibi semper et devótam gérere voluntátem; et maiestáti tuæ sincéro corde servíre. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
Almighty everlasting God, make us always maintain a will devoted to thee and serve thy majesty with a sincere heart. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
