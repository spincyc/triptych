\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 2}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui discípulos Fílii tui in oratióne unanímes congregásti: dissólve supérbiam quæ nos dívidit, sana memóriam vulnerátam, et concéde nobis veritátem in caritáte fácere, ut unum corpus, uno Spíritu vivificátum, ad laudem glóriæ tuæ ædificémur. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who didst gather the disciples of thy Son in one accord of prayer: break the pride that divides us, heal wounded memory, and grant us to do the truth in charity, that we may be built into one Body, made living by one Spirit, unto the praise of thy glory. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
