\bilingualprayer{For the Church's peace}{Received 1962 Latin collect, with its abbreviated same-Spirit conclusion mechanically completed from \textit{Rubricae generales} 115(a) and (e), p.~XIX; received historical English from Lefebvre's 1925 \textit{Daily Missal}, p.~987, with its abbreviated conclusion mechanically completed from pp.~8--9. Historical source approval does not extend to this novena.}{%
Da, qu\'{\ae}sumus, Ecclésiæ tuæ, miséricors Deus: ut, Sancto Spíritu congregáta, hostíli nullátenus incursióne turbétur. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte eiúsdem Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
Grant to Thy Church, we beseech Thee, O merciful God, that having been formed by the Holy Spirit, it may not be troubled by any hostile attacks. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Thy Son, Who liveth and reigneth with Thee in the unity of the same Holy Ghost, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
