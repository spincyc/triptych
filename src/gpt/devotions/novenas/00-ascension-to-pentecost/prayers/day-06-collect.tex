\bilingualprayer{For the light of the Paraclete}{Received Pentecost Ember Wednesday collect from the 1962 \textit{Missale Romanum}; reproduced here devotionally with its full conventional conclusion and a project English translation. Its approval for liturgical use belongs to that historical Missal context, not to this unapproved novena.}{%
Mentes nostras, qu\'{\ae}sumus, Dómine, Paráclitus, qui a te procédit, illúminet: et indúcat in omnem, sicut tuus promísit Fílius, veritátem: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte eiúsdem Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
May the Paraclete, who proceedeth from thee, enlighten our minds, we beseech thee, O Lord, and lead us into all truth, as thy Son hath promised: who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the same Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
