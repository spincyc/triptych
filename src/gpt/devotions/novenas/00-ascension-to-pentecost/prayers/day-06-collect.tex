\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 6}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui super Unigénitum tuum Spíritum sapiéntiæ et intelléctus, consílii et fortitúdinis, sciéntiæ, pietátis et timóris tui requiescere fecísti: dona nobis, in Christo insertis, eídem Spíritui dóciles esse, ut virtútes nostras perfíciat et nos per Fílii tui viam ad te perdúcat. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who didst cause the Spirit of wisdom and understanding, counsel and fortitude, knowledge, piety, and thy fear to rest upon thine Only-begotten Son: grant that we, grafted into Christ, may be docile to that same Spirit, that he may perfect our virtues and lead us unto thee by the way of thy Son. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
