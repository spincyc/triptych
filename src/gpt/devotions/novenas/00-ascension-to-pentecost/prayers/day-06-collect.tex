\bilingualprayer{For the light of the Paraclete}{Received 1962 Latin collect, with its abbreviated relative same-Spirit conclusion mechanically completed from \textit{Rubricae generales} 115(c) and (e), p.~XIX; received historical English from Lefebvre's 1925 \textit{Daily Missal}, p.~980, with its abbreviated relative conclusion mechanically completed from that witness's full formula and response at p.~861. Historical source approval does not extend to this novena.}{%
Mentes nostras, qu\'{\ae}sumus, Dómine, Paráclitus, qui a te procédit, illúminet: et indúcat in omnem, sicut tuus promísit Fílius, veritátem: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte eiúsdem Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
May the Paraclete, Who proceedeth from Thee, enlighten our minds, we beseech Thee, O Lord, and lead us into all truth, as Thy Son hath promised. Who with Thee and the same Holy Spirit liveth and reigneth one God. For ever and ever. Amen.}
