\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 7}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui caritátem tuam per Spíritum Sanctum in córdibus nostris diffúndis: mortífica in nobis ópera carnis, purga quod fructum impédit, et fac nos in Christo manére; ut cáritas, gáudium, pax, patiéntia et omnis sanctitátis fructus ad salútem próximi matúréscant. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who pourest thy charity into our hearts through the Holy Spirit: put to death in us the works of the flesh; cleanse away whatever hinders fruit; and make us abide in Christ, that charity, joy, peace, patience, and every fruit of holiness may ripen for our neighbor's salvation. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
