O God, who makest all things work for good to those who love thee: give our hearts an inviolable love of thee, that the desires conceived by thine inspiration may be altered by no temptation. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
