\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 3}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui beátam Maríam Vírginem, Matrem Iesu, cum Apóstolis in oratióne perseveráre voluísti: eius précibus nos ad Fílium tuum semper perdúc, corda nostra verbo tuo apéri, et fac nos Spíritui Sancto humíliter consentíre, ut Christus in vita nostra mundo maniféstetur. Per eúndem Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who didst will that the Blessed Virgin Mary, the Mother of Jesus, should persevere in prayer with the apostles: by her prayers lead us always to thy Son; open our hearts to thy word; and make us humbly consent to the Holy Spirit, that Christ may be made manifest to the world in our lives. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.}
