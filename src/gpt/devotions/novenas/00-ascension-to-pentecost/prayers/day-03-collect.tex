\bilingualprayer{The Annunciation of the Blessed Virgin Mary}{Received Annunciation collect from the 1962 \textit{Missale Romanum}; reproduced here devotionally with its full conventional conclusion and a project English translation. Its approval for liturgical use belongs to that historical Missal context, not to this unapproved novena.}{%
Deus, qui de beátæ Maríæ Vírginis útero Verbum tuum, Angelo nuntiánte, carnem suscípere voluísti: præsta supplícibus tuis; ut, qui vere eam Genetrícem Dei crédimus, eius apud te intercessiónibus adiuvémur. Per eúndem Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who didst will thy Word, at an Angel's message, to take flesh from the womb of the Blessed Virgin Mary: grant that we, thy suppliants, who believe her truly to be the Mother of God, may be aided by her intercessions before thee. Through the same Jesus Christ, thy Son, our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
