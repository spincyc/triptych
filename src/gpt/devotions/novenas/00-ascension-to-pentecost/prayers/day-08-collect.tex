\bilingualprayer{For the propagation of the Faith}{Received collect from the 1962 \textit{Missale Romanum} votive Mass for the propagation of the Faith; reproduced here devotionally with its full conventional conclusion and a project English translation. Its approval for liturgical use belongs to that historical Missal context, not to this unapproved novena.}{%
Deus, qui omnes hómines vis salvos fíeri et ad agnitiónem veritátis veníre: mitte, qu\'{\ae}sumus, operários in messem tuam, et da eis cum omni fidúcia loqui verbum tuum; ut sermo tuus currat et clarificétur, et omnes gentes cognóscant te solum Deum verum, et quem misísti Iesum Christum, Fílium tuum, Dóminum nostrum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who willest all men to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth: send, we beseech thee, laborers into thy harvest, and grant them to speak thy word with all confidence, that thy word may run and be glorified, and all nations may know thee, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom thou hast sent, thy Son, our Lord: who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
