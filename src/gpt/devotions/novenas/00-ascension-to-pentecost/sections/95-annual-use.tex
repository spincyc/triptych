\section{Annual-Use Sheet}

\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries The novena at a glance}\par\vspace{0.35em}
{\itshape Begin Friday nine days before Pentecost; end on the Saturday Vigil.}
\end{center}

\begin{fourstable}{Day}{Principal Scripture}{Focus}{Concrete response}
1 & Acts 1:1--11 & Ascension, promise, obedience & Complete a neglected duty\\
2 & Acts 1:12--14 & Perseverance and concord & Reconcile or cease detraction\\
3 & Lk 1:26--38; Acts 1:14 & Mary and receptive discipleship & Hidden ecclesial care\\
4 & Jn 16:7--15; Acts 2:37--41 & Conviction and forgiveness & Examine conscience; seek confession\\
5 & Acts 2:5--13; Eph 4:1--16 & Unity in legitimate diversity & Listen across a real difference\\
6 & Isa 11:1--5; Rom 8:14--17 & Sevenfold gifts and docility & Discern one decision\\
7 & Gal 5:13--26; Jn 15:1--17 & Fruit and holiness & Perform a costly work of mercy\\
8 & 1 Cor 12; Acts 13:1--4 & Charisms and mission & Offer a skill for the common good\\
9 & Ezek 37; Acts 2; Jn 20:19--23 & Fire, breath, new creation & Keep vigil and prepare for the feast\\
\end{fourstable}

\subsection*{Daily sequence}

Sign of the Cross $\rightarrow$ intention surrendered to God's will $\rightarrow$ daily invocation $\rightarrow$ Scripture and silence $\rightarrow$ meditation and act $\rightarrow$ received collect $\rightarrow$ \textit{Veni Creator} $\rightarrow$ Lord's Prayer $\rightarrow$ doxology. On Day~9 add \textit{Veni Sancte Spiritus}.

\subsection*{Calendar decision}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Ascension Thursday locally:} begin the next day.
\item \textbf{Ascension transferred to Sunday:} begin Friday anyway to preserve nine days; the transferred solemnity falls on Day~3.
\item \textbf{Authorized parish or institute order:} follow its approved calendar and text.
\item \textbf{A missed day:} resume without superstition; no devotional arithmetic can compel grace.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Best ecclesial setting}

Where possible, join the Church's Vespers and the appointed liturgy; use this private order outside them. Prepare for worthy sacramental participation at Pentecost. The public-novena indulgence in the fourth \textit{Enchiridion}, concession 22, applies according to its actual terms, not merely because this booklet was completed privately.

\begin{studybox}{One final criterion}
The novena has borne its proper fruit when prayer turns outward: deeper worship of the Trinity, firmer union with Christ, docility to the Spirit, repentance, sacramental life, love of the Church, truthful unity, works of mercy, and apostolic witness.
\end{studybox}
