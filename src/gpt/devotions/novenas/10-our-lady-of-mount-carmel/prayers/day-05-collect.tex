\bilingualprayer{Blessed Virgin Mary, Image and Mother of the Church}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 25 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Deus, misericordiárum Pater, cuius Unigénitus, cruci affíxus, beátam Maríam Vírginem, Genetrícem suam, Matrem quoque nostram constítuit, concéde, qu\'{\ae}sumus, ut, eius cooperánte caritáte, Ecclésia tua, in dies fecúndior, prolis sanctitáte exsúltet et in grémium suum cunctas áttrahat famílias populórum. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, Father of mercies, whose Only Begotten Son, nailed to the Cross, appointed the Blessed Virgin Mary, his own Mother, to be our Mother also, grant, we beseech thee, that through the cooperation of her charity thy Church, growing daily more fruitful, may rejoice in the holiness of her children and draw all the families of the nations into her bosom. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
