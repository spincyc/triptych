\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 5}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui sub cruce Fílii tui beátam Maríam discípulo matrem dedísti et eam cum Ecclésia in oratióne perseveráre fecísti: concéde ut ipsam Matrem, Patrónam et Sorórem fidéliter venerémur, eius précibus adiuvémur, et fratres nostros ad obœdiéntiam Christi caritáte perdúcere discámus. Per eúndem Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who beneath the Cross of thy Son gave the Blessed Mary as mother to the disciple and caused her to persevere in prayer with the Church: grant that we may faithfully venerate her as Mother, Patroness, and Sister, be aided by her prayers, and learn in charity to lead our brothers and sisters toward obedience to Christ. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.}
