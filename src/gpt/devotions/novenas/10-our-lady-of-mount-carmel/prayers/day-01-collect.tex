\bilingualprayer{Holy Mary, the New Woman}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 20 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Deus, qui beátam Vírginem, a Spíritu Sancto plasmátam, novæ creatiónis constituísti primítias, concéde nobis, ut, a peccáti vetustáte abstinéntes, novitátem Evangélii toto corde amplectámur et novum amóris mandátum fidéliter observémus. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who didst establish the Blessed Virgin, fashioned by the Holy Spirit, as the firstfruits of the new creation, grant us, turning away from the oldness of sin, to embrace the newness of the Gospel with our whole heart and faithfully to keep the new commandment of love. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
