\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 1}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui per Elíam prophétam cor pópuli tui ad cultum veri nóminis revocásti: aufer a nobis omnem dúplicem ánimum, et, beátæ Maríæ Vírginis de Monte Carmélo intercessióne, concéde ut, in Christo Fílio tuo únice permanéntes, tibi íntegro corde serviámus. Per eúndem Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who through Elijah the prophet called the heart of thy people back to the worship of thy true name: take from us every divided mind and, by the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary of Mount Carmel, grant that, abiding in Christ thy Son alone, we may serve thee with an undivided heart. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.}
