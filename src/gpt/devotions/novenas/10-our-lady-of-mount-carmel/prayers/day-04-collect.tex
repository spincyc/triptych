\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 4}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui beátam Maríam Vírginem Verbum tuum fide audíre, corde conférre et carne párere voluísti: áperi aures nostras ad Scriptúras, purífica cogitatiónes nostras, et da nobis, eius exémplo, quod audívimus diligénter custodíre atque opéribus adimplére. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who willed that the Blessed Virgin Mary should hear thy Word in faith, ponder it in her heart, and bear it in the flesh: open our ears to the Scriptures, purify our thoughts and grant us, after her example, diligently to keep what we have heard and fulfill it in our deeds. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
