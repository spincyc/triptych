\bilingualprayer{Holy Mary, Disciple of the Lord}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 10 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Dómine Deus noster, qui in beáta Vírgine María exémplar nobis dedísti discípuli verba vitæ fidéliter custodiéntis, áperi, qu\'{\ae}sumus, corda nostra ad suscipiéndum verbum salútis, quod, Spíritus Sancti virtúte, in nobis cotídie résonet et ubérrimos gérminet fructus. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
Lord our God, who in the Blessed Virgin Mary hast given us an example of the disciple who faithfully keeps the words of life, open our hearts, we beseech thee, to receive the word of salvation, that by the power of the Holy Spirit it may resound within us each day and bring forth most abundant fruit. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
