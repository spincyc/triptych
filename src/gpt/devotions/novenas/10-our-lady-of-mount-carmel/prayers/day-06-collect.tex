\bilingualprayer{Blessed Virgin Mary, Temple of the Lord}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 23 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Deus, qui in virgináli beátæ Maríæ grémio ineffábili arte sanctum Fílio tuo templum condidísti, concéde ut, grátiam baptísmi fidéliter custodiéntes, tui simus adoratóres in spíritu et veritáte et templum glóriæ tuæ nos quoque pérfici mereámur. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who with ineffable skill didst build a holy temple for thy Son in the virginal womb of Blessed Mary, grant that, faithfully guarding the grace of Baptism, we may be worshippers of thee in spirit and truth and may be found worthy to be brought to perfection as a temple of thy glory. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
