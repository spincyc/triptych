\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 6}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui nos in Baptísmo Christum indúere fecísti: concéde ut, signo hábitus Carmelitáni admóniti et beátæ Maríæ intercessióne protécti, véterem hóminem cum áctibus suis deponámus et misericórdiam, humilitátem, patiéntiam atque caritátem cotídie induámus. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who in Baptism hast caused us to put on Christ: grant that, admonished by the sign of the Carmelite habit and protected by the intercession of Blessed Mary, we may put off the old self with its deeds and daily put on mercy, humility, patience, and charity. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
