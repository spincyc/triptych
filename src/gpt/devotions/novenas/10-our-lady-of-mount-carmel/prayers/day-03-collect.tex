Deus, qui beátam Vírginem, húmilem ancíllam tuam, misericórdi redemptiónis consílio, Christi matrem et sóciam statuísti, tríbue nobis ut, eam assídue spectántes, tibi plenam exhibeámus servitútem et pro hóminum salúte vígiles impendámus curas. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who in thy merciful plan of redemption didst appoint the Blessed Virgin, thy humble handmaid, to be the Mother and companion of Christ, grant us, keeping our gaze continually upon her, to offer thee complete service and to devote watchful care to the salvation of all. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
