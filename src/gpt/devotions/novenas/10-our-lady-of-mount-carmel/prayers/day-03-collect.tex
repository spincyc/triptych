\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 3}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Dómine Iesu Christe, qui nos in obséquio tuo vívere et crucem cotídie portáre vocásti: indue nos misericórdia, humilitáte et caritáte, ut, Maríam Matrem tuam contemplántes, verbum tuum corde custodiámus et in ómnibus vocatióni nostræ fidéliter serviámus. Qui vivis et regnas in s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
Lord Jesus Christ, who hast called us to live in allegiance to thee and to bear the cross each day: clothe us with mercy, humility, and charity, that, contemplating Mary thy Mother, we may keep thy word in our hearts and serve our vocation faithfully in all things. Who livest and reignest through ages of ages. Amen.}
