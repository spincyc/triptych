\bilingualprayer{Collect of the feast}{Received Latin collect in the Roman and Carmelite tradition; project English translation.}{%
Adiúvet nos, qu\'{\ae}sumus, Dómine, gloriósæ Vírginis Maríæ intercéssio veneránda, ut, eius muníti præsídiis, ad montem, qui Christus est, perveníre valeámus. Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
May the venerable intercession of the glorious Virgin Mary help us, we beseech thee, O Lord, that, strengthened by her protection, we may attain the mountain who is Christ. Who lives and reigns with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, through all ages of ages. Amen.}
