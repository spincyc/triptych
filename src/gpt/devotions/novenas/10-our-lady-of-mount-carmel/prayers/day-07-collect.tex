\bilingualprayer{The Immaculate Heart of the Blessed Virgin Mary}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 28 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Dómine Deus noster, qui immaculátum Cor beátæ Vírginis Maríæ domum effecísti Verbi tui et Spíritus Sancti sacrárium; concéde nobis, qu\'{\ae}sumus, cor mundum et dócile, ut, mandátis tuis fidéliter obsequéntes, te super ómnia diligámus et fratrum necessitátibus sollíciti consulámus. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
Lord our God, who didst make the immaculate Heart of the Blessed Virgin Mary the dwelling of thy Word and the sanctuary of the Holy Spirit, grant us, we beseech thee, a pure and docile heart, that, faithfully obeying thy commandments, we may love thee above all things and care attentively for the needs of our brothers and sisters. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
