\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 7}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui mundis corde visiónem tui promíttis: per Maríam Vírginem puríssimam mentes nostras ab omni dolo emúnda, córpora nostra in castitáte secúndum statum nostrum custódi, et verba nostra ita dírige, ut imáginem tuam in omni próximo honorémus. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who promisest the vision of thee to the pure of heart: through Mary the most pure Virgin, cleanse our minds from every deceit, guard our bodies in chastity according to our state, and so direct our words that we may honor thine image in every neighbor. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
