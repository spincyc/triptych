\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 2}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui Elíam lassum cibo refecísti et in síbilo auræ ténuis vocem tuam audíre docuísti: sana in nobis quod labóre turbátum est, compésce quod tumúltu distráhitur, et, beáta María intercédente, fac nos in siléntio veritátem tuam suscípere atque in missiónem tuam redíre. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who refreshed the weary Elijah with food and taught him to hear thy voice in the whisper of a gentle air: heal in us what toil has disordered, quiet what tumult distracts and, through the intercession of Blessed Mary, make us receive thy truth in silence and return to thy mission. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
