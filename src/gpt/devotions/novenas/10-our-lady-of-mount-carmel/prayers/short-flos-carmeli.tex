\bilingualprayer{\textit{Flos Carmeli}: short form}{Received medieval Carmelite text with variant readings; project English translation. This printing uses \textit{esto propitia}; the common variant \textit{da privilegia} is documented in \CarmelFlosVariantReference.}{%
Flos Carmeli, vitis florígera, splendor cæli, Virgo puérpera singuláris. Mater mitis, sed viri néscia, Carmélitis esto propítia, Stella maris.}{%
Flower of Carmel, vine in blossom, splendour of heaven: Virgin Mother, alone beyond compare. Gentle Mother, knowing no man, be gracious to the children of Carmel, Star of the Sea.}
