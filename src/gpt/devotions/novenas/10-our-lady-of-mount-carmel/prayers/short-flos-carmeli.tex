\bilingualprayer{\textit{Flos Carmeli}: short form}{Received medieval Carmelite text using the \textit{da privilegia} variant; public-domain English verse by Kenelm Digby Best, C.O., \textit{A Priest's Poems} (1902), p.~136. Best's text is a historical adaptation, not a current approved liturgical translation. The variant is documented in \CarmelFlosVariantReference.}{%
Flos Carmeli, vitis florígera, splendor cæli, Virgo puérpera singuláris. Mater mitis, sed viri néscia, Carmélitis da privilégia, Stella maris.}{%
Carmel's fair Flower!\\
Rod blossom-laden\\
Smile on thy Dower,\\
Meek Mother-Maiden!\\
None equals thee.\\[0.45em]
Grant us a sign\\
Thou dost protect us,\\
Mark us for thine;\\
Shine, and direct us,\\
Star of the Sea!}
