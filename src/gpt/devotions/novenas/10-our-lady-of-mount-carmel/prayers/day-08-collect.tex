\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 8}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus caritátis, qui multos in Christo unum corpus effícis et dona Spíritus ad commúne bonum largíris: fac ut, Maríæ de Monte Carmélo comitátu, siléntium nostrum in misericórdia fecúndum fiat, labor noster fratres súblevet, et bona nostra necessitátibus Ecclésiæ serviant. Per Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God of charity, who makest the many one body in Christ and bestowest the gifts of the Spirit for the common good: grant that, in the company of Mary of Mount Carmel, our silence may become fruitful in mercy, our labor may lift our brothers and sisters, and our goods may serve the needs of the Church. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.}
