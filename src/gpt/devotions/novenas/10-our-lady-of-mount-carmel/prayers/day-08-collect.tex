\bilingualprayer{Holy Mary, Mother of Unity}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 38 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Dómine, sancte Pater, fons unitátis et orígo concórdiæ, concéde ut cunctæ famíliæ populórum, intercedénte beáta Vírgine María, hóminum Matre, in unum novi f\'{\oe}deris pópulum congregéntur. Per Dóminum nostrum Iesum Christum Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O Lord, holy Father, source of unity and origin of concord, grant that all the families of the nations, through the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of mankind, may be gathered into the one people of the new covenant. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, thy Son, who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
