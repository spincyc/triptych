\bilingualprayer{Flower of Carmel}{Received expanded form of a medieval Carmelite sequence, using the \textit{esto propitia} and \textit{nos ad esse / tecum in saeculum} readings. English from a Carmelite-source transcription attributing it to the joint O.Carm.--OCD \textit{Proper of the Liturgy of the Hours} (1993), reproduced under confirmed approval for use supplied to the project. The translator is not separately named, and Simon Stock attribution remains uncertain.}{%
Flos Carmeli,\\
vitis florigera,\\
splendor caeli,\\
virgo puerpera\\
singularis.\\[0.45em]
Mater mitis\\
sed viri nescia\\
Carmelitis\\
esto propitia\\
stella maris.\\[0.45em]
Radix Iesse\\
germinans flosculum\\
nos ad esse\\
tecum in saeculum\\
patiaris.\\[0.45em]
Inter spinas\\
quae crescis lilium\\
serva puras\\
mentes fragilium\\
tutelaris.}{%
Flower of Carmel,\\
blossoming, bearing one,\\
light of heaven,\\
mother of God’s dear Son,\\
vine and virgin.\\[0.45em]
Gentle parent,\\
pure beyond human love,\\
bless your children,\\
star shining far above\\
this world’s ocean.\\[0.45em]
Root of Jesse,\\
flower in the cradling bud,\\
take us to you,\\
keep us with you in God,\\
his together.\\[0.45em]
All-chaste lily\\
rising despite the thorn,\\
strengthen, help us,\\
so feeble, soon forlorn,\\
great protectress.}

\bilingualprayer{Flower of Carmel (continued)}{Continuation of the same received Latin and English witness; source and use status are stated on the preceding card.}{%
Armatura\\
fortis pugnantium\\
furunt bella\\
tende praesidium\\
scapularis.\\[0.45em]
Per incerta\\
prudens consilium\\
per adversa\\
iuge solatium\\
largiaris.\\[0.45em]
Mater dulcis\\
Carmeli domina,\\
plebem tuam\\
reple laetitia\\
qua bearis.\\[0.45em]
Paradisi\\
clavis et ianua,\\
fac nos duci\\
quo, Mater, gloria\\
coronaris.}{%
Be our armor,\\
valiant for Christ when war\\
rages ’round us,\\
hold high the scapular,\\
strong and saving.\\[0.45em]
In our stumbling\\
guide us on God’s wise way,\\
in our sorrow\\
comfort us as we pray,\\
rich your mercy.\\[0.45em]
Holy Lady,\\
Carmel’s great friend and queen,\\
feast your people\\
from your own bliss, the unseen\\
grace, God’s goodness.\\[0.45em]
Key and gateway\\
opening on Paradise,\\
mother, win us\\
a place with you in Christ,\\
crowned in glory.}
