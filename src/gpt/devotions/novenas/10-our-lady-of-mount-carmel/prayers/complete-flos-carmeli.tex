\carmellatinprayer{Flower of Carmel}{Received medieval Carmelite sequence using the \textit{esto propitia} variant; Simon Stock attribution uncertain. No English text is supplied.}{%
Flos Carmeli,\\
vitis florígera,\\
splendor cæli,\\
Virgo puérpera\\
singuláris.\\[0.45em]
Mater mitis,\\
sed viri néscia,\\
Carmélitis\\
esto propítia,\\
Stella maris.\\[0.45em]
Radix Iesse,\\
gérminans flósculum,\\
hic adésse\\
me tibi sérvulum\\
patiáris.\\[0.45em]
Inter spinas\\
quæ crescis lílium,\\
serva puras\\
mentes fragílium,\\
tuteláris.\\[0.45em]
Armatúra\\
fortis pugnántium,\\
furunt bella:\\
tende præsídium\\
scapuláris.\\[0.45em]
Per incérta\\
prudens consílium,\\
per advérsa\\
iuge solátium\\
largiáris.\\[0.45em]
Mater dulcis,\\
Carméli dómina,\\
plebem tuam\\
reple lætítia\\
qua beáris.\\[0.45em]
Paradísi\\
clavis et iánua,\\
fac nos duci\\
quo, Mater, glória\\
coronáris. Amen.}{%
Best's 1902 public-domain English extends only through the first two stanzas. No complete English witness with both adequate provenance and clear reuse rights was located, so no complete English is reproduced.}
