Flower of Carmel,\\
vine in blossom,\\
splendour of heaven,\\
Virgin Mother,\\
alone beyond compare.\\[0.45em]
Gentle Mother,\\
knowing no man,\\
be gracious\\
to the children of Carmel,\\
Star of the Sea.\\[0.45em]
Root of Jesse,\\
bearing the little Flower,\\
permit me,\\
thy little servant,\\
to stand beside thee.\\[0.45em]
Lily rising\\
amid the thorns,\\
O protecting Lady,\\
keep the hearts of the frail\\
pure.\\[0.45em]
Strong armor\\
of those who struggle,\\
while battles rage,\\
extend over us\\
the Scapular's protection.\\[0.45em]
Through uncertainty\\
grant prudent counsel;\\
through adversity\\
bestow unfailing\\
solace.\\[0.45em]
Gentle Mother,\\
Lady of Carmel,\\
fill thy people\\
with the joy\\
that blesses thee.\\[0.45em]
Key and gate\\
of Paradise,\\
bring us, O Mother,\\
where thou art crowned\\
in glory. Amen.}
