\bilingualprayer{Blessed Virgin Mary, Gate of Heaven}{Received liturgical collect from formulary 46 of the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; Latin approved for its proper liturgical context and printed here devotionally, with the book's abbreviated conclusion expanded conventionally; project English translation. This novena is not approved.}{%
Deus, qui Fílium tuum óstium salútis et vitæ constituísti benígnus, præsta, qu\'{\ae}sumus, ut, præveniénte beáta Vírgine María, in Christi dilectióne maneámus fidéles et cæléstis civitátis nobis páteant límina. Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti, Deus, per ómnia s\'{\ae}cula sæculórum. Amen.}{%
O God, who in thy goodness didst establish thy Son as the door of salvation and life, grant, we beseech thee, that, with the Blessed Virgin Mary going before us, we may remain faithful in the love of Christ and the thresholds of the heavenly city may stand open to us. Who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.}
