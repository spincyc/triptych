\bilingualprayer{Proper collect for Day 9}{Project-composed Latin prayer and project English translation; not an approved liturgical collect.}{%
Deus, qui peregrinántem Ecclésiam ad montem sanctum, Christum Fílium tuum, perdúcis: præsta ut, beátæ Maríæ Matris et Patrónæ nostræ précibus susténti, in grátia tua usque in finem perseverémus, defúnctis misericórdiam implorémus, et ad civitátem tuam sine fine perveniámus. Per eúndem Christum Dóminum nostrum. Amen.}{%
O God, who leadest thy pilgrim Church toward the holy mountain, Christ thy Son: grant that, sustained by the prayers of Blessed Mary our Mother and Patroness, we may persevere in thy grace unto the end, implore mercy for the departed, and attain thy city without end. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.}
