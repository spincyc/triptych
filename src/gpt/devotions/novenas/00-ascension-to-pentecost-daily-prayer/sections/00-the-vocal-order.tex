
\section{The Vocal Order}

\recitationcompanionnotice{The First Novena: From Ascension to Pentecost}

\subsection{Days 1--8}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Make the Sign of the Cross.
\item Name the novena intention and surrender its outcome to the Father's will.
\item Pray the Daily Invocation and Collect.
\item Pray the received collect appointed for the day.
\item Pray the complete \textit{Veni Creator Spiritus}. The full guide permits its first stanza when time is short; the complete hymn remains the preferred form.
\item Pray the Lord's Prayer and the Lesser Doxology.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Day 9}

Follow the same order, but after the complete \textit{Veni Creator Spiritus} add the complete \textit{Veni Sancte Spiritus}; then conclude with the Lord's Prayer and the Lesser Doxology.
