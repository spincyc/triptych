\subsection{Days 1--8}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Make the Sign of the Cross.
\item Name the novena intention and surrender its outcome to the Father's wisdom.
\item Pray the short \textit{Flos Carmeli}.
\item Pray the received collect selected for the day.
\item Pray the Hail Mary and the Lord's Prayer.
\item Pray the Collect of the Feast and the Lesser Doxology.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Day 9}

Follow the same order, but replace the short \textit{Flos Carmeli} with the complete sequence.

\appendix
\section{Scope and Status}

This booklet is the condensed recitation companion to \textit{The Novena of Our Lady of Mount Carmel}. It supplies only the vocal order and complete prayers; the full guide controls the calendar, Carmelite and Scapular status, sources, translation and rights status, and review record.

The assembly is a private, non-liturgical pious exercise, not an official Carmelite novena, liturgical book, enrollment rite, or ecclesiastically approved text. Its principal placement is 7--15 July before the 16 July memorial; that devotional schedule neither transfers a liturgical observance nor overrides a Carmelite or local calendar.

Every vocal text is imported without abridgment from the canonical fragments audited by the full guide. The daily Latin collects come from the 1987 \textit{Collectio Missarum de Beata Maria Virgine}; their approved 2012 English is cited but not reproduced, and English appears elsewhere only from the identified received witnesses. Approval attached to a source text does not approve this assembly.
