
\section{The Vocal Order}

\recitationcompanionnotice{The Novena of Our Lady of Mount Carmel}

\subsection{Days 1--8}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Make the Sign of the Cross.
\item Name the novena intention and surrender its outcome to the Father's wisdom.
\item Pray the short \textit{Flos Carmeli}.
\item Pray the received collect selected for the day.
\item Pray the Hail Mary and the Lord's Prayer.
\item Pray the Collect of the Feast and the Lesser Doxology.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Day 9}

Follow the same order, but replace the short \textit{Flos Carmeli} with the complete sequence.
