{\scriptsize\noindent\textbf{1962 order.} White; votive Mass of II class; \latin{Gloria} is said and, ordinarily, no \latin{Credo}. When the blessing is permitted on a Sunday or day of I class, the occurring Mass replaces this formulary, its Collect commemorates the nuptial Mass under one conclusion, and the nuptial blessing remains. All Souls and the Sacred Triduum prohibit both commemoration and blessing.}

\begin{nuptialproperstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Deus Israel coniungat vos}; \latin{Beati omnes qui timent Dominum} (Tob. 7:15; 8:19; Ps. 127:1)}{God joins; fear walks}{Tobias and Sarah's mercy enters the pilgrim psalm: divine joining becomes a common way of blessing.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{Exaudi nos}: what human ministry performs, let God's blessing \latin{potius impleatur}}{Principal and ministerial agency}{The rite asks that its visible ministry be fulfilled more deeply by the divine cause.}
\properrow{Epistle}{Eph. 5:22--33; \latin{Christus dilexit Ecclesiam, et seipsum tradidit pro ea}}{Christ and the Church}{The one-flesh text is governed by Christ's self-oblation, baptismal washing, nourishment, and holy Bride.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{Uxor tua sicut vitis abundans}; children as olive shoots around the table (Ps. 127:3)}{Vine, olives, house, table}{The primary procreative / educative end appears as gift and vocation, ordered through Sion to worship and peace.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Mittat vobis Dominus auxilium de sancto: et de Sion tueatur vos} (Ps. 19:3)}{Help from sanctuary and Sion}{The couple's natural society is placed inside the worship and guardianship of God's people.}
\properrow{Seasonal chant}{After Septuagesima: Tract, Ps. 127:4--6. Paschaltide: Ps. 19:3 and 133:3, \latin{qui fecit caelum et terram}}{Fear, Sion, Jerusalem, creation}{The same nuptial blessing is heard penitentially as persevering fear or paschally under the risen Creator.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Matt. 19:3--6; \latin{ab initio}; \latin{una caro}; \latin{Quod ergo Deus coniunxit}}{Creation and indissolubility}{Christ answers the divorce test by returning to the Creator's beginning and present act of joining.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{In te speravi}; \latin{in manibus tuis tempora mea} (Ps. 30:15--16)}{Trust and all one's times}{The spouses' future is entrusted where the Church places bread and wine under Christ's unique sacrifice.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{pro sacra connubii lege munus oblatum}; \latin{largitor} and \latin{dispositor}}{Gift, law, and ordering}{God, giver of the marital work, is asked to order both the offering and the life it accompanies.}
\properrow{Nuptial prayers}{\latin{Propitiare, Domine}: \latin{te auctore} / \latin{te auxiliante}. \latin{Deus, qui potestate}: creation, Christ--Church, matriarchs, heaven}{Author, ends, and whole economy}{After the Pater propagation, spousal aid / chastity, and heavenly rest are ordered beneath divine causality.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Ecce sic benedicetur}; descendants; \latin{pax super Israel} (Ps. 127:4, 6)}{Blessing reaches Israel's peace}{At Communion domestic blessing opens into ecclesial peace rather than closing upon the household.}
\properrow{Post-\linebreak communion}{\latin{instituta providentiae}; \latin{legitima societate connectis}; \latin{longaeva pace custodias}}{Providence, bond, perseverance}{The joining becomes a stable social relation requiring God's lifelong custody and peace.}
\properrow{Final blessing / admonition}{\latin{Deus Abraham, Deus Isaac, et Deus Iacob}; generations and \latin{vitam aeternam sine fine}; fear, mutual love / fidelity, chastity, Catholic education, aspersion}{Patriarchs, household rule, final beatitude}{After \latin{Ite, missa est}, temporal generations yield to life without end; the admonition orders primary and secondary ends into a lived rule.}
\end{nuptialproperstable}

\begin{sensestable}
Literal & Tobias and Sarah receive mercy; the psalms bless a God-fearing household; Paul exhorts wives and husbands; Christ appeals to creation against divorce; the Roman prayers bless this bridegroom and bride.\\
Allegorical & The baptized Church, formed from the opened side of the New Adam and nourished by him, is the Bride for whom Christ hands himself over; sacramental marriage effectively signifies that indivisible covenant.\\
Moral & Spouses receive generation and education as marriage's primary objective end and live its intrinsic secondary ends in mutual aid, fidelity, chastity, prayer, peace, and hospitality, without coercion or domination.\\
Anagogical & Sion, Jerusalem, generations, old age, and earthly peace are gathered into the blessing's explicit heavenly rest, celestial kingdom, and life without end---the marriage supper to which the temporal sign points.\\
\end{sensestable}
