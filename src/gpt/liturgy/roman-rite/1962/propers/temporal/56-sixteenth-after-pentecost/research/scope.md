## Organized cross-proper argument

Strongest argument: divine mercy sustains an active, embodied and communal
life whose growth is received rather than possessed as a title to precedence.
The formulary's fullness and glory do not remove need: Paul prays amid
affliction, the guests learn humility, the Church asks purification in her
offering and Communion, and the aging voice continues to need God. This is
an integrated editorial conclusion from the following checked relations, not
an account of a compiler's programme.

The first five units below are the **five settled source-grounded claims**.
Each is a functional unit for the synthesis companion's integrated commentary,
not a run through the appointed order. All ten elements materially contribute
to the whole. The two-page Themes and Movement may compress these into four
units by treating SG-1 and SG-5 together, while preserving their distinct
opening and lifelong horizons. The fuller Source-Grounded Synthesis should
develop the implications and tensions rather than repeat the same paragraphs
or the element-by-element sweep.

### SG-1 — Grace enables the action it commands

- **Elements:** Collect, Epistle, Secret, Communion, Gospel.
- **Basis and mechanism:** THE-001 and THE-008 join preceding/following grace,
  divine inward power, and being made fit for participation. THE-005 gives
  that enabled activity concrete direction in mercy and relinquished rank.
  SCR-002 and SCR-016 hold the request within the one Spirit's dwelling and
  fruitful common life, not an isolated interior sensation.
- **Reception to develop:** Augustine on Psalm70 §§18–21 (PAT-007) says that
  God does not merely show a road and become dispensable. Aquinas,
  *Super Ephesios* 3.4 (PAT-008), joins knees and weak dependence to the gift
  of perseverance. Chrysostom VII (PAT-002–003) insists on faith and love
  while explaining gifts exceeding prior request. LIT-007 places the
  Secret's purification before participation and continued renewal after it.
- **Distinctive payoff:** the prayed-for good work, the received strength,
  and the requested sacramental fitness explain one another without turning
  either activity or Communion into a certificate of self-sufficiency.
- **Control:** the prayers support real action enabled by mercy. They do not
  supply an exhaustive account of operative/cooperative grace, merit,
  validity, reception requirements, or transubstantiation. COV-006's doctrinal
  leads were inspected as records only; they are not needed to inflate this
  bounded claim into a technical treatise.

### SG-2 — Mercy and humility reshape the giving of honor

- **Elements:** Gospel, Collect, Epistle, Offertory, Communion.
- **Basis and mechanism:** THE-002 and THE-005; SCR-005, SCR-009–010,
  SCR-018. Jesus heals before assent is supplied and then corrects the
  guests' own selection of precedence. The host's conferred honor remains;
  Pauline glory includes affliction for others and ends in ecclesial praise
  of God. Remembrance of God's justice and appeal against destructive
  enemies keep humility from becoming either complacency or moral ranking.
- **Reception to develop:** Cyril CI–CII (PAT-010–011) brings together divine
  mercy, holy action and truthful self-knowledge. Ambrose VII.195 (PAT-012)
  explains Christ's gentle correction of ambition. Sales III.5 (PAT-023)
  exposes taking the last place to obtain greater honor and also refuses
  false humility that obstructs charity. Augustine's Letter140 §§63–64
  (PAT-024) relates charity to common life and enemies.
- **Distinctive payoff:** relinquishing self-promotion and acting for another's
  good are compatible expressions of grace-enabled life; humility need not
  erase a good received from someone else.
- **Control:** distinguish honor before dinner companions from God's
  doxological glory; preserve the afflicted apostle. No promise of social
  promotion, generic accusation against Jews or Pharisees, literal diagnosis
  of dropsy as vice, or identification of the psalm's enemies with the meal's
  guests is warranted. The more searching shame/self-correction conjunction
  remains exploratory IP-5.

### SG-3 — The inward dwelling opens into a people of praise

- **Elements:** Epistle, Gradual, Alleluia, Introit, Communion.
- **Basis and mechanism:** THE-004; SCR-001–004, SCR-008, SCR-011–013,
  SCR-016, SCR-018–019. The Pauline prayer is Trinitarian and communal:
  Christ dwells in hearts, comprehension is with all saints, glory is in
  the Church. The Gradual's rebuilt Sion and nations and the Alleluia's
  universal song develop a public horizon already present in the complete
  Introit and Communion psalms.
- **Reception to develop:** Augustine's Church-building and future judgement
  in Psalm101 (PAT-004) beside Theodoret's explanation of why restoration
  does not exhaust universal conversion (PAT-015), and Bellarmine's present
  building/future appearance (PAT-020). PAT-005, PAT-016 and PAT-021 explain
  new song as new life, worship and Christ's works. PRE-006 and PRE-009
  locate a direct indwelling–Sion–praise relation in the corrected
  *Liturgical Year* continuation pp.359,363.
- **Distinctive payoff:** the scale of charity is not the enlargement of a
  solitary self; the prayer's inward gift and the chants' assembled praise
  express a common divine work.
- **Control:** city, heart, Church and nations retain their textual referents.
  The sources' actual ecclesial interpretation is attributed, with no
  invented political programme or abolition of Israel. Pauline dimensions
  receive Chrysostom's immensity reading and Augustine/Aquinas's Cross
  reading as distinct explanations, not a hidden architectural code.

### SG-4 — Purification concerns the whole embodied person

- **Elements:** Gospel, Epistle, Secret, Postcommunion, Alleluia.
- **Basis and mechanism:** THE-003 and THE-008; SCR-004–005, SCR-008–009.
  Bodily healing in the house, strengthening of the inner person by the
  Spirit, cleansing through the present sacrifice, and heavenly mysteries'
  renewal with present/future bodily assistance give distinct concrete
  forms to the divine help sought.
- **Reception to develop:** Cyril CI (PAT-010) on holy works and mercy;
  Augustine on Psalm97 §1 (PAT-005) on bodily and inward healing, retaining
  his Nain context; Aquinas *Super Ephesios* 3.5 (PAT-009) on knowing
  without containing God; the continuation pp.370–371 (LIT-007) on the
  Secret and the body's present/future good. LIT-001 and LIT-003 supply
  actual historical pairing of the sacramental prayers, without proving
  the history of their connection to this Gospel.
- **Distinctive payoff:** the prayers' interior language does not make bodily
  need irrelevant, and the bodily miracle does not exhaust the fullness for
  which Paul prays.
- **Control:** do not identify Eucharistic reception with the immediate cure
  narrated in Luke, or participation in divine gifts with identity of divine
  essence. The final bodily horizon is supported, but its detailed doctrine
  is not supplied by this juxtaposition alone. The pathological swelling
  contrast goes to IP-1, not this established claim.

### SG-5 — The gift remains necessary across a lifetime

- **Elements:** Introit, Offertory, Communion, Collect, Postcommunion, Epistle.
- **Basis and mechanism:** THE-006 with the restrained textual content of
  THE-007; SCR-006–007, SCR-014–015, SCR-017, SCR-019. All-day prayer,
  renewed rescue, remembrance from youth to age, preceding/following grace,
  and present/future help coexist with abundant power and glory through
  generations. Continued petition after Communion is consistent with
  received help, not a narrative that the earlier prayers failed.
- **Reception to develop:** Augustine's Psalm85 prayer through successive
  generations (PAT-001), Psalm39 suffering members and physician (PAT-006),
  and Psalm70 conversion/perseverance (PAT-007); Theodoret's threatened
  Church (PAT-017) and different law-to-Church reading of age (PAT-018);
  Bellarmine's personal testimony (PAT-022). Compare the actual distinctions
  instead of combining all speakers into one biography.
- **Distinctive payoff:** a received history of mercy gives reasons to ask
  again and to transmit praise; need does not refute divine abundance.
- **Control:** no prosperity, protection-from-every-harm, longevity or
  biological-rejuvenation promise. The new-song/old-age paradox and the
  coordination of different temporal scales are developed as IP-6 and IP-2,
  preserving discovery beyond this source-grounded account.
