\weektitle{Sixteenth Sunday after Pentecost}{DOMINICA DECIMA SEXTA post Pentecosten}{1962 Roman Missal \quad II classis \quad Grace, humility, and the fullness of charity}
\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Miserére mihi, Dómine} (Ps.~85:3,5; v.~1)}{Mercy and need}{The all-day cry rests on God's abundant mercy; poverty becomes confident prayer.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{Tua nos, quǽsumus, Dómine, grátia}}{Grace and good works}{Grace goes before and follows, sustaining the active life the Church asks to lead.}
\properrow{Epistle}{Eph.~3:13--21}{Indwelling and charity}{Paul kneels amid affliction, asking the Father for strength through the Spirit and Christ's dwelling with all the saints.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{Timébunt gentes} (Ps.~101:16--17)}{Sion and glory}{God builds Sion; nations and kings recognize his glory. Inward strength opens into common praise.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Cantáte Dómino cánticum novum} (Ps.~97:1)}{Wonders and new song}{God's wonderful deeds summon praise before the Gospel shows Christ relieving bodily distress.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Luke~14:1--11; Credo}{Mercy and received honor}{Jesus heals on the Sabbath, then corrects the guests' pursuit of the first places. The inviter confers honor.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{Dómine, in auxílium meum réspice} (Ps.~39:14--15, selected)}{Rescue from harm}{The repeated appeal asks protection and the confounding of those seeking the petitioner's life.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{Munda nos}; Trinity Preface}{Purification and participation}{The present sacrifice cleanses; divine mercy is asked to make the people fit to share in it.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Dómine, memorábor} (Ps.~70:16--18, selected)}{Justice and lifelong help}{Remembrance of God's justice accompanies the request not to be abandoned in old age.}
\properrow{Postcommunion}{\latin{Purífica, quǽsumus, Dómine}}{Renewal, now and hereafter}{Heavenly mysteries renew the mind, and the prayer seeks bodily assistance in both present and future life.}
\end{properstable}
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & Needy petition, Paul's prayer for his Gentile hearers, Sion's restoration, universal praise, Christ's Sabbath cure and seating instruction, and the Church's purification and renewal.\\
Allegorical & Augustine's psalm expositions (abridged NPNF) hear Christ and the Church; Augustine, Theodoret and Bellarmine read Sion ecclesially. Augustine and Aquinas unfold Paul's dimensions through the charity of the Cross.\\
Moral & Mercy relieves distress; humility receives honor without grasping it. Grace sustains good works and the perseverance taught by Augustine, Cyril and Aquinas.\\
Anagogical & Paul's ecclesial doxology reaches all generations; Augustine (abridged NPNF) and Bellarmine see Christ's glorious judgement in the Gradual. The Postcommunion seeks bodily help hereafter.\\
\end{sensestable}
