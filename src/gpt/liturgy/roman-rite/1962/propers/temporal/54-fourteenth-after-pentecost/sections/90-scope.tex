\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}
\begin{studyframe}
\textbf{Edition and occasion.} The recurring \latin{Dominica Decima Quarta
post Pentecosten}, II class, in the 1962 Vatican typical Roman Missal.
Civil-date occurrence, commemorations, and particular-calendar precedence
belong to assembly. This study is not an official liturgical book.

\textbf{Appointed text.} The Latin, rank, headings, boundaries, references,
Creed, and Trinity Preface follow the Vatican typical edition. The
August~26, 2026 collation record was corrected from the September~5 image
checks; Benziger's differing Offertory punctuation and Secret conclusion
remain separate edition readings. Public-domain
Douay--Rheims/Challoner Scripture and Cummiskey's anonymous 1861 oration
English are historical witnesses. Their differences from the appointed Latin
remain visible beside the texts.

\textbf{Sources and bounds.} The September~5, 2026 research brief supplies
complete biblical contexts, the named reception loci, edition-mediated
liturgical history, five cultural cases, and six proposal audits. The sweep
is selective. Latin and Greek reception is paraphrased; English reception
retains the identified translation or its stated uncertainty. Patristic
passages embedded in Hugh remain mediated attributions. Anthony's medieval
Sunday assignments differ from the 1962 arrangement. The chronology table
reports the corpus's profiles, alternatives, and unresolved distinctions.

\textbf{Afterlife and exploration.} The five English-language cultural cases
all reuse Gospel language; the bounded search supplied no additional
qualified case for the other appointed texts. This does not establish an
absence across other languages or unsurveyed works. None of the five cases
establishes dependence on this particular Mass. The six proposals retain
local precedents and external near analogues without claiming universal
novelty or recovered redactor intention.

\textbf{Rights and record.} Protected modern texts are paraphrased or quoted
briefly where needed for comparison; third-party works retain their rights.
Full appointed texts and the element-by-element commentary belong to the
research edition; the synthesis edition carries integrated commentary from
the same canonical leaf. Source checks and their extents are recorded in
\nolinkurl{propers/verified.md} and \nolinkurl{research/scope.md}.
\end{studyframe}
