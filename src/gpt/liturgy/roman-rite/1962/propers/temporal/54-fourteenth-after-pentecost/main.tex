\weektitle{Fourteenth Sunday after Pentecost}{DOMINICA DECIMA QUARTA post Pentecosten}{1962 Roman Missal propers: undivided service, Spirit-led freedom, filial providence, Eucharistic purification, and the kingdom sought first}

\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Protéctor noster, áspice, Deus} (Ps.~83:10--11, 2--3)}{Protector, Christ, courts}{The assembly asks God to behold the face of his Christ and declares one day in God's courts better than thousands.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{Ecclésiam tuam propitiatióne perpétua}}{Mercy directs mortal life}{Because unaided mortality slips, the Church asks perpetual favor, withdrawal from harms, and direction toward saving things.}
\properrow{Epistle}{Gal.~5:16--24}{Spirit against disordered flesh}{Paul contrasts works issuing from disordered desire with the Spirit's one fruit and the crucifixion of passions in those who belong to Christ.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{Bonum est confídere in Dómino} (Ps.~117:8--9)}{Final trust in God}{Human and princely help is relativized without denying the created instruments through which providence may work.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Veníte, exsultémus Dómino} (Ps.~94:1)}{Joyful approach}{The call to praise moves the assembly toward the Gospel and altar under the psalm's larger summons to obedient hearing.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Matt.~6:24--33; Credo}{One master; the kingdom first}{Christ rejects divided service and anxious mastery, then grounds confidence in the Father's knowledge and orders necessities beneath the kingdom.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{Immíttet Angelus Dómini} (Ps.~33:8--9)}{Encampment, tasting, hope}{Angelic protection surrounds those who fear God; tasting divine goodness leads to hope as the gifts are prepared.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{hæc hóstia salutáris}}{Victim, purification, propitiation}{The Church asks that the saving victim purify sins and stand as propitiation before divine power.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Primum quǽrite regnum Dei} (Matt.~6:33)}{Priority repeated}{Reception is governed by the Gospel's command: seek the kingdom first; necessities are added, not enthroned.}
\properrow{Postcommunion}{\latin{Puríficent semper et múniant}}{Purified, strengthened, led}{The sacraments are asked always to purify and strengthen the recipients and lead them to perpetual salvation's effect.}
\end{properstable}
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & Pilgrims desire God's courts; Paul contrasts works of the flesh with the Spirit's fruit; Christ orders food and clothing beneath the Father's kingdom; the Church asks preservation, purification, and final direction.\\
Allegorical & Eusebius and Augustine receive the anointed face christologically; Augustine names Christ as the encircling Angel and reads tasting Eucharistically; the Secret names the saving victim.\\
Moral & Walk by the Spirit, refuse mammon's mastery, rely finally on God while receiving created help, work without anxious servitude, and seek the kingdom before every subordinate good.\\
Anagogical & Augustine's everlasting day, Matthew's kingdom sought first, the Spirit's first fruits of glory, and the Postcommunion's perpetual salvation join present worship to the final end.\\
\end{sensestable}
