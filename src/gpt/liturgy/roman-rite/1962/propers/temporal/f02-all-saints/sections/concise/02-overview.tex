\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & John describes God's sealed servants and their praise; Jesus promises happiness to his disciples; the prayers seek mercy and protection.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & Christ gathers one body across nations and death. His humility and victory form God's children, who praise through him with heaven.\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Upright praise requires submission to God; humility, mercy, purity, and reconciliation describe the conversion still needed by those celebrating the saints.\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & The saints' peace and the promised sight of God anticipate completed communion and bodily resurrection; present participation still needs perseverance.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
