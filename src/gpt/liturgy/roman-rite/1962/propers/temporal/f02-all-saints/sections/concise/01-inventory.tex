\begin{properinventory}{Proper}{Text and meaning}
Introit & \work{Gaudeamus}, rejoicing in the Lord; Ps.~32:1 (33:1). Angels praise the Son; praise befits the upright.\\
Collect & \work{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus}. One feast honors all the saints; the living seek mercy through many intercessors.\\
Sunday Collect & \work{Absolve, quaesumus}. God's people ask release from sins contracted through frailty.\\
Lesson & Apoc.~7:2--12. The sealed tribes and an uncountable multitude from every nation worship God and the Lamb.\\
Gradual & Ps.~33:10,11b (34:10,11b). The saints fear God; those seeking him will not lack good.\\
Alleluia & Matt.~11:28. Christ invites the burdened to receive refreshment.\\
Gospel & Matt.~5:1--12a. Jesus blesses humility, mercy, purity, peace, and fidelity under persecution.\\
Offertory & Wis.~3:1--3, selected and adapted. The righteous dead remain in God's hand and peace.\\
Secret & \work{Munera tibi, Domine}. Offerings honor all the just; the offerers ask saving benefit through mercy.\\
Sunday Secret & \work{Pro nostrae servitutis}. The sacrifice of praise asks increased service and completion of an unmerited gift.\\
Common Preface & \work{Vere dignum}. Thanksgiving through Christ seeks admission among the heavenly praises.\\
Communion & Matt.~5:8--10. Purity, peacemaking, and persecution for justice: sight of God, sonship, and kingdom.\\
Postcommunion & \work{Da, quaesumus}. The faithful ask lasting joy in the saints and their continual supplication.\\
Sunday Postcommunion & \work{Quaesumus, omnipotens}. Those sharing in divine gifts ask protection from human dangers.\\
\end{properinventory}
