\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Apoc 5:12; 1:6; Ps 71:1 (72) & Heavenly acclaim of the slain Lamb; royal judgment sought from God.\\
Collect & Composed oration & Sin's division among human families calls for Christ's healing dominion.\\
Epistle & Col 1:12--20 & Rescue into the Son's kingdom; creation and reconciliation through him.\\
Gradual & Ps 71:8,11 (72) & Worldwide dominion and homage; the surrounding psalm describes rescue of the needy.\\
Alleluia & Dan 7:14, final clauses & The Son of Man's kingdom endures beyond destruction.\\
Gospel & John 18:33--37 & Before Pilate, Jesus identifies his kingdom's origin and the response of hearing.\\
Offertory & Ps 2:8 & The Father commands the Son to ask and promises the nations as his inheritance.\\
Secret & Composed oration & The Father is asked that the sacrificial Son give unity and peace to the peoples.\\
Preface & Proper of Christ the King & The eternal Priest and universal King offers himself on the Cross.\\
Communion & Ps 28:10--11 (29), selected clauses & The eternal King's blessing is peace for his people.\\
Postcommunion & Composed oration & Those already nourished and serving under Christ's standard ask to reign with him in heaven.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
