\section{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}
The universal 1962 feast of Christ the King, I class, falls on 25 October 2026, with Gloria, Creed, proper Preface and no displaced-Sunday prayers. Local privileged occurrences are excluded. The expansive study retains the inactive seasonal votive alternatives. Psalm references give Vulgate numbering, then Hebrew in parentheses. The Introit's petition is Psalm 71:1 in the Missal but 71:2 in the American-1899 Douay delivery, whose title occupies verse 1.

The readings synthesize the witnesses' arguments about particular texts; they add no independent research. The canonical expansive study, \emph{Hearing the King and Receiving His Peace}, supplies full Latin and historical Lasance--Walsh study English, detailed commentary and page 2's historical evidence. This companion contains commentary, not full propers or substitute translations; historical English is not approved for celebration.

Research bounds: \texttt{research/scope.md} and \texttt{propers/verified.md}. Augustine's NPNF Psalm 71 has omissions; Victorinus has attribution and interpolation qualifications.
