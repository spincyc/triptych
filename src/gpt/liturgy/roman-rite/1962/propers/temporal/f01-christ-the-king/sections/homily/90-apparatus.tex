\section{Note on Sources and Delivery}
For an adult parish assembly celebrating Our Lord Jesus Christ the King in the 1962 Roman Missal on 25 October 2026. The spoken text ends before this note. It develops the accepted study's interpretation \emph{The King Makes a People Capable of Hearing Him}, with the compatible account of sacrificial peace and its demands from \emph{The Creator Restores Communion by Giving Himself}. Augustine explains the kingdom's origin and grace-enabled hearing; Chrysostom explains fitness for the inheritance and the demand for peace among the Church's members. The connections to the Secret, the trial's place before the Passion and the response to a resented correction are the homily's synthesis and application. The Secret addresses the Father and asks that the sacrificial Son give unity and peace. The Postcommunion asks for heavenly reign for those already nourished who glory in service; the homily relates their service to present perseverance.

The speech contains 1,259 words, counted from its Pandoc plain-text rendering. At an unhurried 110--125 words per minute, its calculated duration is 10.1--11.4 minutes. The prose has been read silently for sense, transitions and oral clarity; no audible rehearsal or timed human delivery is claimed. The correction example is an application, not a reported experience or attributed patristic anecdote. No newly composed prayer is offered for recitation.
