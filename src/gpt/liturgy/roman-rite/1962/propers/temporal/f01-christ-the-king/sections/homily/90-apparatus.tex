\section{Note on Sources and Delivery}
For an adult parish assembly celebrating Our Lord Jesus Christ the King in the 1962 Roman Missal on 25 October 2026. The spoken text ends before this note. It develops the accepted study's reading \emph{The King Makes a People Capable of Hearing Him}, with the compatible account of sacrificial peace and its demands from \emph{The Creator Restores Communion by Giving Himself}. Augustine's distinction between the kingdom's origin and presence turns the opening question about power toward grace-enabled hearing; the Epistle and the Secret connect that hearing to received reconciliation.

The speech contains 1,276 words, approximately 10--12 minutes at an unhurried 110--125 words per minute. This is a calculated estimate. The prose has been read silently for sense, transitions and oral clarity; no audible rehearsal or timed human delivery is claimed. The practical examples are applications, not reported experiences or attributed patristic anecdotes. No newly composed prayer is offered for recitation.
