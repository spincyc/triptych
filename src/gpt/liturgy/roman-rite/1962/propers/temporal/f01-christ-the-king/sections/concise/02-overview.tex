\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & Jesus answers Pilate; Colossians gives thanks for deliverance and names the Son as Creator; the prayers ask for unity and shared reign.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & The incarnate King gathers the Church through grace and sacrifice; the Creator reconciles humanity with God and the angels.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Hearing becomes obedient faith; received peace reaches the helpless and the neighbor with whom one maintains a feud.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & Pilgrims hope for enduring reign with Christ; universal acknowledgment at judgment remains distinct from willing, saving communion.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
