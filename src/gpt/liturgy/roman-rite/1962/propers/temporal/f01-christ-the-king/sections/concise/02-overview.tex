\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & A royal interrogation stands beside thanksgiving for deliverance, the Son's creative primacy, royal psalms and petitions for unity.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & The incarnate King makes a people through grace and sacrifice; the Creator reconciles humanity with heavenly communion.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Hearing becomes obedient faith; received peace reaches the helpless and the neighbor with whom one maintains a feud.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & Pilgrims hope for enduring reign with Christ; universal acknowledgment at judgment remains distinct from willing, saving communion.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
