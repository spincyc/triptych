\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Jer 29:11,12,14; Ps 84:2 (Hebrew 85) & God promises peace and gathering; his people call upon him.\\
Collect & \textit{Praesta, quaesumus} & Thought is to become words and deeds pleasing to God.\\
Epistle & 1 Thess 1:2--10 & Faith received in affliction becomes an example; the risen Son is awaited.\\
Gradual & Ps 43:8--9 (Hebrew 44) & Rescue is God's gift; his people promise enduring praise.\\
Alleluia & Ps 129:1--2 (Hebrew 130) & A cry from the depths asks for a hearing.\\
Gospel & Matt 13:31--35 & Mustard seed and hidden leaven describe the kingdom's growth.\\
Offertory & Ps 129:1--2 (Hebrew 130) & The cry returns at the offering, with its opening repeated.\\
Secret & \textit{Haec nos oblatio} & The offering is asked to cleanse, renew, govern and protect the people.\\
Preface & Of the Most Holy Trinity & Thanksgiving confesses distinct persons, one essence and equal majesty.\\
Communion & Mark 11:24 & Christ assures those who pray of God's hearing.\\
Postcommunion & \textit{Caelestibus, Domine} & Those already nourished ask for enduring desire for heavenly gifts.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
