\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & Seed grows and leaven permeates meal. Paul thanks God for exemplary converts. The prayers ask for faithful conduct, renewal and lasting desire.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Christ makes the Gospel fruitful in his Church; his sacrifice grounds the sinner's hope. The assembly worships the Father, Son and Holy Spirit.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Faith becomes work and costly charity. Understanding, anger and desire are directed toward virtue; those asking mercy forgive others.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & The risen Son is awaited. The Church hopes for resurrection and complete deliverance, beyond the present reach of its preaching and renewal.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
