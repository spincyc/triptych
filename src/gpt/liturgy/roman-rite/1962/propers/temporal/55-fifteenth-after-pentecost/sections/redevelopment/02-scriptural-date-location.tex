\section*{Scriptural Date and Location}

\begin{dossiertable}
Offertory & Ps.~39:2--4 (modern 40) & Davidic heading; no secure place of composition &
\chronodate{offertory}{Undated in the traditional chronology corpus.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{The Clementine and Challoner titles attribute the psalm to David. Modern Catholic orientation treats verses~2--13 as thanksgiving joined to a later lament, with the rescued speaker proclaiming before an assembly. The sources fix no composition place, first assembly, or episode within David's life.}
\midrule
Introit & Ps.~85:1--4 (modern 86) & Davidic heading; an individual lament before God &
\chronodate{introit}{Undated in the traditional chronology corpus.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{The Clementine and Challoner titles attribute the psalm to David. Modern Catholic orientation reads an individual lament spoken by a poor, oppressed, devoted petitioner. The sources establish no precise compositional horizon, place, first human audience, or episode in David's life.}
\midrule
Gradual & Ps.~91:2--3 (modern 92) & Sabbath thanksgiving; no human author named &
\chronodate{gradual}{Undated in the traditional chronology corpus.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{The biblical title appoints a Sabbath canticle but names no human author. Modern Catholic orientation reads a hymn of praise and thanksgiving spoken by one whom God has protected. The sources establish no secure composition place, first congregation, or authorial life stage.}
\midrule
Alleluia & Ps.~94:3 (modern 95), adapted with Ps.~46:3 & Israel's worship and universal kingship; no composition place &
\chronodate{alleluia}{Undated in the traditional chronology corpus.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{The Clementine title attributes Psalm~94, a song of praise, to David; Psalm~46 is attributed separately to the sons of Core. Modern notes treat the two as corporate kingship or worship hymns. Neither has a secure composition place, original congregation, or authorial life stage, and their distinct titles are not merged.}
\midrule
Gospel & Luke~7:11--16 & Naim in Galilee; traditional Achaia or Boeotia; modern place uncertain &
\chronodate{gospel}{Disputed composition: \chronology{composition.gospel-of-luke}{composition}{About the year 70}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-luke}{composition}{before the end of the Roman imprisonment, when the Acts was finished}.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierevent{Narrated event: after Capharnaum, Christ meets the widow's funeral procession at Naim's gate; the corpus supplies no narrated-event date.}
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Tradition identifies Luke as the Antiochene physician and Pauline companion and associates the Gospel with Achaia or Boeotia. The modern Catholic orientation gives it a later compositional horizon, addresses Theophilus within a largely Gentile, non-Palestinian readership, and leaves the writing place uncertain. Naim belongs to the narrated Galilean ministry rather than the composition geography.}
\midrule
Communion & John~6:52 (modern 6:51b) & Capharnaum discourse; traditional Ephesus; modern place uncertain &
\chronodate{communion}{Disputed composition: \chronology{composition.gospel-of-john}{composition}{from the year 90 to 100 (approximately)}; \chronology{composition.gospel-of-john}{composition}{96 or one of the succeeding years}.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierevent{Narrated setting: the discourse follows signs around the Sea of Galilee and is delivered at Capharnaum near Passover; the corpus supplies no narrated-event date.}
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Tradition places the aged apostle John at Ephesus, writing for later-generation Christians in Asia Minor. The modern Catholic orientation treats the present Gospel as a multistage Johannine product for Christians amid synagogue conflict and supplies no composition city. Capharnaum belongs to the narrated discourse rather than the composition geography.}
\midrule
Epistle & Gal.~5:25--6:10 & Galatian churches; northern and southern audiences disputed &
\chronodate{epistle}{Disputed composition: \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{A.D. 49-50}; \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{about A.D. 53, 54}; \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{(1 and 2 Corinthians; Galatians), 56}; \chronology{composition.epistle-to-the-galatians}{composition}{A.D. 57 or 58}.}\\
\cmidrule(lr){1-4}
\dossierprose{Pauline authorship and a predominantly Gentile-Christian audience are secure. The principal modern Catholic orientation favors northern Galatia around Ancyra and writing from Ephesus during Paul's later missionary labor; the less likely southern model permits an earlier setting. Writer location and recipient geography remain distinct.}
\end{dossiertable}
