\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

This study concerns the recurring \latin{Dominica Decima Quinta post
Pentecosten}, II classis, in the 1962 Vatican typical Missal, printed
pp.~396--397, nos.~1582--1591. Civil-date occurrence, precedence,
commemorations and particular calendars lie outside it. It is a study
companion, not an official liturgical book or a ruling on present use.

The Latin text, rubric and boundaries retain the facsimile collation
recorded in \nolinkurl{propers/verified.md}: Vatican 1962 controls,
Benziger 1962 supplies an independent comparison, and the public-domain
Mame 1922 sequence supplies the recorded United States republication
antecedent. Brief biblical English uses the identified public-domain
Challoner witness; the cultural gallery's comparison is explicitly the
American 1899 edition. Cummiskey's 1861 hand missal supplies a checked
historical English oration witness, with its erroneous XVI running head
resolved by the actual sequence. No complete English oration is reproduced.
These historical translations are not approved 1962 liturgical English.

The passage-by-passage research and section coverage are recorded in
\nolinkurl{research/scope.md}. Complete scriptural contexts and the
identified patristic, medieval, saintly and liturgical loci support the
commentary. Theodoret's target Psalms and Galatians~6 were read in Greek
with facing Latin. Ambrose and Bede were read in continuous Latin
transcriptions; Augustine and Chrysostom's named English witnesses are
historical translations. Cyril's Luke witness includes the editor's
Greek-catena supplements to the Syriac transmission; his John witness is
a separate continuous English commentary. No fresh Syriac or Greek
collation of Cyril is claimed. O'Sullivan omits Bellarmine's philological
discussions and replaces his psalm prefaces with Douay headings.

The chronology follows the generated corpus record, retaining composition
and narrated-event relations and competing source positions. The psalm
annotations give a critical Psalter boundary, not secure individual dates.
Page~2's unestablished geography, audience, life-stage and independent modern
critical particulars remain gaps in the present evidence. The Wilson
sacramentaries establish the prayer group, not the first complete formulary,
its first compiler or the cause of changed Sunday numbering. Schuster's
Lenten reuses remain his observations. The current join supplies no fresh
Venice 1570-dependent historical claim.

The research audit names unexamined continuous Galatians commentaries,
unreached transmission witnesses and bounded negative searches. Its
Notable-and-quotable and Interpretive-proposal audits own the exact gallery
and proposal evidence. The four proposals have precedent in the earlier
version of this guide; the named independent works supply near analogues,
not universal originality claims. Film interpretation rests on custodial
accounts, not a viewing. Protected lyrics, film, modern Bible and
institutional prose appear only through brief identification or paraphrase.
Source works retain their own rights; the project does not relicense them.

The recorded source checks and this guide's interpretation confer no
imprimatur or ecclesiastical approval. The cited sources remain the
substantive authorities; the audit records state the actual extent of
inspection and verification.
