## Dated addition: propagation of the faith

Rubric 453, printed p. XXXII, PDF p. 30, appoints the prayer *Pro Fidei
propagatione* on the penultimate Sunday of October **or another day fixed by
the local Ordinary for the missions**, in all Masses except those ranked
1, 2, 3, or 8 in the precedence table. This Sunday is rank 15 and is not
excepted. Rubrics 444–446 on the same page place this addition under one
conclusion with the Mass prayer and include sung Masses. Rubric 480 (p.
XXXIV, PDF 32) gives Secrets the same number and order as the opening
prayers; rubric 505 (p. XXXV, PDF 33) does likewise for Postcommunions.

## Dated commemoration: St Luke

18 October is also *S. Lucae Evangelistae*, II class, printed pp. 689–690,
PDF pp. 770–771. Under the general precedence table, rubric 91, printed p.
XVII, PDF 15, the II-class Sunday (rank 15) takes precedence over this
universal II-class feast (rank 16). Rubric 95 on that page does not transfer
this II-class feast. Its occurrence may instead yield an ordinary
commemoration as follows.

Rubrics 107–111, printed p. XVIII, PDF 16, distinguish privileged from
ordinary commemorations. St Luke here is ordinary. Rubric 108 admits ordinary
commemorations in conventual Masses and all low Masses; rubric 111(a) admits
only a privileged commemoration in a nonconventual sung Mass. Rubric 111(b)
allows the one II-class feast commemoration on a II-class Sunday when no
privileged commemoration is required. The mission addition is joined under
the Sunday's conclusion and counted with it (rubrics 444–446); it does not
consume the allowance for St Luke's separate commemoration.
