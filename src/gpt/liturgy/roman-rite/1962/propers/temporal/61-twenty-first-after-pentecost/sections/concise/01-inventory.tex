\begin{properinventory}{Proper}{Text and movement}
Introit & \textit{In voluntate tua}; Esth.~13:9--11; Ps.~118:1. The creator's people ask rescue and walk in his law.\\
Sunday Collect & \textit{Familiam tuam}. Continuing mercy frees God's family for good works.\\
Mission Collect* & \textit{Deus, qui omnes}. Workers carry God's word confidently to all peoples.\\
St Luke Collect† & \textit{Interveniat pro nobis}. The cross-bearing Evangelist intercedes.\\
Epistle & Eph.~6:10--17. God's armor equips resistance to spiritual evil.\\
Gradual & Ps.~89:1--2. God is refuge before the mountains and through generations.\\
Alleluia & Ps.~113:1. Israel leaves Egypt: deliverance begins a journey.\\
Gospel & Matt.~18:23--35. An immense pardon meets a servant's refusal to forgive.\\
Offertory & \textit{Vir erat}; Job~1--2. Upright Job suffers losses and wounds.\\
Sunday Secret & \textit{Suscipe, Domine}. God wills reconciliation and restored salvation.\\
Mission Secret* & \textit{Protector noster}. Christ's redemption opens worship to all nations.\\
St Luke Secret† & \textit{Donis caelestibus}. Heavenly gifts bring free service, healing and glory.\\
Trinity Preface & \textit{Qui cum unigenito}. One God; distinct Persons; equal majesty.\\
Communion & Ps.~118:81,84,86. Hope asks for judgment and help against persecution.\\
Sunday Postcommunion & \textit{Immortalitatis alimoniam}. Immortal food must bear inward fruit.\\
Mission Postcommunion* & \textit{Redemptionis nostrae}. Redemption nourishes continuing growth in true faith.\\
St Luke Postcommunion† & \textit{Praesta, quaesumus}. The altar's gift sanctifies and safeguards the soul.\\
\end{properinventory}
{\footnotesize *Mission prayers: added under the Sunday conclusions unless the local Ordinary appoints another mission day. †St Luke: separate commemorative conclusions at low or conventual Mass; absent at nonconventual sung Mass. The second-class Sunday is green; its Gospel and Trinity Preface remain. Psalm numbers are Vulgate: 89=90, 113:1=114:1, 118=119 in Hebrew numbering.\par}
