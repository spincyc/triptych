\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & Endangered Israel prays; Paul arms believers; the pardoned servant is cruel; upright Job suffers; the persecuted hope.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & Christ delivers and gathers his Church, whose baptismal Exodus continues toward rest, sustained by his sacrifice and food.\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Received mercy calls for inward pardon; protection strengthens truthful, peaceful endurance without condemning the wounded.\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & Judgment tests the forgiven heart and ends persecution; immortal nourishment leads God's family toward lasting communion.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
