\section{Source and delivery note}
This homily addresses an adult parish assembly for the Twenty-first Sunday
after Pentecost, 18 October 2026, in the 1962 Roman Missal. Its argument
develops the expansive study's \textit{The forgiven servant must become
merciful} and the corresponding comparison in the concise study. Job's
uprightness supplies the same pastoral distinction retained in both
studies. The repeated plea in Matthew 18:26,29 changes the question from
what a neighbor owes to what received mercy requires of the hearer.
The connections to the Epistle and orations are the homily's application
of that reviewed reading.
Schuster's explanation of the Postcommunion informs the concluding
movement from reception to the interior life.

The speech contains 1,257 words, counting alphabetic words and retaining
internal apostrophes and hyphens. At an unhurried 110--120 words per minute,
delivery is estimated at 10.5--11.4 minutes before pauses, approximately
11--12 minutes with brief pauses. The title, this note, References and
revision colophon are outside the count and speech. Textual review for
sense and oral clarity was performed; no timed human or audible rehearsal
is claimed. The biblical quotations use the
Douay--Rheims/Challoner witness. Explanations of the Mass's prayers are
commentary, not translations offered for liturgical recitation. No new
closing prayer is supplied.
