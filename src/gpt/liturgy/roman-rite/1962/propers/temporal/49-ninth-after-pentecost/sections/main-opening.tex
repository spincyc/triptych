\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Ecce Deus adiuvat me}; \latin{Deus, in nomine tuo salvum me fac} (Ps. 53:6--7, 3)}{God helps and sustains}{The threatened speaker entrusts rescue and truthful judgment to the Lord and invokes his saving name.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{Pateant aures misericordiae tuae}}{Mercy reforms desire}{Before asking that desires be granted, the Church asks God to make the petitioners seek what pleases him.}
\properrow{Epistle}{1~Cor. 10:6--13}{Warning, humility, fidelity}{Israel's wilderness failures admonish the baptized Church; God remains faithful within temptation and supplies endurance.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{Domine Dominus noster} (Ps. 8:2)}{Majesty above the heavens}{Praise of God's universal name follows Paul's warning against confidence in one's own standing.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Eripe me de inimicis meis} (Ps. 58:2)}{Deliverance entrusted to God}{The singer asks rescue from those rising against him before Christ weeps over the city.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Luke 19:41--47; Credo}{Tears, visitation, house of prayer}{Jesus laments unrecognized peace, foretells siege, cleanses the Temple, and teaches there daily.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{Iustitiae Domini rectae}; \latin{dulciora super mel} (Ps. 18:9--12)}{Right judgments delight}{God's instruction is sweeter than honey, warns the servant, and is kept as gifts are prepared.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{quoties huius hostiae commemoratio celebratur}; Trinity Preface}{Memorial enacts redemption}{The Church asks worthily to frequent the mysteries because the sacrifice's memorial enacts redemption's work.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Qui manducat meam carnem} (John 6:57)}{Reception and mutual abiding}{Eating Christ's flesh and drinking his blood are joined to abiding in him and his abiding in the communicant.}
\properrow{Postcommunion}{\latin{purificationem conferat}; \latin{tribuat unitatem}}{Purification and unity}{The prayer after reception asks that sacramental communion cleanse the recipients and bestow ecclesial unity.}
\end{properstable}
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & A threatened psalmist asks rescue; Paul warns Corinth from Israel's wilderness history; Jesus weeps over Jerusalem and cleanses the Temple; the Church asks disciplined desire, worthy celebration, purification, and unity.\\
Allegorical & Christ is the faithful rescuer, the compassionate visitor and teacher, and the giver who sacramentally enacts redemption and abides in those who receive him; the Christian assembly is the house continually recalled to prayer.\\
Moral & Receive the warning as addressed to the baptized Church: ask rightly, recognize visitation, accept correction, keep God's judgments, and seek communion rather than private mastery.\\
Anagogical & Rescue from evil, the reckoning of visitation, redemption enacted in mystery, mutual abiding, purification, and unity orient the temporal assembly toward perfected communion in God.\\
\end{sensestable}
