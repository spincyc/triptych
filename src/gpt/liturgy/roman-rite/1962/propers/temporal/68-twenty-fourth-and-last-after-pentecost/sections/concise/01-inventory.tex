\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Jer 29:11,12,14; Ps 84(85):2 & God promises peace and return; the psalm verse speaks of captivity ended.\\
Collect & \textit{Excita, quaesumus} & The faithful ask for renewed wills, fruitful work and further remedies.\\
Epistle & Col 1:9--14 & Paul prays for growth and endurance, thanking God for rescue into the Son's kingdom.\\
Gradual & Ps 43(44):8--9 & Praise for deliverance promises enduring thanksgiving amid the whole Psalm's affliction.\\
Alleluia & Ps 129(130):1--2 & A cry from the depths asks God to hear.\\
Gospel & Mt 24:15--35 & Jesus warns of flight and deception, promises public coming and gathering, and affirms his abiding words.\\
Offertory & Ps 129(130):1--2 & The cry returns at the offering, with its opening repeated.\\
Secret & \textit{Propitius esto} & Offerings and prayers accompany a petition for hearts turned toward God and heavenly desire.\\
Preface & Most Holy Trinity & Thanksgiving confesses distinct persons, one essence and equal majesty, with the angels' praise.\\
Communion & Mk 11:24 & Christ assures those who pray of an answer.\\
Postcommunion & \textit{Concede nobis} & Communicants ask the medicinal gift to cure their remaining faults.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
