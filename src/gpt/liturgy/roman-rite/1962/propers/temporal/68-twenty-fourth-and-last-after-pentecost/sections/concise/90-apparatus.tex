\section{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

This concise study concerns the universal 1962 Twenty-fourth and Last
Sunday after Pentecost on 22 November 2026, a second-class Sunday with
Gloria, Creed and Trinity Preface. No particular calendar or institutional
additional prayer is included. It compares the two interpretations developed
in the expansive study from the same research.

The inventory covers all eleven appointments. The expansive study gives
the complete Latin, the cited verses from the Bible in Challoner's English
and the historical liturgical English from Lasance/Walsh. Both English
sources serve as study witnesses. The inherited words remain outside the
project licence. The United States publication bases apply to the
registered public-domain antecedents and historical English wording.

The two interpretations bring together explanations of particular texts.
Theophylact of Ohrid directly explains Mk 11:24--25. Bede explains the
surrounding sayings on faith, the mountain and forgiveness. Augustine's
Psalm expositions extend beyond the appointed verses, and Aquinas's
Colossians lectio 4 explains verses beyond the appointed Epistle. The
Trinity and sacramental passages are doctrinal analogues. Lucien Fromage's
continuation comments on the actual Collect, Secret and Postcommunion.
The Psalm citations distinguish Augustine's Latin sections from those in
the abridged NPNF English where the numbering differs. Bellarmine is cited
through the abridged transcription of O'Sullivan's historical English.

The generated Scriptural Date and Location page and the expansive study's
evidence notes share one chronology record. Exact source identities,
inspection extents, rights, search limits and completed production checks
are recorded in \texttt{propers/verified.md}, \texttt{research/scope.md},
the source bindings and \texttt{research/production-review.md}.
