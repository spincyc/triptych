\section{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

This concise study concerns the universal 1962 Twenty-fourth and Last
Sunday after Pentecost on 22 November 2026, a second-class Sunday with
Gloria, Creed and Trinity Preface. No particular calendar or institutional
additional prayer is included. It compares the two interpretations developed
in the expansive study from the same research.

The inventory covers all eleven appointed texts. Complete Latin and
separately identified Challoner biblical English and Lasance/Walsh historical
liturgical English are in the expansive study. Neither English witness is
an approved English edition of the 1962 Missal. Commentary here supplies
no new prayer translation. The inherited words remain outside the project
licence; the registered United States antecedent and nonrenewal findings
do not clear modern whole scans or website matter.

The whole-Mass interpretations are editorial syntheses. Augustine's Psalm
expositions extend beyond the appointed verses. Bede explains the immediate
context of Mk 11:24; Aquinas's Colossians lectio 4 explains verses beyond
the appointed Epistle. Trinity and sacramental analogues are not direct
commentaries on these prayers. Fromage's continuation supplies direct
liturgical reception without being counted as a carrying saint.
NPNF is historical English reception, with an abridged Augustine Psalm text;
Bellarmine is used through an abridged transcription; no new Greek or
historical-print collation is claimed.

The generated Scriptural Date and Location page and the expansive study's
evidence notes share one chronology record. Exact source identities,
inspection extents, rights, search limits and completed production checks
are recorded in \texttt{propers/verified.md}, \texttt{research/scope.md},
the source bindings and \texttt{research/production-review.md}.
This is a study aid, not an official liturgical book or ecclesiastically
approved commentary.
