\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & Exiles await return; Paul prays for believers already rescued; Jesus warns of real danger. The prayers ask fruitful wills, converted hearts and cure.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Redemption through Christ's Blood brings the faithful into his kingdom. His risen body is the first fruits of his members' resurrection.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Endure adversity and delay, heed Christ rather than secret claimants, and seek God's will. Confident petition includes forgiveness and the conversion of desire.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & Final gathering and resurrection complete deliverance; full healing and communion with the triune God fulfil heavenly desire.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
