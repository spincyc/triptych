\section*{The Propers: Interpretive Possibilities}

\noindent\emph{These proposals arise from AI-assisted editorial comparison.
They are attributed to none of the cited witnesses and make no claim about a
compiler's intention. Each gives its evidence class and controlling limit.}

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{Unexplained desolation entrusted across time.\enspace\properrefs{Int., Ep., All., Off.}}
\emph{Classification: near analogues located.} Psalm~73 asks why rejection
continues and appeals to covenant; Paul marks 430 years without allowing time
to void promise; Psalm~89 names refuge across generations; Psalm~30 entrusts
``my times'' to God. Augustine contrasts divine eternity with temporal
mutability and depicts covenant-seekers awaiting what is promised, but no
checked witness makes the four-way relation. The synthesis must not turn
waiting into a chronology of the world's end or treat distress as illusory.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{A petitioning voice becomes a thankful voice.\enspace\properrefs{Int., Gosp.}}
\emph{Classification: not located in the checked corpus.} The Introit asks
that God not forget the voices of those seeking him; the ten raise one voice
for mercy; the one who sees his cleansing returns with a great voice to
glorify God. The verbal movement can train prayer to return as thanksgiving.
No located ancient, medieval, or liturgical commentator explicitly joins the
chant's voices to Luke's two cries, and gratitude is not a price paid for cure.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{Manna becomes first-person thanksgiving.\enspace\properrefs{Gosp., Comm.}}
\emph{Classification: near analogue located.} Wisdom speaks of bread given
to Israel as ``them''; the antiphon addresses God with \latin{dedísti nobis,
Dómine}. Heard after the stranger returns to give thanks, the grammatical
adaptation can become the assembly's answer: a people receives Israel's manna
memory as gift rather than possession. Rabanus and John Paul~II establish
Eucharistic reception of Wisdom, not this Gospel link. The move may never
erase Israel, appropriate its history against the Jewish people, or imply
that only Christians can give thanks.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{Obedience precedes sight and remains unfinished.\enspace\properrefs{Coll., Gosp., Postcomm.}}
\emph{Classification: near analogue located.} The Collect asks love of what
God commands; the lepers are cleansed while carrying out Jesus' command; the
Postcommunion asks continuing advance after sacramental reception. Bede
distinguishes mere cleansing from faith's grateful operation, but does not
join the two orations. Obedience is not a technique that compels a miracle,
and illness or delayed healing cannot be blamed upon insufficient faith.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{The Mediator's hand and the worshipper's times.\enspace\properrefs{Ep., Off.}}
\emph{Classification: near analogue located.} The law is ordered
\latin{in manu mediatóris}; at the altar the singer confesses that his times
are \latin{in mánibus tuis}. Augustine says salvation is not in one's own hand
but in the Mediator's, which supplies a genuine lexical analogue. He does not
join the Epistle to this Offertory, and the relation neither identifies every
contingency with God's positive will nor removes human responsibility.
