\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

\begin{studyframe}
\textbf{Scope.} This hand-missal companion studies the recurring
\latin{Dominica Undecima post Pentecosten}, II class, in the Vatican 1962
typical Missal. It is not an official liturgical book, critical edition,
homily, civil-date assembly sheet, or exhaustive census of Christian
interpretation. Latin was collated from the identified facsimile; English is
quoted from registered public-domain historical witnesses and is not official
1962 liturgical English. Scripture, ancient Greek and Latin reception,
medieval Doctors, later saints, liturgical history, Catholic doctrine, and
cultural afterlives were researched within the audited bounds. The precise
Epistle cutoff, textual variants, attribution disputes, disability guardrail,
Jewish-relations boundary, and historical changes of formulary pairing remain
claim-local throughout. No compiler intent, universal consensus, guaranteed
temporal cure, prosperity promise, or universal novelty is inferred.
Independent content, citation/binding, profile/component, originality, and
visual audits through 21 August 2026 supplied findings incorporated into this
revision. The accompanying research scope records its local validation and
page-inspection disposition. Maintainer acceptance and installation remain
separate publication steps; no PDF or web output was installed from this leaf.
