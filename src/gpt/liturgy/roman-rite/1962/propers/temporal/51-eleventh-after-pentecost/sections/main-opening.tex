\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Deus in loco sancto suo} (Ps.~67:6--7, 36; 2)}{God gathers and strengthens}{God dwells in holiness, establishes concord in a house, gives strength to his people, and scatters those who hate him.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{abundantia pietatis tuae}}{Mercy exceeds measure}{The Church asks pardon for what conscience fears and gifts beyond what prayer presumes.}
\properrow{Epistle}{1~Cor.~15:1--10 (ending mid-v.~10)}{Gospel received and handed on}{Paul rehearses Christ's death, burial, Resurrection, appearances, and the grace that transformed a persecutor into an apostle.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{In Deo speravit cor meum} (Ps.~27:7, 1)}{A heart helped; flesh renewed}{The singer trusts, receives help, flourishes again, praises, and pleads that God neither be silent nor depart.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Exsultate Deo adiutori nostro} (Ps.~80:2--3)}{Help becomes praise}{The assembly rejoices in its helper and takes up a joyful psalm with the harp.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Mark 7:31--37; Credo}{Ears opened; speech made right}{Jesus takes a deaf man with impaired speech apart, touches him, looks heavenward, groans, commands \latin{Ephphetha}, and restores hearing and speech.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{Exaltabo te, Domine} (Ps.~29:2--3)}{Healing becomes thanksgiving}{The one upheld and healed extols God while the gifts are prepared.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{nostrae fragilitatis subsidium}; Trinity Preface}{Service received as help}{The Church asks that her offering be acceptable to God and become assistance in human frailty.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Honora Dominum de tua substantia} (Prov.~3:9--10)}{Firstfruits and abundance}{Substance and firstfruits honor the Lord; barns and presses supply the sapiential image of overflowing provision.}
\properrow{Postcommunion}{\latin{subsidium mentis et corporis}}{Whole-person remedy}{Sacramental reception is asked to bring help to mind and body and the fullness of heavenly remedy.}
\end{properstable}
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & God strengthens a gathered people; Paul transmits the Resurrection gospel; Christ heals hearing and speech; the Church offers, receives, and asks bodily and spiritual help.\\
Allegorical & The risen Christ opens the Church to hear the apostolic gospel and confess it rightly; grace makes a former persecutor a witness and sacramental communion serves the salvation of the whole person.\\
Moral & Receive before speaking, hand on what was received, return gifts in thanksgiving and service, and bring conscience, weakness, possessions, mind, and body under divine mercy.\\
Anagogical & The holy dwelling, risen body, renewed flesh, abundant storehouse, and fullness of heavenly remedy point toward the concord and incorruption of God's completed household.\\
\end{sensestable}
