\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Réspice, Dómine, in testaméntum tuum} (Ps.~73:20, 19, 22--23, 1)}{The covenant pleaded}{A desolated and poor people asks God to remember his covenant, judge his own cause, hear seekers, and regard his scattered flock.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{fidei, spei et caritátis augméntum}}{Virtues increased}{The Church asks growth in faith, hope, and charity, and love of the command as the way toward the promise.}
\properrow{Epistle}{Gal.~3:16--22}{Promise not annulled}{Paul names Christ as Abraham's one seed and distinguishes the promise's gift from a law unable by itself to give life.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{Réspice, Dómine}; \latin{causam tuam} (Ps.~73:20, 19, 22)}{The plea repeated}{The covenant appeal returns between lesson and Gospel, now remembering the reproach borne by God's servants.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Dómine, refúgium factus es nobis} (Ps.~89:1)}{Refuge through generations}{Before the Gospel, the assembly acclaims the enduring refuge of the God who remains one across covenantal generations.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Luke 17:11--19; Credo}{Cleansed while going; one returns}{Ten obey Jesus' command and are cleansed; one Samaritan returns, glorifies God, gives thanks at Jesus' feet, and hears that faith has saved him.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{in mánibus tuis témpora mea} (Ps.~30:15--16)}{Times entrusted}{The offering is prepared beneath the confession that God holds the worshipper's times.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{propitiáre} over people and gifts; Trinity Preface}{People and gifts under mercy}{The doubled imperative asks God to receive people and gifts, then to grant pardon and the petitions sought.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Panem de cælo dedísti nobis} (Wis.~16:20)}{Heavenly bread received}{Wisdom's manna becomes the communicants' first-person address to the Lord who has given bread bearing every delight.}
\properrow{Postcommunion}{\latin{proficiámus augméntum}}{Growth toward redemption}{After receiving the heavenly sacraments, the communicants ask that they themselves may advance toward the increase of eternal redemption.}
\end{properstable}
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & A ruined people pleads covenant; Paul distinguishes promise and law; Jesus cleanses ten travelers and receives one grateful Samaritan; the Church asks growth, mercy, bread, and progress.\\
Allegorical & Christ is Abraham's promised seed and the Mediator; in him covenant mercy gathers the alien, the Eucharistic bread nourishes his people, and sacramental redemption advances.\\
Moral & Love what God commands, obey before seeing the outcome, return received mercy in thanksgiving, entrust one's times to God, and keep growing after reception.\\
Anagogical & The eternal refuge carries every generation toward the promised inheritance, where faith, hope, and charity reach their end and redemption no longer admits loss or incompleteness.\\
\end{sensestable}
