\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & The owner protects wheat while postponing separation. Paul commands forgiveness and instruction. Exiles and petitioners seek promised mercy.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Christ forms his body through mercy. Augustine explains the sinner's hope through Christ's sacrifice and the members' future resurrection through their risen Head.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Seek another's conversion without destructive haste; forgive while praying, ask for saving good, accept instruction and give thanks.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & The harvest brings gathering and judgement. The pledge anticipates salvation's completion and the resurrection for which Christ's members wait.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
