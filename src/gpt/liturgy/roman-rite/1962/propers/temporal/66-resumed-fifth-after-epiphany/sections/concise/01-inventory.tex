\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Jer 29:11,12,14; Ps 84:2 (Hebrew 85) & Peace and regathering are promised to exiles who must call upon God.\\
Collect & \textit{Familiam tuam} & God's family asks continual protection, relying on heavenly grace.\\
Epistle & Col 3:12--17 & The forgiven must forgive, teach, sing and give thanks as one body.\\
Gradual & Ps 43:8--9 (Hebrew 44) & God rescues his people; they praise him within a psalm of lament.\\
Alleluia & Ps 129:1--2 (Hebrew 130) & The cry from the depths asks God to hear.\\
Gospel & Matt 13:24--30 & Uprooting tares could destroy wheat; separation awaits harvest.\\
Offertory & Ps 129:1--2 (Hebrew 130) & The same cry for hearing returns at the offering.\\
Secret & \textit{Hostias tibi} & The offerers ask pardon and guidance for wavering hearts.\\
Preface & Of the Trinity & Thanksgiving worships distinct Persons, one essence and equal majesty.\\
Communion & Mark 11:24 & Christ promises reception to believing prayer.\\
Postcommunion & \textit{Quaesumus, omnipotens Deus} & Recipients of salvation's pledge ask for its effect.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
