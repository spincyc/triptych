\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & A royal invitation leads to a mixed gathering and searching judgment. Paul commands a renewed common life; the chants and prayers ask for hearing, accepted worship, protection and obedient ways.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Augustine and Gregory see Christ's union with the Church in the wedding. Charity honours the Bridegroom; Christ renews his members, whose prayer rises in his self-offering.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Fruitful belonging becomes truthful speech, restraint of resentment and help for need. Love reaches enemies. The garment must grow through grace; weakness differs from refusing to love.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & The present assembly looks toward the perfected feast. Judgment summons perseverance, and Christ's Passion opens toward resurrection: trial, imperfect love and labour are not the final condition.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
