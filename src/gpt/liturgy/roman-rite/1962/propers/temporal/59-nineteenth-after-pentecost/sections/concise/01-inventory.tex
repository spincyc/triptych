\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & \textit{Salus populi}; Ps 77(78):1 & God's saving promise calls a people to hear; reception begins in dependence.\\
Collect & \textit{Omnipotens et misericors Deus} & Freedom from adversity serves a purpose: body and mind made ready for God.\\
Epistle & Eph 4:23--28 & The new person speaks truth, restrains anger, works honestly and shares with need.\\
Gradual & Ps 140(141):2 & Prayer and uplifted hands seek acceptance as incense and evening sacrifice.\\
Alleluia & Ps 104(105):1 & Praise, invocation and proclamation carry God's deeds to the nations.\\
Gospel & Matt 22:1--14 & The Son's feast meets refusal; further guests are gathered and the garment examined.\\
Offertory & Adapted Ps 137(138):7 & God's saving hand sustains life in tribulation; the guest still needs protection.\\
Secret & \textit{Haec munera} & The offerings before divine majesty are petitioned to be saving for their participants.\\
Communion & Ps 118(119):4--5 & The command to observe God's law awakens a petition for directed ways.\\
Postcommunion & \textit{Tua nos, Domine} & Healing frees from perversity and sustains adherence to God's commands.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
