## Research and limits

The replacement follows the complete psalm beyond the excerpt: initial
elevation and trust lead into instruction in God's ways, pardon of youthful
sins, covenantal guidance, integrity, deliverance, and the closing petition
for Israel. Augustine §§1–11 and 15–22 support the distinction between hope in
God and presumption in oneself, waiting as perseverance before visible
fulfillment, and the movement through instruction, pardon, covenant,
affliction, and deliverance. Cassiodorus's *divisio* and exposition at
vv. 1–3 turn from earthly conduct toward heavenly things. Aquinas's *Super
Psalmo 24* nos. 1–3 (Corpus Thomisticum ids 86993–86995) supplies the exact
prayer-grammar of elevation, confidence, three sources of shame, and
long-suffering expectation. Theodoret, PG 80, cols. 1033–1037, and Bellarmine,
Psalm 24, scan pp. 68–72, remain checked witnesses for which the publication
claims no distinct interpretive move.

The Roman/Clementine-liturgical wording is the controlling wording:
`neque irrideant me inimici mei` and `qui te exspectant`. The Nova Vulgata at
Psalm 25(24):2–3 instead has `neque exsultent super me inimici mei` and
`universi, qui sustinent te`; its lexical forms must not be silently
substituted into analysis of the proper.

The 1962 Missal facsimile was checked for the identical Offertory at the First
Sunday of Advent. This demonstrates a second Roman liturgical horizon only.
It does not establish chronological priority, common authorship, compiler
intent, or an Advent identity for the Tenth Sunday. No checked witness
explained the relation between the assignments. The lowered-eyes/lifted-soul
relation remains a source-grounded observation, limited by the facts that
posture does not prove humility and enemy language may not be assigned to
contemporary opponents.

The repository's Third Sunday after Pentecost also appoints Psalm 24:16, 18
with vv. 1–2 as its Introit psalm verse. This is an additional intra-temporal
reuse of the opening amid loneliness, affliction, sin, and mercy; it likewise
demonstrates polyvalent reception without explaining historical selection.
