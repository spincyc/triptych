# Page-2 dossier redevelopment

## Integration boundary

`sections/redevelopment/02-scriptural-date-location.tex` is an
integration-ready replacement for the complete `Scriptural Date and Location`
block in `main.tex`, from that section heading through the page's
`longtable`. It deliberately remains unimported while the Sunday-wide
integrator serializes the monolithic source. The integrator must preserve the
forced page boundaries immediately before and after the replacement.

The fragment restores Catholic canonical order: the five Psalm dossiers,
First Corinthians, then Luke. It retains every distinct directly appointed
biblical passage exactly once.

## Corrections embodied in the fragment

- Each Psalm dossier now distinguishes its whole poem's literary horizon from
  the received heading and from unrecoverable historical coordinates. The
  Davidic headings are not treated as recovered writing scenes.
- Psalm 50 (51) distinguishes the David--Nathan penitential frame from the
  widespread but non-certain judgment that vv. 20--21 are a post-587 BC
  liturgical layer.
- Psalm 54 (55) treats betrayal, violence in a city, and sanctuary prayer as
  literary coordinates without silently identifying a historical Jerusalem
  crisis.
- Psalm 64 (65) keeps Zion worship, creation, and harvest together while
  distinguishing its Davidic Hebrew heading from the exilic-return
  orientation of the inherited Greek and Latin title.
- First Corinthians identifies both ends of the letter's geography, places
  Paul in the mature missionary and pastoral stage of the third missionary
  journey after the Corinthian foundation, and names the Corinthian
  recipients rather than giving only the writing site.
- Luke's ancient Lukan/Achaian and c. AD 60 orientation is visibly
  distinguished from the modern Catholic judgment of final composition about
  AD 80--90 outside Palestine for a substantially Gentile audience.
- The former `Narrated parable` row with a supposed c. AD 29--30 date is
  removed. The Temple is the setting of fictional worshipers within Jesus'
  parable, not the site of an independently recoverable historical incident.

## Evidence and limits

The historical orientation is controlled by the canonical contexts and the
introductions and notes already cited in the publication's reference list:
Psalms 17, 25, 51, 55, and 65; First Corinthians, especially chapters 12--14;
and Luke 18 in its final-journey sequence. Protected modern Scripture is
summarized, not reproduced.

No new source binding is claimed by this handoff. The Sunday-wide integrator
should retain the existing Scripture bindings and reconcile any broader
source-library work separately. A one-page build and visual check remain
required after import; if the evidence does not fit legibly, tighten prose
without deleting the dossier hierarchy or collapsing composition, audience,
life stage, geography, tradition, and parabolic setting into one another.
