# Deep-and-broad redevelopment audit — 2026-07-29

## Trigger and verdict on the prior edition

The user identified the published guide as seriously deficient in content and
research depth. That qualitative finding supersedes the earlier same-day
staleness verdict for editorial action. The prior edition had a strong source
inventory and accurate appointed texts, but compressed most reception into
short per-element paragraphs. It too often named witnesses without showing
their different questions, genres, or limits.

## Negative and bounded results

- No checked witness was found that comments on this complete ten-element 1962
  sequence as one authored composition.
- No source justifies identifying the Psalm 54 enemies with a present group.
- No source makes the Pharisee a type of the Jewish people or treats fasting,
  tithing, and good works as intrinsically culpable.
- No checked source collapses Pauline charism into canonical office or private
  authority.
- The Gelasian witness controls an early wording state, not authorship or the
  date of the later Sunday assignment.

## Publication consequence

The prior appointed-text, rights, and calendrical controls remain sound. The
material change is a substantial comparative-reception layer and a clearer
account of disagreement, source function, and inferential limits. A fresh
build, web generation, staleness comparison, installed-PDF review, and
baseline are required for the new snapshot.
