\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & A child is healed; captives lament; the faithful ask mercy and learn wise, grateful conduct. These are distinct histories and petitions.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Christ's word heals and gathers his people; the pilgrim Church receives heavenly medicine while longing for Zion.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Trust takes an obedient step; thanksgiving becomes attentive praise, generosity and mutual service, with room for truthful lament.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & Present gifts sustain hope for resurrection, lasting fellowship and the sight of God, beyond every temporary cure.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
