\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Dan.~3:31,29,35; Ps.~118:1 (119) & Confession appeals to mercy; the psalm blesses the obedient way.\\
Collect & \emph{Largire} & Pardon and peace free the faithful for service.\\
Maternity Collect* & \emph{Deus, qui de beatae} & The incarnate Word's Mother intercedes for believers.\\
Epistle & Eph.~5:15--21 & Wise walking, inward song, thanksgiving, mutual submission.\\
Gradual & Ps.~144:15--16 (145) & The open divine hand feeds all in due season.\\
Alleluia & Ps.~107:2 (108), adapted & The prepared heart answers God with praise.\\
Gospel & John~4:46b--53 & A father believes the healing word; his household comes to faith.\\
Offertory & Ps.~136:1 (137), adapted & Captives remember Zion with tears.\\
Secret & \emph{Caelestem nobis} & The mysteries give heavenly medicine and cleanse the heart.\\
Maternity Secret* & \emph{Tua, Domine} & Offering and intercession seek present and perpetual peace.\\
Trinity Preface & \emph{Qui cum unigenito} & One divine essence, distinct Persons, equal majesty.\\
Communion & Ps.~118:49--50 (119), selected & The promised word gives hope and comfort in humiliation.\\
Postcommunion & \emph{Ut sacris} & Obedience is asked as a gift for worthy reception.\\
Maternity Postcommunion* & \emph{Haec nos communio} & Communion cleanses and gives fellowship in heavenly remedy.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
{\footnotesize *The three Maternity prayers follow their Sunday counterparts at Low or conventual Mass; they are omitted at nonconventual sung Mass. The Trinity Preface remains. Gloria and Creed are said; no Marian lesson or chant is added.\par}
