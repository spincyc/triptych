# Twelfth Sunday after Pentecost — verified propers

## Textual control

- **Edition:** *Missale Romanum*, Vatican typical edition, 1962; source-library
  edition `edition.catholic-church.missale-romanum.vatican-typica-1962`.
- **Formulary:** `DOMINICA DUODECIMA / post Pentecosten`; printed `II classis`.
  Green follows the general temporal color rule and is not printed on these
  formulary pages.
- **Controlling artifact:** CMAA facsimile
  <https://media.churchmusicassociation.org/pdf/missale62.pdf>, SHA-256
  `648fdb8fe830ed65a08aa4a95de6f94424c533ddf2398c8fc26b18735fd3518a`,
  82,815,941 bytes, 1,088 PDF pages.
- **Locus:** printed pp. 392–393 = artifact PDF pp. 473–474; marginal nos.
  1552–1561. The preceding boundary is the Eleventh Sunday's Postcommunion,
  no. 1551; the following boundary is the Thirteenth Sunday heading and
  Introit, no. 1562.
- **Method:** all ten elements, headings, references, accents, punctuation,
  conclusions, the rank, the Credo rubric, the Trinity Preface rubric, and the
  two boundaries were read on page images. OCR in `retrieved.txt` was used only
  to locate the pages.
- **Independent 1962 comparison:** the Benziger *editio iuxta typicam*, printed
  pp. 386–388, was inspected as a second edition. It differs in heading
  capitalization; Introit-heading expansion and antiphon-repeat cue; Gradual
  layout; omission of the Alleluia's comma after `Dómine`; omission of the
  Gospel comma after `iumentum suum`; omission of the Secret comma after `ut`;
  and the shorter Secret conclusion cue. The Vatican typical readings below
  control; no form was blended.
- **Verification date:** 2026-08-20. No unresolved reading remains.
- **Boundary:** recurring temporal formulary only. A civil date, occurrence,
  precedence, commemoration, or particular calendar is not established here.

## Appointed text in liturgical order

### 1. Introit — Psalm 69:2–3, with verse 4a (modern Psalm 70:1–3)

> Deus, in adiutórium meum inténde: Dómine, ad adiuvándum me festína:
> confundántur et revereántur inimíci mei, qui quærunt ánimam meam. Ps.
> ibid., 4 Avertántur retrórsum, et erubéscant: qui cógitant mihi mala. ℣.
> Glória Patri.

The chant psalter has `inimíci mei`, absent from Clementine Ps. 69:3, and
`cógitant mihi mala` where the Clementine and Douay have `volunt`/“desire.”
Those differences are published as witness boundaries, not silently
translated.

### 2. Collect

> Omnípotens et miséricors Deus, de cuius múnere venit, ut tibi a fidélibus
> tuis digne et laudabíliter serviátur: tríbue, quǽsumus, nobis; ut ad
> promissiónes tuas sine offensióne currámus. Per Dóminum.

### 3. Epistle — 2 Corinthians 3:4–9

> Léctio Epístolæ beáti Pauli Apóstoli ad Corínthios.
>
> Fratres: Fidúciam talem habémus per Christum ad Deum: non quod sufficiéntes
> simus cogitáre áliquid a nobis, quasi ex nobis: sed sufficiéntia nostra ex
> Deo est: qui et idóneos nos fecit minístros novi testaménti: non líttera,
> sed spíritu: líttera enim occídit, spíritus autem vivíficat. Quod si
> ministrátio mortis, lítteris deformáta in lapídibus, fuit in glória; ita ut
> non possent inténdere fílii Israël in fáciem Móysi, propter glóriam vultus
> eius, quæ evacuátur: quómodo non magis ministrátio Spíritus erit in glória?
> Nam si ministrátio damnatiónis glória est: multo magis abúndat ministérium
> iustítiæ in glória.

The Missal's `Fratres:` replaces Paul's connective `autem`; the lesson ends
before v. 10 and the continuation of the comparison.

### 4. Gradual — Psalm 33:2–3 (modern Psalm 34:1–2)

> Benedícam Dóminum in omni témpore: semper laus eius in ore meo. ℣. In
> Dómino laudábitur ánima mea: áudiant mansuéti, et læténtur.

### 5. Alleluia — Psalm 87:2 (modern Psalm 88:1)

> Allelúia, allelúia. ℣. Dómine, Deus salútis meæ, in die clamávi et nocte
> coram te. Allelúia.

The comma after the vocative `Dómine` is printed in the typical edition.

### 6. Gospel — Luke 10:23–37

> Sequéntia sancti Evangélii secúndum Lucam.
>
> In illo témpore: Dixit Iesus discípulis suis: Beáti óculi, qui vident quæ
> vos vidétis. Dico enim vobis, quod multi prophétæ et reges voluérunt vidére
> quæ vos vidétis, et non vidérunt: et audíre quæ audítis, et non audiérunt.
> Et ecce quidam legisperítus surréxit, tentans illum, et dicens: Magíster,
> quid faciéndo vitam ætérnam possidébo? At ille dixit ad eum: In lege quid
> scriptum est? quómodo legis? Ille respóndens, dixit: Díliges Dóminum Deum
> tuum ex toto corde tuo, et ex tota ánima tua, et ex ómnibus víribus tuis,
> et ex omni mente tua: et próximum tuum sicut teípsum. Dixítque illi: Recte
> respondísti: hoc fac, et vives. Ille autem volens iustificáre seípsum, dixit
> ad Iesum: Et quis est meus próximus? Suscípiens autem Iesus, dixit: Homo
> quidam descendébat ab Ierúsalem in Iéricho, et íncidit in latrónes, qui
> étiam despoliavérunt eum: et plagis impósitis abiérunt, semivívo relícto.
> Accidit autem ut sacérdos quidam descénderet eádem via: et viso illo
> præterívit. Simíliter et levíta, cum esset secus locum, et vidéret eum,
> pertránsiit. Samaritánus autem quidam iter fáciens, venit secus eum: et
> videns eum, misericórdia motus est. Et apprópians alligávit vúlnera eius,
> infúndens óleum et vinum: et impónens illum in iuméntum suum, duxit in
> stábulum, et curam eius egit. Et áltera die prótulit duos denários, et dedit
> stabulário, et ait: Curam illíus habe: et quodcúmque supererogáveris, ego
> cum redíero, reddam tibi. Quis horum trium vidétur tibi próximus fuísse
> illi, qui íncidit in latrónes? At ille dixit: Qui fecit misericórdiam in
> illum. Et ait illi Iesus: Vade, et tu fac simíliter.

**Rubric:** `Credo.` The Missal replaces Luke's own v. 23 narrative opening
with its liturgical incipit. The comma after `iumentum suum` is printed.

### 7. Offertory — Exodus 32:11, 13, 14 (with matter from v. 12)

> Precátus est Móyses in conspéctu Dómini Dei sui, et dixit: Quare, Dómine,
> irásceris in pópulo tuo? Parce iræ ánimæ tuæ: meménto Abraham, Isaac, et
> Iacob, quibus iurásti dare terram fluéntem lac et mel. Et placátus factus
> est Dóminus de malignitáte, quam dixit fácere pópulo suo.

This is an Old-Latin/Septuagint-shaped adaptation rather than a continuous
Clementine excerpt. Its unprinted v. 12 matter, Jacob, milk-and-honey promise,
and closing construction are disclosed in the guide. The typical edition has
no comma in `Dómini Dei sui`, and does have `Abraham, Isaac, et Iacob`.

### 8. Secret

> Hóstias, quǽsumus, Dómine, propítius inténde, quas sacris altáribus
> exhibémus: ut, nobis indulgéntiam largiéndo, tuo nómini dent honórem. Per
> Dóminum nostrum.

**Rubric:** `Præfatio de Ssma Trinitate.` The commas enclosing `nobis
indulgéntiam largiéndo` and the conclusion `Per Dóminum nostrum` are the
typical-edition readings.

### 9. Communion — Psalm 103:13 and 14–15

> De fructu óperum tuórum, Dómine, satiábitur terra: ut edúcas panem de
> terra, et vinum lætíficet cor hóminis: ut exhílaret fáciem in óleo, et panis
> cor hóminis confírmet.

The printed reference is exactly `Ps. 103, 13 et 14-15`. The antiphon starts at
v. 13b, changes word order, adds `Dómine`, omits v. 14a, and continues through
v. 15.

### 10. Postcommunion

> Vivíficet nos, quǽsumus, Dómine, huius participátio sancta mystérii: et
> páriter nobis expiatiónem tríbuat, et munímen. Per Dóminum.

The comma in `tríbuat, et munímen` is printed.

## English witnesses and rights

Scriptural English is taken from the repository's tracked public-domain
Douay–Rheims/Challoner verse files. The Collect, Secret, and Postcommunion use
the anonymous English in the 1861 Cummiskey Roman Missal, printed pp. 420,
422, and 423, registered as `post-pentecosten-12`. Each line was matched to the
collated Latin. Cummiskey's Collect compresses the two Latin adverbs into
“worthily”; its Secret recasts the agency and reverses the order of honor and
pardon; its
Postcommunion renders `tríbuat` and `páriter` less explicitly. The printed
abbreviation `Thro'.` is not expanded.

The shared rights inventory leaves the 1962 edition and scan unresolved. The
guide republishes no facsimile image and treats the focused Latin formulary as
pre-existing material under the project's recorded 17 U.S.C. §103(b) analysis.
This is a repository rights judgment, not legal advice or a grant to third
parties.
