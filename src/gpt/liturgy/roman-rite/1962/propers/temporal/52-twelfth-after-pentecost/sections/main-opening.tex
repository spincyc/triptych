\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Deus, in adiutórium meum inténde} (Ps.~69:2--3, with 4a)}{Help sought at once}{A persecuted singer confesses continual need and asks God to frustrate those who seek his life.}
\properrow{Collect}{\latin{de cuius múnere venit, ut tibi \ldots{} serviátur}}{Service itself is gift}{The Church asks to run without stumbling toward God's promises, because worthy service already comes from him.}
\properrow{Epistle}{2~Cor.~3:4--9}{Sufficiency from God}{Paul contrasts self-sufficiency with fitness received through Christ for the life-giving ministry of the new covenant.}
\properrow{Gradual}{\latin{Benedícam Dóminum in omni témpore} (Ps.~33:2--3)}{Praise in every season}{Blessing fills the mouth; the meek hear a soul glory in the Lord and rejoice.}
\properrow{Alleluia}{\latin{Dómine, Deus salútis meæ} (Ps.~87:2)}{Day-and-night appeal}{The darkest psalm supplies an Alleluia verse: lament addresses the God who remains the singer's salvation.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Luke 10:23--37; Credo}{Law fulfilled as mercy}{Jesus moves from blessed sight, through the double commandment, to a wounded stranger and the command to become a neighbour by action.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{Precátus est Móyses} (Exod.~32:11, 13--14, with matter from v.~12)}{Intercession before God}{Moses appeals to covenant promise for an undeserving people; the chant's Old-Latin form is not a verbatim Vulgate extract.}
\properrow{Secret}{\latin{nobis indulgéntiam largiéndo}}{Pardon honors the divine name}{The Church asks God to regard the offerings favorably so that their obtaining pardon may give honor to his name.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{panem \ldots{} vinum \ldots{} óleo} (Ps.~103:13b, 14b--15, adapted)}{Creation's gifts at Communion}{Earth is filled from God's works; bread strengthens, wine gladdens, and oil brightens.}
\properrow{Postcommunion}{\latin{expiatiónem \ldots{} et munímen}}{Life, expiation, protection}{Participation in the holy mystery is asked to vivify, cleanse, and defend its recipients.}
\end{properstable}
\begin{sensestable}
Literal & Help is requested; Paul locates ministerial fitness in God; Jesus commands concrete mercy; Moses intercedes; the Church offers, communicates, and asks life and protection.\\
Allegorical & The Fathers see Christ as the compassionate traveller who heals fallen humanity, bears it in his Incarnation, and entrusts it to the Church; healed by his gifts, humanity is restored to love as he loved.\\
Moral & Read God's command so that it becomes merciful action: receive sufficiency as gift, bless God in every circumstance, approach the wounded, intercede for sinners, and spend oneself without self-justification.\\
Anagogical & The promised destination is eternal life: day-and-night prayer, new-covenant glory, the Samaritan's promised return, and vivifying Communion draw the pilgrim through the Church toward resurrection, judgment, and unbroken praise.\\
\end{sensestable}
