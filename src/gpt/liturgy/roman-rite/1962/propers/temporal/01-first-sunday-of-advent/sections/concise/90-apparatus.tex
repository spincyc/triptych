\section{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

This is the Mass of the First Sunday of Advent on the universal 1962 Roman calendar, 29 November 2026. The Sunday is of the first class, in violet, with the station at St Mary Major, the Creed and the Preface of the Most Holy Trinity. The Gloria is omitted. The ordinary commemoration of St Saturninus does not enter this Sunday's Mass (general rubrics §§109, 111(a)).

The expansive study gives the appointed Latin and historical English for all eleven texts. Psalm numbers here follow the Missal, with modern numbering in parentheses. English Scripture follows Challoner, with the forms the texts take at Mass distinguished in the expansive study. Lasance and Walsh provide historical hand-missal English. The project license covers the commentary. Inherited liturgical wording and third-party translations retain their own rights status.

The comparisons of the prayers, chants and lessons are the author's. The biblical commentators address the individual passages cited. Guéranger comments directly on the chants of this Sunday.
