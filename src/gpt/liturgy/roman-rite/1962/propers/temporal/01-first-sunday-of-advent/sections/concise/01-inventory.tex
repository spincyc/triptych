\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Ps 24(25):1--4 & A man in danger of ridicule tells God he trusts him and asks to learn his ways.\\
Collect & Prayer for deliverance & The Church asks the Son to come to the aid of people endangered by their own sins.\\
Epistle & Rom 13:11--14a & Salvation is nearer now. Paul calls on those who believe to wake and put on Christ.\\
Gradual & Ps 24(25):3--4 & All who wait on God can ask him to teach them his paths.\\
Alleluia & Ps 84:8 (85:7) & The singers ask to see God's mercy and receive his salvation.\\
Gospel & Lk 21:25--33 & Amid the terror of the nations, Jesus tells his disciples to look up for their redemption.\\
Offertory & Ps 24(25):1--3 & The same words of trust sung at the entrance accompany the offering.\\
Secret & Prayer over the gifts & The priest asks that the gifts may cleanse those who would approach their giver.\\
Preface & Most Holy Trinity & The Church gives thanks to the Father and adores the equal majesty of Father, Son and Spirit.\\
Communion & Ps 84:13 (85:12) & The Lord will give goodness. The earth will give its fruit.\\
Postcommunion & Prayer for mercy & The faithful ask to receive mercy and prepare for the approaching solemnities.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
