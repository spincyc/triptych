## Occurrence and authority

The fourth Sunday before Friday, 25 December 2026 is Sunday, 29 November. The `roman-1962` calendar computation returns Advent I for that day, at the start of the liturgical year ending in 2027. It also returns St Saturninus as a fixed-date commemoration candidate and marks its list unranked. The Missal settles precedence. Its printed heading is *Dominica prima Adventus*, I classis, with the Roman station at St Mary Major. The 1962 *Rubricae generales* (RG) 71 begins Advent with first Vespers of this Sunday. There is one annual Epistle and Gospel at this Mass.

The seasonal rubric on p. [1] omits *Gloria in excelsis* at Masses of the season through the Nativity Vigil inclusive. The Introit retains *Gloria Patri*. The rubric following the Gospel prescribes the Creed on Sunday. The Roman Canon is the ordinary Canon, with no special insertion appointed here. There is no Tract, Sequence, alternative reading or ritual blessing in these pages. The Sunday has no choice between the Trinity Preface and a local Advent Preface.

The weekday reuse differs at three points. On an Advent feria for which the preceding Sunday's Mass is used, keep the Gradual alone, omit the Creed and use the common Preface. This supplies no options for Sunday. The rubric following the Postcommunion extends across pp. 2–3. When no own ferial Mass is assigned, use the preceding Sunday's Mass, and take Sunday's prayers where the feria lacking a Mass of its own has to be commemorated.
