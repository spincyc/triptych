\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & A psalmist waits for help. Paul wakes believers. Jesus compares the signs of the kingdom to fruit announcing summer.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Christ speaks for his people in their distress. In the earth yielding its fruit, Bellarmine sees Mary bearing the Son.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & A disciple awaiting the Judge can lay down a quarrel. Someone preparing for Christmas can give a neighbor the help he needs today.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & Those who love the Lord hope to see him when earth and sky pass away. The Son they receive in Communion will come in majesty.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
