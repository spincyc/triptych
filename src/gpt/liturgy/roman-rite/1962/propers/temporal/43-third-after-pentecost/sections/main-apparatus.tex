\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

\begin{studyframe}
\textbf{Text.} All ten propers were collated with the 1962 typical-edition facsimile, pp.~380--382. \textbf{Historical orientation.} Six distinct direct biblical passages appear in canonical order; traditional composition metadata, Luke's narrated setting, and historical alternatives are distinguished. \textbf{Direct reception.} All four appointed psalms and Luke 15; Anthony supplies partial direct medieval reception of 1~Peter 5 while no complete patristic line-by-line commentary on all appointed verses was retained. \textbf{Related illumination.} Ascetical reuse, oblation, sanctification, Eucharistic reception, and ecclesial joy. \textbf{Material limits.} Psalm origins and sites, Luke's exact event and composition locations, and 1~Peter's authorship and date remain disputed; no exhaustive manuscript, untranslated, subscription, or cultural-reception survey is claimed. \textbf{Full records.} \nolinkurl{propers/verified.md}; \nolinkurl{research/scope.md}.
\end{studyframe}
