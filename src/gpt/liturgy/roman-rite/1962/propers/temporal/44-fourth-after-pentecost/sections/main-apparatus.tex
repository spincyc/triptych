\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

\begin{studyframe}
\textbf{Text.} Ten propers collated with the 1962 typical-edition facsimile, pp.~382--383. \textbf{History.} Seven direct passages in canonical order; tradition, narrated setting, and historical judgment distinguished. \textbf{Reception.} Direct exposition of all five psalm chants, Romans 8, and Luke 5; Greek, Latin, and Antonian witnesses illuminate order, resurrection, John 21, grace, and Eucharistic reception. \textbf{Limits.} Psalm origins and Luke's setting remain disputed; the public survey is not exhaustive. \textbf{Full records.} \nolinkurl{propers/verified.md}; \nolinkurl{research/scope.md}.
\end{studyframe}
