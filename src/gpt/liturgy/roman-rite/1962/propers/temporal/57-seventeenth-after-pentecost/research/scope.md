## Exact checked witnesses and arguments

**Chrysostom on Matthew71.** Retained CCEL NPNF1 volume10, Prevost/Riddle English1888, physical lines39940–40085: the two love commands depend on each other; the questioner can be improved by Christ's reply; Davidic descent is not denied when Christ reveals his divine lordship. Reading was of the complete opening exposition through these arguments, not the entire retained homily or Greek original. The homily's remaining moral exhortation is not needed as independently rechecked evidence. Comparison locator: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200171.htm . Bind the retained CCEL passage, not an assumed byte-identical New Advent response.

**Chrysostom on Ephesians9–11.** Retained NPNF1 volume13 constituent: hom9, physical9360–9480, bonds and Spirit-given unity across human divisions; hom10 opening9619–9690, one body across places and times; hom11,9920–10080, common salvation/inheritance and different ministries. His several readings of one spirit—received Spirit, concord, zeal—remain alternatives. Equality in common saving gifts does not erase ecclesial offices. Comparisons: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/230109.htm , /230110.htm and /230111.htm . The whole commentary is retained but was not read in full for this task.

**Augustine, Enarrationes in Psalmos.** Retained CCEL NPNF1 volume8 English: Ps32 first exposition §§1–22 (7871–7995); Ps75 §3 (36560–36589), §§8–13 (36663–36879); Ps101 §§1–3 (50150–50233); Ps118 §§34–41 (57268–57372), paragraphs123–126 and127 opening (58941–58995),136 (59168–59185). Modern English headings are33,76,102,119; those are not different psalms. The edition is explicitly abridged. Its paragraph123 and136 must not be silently converted into Latin sermon numbers; the current source grammar limits numeric sections to99, so those two are ordinary bibliographic loci on the bound artifact rather than invented passage IDs.

Ps118's petitions disclose dependence on mercy and grace while divine justice remains real; §41 connects Christ with the neighbor. Ps32 reads the righteous as heavens established through Word and Spirit and the chosen people as the heavenly city. Ps101 treats the suppliant as Christ with his members, assigning sinful confession to members rather than personal sin of the Head. Ps75 connects confession with peace, judgment with the meek's salvation, Christian remembrance with baptism and daily sacrifice, and vows with both common obligations and particular commitments enabled by God. Its final paragraphs oppose private possession of truth and read the humbled princes through pride and self-rule. Ancient polemical collective descriptions of Jewish people are not adopted as contemporary assertions. Comparison URLs: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1801033.htm , /1801076.htm , /1801102.htm , /1801119.htm .

**Augustine on the love command and the questioner.** Retained CCEL NPNF1 volume2, DDC I22.20–21 (44934–44991), I30.31–33 (45212–45270): love directs the whole self and neighbor toward God; neighbor includes every human being, including an enemy, and Christ is neighbor. **Thomas, Super Ephesios4 lect1–2.** All six retained Dessain1857 volumeII images, printed312–317 / artifact316–321, were independently read from CAPUT IV to the next LECTIO3 heading. ## Interpretive decisions and remaining limits

Real differences are preserved: possible motives of the lawyer; meanings of one spirit; creation and spiritual heavens; individual and Head-with-members voice; personal and representative confession; common fidelity and particular vows; temporal sovereign power and pride.
