\begin{concisemaptable}
Introit & Ps.~118:137, 124; v.~1 & The servant confesses God's just judgment
and begs the mercy that teaches him to walk in the law.\\
Collect & \latin{Da, quaesumus, Domine} & The people asks freedom from
diabolical contagion and an undivided, pure-minded following of God alone.\\
Epistle & Eph.~4:1--6 & Humility, meekness and patient charity guard the one
body, Spirit, hope, Lord, faith, baptism and Father already shared.\\
Gradual & Ps.~32:12, 6 & The people the Lord chose is blessed; the heavens
were established by his word and his spirit.\\
Alleluia & Ps.~101:2 & An afflicted voice begs to be heard; the plea is
carried into the hearing of the Gospel.\\
Gospel & Matt.~22:34--46; Credo & The twofold command of love, and the
question that shows David's Son to be David's Lord.\\
Offertory & Dan.~9:17--19, adapted & Daniel begs mercy for the desolate
sanctuary and the people called by God's name.\\
Secret & \latin{Maiestatem tuam}; Trinity Preface & The holy action is asked
to free the people from offenses past and future.\\
Communion & Ps.~75:12--13 & Vows are paid and gifts brought before the feared
Lord who humbles earthly princes.\\
Postcommunion & \latin{Sanctificationibus tuis} & The mysteries are asked to
cure vices and to become an eternal remedy.\\
\end{concisemaptable}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:inventory:end}

\vspace{0.2em}
{\footnotesize\noindent All ten elements are appointed; the Credo follows the
Gospel and the Preface of the Holy Trinity is assigned to the Sunday. Psalm
references follow the Missal's numbering (the common modern numbers are 33,
76, 102 and 119); the appointed texts themselves are given in the full
study.\par}
