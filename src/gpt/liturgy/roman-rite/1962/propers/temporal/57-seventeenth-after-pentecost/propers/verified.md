# Collated Latin proper

Independently image-collated 17 September 2026. Controlling witness: *Missale Romanum*, Vatican typical edition 1962, printed 398–400 / PDF 479–481, marginal 1602–1611, **Dominica decima septima post Pentecosten, II classis**. The complete ten bodies were compared with Pustet, Ratisbon 1862, printed 341–342, retained IA leaves n426–427. The older witness establishes the inherited Latin publication route under the repository facsimile-rights inventory; it does not control the 1962 appointment. Source IDs, exact hashes and image locations are in research/source-audit.md and research/source-bindings.toml.

This is a normalized reading transcription, not a facsimile: stress accents are removed, ae/oe ligatures expanded, drop capitals joined, typographical line breaks removed, and the printed versicle sign represented by V. Target I/J practice and word order are retained. Conclusions stop where the target stops. The 1862 bodies agree in words apart from orthographic forms; its punctuation, shortened conclusions and additional seasonal orations are not imported. No missing liturgical wording or English has been composed.

## 1. Introit — Ps 118:137,124; verse 1 — no. 1602

Iustus es, Domine, et rectum iudicium tuum: fac cum servo tuo secundum misericordiam tuam.

Ps. Beati immaculati in via: qui ambulant in lege Domini.

V. Gloria Patri.

## 2. Collect — no. 1603

Da, quaesumus, Domine, populo tuo diabolica vitare contagia: et te solum Deum pura mente sectari. Per Dominum nostrum.

## 3. Epistle — Eph 4:1–6 — no. 1604

Lectio Epistolae beati Pauli Apostoli ad Ephesios.

Fratres: Obsecro vos ego vinctus in Domino, ut digne ambuletis vocatione, qua vocati estis, cum omni humilitate et mansuetudine, cum patientia, supportantes invicem in caritate, solliciti servare unitatem spiritus in vinculo pacis. Unum corpus, et unus spiritus, sicut vocati estis in una spe vocationis vestrae. Unus Dominus, una fides, unum baptisma. Unus Deus, et Pater omnium, qui est super omnes, et per omnia, et in omnibus nobis. Qui est benedictus in saecula saeculorum. Amen.

## 4. Gradual — Ps 32:12,6 — no. 1605

Beata gens, cuius est Dominus Deus eorum: populus, quem elegit Dominus in hereditatem sibi.

V. Verbo Domini caeli firmati sunt: et spiritu oris eius omnis virtus eorum.

## 5. Alleluia — Ps 101:2 — no. 1606

Alleluia, alleluia. V. Domine, exaudi orationem meam, et clamor meus ad te perveniat. Alleluia.

## 6. Gospel — Mt 22:34–46 — no. 1607

Sequentia sancti Evangelii secundum Matthaeum.

In illo tempore: Accesserunt ad Iesum pharisaei: et interrogavit eum unus ex eis legis doctor, tentans eum: Magister, quod est mandatum magnum in lege? Ait illi Iesus: Diliges Dominum Deum tuum ex toto corde tuo, et in tota anima tua, et in tota mente tua. Hoc est maximum et primum mandatum. Secundum autem simile est huic: Diliges proximum tuum sicut teipsum. In his duobus mandatis universa lex pendet, et prophetae. Congregatis autem pharisaeis, interrogavit eos Iesus, dicens: Quid vobis videtur de Christo? cuius filius est? Dicunt ei: David. Ait illis: Quomodo ergo David in spiritu vocat eum Dominum, dicens: Dixit Dominus Domino meo, sede a dextris meis, donec ponam inimicos tuos scabellum pedum tuorum? Si ergo David vocat eum Dominum, quomodo filius eius est? Et nemo poterat ei respondere verbum: neque ausus fuit quisquam ex illa die eum amplius interrogare.

Credo.

## 7. Offertory — Dan 9:17,18,19, adapted — no. 1608

Oravi Deum meum ego Daniel, dicens: Exaudi, Domine, preces servi tui: illumina faciem tuam super sanctuarium tuum: et propitius intende populum istum, super quem invocatum est nomen tuum, Deus.

## 8. Secret — no. 1609

Maiestatem tuam, Domine, suppliciter deprecamur: ut haec sancta, quae gerimus, et a praeteritis nos delictis exuant, et futuris. Per Dominum nostrum Iesum Christum, Filium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitate.

Praefatio de Ssma Trinitate.

## 9. Communion — Ps 75:12–13 — no. 1610

Vovete et reddite Domino Deo vestro, omnes, qui in circuitu eius affertis munera: terribili, et ei qui aufert spiritum principum: terribili apud omnes reges terrae.

## 10. Postcommunion — no. 1611

Sanctificationibus tuis, omnipotens Deus, et vitia nostra curentur, et remedia nobis aeterna proveniant. Per Dominum nostrum.

## Boundaries and English presentation

The next heading is September Ember Wednesday; none of its text belongs here. Modern psalm equivalents are 119, 33, 102:1 and 76:11–12 respectively. The Epistle adds Fratres and its final doxology to canonical Eph 4:1–6. The Gospel replaces canonical verse 34's opening and includes both love commandments and the Son/Lord dialogue. The Offertory selects and rearranges Daniel's petitions with a self-introduction: continuous Dan 9:17–19 is context, not its exact text.

The selected English route is historical Cummiskey 1861 for the three orations and registered Douay–Rheims canonical study English for the scriptural material. Label canonical English as such wherever the Latin is adapted; leave the Latin adaptation visible and explain it editorially without generating an English liturgical substitute. The Gloria Patri and prayer conclusions remain printed cues. The Trinity Preface is appointed (1962 printed 293, no.1082), but its full text is not part of this transcription or selected reproduction plan.
