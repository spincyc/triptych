\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:start}
\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & Distinct voices confess
justice, seek mercy, command love, intercede and undertake fidelity. The
three prayers ask God to sustain the life the readings require.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & David's Son is
Lord; Word and Spirit establish the people whom Christ gathers, addresses as
its Head, and nourishes through his mysteries.\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Grace forms an undivided heart,
a patient common life and fulfilled commitments. Love seeks the neighbor's
good through truthful mercy and fitting correction.\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & One hope, the Lord's
judgment and the eternal remedy direct present healing toward perfected
charity and communion with God and the saints.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
