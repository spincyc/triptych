\section{Sources, Editorial Method, and Limits}

\subsection{Controlling sources}

\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=0.28\linewidth,labelwidth=0.25\linewidth,itemsep=0.55em]
\item[Promulgation] John XXIII, apostolic letter issued \latin{motu proprio}, \latin{Rubricarum instructum}, 25 July 1960, \latin{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 52 (1960), 593--595; see the \href{https://www.vatican.va/content/john-xxiii/la/motu_proprio/documents/hf_j-xxiii_motu-proprio_19600725_rubricarum-instructum.html}{official Vatican Latin text}.
\item[Rubrical code] \latin{Codex rubricarum Breviarii ac Missalis Romani}, with the decree and associated material in \latin{Acta Apostolicae Sedis} 52 (1960), 596--740; see the \href{https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-52-1960-ocr.pdf}{official AAS volume}.  This source controls the classes, precedence table, occurrence, transfer, calendars, and the general structure incorporated into the Missal.
\item[Typical Missal] \latin{Missale Romanum ex decreto Sacrosancti Concilii Tridentini restitutum, Summorum Pontificum cura recognitum}, editio typica, Typis Polyglottis Vaticanis, 1962.  The checked public facsimile is hosted by the Church Music Association of America: \url{https://media.churchmusicassociation.org/pdf/missale62.pdf}.  Its \latin{Rubricae generales Missalis Romani}, \latin{Ritus servandus in celebratione Missae}, \latin{Ordo Missae}, Canon, temporal and sanctoral formularies, Commons, votives, Requiems, and ritual formularies control assembly at the altar.
\end{description}

The numbered English propositions in this manual are close editorial paraphrases of the controlling Latin, not an official English promulgation.  Where a concise paraphrase could hide an exception, the rubric number is given so that the whole Latin provision can be read.

\subsection{Repository companions actually consulted}

The collection's source-audited \emph{Ordinary of the Mass} exposition was used to keep the stable Order distinct from the proper slots.  Its authoritative records are at \nolinkurl{liturgy/roman-rite/1962/ordinary/00-ordinary-of-the-mass/research/}.  The repository's \emph{Nuptial Mass: Missa votiva pro sponsis} and its visually checked \nolinkurl{propers/verified.md} record controlled the three nuptial insertion points summarized in Case 30.  These companions explain text and ritual sequence; they do not replace the typical Missal's rubrics.

No automated calendar, commercial hand missal, devotional booklet, or crowd-sourced rank table was used as controlling evidence.  No particular diocesan, national, religious, patronal, titular, dedication, or indult calendar is silently assumed.  Hypothetical local cases therefore remain conditional upon production of the approved proper calendar named in the case.

\subsection{What this manual establishes}

This is an edition-controlled method for answering three questions: what the 1960 rubrical code says occurs; what category of Mass that code admits; and how the admitted material fits into the 1962 \latin{Ordo Missae}.  The thirty-five cases test named branches of that method.  They are not substitute calendar decrees, permissions, or parish schedules.

The book intentionally does not decide present authorization to celebrate with the 1962 Missal, the current force of later universal or particular legislation, priestly faculties, sacramental canonical form, civil requirements, or a local Ordinary's practical direction.  Those are mutable questions for the competent authority and a dated current-law inquiry.  The warning is especially material for funerals, weddings, exposition, ordination, external solemnities, and every proposed proper-calendar or indult case.

\subsection{How to cite an actual answer}

A reproducible local answer should cite, in this order: the universal calendar entry; every applicable proper calendar or indult; the precedence-table row and any express exception; the rule for the losing item; the Mass-category permission and prohibition; each assembly rubric; and the exact pages of the proper, Common, or ritual text used.  It should also name the place, Mass form, number of Masses, the specified exercises or ritual action where relevant, and the as-of date for any present-law conclusion.
