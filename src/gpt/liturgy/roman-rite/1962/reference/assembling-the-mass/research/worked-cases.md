# Assembling the Mass — Worked-Case Ledger

## Check protocol

Each published case is recorded below in the required order: calendar facts; governing calendar; competitors and precedence; losing disposition; Mass category; assembly insertions; and answer check. “Universal + conditional proper” means the universal rules control but the local premise must be proved from an approved particular calendar. No case claims a named civil year.

Status `A` means the drafting calculation was repeated from the cited rubrics after writing. Status `I` means a separate internal publication pass independently recomputed the answer from the raw premises and incorporated any resulting correction. Neither mark claims credentialed external review, an approved local calendar, or present-law authorization.
