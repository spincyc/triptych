# Assembling the Mass — Rubric Index

## Classification key

- **D — direct rule:** a close English paraphrase or organized restatement of the cited Latin rubric.
- **I — necessary inference:** a result obtained by applying two or more direct rules to stated facts; it creates no new permission.
- **E — editorial workflow:** a reproducibility or error-prevention method proposed by this publication, not itself a liturgical rubric.
