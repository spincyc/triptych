## Required edition audit fields

For every admitted edition record, add: full title; responsible authority or
author; place, publisher, and date; edition or typical-edition status; language;
territorial scope; effective period; exact artifact identity and storage
disposition; rights basis; checked loci; source role; discrepancies; and the
highest evidence state actually reached.
