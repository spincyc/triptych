# Low-Mass chronology object audit

Status: **audited internal slice; canonical-record creation held**

## Purpose and evidence ceiling

This standalone slice reconciles the objects encountered in the complete
two-server Low-Mass chronology (`LM-001`--`LM-043`) with the pictorial
dictionary's proposed canonical inventory. It does not edit or populate the
aggregate inventory. The action rows were checked against
`altar-server-guides/research/ceremonial-inventory.md` and their rendered owner,
`altar-server-guides/shared/low-mass-ceremony.tex`.

This is an audit of repository claims and omissions, not a new external-source
verification event. The dictionary source audit still records zero
claim-level verified objects. Accordingly, the terms below remain candidate
records and receive no `obj-...` IDs or publication-ready state. Citations in
the action inventory show the evidence already claimed by the server-guide
project; they must be checked and bound independently under the dictionary
profile before migration into canonical records.

## Handling reconciliation

The selected model creates four handling classes that canonical records must
preserve:

1. **Assigned server handling:** Missal with stand, wine and water cruets,
   Lavabo basin and towel, bell, conditional Communion plate, and optional
   biretta.
2. **Limited contact without possession:** nearest lower chasuble edge at the
   elevations and, if locally assigned, assistance with the alb.
3. **Recognition only:** altar, tabernacle, credence, paten, pall, corporal,
   burse, and the covered chalice composition.
4. **Priest/sacristan only in this model:** chalice preparation, sacred-vessel
   handling, chalice veil removal and replacement, purification and fragment
   clearance, and recession with the prepared chalice.

The dictionary must not collapse `presents`, `pours`, `carries`,
`limited-contact`, and `recognizes-only` into a generic “server handles”
mark. In particular:

- the wine and water cruets have different assigned bearers but both return
  to the same bearer who presented them;
- the Communion plate is not the paten and does not authorize contact with
  fragments;
- pouring into a chalice held by the priest is not possession of the chalice;
- carrying the Missal and stand does not authorize contact with the adjacent
  covered chalice;
- touching the lower chasuble edge is a momentary ceremonial assistance, not
  vestment preparation; and
- optional local handling of the biretta or alb must remain visibly
  conditional.
