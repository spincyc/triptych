## Pectoral-cross boundary

The figure depicts one plain cross-shaped pendant and one integral bail. It
does not copy the ornate morphology of Metropolitan Museum object 810813 and
does not present that object's gems, enamel, filigree, pearls, dimensions, or
chain as normative. No chain or cord is drawn, so the figure cannot teach one
suspension pattern as universal. The checked collection record is used only
as material evidence that an integral-bail pectoral-cross construction exists.

Visual inspection found no second object, corpus, heraldry, inscription,
ornament, person, display support, label, border, watermark, or rectangular
background.

## Watterson-bugia boundary

The figure is an isolated reconstruction from the registered Watterson
documentary photograph. Its low circular pan, stepped central socket, small
feet, and candle follow visible features of that one exemplar. The apparent
form extending toward picture-left belongs to a separate display object and
does not establish a handle; the corrected asset therefore has no handle,
grip, rod, or lateral projection. The record expressly prevents the depicted
details from being generalized as the universal form of a bugia.

The lighted candle is also consistent with *Cæremoniale Episcoporum* I.XX.1,
but that locus controls only the small candle-bearing instrument and its
ceremonial state. It does not control the exemplar's pan, feet, dimensions,
ornament, or the presence of any handle. Visual inspection found no retained museum setting,
display card, books, table, surrounding objects, person, label, border,
watermark, or rectangular background.
