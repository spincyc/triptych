# Isolated Lavabo basin and manuterge artwork audit

Audit date: 2026-07-28

## Result

Two source-minimal drawings give the Offertory Lavabo basin and manuterge
clear separate identities:

- `RPD-FIG-service-objects-0107-iso-lavabo-basin-v2.png` depicts only one
  complete shallow empty bowl.
- `RPD-FIG-linens-0007-iso-manuterge-v2.png` depicts only one complete broad
  plain rectangular towel with one simple fold.

The combined
`RPD-FIG-service-objects-0106-lavabo-basin-manuterge.png` remains as one
relationship view on the manuterge record. Its explicit `render_owner`
prevents the shared formation from being repeated on the basin record.

## Source and generation control

The 1962 *Missale Romanum* controls the presence and Offertory use of the
small basin and manuterge. It supplies no basin material, dimensions,
ornament, or silhouette and no towel material, dimensions, ornament, or
fold. Joseph Braun's 1910 terminology note controls only the historical
English name “manuterge” and its distinction from the sacristy towel. The
Met lavabo record remains variation and exclusion evidence rather than a
Mass exemplar.

The prior project-generated paired plate was supplied to each generation
only for its restrained graphite field-guide style and minimal recognition
class. It is production provenance, not source evidence. The canonical
artwork manifest preserves both exact prompts, the unexposed built-in
generator identity, received chroma-output identities, local
background-removal step, final normalized identities, source ceilings,
corrections, rights basis, consumer sets, and review results.

## Boundary and technical review

Local background removal replaced each generated chroma-key field with
transparency without changing the depicted object. The selected outputs were
then stripped and converted to 8-bit grayscale-plus-alpha PNGs. Their custody
rows declare `boundary_treatment = "transparent"`, so the drawings blend into
the TeX page ground rather than appearing as floating rectangles.

Both complete contours and all diagnostic parts remain visible with generous
padding. Full-size inspection found no companion object, text, watermark,
border, floor, cast shadow, reflection, green fringe, accidental crop, or
unsafe action. At the declared maximum placements both files remain above
300 effective DPI.

## Alpha concerns

Source integrity passes through the claim-verified Missal bindings and the
explicit morphology ceiling. Rights pass through the recorded
project-generation chain. Safety passes because neither drawing shows a
handler or action. Reproducibility and artifact identity pass through the
exact prompts and byte identities. Mechanical validity and basic visual
usability remain subject to rebuild and full-page review in every affected
consumer before installation.
