# Single altar-cruet artwork review

Reviewed on: 2026-07-28

## Decision

`RPD-FIG-service-objects-0104-single-altar-cruet-v1-alpha.png` is accepted
as the canonical isolated recognition view for `obj-altar-cruet`.

The figure depicts one small, stable, footed pouring vessel with a lip,
handle, and hinged lid. Those features fit within the variation documented by
the checked Gorham catalog and Metropolitan Museum exemplars. The figure does
not establish its metal, capacity, ornament, closure, handle, foot, or exact
silhouette as universal. TeX must own the object name and this variation
boundary.

The asset is an 8-bit grayscale-alpha PNG with a transparent boundary. Visual
inspection found one complete vessel, no companion cruet, tray, contents,
letters, labels, cross, watermark, border, floating rectangular ground, or
cropped diagnostic part.

## Relationship view

`RPD-FIG-service-objects-0103-paired-cruets-v2-alpha.png` remains accepted as
a separate, explicitly labeled relationship view: two altar cruets are
ordinarily prepared together for the wine-and-water service. It is not the
canonical isolated object identity and must not imply a universal left-right
order, marking, closure, handle, tray, material, or silhouette.
