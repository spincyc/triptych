# Presentation research

Status: **agreed design brief; untested in finished dictionary plates**
Audit date: 2026-07-27

## Agreed format

- US Letter portrait.
- Monochrome graphite/pencil drawings on white.
- Adult general-reference wording in the comprehensive volume.
- Image-dominant plates, approximately 65--75 percent artwork.
- Several images per page: normally six to ten distinctive objects, four to
  six confusable objects, two or three multi-view vestments, or one major
  composition with detail insets.
- English and Latin names on entries; pronunciation only in a selective
  terminal appendix and selectively at first use in the young-server edition.
- Numbered endnotes rather than citations crowding the plates, except for a
  necessary local status qualification.
- Brief symbolism only where sourced and useful.

## Ambiguity strategy

An isolated portrait is insufficient when silhouette does not identify the
object. Confusable linens, vestments, nested vessel parts, and objects known
mainly in use receive compact folded, unfolded, reverse, worn, placed, or
in-use insets. Relative scale is preserved where diagnostic; otherwise TeX
states `not to common scale` or supplies a sourced scale.

## Reader order

The comprehensive leaf opens with title, a compact plate/symbol key, and the
actual sanctuary-composition plate. It proceeds directly into functional
plates. Historical material follows chronologically and then by type.
Pronunciation, bilingual indexes, visual index, variant notes, scope,
qualifications, sources, metadata, and rights appear terminally.

Derived editions begin with useful visual content and select the same
canonical records and art. They do not reproduce long policy, qualification,
or checklist front matter.

## Typography questions requiring proof

- Minimum legible label, endnote key, and compact handling-mark sizes at
  actual-size print.
- Maximum density before graphite values merge in office printing or
  photocopying.
- Whether narrow Latin headwords require a different line-breaking rule.
- How many context insets remain intelligible without turning the plate into a
  collage.
- Accessible text alternatives and reading order for web editions.

## Required presentation tests

Create representative dense-object, white-linen, multi-view vestment,
sanctuary-composition, and pontifical plates only after their facts and art are
auditable. Inspect each at full size, print at actual size, photocopy in
monochrome, test extracted text and reading order, and record revisions. No
such completed test is recorded here.
