## Synthesis ceiling

The witnesses jointly support a small pair of altar service vessels and show
that stable footing and a controlled pouring edge are functionally diagnostic.
A graspable handle is well supported by Gorham models and Met object 467483,
but Met object 200141 demonstrates that it is not universal. The generated
teaching plate therefore uses a deliberately generic handled pair for clear
server recognition while its caption says that form varies.

The plate may not encode:

- `V` or `A`, color, IHS, cross, grapes, wheat, or other motifs;
- material, transparency, lid, stopper, capacity, fixed order, or tray;
- left/right semantic identity; or
- one form as universal.

Wine and water remain TeX labels. The page identifies the pair by small
liturgical context, function, and handling, not by a supposedly universal
shape. A household decanter is distinguished by the paired altar-service
context and hand scale. A chalice is distinguished structurally and by the
server's handling boundary.
