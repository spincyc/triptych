## Required source and caption corrections

Before any reviewer marks a family publication-ready:

1. verify the exact Latin headwords and loci for Communion cloth, credence
   cloth, cere-cloth, humeral veil, gremial, and vimpae;
2. bind an official or competent ceremonial source for every handling claim;
3. classify each fact as universal Roman provision, condition, manual
   convention, documented variant, or historical lead;
4. remove generated ornamental details that lack an identification purpose;
5. ensure the child/server rendering says what the server may do without
   implying permission to handle every article pictured;
6. keep symbolism out until a brief statement is tied to an exact checked
   source; and
7. record the reviewer’s name/role, date, exact asset hashes or commit
   snapshot, decisions, and remaining exceptions in the production audit.
