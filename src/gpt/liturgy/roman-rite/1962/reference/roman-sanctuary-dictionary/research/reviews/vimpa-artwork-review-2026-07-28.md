# Vimpa artwork review — 2026-07-28

## Review boundary

This is an asset-local production review for three project-generated graphite
figures. It does not admit the files to the shared artwork manifest, revise a
canonical object record, or approve publication.

The checked evidence supports a deliberately qualified three-part treatment:

- Fortescue identifies the familiar headword *vimpa*, describes a thin silk
  scarf like a broad stole, worn around the neck and tied in front, and records
  matching use by the mitre- and crosier-bearers.
- *Cæremoniale Episcoporum* I.XI.6 expressly provides the mitre minister with
  a neck-hung oblong silk *velum* or *mappa* for supporting the mitre without
  bare-hand contact.
- The checked official locus does not expressly prescribe a *vimpa* for the
  crosier-bearer. Its section 5 instead says to cover the right hand with the
  end of the cotta. The separate crosier-bearer figure is therefore a
  Fortescue-attested customary context, not an official-text claim.
