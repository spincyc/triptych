## Generation provenance

All seven received rasters were generated on 2026-07-27 through the OpenAI
built-in image-generation interface. The interface exposed neither a model
identifier nor runtime version. No reference image, third-party artwork, or
composition sheet was supplied. Each call requested one isolated,
publication-oriented scientific/educational figure in historically attentive
monochrome graphite on a white ground. The prompts prohibited generated
lettering, numerals, arrows, captions, watermarks, borders, color, people,
architectural scenery, unrelated objects, mechanically impossible parts, and
anachronistic details.

The object-specific prompt requirements were:

- processional cross: one tall carrying staff with a crucifix, coherent socket
  and restrained representative metalwork;
- torch: one lighted wax candle, socket, broad wax catcher, carrying shaft,
  and lower grip or base;
- thurible: one closed pierced bowl and lid with foot, three suspension
  chains, a distinct lid-lifting chain, and upper ring;

  and one separate spoon;
