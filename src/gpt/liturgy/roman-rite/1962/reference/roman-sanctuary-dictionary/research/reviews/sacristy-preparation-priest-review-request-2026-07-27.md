## Core object questions

1. **Sacristy boundary — `prq-sacristy-composition`.** Does the proposed
   functional boundary include everything a server must understand about the
   room while correctly excluding ordinary office and utility furniture?
   What essential category is missing?

2. **Vesting table — `prq-vesting-table-form`.** Is “vesting table” the
   natural English category? Is *mensa ad paramenta* acceptable merely as an
   index phrase, or should the Latin headword remain unresolved until a
   period lexical source is found?

3. **Vestment storage — `prq-vestment-case-variants`.** Should a flat vestment
   press or broad shallow drawers be distinguished from a hanging wardrobe?
   Are these substantive variants, separate functional objects, or only local
   cabinet forms?

4. **Cross or image — `prq-sacristy-cross-form`.** Does the record correctly
   preserve the rubric's cross-or-image alternative? Should the final
   comparison show both rather than selecting a crucifix alone?

5. **Preparatory handwashing — `prq-sacristy-lavatory-form`.** Is the
   distinction among the priest's preparatory handwashing place, the portable
   Offertory lavabo set, an ordinary utility sink, and a piscina or sacrarium
   stated correctly? What should a young server be told never to infer from
   appearance alone?

6. **Piscina or sacrarium — `prq-piscina-status-art`.** Is the bounded
   functional description appropriate to a 1962 Roman dictionary? How should
   location, actual local identification, ordinary handler, and server
   handling boundary be stated? Are “piscina” and “sacrarium” safely indexed
   together here?

7. **Departure bell — `prq-sacristy-bell-custom`.** Is this customary signal
   important enough for the comprehensive and altar-server editions? What
   wording best keeps its use and operator local rather than universal?
