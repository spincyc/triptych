# Rights audit

Status: **open; no external artwork or source artifact cleared for publication**
Audit date: 2026-07-27

## Current owned material

Project-created organization, schema text, research scaffolds, captions once
created, and original editorial descriptions fall under the repository's
project-content terms, subject to the exclusions in `LICENSE` and
`THIRD_PARTY.md`. This statement does not relicense official texts, historic
images, museum photography, third-party drawings, fonts, or reference works.

## Artwork reference rule

Public visibility, age, downloadability, and citation do not establish reuse
permission. A reference image may be restricted even if the depicted artifact
is old or public domain. Every retained external byte requires an affirmative
artifact-level distribution basis under `guidance/sources.md`; otherwise keep
only lawful metadata or a remote/restricted disposition.

## Release hold

No plate may be installed until every depicted asset and reproduced text has a
recorded rights disposition, the final-page colophon is present without a
spill page, and the installed PDF's local notices accurately distinguish
project-created material from exclusions.
