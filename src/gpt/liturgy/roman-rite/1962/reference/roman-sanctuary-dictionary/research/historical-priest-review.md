## Questions for the priest or competent ceremonial reviewer

Record **yes**, **correction needed**, or **outside my competence** for each.

1. Does the opening boundary make it unmistakable that this is a selective
   historical plate set and not a ceremonial direction for 1962?
2. Are *flabellum*, *columba eucharistica*, and *turris eucharistica*
   intelligible and defensible headwords for the three early/medieval entries?
3. Does the text properly distinguish the Eucharistic dove as either an
   immediate vessel or an outer vessel enclosing a pyx, without claiming one
   construction was universal?
4. Is the Eucharistic tower caption sufficiently cautious about placement
   and suspension?
5. Does “historical in the revised Holy-Week route” correctly prevent the
   folded chasuble and broad stole from being mistaken for the guide’s 1962
   Holy-Week equipment?
6. Should the broad stole remain in this first audited slice, or be held until
   an exact pre-reform rubric locus is bound in addition to the historical
   summary?
7. Does the triple-candle caption accurately describe its place in the older
   Easter-Vigil entrance and its replacement in the reformed route?
8. Is any wording likely to imply that survival of an artifact, revival,
   privilege, or exceptional later use has been ruled out?
9. Are the brief symbolic statements for the flabellum and Eucharistic dove
   appropriate and no stronger than their cited sources permit?
10. Is any omitted historical object so necessary to understanding one of
    these six that the present comparison is misleading without it?

## Artwork review questions

Artwork review concerns whether the reconstruction teaches the supported
morphology. It does not authenticate a particular surviving artifact.

1. Does `RSD-HIST-001` clearly distinguish a hand-held flabellum, suspended
   Eucharistic dove, and architectural Eucharistic tower?
2. Is the corrected dove visibly supported only by chains, with no impossible
   pedestal or conflicting means of support?
3. Does the tower read as a receptacle rather than a miniature tabernacle
   presented as a universal form?
4. In `RSD-HIST-002`, does the left figure read as a chasuble folded upward
   rather than merely a short-cut chasuble?
5. Does the center figure clearly show a broad diagonal band rather than an
   ordinary narrow deacon’s stole?
6. Does the arundo show exactly three distinct candles and avoid resemblance
   to the Paschal candle?
7. Are any generated ornaments, fastenings, folds, chains, or proportions
   misleading enough to require redrawing before publication?
