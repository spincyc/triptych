# Three altar cloths: exact-source and artwork audit

Audit date: 2026-07-28
State: **source-audited altar-server recognition family**

## Exact governing control

The registered 1962 Vatican typical Missal facsimile was checked at
*Rubricae generales Missalis romani* 526, printed p. XXXVI / artifact PDF
p. 34. The locus directs that the altar be covered with three clean blessed
cloths. It controls the number, their clean and blessed state, and their
relationship to the altar.

It does not prescribe material, weave, dimensions, markings, ornament, folds,
edge drop, relative extent, arrangement method, or altar construction. The
altar-server entry therefore stops at recognition of the three-cloth covering
relationship and assigns preparation and disturbance to the sacristan rather
than inventing a server procedure.

## Confusables

- The **frontal or antependium** dresses an altar front; it is not counted as
  one of the three cloths in this entry.
- A **protective or dust cover** is not counted as one of the three cloths.
- The **corporal** is the separate wholly white linen spread in the middle of
  the altar for the sacred vessels; it is not one of the three altar cloths.

The first two distinctions are negative recognition boundaries only. This
audit does not promote a frontal or dust cover to a canonical 1962 record or
assert morphology, use, or status for either.

## Artwork disposition

The earlier `RSD-ART-002-altar-textiles.png` remains a held multi-object
research plate. Its cutaway combines altar cloths with unrelated textile
families and can make the explanatory layers look like construction courses
or cloths inserted between altar elements. It is not suitable for the
altar-server leaf.

The replacement
`RPD-FIG-linens-0006-three-altar-cloths.png` was generated without reference
images as a source-minimal, text-free graphite relationship drawing. It shows
exactly three plain cloth sheets separated above a single neutral thin
altar-top silhouette. It contains no masonry, frontal, dust cover, corporal,
cross, mark, dimensions, person, action, vessel, or ornament. TeX explicitly
labels the view as exploded and states that extents, hanging edges, folds,
spacing, and the support silhouette are illustrative rather than normative.

The received RGB file was converted to a stripped 8-bit grayscale PNG without
content editing. Exact prompt, hashes, dimensions, corrections, consumer,
rights, and review states are recorded in `artwork-manifest.toml`.

## Reliance ceiling

This family teaches only that the altar prepared for Mass has three clean
blessed cloths covering it, and that an altar server recognizes but does not
disturb them. It is not a folding, marking, measurement, preparation, altar
construction, or textile-material guide.
