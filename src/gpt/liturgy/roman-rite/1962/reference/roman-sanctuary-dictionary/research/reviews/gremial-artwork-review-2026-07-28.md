## Visual and production review

The first handling-context generation was rejected during independent review:
its crossed and parallel insignial bands over a broad outer garment taught an
impossible stole-or-pallium arrangement. It was replaced rather than admitted.
The replacement uses a deliberately plain continuous chasuble silhouette over
simple underlayers and contains no pallium, stole, crossed band, orphrey strip,
pectoral cross, or other insignial band.
