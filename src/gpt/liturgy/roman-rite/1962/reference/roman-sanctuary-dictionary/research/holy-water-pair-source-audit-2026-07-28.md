# Holy-water vessel and aspergillum source audit

Audit date: 2026-07-28
Status: **source-audited canonical alpha pair**

## Result

`obj-holy-water-vessel` and `obj-aspergillum` are admitted together as
related-ceremony records at the 1962 horizon. The bounded claims are:

- a portable metal vessel holds holy water;
- the sprinkler is dipped into that water and used in the named Asperges;
- in the checked solemn route, the deacon hands the sprinkler to the priest;
  and
- brush-ended and perforated hollow-globe sprinkler forms are documented,
  while the current plate depicts only the globe form.

No claim is made that the vessel's bail, tapered body, foot, visible
waterline, dimensions, proportions, finish, or ornament is universal. No
sacristan preparation or storage procedure, acolyte carrier, lay-server
handoff, or role outside the named Asperges is supplied.

## Exact witnesses

edition was acquired and matched its registered SHA-256
`378d281408154d869833e61d8b4d2dc4cc3ec16340c7d1128bf51fb592666d36`.

The preliminary p. 17 locator was rejected for this edition. The paragraph is
on printed p. 16; printed p. 17 is the corresponding locus in the revised
1943 edition.

### Fortescue--O'Connell seventh revised edition, pp. 17 and 79--81

The exact 1943 facsimile was acquired and matched its registered SHA-256

by the deacon to the priest, used by the priest, and returned. Other
ministerial branches on those pages are not generalized by these records.

## Artwork reconciliation

The original project-generated pencil assets correctly depicted one portable
metal vessel and one perforated hollow-globe sprinkler, but their normalized
467-by-700 and 750-by-500 pixel forms could not meet the 300-effective-dpi
consumer gate at the intended page placements. They remain registered as
held predecessors.

The image-generation skill produced two new high-resolution sibling assets
using the predecessors only as identity and composition references. The
exact prompts are retained in `artwork-manifest.toml`. The selected outputs
were converted to stripped 8-bit grayscale PNG without changing pixel
geometry.

Full-size comparison confirmed one coherent object in each image, restrained
pencil rendering, sufficient crop margin, absent lettering and action, and no
impossible connection. The vessel plate's bail, taper, foot, and water surface
remain explicitly illustrative; the aspergillum plate is bound only to the
documented globe variant. Exact received and normalized hashes, dimensions,
consumers, rights, prompts, and review states are in the manifest.
