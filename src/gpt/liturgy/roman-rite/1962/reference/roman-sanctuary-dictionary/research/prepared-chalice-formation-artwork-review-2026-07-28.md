# Prepared-chalice formation artwork review

## Scope

This review controls
`RPD-FIG-prepared-chalice-0001-formation-alpha.png`. The illustration is a
project-generated teaching composition, not source evidence. The checked
object records and their 1962 Missal loci control the relationship.

## Prompt and production

The built-in OpenAI image-generation interface produced a graphite object
study on a flat green removal ground. The prompt required, from bottom to top,
chalice, purificator, paten with host, pall, veil, and burse, with the folded
corporal separate because it is carried with the formation but is not a
stacked layer. It prohibited labels, arrows, borders, extra vessels, and
modern forms.

The selected output was converted to an alpha PNG by removing only the flat
green ground. Pillow was unavailable to the installed helper, so ImageMagick
performed a ten-percent fuzzy removal. Inspection confirmed transparent
corners, continuous pencil edges, no green rectangular field, and no clipped
subject.
The retained pencil subject was then normalized to grayscale while preserving
alpha, eliminating residual green edge color without introducing a
rectangular matte.

## Factual and visual disposition

- The covered three-quarter view reads as one prepared chalice.
- The adjacent exploded cutaway shows the required order without pretending
  that the pictured ornament or proportions are universal.
- The folded corporal is visibly separate from the stacked formation.
- No text, arrows, labels, watermark, unrelated vessel, or modern ciborium is
  present.
- The cross ornament and host marking are representative decoration, not
  evidence for a universal design.

The asset is admitted for the combined-formation role only. It does not replace
the isolated chalice, paten, linen, veil, pall, burse, or corporal studies.
