## Publication ceiling and safety

The altar-server leaf may teach recognition of practical/local service
equipment, including the possibility of separate lighter and snuffer tools.
It may not claim that Roman books prescribe it or that a server ordinarily
uses it. Flame work remains an adult-controlled, locally trained assignment.
The page gives only non-operational cautions: await assignment, keep flame and
hot metal away from persons and textiles, and confirm the taper is out before
storage. It does not teach ignition, approach angles, wick treatment, or
extinguishing technique.
