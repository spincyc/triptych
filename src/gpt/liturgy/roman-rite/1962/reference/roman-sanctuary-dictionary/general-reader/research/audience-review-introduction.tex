This packet asks whether a small group of familiar sanctuary objects can
orient an adult non-specialist without turning one pictured form into a Roman
norm.  It is a review sample, not a miniature comprehensive dictionary.

\textbf{What is admitted.}  The entries below are limited to canonical
records whose identity and 1962-horizon use have reached the source-audited
gate and whose candidate artwork is admitted for this bounded review.  The
drawings remain under review.  A pictured material, ornament, proportion, or
arrangement is representative only unless the entry expressly binds it to a
source.

\textbf{How to read the marks.}  \emph{Universal Roman} describes the
recorded liturgical status, not the universality of the pictured decorative
form.  \emph{ARTWORK UNDER REVIEW} means that the drawing is being tested for
recognition and physical plausibility; it is not an approval mark.  Evidence
caveats identify claim types that still require correction or a more exact
visual treatment.

\textbf{What this sample does not yet cover.}  It contains no sanctuary
orientation composition, linens, vestments, pontifical insignia, historical
objects, substantive-variant comparisons, symbolism entries, or completed
indexes.  Those omissions prevent this packet from answering what a complete
Roman sanctuary contained at the 1962 horizon.  Deliberate exclusion does not
mean that an omitted object or form is unimportant, non-Roman, or obsolete.

Review the entries first for immediate recognition and purpose.  Then ask
whether a non-specialist could mistake the illustrated form for the only
permitted form, confuse the chalice with the paten, or confuse an altar candle
with its candlestick.  Record corrections by stable object ID and page.
