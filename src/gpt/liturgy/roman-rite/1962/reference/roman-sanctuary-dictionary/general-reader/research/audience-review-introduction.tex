These plates use familiar sanctuary objects to orient an adult reader without
turning one pictured form into a Roman norm.

\textbf{What is admitted.} The entries below are limited to canonical records
with source-audited identity and 1962-horizon use. A pictured material,
ornament, proportion, or arrangement is representative unless the entry binds
it to a source.

\textbf{How to read the marks.} \emph{Universal Roman} describes the
recorded liturgical status, not the universality of the pictured decorative
form. Evidence caveats identify source-bounded limits on a particular entry.
