This bounded alpha uses familiar sanctuary objects to orient an adult reader
without turning one pictured form into a Roman norm.

\textbf{What is admitted.} The entries below are limited to canonical records
with source-audited identity and 1962-horizon use. A pictured material,
ornament, proportion, or arrangement is representative unless the entry binds
it to a source.

\textbf{How to read the marks.} \emph{Universal Roman} describes the
recorded liturgical status, not the universality of the pictured decorative
form. Evidence caveats identify source-bounded limits on a particular entry.

\textbf{Coverage.} It contains no sanctuary
orientation composition, linens, vestments, pontifical insignia, historical
objects, substantive-variant comparisons, symbolism entries, or completed
indexes. It therefore does not claim to show everything present in a Roman
sanctuary at the 1962 horizon. Deliberate exclusion does not
mean that an omitted object or form is unimportant, non-Roman, or obsolete.
