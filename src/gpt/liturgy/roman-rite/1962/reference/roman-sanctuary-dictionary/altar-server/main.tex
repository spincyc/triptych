\section*{\LitDisplay{Read the boundary first}}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
  \ObjectHeading{Handles}{What the server may handle}{KEY-H}
  The checked record assigns a server action. Learn the appointed cue and local
  method before acting.
  \Boundary{HANDLE ONLY AT THE APPOINTED CUE}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.47\linewidth}
  \ObjectHeading{Recognizes only}{What the server identifies but leaves alone}{KEY-R}
  Recognition is part of serving. It does not turn a clergy or sacristan action
  into a server action.
  \Boundary{DO NOT TOUCH UNLESS TRAINED AND DIRECTED}
\end{minipage}

\vspace{0.28in}
\begin{studybox}{The six objects in this alpha}
\textbf{Handles:} altar bells. \quad
\textbf{Varies by ceremony:} altar candle and candlestick. \quad
\textbf{Recognizes only:} sanctuary lamp. \quad
\textbf{Must not handle in this Low-Mass model:} chalice and paten.
\end{studybox}

\section*{\LitDisplay{Three confusions to prevent}}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The \textbf{candle} is the wax light; the \textbf{candlestick} is its
    support. An assembled drawing contains both.
  \item The \textbf{chalice} is the priest's sacred vessel; it is not an altar
    cruet.
  \item The priest's \textbf{paten} is not the Communion plate held beneath a
    communicant's chin.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{\LitDisplay{Scope and qualifications}}
This alpha renders six source-audited canonical records from the Low-Mass
altar-server queue: altar bells, altar candle, altar candlestick, sanctuary
lamp, chalice, and paten. It does not yet cover the server's complete route,
the complete altar composition, cruets and Lavabo objects, Missal and support,
Communion plate, vesture, sung or solemn branches, special Masses, Holy Week,
pontifical ceremonies, or related ceremonies. It therefore makes no claim of
dictionary completeness.

The official books control the printed liturgical claims. The generated
drawings are project-produced teaching aids, not evidence. Where an image's
particular morphology is not established by the cited locus, the page says so.
The paten candidate is omitted rather than teaching an ambiguous silhouette.
