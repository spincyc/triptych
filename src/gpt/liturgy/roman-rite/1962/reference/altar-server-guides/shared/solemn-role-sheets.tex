\section{My Role from Sacristy to Sacristy}

Start here after the MC completes \emph{Today's Mass}. Your trainer reads the
bold scene name; you say the cue, action, finish, and next scene. The SO numbers
lead your trainer to the complete team chronology; you do not need to learn a
different role before beginning your own.

Use the printed page range for your role on the start sheet. A shared boundary
page is intentional when two roles meet on the same page.

\begin{learnerbox}{Practise your role in four passes}
\textbf{Pass 1 --- point:} name every place and partner. \quad
\textbf{Pass 2 --- walk:} move with empty hands. \quad
\textbf{Pass 3 --- hand off:} use cold, empty practice objects and name every
giver, receiver, and final place. \quad
\textbf{Pass 4 --- recover:} your trainer withholds one cue; stop, secure the
object, face the altar, and look to the MC.
\end{learnerbox}

\newcommand{\SolemnEveryRoleTrack}{%
\par\smallskip\noindent
\begingroup
\small
\textbf{EVERY SERVER --- common track.}
Follow the MC's common posture and gesture cues (SO-005--060); candle bearers
remain upright at a Gospel genuflection. Use the marked ending (SO-061--070);
at \emph{Benedicamus}, use the parish's separate procession sheet, which is not
included in this guide. Sacred-minister and choir words cue timing, not
automatic answers.
\endgroup}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:mc:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{MC --- master of ceremonies}
\rolecard{Master of ceremonies --- MC}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=5.1em,labelwidth=4.4em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left,label=\textbf{Scene \arabic*:}]
\item \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-001, 005, 007.} Check every object and
route, form the team, receive all three birettas, put them in their marked
sedilia places, and return to the middle.
\item \textbf{First incense and lessons --- SO-010--017.} Assist the first
incense, clear and replace the Missal, then manage the books, seats, and
Epistle route. A1 serves the celebrant's and deacon's birettas; A2 serves the
subdeacon's.
\item \textbf{Gospel and Creed --- SO-019--029.} Cue the Gospel group. At
SO-024 remain for the book and take it to the credence while the acolytes use
their separate candle-return lane. Keep the deacon's burse lane clear.
\item \textbf{Offertory --- SO-030--040.} Signal the start, place the humeral
veil, keep the covered-paten lane clear, move the Missal, and protect the
concurrent inner Lavabo lane from the outer incense route.
\item \textbf{Canon and elevations --- SO-041--049.} Cue torches and bell. At
SO-044 receive boat and spoon from Bb, put unblessed incense into Th's open
thurible, and return the boat to Bb. Th returns independently at SO-048.
\item \textbf{Pax, Communion, ablutions --- SO-050--060.} Clear the paten and
fixed pax routes, cue A1's bell-to-plate route when the faithful receive, cue
the extended torches at the first wine ablution, and attend the Missal after
the credence reset.
\item \textbf{Ending --- SO-061--070.} Call the marked ending. On the normal
or third-Christmas route, take the birettas at SO-067, form the exit, give the
deacon his and the celebrant's and give the subdeacon his, lead the team, and
count every person and object before anyone unvests.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
\textbf{Recovery:} protect the object and route first; one quiet cue is
enough. Never send two servers to repair the same missed action.}
\label{solemn-role:mc:last}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:th:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Th --- thurifer}
\rolecard{Thurifer --- Th}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=5.1em,labelwidth=4.4em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left,label=\textbf{Scene \arabic*:}]
\item \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-002, 005--007.} With the trained adult,
prepare the thurible and safe station. Enter beside Bb, make the rehearsed
reverence, and go to the incense station.
\item \textbf{First incense and lessons --- SO-010--018.} Serve the blessed
first incense at SO-010--011, return the thurible safely, follow the common
posture track, then prepare for the Gospel after the Epistle.
\item \textbf{Gospel and Creed --- SO-019--025.} Serve the blessed Gospel
incense and join the procession without Bb. Hold the closed thurible still;
follow the SO-023 genuflection cue, then receive it from the deacon after he
incenses the celebrant and return it safely.
\item \textbf{Offertory --- SO-030, 035--040.} Serve the blessed incense, use
the outer lane while A1/A2 make the Lavabo inside, bow with every named
recipient, incense deacon, servers, and people, then complete the turning
genuflection and close the thurible.
\item \textbf{Canon and elevations --- SO-040--049.} Lead the empty-handed
torch party out and return with them. At SO-044 present the open thurible while
MC loads the unblessed incense; incense each elevation at SO-046--047. At
SO-048 return the closed thurible to its metal-safe station and come back
empty-handed, whatever the torchbearers' branch.
\item \textbf{Pax, Communion, and ablutions --- SO-050--060.} Follow the
complete team track, keep the pax and Communion lanes clear, and return to the
assigned base after the credence reset.
\item \textbf{Ending --- SO-061--070.} Follow the ending marked on
\emph{Today's Mass}. At SO-067--070 lead the normal or third-Christmas recession
empty-handed beside Bb with the boat; use the free-handed final-reverence
branch and check the thurible only in the sacristy.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
This model always has a separate Th and Bb; it has no combined-role route.}
\label{solemn-role:th:last}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:bb:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Bb --- boat bearer}
\rolecard{Boat bearer --- Bb}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=5.1em,labelwidth=4.4em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left,label=\textbf{Scene \arabic*:}]
\item \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-002, 005--007.} Take the boat, enter
beside Th, make the rehearsed reverence, and go to the incense station.
\item \textbf{First incense --- SO-010--011.} Give boat and spoon to the
deacon, receive them back, and return the boat to its marked station.
\item \textbf{Gospel --- SO-019.} Give boat and spoon to the deacon, receive
them back, and return to station or base. Do not join the Gospel procession.
\item \textbf{Offertory --- SO-030, 035.} Bring the boat at the MC's signal;
give it to the deacon, receive it back, and return it to its station while the
incense route continues.
\item \textbf{Elevations --- SO-044--047.} Give boat and spoon to MC, not the
deacon. Receive them after MC loads Th's open thurible without a blessing,
return the boat to its station, and kneel at base.
\item \textbf{Pax through reset --- SO-050--060.} Remain at the incense base
without the boat, follow the common posture cues, and keep the route clear for
the pax, Communion, ablutions, and vessel reset. Retrieve nothing until the
marked ending pickup.
\item[\textbf{Scene 7:}] \textbf{Ending --- SO-061--070.} Follow the marked
ending until exit pickup. On the normal or third-Christmas route, take the
boat, lead beside empty-handed Th, remain upright and bow the head at
SO-068, then give the boat to the adult at the sacristy metal-safe station.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
Offer the boat only at the named preparation; after every handoff, know its
next station before moving.}
\label{solemn-role:bb:last}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:a1:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{A1 --- first acolyte}
\rolecard{First acolyte --- A1}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=5.1em,labelwidth=4.4em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left,label=\textbf{Scene \arabic*:}]
\item \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-003, 005--009.} Take the right-hand
candle from the adult, enter paired with A2, place it at the credence at
SO-008, and go to base for the foot prayers.
\item \textbf{First incense and lessons --- SO-013--017.} Serve the
celebrant's and deacon's birettas in the printed order and help with the seat
route; A2 serves the subdeacon's.
\item \textbf{Gospel and Creed --- SO-020--024, 027--029.} Carry the Gospel
candle with A2, remain upright if a Gospel word requires a genuflection, and
return by the candle lane. Use the same celebrant-and-deacon biretta assignment
during the Creed; verify veil and cruets after the seat return.
\item \textbf{Offertory --- SO-030, 032, 037--039.} Bring the cruets and
follow the subdeacon at SO-032. As soon as the celebrant is incensed, serve the
Lavabo towel by the inner lane and return before Th incenses you.
\item \textbf{Canon and elevations --- SO-043, 045--047.} Give the model's
one Sanctus signal, one warning signal, and three distinct strokes at each
elevation. A continuous elevation ring is permitted, but it is not this
guide's default.
\item \textbf{Pax, Communion, ablutions --- SO-050--058.} Receive and fold the
humeral veil. Receive the pax from the subdeacon, answer \emph{Et cum spiritu
tuo}, then give it to A2 by the printed form. If the faithful receive, give the
warning, set down the bell, serve the plate, and give it directly to the
celebrant for the fragment check. Give both cruets to the subdeacon at SO-057;
receive both after the second ablution at SO-058 and return them.
\item \textbf{Ending --- SO-066--070.} On the normal route genuflect
empty-handed at SO-066 and take the candle; on the third-Christmas route take
it at SO-067 after the blessing. Use the object-bearer's head bow at SO-068,
recess with A2, and place the candle in its stand.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
Never leave the Communion plate or a cruet without its named receiver.}
\label{solemn-role:a1:last}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:a2:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{A2 --- second acolyte}
\rolecard{Second acolyte --- A2}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=5.1em,labelwidth=4.4em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left,label=\textbf{Scene \arabic*:}]
\item \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-003, 005--009.} Take the left-hand
candle from the adult, enter paired with A1, place it at the credence at
SO-008, and go to base for the foot prayers.
\item \textbf{First incense and lessons --- SO-013--017.} Serve only the
subdeacon's biretta and help with the seat route; A1 serves the celebrant's and
deacon's.
\item \textbf{Gospel and Creed --- SO-020--024, 027--029.} Carry the Gospel
candle with A1, remain upright if a Gospel word requires a genuflection, and
return by the candle lane. Use the same subdeacon-biretta assignment during the
Creed; verify veil and cruets after the seat return.
\item \textbf{Offertory --- SO-030, 033, 037--039.} Receive and fold the
chalice veil. As soon as the celebrant is incensed, serve water and basin by
the inner Lavabo lane and return before Th incenses you.
\item \textbf{Canon and elevations --- SO-041--049.} Follow the complete team
posture and gesture track; keep the credence route clear for A1's bells and the
torch party.
\item \textbf{Pax through reset --- SO-053, 059--060.} Receive the pax
directly from A1, answer \emph{Et cum spiritu tuo}, and end the lay-server
chain. Carry the folded chalice veil to the Gospel side at SO-059; at SO-060
stay clear while the subdeacon takes the prepared chalice to the credence,
then help A1 reset the other objects.
\item \textbf{Ending --- SO-066--070.} On the normal route genuflect
empty-handed at SO-066 and take the candle; on the third-Christmas route take
it at SO-067 after the blessing. Use the object-bearer's head bow at SO-068,
recess with A1, and place the candle in its stand.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
Stay paired with A1 for every candle movement and check the path before
carrying the folded veil.}
\label{solemn-role:a2:last}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:tb:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Tb1--Tb4 --- torchbearers}
\rolecard{Torchbearers --- Tb1--Tb4}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=6.4em,labelwidth=5.7em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left]
\item[\textbf{Scene 1:}] \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-004--009.} Enter in two pairs with hands
joined; the torches remain at their prepared station. Go to base for the foot
prayers.
\item[\textbf{Scenes 2--4:}] \textbf{Lessons through Offertory --- SO-012--040.} Follow the complete
team track. When Th incenses the servers, bow with the named recipient group.
At SO-040 follow Th to the sacristy empty-handed.
\item[\textbf{Scene 5:}] \textbf{Canon and elevations --- SO-041--047.} The adult lights and
gives one torch to each bearer. Return behind Th, genuflect in one line, kneel
at the four marked places, and hold every torch upright and equally high at
both elevations.
\item[\textbf{Scenes 5--6:}] \textbf{Take today's branch --- SO-048 or SO-057.} On an ordinary
non-fast day without faithful Communion, leave after the chalice elevation at
SO-048. If the faithful receive or it is a fast day, remain through the first
wine ablution and leave at SO-057. Put the torches on the four named stands for
the adult to receive and extinguish; return empty-handed to base. Th's return
is separate.
\item[\textbf{Scene 7:}] \textbf{Ending --- SO-061--070.} Follow the marked ending, join the
normal or third-Christmas recession empty-handed in two pairs, make the
free-handed final reverence, and confirm all four torches at SO-070.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
The MC marks the departure branch before Mass; never decide it while holding a
lighted torch.}
\label{solemn-role:tb:last}

\phantomsection
\label{solemn-role:cb:first}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Cb --- optional cross-bearer}
\rolecard{Optional cross-bearer --- Cb}{%
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=6.4em,labelwidth=5.7em,labelsep=0.5em,align=left]
\item[\textbf{Scene 1:}] \textbf{Ready and enter --- SO-004--008.} Take the cross, enter between
A1 and A2, remain upright while carrying it at the arrival, and place it in its
stand before going to base.
\item[\textbf{Scenes 2--6:}] \textbf{During Mass --- SO-009--060.} Follow the complete team posture
and gesture track from the assigned base place. The cross stays in its stand
unless the parish has assigned another explicit duty.
\item[\textbf{Scene 7:}] \textbf{Ending --- SO-061--070.} Follow the marked
ending. On the normal route genuflect empty-handed at SO-066 and take the
cross; on the third-Christmas route take it
at SO-067 after the blessing. Remain upright and make the assigned head bow at
SO-068, lead between the candle acolytes after Th and Bb, and return the cross
securely in the sacristy.
\end{enumerate}

\SolemnEveryRoleTrack

\smallskip
Never genuflect or try to bow the processional cross while carrying it. When
Cb is absent, A1 and A2 remain a candle pair.}
\label{solemn-role:cb:last}

\begin{learnerbox}{Partner check before the team chronology}
Name the giver, receiver, and final place for every object in your route. If
any answer is \textit{someone} or \textit{somewhere}, return to that scene with
the MC before joining the full team rehearsal.
\end{learnerbox}
