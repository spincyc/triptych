\section{Your Role from Sacristy to Sacristy}

Find the role written on ``Today's Mass'' and use the page range on the start
sheet. Read your short route before opening the complete team reference. Each
row follows the eight-scene map. Shorter or optional jobs combine scenes in
which you wait. An action ID is your trainer's lookup key; you do not need to
memorize it.

% Keep this strip short enough to repeat on every independently printable role
% route. Moving it to a single earlier page would save a few lines, but a server
% handed only the assigned role pages would lose the voice, object-safety, incense,
% and missed-cue rules that apply in every scene.
\newcommand{\MCRoleAlways}{Hands joined when free; follow all-server cues and
sing publicly only if assigned. Use your named MC-032 incense duty and taught
object reverence. If a cue is missed, stop safely and rejoin at the next known
cue (MC-003).}

\begin{watchbox}{}
\textbf{How to read your role route.} Read down the scene column. ``Wait''
means stay at the named place, attentive and still, until the next listed cue.
The action IDs take your trainer to the complete team reference when a handoff
or branch needs more detail.
\end{watchbox}

\mcroleheading{MC --- master of ceremonies}
\label{mc-role:mc:first}

\begin{mcroleroute}{MC}
  \mcrolerow{1. Get ready}{Check every named object and return place, confirm the
  roster and branches, and ask each server for his first three jobs. Receive
  the priest's biretta at the sanctuary, place it at the sedilia, make the
  arrival reverence, and meet him at the center foot (MC-001, 003--007).
  }
  \mcrolerow{2. Begin}{Kneel with A1 and A2 and make the quiet answers and gestures.
  Clear the center when the priest ascends. On the incense track coordinate the
  first handoff, incense the priest, and return the thurible to TH. Use the full
  escort-and-biretta route whenever the priest sits (MC-008--016).
  }
  \mcrolerow{3. Listen}{Cue L only on the selected alternate lesson route. Time the
  Gospel preparation, keep TH and the candle pair from crossing, pass the
  Gospel thurible between TH and the priest, and coordinate the sermon and
  Credo seating routes (MC-020--029).
  }
  \mcrolerow{4. Prepare}{Clear the Offertory incense route. With incense, cue the
  Lavabo after the priest is incensed; without incense, cue it when he reaches
  the Lavabo place. Keep the acolytes' inner lane clear of TH's outer lane, then
  face TH and bow before and after his one-double incensation of you. Make the
  quiet \textit{Suscipiat} with A1 and A2 (MC-032--036).
  }
  \mcrolerow{5. Adore}{Cue the torch entry, A1's exact bell signals, and elevation
  incense. Know whether torches return at MC-041 or remain until MC-048
  (MC-037--042).
  }
  \mcrolerow{6. Receive}{Cue the Communion bell and plate only if the faithful will
  receive. Coordinate both ablutions and the applicable torch return; then
  carry the Missal and stand from the Gospel side to the Epistle side
  (MC-043--050).
  }
  \mcrolerow{7--8. Finish / return}{Cue the day's posture, dismissal, blessing,
  and Last-Gospel branch. Take the biretta, form the recession, make the final
  reverence, give the biretta to the priest, and count every person and object
  in the sacristy (MC-051--057).
  }
\end{mcroleroute}
\label{mc-role:mc:last}

\mcroleheading{A1 --- first acolyte, bell, and Communion plate}
\label{mc-role:a1:first}

\begin{mcroleroute}{A1}
  \mcrolerow{1. Get ready}{Check the wine cruet, towel, bell and pad, Communion
  plate when needed, your candle stand, and your partner's lane. Receive a
  lighted candle from the adult, enter single file through the nave immediately
  before A2, form level only at the stationary arrival, set it at the credence,
  and go to the foot-prayer place (MC-001, 003--008).
  }
  \mcrolerow{2. Begin}{With MC and A2, make every quiet foot-prayer answer and its
  printed gesture. Remain at base while MC serves the first incensation and
  sedilia route (MC-008--018).
  }
  \mcrolerow{3. Listen}{Stand at base for the chanted Epistle and add no \textit{Deo
  gratias}. At the Gospel cue, take your candle with A2, form without crossing
  TH, stand level at the Gospel, remain upright with the candle at any prescribed
  Gospel genuflection, then replace the candle with A2 (MC-019, 023--029).
  }
  \mcrolerow{4. Prepare}{Take the wine cruet and present it first. For the Lavabo,
  present and receive the towel while A2 pours water over the priest's fingers.
  Use the inner credence lane; with A2, face TH and bow before and after your
  pair's one-double incensation. Make the quiet \textit{Suscipiat} with MC and
  A2 (MC-030--034).
  }
  \mcrolerow{5. Adore}{Give one Sanctus signal, one warning before the Consecration,
  and three distinct strokes at each elevation. Keep the bell low and still
  between signals; kneel at the assigned step (MC-038--042).
  }
  \mcrolerow{6. Receive}{If the faithful receive, give the one warning signal, then
  carry the plate level under each communicant's chin and complete its handoff and
  return. At the ablutions pour the first wine at MC-048; at MC-049 pour wine
  while A2 pours water (MC-043--049).
  }
  \mcrolerow{7--8. Finish / return}{With MC and A2 make the three quiet Last-Gospel
  answers when it is said. Take the exit candle with A2, use the assigned
  object-bearer's final reverence, recess single file through the nave
  immediately before A2, and set the candle in its sacristy stand for the
  adult to extinguish (MC-051--057).
  }
\end{mcroleroute}

\mcroleheading{TH --- thurifer, on the incense track}
\label{mc-role:th:first}

\begin{mcroleroute}{TH}
  \mcrolerow{1. Get ready}{Under adult supervision, test the chains and lid and
  prepare a steady coal. Process with BB in the chosen lead position, then take
  the thurible to the safe station (MC-002, 004--006).
  }
  \mcrolerow{2. Begin}{Bring the open thurible for the first incensation, receive it
  after incense is imposed, and wait while the altar is incensed. Receive it
  from MC after he incenses the priest, close it, and return with BB
  (MC-011--012).
  }
  \mcrolerow{3. Listen}{Return for the Gospel, receive the thurible after incense is
  imposed, lead the formation, and pass it to MC for the book incensation.
  Receive it back and hold it closed. If the Gospel text requires a
  genuflection, face the altar and genuflect while holding it closed; then
  return it to the safe station after the Gospel (MC-022--026).
  }
  \mcrolerow{4. Prepare}{Bring the open thurible for the Offertory and receive it
  from MC after he incenses the priest. Use the outer lane while A1/A2 complete
  the Lavabo. Then incense, with the stated bows and one double swing for each
  named individual or pair: MC; A1/A2; BB; T1/T2; T3/T4; optional CB; optional
  L; then the people. Close and return it (MC-032--033).
  }
  \mcrolerow{5. Adore}{Before the Consecration, open the thurible while MC adds
  unblessed incense from BB's boat. Kneel at the assigned station, incense the
  Host three times and the chalice three times, then close and return the
  thurible independently of the torch route (MC-037--040).
  }
  \mcrolerow{6. Receive}{Remain at the safe station and follow the applicable
  all-server posture; do not invent another incense visit (MC-041--050).
  }
  \mcrolerow{7--8. Finish / return}{After the Last Gospel, take the closed
  thurible, use the assigned object-bearer's final reverence, recess single
  file through the nave immediately before BB, and give it to the adult at
  the metal-safe station (MC-051--057).
  }
\end{mcroleroute}
\label{mc-role:th:last}

\mcroleheading{BB --- boat bearer, on the incense track}

  }
  \mcrolerow{2. Begin}{At the first incense cue, give the boat to the priest and

  return with TH (MC-011--012).
  }
  \mcrolerow{3. Listen}{At the Gospel preparation, give the boat to the priest and
  receive it back, return it to the safe station, and wait there while TH joins

  }

  it back. Wait clear during the remaining incensations. When TH reaches you as

  \mcrolerow{5. Adore}{Before the Consecration, give the boat and spoon to MC and
  receive them back after he adds unblessed incense. Return the boat to the safe
  station while TH takes the elevation place (MC-037--040).
  }
  \mcrolerow{6. Receive}{Remain at the safe station and follow the applicable
  all-server posture; do not invent another boat handoff (MC-041--050).
  }
  \mcrolerow{7--8. Finish / return}{After the Last Gospel, take the boat, use the
  assigned object-bearer's final reverence, recess single file through the
  nave immediately after TH, and give it to the

\mcroleheading{T1--T4 --- torchbearers}
\label{mc-role:t:first}

\begin{mcroleroute}{T1--T4}
  \mcrolerow{1. Get ready}{Check your numbered stand, partner, entrance place, and
  return branch. Process and recess empty-handed; reach your base place without
  touching a torch (MC-001, 003--007).
  }
  \mcrolerow{2--4. Begin / listen / prepare}{Follow the repeated all-server cues from
  base. Before the Preface ends, know whether today's route is ordinary or
  extended for faithful Communion or a fast day. On the incense track, face TH
  with your partner and bow before and after your pair's one-double incensation
  (MC-008--036).
  }
  \mcrolerow{5. Adore}{After the adult lights the four torches, take your assigned
  one at MC's cue. Form in pairs, make the taught object-bearer's reverence,
  divide evenly, and kneel. Hold the torch with both hands and use only the safe
  bow cued at each elevation (MC-037--041).
  }
  \mcrolerow{6. Receive}{On an ordinary day without faithful Communion, return in
  pairs after the chalice elevation at MC-041. If the faithful receive, or on a
  fast day, remain through Communion and the first wine ablution at MC-048.
  At the applicable cue, return in pairs, set every torch securely, let the
  adult extinguish it, and return empty-handed to base (MC-041--048).
  }
  \mcrolerow{7--8. Finish / return}{Remain empty-handed. At the final reverence,
  genuflect if the Blessed Sacrament is reserved at this altar and make a
  profound bow if it is not; then recess single file through the nave in your
  assigned order and report to the adult
  only for the tasks named before Mass (MC-051--057).
  }
\end{mcroleroute}

\begin{learnerbox}{One-minute role check}
Without looking, name your starting place, first object, first handoff, Gospel
waiting place, elevation job, Communion branch, and final object. If you miss
one, practise only that scene again, not the whole Mass. Your trainer may use
the action IDs to check the detail.
\end{learnerbox}

\section{Practice one scene at a time}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Point through the eight-scene map and name where your role waits.
  \item Walk scenes 1--2 with no objects and no words.
  \item Add scenes 3--4, still with no objects; then use cold, empty objects.
  \item Add scenes 5--6 and rehearse the ordinary and extended torch cues.
  \item Add scenes 7--8, the ending branch chosen on ``Today's Mass,'' and the
  final reverence assigned to your object or free hands.
  \item Add priest and choir cue words. Use safe lighted candles, and then
  incense, only under the named adult supervision.
  \item Run the complete form without prompts. Stop after each scene to repair
  one mistake, then repeat that scene correctly before moving on.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{coachbox}{FOR THE TRAINER: Day and ending branches}
At the named ferial Masses, kneel for the Collects at MC-018 and for the
Postcommunion at MC-051. From Ash Wednesday through Wednesday of Holy Week,
MC-051 also keeps the team kneeling for \textit{Humiliate capita vestra Deo}
and the Prayer over the People. The Canon posture runs from the
\textit{Sanctus} at MC-038 through the beginning of the Lord's Prayer at
MC-042. Ordinary torches return at MC-041; extended torches remain through the
first ablution at MC-048. At MC-056, free-handed servers use the
reserved/unreserved branch; object-bearers use the model's named head bow while
MC delivers the biretta.
\end{coachbox}
