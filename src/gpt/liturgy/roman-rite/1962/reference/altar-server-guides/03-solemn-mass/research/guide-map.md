# Guide map: Solemn Mass

- Stable guide ID: liturgy/roman-rite/1962/reference/altar-server-guides/03-solemn-mass
- Form: ordinary non-pontifical Solemn Mass with celebrant, deacon, and
  subdeacon according to the 1962 Roman Missal
- Reader: altar server, approximately ages eight to fourteen
- Core model roster: MC, thurifer, separate boat bearer, two acolytes, and
  four torchbearers
- Alternate role: optional processional cross-bearer (Cb is not part of the
  core roster); this guide requires separate Th and Bb roles
- Imported selections: SO-001--SO-070, A1--A22; C01--C13, C14A--C14C, C15,
  C16A--C16B, C17, C18A--C18B, C19--C20; and
  P01--P07, P08A--P08C, P09--P22, and SO-A01--SO-A12

## Reader order

1. Title and contents.
2. Family start page, MC-completed Today's Mass sheet, seven-scene overview,
   form boundary, roster, source labels, movement vocabulary, and safe reset.
3. Complete learner-first, sacristy-to-sacristy route for the child's selected
   role. Each rendered route includes the common team track and needs no
   separate common-route lookup.
4. SO-001--SO-070 coach-and-MC chronology, still complete for every roster
   state, object, handoff, branch, and waiting state.
5. Full-run ceremonial mastery check.
6. Pronunciation key, six easy-to-hard response lessons, form-specific voice
   map and Mass-order consolidation, followed by cumulative verbal drills.
7. Twenty-four cue-to-response cards; P01--P07, P08A--P08C, P09--P22
   Latin-to-pronunciation-and-meaning cards; and SO-A01--SO-A12 action and
   rehearsal cards.
8. Scope, edition, qualifications, references, and generation metadata.

## Branches and rejoins

The route accounts for separate Th and Bb service at four incense stations;
it offers no combined-role shortcut. Bb returns the boat to its station and goes to base after the Gospel
preparation; Bb does not enter the Gospel procession. MC's Gospel-book return
and the acolytes' candle return have separate lanes. At the elevation station,
MC loads the unblessed incense and Th returns the closed thurible independently
at SO-048. At Masses of the season from Passion Sunday through Holy Thursday,
where this ordinary route is otherwise applicable, SO-009 preserves the
opening Introibo exchange, omits Psalm 42 and its Gloria-Patri bow, and rejoins
at Adiutorium.

At a prescribed sung Gospel genuflection, the celebrant, MC, Th, and all other
free-handed servers face the altar and genuflect; A1/A2 remain upright with the
candles. During the Offertory, A1/A2 begin the Lavabo by the inner lane as soon
as the celebrant is incensed while the deacon and Th continue outside. Th then
incenses the deacon, MC, Bb, each torchbearer, optional Cb, and the returned
A1/A2 with each named recipient's bows; after the people he completes the
turning genuflection, closes the thurible, and leads the torch party to its
reset while the boat stays at its sanctuary station.

The adult lights, gives, receives, and extinguishes the four torches. The
ordinary torch branch leaves after the chalice elevation; the faithful-
Communion and fast-day branches remain through the first wine ablution at
SO-057, then return while the second ablution continues at SO-058.
A1's model route uses
one Sanctus signal, one warning signal, and three distinct strokes at each
elevation; a continuous elevation ring remains the Missal-permitted alternate.
The pax chain is fixed as subdeacon to A1 to A2. The Communion route is fixed
as bell to Communion plate, with the plate handed directly to the celebrant
for the fragment check before A1 receives it back. The ablution cruets pass
from A1 to the subdeacon and back after wine, then wine and water. The prepared
chalice returns to the credence by default.

The day branches identify ferial kneeling and the Lenten Prayer over the
People, whose audible conclusion receives A9; the double alleluia at Masses
of the season within the Easter octave; the Benedicamus replacement of
SO-064--SO-067 by a separately rehearsed procession sheet; and the
third-Christmas omission of the Last Gospel after the blessing. At SO-067 Bb
takes the boat for the normal or third-Christmas exit: the formation is
empty-handed Th beside Bb with
the boat; optional Cb between the candle acolytes, or the acolytes as a pair;
four empty-handed torchbearers in two pairs; MC; and the sacred ministers. At
SO-068 free-handed Th, torchbearers, and MC genuflect if the Sacrament is
reserved and make a profound bow if it is not; boat, candle, and cross bearers
remain upright and bow the head. Bb gives the boat to the adult at SO-070.
Special rites require separate sheets.

Sacred-minister and choir actions appear only as cues and handoff points. Lay
servers do not add Deo gratias after the chanted Epistle or Laus tibi, Christe
after the chanted Gospel, do not take the sacred ministers' quiet dialogue,
and do not insert a second Communion Confiteor. The subdeacon, not a lay
server, makes the three quiet Last-Gospel answers in the selected Solemn-Mass
model.

The C deck has 24 card IDs. Its shared print key states the form-specific
actor/mode distinction once; fixed cue faces print complete formulas or
verses, while the two variable-reading cards require the complete appointed
ending. C11--C13 are three study parts of A8, not response IDs. Composite C14,
C16, and C18 are retired in favor of singular-point C14A--C14C, C16A--C16B,
and C18A--C18B. The P deck has 24 card IDs:
P01--P07, P08A--P08C, and P09--P22. The Solemn action deck has 12 IDs,
SO-A01--SO-A12. The guide therefore selects 60 cards on ten complete duplex
sheets: four C, four P, and two action pairs. Every US-letter portrait sheet
places six cards on each face in a two-column by three-row grid; each back
mirrors every row for long-edge printing and has no blank card.

The action-card cross-view is anchored to the chronology: SO-A06 records the
concurrent Lavabo and closed-thurible reset; SO-A07 and SO-A03 distinguish the
SO-057 first-wine/torch-return cue from the SO-058 second-ablution cruet
return; and SO-A02 with SO-A08 records Bb's SO-067 boat pickup and
boat-bearing exit formation. SO-A09 rehearses the seven existing action
ranges, SO-A10 rehearses the documented pre-Mass branch call, SO-A11 isolates
the existing SO-050--060 acolyte handoff chain, and SO-A12 applies the guide's
existing partner, recovery, and safe-finish rules. The cards add no handoff
absent from SO-001--SO-070.

## Mastery evidence

The team completes a dry run and a supervised real-weight run, names every
object's giver, receiver, and destination, identifies every day, terminal,
bell, and torch branch, and demonstrates the safe reset after a deliberately
missed action cue: stop, secure the object, face the altar, and look to the MC.
For a missed verbal cue, ask for it again; after a second miss the trainer gives
the bank ID and returns the cue after three unrelated cards. Pronunciation
requires a competent human listener.
