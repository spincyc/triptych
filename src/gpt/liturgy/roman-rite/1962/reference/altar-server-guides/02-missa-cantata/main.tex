\section{Begin with Your Role}

A \textit{Missa Cantata} is a sung Mass without deacon and subdeacon. Write
down your assigned role, then turn directly to that role below. Your route
runs from the sacristy before Mass until every person and object is accounted
for there afterward.

\begin{learnerbox}{Before you walk}
Point out where you start, the first object you touch, where you wait during
the Gospel, and the last object you put away. If you lose your place, do not
guess: stop, hold any object safely and still, watch the MC, and rejoin at the
next cue you know.
\end{learnerbox}

\begin{watchbox}{Two voices}
MC, A1, and A2 form this model's quiet response team. The choir and people
make the public sung responses; join those only when the director assigns you
to sing. The priest or MC must settle incense, the entrance, the Epistle
singer, Communion, the torch route, and the day's sung parts before rehearsal.
\end{watchbox}

\begin{learnerbox}{Choose your role}
Turn to the complete route for the role written on your assignment:
\textbf{MC} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:mc:first}),
\textbf{A1} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:a1:first}),
\textbf{A2} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:a2:first}),
\textbf{TH} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:th:first}),
\textbf{BB} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:bb:first}),
\textbf{T1--T4} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:t:first}),
\textbf{CB} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:cb:first}), or
\textbf{L} (p.~\pageref{mc-role:l:first}).
The role sheet is your learner route. The whole-team rehearsal and complete
trainer chronology follow it.
\end{learnerbox}

\begin{description}[style=multiline,leftmargin=0.19\linewidth,labelwidth=0.16\linewidth]
  \item[1. Get ready.] Check your objects, line up, process, and land at your
  named place.
  \item[2. Begin.] Serve the foot prayers and the opening chants in your role.
  \item[3. Listen.] Follow the Epistle, Gospel, sermon, and Credo routes.
  \item[4. Prepare.] Serve the Offertory, incense branch, Lavabo, and
  \textit{Suscipiat} as assigned.
  \item[5. Adore.] Follow the Preface, torch, Canon, bell, and elevation cues.
  \item[6. Receive.] Follow Communion, ablutions, and the book transfer.
  \item[7. Finish.] Follow the Postcommunion, dismissal, blessing, and Last
  Gospel branches.
  \item[8. Return.] Form the recession and account for every object in the
  sacristy.
\end{description}

\newcommand{\GuideFormStatement}{Missa Cantata: sung Mass without deacon and
subdeacon, according to the Order and rubrics of the 1962 Roman Missal.}
\newcommand{\GuideBoundaryStatement}{The guide accounts for the complete
ordinary route of its declared roster, with separate public-sung and
quiet-minister voice tracks and explicit with-incense and without-incense
branches. Special rites require their own sheets.}
\newcommand{\GuideRosterStatement}{The core model uses an MC, two acolytes,
and four torchbearers. The with-incense branch requires a separate thurifer
and boat bearer;
a cross-bearer is added when the selected entrance uses a cross, and an
assigned lesson singer is added only on that alternate branch. MC, A1, and A2
form the quiet response team. No object or duty disappears when an optional
role is omitted: the corresponding branch is omitted or its reassignment is
written before vesting.}
\newcommand{\GuideSafetyStatement}{A young server needs supervised,
site-specific practice before handling flames, hot charcoal, vessels, a
Communion plate, or unfamiliar steps.}

(1962), names the selected ceremonial family. The directly read 1917 edition
and the Archconfraternity of Saint Stephen's 2022 handbook were used only as
subordinate traffic and discrepancy controls; the 1962 Missal overrides both.}
