# Guide map: Missa Cantata

- Stable guide ID: liturgy/roman-rite/1962/reference/altar-server-guides/02-missa-cantata
- Form: Missa Cantata, a sung Mass without deacon and subdeacon, according to
  the 1962 Roman Missal
- Reader: altar server, approximately ages eight to fourteen
- Core model roster: MC, two acolytes, and four torchbearers
- Branch roles: separate thurifer and boat bearer on the incense branch, optional
  processional cross-bearer, and an assigned lesson singer on the alternate
  lesson branch
- Imported training selections: MC-001--MC-057 and A1--A22
- Paired companion selections: R01--R07, R08A--R08C, R09--R22, and
  MC-A01--MC-A12; the full guide owns the card key and catalog pointer but
  renders no card faces

## Reader order

1. Title and contents.
2. Trainer-and-server Start Here path, Today's Mass fill-in, and the server's
   eight-scene whole-Mass map.
3. Form boundary, model roster, source labels, movement vocabulary, and
   incense safety.
4. Standalone MC, A1, A2, TH, BB, torchbearer, CB, and L role routes, followed
   by scene-sized rehearsal and mastery checks.
5. MC-001--MC-057 complete chronological trainer reference and branch summary.
6. Pronunciation key, six short response lessons, Missa-Cantata voice map and
   Mass-order consolidation, followed by cumulative verbal drills.
7. A compact printing, actor, roster, branch, and safety key that points to the
   paired downloads in the same catalog row. No card faces are embedded in
   this guide.
8. Scope, edition, qualifications, references, and generation metadata.

## Branches and rejoins

The guide declares both with-incense and without-incense tracks. It also
locates omitted Psalm 42, absent Gloria or Credo, the default priest-sung
Epistle versus the assigned-L-sung alternate, faithful-Communion torch timing,
the Mass-of-the-season Easter dismissal, Last-Gospel conditions, optional cross,
and site-specific traffic. MC-018 gives the named ferial Collect posture;
MC-051 gives the matching Postcommunion posture and, where applicable, the
Prayer over the People. Its conclusion receives A9. A procession-linked
Benedicamus has no blessing or Last Gospel.
At the third Mass of Christmas the blessing remains but the Last Gospel is
omitted. A branch that requires a special rite receives a warning and
separate-rehearsal direction rather than a compressed substitute.

Quiet ministerial answers and public sung answers remain separate. A chanted
Epistle receives no added Deo gratias, and a chanted Gospel receives no added
Laus tibi, Christe. In the selected model MC, A1, and A2 together make the
three quiet Last-Gospel answers when that Gospel is said. There is no second
Communion Confiteor.

At entrance the adult lights and gives the two candles to A1/A2, while optional
CB takes the cross from its named stand. MC receives and stores the priest's
biretta, takes it again at MC-055, and gives it back only after the final
reverence at MC-056. If a sung Gospel word requires a genuflection, MC and
every other free-handed server face the altar and genuflect with the priest;
TH also genuflects while holding the closed thurible, while A1/A2 remain
upright with the candles.

On the alternate lesson branch, L chants from the pre-positioned book and
returns empty-handed. The book remains on its lectern until the named adult
recovers it after the concluding sacristy reverence and L confirms it is
accounted for.

On the incense route, A1/A2 begin the Lavabo by the inner credence lane as soon
as the priest has been incensed while TH continues by the outer route. On the
no-incense track, MC instead cues the pair when the priest reaches the Lavabo
place. This core model assumes no clergy seated in choir; any such clergy need
a separate rank-and-count sheet. After A1/A2 rejoin, TH uses one double swing
for MC, the A1/A2 pair, BB, each torchbearer pair, and optional CB and L
separately before the people, with the named bows, closure, and reset. At
MC-056 free-handed T1--T4 and L genuflect if the Blessed
Sacrament is reserved and make a profound bow if it is not; the named
object-bearers remain upright and bow the head.

The paired companion has twenty-four integrated response cards, one for each
stable A-bank response or A8 study chunk. R01--R07, R08A--R08C, and R09--R22
combine cue recall, canonical answer, learner syllables and stress, sound line,
and meaning on one front/back pair. Response cards follow the six lesson groups
rather than numerical order and carry an `MC` form marker with the visible ID.
Every front gives the complete formula or verse selected for practice; a
variable-reading example is complete, facsimile checked, and labeled as an
example rather than a universal proper ending. The paired full guide's key
states the form-specific actor and mode distinction once.

MC-A01--MC-A12 follow the integrated response run in the companion. The
companion begins with its first response front on physical page 1; the action
run begins on the next odd page after four complete six-up response sheet
pairs. The full guide has no embedded card range. The final full guide is
twenty-nine physical pages; the companion is twelve. Production review
confirmed response fronts on pages 1, 3, 5, and 7, action fronts on pages 9
and 11, mirrored backs, border alignment, legibility, and installed identity.

The action-card cross-view is anchored to the chronology: MC-A01 links the
adult entrance handoff and MC's complete biretta exit lifecycle through the
post-reverence handoff; MC-A03 rehearses all four incense stations and returns
both boat and thurible to the safe station; and MC-A05 links both Lavabo cues
and the two-stage ablutions. MC-A08 links the rehearsed recovery rule to
MC-003. MC-A09 records the Gospel-genuflection object exceptions; MC-A10
records L's empty-handed lectern route and the adult's recovery; MC-A11 and
MC-A12 keep the procession-linked and third-Christmas endings separate.
Ordinary torches return after the chalice
elevation at MC-041; Communion and fast-day extensions remain through the
first wine ablution at MC-048. The cards add no handoff absent from
MC-001--MC-057, and every action-card face names Missa Cantata.

## Mastery evidence

The learner first names his role across the eight-scene map, rehearses one
scene at a time empty-handed, repeats it with cold objects, adds cues, then
works with flame or incense only under adult supervision. The final run checks
roles, handoffs, branches, recovery, and shuffled verbal cues. For
a missed action cue, stop guessing, hold the object safely, and watch the MC.
For a missed verbal cue, ask for it again; after a second miss the trainer gives
the bank ID and returns the cue after three unrelated cards.
