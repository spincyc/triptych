\section{Start Here: Trainer and Server}

\begin{learnerbox}{Your one job}
Learn to recognize the priest's cue, begin without delay, pronounce every
syllable, and finish the complete response cleanly---together with another
server when one is assigned.
This book teaches words only. It deliberately leaves every action to a
separate lesson with your trainer.
\end{learnerbox}

\begin{coachbox}{FOR THE TRAINER: A ten-minute lesson}
In this guide, the trainer is the responsible adult who helps the server
practise. Work on one small group at a time.
Read the cue aloud; let the server read and echo the answer; then cover the
answer and give the cue again. End by mixing
the new cue with two already learned. Correct the whole response kindly and
bring a missed cue back after three different cues. Stop while the server is
still attentive.
\end{coachbox}

\subsection*{Six small lessons}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=0.18em]
\item \textbf{Three anchors:} A9 \CanonLatin{Amen}, A13
  \CanonLatin{Et cum spiritu tuo}, and A15 \CanonLatin{Deo gratias}.
\item \textbf{Gospel and Preface:} A16, A17, and A19--A21.
\item \textbf{Short opening answers:} A1, A6, and A10--A12.
\item \textbf{The psalm chain:} A2--A5, one sense-sized piece at a time.
\item \textbf{The long answers:} A7, A8, and A18, joined only after their
  smaller pieces are secure.
\item \textbf{Kyrie and endings:} A14 and A22; then put every learned answer
  into the complete Mass-order route.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{coachbox}{FOR THE TRAINER: Print only what you need now}
Print the response course first (numbered pages
\pageref{course:first}--\pageref{course:last}). For detachable practice, open
the separate \emph{Low Mass Cue Cards} PDF from the same catalog row. Its 24
integrated cue, response, pronunciation, and meaning cards occupy physical
pages 1--8; printing directions and the actor key appear later in this guide.
\end{coachbox}
