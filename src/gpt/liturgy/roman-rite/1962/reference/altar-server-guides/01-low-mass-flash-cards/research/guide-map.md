## Content order and exceptions

The twenty-two physical cards follow the first applicable occurrence of each
selected response class in Mass order. Their stage labels are `FOOT/ALTAR`,
`READINGS`, `OFFERTORY`, `CANON`, `COMMUNION`, and `ENDING`. The Preface
dialogue is placed with `CANON` so the cards match the child booklet's learner
stage; that stage explains that the Preface leads into the Canon.

Every front contains one complete priest formula, psalm verse, or final
sentence used as the cue; no front uses only a last word or an ellipsis. Cards
15--17 identify their complete facsimile-checked variable-reading cues as
examples on the face. Cards 02--07 and 09--11 carry the applicable opening-
prayer or Psalm-42 qualification, and card 22 carries its Mass-of-the-season
within-the-Easter-octave condition. Every qualification is set at the same
fixed 11.5-point size as the cue. Every back contains only the complete
canonical response in bold. Card 08 uses the
first *Misereatur vestri* occurrence as the single exemplar for the repeated
*Amen* class; card 12 uses *Dominus vobiscum* for the repeated
*Et cum spiritu tuo* class; card 15 uses the checked Fifth-Sunday-after-
Pentecost Epistle ending for the ordinary *Deo gratias* class; and card 16 uses
one complete Matthew Gospel announcement. The distinct Easter-octave
double-*alleluia* response remains card 22.

The servers' long opening *Confiteor* is deliberately not compressed onto an
eight-up card. It is taught in the child booklet and mirrored trainer manual as
a full-width phrase ladder. Splitting that prayer into detached card fragments
would violate the one-cue/one-complete-response rule; shrinking it would defeat
the fixed readable type size. The child booklet and trainer manual own the full
Kyrie three-three-three pattern, response repetition, expanded branch
explanation, and the prohibition against adding a second Communion
*Confiteor*. The card face nevertheless retains every short condition needed
to prevent its exceptional cue from being practised as universal.

The selected two-server model has both acolytes make the verbal replies
together. Every back therefore bears `BOTH`; `FIRST` and `SECOND` distinguish
ceremonial actions in the booklets, not the response deck.

## Print map and boundary

This standalone card-face companion has six physical pages and no cover,
contents, instructions, actor key, lesson, assessment, parity leaf, or
terminal-reference page. Pages 1, 3, and 5 are eight-up fronts in natural
reading order. Pages 2, 4, and 6 mirror each row left-to-right for long-edge
duplex printing. The last sheet contains cards 17--22; its final two grid
positions are unbordered and do not create filler cards or blank backs.

Print US letter, portrait, two-sided, at actual size, flipped on the long edge,
and cut on the card borders. Each face uses fixed 11.5-point Libertinus Serif
body text. Front qualifications and back responses are bold, cues are regular,
and all remain top-aligned. The three header zones read
`NUMBER · PRIEST / LOW MASS / STAGE` on fronts and
`NUMBER · BOTH / LOW MASS / STAGE` on backs. Print hints stay outside the card
borders. The compact inherited revision and rights notice occupies unused
space outside the final back grid and adds no page.

The trainer manual supplies the printing, branch, actor, and use key. That key
must specify letter paper in portrait orientation, two-sided printing at 100
percent or actual size, long-edge duplex, natural row-wise cutting on the
borders, the two intentionally unused and unbordered final positions, and a
one-sheet front/back alignment check before the remaining sheets are printed
or cut. It must direct the trainer to read the complete `PRIEST` cue and have
`BOTH` acolytes give the bold response; honour every condition printed on the
affected face; use the identified Missal ending for the actual day's variable
reading; never insert a second Communion *Confiteor*; and keep pronunciation,
branch correction, object work, and any competence judgment in live,
responsible adult training. This leaf inherits its generation provenance and
remains governed by the owner-level source, response, card, rights, and
production records.
