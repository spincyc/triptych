## Superseded acceptance

The maintainer rejected the previous movement depictions and scene continuity.
Routes represented mainly by arrows between stationary endpoints did not show
enough of the actual travel. Several action close-ups isolated a figure or a
separately drawn altar strip instead of preserving the sanctuary surrounding
that action. Accordingly, the movement and scene-continuity acceptance of the
previous 44-page Low Mass pair is withdrawn. Earlier recorded hashes, page
counts and completed byte/layout checks remain facts about those snapshots;
they do not establish acceptance of the replacement. The earlier
[Low Mass fidelity record](low-mass-fidelity-2026-11-08.md) and
[7 November scene record](scene-revision-2026-11-07.md) remain dated history.

This correction affects the Low Mass route and the common visual rule for
altar actions across Low Mass, Missa Cantata and Solemn Mass. It does not
promote a manual convention, a local coordinate or generated anatomy into a
1962 rubric. [Source audit](source-audit.md) and the
[ceremonial inventory](ceremonial-inventory.md) retain control of actors,
cues, gestures, stations, handoffs and branches. Open source questions must
be resolved there, never by choosing whichever generated arrangement looks
plausible.

## Replacement composition and movement contract

1. Compose the architectural master `032`, form-specific appointments, movable
   objects, people, labels and routes in one fixed coordinate system. Altar,
   steps, tabernacle, crucifix and credence do not change between moments.
2. Save the **complete composed scene** once. Its close-up clips and enlarges
   that same saved scene. The wide and close views must retain identical
   people, hands, objects, facing, occlusion, altar geometry and appointments.
   An isolated actor crop or newly generated altar is not an action viewport.
   `ASGSaveScene`, `ASGShowSavedScene` and `ASGSceneViewport` in
   [sanctuary-scenes.tex](../shared/sanctuary-scenes.tex) express this shared
   source rule; final output must still be checked for registration and fit.
3. Keep actor layers free of altar, cloth strip, furniture and steps. Register
   feet to the intended tread and every resting vessel base to its actual
   support. A transparent layer without an altar is not itself a completed
   action scene. A separately labelled object/contact study, such as the
   cruet-kiss contact point, may supplement but not replace that scene.
4. Show the meaningful intermediate states of a journey where applicable:
   pickup, ascent or descent with the object, an altar-facing centre stop,
   equipment change, give-way and pass, placement, and empty-handed return.
   Each frame represents one moment and each actor appears once. Arrows can
   connect these moments; they cannot supply missing states. Do not inflate
   counts with indistinguishable poses.
5. Match facing, stride, posture, hands and visible equipment to the moment.
   Rear ascending figures do not depict forward descent toward the nave.
   A striding figure does not depict a stationary stop. Handed carriers must
   not be mirrored: anatomical right and left remain fixed even when their
   picture-side positions change.
6. Retain the existing **no depicted genuflection** rule. State any required
   gesture in words and let the trainer demonstrate it. A standing centre
   arrival or finish is not labelled as the gesture itself.
7. Preserve named roles and all object states between frames. Resting and
   carried forms must remain recognizably the same object, without duplicate
   books, bells or cruets introduced by the background. The two Low Mass main
   lanes must retain matching action and page identities. Sung-form action
   groups retain their own ministers and server assignments.
