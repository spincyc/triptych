## Source-class rule used in the publications

- MISSAL means a rule or text controlled by the identified 1962 Missal.
- OFFICIAL means another identified official act or book applicable to its
  stated matter.
- MODEL means this guide’s original synthesis within the declared
  ceremonial-manual family.
- LOCAL means a choice the priest or MC must settle for the actual place.
- SAFETY means original practical safeguarding instruction, not a claim to
  liturgical authority.

The labels do not erase source hierarchy. In particular, the handbook is never
used to override General Rubric 426 or *Ritus servandus* X.6.
