# Ceremonial inventory for the altar-server guides

This non-publishable record audits the two-server model governing
`shared/low-mass-ceremony.tex` and the complete action sequences rendered from
`shared/missa-cantata-ceremony.tex` and `shared/solemn-ceremony.tex`. The
Low-Mass inventory was checked on 2026-07-24; the two sung inventories remain
the snapshots checked on 2026-07-22. Clergy and choir appear below only when
their act is the cue for a lay server or when a rendered action row
deliberately distinguishes the public singer from the server.

## Keys and audit conventions

Place codes are `Sac` (sacristy), `Line` (entrance or recession line), `Nave`,
`San` (sanctuary threshold or rehearsed landing), `Ctr` (the middle before the
altar), `Foot` (center at the foot), `Altar`, `E` (Epistle side), `G` (Gospel
side), `Cred` (credence), `Base` (assigned server place), `Floor` (sanctuary
floor), `Step1` (first altar step), `Step2` (second altar step), `Pred`
(predella), `Sed` (sedilia or seats), `Lect` (appointed reading place), `G-stn`
(Gospel station), `Thr` (thurible station), `Torch` (torch station), and
`Rail` (Communion route). An asterisk means that the exact site is fixed in the
local sanctuary rehearsal. The Low-Mass levels are the declared model shown in
the diagrams; they do not assert that every real altar has the same stair
count or geometry.

Posture and movement codes are `St` (stand), `HJ` (hands joined), `Kn2` (kneel
on both knees), `Gen` (genuflect), `HB` (head bow), `DB` (deep or body bow),
`SoC` (sign of the Cross), `Pair` (matched movement), and `Rev*` (the reverence
demonstrated for the place or carried object). “Hold” means steady and upright,
not merely possessed.

Source classes are:

- `MR-GR`: 1962 *Missale Romanum*, *Rubricae generales*, exact numbered locus;
- `MR-RS`: the same Missal, *Ritus servandus in celebratione Missae*, exact
  section and paragraph;
- `MR-OM`: the same Missal, *Ordo Missae*, printed page or page range;
- `DMS`: Sacred Congregation of Rites, *De musica sacra et sacra liturgia*
  (1958), exact paragraph;
- `ACSS`: Archconfraternity of Saint Stephen, *Serving Low Mass with 2
  Servers (Ac1 & Ac2)*, current twelve-page role sheet accessed 2026-07-23,
  exact PDF page; a subordinate practical manual, not a controlling rubric;
- `CARMODY`: Charles J. Carmody, *Learning to Serve* (1961), exact printed
  page; a subordinate training manual, not a controlling rubric;
- `OCO1943`: Laurence J. O'Connell, *The Book of Ceremonies* (1943), exact
  printed page; a subordinate ceremonial manual, not a controlling rubric;
- `MODEL`: the guide's original practical route within the declared
  Fortescue--O'Connell ceremonial family, including the documented Low-Mass
  synthesis checked against O’Connell 1943, Fortescue 1917, ACSS, and Carmody;
  its exact controlling project locus is the named action row, and no
  uncollated 1962-manual page is implied;
- `LOCAL`: a site, assignment, or permitted-choice decision that must be fixed
  before rehearsal; its exact project locus is the named action row;
- `SAFETY`: an original safeguarding instruction, not a liturgical rubric.

In the interaction column, `H:` identifies a giver-to-receiver handoff and
`C:` identifies a collision, lane, or simultaneous-duty check. In the last
column, `OK` means that the row leaves every named actor, place, and object in a
defined state for the next row. A branch may jump over IDs; the rejoin is named
explicitly.

## Model rosters and reassignments

### Low Mass

The core model is exactly two servers, named **First Acolyte** and **Second
Acolyte** in full and **First**, **Second**, or **Both** in the learner action
labels. Both make every ordinary server response. From a front view First
begins picture-right, on the priest's right or Epistle side, and Second begins
picture-left, on the priest's left or Gospel side. First sets the common pace
and owns wine, towel, bell, optional biretta, the Epistle-to-Gospel Missal
move, and the Communion plate. Second owns water, bowl, and the
Gospel-to-Epistle Missal return. Neither server transfers the chalice veil or
chalice in this selected model. At the Offertory the priest places the loose
veil at the rehearsed altar resting place he will use after the ablutions.

The Communion-plate assignment is intentionally continuous: First takes and
carries the plate, leaves it at the altar for the priest's purification, and
returns the purified plate to the credence. This selected route follows the
checked O'Connell allocation and removes the cross-role transfer in the
compared ACSS route. The priest's paten and prepared chalice remain distinct:
the priest reassembles, veils, and centers the chalice at the altar and carries
it out. No rail-cloth route is part of the core model. Cruet kisses are a terse
trainer detail, not a child-facing action unit. A pax-instrument route is
outside the core and requires separate local instruction if appointed.

The model uses the floor for the prayers at the foot, the first step for the
normal kneeling places, the second step for cruets, Lavabo, the Gospel-side
Missal attendant, and the bell's resting place, and the predella for elevation
assistance and the first wine purification. The actual doorway, step mapping,
credence, reserved/unreserved reverence, Communion lane, and optional biretta
place are `LOCAL` and must be fixed before rehearsal. Diagrams show stable
standing or kneeling positions; they deliberately do not depict a
genuflection.

### Missa Cantata

The core model uses MC, A1, A2, and four torchbearers T1--T4. MC, A1, and A2
make the quiet minister's answers. MC owns the biretta route from entrance
receipt through exit delivery and owns the Missal/book transfer. A1 is the
bell and Communion-plate server; A1 and A2 receive the adult-lit entrance
candles, own the candle pair and cruets, and divide the Lavabo so A2 brings
water and basin while A1 presents the towel.
The priest chants the Epistle unless alternate lesson singer L is selected
before vesting. The with-incense track adds separate TH and BB roles; this
model offers no combined-role shortcut. CB is optional; without CB,
A1 and A2 process as the candle pair. The no-incense track removes TH, BB, and
every incense handoff. The adult sacristan is a named safety actor for coal,
the entrance-candle handoff, torch lighting and extinguishing, and final
receipt of hot objects. Optional CB takes the cross directly from its named
stand. This model does not offer a two- or six-torch shortcut.

### Solemn Mass

The core model is MC, thurifer (Th), separate boat bearer (Bb), A1, A2, and four
torchbearers (Tb1--Tb4). Both Th and Bb are required by this teaching route.
The optional cross-bearer (Cb) appears only on the entrance and recession route
unless a separate local sheet assigns more. Bb returns the boat to its marked
station after each preparation, takes it again for the exit, and gives it to
the adult at the metal-safe station. A1 is the model bell and
Communion-plate server; A2 is the model chalice-veil server. Those two divisions
may be reversed before Mass, never during a handoff. MC owns the three-biretta
entrance and exit routes and the Missal service. The adult sacristan lights
candles and torches, receives torches, extinguishes flames, and handles hot
charcoal; this safety actor is not
part of the lay procession roster. The sacred ministers are not part of that
roster either.
