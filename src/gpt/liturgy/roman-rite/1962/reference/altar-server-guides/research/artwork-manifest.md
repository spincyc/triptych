# Artwork manifest: 1962 altar-server guides

Audit date: 2026-07-27 (America/Chicago).

The illustrations are project-created editorial teaching aids showing a
generic ad-orientem Roman altar frontally from the nave. They do not establish
a rubric, sanctuary arrangement, or ceremonial fact. TeX owns every role
label, level marker, route, cue, and finish-state annotation.

## Size audit

The repository-wide 2026-07-27 screen found no installed PDF that required
automatic optimization. The 169 PDFs totalled 87.46 MiB; the median was
514 KiB and 18.7 KiB per page. One PDF exceeded 1 MiB, but its per-page cost
was low, and none contained an embedded publishing raster. Fourteen tracked
JPEG evidence artifacts totalled 7.90 MiB; their exact acquired bytes remain
unchanged because source records bind them.
