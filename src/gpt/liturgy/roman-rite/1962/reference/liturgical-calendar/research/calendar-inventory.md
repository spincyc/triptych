“Row” means a civil date with at least one printed universal item. Multiple commemorations on one date remain one row so that no false second date is introduced. Blank dates are represented by the scope rule: the seasonal feria or a legitimate particular celebration governs.

## Movable items removed from fixed-date inventory

- Holy Name of Jesus: Sunday from January 2 through 5, otherwise January 2.
- Holy Family: first Sunday after Epiphany.
- Seven Sorrows of the Blessed Virgin Mary: commemoration on Friday after the first Sunday of the Passion.
- Christ the King: last Sunday of October.
- Office of the Blessed Virgin Mary on Saturday: recurring on an otherwise unimpeded class-IV Saturday.

They are printed near month tables but are generated by rules, so they appear in the Temporale section.
