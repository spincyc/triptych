# Unit source matrix: received text, Scripture, and historical witness

## Purpose and certainty language

This claim-level matrix preserves coverage through the stable row numbers of the superseded thirty-three-unit exposition. Those numbers are now an audit concordance, not the reader-facing architecture. The matrix supplements, but does not replace, direct checking of the 1962 typical-edition facsimile. It records a first secure witness or a bounded terminus rather than supplying an invented date or author where the evidence preserves only gradual reception. It does not require every row to receive equal space, a Father, Aquinas, or the same analytical sequence. It also does not authorize project-authored English prayer wording or translation: English prose may state ritual function in the third person, while any reproduced English prayer wording requires an exact, identified historical or approved human witness.

- **Secure:** a dated primary witness, promulgated book, decree, or textually close witness supports the claim.
- **Probable:** the bounded range represents responsible historical synthesis, but the first form or direction of dependence remains debated.
- **Traditional:** a retrospective attribution is historically important but is not contemporary proof.
- **Unresolved:** the source record does not justify a unique date, author, or interpretation.

Biblical use is classified as **literal** when the rite quotes or directly prays the passage, **canonical** when several texts supply the doctrinal economy, **typological** when an earlier person or cultic reality is received through Christ, **allusive** when the relation is thematic, and **analogical** when a biblical action illuminates without instituting the later rite.

## Post-Mass annex

The Leonine prayers are deliberately absent from the legacy concordance. The 1962 *Ordo Missae* ordinarily ends with the Last Gospel and *Deo gratias*, subject to General Rubric 510's listed omissions. Leo XIII’s 1884 universal prescription, the 1887 immediate-after-Last-Gospel rule, Pius XI’s 1930 Russian intention, the Sacred Congregation of Rites' controlling 1960 exceptions, and suppression effective 7 March 1965 are audited separately in the source audit and the final exposition. They are historically adjacent to Mass without being a constituent text of the *Ordo Missae*.

## Outstanding review

Independent review is still required for the narrower manuscript history of each medieval private prayer, disputed Sequence authorships, and the direction of dependence among late-fourth-century Western Eucharistic witnesses. The Leonine exception corpus at the 1962 horizon is now controlled by AAS 52 (1960), 360; the central Roman promulgation locus for the 1886 revision remains unlocated. These limits are preserved rather than silently harmonized.
