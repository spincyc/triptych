# Sequence inventory and legacy thirty-three-step concordance

The table below is a research coverage map for the earlier edition, not the architecture of the revised publication and not an official division of the 1962 Missal. Its stable row numbers make older claims, source checks, and omissions traceable while the published exposition now combines or divides material at natural textual and ritual joints. The Missal, rather than the number, controls sequence and boundary.

This inventory preserves the editorial map used by the superseded edition; it is not a numbered list promulgated by the Missal and no longer supplies the publication's chapter boundaries. It acknowledges the thirty-three-step catechetical precedent in John T. McMahon's *Pray the Mass* (13th rev. ed., 1959) while ensuring that the complete Order remains auditable after the revision. `Fixed` means part of the stable order; `conditional` covers omissions or variants; `proper slot` is a stable position whose actual text varies by Mass.

## Completeness notes

- The nine proper positions are structural elements of the complete Order but their changing texts remain outside this work.
- The sermon is analyzed beside units 12--13 because the rubrics place it there, but it is not counted as an invariant ordinary text.
- Communion of the faithful is preserved in legacy row 31 even though its occurrence and detailed administration are conditional; the revised publication gives it a distinct movement because an exposition of the complete Order cannot reduce Communion to the celebrant alone.
- The 1962 typical edition moves directly to *Ecce Agnus Dei* at `Ritus servandus` X.6 and contains no second Communion *Confiteor* complex at that point.
- On the prescribed Lenten ferias, the prayer over the people belongs after the Postcommunion and before the final greeting (`Ritus servandus` XI.2), not as an undifferentiated part of the dismissal.
- Requiem, Passiontide, Holy Week, procession-following, and seasonal variants are identified locally rather than expanded into parallel orders.
- The Leonine prayers are treated in a final unnumbered appendix because they were historically prescribed after Masses without chant and remained operative in 1962. They are absent from the typical Missal's *Ordo Missae*, outside the Mass, and are not a thirty-fourth unit.
