# Sequence inventory: thirty-three analytical units

This inventory is an editorial map of the 1962 `Ordo Missae`, not a numbered list promulgated by the Missal. It acknowledges the thirty-three-step catechetical precedent in John T. McMahon's *Pray the Mass* (13th rev. ed., 1959), but reconstructs the boundaries so that the complete Order is covered at coherent textual and ritual joints. `Fixed` means part of the stable order; `conditional` covers omissions or variants; `proper slot` is a stable position whose actual text varies by Mass.

| No. | Unit | Kind | Principal 1962 Missal locus | Governing relation |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Entrance and approach to the altar | Fixed ritual framework | `Ritus servandus` II--IV; `Ordo Missae`, p. 216 | A ministerial body is assembled and approaches the place of sacrifice. |
| 2 | Sign of the Cross, *Introibo*, and Psalm 42 | Fixed, seasonally omitted in part | `Ordo Missae`, pp. 216--217 | Baptismal naming and desire for God's altar place the rite under divine initiative. |
| 3 | *Confiteor*, *Misereatur*, and *Indulgentiam* | Fixed, with role-specific forms | `Ordo Missae`, pp. 216--217 | Sins are confessed and mercy is implored before ascent. |
| 4 | Versicles, *Aufer a nobis*, *Oramus te*, altar kiss and first incense | Fixed; incense conditional | `Ordo Missae`, p. 217; `Ritus servandus` IV | Purification becomes approach, veneration, and entry into the saints' altar. |
| 5 | Introit | Proper slot in a fixed action | `Ordo Missae`, p. 217 | Scriptural procession gives the particular Mass its first ecclesial voice. |
| 6 | Kyrie | Fixed Ordinary chant | `Ordo Missae`, p. 217 | Ninefold supplication gathers need into Christological and Trinitarian mercy. |
| 7 | Gloria | Conditional Ordinary hymn | `Ordo Missae`, pp. 217--219 | Mercy flowers into angelic praise and confession of the Lamb. |
| 8 | Greeting, *Oremus*, and Collect | Fixed dialogue plus proper slot | `Ordo Missae`, p. 219 | The Church's dispersed petitions are collected and offered through Christ. |

| 10 | Gradual, Alleluia, Tract, and Sequence | Proper/seasonal slots | `Ordo Missae`, p. 219 | The Church receives the lesson and prepares to acclaim the Gospel. |

## Completeness notes

- The nine proper positions are structural elements of the complete Order but their changing texts remain outside this work.
- The sermon is analyzed beside units 12--13 because the rubrics place it there, but it is not counted as an invariant ordinary text.
- Communion of the faithful belongs to unit 31 even though its occurrence and detailed administration are conditional; an exposition of the complete Order cannot reduce Communion to the celebrant alone.
- Requiem, Passiontide, Holy Week, procession-following, and seasonal variants are identified locally rather than expanded into parallel orders.
- The Leonine prayers are treated in a final unnumbered appendix because they were historically prescribed after Masses without chant and remained operative in 1962. They are absent from the typical Missal's *Ordo Missae*, outside the Mass, and are not a thirty-fourth unit.
