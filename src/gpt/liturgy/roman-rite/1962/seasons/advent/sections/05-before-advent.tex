\section*{Before Advent}

Open your Bible at Psalm 24 (Psalm 25 in many Bibles). The man who lifts
his soul to God in the first verse has still to ask the way in the fourth.
Read verses 1--10 before the First Sunday. Whose name will you carry into
Advent with you? If the season has already begun, turn to \hyperref[sec:advent-daily-readings]{Readings
for each day} and find today. There you will find Scripture, a reading
from the tradition and words to pray. Read the chapter for your Sunday
before Mass when you can. On the evenings of December 17--23, the seven
antiphons in \hyperref[sec:advent-home-prayers]{Praying at home} give you a different name by which
to call on Christ each night.
