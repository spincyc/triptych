\section*{Before Advent}

If the First Sunday is still ahead, open your Bible tonight at Psalm 24
(Psalm 25 in many Bibles). Read verses 1--10 and notice what a man who
trusts God still needs to ask him. In \emph{Keeping Advent}, choose a time
to come back to prayer tomorrow. When Sunday comes, read its chapter with
the Gospel before Mass. If Advent is already under way, begin with today
in \emph{Readings for each day}. The prayers for December 17--23
are in \emph{Praying at home}.
