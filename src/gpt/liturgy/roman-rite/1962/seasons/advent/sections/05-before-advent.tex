\section*{Before Advent}

If the First Sunday is still ahead, read Psalm 24:1--10 after supper tonight.
Say the Our Father and pray by name for someone who needs help. When the
season begins, turn to \emph{Readings for each day}. Choose a time for daily
prayer you can keep and find a time when you can go to confession. Return to
the chapter on each Sunday when it comes.
