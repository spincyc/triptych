## Relationship classification

- Isaiah 55 and Matthew 13 are **officially correlated** through rain, seed, efficacious word, reception, and fruit.
- Psalm 65 is **responsorial** to Isaiah 55; its appointed response, marked Luke 8:8, also makes a documented synoptic seed connection without turning Luke into another proclaimed reading.
- The composed acclamation is **acclamatory**. It names Christ as sower but has no assigned Bible citation; that liturgical attribution is retained as composed Lectionary wording.
- Romans 8:18–23 is **semi-continuous**. Its creation imagery may share agricultural language with the other texts, but the Lectionary’s governing explanation does not establish it as part of the first-reading/Gospel harmony.
- Communion Antiphon B belongs to John 6 and the Eucharistic action, not to the Matthew Gospel branch. Any wider link is branch-specific synthesis, not official correlation.

## Rights, discrepancies, and disposition

The U.S. Lectionary, ICEL Missal translation, Psalm response, and antiphon translations are protected. This record retains citations, pericope boundaries, a minimal acclamation incipit, Latin incipits, and original analytical summaries only. It reproduces no complete reading, acclamation, or English oration and makes no permission claim. No discrepancy was found in the Lectionary path, one Psalm response, long/short Gospel boundaries, or antiphon assignments. Full U.S. publisher-specific altar-book English-oration collation and independent theological review remain outstanding; the leaf is working and source-audited at the stated layers, but not publication-final.
