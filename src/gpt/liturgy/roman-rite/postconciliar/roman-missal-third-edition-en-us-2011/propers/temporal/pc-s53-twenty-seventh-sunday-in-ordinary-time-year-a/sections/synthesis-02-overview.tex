\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & A threatened people prays; Isaiah accuses injustice; the wounded vine asks for life; Paul urges prayer and practice; Jesus confronts violent stewards. Gift calls for fruit, while wounded life seeks renewal.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & Christ, the rejected Son and cornerstone, gives fruitful life and perfects the vineyard. Augustine and Bellarmine understand its renewal through him; the Corinthians Communion option names the many as one body.\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Charity answers Christ's initiative. Justice, thanksgiving and truthful reconciliation bear fruit; renewed unity neither hides wrong nor silences the injured. Lamentations' Communion option sustains patient hope.\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & Lasting fruit reaches beyond visible success: God himself is the reward. Grace tends toward glory, sacramental nourishment toward bodily incorruption. Present belonging does not remove judgment or the need to persevere.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
