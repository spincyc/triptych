1. Preserve Lectionary 142's Isaiah 25:10a endpoint, Philippians' gap at
   4:15–18, Psalm 23's response 6cd, the Ephesians adaptation, and the two
   Matthew lengths. Verify Vulgate/Douay numbering against the edition-owned
   boundaries; never use verse-number equality as proof of textual equality.
2. Give public-domain biblical study text with an explicit statement that
   the approved U.S. Lectionary controls proclamation. Antiphon adaptations
   get exact locators and a distinction between their biblical source and
   their liturgical form. Do not reconstruct restricted words from a Bible.
3. Orations get W28's minimal Latin identifiers and original explanation.
   There is no AI translation route. Historical antecedents in
   `postconciliar-proper-translations-v1.toml` require direct source and
   relation checks; none is adopted as the present English prayer.
4. Complete the common owner's 2008/2011 collation and English-offerings
   exemplar search. If an exact book stays inaccessible, record the bounded
   second pass and state what the inspected witnesses do and do not establish.
5. Independently inspect the U.S. GIRM and applicable Ordinary Time rubrics
   for all adopted branches. Register or reuse exact evidence and declare
   controlling external owners in `review-dependencies.toml`, including rights
   inventories when binding ancestry does not already reach them. Preserve
   source-owner separation and the exact W28 dependency.
6. Record findings in `scope.md`, supported whole-formulary readings in
   `interpretations.md`, and actual production/review facts only in
   `production-review.md`. None of those stages is claimed complete here.
