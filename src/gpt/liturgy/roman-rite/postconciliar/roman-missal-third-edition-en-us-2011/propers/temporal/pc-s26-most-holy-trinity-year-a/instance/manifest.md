17. Proper Prayer after Communion.
18. Ordinary blessing and dismissal; no proper concluding formula is appointed.

## Weekday spillover

The solemnity occupies the Sunday on which the Ninth Sunday in Ordinary Time would otherwise fall; it does not erase Week IX. Monday, 2026-06-01, is the obligatory Memorial of Saint Justin, Martyr. The national calendar assigns its independent Weekday Cycle II readings, 2 Peter 1:2–7 and Mark 12:1–12 (Lectionary 353); it does not reuse Year A's Sunday readings, the Sunday's Gloria or Creed requirements, or the proper Trinity Preface. A later eligible feria may use an admitted Ordinary Time formulary only under the Missal and GIRM rules recorded in the collection guidance, not because this Sunday proper spills into it.
