\cmidrule(lr){1-4}

\dossierprose{Traditional author: Matthew the Apostle. Jacquier's disputed

NABRE introduction gives a ``post-A.D.~70 date'', ``probably at least a
decade later'', not a closed range. Jacquier reports publication in Judea for
Jewish Christians; NABRE proposes an unknown author, plausibly
at Antioch in Syria, for Jewish and Gentile Christians.}
