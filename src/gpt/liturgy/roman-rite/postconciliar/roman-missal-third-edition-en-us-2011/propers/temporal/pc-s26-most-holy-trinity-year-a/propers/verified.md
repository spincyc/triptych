## Occurrence and weekday disposition

The USCCB calendar places Trinity on 2026-05-31, displacing the Ninth Sunday in Ordinary Time as a Sunday celebration but not erasing Week IX. Monday, June 1, is the obligatory Memorial of Saint Justin, Martyr; the official [June 1 occurrence page](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/060126.cfm) assigns 2 Peter 1:2–7 and Mark 12:1–12 (Lectionary 353) within Weekday Cycle II. The Sunday Lectionary path, Gloria, Creed, and proper Preface do not spill into the memorial. Later eligible ferias remain independent; no weekday fallback target or transferred Trinity formulary is created.
