## Appointed formulary

The printed Latin heading is *Feria V post Sanctissimam Trinitatem: Sanctissimi Corporis et Sanguinis Christi*, with the rank *Sollemnitas*. Its transfer rubric assigns the following Sunday as the proper day where the Thursday is not observed as a holy day of obligation; the U.S. calendar applies that branch. The heading explicitly requires the Gloria and Creed. GIRM 64 governs the optional sequence, while the formulary's terminal rubric governs a procession after Mass.

| Element | Minimal identifier | Function and verification |

| Entrance Antiphon | cf. Psalm 81:17 | Appointed Missal alternative within the authorized Entrance layer; Latin and official English ICEL witnesses checked. |
| Collect | *Deus, qui nobis sub sacramento mirabili passionis tuae memoriam reliquisti* | Proper oration: Eucharistic veneration is ordered to the abiding fruit of redemption; p. 489. |

| Preface branch | Eucharist I or II | The Latin rubric appoints either Preface II or Preface I of the Most Holy Eucharist; the guide treats both as authorized alternatives, not simultaneous texts. |
| Communion Antiphon | John 6:57 | Appointed Missal alternative within the authorized Communion layer; Latin and official English ICEL witnesses checked. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Fac nos… divinitatis tuae sempiterna fruitione repleri* | Proper oration: present reception prefigures eternal participation in divine life; p. 491. |
| Eucharistic procession | post-Mass procession rubric | Permitted and commended branch. When it follows this Mass, the Host is consecrated at the Mass and the concluding rites are omitted after the Prayer after Communion while the procession forms. |

Gloria and Creed are required. The optional full, shorter, or omitted *Lauda Sion* sequence is a Lectionary/GIRM branch recorded by the formula leaf. The Missal supplies no proper Offertory antiphon and no proper Eucharistic Prayer insert. Because the formulary appoints either Preface I or II of the Most Holy Eucharist, GIRM 365(d) excludes Eucharistic Prayer IV, whose Preface is invariable; another compatible Eucharistic Prayer remains to be selected according to the Missal.

## Rights, variants, and disposition

Latin, ICEL, and sequence wording remains third-party material. Only incipits, citations, and analytical summaries are retained. The directly collated 2002 formulary is verified at the Latin-text, element, procession-rubric, preface-option, and official-English-antiphon levels; the official 2008 published-variation audit found no listed substantive change for pp. 489–491, while direct comparison of 2008 page-image typography and layout remains outside the audit. Exact complete U.S.-English altar-book collation and independent liturgical review remain outstanding. No discrepancy was found in the compared material.
