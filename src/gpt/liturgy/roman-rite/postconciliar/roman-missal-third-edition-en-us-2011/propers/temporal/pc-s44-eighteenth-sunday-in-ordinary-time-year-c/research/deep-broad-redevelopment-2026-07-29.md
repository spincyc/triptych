# Deep-and-broad redevelopment audit — PC-S42-C — 2026-07-29

## Prior-edition deficiency

Year C was the thinnest of the three research editions. It had sound textual
limits and five promising afterlife entries, but reception witnesses were
named without source bindings and the synthesis compressed Qoheleth, Psalm 90,
Colossians, Luke, poverty of spirit, property, and sacramental reception into
short assertions.

## Consequential corrections

The new dossier:

- distinguishes transience from nihilism;
- shows why Psalm 90's final petition prevents an anti-work reading;
- analyzes Luke's audience changes and possessive grammar;
- restores Colossians' omitted ethical context and corporate anthropology;
- distinguishes poverty of spirit from romanticized deprivation; and
- explains what artistic, musical, papal, and official-homiletic reception can
  and cannot establish.

## Negative results

- Luke does not decide the brothers' civil case or narrate a famine.
- No evidence establishes that Colossians was selected as commentary on Luke.
- No source permits identifying “rich toward God” with one exhaustive economic
  formula.
- Neither Communion alternative is resolved for a generic recurring Year C
  occurrence.

The 29 July pass added exact bindings for Augustine on Psalm 90 and
Chrysostom on Colossians 3 and records the remaining exact-source ceilings for
the other reception witnesses. The leaf still requires fresh production, web,
visual, and staleness review.
