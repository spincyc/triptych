\section{The texts of the Mass}
\begin{maptable}
Entrance & Cf. Psalm 87:3 Vulgate / 88:3 Hebrew & A plea that prayer be received and heard.\\
Collect & \textit{Omnipotens et misericors Deus} & Freedom from impediments for service to God.\\
First reading & Wisdom 6:12--16; Vulgate/Challoner 6:13--17 & Wisdom is sought by those who love her and goes out to meet them.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Psalm 63:2--8 Hebrew / 62:2--8 Vulgate; response 2b & Thirst, praise, remembrance and confidence in God's help.\\
Second reading & 1 Thessalonians 4:13--18, or short form 4:13--14 & Hope for the dead through Jesus' death and resurrection; the long form describes the common meeting with the Lord.\\
Acclamation & Cf. Matthew 24:42a, 44 & Watchfulness because the hour is unknown.\\
Gospel & Matthew 25:1--13 & Ten virgins await the bridegroom; the prepared enter the wedding.\\
Offerings & \textit{Sacrificiis praesentibus} & The Son's Passion is celebrated sacramentally and received inwardly.\\
Communion & Cf. Psalm 22:1--2 Vulgate / 23:1--2 Hebrew; or cf. Luke 24:35 & The shepherd's provision; alternatively, the disciples' recognition of Jesus in the breaking of bread.\\
After Communion & \textit{Gratias tibi, Domine, referimus} & Thanksgiving and petition for the Spirit to preserve integrity.\\
\end{maptable}

The opening Latin words identify the prayers; their subjects are explained below without supplying substitute translations. The Bible passages printed later are public-domain study English, not the approved U.S. Lectionary or the Missal's adapted antiphons. The ten ritual units include twelve separately identifiable texts because of the two alternative pairs.
