# Week XVIII Reconciliation — PC-S42-B

## Defect and correction

The leaf named the Week XVIII owner but its rendered Missal layer still
contained the Week XVII formulary. This was a substantive ownership failure,
not a numbering typo. The stale units were removed throughout the inventory,
orientation, appointed-text description, commentary, synthesis, branch table,
and research controls.

| Unit | Rejected stale value | Week XVIII value now consumed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance | Ps 67:6–7, 36; *Deus in loco sancto suo* | Ps 69:2, 6 (modern 70) |
| Collect | *Protector in te sperantium, Deus* | *Adesto, Domine, famulis tuis* |
| Offerings | *Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine, munera* | *Propitius, Domine, quaesumus, haec dona sanctifica* |
| Communion A | Ps 102:2 (modern 103) | Wis 16:20 |
| Communion B | John 6:35 | John 6:35 (unchanged but rechecked) |
| After Communion | *Sumpsimus, Domine, divinum sacramentum* | *Quos caelesti recreas munere* |

The corrected values come only from the canonical Week XVIII owner and its
registered *Missale Romanum* passage. This leaf does not duplicate the owner's
collation or claim inspection of protected U.S. Missal English.

## Compositional consequences

The correction changes the exposition materially:

- the Entrance now establishes urgent dependence on the divine helper rather
  than location in God's holy place;
- the Collect now supplies creation, governance, restoration, and preservation
  rather than a passing/enduring-goods contrast;
- the offerings prayer turns sanctification of gifts toward transformation of
  the offerers into an enduring gift;
- Communion A now deepens the manna sequence through Wisdom's heavenly bread,
  delight, and accommodation, while Communion B remains the direct return to
  John 6:35; and
- the final prayer orders heavenly refreshment toward protection and eternal
  redemption, not a Passion-memorial formula.

Wisdom 16:15–29 was checked in the registered public-domain Challoner artifact
so the optional antiphon is interpreted inside its own literary context.
Claims about the protected Missal translation remain descriptive.

## Residual limits

The first reading and Gospel are officially correlated; Ephesians remains
semi-continuous. Cross-connections between the Week XVIII orations, Wisdom
option, and Year B readings are source-grounded or expressly exploratory
synthesis, not claims about compiler intent. The two Communion alternatives
must never be narrated as one enacted sequence.
