# Ordinary Time Week XI — Canonical Formulary Audit

**Owner:** Ordinary Time Week 11, consumed on Sunday by `PC-S35`
**Last collated:** 2026-07-15

## Witnesses and limits

- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia (Typis Vaticanis, 2002), printed heading *Dominica XI per annum*, p. 461. The complete Latin formulary was checked on 2026-07-15 against the page-image facsimile at <https://media.musicasacra.com/books/latin_missal2002.pdf> (SHA-256 `0b458944824d2ee92854b9664f83e553af0342e0b15a9dc14b00e846405523d7`). The title page identifies the official book; the non-ecclesiastical host is only a secondary delivery witness.
- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia, reimpressio emendata (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2008), is the Latin base of the U.S. edition. The Congregation's official “Variationes et additiones in reimpressione emendata ‘Missalis Romani’,” *Notitiae* 44 (2008), pp. 368–387, <https://www.cultodivino.va/content/dam/cultodivino/rivista-notitiae/2000/notitiae-44-%282008%29/Notitiae-503-504-2008.pdf> (PDF SHA-256 `74c8d1740284984d0f4d64b2223edf805dadfa55aec8a92f8cfba9ab901eca0f`), lists the only *Tempus per annum* changes at pp. 457 and 471 and none at p. 461. This formula-level variation audit therefore found no listed substantive change to Week XI. The 2008 reprint page image itself was not directly inspected, so purely typographical or layout differences remain outside this audit.
- *Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America*, English, confirmed by the Holy See in 2010 and implemented 2011-11-27; official status: <https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/the-mass/general-instruction-of-the-roman-missal/girm-foreword>.
- Bishops’ Conference of England and Wales, *Antiphonary: Excerpted from the Roman Missal* (ICEL, 2010), p. 72, <https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Missal/Music/Antiphonary.pdf>. This official witness verifies the common ICEL Entrance and Communion antiphons but does not replace the U.S. altar book.

The controlling Latin Ordinary Time directions are *Tempus per annum*, rubrics 1–6; GIRM 363 governs selection among Ordinary Time orations. The formulary serves the Eleventh Sunday in Ordinary Time and is also a permitted source on eligible ferias under those rules. No complete official digital U.S. altar book was available; complete English orations are neither reproduced nor used as quotation evidence. Exact U.S. publisher, printing, and page collation remains outstanding.

## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | cf. Psalm 27:7, 9 | Plea for God’s hearing and help. |
| Collect | *Deus, in te sperantium fortitudo* | Grace aids mortal frailty so resolve and action may please God. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Deus, qui humani generis utramque substantiam* | Food sustains bodily nature and Sacrament renews it; aid is asked for body and mind. |
| Communion Antiphon A | Psalm 27:4 | Desire to dwell in the Lord’s house. |
| Communion Antiphon B | John 17:11 | Appointed alternative praying for ecclesial unity. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Haec tua… sacra communio… unionem praesignat* | Reception signifies and is asked to effect the Church’s unity. |

For a Sunday consumer, Gloria and Creed are required. *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 govern the dependent Preface/Prayer selection: an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface is used unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own Preface. In particular, GIRM 365(d) binds Eucharistic Prayer IV to its invariable Preface; the exact compatible pairing otherwise remains unresolved. The Missal supplies no proper Offertory antiphon or Eucharistic Prayer insert. On an eligible feria this owner may be selected under the Ordinary Time rubrics, but Sunday readings, Gloria, Creed, and Sunday Preface do not spill into the weekday.

The directly collated 2002 Latin text, element boundaries, and official English antiphons are verified; the official 2008 published-variation audit found no listed substantive change to this formulary, while direct comparison of 2008 page-image typography and layout remains outside the audit. Complete U.S.-English oration collation and independent review remain outstanding.
