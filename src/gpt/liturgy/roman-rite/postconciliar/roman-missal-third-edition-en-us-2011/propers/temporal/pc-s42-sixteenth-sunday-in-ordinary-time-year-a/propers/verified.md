## Relationship classification

- Wisdom 12 and Matthew 13 are **officially correlated**: sovereign divine power appears as lenient judgment and patient permission of repentance rather than inability to judge.
- Psalm 86 is **responsorial** to Wisdom, turning the doctrine of mercy and universal rule into petition and praise.
- The acclamation is **acclamatory** and comes from Matthew 11, not from the Gospel’s chapter 13 setting.
- Romans 8:26–27 is **semi-continuous**. The Spirit’s intercession must be studied fully without claiming that Paul was selected as an explanation of the weeds.
- Communion Antiphons A and B belong to the Eucharistic action and are mutually exclusive printed alternatives. Revelation 3:20 retains its ecclesial call to repentance; it is not merely a generic private invitation.
