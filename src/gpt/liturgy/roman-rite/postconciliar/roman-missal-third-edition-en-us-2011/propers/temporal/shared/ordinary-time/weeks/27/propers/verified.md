## Common textual elements

These are identifiers and original analytic summaries, not liturgical
translations or substitutes for the approved wording.

| Element key | Received identifier / biblical locator | Scope of the unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `entrance` | *In voluntate tua, Domine*; cf. Esther 4:17 | God's sovereignty over creation. Scriptural adaptation of Mordecai's prayer; the Douay–Rheims source passage is Esther 13:9–11, not its 4:17. |
| `collect` | *Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, qui abundantia* | Divine generosity exceeds merit and petition; mercy, forgiveness, and gifts beyond what the petitioner dares request. |
| `offerings` | *Suscipe, quaesumus, Domine* | The prescribed sacrifice and duty of worship are joined to the sanctifying accomplishment of redemption. Latin reproduction inspected; exact approved-English exemplar remains outstanding. |
| `communion-lamentations` | Lamentations 3:25 | The first appointed Communion antiphon. |
| `communion-corinthians` | cf. 1 Corinthians 10:17 | The alternative Communion antiphon. Its mention of the chalice is part of the liturgical adaptation; a bare rendering of 1 Corinthians 10:17 does not reproduce the entire antiphon. |
| `after-communion` | *Concede nobis, omnipotens Deus* | Sacramental nourishment and transformation into what is received. |

The Latin heading, entrance, Collect, offerings, first Communion, and beginning
of the alternative Communion occur on physical p. 290. The alternative ends
on p. 291, followed by the after-Communion prayer and the next Sunday's heading.
The Antiphonary keeps the entrance and both Communion alternatives together.
Its parenthetical psalm suggestions are chant accompaniment references, not
additional mandatory proper elements.

No Sequence, proper Week 27 Preface, special Eucharistic Prayer insertion,
Offertory antiphon, or distinctive concluding text occurs in these inspected
Week 27 blocks. This finding does not select any permitted chant, Preface,
Eucharistic Prayer, blessing, or local ritual. The leaf records those open
selection classes and the controlling rubric loci.

## Rights, variants, and research disposition

The exact Latin reproduction is restricted. The Antiphonary's ICEL text and
FDLC's embedded ICEL orations have no permission here for public Git or
downloadable study PDFs. The source-library artifact records control those
dispositions; the project license does not replace them. Retain short Latin
incipits, scriptural locators, and original analysis. Use identified
public-domain Bible text only as study Scripture, never as approved English
or a reconstruction of a Missal adaptation. Compose no prayer translation.

The separate `postconciliar-proper-translations-v1.toml` inventory carries a
Gelasian antecedent lead for the Collect, an offerings English `no-exemplar`
state, and the FDLC after-Communion exemplar. Its project-created translations
are not adopted for this study. The antecedent and its exact relation require
independent research before any historical or textual claim uses them.

The governing 2008 emended Latin altar book and a complete U.S. 2011
publisher-specific altar Missal remain uncollated. The official 2008 variation
list has now been independently acquired and checked: *Notitiae* 44,
503–504 (2008), printed pp. 367–372, physical PDF pp. 49–54, especially the
complete *Tempus per annum* subsection on printed p. 371 / PDF p. 53,
whose image was viewed. It lists changes at Missal pp. 457 and 471 only.
This limited list does not report a Week 27 change; its silence does not
prove exact identity of the 2002 digital reproduction and 2008 book.
The notice on printed p. 367 expressly describes a list directed to translation
work, alongside typographic and other corrections. The exact whole issue
matches SHA-256 `74c8d1740284984d0f4d64b2223edf805dadfa55aec8a92f8cfba9ab901eca0f`
and is registered as
`artifact.holy-see.notitiae.volume-44-numbers-503-504-2008.official-pdf-74c8d174`.
