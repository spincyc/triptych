# Deep-and-broad redevelopment audit — PC-S42-A — 2026-07-29

## Prior-edition deficiency

The prior guide accurately resolved the 2026 occurrence and contained a useful
appointed-text sheet, but its reception evidence was substantially richer in
the prose than in its four-entry binding ledger. Its main synthesis also moved
too quickly from “bread” to a single theme, with too little differentiation
among covenant speech, bodily feeding, word, Eucharistic reception, and the
independent Romans course.

## Research passes and results

| Unit | Wider context | Direct reception retained | Changed-register reception | Controlling limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isaiah 55:1–3 | Isaiah 54–55; Davidic covenant and restoration | Ancient sacramental trajectories; Augustine as theological analogue | Thoreau, *Walden*, “Economy” | No claim that free grace abolishes labor, price justice, or stewardship |
| Psalm 145 | Whole psalm's oppressed, hungry, prisoner, stranger, widow, and orphan field | Augustine, *Enarratio in Psalmum* 144 | Social-distribution application | No prosperity guarantee |
| Matthew 14:13–21 | Herod's banquet and John's death; Synoptic parallels | Chrysostom, *Homily 49 on Matthew* | 1783 political etching, *The Loaves and Fishes* | Eucharistic resonance is reception, not identity; no “sharing-only” reduction |
| Romans 8:35, 37–39 | Romans 8:18–39, including omitted v. 36 | Chrysostom, Homily 15; Aquinas, *Super Romanos* 8.7 | Arnold, *Dover Beach* 29–37 | No command to remain in danger or deny suffering |
| Communion A/B | Wisdom 16 and John 6 in their respective contexts | Augustine and Aquinas on John 6 | Liturgical reuse | Alternatives remain distinct and unresolved |

The new comparative dossier explicitly compares source, reception, and
liturgical juxtaposition. No protected U.S. Lectionary or ICEL wording was
added.

## Negative results

- No evidence was found that Romans 8 was selected to interpret Matthew 14.
- No checked source permits collapsing Matthew's narrated feeding into a
  “sharing-only” explanation or identifying it directly with the Eucharist.
- The 1783 print reuses the Synoptic loaves-and-fishes tradition but does not
  establish exclusive dependence on Matthew.
- The shared Week XVIII orations were not composed for Year A and do not
  resolve either Communion-antiphon branch.

## Publication consequence

This is a material redevelopment, not a metadata refresh. The source ledger
was reconciled on 29 July for the retained Augustine, Chrysostom, and Aquinas
passages. The leaf still requires fresh PDF, web, visual, and staleness review
before release.
