## Resolved structure and unresolved selections

The dated occurrence resolves the Year A path: Isaiah 55:10–11; Psalm 65:10, 11, 12–13, 14 with the response marked Luke 8:8; Romans 8:18–23; the Lectionary’s composed acclamation beginning “The seed is the word…” without an assigned biblical citation; and Matthew 13:1–23 **or** the shorter Matthew 13:1–9.

The following remain unresolved because date and territory do not identify an enacted selection:

- whether `long-gospel` or `short-gospel` supplied the proclamation;
- the `entrance-chant-selection`, `offertory-chant-selection`, and `communion-chant-selection` made under the governing norms;
- whether `compatible-preface-and-eucharistic-prayer` used a Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface, or an authorized non-IV Eucharistic Prayer's own Preface, with the compatible Prayer;
- whether the mutually exclusive `eucharistic-prayer-iv-with-preface` path supplied EP IV with its inseparable Preface; no proper insert belongs to this formulary; and
- whether `communion-antiphon-psalm-84` or `communion-antiphon-john-6` supplied the printed Communion-antiphon layer.

The Penitential Act is present unless the permitted Sunday sprinkling rite replaces it; `sunday-sprinkling` is unresolved. The Gloria and Creed are required Sunday structural units. Under GIRM 69, the Prayer of the Faithful should ordinarily be present at Masses with the people and is locally formulated; none of these is a proper-text row.

Ordinary Time rubric 5 governs the first path: use a Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own. GIRM 365(d) governs the mutually exclusive EP IV path: because this Sunday has no proper Preface, EP IV may be lawfully chosen, but its own Preface is then inseparable. No path is inferred here.
