The U.S. Missal was issued by seven approved ritual-book publishers, so publisher pagination is not universal. No complete official digital U.S. altar-book witness was available for this audit. The exposition therefore reproduces no complete modern English oration and bases its oration analysis on the directly collated Latin typical text. A later human collation should add the publisher, printing, and page locators of the actual U.S. altar book without changing the canonical ownership.

## Appointed formulary

The printed Latin heading is *Dominica I post Pentecosten: Sanctissimae Trinitatis*, with the rank *Sollemnitas*. The General Roman Calendar assigns it to the Sunday after Pentecost. The proper heading explicitly requires the Gloria and Creed; GIRM 53 and 68 supply the general structural rules.

| Element | Minimal identifier | Function and verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | *Benedictus sit Deus Pater* | Appointed Missal alternative within the authorized Entrance layer; official English ICEL antiphon verified at Antiphonary p. 84. |
| Collect | *Deus Pater, qui, Verbum veritatis et Spiritum sanctificationis mittens* | Proper oration confessing revelation of Trinity and Unity; complete Latin checked at p. 485. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Sanctifica… per tui nominis invocationem* | Proper oration asking that gifts and worshippers become an enduring offering; complete Latin checked at p. 485. |
| Preface | *De mysterio Sanctissimae Trinitatis* | Proper preface; the one God is confessed in distinction of Persons, unity of essence, and equality of majesty; pp. 486–488. |
| Communion Antiphon | Galatians 4:6 | Appointed Missal alternative within the authorized Communion layer; official English ICEL wording verified at Antiphonary p. 84. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Proficiat nobis ad salutem corporis et animae* | Proper oration joining sacramental reception to confession of the eternal Trinity and indivisible Unity; p. 488. |

Gloria and Creed are required by the rank. No proper Offertory antiphon is supplied by the Roman Missal, no proper Eucharistic Prayer insert is appointed, and the ordinary concluding rites remain in force. The actual Entrance, Offertory, and Communion music is a local selection under the governing U.S. norms and is not owned by this formulary record. Because this formulary appoints its proper Trinity Preface, GIRM 365(d) excludes Eucharistic Prayer IV, whose Preface is invariable. Another compatible Eucharistic Prayer remains to be selected according to the Missal; none receives a Trinity-specific insert here.

## Rights, variants, and disposition

Latin and ICEL liturgical wording remains third-party material and is not relicensed by Triptych. Only brief incipits, citations, and source-grounded summaries are retained here. The directly collated 2002 formulary is verified at the Latin-text, element, rubric, preface, and official-English-antiphon levels; the official 2008 published-variation audit found no listed substantive change for pp. 485–488, while direct comparison of 2008 page-image typography and layout remains outside the audit. Exact collation of the complete English orations against a named current U.S. printing and independent liturgical review remain outstanding. No material textual discrepancy was found in the witnesses actually compared.
