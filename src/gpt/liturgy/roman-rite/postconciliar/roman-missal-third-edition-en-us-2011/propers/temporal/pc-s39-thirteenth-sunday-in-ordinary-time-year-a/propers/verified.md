## Relationship classification

- 2 Kings 4 and Matthew 10 are **officially correlated** through receiving the prophet or Christ’s messenger and the promised reward.
- Psalm 89 is **responsorial** to the covenant mercy and blessing encountered in the First Reading.
- 1 Peter 2:9 is **acclamatory**, welcoming Christ’s word with the identity and vocation of a called people.
- Romans 6 is **semi-continuous** and remains an independent baptismal strand; any cross/losing-life link with Matthew is synthesis, not demonstrated selection history.
- The Week XIII orations and antiphons are shared across A/B/C. Their light, life, charity, and unity language may be compared under exact textual observation or disclosed synthesis only.
