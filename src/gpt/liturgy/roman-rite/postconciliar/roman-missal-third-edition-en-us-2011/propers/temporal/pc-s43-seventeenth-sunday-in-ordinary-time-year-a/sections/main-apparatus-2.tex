\textbf{Reception and doctrinal illumination}
{\fontsize{7.8}{8.5}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0em,topsep=0.15em,parsep=0pt]
\item St.~Ambrose, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/34012.htm}{\emph{On the Duties of the Clergy} II.8, 44--49}, on Solomon's prudent heart, hearing, and just judgment; and \sourceurl{https://fgh.perseids.org/read/latinLit/stoa0022/stoa052/opp-lat1}{\emph{Expositio Psalmi CXVIII}}, VIII, 2--4 and IX, 21--22, Latin text in M.~Petschenig, ed., CSEL 62 (1913), on the Lord as portion and the law above gold.
\item St.~Augustine, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1801068.htm}{\emph{Exposition on Psalm 68}}; \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1801103.htm}{\emph{Exposition on Psalm 103}}; and \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1801119.htm}{\emph{Exposition on Psalm 119}}.
\item St.~Robert Bellarmine, \sourceurl{https://www.ecatholic2000.com/bellarmine/commentary-on-psalms.shtml}{\emph{Commentary on the Psalms}}, Psalms 67, 102, and 118 at the appointed loci.
\item St.~John Chrysostom, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200115.htm}{\emph{Homily 15 on Matthew}}, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200138.htm}{\emph{Homily 38}}, and \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200147.htm}{\emph{Homily 47}}; St.~Augustine, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/16011.htm}{\emph{Sermon on the Mount} I} and \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/160324.htm}{\emph{Sermon 24}}; Origen, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/101610.htm}{\emph{Commentary on Matthew} X}.
\item St.~John Chrysostom, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/210215.htm}{\emph{Homily 15 on Romans}}, direct treatment of Romans 8:28--30 within adversity and divine purpose.
\item Benedict XVI, \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/angelus/2011/documents/hf_ben-xvi_ang_20110724.html}{Angelus, 24 July 2011}, on Solomon's listening heart, conscience, truth, justice, and peace; Francis, \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/angelus/2014/documents/papa-francesco_angelus_20140727.html}{Angelus, 27 July 2014}, and \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/angelus/2020/documents/papa-francesco_angelus_20200726.html}{Angelus, 26 July 2020}, on discovery, search, grace, willingness, joy, and the Kingdom. These are exact-Sunday receptions, not compositional evidence.
\item Giuseppe Ferraro, S.J., \sourceurl{https://www.cultodivino.va/content/dam/cultodivino/rivista-notitiae/2010/notitiae-49-\%282013\%29/Notitiae-565-566-2013.pdf}{\emph{La colletta Protector in te sperantium, Deus: Commento Biblico}, \emph{Notitiae} 49 (2013), 472--478}, a signed biblical study of the Week XVII Collect; used as scholarly illumination rather than a magisterial act.
\item St.~Thomas Aquinas, \sourceurl{https://aquinas.cc/la/en/~Rom.C8.L6}{\emph{Super Romanos} VIII, lect.~6, nn.~686--713}; \sourceurl{https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/cma0500.html}{\emph{Super Matthaeum} V, lect.~2}; \sourceurl{https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/cml0619.html}{XI, lect.~3}; and \sourceurl{https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/cml13.html}{XIII, lect.~4}. Latin loci controlled the paraphrases. His \sourceurl{https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/cmt13.html}{\emph{Catena aurea on Matthew 13}} was used as a medieval compilation, not made the original author of its patristic extracts.
\item Peter Comestor, \sourceurl{https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Historia_Scholastica_\%28III_Kings\%29}{\emph{Historia scholastica}, III Kings, ``De somnio Salomonis''}; medieval retelling, not an independent ancient-event witness.
\item George Leo Haydock, later Catholic compilation on \sourceurl{https://www.studylight.org/commentaries/eng/hcc/1-kings-3.html}{1~Kings 3} and \sourceurl{https://www.studylight.org/commentaries/eng/hcc/romans-8.html}{Romans 8}; not treated as an ancient witness or substitute for direct sources.
\end{itemize}}
