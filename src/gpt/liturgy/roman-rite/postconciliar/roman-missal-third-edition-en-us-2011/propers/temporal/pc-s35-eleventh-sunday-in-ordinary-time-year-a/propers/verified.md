## Relationship classification

- Exodus 19 and Matthew 9:36–10:8 are **officially correlated**: the covenant people called a priestly kingdom and the Twelve sent to Israel belong to the designed First Reading–Gospel relation.
- Psalm 100 is **responsorial** to Exodus and confesses the people as God’s flock.
- Mark 1:15 is **acclamatory**, welcoming the Gospel’s proclamation and mission of the Kingdom.
- Romans 5:6–11 is **semi-continuous** and retains its independent reconciliation argument.
- The shared Week XI orations and antiphons serve Years A/B/C; no historical design for this Year A reading set is claimed. Any unity–mission or gift–“without cost” connection beyond exact wording is labeled synthesis.

## Rights, discrepancies, and disposition

The U.S. Lectionary, ICEL Missal translation, and antiphon translations are protected. This audit retains citations, boundaries, Latin incipits, and original analytical summaries only. No discrepancy was found in the Lectionary path or antiphon assignments. Complete U.S. publisher-specific altar-book English-oration collation and independent theological review remain outstanding; this leaf is source-audited but not publication-final under the profile’s completion gate.
