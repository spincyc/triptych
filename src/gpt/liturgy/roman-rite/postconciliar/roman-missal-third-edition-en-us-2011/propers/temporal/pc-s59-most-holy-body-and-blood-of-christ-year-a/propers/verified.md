## Occurrence and weekday disposition

Universal Norms 7 assigns Corpus Christi to Thursday after Trinity and permits its Sunday assignment where it is not a holy day of obligation. The USCCB calendar places the transferred solemnity on 2026-06-07, displacing the Tenth Sunday in Ordinary Time as a Sunday celebration but not erasing Week X. Monday, June 8, retains its independent Week X / Weekday Cycle II structure. No duplicate Thursday formula target is created; the same owner and Year A target govern the competent-calendar occurrence.
