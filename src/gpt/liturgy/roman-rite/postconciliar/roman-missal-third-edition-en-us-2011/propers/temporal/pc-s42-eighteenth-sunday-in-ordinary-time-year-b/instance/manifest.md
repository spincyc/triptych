## Ordered composition

The Year B Lectionary path is Exod 16:2–4, 12–15; Ps 78:3–4, 23–25, 54,
response v. 24b; Eph 4:17, 20–24; Matt 4:4b; John 6:24–35. The official USCCB
page for 4 August 2024 was checked as a dated witness to this recurring
structure. It does not turn this leaf into a record of that occurrence.

The Week XVIII owner supplies the Entrance Antiphon, Collect, Prayer over the
Offerings, two alternative Communion Antiphons, and Prayer after Communion.
The owner alone retains the Missal collation evidence.

## Unresolved branches

- permitted Sunday sprinkling in place of the Penitential Act;
- locally selected Entrance, Offertory, and Communion chants;
- compatible Preface and Eucharistic Prayer;
- Communion Antiphon A (Wis 16:20) or B (John 6:35); and
- every proper-calendar overlay and local enactment.

The Gloria and Creed are required Sunday units but are not proper-text
components. No shorter Gospel or alternative reading boundary is appointed
for this target.

## Rights disposition

The approved U.S. Lectionary and ICEL Missal English are protected and not
reproduced. The reader-facing Scripture sheet uses the public-domain
Douay–Rheims (Challoner), explicitly identified as a study translation rather
than the text proclaimed at Mass.
