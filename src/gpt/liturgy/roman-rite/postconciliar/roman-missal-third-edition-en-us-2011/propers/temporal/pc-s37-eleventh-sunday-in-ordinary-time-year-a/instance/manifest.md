## Resolved structure and unresolved selections

The dated occurrence resolves the Year A path: Exodus 19:2–6a; Psalm 100:1–2, 3, 5, with response from verse 3c; Romans 5:6–11; the acclamation from Mark 1:15; and Matthew 9:36–10:8. No shorter Gospel form is appointed.

The date alone does not resolve:

- `sunday-sprinkling`: whether the lawful Sunday sprinkling rite replaced the Penitential Act;
- `entrance-chant-selection`, `offertory-chant-selection`, and `communion-chant-selection`: which authorized chant or local music, if any, supplied each layer;
- `compatible-preface-and-eucharistic-prayer`: which compatible non-IV Eucharistic Prayer was used and whether it took an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface or its own authorized Preface under *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 (the Week XI formulary activates no proper insert);
- `eucharistic-prayer-iv-with-preface`: whether Eucharistic Prayer IV was used with its inseparable, invariable Preface under GIRM 365(d); and
- `communion-antiphon-psalm-27` or `communion-antiphon-john-17`: which Missal Communion Antiphon supplied the text if the Missal rather than another authorized chant supplied that layer.

Gloria and Creed are required. The Prayer of the Faithful is governed by the ordinary norm and locally formulated. There is no sequence and no Missal Offertory antiphon.
