## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | `cf.` Psalm 67:6–7, 36 (Vulgate/LXX numbering; Psalm 68 in modern Hebrew numbering) | God is confessed in the holy place as the one who gathers people into a united dwelling and gives strength to the people. |
| Collect | *Protéctor in te sperántium, Deus* | The prayer invokes God as protector, asks multiplied mercy, and asks that divine rule and guidance enable right use of passing goods and faithful adherence to what endures. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Súscipe, quǽsumus, Dómine, múnera* | Gifts received from divine bounty are offered back; the mysteries are asked to sanctify present conduct and lead to lasting joy. |
| Communion Antiphon A | Psalm 102:2 (Vulgate/LXX numbering; Psalm 103 in modern Hebrew numbering) | The soul blesses the Lord and is summoned not to forget divine benefits. |
| Communion Antiphon B | Matthew 5:7–8 | The two adjacent beatitudes name mercy and purity of heart with their promised ends. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Súmpsimus, Dómine, divínum sacraméntum* | Reception of the sacrament is joined to perpetual remembrance of the Son's Passion; the gift of ineffable love is asked to advance salvation. |

Sunday consumers require the Gloria and Creed. *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 govern the dependent Preface and Eucharistic Prayer selection: an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface is used unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own Preface. In particular, GIRM 365(d) binds Eucharistic Prayer IV to its invariable Preface. No Week XVII proper Preface, Eucharistic Prayer insert, or Missal Offertory antiphon is supplied. Gospel length and Lectionary readings belong to the cycle-specific leaf, not to this reusable owner.
