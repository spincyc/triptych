Separate liturgical resolution identifies the seasonal sung response, both alternate Advent psalms and the optional wreath rite. Each of two interpretations includes its own four senses. Jerome and Augustine explain the people’s appeal for the ability to turn to God. Bede and Chrysostom explain the duties of a gifted household whose master will return.

## Homily

The homily for the adult parish assembly of 29 November 2026 follows “The people who ask God to turn them.” The decisive detail is Jesus’ instruction in Mark 14:38 to the friends he has found asleep. Peter has heard the warning given to everyone (13:33–37) and has already declared his willingness to die with Jesus (14:29–31). Jesus asks the three to watch in 14:34. Only on his return does he ask Peter whether he could stay awake one hour (14:37). Peter, who had promised so much, still needs to pray for help. Read beside Isaiah 63:16b–17, 19b and 64:2–7, Psalm 80:18–19 and the First Sunday Collect, the gap between intention and action becomes something the assembly can bring to the Father. Jerome on Isaiah 64:7–8 (book XVII, pp. 158–159) explains the children’s freedom to speak. Augustine on Psalm 79, §§3 and 11, explains how God gives the people the life to call on him. The application to a promised visit follows from Jesus asking his friends to stay while he is in distress.

The spoken component contains 1,104 whitespace-delimited words, excluding its title and terminal matter. At 105 words per minute the estimated duration is 10 minutes 30 seconds. A silent textual rehearsal checked the scene changes, the distinction between the Sunday Gospel and the later garden scene, pronouns, and the ending. The invitation was compared with the service without thanks in the Thirty-second Sunday Year A homily and the duties associated with the coin in the Twenty-ninth Sunday Year A homily. No human reading was timed.

The homily component and content checks passed, including use of all four References entries. The three-output content schema, run provenance and conversion of the homily entrypoint to its web reader also passed at author submission.
