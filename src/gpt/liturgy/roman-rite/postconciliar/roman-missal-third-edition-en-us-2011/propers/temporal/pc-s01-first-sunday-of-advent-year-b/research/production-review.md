## Homily

The homily for the adult parish assembly of 29 November 2026 follows “The people who ask God to turn them.” The decisive detail is Jesus’ instruction in Mark 14:38 to the friends he has found asleep. Peter has heard the warning given to everyone (13:33–37) and has already declared his willingness to die with Jesus (14:29–31). When the hour to keep him company arrives, Peter still needs to pray for help. Read beside Isaiah 63:16b–17, 19b and 64:2–7, Psalm 80:18–19 and the First Sunday Collect, the gap between intention and action becomes something the assembly can bring to the Father. Jerome on Isaiah 64:7–8 (book XVII, pp. 158–159) explains the children’s freedom to speak. Augustine on Psalm 79, §§3 and 11, explains how God gives the people the life to call on him. The application to a promised visit follows from Jesus asking his friends to stay while he is in distress.
