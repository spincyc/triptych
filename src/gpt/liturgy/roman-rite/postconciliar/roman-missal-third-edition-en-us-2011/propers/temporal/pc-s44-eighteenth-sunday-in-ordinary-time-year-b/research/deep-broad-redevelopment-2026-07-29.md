# Deep-and-broad redevelopment audit — PC-S42-B — 2026-07-29

## Prior-edition deficiency

The corrected Week XVIII ownership and Lectionary boundaries were sound, but
the research edition was markedly thinner than Year A. Its “notable” section
mostly repeated words or concepts internal to the readings rather than
documenting reception in changed registers. Named patristic witnesses were not
represented in the leaf's source-binding ledger.

## Consequential corrections

- The new dossier replaces a one-theme “bread” synthesis with three distinct
  histories: event, remembered event, and Johannine reception.
- The crowd's motive is analyzed through the complete dialogue, not merely
  labeled materialistic.
- Ephesians is restored to its corporate and ethical continuation.
- Source- and locus-checked political satire, military-humanitarian naming,
  and congregational hymn reception replace internal-word repetition in the
  notable lane.

## Negative results

- No evidence establishes that Ephesians 4 was chosen to interpret John 6.
- No checked witness permits a magical reception of Eucharist or a denial of
  bodily hunger.
- No occurrence evidence resolves the Communion antiphon alternative.
- The shared Week XVIII owner does not establish a Year B compositional
  intention.

The patristic binding ledger was reconciled on 29 July. The redevelopment
still requires fresh production, web, visual, and staleness review.
