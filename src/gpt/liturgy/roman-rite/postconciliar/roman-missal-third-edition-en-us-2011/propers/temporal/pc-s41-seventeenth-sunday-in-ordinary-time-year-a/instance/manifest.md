## Occurrence resolution

The General Roman Calendar assigns 26 July to the Memorial of Saints Joachim and Anne, Parents of the Blessed Virgin Mary. In 2026 the date is the Seventeenth Sunday in Ordinary Time. Under the table of liturgical days, the Ordinary Time Sunday outranks the memorial. The Sunday is celebrated, and the memorial is omitted for this occurrence without commemoration or transfer. No prayer, reading, or title from the memorial enters this Sunday formula.

The official USCCB annual calendar lists the Sunday on 26 July and proceeds to the next weekday on 27 July; its omission of the memorial on the occupied date agrees with that precedence result. The recurring calendar owner retains Saints Joachim and Anne on 26 July as a universal memorial, so the annual omission is not treated as removal from the General Calendar. No supplied proper calendar raises the memorial or another celebration to a rank capable of changing this resolution.
