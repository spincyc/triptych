## Relationship classification

- Jeremiah 20 and Matthew 10 are **officially correlated**: the threatened prophet and disciples commanded to fearless witness form the designed First Reading–Gospel relation.
- Psalm 69 is **responsorial** to Jeremiah’s affliction, petition, and praise.
- John 15 is **acclamatory**, naming the Spirit’s witness and the disciples’ answering testimony.
- Romans 5:12–15 is **semi-continuous**; Adam and Christ are not forced into the persecution/witness relationship.
- The shared Week XII Missal texts serve A/B/C. Good-Shepherd and providence connections are textual or source-grounded synthesis when used, not claims of one historical composition.
