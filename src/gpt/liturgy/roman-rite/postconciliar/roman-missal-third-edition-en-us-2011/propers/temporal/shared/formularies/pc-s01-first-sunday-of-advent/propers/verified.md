# First Sunday of Advent — shared U.S. Missal formulary

The registry assigns the common Advent I Missal material of PC-S01 to this owner across the three Sunday cycles. The target is the *Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America* (English, implemented in 2011), based on the Latin third typical edition, emended reprint of 2008. This context collation on 23 November 2026 identifies the appointments and the branches they govern.

| Key | Locus and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `entrance` | Entrance antiphon, *Ad te levavi*, cf. Psalm 25 (24):1–3. The printed comparison marks an adaptation. One antiphon. The choice of entrance music in an actual celebration is open. |

## Research collation, 23 November 2026

The USCCB's *Commentary on the Proper Prayers of Advent from the Roman Missal* supplies all three First Sunday orations from the approved translation. Collation against the complete 2011 U.S. witness confirms the proper bodies and the current Collect conclusion. Its offering prayer adds punctuation after “below” without changing the petition. The USCCB Committee on Divine Worship's February 2021 newsletter (vol. LVII, pp. 7–8) dates the removal of “one” before “God” in the Collect conclusion to Ash Wednesday, 17 February 2021. The antecedent is Christ. The older printing's conclusion is superseded. The U.S. page's comments interpret the two-way gift in the offering and the educative use of passing goods after Communion. Its historical notes associate the Collect with seventh-century Rome and Gaul, the offering with a sixth-century Roman collection, and the final oration with a 1970 composition drawn from two older Roman prayers. These are the committee website's reports: no ancient sacramentary was collated here and the explanator does not claim a design for the Year B sequence. [USCCB commentary](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/advent/commentary-on-advent-proper-prayers) · [February 2021 newsletter](https://www.usccb.org/resources/newsletter-2021-02.pdf)

The 2008 *Notitiae* issue, physical pp. 49–69 / printed pp. 367–387, names this an emended reprint. The Advent list on printed p. 369 gives changes at Missal pp. 142, 144 and 148 concerning a Haggai citation, a different postcommunion and an Isaiah citation. None changes an Advent I prayer or antiphon. The Order of Mass list (pp. 371–372) amends the Apostles' Creed rubric, an Easter Preface at p. 530, a Eucharistic Prayer, dismissals and the introductory invitation for Prayers over the People at p. 616. The Latin contents and the two preceding rubrics distinguish that group from Solemn Blessings, which begin at p. 606. The latter already directs the deacon or priest to the specified invitation. The Advent solemn blessing and its three invocations are unaffected by the p. 616 change. The U.S. witness and publisher sample agree on the solemn-blessing invitation. No change to Advent Preface I at p. 518 appears here. The full 2008 altar book was not available for independent page-by-page collation; the variation list, distinct Latin 2002 digital witness, current U.S. orations, Antiphonary and U.S. publisher sample have different extents.
