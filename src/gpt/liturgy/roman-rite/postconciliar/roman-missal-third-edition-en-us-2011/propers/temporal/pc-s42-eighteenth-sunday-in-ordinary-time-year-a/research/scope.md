# Research Scope — PC-S42-A

**As of:** 2026-07-28

## Coverage and source roles

The audit covered the full Year A composition, immediate canonical contexts, the Week XVIII Missal owner, branch logic, scriptural historical orientation, bounded patristic and medieval reception, and a targeted afterlife and interpretive-proposal check. The USCCB occurrence page controls Lectionary boundaries; the shared owner controls the Missal formulary; modern biblical introductions provide historical orientation; reception sources illuminate rather than establish liturgical appointment.

## Notable-and-quotable audit

- Isaiah 55's spending question in Thoreau's *Walden*, “Economy”: direct wording and economic redirection checked in the public-domain text.
- Romans 8's height/depth pair in memorial and consolatory use: retained as a broad documented reception category; no single institutional adoption is claimed.
- Five loaves and two fish as food-ministry nomenclature and emblem: retained as a recurrent reception pattern, not as evidence of miraculous equivalence.
- Generic “bread,” “open hand,” abundance, restaurant, and commercial names lacking demonstrable scriptural dependence were rejected.
