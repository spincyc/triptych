## Notable-and-quotable audit

- Isaiah 55's spending question in Thoreau's *Walden*, “Economy”: direct wording and economic redirection checked in the public-domain text.
- The anonymous 1783 etching *The Loaves and Fishes*: the British Museum
  object record and a public-domain scan were checked. Its title and pictured
  loaves and fish convert the feeding tradition into a satire on political
  patronage. The object does not name a single Synoptic narration, so the
  guide treats it as an afterlife of the tradition appointed here in Matthew,
  not as evidence of exclusive dependence on Matthew 14.
- Matthew Arnold's *Dover Beach*, lines 29–37: the identified 1867
  first-edition transcription was checked. Its plea for human fidelity and
  accumulating neither/nor catalogue around love make a strong structural
  and verbal echo of Romans 8:38–39 in a secular lyric register. No claim of
  conscious borrowing or authorial intention is made.
- Romans 8's height/depth pair in generic memorial and consolatory use was
  screened but rejected because no exact, primary-source changed-register use
  was bound.
- Food-ministry names using the five-loaves-and-two-fish image were screened
  but rejected because generic naming does not satisfy the primary-source and
  changed-register requirements.
- Generic “bread,” “open hand,” abundance, restaurant, and commercial names lacking demonstrable scriptural dependence were rejected.
