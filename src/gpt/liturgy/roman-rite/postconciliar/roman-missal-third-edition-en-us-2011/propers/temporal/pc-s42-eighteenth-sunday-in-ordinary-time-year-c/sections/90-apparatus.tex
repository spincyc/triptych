\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}
This source-first guide treats the recurring U.S. Year C formula, not a parish
record or local Ordo. The Missal owner controls reusable formulary evidence;
the leaf audit controls Lectionary composition and branches. Protected ICEL,
CCD, and modern Bible texts are not reproduced. The public-domain
Douay--Rheims appointed-text sheet is a study translation only. The official
occurrence page's Psalm-response marker is preserved as a discrepancy pending
named altar-book typography-level collation. Jerome and an uncertain Ambrose
psalm locator were excluded rather than promoted.
