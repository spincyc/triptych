\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

\begin{studyframe}
\textbf{Text.} Year A Lectionary no.~164, its single Daniel response, the Latin formulary, proper Preface, and official ICEL antiphons were collated; complete U.S.-English oration collation against a named altar-book printing remains outstanding. \textbf{History.} Every direct passage and marked adaptation was studied in canonical order, with narrated events distinguished from composition. \textbf{Reception.} Greek and Latin Johannine and Pauline homilies, a Syriac/Greek-scriptural reception search, Cappadocian, Jerusalem, and North African witnesses, medieval controls, and feast history were checked. Direct exposition, verse-level reuse, adjacent-canticle use, and doctrinal synthesis remain differentiated in the research matrix. \textbf{Review.} Internal source audit is complete for the recorded layers; independent theological, liturgical, biblical, and historical review is not. \textbf{Full records.} \nolinkurl{instance/manifest.md}; \nolinkurl{propers/verified.md}; \nolinkurl{../shared/formularies/pc-s26-most-holy-trinity/propers/verified.md}; \nolinkurl{research/scope.md}.
\end{studyframe}
