\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & Israel asks for life. Paul thanks God for gifts a divided church has received. A householder assigns work before leaving.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & Augustine sees Christ's face hidden in his suffering. Bede sees the ascended Christ giving the Spirit to his household.\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & A thief learns to enjoy giving. A servant guards his heart against the envy that spoils another's gift for him.\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & Those who ask for mercy hope to see Christ in glory. Each generation answers for its service to him.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
