## Witnesses and limits

- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia (Typis Vaticanis, 2002), printed heading *Dominica XV per annum*, pp. 464–465. The complete Latin formulary was checked on 2026-07-15 against the page-image facsimile at <https://media.musicasacra.com/books/latin_missal2002.pdf> (SHA-256 `0b458944824d2ee92854b9664f83e553af0342e0b15a9dc14b00e846405523d7`). The title page identifies the official book; the non-ecclesiastical host is only a secondary delivery witness.
- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia, reimpressio emendata (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2008), is the Latin base of the U.S. edition. The Congregation's official “Variationes et additiones in reimpressione emendata ‘Missalis Romani’,” *Notitiae* 44 (2008), pp. 368–387, <https://www.cultodivino.va/content/dam/cultodivino/rivista-notitiae/2000/notitiae-44-%282008%29/Notitiae-503-504-2008.pdf> (PDF SHA-256 `74c8d1740284984d0f4d64b2223edf805dadfa55aec8a92f8cfba9ab901eca0f`), lists the only *Tempus per annum* changes at pp. 457 and 471 and none at pp. 464–465. This formula-level variation audit therefore found no listed substantive change to Week XV. The 2008 reprint page images themselves were not directly inspected, so purely typographical or layout differences remain outside this audit.
- *Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America*, English, confirmed in 2010 and implemented 2011-11-27; official status: <https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/the-mass/general-instruction-of-the-roman-missal/girm-foreword>.
- Bishops’ Conference of England and Wales, *Antiphonary: Excerpted from the Roman Missal* (ICEL, 2010), p. 74, <https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Missal/Music/Antiphonary.pdf>. It verifies the common ICEL antiphons but does not replace the U.S. altar book.

The controlling Latin Ordinary Time directions are *Tempus per annum*, rubrics 1–6; GIRM 363 governs selection among Ordinary Time orations. The formulary serves the Fifteenth Sunday in Ordinary Time and is also a permitted source on eligible ferias. No complete official digital U.S. altar book was available; publisher-specific English oration collation remains outstanding.

## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | cf. Psalm 17:15 | The just speaker hopes to behold and be filled with divine glory. |
| Collect | *Deus, qui errantibus… veritatis tuae lumen ostendis* | Truth recalls wanderers; Christians ask to reject what contradicts their name and pursue what accords with it. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Respice… munera supplicantis Ecclesiae* | The Church’s gifts are asked to advance believers’ sanctification. |
| Communion Antiphon A | cf. Psalm 84:4–5 | Dwelling and praise at the Lord’s altars. |
| Communion Antiphon B | John 6:57 | Appointed Eucharistic alternative of mutual abiding. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Sumptis muneribus… cum frequentatione mysterii* | Repeated participation is asked to deepen the saving effect. |

Sunday consumers require Gloria and Creed. *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 govern the dependent Preface/Prayer selection: an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface is used unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own Preface. In particular, GIRM 365(d) binds Eucharistic Prayer IV to its invariable Preface; the exact compatible pairing otherwise remains unresolved. No proper Offertory antiphon or Eucharistic Prayer insert is supplied. The long and short Gospel forms belong to the Lectionary leaf, not this owner.

## Rights and disposition

Only citations, brief Latin incipits, and original summaries are retained. The directly collated 2002 Latin text, element boundaries, and official English antiphons are verified; the official 2008 published-variation audit found no listed substantive change to this formulary, while direct comparison of 2008 page-image typography and layout remains outside the audit. Complete U.S.-English altar-book collation and independent review remain outstanding.
