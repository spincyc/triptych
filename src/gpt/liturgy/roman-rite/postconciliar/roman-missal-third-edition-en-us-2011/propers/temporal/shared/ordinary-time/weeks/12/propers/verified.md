## Witnesses and limits

- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia (Typis Vaticanis, 2002), printed heading *Dominica XII per annum*, pp. 462–463. The complete Latin formulary was checked on 2026-07-15 against the page-image facsimile at <https://media.musicasacra.com/books/latin_missal2002.pdf> (SHA-256 `0b458944824d2ee92854b9664f83e553af0342e0b15a9dc14b00e846405523d7`). The title page identifies the official book; the non-ecclesiastical host is only a secondary delivery witness.
- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia, reimpressio emendata (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2008), is the Latin base of the U.S. edition. The Congregation's official “Variationes et additiones in reimpressione emendata ‘Missalis Romani’,” *Notitiae* 44 (2008), pp. 368–387, <https://www.cultodivino.va/content/dam/cultodivino/rivista-notitiae/2000/notitiae-44-%282008%29/Notitiae-503-504-2008.pdf> (PDF SHA-256 `74c8d1740284984d0f4d64b2223edf805dadfa55aec8a92f8cfba9ab901eca0f`), lists the only *Tempus per annum* changes at pp. 457 and 471 and none at pp. 462–463. This formula-level variation audit therefore found no listed substantive change to Week XII. The 2008 reprint page images themselves were not directly inspected, so purely typographical or layout differences remain outside this audit.
- *Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America*, English, confirmed in 2010 and implemented 2011-11-27; official status: <https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/the-mass/general-instruction-of-the-roman-missal/girm-foreword>.
- Bishops’ Conference of England and Wales, *Antiphonary: Excerpted from the Roman Missal* (ICEL, 2010), pp. 72–73, <https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Missal/Music/Antiphonary.pdf>. It verifies the common ICEL antiphons but does not replace the U.S. altar book.

The controlling Latin Ordinary Time directions are *Tempus per annum*, rubrics 1–6; GIRM 363 governs selection among Ordinary Time orations. The formulary serves the Twelfth Sunday in Ordinary Time and is also a permitted source on eligible ferias. A complete official digital U.S. altar book was unavailable, so exact publisher, printing, and page collation of the English orations remains outstanding and no complete English prayer is reproduced.

## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | cf. Psalm 28:8–9 | The Lord is strength and saving refuge; the people ask blessing and governance. |
| Collect | *Sancti nominis tui… timorem pariter et amorem* | Perpetual reverence and love rest in God’s unfailing governance. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Suscipe… sacrificium placationis et laudis* | Sacrifice of reconciliation and praise is ordered to purification and a pleasing interior offering. |
| Communion Antiphon A | Psalm 145:15 | All look to God for food in due season. |
| Communion Antiphon B | John 10:11, 15 | Appointed Good-Shepherd alternative. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Sacri Corporis et Sanguinis pretiosi alimonia renovati* | Repeated celebration is asked to become a sure possession of redemption. |

Sunday consumers require Gloria and Creed. *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 govern the dependent Preface/Prayer selection: an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface is used unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own Preface. In particular, GIRM 365(d) binds Eucharistic Prayer IV to its invariable Preface; the exact compatible pairing otherwise remains unresolved. No proper Offertory antiphon or Eucharistic Prayer insert is supplied. Ferial reuse never imports the Sunday Lectionary or Sunday structural parts.

## Rights and disposition

Only citations, brief Latin incipits, and original summaries are retained. The directly collated 2002 Latin text, element boundaries, and official English antiphons are verified; the official 2008 published-variation audit found no listed substantive change to this formulary, while direct comparison of 2008 page-image typography and layout remains outside the audit. Complete U.S.-English altar-book collation and independent review remain outstanding.
