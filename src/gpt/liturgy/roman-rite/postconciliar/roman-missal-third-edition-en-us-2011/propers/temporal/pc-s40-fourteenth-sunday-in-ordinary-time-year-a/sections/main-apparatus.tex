\section*{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}

\begin{studyframe}
\textbf{Formula.} Lectionary 100, both Psalm responses, both Communion alternatives, and the Week XIV orations and antiphons were audited; the England and Wales bishops' \emph{Antiphonary} corroborates the antiphon assignments. \textbf{Historical study.} Every directly appointed scriptural passage and marked adaptation appears in canonical order, with Matthew's composition separated from the narrated event. \textbf{Reception.} A passage-by-passage search retained direct Greek, Latin, and medieval witnesses for the appointed psalms, Zechariah 9, Romans 8, and Matthew 11, including Justin, Origen, Cyril, Clement, Chrysostom, Augustine, and Aquinas; Ambrose and Leo are explicitly marked as illuminating reuse. No witness is made commentator on the complete modern formulary. \textbf{Rights.} Only citations, short incipits, and original summaries appear. \textbf{Outstanding.} 2008 page-image typography and layout, full U.S. altar-book English-oration collation, and independent theological review; this working guide is not publication-final. \textbf{Audit trail.} \nolinkurl{instance/manifest.md}; \nolinkurl{propers/verified.md}; \nolinkurl{../shared/ordinary-time/weeks/14/propers/verified.md}; \nolinkurl{research/scope.md}.
\end{studyframe}
