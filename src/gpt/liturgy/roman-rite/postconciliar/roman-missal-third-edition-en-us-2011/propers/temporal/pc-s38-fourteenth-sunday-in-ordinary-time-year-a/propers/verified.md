## Relationship classification

- Zechariah 9 and Matthew 11 are **officially correlated** in the Ordinary Time Year A Lectionary path: the humble king and Jesus’ meekness belong to the designed first-reading/Gospel relation.
- Psalm 145 is **responsorial** to the first reading and praises the gracious kingship proclaimed there.
- The acclamation is **acclamatory** and reuses a clause from the Gospel’s opening thanksgiving.
- Romans 8:9, 11–13 is **semi-continuous**. Its Spirit–flesh contrast must be studied fully without claiming that it was selected to gloss Zechariah or Matthew.
- Communion Antiphon B has a **textual relation** to the Gospel; Antiphon A and the shared orations remain independent common Week XIV elements unless a further relation is explicitly labeled synthesis.
