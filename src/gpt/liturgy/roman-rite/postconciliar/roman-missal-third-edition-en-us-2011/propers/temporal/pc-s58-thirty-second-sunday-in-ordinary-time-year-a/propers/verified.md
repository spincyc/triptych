Read the complete held Challoner contexts Wisdom 5–7, Psalms 22, 62 and 87,
1 Thessalonians 4–5, Matthew 24–25 and Luke 24. The exact book artifacts are
bound in `research/source-bindings.toml`. These are Gutenberg 1581/Challoner,
not the 1899 American printing. The existing Thessalonians projection was
checked against the actual chapter; its offsets must travel into every
quotation or source locator. Modern Wisdom numbering is likewise not a
blanket `vulgate` declaration.

The responsorial psalm ends at the shelter of God's wings; the poem's pursuit
and royal concluding verses are context, not appointed material. Matthew's
mountain discourse context supplies readiness, entrusted service and final
judgment; the talents and sheep/goats are not additional appointments. Luke
24:35 is the disciples' report in Jerusalem of the encounter at Emmaus; a
chronology or place claim must distinguish that report from the narrated
recognition it recalls.

## Publication boundary

No approved-English readings, refrain, acclamation or Missal text is retained
here. Full public-domain biblical study text may be drawn from the exact bound
artifacts with the numbering and textual differences above stated. It must not
be labelled approved for proclamation or presented as a recovered liturgical
adaptation. No public-domain prayer body has been independently cleared by
this research. Brief Latin incipits are identifiers; original commentary is
allowed; close substitute translations of restricted prayers are not.
