## Relationship classification

- Ecclesiastes and Luke are **officially correlated**: anxious acquisition and
  stored abundance both fail as guarantees against death.
- Psalm 90 is **responsorial**: mortality becomes a petition for wisdom,
  mercy, and the establishment of human work.
- Matthew 5:3 is **acclamatory** and is not part of Luke's narrated scene.
- Colossians is **semi-continuous**. Its treatment of greed as idolatry and
  life hidden with Christ may be synthesized with the Gospel, but was not
  selected as an explanatory reading for Luke.
- The two Communion antiphons are mutually exclusive alternatives.
- The Week XVIII Missal texts are shared by Years A, B, and C and are not
  claimed to have been composed for Lectionary no. 114.

## Rights and disposition

The protected U.S. Lectionary and ICEL texts are not reproduced. This audit
retains identifiers, boundaries, Latin incipits, and original summaries. The
official occurrence page controls the packet; its printed Psalm-response
marker is preserved as a discrepancy pending named-book collation rather than
silently reassigned. This remains a source-audited study guide for use beside
approved liturgical books, not a worship aid, parish record, or ecclesiastical
approval.
