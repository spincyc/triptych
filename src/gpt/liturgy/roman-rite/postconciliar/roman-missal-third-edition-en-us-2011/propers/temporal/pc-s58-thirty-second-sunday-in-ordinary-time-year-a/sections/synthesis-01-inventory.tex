\begin{properinventory}{Textual unit}{Text / citation}
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Introductory Rites}}\\
Entrance & Cf. Psalm 87:3 Vulgate / 88:3 Hebrew: a plea to be heard.\\
Collect & \textit{Omnipotens et misericors Deus}: freedom for service to God.\\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Liturgy of the Word}}\\
First reading & Wisdom 6:12--16 (Vulgate/Challoner 6:13--17): Wisdom seeks those who seek her.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Psalm 63:2, 3--4, 5--6, 7--8 Hebrew / 62:2--8 Vulgate; response 2b: thirst and remembrance.\\
Second reading & 1 Thessalonians 4:13--18; or short form 4:13--14: hope through Christ's resurrection.\\
Acclamation & Cf. Matthew 24:42a, 44: the hour is unknown.\\
Gospel & Matthew 25:1--13: the ten virgins and the bridegroom.\\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Liturgy of the Eucharist and Communion}}\\
Offerings & \textit{Sacrificiis praesentibus}: the Passion celebrated and inwardly received.\\
Communion & Cf. Psalm 22:1--2 Vulgate / 23:1--2 Hebrew: the shepherd's care; or cf. Luke 24:35: recognition in breaking bread.\\
After Communion & \textit{Gratias tibi, Domine, referimus}: thanksgiving and the Spirit's preservation of integrity.\\
\end{properinventory}
