\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & God pardons, calls and feeds; people refuse, gather and share distress. Isaiah promises death's defeat; Paul still knows hunger. The long Gospel alone inspects the garment.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Christ gathers and nourishes his Church. Gregory's garment is charity; Augustine's Shepherd guides it; Thomas distinguishes its present banquet from heaven.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Let mercy become love of God and neighbor. Give and receive help without domination; another's contentment never excuses neglect. Grace sustains action and perseverance.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & The mixed gathering awaits judgment and perfected communion. Present nourishment anticipates death's end and, in the Johannine Communion option, likeness through sight.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
