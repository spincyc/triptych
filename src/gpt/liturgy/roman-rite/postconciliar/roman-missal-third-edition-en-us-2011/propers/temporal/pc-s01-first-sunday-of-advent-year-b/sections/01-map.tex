\section{The Mass of the First Sunday of Advent}

\begin{maptable}
Entrance & Cf. Psalm 25 (24):1--3 & The speaker risks the mockery of enemies by trusting God.\\
Collect & \textit{Da, quaesumus} & The people ask God for the will to meet Christ with righteous deeds.\\
First reading & Isaiah 63:16b--17, 19b and 64:2--7 (U.S.) & Israel appeals to its Father, recounts its failure to remember him and acknowledges his hands at work.\\
Responsorial psalm & Psalm 80 (79):2--3, 15--16, 18--19, with response from v.~4 & The people ask the God who planted the vine to visit it. Given life, they will call on him.\\
Second reading & 1 Corinthians 1:3--9 & Paul thanks God for the gifts already bestowed on the church and looks forward to the revelation of Christ.\\
Alleluia & Psalm 85 (84):8 & The assembly asks God for mercy and salvation.\\
Gospel & Mark 13:33--37 & Before leaving, a householder assigns his servants work. His return may fall at any watch of the night.\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \textit{Suscipe, quaesumus} & The people can bring material gifts because they have already received them from God.\\
Preface & Preface I of Advent & Thanksgiving for the Son's humble coming in the flesh looks toward his coming in glory.\\
Communion & Psalm 85 (84):13 & The Lord's goodness is promised, and fruit is expected from the earth.\\
Prayer after Communion & \textit{Prosint nobis} & Those who have received the sacrament ask to learn to love what endures.\\
Solemn blessing, if chosen & Solemn Blessings I.1, Advent & Three invocations recall Christ's coming, ask for help in the present race and look toward his appearing.\\
\end{maptable}

The larger psalm number in each pair follows Hebrew numbering. The number in parentheses follows the Vulgate. Thus the two references to Psalm 85 identify different verses of the same prayer. At the Alleluia the congregation is still petitioning. At Communion the verse speaks of a gift the people expect God to give.
