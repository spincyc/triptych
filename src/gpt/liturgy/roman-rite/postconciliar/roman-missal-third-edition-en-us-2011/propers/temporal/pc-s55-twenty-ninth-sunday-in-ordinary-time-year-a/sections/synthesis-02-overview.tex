\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & God strengthens the unknowing Cyrus; Jesus answers a tribute trap; Paul thanks God for active faith, love and hope. The prayers seek readiness, purification and lasting benefit.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Cyrus's limited liberation anticipates Christ. God's image is returned to its maker; the Church receives light and life from Christ, whose ransom grounds its service.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & Fulfil rightful duties without making power absolute; acknowledge the giver, labour in charity, and accept purification. Received grace makes willing service possible.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & Present gifts sustain a pilgrimage toward eternal fulfilment. Neither worldly strength nor religious achievement supplies the final good for which hope waits.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
