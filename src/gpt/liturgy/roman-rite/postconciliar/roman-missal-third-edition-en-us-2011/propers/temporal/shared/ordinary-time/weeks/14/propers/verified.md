## Witnesses and limits

- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia (Typis Vaticanis, 2002), printed heading *Dominica XIV per annum*, p. 464. The complete Latin formulary was checked on 2026-07-15 against the exact secondary-host digitally typeset PDF reproduction at <https://media.musicasacra.com/books/latin_missal2002.pdf> (SHA-256 `0b458944824d2ee92854b9664f83e553af0342e0b15a9dc14b00e846405523d7`). The title page identifies the official book; the non-ecclesiastical host is only a secondary delivery witness.
- *Missale Romanum*, editio typica tertia, reimpressio emendata (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2008), is the Latin base of the U.S. edition. The Congregation's official “Variationes et additiones in reimpressione emendata ‘Missalis Romani’,” *Notitiae* 44 (2008), pp. 368–387, <https://www.cultodivino.va/content/dam/cultodivino/rivista-notitiae/2000/notitiae-44-%282008%29/Notitiae-503-504-2008.pdf> (PDF SHA-256 `74c8d1740284984d0f4d64b2223edf805dadfa55aec8a92f8cfba9ab901eca0f`), lists the only *Tempus per annum* changes at pp. 457 and 471 and none at p. 464. This formula-level variation audit therefore found no listed substantive change to Week XIV. The 2008 reprint page image itself was not directly inspected, so purely typographical or layout differences remain outside this audit.
- *Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America*, English, confirmed in 2010 and implemented 2011-11-27; official status: <https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/the-mass/general-instruction-of-the-roman-missal/girm-foreword>.
- Bishops’ Conference of England and Wales, *Antiphonary: Excerpted from the Roman Missal* (ICEL, 2010), p. 74, <https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Missal/Music/Antiphonary.pdf>. It verifies the common ICEL antiphons but does not replace the U.S. altar book.

The controlling Latin Ordinary Time directions are *Tempus per annum*, rubrics 1–6; GIRM 363 governs selection among Ordinary Time orations. The formulary serves the Fourteenth Sunday in Ordinary Time and is also a permitted source on eligible ferias. Complete U.S. publisher-specific oration collation remains outstanding because no complete official digital U.S. altar book was available.

## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | cf. Psalm 48:10–11 | Mercy is received in the Temple and praise reaches earth’s ends. |
| Collect | *Deus, qui in Filii tui humilitate iacentem mundum erexisti* | The Son’s humility raises the fallen world from slavery to sin toward eternal joy. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Oblatio nos… tuo nomini dicata purificet* | The offering purifies and transfers conduct toward heavenly life. |
| Communion Antiphon A | Psalm 34:9 | Taste the Lord’s goodness and take refuge in him. |
| Communion Antiphon B | Matthew 11:28 | Gospel-correlated invitation to the burdened. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Tantis… repleti muneribus* | The recipients ask for salvation and unceasing praise. |

Sunday consumers require Gloria and Creed. *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 govern the dependent Preface/Prayer selection: an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface is used unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own Preface. In particular, GIRM 365(d) binds Eucharistic Prayer IV to its invariable Preface; the exact compatible pairing otherwise remains unresolved. No proper Offertory antiphon or Eucharistic Prayer insert is supplied. The second Communion antiphon directly corresponds to the Year A Gospel but remains an authorized choice.
