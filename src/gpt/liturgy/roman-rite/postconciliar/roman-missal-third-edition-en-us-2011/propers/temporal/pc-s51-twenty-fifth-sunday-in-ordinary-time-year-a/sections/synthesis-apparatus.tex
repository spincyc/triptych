\section{Appendix: Scope and Qualifications}
This concise comparison accompanies \textit{The Gift We Share}, the expansive study of the Twenty-fifth Sunday in Ordinary Time, Year A, 20 September 2026: Roman Missal, Third Edition, U.S. English 2011, and U.S. Lectionary, second typical edition, no. 133. The general United States calendar is the dated scope; no local solemnity or parish selection is resolved. The studied layer comprises the Missal Entrance, three orations, both alternative Communion texts, and Lectionary 133. Sung replacements, Preface and Eucharistic Prayer remain unselected.

The three whole-formulary readings are editorial theological syntheses of the cited arguments, not interpretations of this modern assemblage attributed to its ancient commentators. Isaiah and Matthew are correlated under the Lectionary's arrangement; Philippians is semi-continuous, and the Missal prayers are shared across cycles. Their theological connections establish no single historical compositional design. Direct ancient commentary on the three orations was not verified. The canonical Week 25 audit retains the limitation that complete official 2008 Latin and U.S. 2011 altar-book collation has not been performed.

The canonical study uses public-domain Douay--Rheims Challoner English for study, not the approved proclaimed English. This comparison provides analytical prose and locators, not replacement liturgical wording; restricted ICEL and CCD bodies are not reproduced. Historical source wording retains its own status. Neither work is a book for liturgical recitation or an ecclesiastically approved commentary. Source and search limits remain in the canonical research records; no exhaustive reception survey is claimed.
