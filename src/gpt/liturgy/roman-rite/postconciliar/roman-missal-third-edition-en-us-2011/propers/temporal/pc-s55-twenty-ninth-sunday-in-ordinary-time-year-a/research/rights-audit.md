the approved proclaimed or recited texts. The same limits govern all three
PDFs and the bundled web edition.

The Bede repair adds a complete public-domain Latin derivative and one
exact PL92 print leaf to the reusable source library. No protected source
payload has been added to tracked publication. New records retain hashes, extents,
retrieval URLs, provenance and source-specific rights limits. Public-domain
source payloads already retained by the library are reused without changing
their rights classifications. Original commentary may be published under
the repository's own license while the separate source layers retain their
declared status. No permission grant has been invented or requested as a
substitute for the established citation-and-analysis route.
