The official reprint notice establishes the edition relationship; the official published variation list, *Notitiae* 44 (2008), pp. 368–387, lists its only *Tempus per annum* changes at printed pp. 457 and 471, not Week XVIII at pp. 467–468. This is bounded no-listed-change evidence, not direct inspection of the 2008 Week XVIII pages or proof of identity in every typographical detail.
- *Roman Missal, Third Edition, for Use in the Dioceses of the United States of America*, English, confirmed in 2010 and implemented 2011-11-27. No complete official digital U.S. altar book was inspected; protected English wording and publisher-specific typography are outside this owner.

The controlling Latin Ordinary Time directions are *Tempus per annum*, rubrics 1–6. Rubric 3 assigns the numbered Mass to its Sunday and permits any of the thirty-four formularies on eligible weekdays; GIRM 363 independently permits the preceding Sunday's orations or another Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time set on such weekdays. The formulary therefore serves the Eighteenth Sunday in Ordinary Time and is a permitted, not automatic, source on eligible ferias. This audit retains identifiers, short Latin incipits, and original analytical summaries, not complete protected liturgical texts.

## Appointed elements

| Element | Identifier | Verified role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance Antiphon | Psalm 69:2, 6 (Vulgate/LXX numbering; Psalm 70 in modern Hebrew numbering) | The worshipper urgently asks divine help and names the Lord as helper and deliverer. |
| Collect | *Adésto, Dómine, fámulis tuis* | God's servants ask enduring kindness so that the creator and governor may restore what was made and preserve what has been restored. |
| Prayer over the Offerings | *Propítius, Dómine, quǽsumus, hæc dona sanctífica* | The gifts are asked to be sanctified; acceptance of the spiritual offering is joined to the petition that the worshippers themselves become an enduring gift to God. |
| Communion Antiphon A | Wisdom 16:20 | Bread from heaven is confessed as containing delight and sweetness. |
| Communion Antiphon B | John 6:35 | Christ identifies himself as the bread of life and promises freedom from hunger and thirst to those who come and believe. |
| Prayer after Communion | *Quos cælésti récreas múnere* | Those refreshed by the heavenly gift ask continuing protection and worthiness for eternal redemption. |

Sunday consumers require the Gloria and Creed. *Tempus per annum* rubric 5 and GIRM 365 govern the dependent Preface and Eucharistic Prayer selection: an authorized Sunday-in-Ordinary-Time Preface is used unless the selected authorized Eucharistic Prayer carries its own Preface. In particular, GIRM 365(d) binds Eucharistic Prayer IV to its invariable Preface. No Week XVIII proper Preface, Eucharistic Prayer insert, or Missal Offertory antiphon is supplied. Lectionary readings and cycle-specific branches belong to the leaves, not to this reusable owner.
