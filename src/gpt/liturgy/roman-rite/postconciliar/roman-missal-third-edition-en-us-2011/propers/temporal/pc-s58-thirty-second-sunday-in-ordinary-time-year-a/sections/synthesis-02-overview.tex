\begin{conciseoverview}
Literal\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal} & Wisdom approaches seekers; Paul consoles the bereaved; Jesus warns that preparation must precede the arrival. All ten virgins sleep.\\
Allegorical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical} & Christ is Bridegroom, risen Lord and shepherd; at Emmaus he is welcomed before he is recognized.\\
Moral\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral} & Chrysostom asks whether mercy has been withheld; Augustine and Gregory ask whether good works seek praise instead of proceeding from charity.\\
Anagogical\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical} & The final meeting discloses each person's life and purpose. Christ gathers the faithful dead; another's deeds or approval cannot supply the preparation that is missing.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:overview:end}
