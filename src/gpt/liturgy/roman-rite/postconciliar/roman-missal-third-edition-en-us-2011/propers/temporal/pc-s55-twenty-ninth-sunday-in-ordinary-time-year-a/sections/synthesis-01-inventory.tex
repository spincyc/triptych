\begin{properinventory}{Textual unit}{Text / citation}
\textbf{Entrance and prayer} & \\
Entrance & \textit{Ego clamavi}; cf. Psalm 16:6,8 (Vulgate), 17:6,8 (Hebrew); Missal adaptation.\\
Collect & \textit{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus, fac nos}; Week XXIX.\\
\textbf{Liturgy of the Word} & \\
First reading & Isaiah 45:1,4--6; Cyrus and the one Lord.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Psalm 96:1,3,4--5,7--8,9--10a,c (Hebrew; Vulgate 95); response 7b. Verse 10b is omitted.\\
Second reading & 1 Thessalonians 1:1--5b; ends before the final clause on the missionaries' conduct.\\
Alleluia verse & Philippians 2:15d,16a; two short verse portions.\\
Gospel & Matthew 22:15--21; tribute to Caesar; no shorter form.\\
\textbf{Eucharistic liturgy} & \\
Prayer over the Offerings & \textit{Tribue nos, Domine, quaesumus}; Week XXIX.\\
Communion & Either \textit{Ecce oculi Domini}, cf. Psalm 32:18--19 (Vulgate), 33:18--19 (Hebrew), an adaptation; or \textit{Filius hominis venit}, Mark 10:45, the ransom excerpt. Neither is selected here.\\
Prayer after Communion & \textit{Fac nos, quaesumus, Domine}; Week XXIX.\\
\end{properinventory}
