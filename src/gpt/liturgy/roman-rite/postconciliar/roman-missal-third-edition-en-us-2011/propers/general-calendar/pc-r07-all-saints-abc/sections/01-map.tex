\section{The texts of the Mass}
\begin{properinventory}{Textual unit}{Text / citation}
Entrance antiphon & \textit{Gaudeamus omnes in Domino}; composed festive antiphon.\\
Collect & \textit{Omnipotens sempiterne Deus}.\\
First Reading & Revelation 7:2--4,9--14.\\
Responsorial Psalm & Psalm 24:1bc--2,3--4ab,5--6; response cf. 6 (Hebrew numbering; Vulgate Psalm 23).\\
Second Reading & 1 John 3:1--3.\\
Gospel acclamation & Matthew 11:28.\\
Gospel & Matthew 5:1--12a.\\
Prayer over the Offerings & \textit{Grata tibi sint, Domine, munera}.\\
Proper Preface & The glory of Jerusalem, our mother.\\
Communion\newline antiphon & Matthew 5:8--10.\\
Prayer after Communion & \textit{Mirabilem te, Deus}.\\
Optional solemn blessing & All Saints, Solemn Blessing no. 18, if selected.\\
\end{properinventory}
The annual readings are common to all three Sunday cycles. Gloria and Creed are said. The proper Preface and optional solemn blessing retain their stated conditions. General chant permissions remain open; no parish choices are established.
