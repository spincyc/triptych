\begin{conciseoverview}
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:literal}Literal & God's children await manifestation and purify themselves in hope. The psalm asks who may approach God; Jesus blesses purity, peacemaking and fidelity under persecution. The multitude is cleansed in the Lamb's blood.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:allegorical}Allegorical & Christ cleanses and gathers his Church. Augustine explains the psalm through the risen Lord; Bede explains the robes through baptism, and Chrysostom explains peacemakers through the reconciling Son.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:moral}Moral & The will consents to God's help through innocent action, undivided desire and mercy. Reconciliation requires work among divided neighbors; opposition must be tested by justice, truth and fidelity to Christ.\\
\zlabel{triptych:concise:sense:anagogical}Anagogical & The saints stand together before God. The assembly hopes for the sight of God and the heavenly banquet in perfected charity, while still needing grace and perseverance.\\
\end{conciseoverview}
