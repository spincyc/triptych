### Bounded subjects for original commentary

These describe the inspected evidence for analysis, not substitute prayer
texts or translations. The Entrance concerns common festive joy and angelic
praise of the Son. The Collect concerns celebrating all the saints and
seeking divine mercy with their intercession. The Offerings prayer concerns
their continuing concern for the salvation of those still on the way. The
proper Preface concerns the heavenly mother-city, pilgrimage, and the saints'
help and example. Communion selects purity, peacemaking and persecution.
After Communion concerns completed charity and the heavenly banquet. The
permitted blessing concerns intercession, holy example, service and shared
final joy. No description establishes permission to print the underlying body.
