\section{References}

\subsection{Controlling books and norms}

\begin{itemize}
\item Paul VI, apostolic letter \emph{Mysterii Paschalis}, 14 February 1969, and the accompanying \emph{Calendarium Romanum} and Universal Norms.
\item \emph{Missale Romanum}, editio typica tertia (Vatican City: Typis Vaticanis, 2002), \emph{Normae universales de anno liturgico et de calendario} and \emph{Calendarium Romanum generale}; editio typica tertia, \emph{reimpressio emendata} (2008).
\item \emph{The Roman Missal}, Third Edition, for use in the Dioceses of the United States of America (2011), together with the 2010 confirmations and later amendments to the United States proper.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Historical reconstruction}

Paul F. Bradshaw and Maxwell E. Johnson, \emph{The Origins of Feasts, Fasts, and Seasons in Early Christianity} (London: SPCK; Collegeville: Liturgical Press, 2011), supplies the modern historical control for the early weekly, Paschal, Nativity--Epiphany, and saints cycles. Earlier Roman reforms are checked against Pius~V's \emph{Quod a nobis} (1568) and \emph{Quo primum} (1570); Pius~X's \emph{Divino afflatu} (1911); the 1955 decrees \emph{Cum hac nostra aetate} and \emph{Maxima redemptionis nostrae mysteria}; and John~XXIII's \emph{Rubricarum instructum} and \emph{Codex rubricarum} (1960).

\subsection{Official amendments and territorial controls}

The Dicastery for Divine Worship and the Discipline of the Sacraments maintains an \href{https://www.cultodivino.va/en/formazione/pubblicazioni/libri-liturgici/aliae/calendarium-romanum.html}{official decree index} for amendments through 2021. The later decrees for \href{https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2025/02/11/250211b.html}{St Teresa of Calcutta} and \href{https://www.cultodivino.va/en/attivita/2026/inscription-of-s-john-henry-newman-in-the-general-roman-calendar.html}{St John Henry Newman} are separately controlled. The current \href{https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/proper-calendar}{United States proper}, \href{https://www.usccb.org/committees/divine-worship/special-observances}{special-observance guidance}, and the \href{https://www.usccb.org/resources/2026cal.pdf}{2026 annual control} are published by the USCCB. Exact decree dates and effects are summarized in the timeline and audited in the research records.
