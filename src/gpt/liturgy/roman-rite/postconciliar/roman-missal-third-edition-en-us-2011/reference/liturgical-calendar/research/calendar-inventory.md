# Calendar Inventory and Reconciliation

## Inventory contract

The reader-facing tables in `sections/40-temporale.tex`, `sections/50-general-calendar.tex`, and `sections/60-us-calendar.tex` are the canonical row-level inventory. This record audits their completeness, source ownership, counts, amendment overlays, and collision-sensitive rows. A fixed date can contain more than one distinct optional memorial. Every celebration occupies its own singular row; dates therefore repeat where the calendar names coequal choices. The first keeps the date registry and each later row carries a unique, zero-width `\caldiscriminator{lowercase-kebab}` source identity; the discriminator states neither rank nor precedence.

### B1. Base transcription rule

- Rows printed with `Sollemnitas`, `Festum`, or `Memoria` in the 2002 calendar retain that rank unless an amendment says otherwise.
- Rows printed without a rank are optional memorials under the January footnote, “Quando non indicatur gradus celebrationis, fit Memoria ad libitum.”
- All Souls is inventoried as the sui generis commemoration printed by the General Calendar at position 3 of the Table of Liturgical Days; it precedes an Ordinary Time Sunday and is not the restricted commemoration of a memorial.

### B2. Proper Vigil forms

The fixed table has no duplicate date rows for Vigil Masses. The 2002 Missal supplies proper Vigil and Day forms for the Nativity of St John the Baptist (24 June), Sts Peter and Paul (29 June), and the Assumption (15 August). Together with the temporal vigils identified above, every proper Vigil attached to a calendar celebration is accounted for as a form of that celebration, not as a separate rank or date.

### C1. Proper of Time policies

1. Epiphany: Sunday 2–8 January.
2. Baptism: Monday after Epiphany when that Sunday is 7 or 8 January.
3. Ascension: Thursday in Boston, Hartford, New York, Omaha, and Philadelphia provinces; Seventh Sunday of Easter elsewhere, as confirmed by the 2026 USCCB calendar.
4. Corpus Christi: Sunday after Trinity throughout the territory.
5. Day of Prayer: when 22 January is Sunday, the observance moves to 23 January and both St Vincent and St Marianne Cope are omitted.
6. Immaculate Conception: occurrence transfer follows the Universal Norms; holy-day obligation is separately checked.
