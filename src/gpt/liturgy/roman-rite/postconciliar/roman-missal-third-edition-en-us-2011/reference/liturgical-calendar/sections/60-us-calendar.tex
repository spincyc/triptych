\subsection{What is optional}

The rows marked optional memorial are the national “optional feast days” in ordinary speech. They are genuine liturgical options, but their technical rank is \emph{optional memorial}, not optional feast. If a universal optional memorial shares the date---as St John of Avila does with St Damien or St Faustina with Blessed Francis Xavier Seelos---the celebrant may choose one permitted observance; their presence does not merge them into a composite celebration.
