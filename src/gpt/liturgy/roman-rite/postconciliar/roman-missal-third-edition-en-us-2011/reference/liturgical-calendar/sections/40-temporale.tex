\section{Complete Recurring Proper of Time}

Dates below are rules, not a 2026 computation. “Sunday” includes its liturgical day from First Vespers where the books provide it. Ordinary Time contains thirty-four numbered weeks, but the Sunday following the Baptism is called the Second Sunday in Ordinary Time; there is no Sunday titled the First Sunday in Ordinary Time.

Holy Week & Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday of Holy Week & Privileged weekdays.\\
Holy Week & Thursday of Holy Week, daytime & Lent continues until the Evening Mass of the Lord's Supper begins. The Chrism Mass is normally celebrated by the bishop with his presbyterate on this morning, subject to the book's pastoral provision for another nearby day.\\
Sacred Triduum & Evening Mass of the Lord's Supper & Evening of Holy Thursday; begins the Paschal Triduum.\\
Sacred Triduum & Friday of the Passion of the Lord & Good Friday; celebration of the Passion, not Mass.\\
Sacred Triduum & Holy Saturday & The Church remains at the Lord's tomb; no Mass before the Vigil.\\
Easter & Easter Vigil and Easter Sunday of the Resurrection & Vigil during the night and the Sunday following the ecclesiastical full moon on or after 21 March; if that full moon is Sunday, Easter is the following Sunday. Solemnity of solemnities.\\
Easter octave & Monday through Saturday within the octave & Each day ranks with Easter octave precedence; lower celebrations are omitted.\\
Easter & Second Sunday of Easter, or of Divine Mercy & Octave Sunday; followed by the Third through Seventh Sundays of Easter.\\
Easter & Weekdays of Easter Time & Monday after the octave through Saturday before Pentecost, with the sixth and seventh weeks shaped by the Ascension date.\\
Easter & Ascension of the Lord & Thursday of the sixth week, the fortieth day of Easter, or the Seventh Sunday where transferred; solemnity, with Vigil and Day forms.\\
Easter & Pentecost Sunday & Fiftieth day of Easter; solemnity, with Vigil and Day forms; ends Easter Time.\\
After Pentecost & Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of the Church & Monday after Pentecost; obligatory memorial added universally in 2018. If it coincides with another memorial of a saint or blessed, this memorial prevails under the 24 March 2018 notification.\\
Ordinary Time II & Resumption of numbered weeks & The Monday after Pentecost resumes the week number determined by counting backward from Week XXXIV; continues through Saturday before Advent.\\
After Pentecost & Most Holy Trinity & First Sunday after Pentecost; solemnity.\\
After Pentecost & Most Holy Body and Blood of Christ & Thursday after Trinity, or the following Sunday where transferred; solemnity.\\
After Pentecost & Most Sacred Heart of Jesus & Friday after the second Sunday after Pentecost; solemnity.\\
After Pentecost & Immaculate Heart of the Blessed Virgin Mary & Saturday following the Sacred Heart; obligatory memorial. When it coincides with another obligatory memorial, both are treated as optional memorials under the 1998 notification.\\
Ordinary Time & Sundays II through XXXIII & Recurring numbered Sundays that occur whenever not occupied by a higher celebration. The number sequence bridges the two portions of Ordinary Time.\\
Ordinary Time & Sunday of the Word of God & The Third Sunday in Ordinary Time, designated by \emph{Aperuit illis} in 2019; the act prescribes no replacement rank or lectionary for the existing Sunday.\\
Ordinary Time & Weekdays of Weeks I through XXXIV & Monday--Saturday formularies and weekday lectionary cycle, subject to celebrations and seasonal boundaries.\\
Ordinary Time & Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe & Last Sunday in Ordinary Time, taking the place of a separately titled Thirty-fourth Sunday; solemnity.\\
Year boundary & First Sunday of Advent & Sunday after Christ the King, between 27 November and 3 December; begins the next liturgical year.\\
