\section{Using the Inventory Responsibly}

For a concrete day, first locate the temporal day, then add the universal fixed entry, the United States overlay, and every diocesan or local proper. Rank all candidates with the current table of liturgical days. Apply the explicit rules for transfer, omission, and optional memorials; then consult the competent Ordo for local resolutions and current pastoral directions.

This recurring inventory does not decide holy-day obligations. Those obligations arise from canon law and territorial complementary norms and can differ in Hawaii or when a date falls on Saturday or Monday. Nor does a blank date prohibit a votive Mass, Mass for Various Needs, or Ritual Mass; those choices are governed by the Missal and the rank of the day.

Saturday memorials of the Blessed Virgin Mary are not printed on every blank Saturday. The Universal Norms permit that optional memorial on Saturdays in Ordinary Time when no obligatory memorial occurs. It is a recurring option generated by a weekday condition rather than a fixed sanctoral row.

The Universal Norms also retain Rogation Days and Ember Days as occasions of prayer for human needs and the harvest, while assigning their regulation to episcopal conferences. In the United States, the diocesan bishop designates the days or periods and their manner of observance under GIRM 373 and current USCCB guidance. They therefore create no additional recurring universal or nationwide dated row in this inventory.

\subsection{Layers deliberately omitted}

This publication does not inventory diocesan patrons, cathedral and parish dedications, titles of churches, saints or blesseds proper to a diocese, or religious-family calendars. It also does not infer a national celebration merely because a saint lived or worked in the United States. Those layers remain real, but their competent owner must establish them.
