# Advent history and spiritual readings

Reviewed November 23, 2026. The historical chapter identifies the place, witness and genre for each change of practice. The readings chapter interweaves checked historical English quotations, attributed paraphrases and suggestions for prayer at home. Those suggestions are the handbook's. The notes in each chapter identify the editions for a reader.

The historical English quotations are from the identified public-domain Gifford, Mount Melleray, Prevost/Riddle, Cunningham and Lewis versions. Printed before 1931, they are public domain in the United States at the date of review. The historical source words retain that status separately from the project's new prose. The accounts of the current Missal, Lectionary, Hours and modern ecclesiastical teaching are original descriptions or paraphrases. No current English oration, chant text, biblical lection or Office reading has been copied from those books.
