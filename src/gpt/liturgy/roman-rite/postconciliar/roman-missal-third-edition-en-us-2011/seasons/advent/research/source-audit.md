# Advent sources and limits

The comparative question, alternatives and decision are in [scope](scope.md).
The [lectionary audit](lectionary-audit.md) identifies the actual U.S. witnesses
and options; the [historical audit](tradition-audit.md) identifies the sources
for the narrative and the spiritual readings. Sections give their loci to the
reader. The following cases determine the structure of the book.

| Claim key | Witness and locus | What the witness establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| comparative-missal | *Missale Romanum*, Vatican, 1962, Proper of Time, pp. 1–16, especially p. 2 after Advent I, pp. 5–14 for the Ember Days and p. 15 for the collision with the Vigil. [Facsimile](https://media.churchmusicassociation.org/pdf/missale62.pdf) | The annual Sunday pair, dependent ferial Mass, particular Ember readings and explicit Vigil rubric. Compared to the documented 1962 handbook and the page images. No 1962 text is transcribed. |
| advent-boundaries | Universal Norms 3–5, 39–42, 45–47, 59–60, promulgated with Paul VI's *Mysterii Paschalis*. [Approved English text](https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Resources/GIRM/Documents/GNLY.pdf) | Twofold expectation and Evening Prayer boundaries. Sunday precedence, the December 17–24 weekdays, and the regional competence for Ember celebrations. U.S. proper celebrations are identified separately. |
| reading-course | *Ordo lectionum Missae* (1981), Praenotanda 65–69, 93–94 and nn. 175–201; U.S. Lectionary, nos. 1–12, 175–201. [Introduction](https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Resources/GIRM/Documents/Lectionary.pdf) | A/B/C Sunday structure, annual weekdays, options and changed key on December 17. The shared Latin source record establishes arrangement; the U.S. witnesses establish the appointments and numbering printed here. |
| four-collects | U.S. *Roman Missal*, Third Edition, Proper of Time, collects of Advent I–IV. [USCCB, texts and commentary](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/advent/commentary-on-advent-proper-prayers) | Four independent petitions. The Fourth Sunday collect and the Angelus are also joined by Paul VI in *Marialis cultus* 41. Exposition, no reproduction of ICEL text. |
| liturgy-and-charity | Vatican II, *Sacrosanctum Concilium* 7, 51, 83–84, 100, 102; General Instruction of the Roman Missal 53, 61, 68, 305, 313, 346, 355, 357. [Council text](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19631204_sacrosanctum-concilium_en.html) | Liturgical year, proclamation, psalm, Creed, participation in the Hours. Matthew 25 and 1 Cor 11 give a spiritual application to mercy and sharing. |
| hours | *Liturgy of the Hours*, U.S. ed., I, Office of Readings, Advent I, Sunday and Wednesday, and Evening Prayer, December 17–23. [General Instruction 37–56, 59, 64–65](https://www.liturgyoffice.org.uk/Resources/Rites/GILH.pdf); [Homiletic Directory 78–80](https://www.usccb.org/resources/Homiletic-Directory-CDWDS.pdf); [USCCB, O Antiphons](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-worship/liturgical-year/advent) | The two canticles, two readings, Cyril and Bernard, order of the O titles. The seven suggested biblical passages and household practices are editorial selections. |
| grace-and-penance | *Catechism* 524, 1451–1460; 1983 CIC, cc. 1249–1253; [U.S. pastoral statement](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/lent/us-bishops-pastoral-statement-on-penance-and-abstinence), nos. 7, 22–25, read with the U.S. complementary norm. | A freely chosen Advent sacrifice, continuing Friday practice and restitution after confession have different grounds. U.S. Latin Church horizon, checked November 23, 2026. |
| vigil-and-crib | U.S. Lectionary 13–16, [Christmas Vigil and links to the three Masses](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/122524-Vigil.cfm); Francis, [*Admirabile signum* 5–8](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/apost_letters/documents/papa-francesco-lettera-ap_20191201_admirabile-signum.html) | Evening Vigil and the proper sets for night, dawn and day; editorial meditation on Ruth and the genealogy. The Diocese of Orlando's [Advent and Christmas notes](https://www.orlandodiocese.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2025-Advent-Christmas.pdf), p. 5, corroborate the pastoral choice of sets. The pope's invitation to include the poor in the crib is paraphrased. |

The book gives references and original exegesis. U.S. lectionary translations,
modern psalm responses, ICEL prayers and the modern Office are consulted in
their approved books or by link. Their full texts are not republished here.
