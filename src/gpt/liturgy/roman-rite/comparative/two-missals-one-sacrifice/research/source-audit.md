## Rejected or materially corrected claims

1. “Pius V abolished every non-Roman liturgy” — rejected by the explicit 200-year exception.
2. “Nothing was suppressed in 1570” — rejected; unprotected younger Roman variants were displaced by class.
3. “Vatican II wrote the Novus Ordo and required hand Communion / facing the people / no Latin” — rejected by the council’s text and later chronology.
4. “The postconciliar Mass or revised sacraments are invalid” — rejected by the sacramental essentials, promulgating constitutions, and Church’s authoritative reception.
5. “Every liturgical abuse invalidates” — rejected; the affected validity element must be identified.
6. “Communion in the hand is universally required” and “always intrinsically sacrilegious” — both rejected under exact current law.
7. “Extraordinary ministers are ordinary parish staffing” — rejected by *Redemptionis Sacramentum*; their true necessity is preserved.
8. “Canonical erection proves unrestricted 1962 faculty” — rejected by the distinction between status, proper law, special law, and place.
9. “The SSPX remains merely irregular after 2 July 2026” — obsolete under the DDF act.
10. “Every lay visitor at SSPX is automatically excommunicated” — not the formal-adherence rule stated by the DDF.
