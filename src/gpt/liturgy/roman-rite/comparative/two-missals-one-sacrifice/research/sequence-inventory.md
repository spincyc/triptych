This inventory maps the stable 1962 Order to the 2002/2008 postconciliar Order. It is editorial, not a promulgated numbering scheme. “Correspondence” means comparable ritual function, not equal origin, wording, weight, or dramatic effect.

## Whole-order synthesis used in publication

- **Backward relation:** every unit is read from preparation toward Word, sacrifice, Presence, Communion, and mission rather than as an isolated prayer contest.
- **Forward relation:** a prayer can anticipate what the Canon effects without confusing unconsecrated gifts with the Victim; a Communion rite presupposes consecration and disposes reception.
- **Text versus ecology:** audibility, language, posture, orientation, chant, architecture, and options affect ritual perception but are not all fixed by the same source layer.
- **No symmetric inference:** a blank cell does not automatically mean a doctrine disappeared. The theme may be relocated or entrusted to another unit; the loss of repeated ritual emphasis can nevertheless be historically and pastorally material.

## Verification state

The order and principal incipits were checked against the named editions. Historical dossiers are calibrated ranges rather than author legends. Full independent Latin, rubrical, chant, and lectionary review remains outstanding.
