# Older-Liturgy Community Census — Row Audit

Cutoff: 2026-07-13. This is an audit trail for the table in *Two Missals, One Sacrifice*, not an official canonical directory or advice about a particular sacrament.

## Inclusion and search protocol

A row qualifies when a corporate jurisdiction, institute, society, public association, autonomous monastery, or organized separated body was located and stable common life materially centers on the 1962 Roman books or another pre-reform Latin use. One centralized institute receives one row; a canonically autonomous monastery can receive its own row. The audit excludes individual Mass sites, isolated priests, lay promotional groups, Eastern Catholics, Anglican Ordinariates, and ordinary institutes with only an occasional local old-form Mass.

The search used, in order:

1. Holy See and diocesan erection, suppression, reconciliation, and penalty acts.
2. Public proper or special law, including the FSSP decree.
3. Official institute, abbey, monastery, and community records for self-description and current activity.
4. Catholic hierarchical directories as secondary leads, never as an unqualified substitute for a primary decree.
5. The dynamic Wikipedia list (revision 12 July 2026), Latin Mass directories, and targeted searches as discovery only.
6. Negative searches for suppression, departure from communion, renaming, and post-2021 authorization.

No public universal Holy See registry answering this exact category was located. “Complete” in the publication means complete under this reproducible search boundary.

## Status vocabulary

- **Catholic — pontifical / jurisdictional:** a competent papal or dicastery act establishes the body’s canonical place.
- **Catholic — diocesan / autonomous:** a local ordinary or recognized monastic structure establishes communion; exact rank is stated only when found.
- **Catholic — qualified:** first-party and ecclesial evidence supports communion, but the primary juridic act or current old-book faculty is not public.
- **Mixed use:** older liturgy materially belongs to the community but is not exclusive.
- **Former / suppressed:** an older canonical description is no longer current.
- **Separated / schismatic:** a competent act or the body’s self-declaration establishes rupture.
- **Independent / no recognition located:** no current Catholic canonical recognition was found; this is not a global declaration about sacramental lineage.

Canonical status and liturgical faculty are recorded separately. A public Mass schedule proves neither.

### Candidates deliberately not promoted

- Buffalo, Kensington, Traverse City, and other Carmels had dated or dead schedule evidence but no current stable-use corroboration located.
- Ordinary Dominican priories, Oratories, and Norbertine houses with an occasional old-use Mass fail the corporate-center test.
- Franciscan Friars of the Immaculate remain Catholic but do not meet a present community-wide TLM-center rule after the 2013 restriction absent a newer competent act.
- Bellefontaine had 2025 secondary reporting of a traditional renewal, but no sufficiently authoritative current erection/faculty record was located.
- Mass-site directories were not counted as congregations.

## Negative-result and maintenance notes

- No universal Holy See register of all stable older-Latin-liturgy corporate bodies was found.
- No public Leo XIV repeal of *Traditionis custodes*, the Responsa, or the 2023 rescript was found through cutoff.
- No public decree giving every former Ecclesia Dei institute the FSSP’s exact 2022 faculty was found.
- Absence of an online decree is not proof that a private rescript or proper-law provision does not exist.
- The SSPX and FSSR rows must never be copied from a pre-May/July 2026 source without updating the endpoint.
- Refresh high-priority and highest-priority rows before every new publication; refresh every row at least annually.
