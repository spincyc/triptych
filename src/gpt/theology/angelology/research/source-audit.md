# Source audit: GPT angelology

Inspected and consolidated 29 September 2026. This index and the linked detailed audits jointly record every controlling witness, edition or translation, actual reading scope, rights basis, and verification ceiling. Exact artifact identities, acquisition states, and fingerprints are in [source-bindings.toml](source-bindings.toml). Scratch paths in the detailed records preserve acquisition history; durable registered sources and bindings control reproducibility.

The complete systematic reading covers all 118 articles of *Summa theologiae* I qq.50–64 and 106–114 (72 + 46), including objections, *sed contra*, corpus, and replies. All fifteen chapters of the *Celestial Hierarchy* were read in Parker's English translation. Complete acquisition of another work is not a claim that the whole work was read. An authority quoted by Aquinas remains an authority invoked by Aquinas unless a separate original-locus inspection is recorded below.

## Controlling corrections

- John Damascene's *Exposition* is translated by **S. D. F. Salmond**. The selected CCEL volume and constituent title establish the **Edinburgh 1898** witness. New Advent's volume-wide Watson/Pullan and 1899 footer does not control the constituent translator or selected printing.
- Parker's *Works*, part II, is **1899**, confirmed on the facsimile title. Inclusive chapter ranges include shared transition pages; I is pp.1–4 and III pp.13–16. The full corrected fifteen-row map is in the Dionysian audit.
- The grade **Thomist position** identifies Aquinas's systematic determination. **Disputed** requires an actual open division among identified witnesses; it does not mean that a consensus survey was not performed. [Question inventory](question-inventory.md) carries the integrated proposition grades.
- Bonaventure's Quaracchi volume II is **1885**, read on its title image; generic Internet Archive set metadata giving 1882 does not control that volume.
- Gregory's order discussion is *Homiliae in Evangelia* **XXXIV**. Several English Summa locators print XXIV; direct Gregory reading controls. Augustine's passage invoked in I.109.4 was verified in *De Trinitate* **III.4.9**, despite the received Summa reference to II.
- The Directory on Popular Piety's decree is dated **17 December 2001**, following papal authorization on 14 December. A 2002 component in its web URL is not independently a promulgation date.

## Research boundaries and records

The linked audits preserve rejected finding aids, mistaken locators and subsequent corrections so that they cannot silently become evidence. They distinguish an author's own statement from a medieval attribution or modern editorial note. They claim neither exhaustive patristic coverage nor exhaustive modern scholarship, and no independent specialist theological review. Root records integrated production checks separately.

The [order inventory](order-inventory.md) controls comparative ranks and source-term mappings. The [terminology audit](terminology-audit.md) records preferred terms and translation collisions. The [question inventory](question-inventory.md) controls article completeness and proposition grades. Each has a different purpose from the artifact identity and fingerprint record in [source-bindings.toml](source-bindings.toml).
