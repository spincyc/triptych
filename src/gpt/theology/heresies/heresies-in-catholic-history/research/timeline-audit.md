# Historical Timeline — Row Audit

This record controls the event-based appendix in `sections/95-timeline.tex`. It is divided into two dossier-level ledgers so that every rendered row retains a reproducible source, event type, and qualification:

- [`timeline-audit-ancient-medieval.md`](timeline-audit-ancient-medieval.md): apostolic period through Lateran V (1513);
- [`timeline-audit-modern.md`](timeline-audit-modern.md): *Exsurge Domine* (1520) through the SSPX consecrations of 1 July 2026 and the DDF decree and explanatory note of 2 July, with currentness checked through 16 July 2026.

## Audit rule

Every timeline row must identify:

1. date or date range and whether it is approximate;
2. place and competent authority;
3. exact object: proposition, person, writing, movement, communion, or enforcement event;
4. act and exact locus, or a named near-contemporary witness where no act survives;
5. response type: apostolic text, refutation, episcopal act, local or regional synod, papal act, ecumenical council, profession, inquisitorial judgment, or civil enforcement;
6. immediate effect and any materially qualifying later reception;
7. evidence grade; and
8. the corresponding dossier or contextual disposition in `corpus-inventory.md`.

An asterisk in the rendered appendix marks an event whose controlling character is civil or coercive. Its audit row must name the civil authority and must not attribute exile, confiscation, crusade, torture, imprisonment, or execution to a creed or dogmatic canon. A date range may consolidate closely connected sessions only when the companion ledger identifies the constituent acts.

## Cross-file checks

- The 543 and 553 rows keep the anti-Origenist dossier and the formal Three Chapters proceedings distinct.
- The 680–681 row and its ledger distinguish Constantinople III's sentence from Leo II's 682 confirmation.
- Hieria, Nicaea II, Frankfurt, and the 843 restoration remain separate events.
- Berengar's 1059 and 1079 professions are not treated as verbally identical.
- Lateran IV's doctrinal constitutions and coercive legislation are identified separately within the row/ledger.
- Constance's proposition censures, personal sentences, secular executions, and Martin V's later confirmation/interrogation instrument remain distinct.
- Trent's sessions are identified by session and doctrinal object rather than compressed into a denominational condemnation.
- Every post-Tridentine proposition list preserves an individual or collective censure as the act actually supplies it.
- The 1973, 1984, 1994, 1999, and 2001 rows are later qualifications or agreements, not retroactive cancellations of historical acts.
- The modern ledger and rendered appendix contain 65 rows and keep the 13 May 2026 DDF warning, 29 June papal letter, and 2 July decree/note responding to the completed 1 July act as three distinct responses.
- Currentness is stated in the terminal `Scope, Terms, and Qualifications` appendix and source audit rather than inserted as a fictitious response event in the chronology.

The final page and source audits compare every rendered date and proper name against these ledgers. A timeline row without a ledger entry is a publication blocker.
