# Dossier Source Audit — Sections 60–80

## Scope, method, and status language

This companion to [`source-audit.md`](source-audit.md) maps every full dossier in sections 60, 70, and 80 to the target-side witness actually needed to state the proposition and the response-side act actually needed to state its condemnation, correction, permission, or later qualification. It covers 46 dossiers: 10 Reformation, 13 post-Reformation, and 23 modern or contemporary dossiers. The inventory, timeline, and catalog remain controlled by their own records.

The audit was last updated on 2026-07-16. “Mapped” means that a concrete source and locus have been identified; it does not claim that every edition, translation, attribution, or archival witness has been collated. “Checked” means that the named formal act or official current source was inspected at the level stated. “Open” identifies work still required before expanding direct quotation or making a finer attribution. Denzinger–Hünermann numbers and online Denzinger transcriptions are locators, not the promulgating authority. A target extracted by an opponent, inquisitor, or Roman examiner remains a mediated target even when the response act is authentic.

## Priority unresolved checks

2. **Extracted Roman lists:** build proposition-to-target-work concordances for Baius, Jansenius, the laxist and rigorist lists, Molinos, Quesnel, Fénelon, Pistoia, Rosmini, and *Lamentabili*. Until then, the act’s extracted wording is secure but complete authorial attribution is not.
3. **Confessional corpora:** designate critical editions and stable translations for Lutheran, Zwinglian, Reformed, Anabaptist, Anglican, spiritualist, Servetan, and Socinian target texts.
4. **Book prohibitions without numbered notes:** inspect the original Index or Holy Office registers for Hermes, Günther, Febronius, and related acts before any finer censure claim.
5. **Contemporary status:** preserve 2026-07-16 as the SSPX as-of date. The 13 May 2026 DDF statement is material but prospective; absence of a later identified dispositive act is an unresolved documentary limit, not proof that no later event occurred.

## Rights and quotation boundary

The descriptions and audit judgments above are project-created. Official, confessional, archival, scholarly, and translated source texts remain third-party or public-domain material according to their own status and the repository-wide exclusions. This record gives focused bibliographic locators and paraphrases; it does not reproduce the underlying editions or imply permission from online availability.
