# Heresies in Catholic History — Source Audit

## Audit identity and method

This audit controls `main.tex` and its imported sections. It is read together with:

- [`scope.md`](scope.md), which fixes the question, audience, corpus rule, and limitations;
- [`corpus-inventory.md`](corpus-inventory.md), which controls normalized names, aliases, brief notices, contexts, and exclusions;
- [`timeline-audit.md`](timeline-audit.md), which accounts for every event in the rendered chronology;
- `source-audit-ancient-medieval.md`, which gives dossier-level ancient and medieval sources and qualifications; and
- `source-audit-modern.md`, which gives dossier-level Reformation, post-Tridentine, modern, and contemporary sources and qualifications.

The audit asks of every consequential claim:

1. Is the wording the target's, a formal act's, a judicial extract, an opponent's paraphrase, or a later catalogue's?
2. Does the cited authority have the jurisdiction and genre attributed to it?
3. Is the censure individual, collective, `in globo`, disciplinary, penal, or civil?
4. Does a positive definition establish the proposition reported, or is the narrative drawing a source-grounded historical synthesis?
5. Does the aftermath document continuity, recurrence, suppression, reconciliation, or only later similarity?
6. Does current terminology or an ecumenical agreement materially qualify the inherited label?

No private compilation is treated as the promulgating authority. Denzinger–Hünermann numbers, Tanner, Mansi, New Advent, Clerus, sourcebooks, OCR, and online transcriptions are locators or working texts. Where the exact censure or formula matters, the named act, its official record, or a critical original-language edition controls.

## High-risk censure and attribution checks

The following traps received explicit controls in the narrative, inventory, and timeline:

1. **Ancient group multiplication:** source-specific aliases are crosswalked rather than added as independent organizations.
2. **Montanism:** lost regional acts are not invented; original prophecy, later discipline, and Tertullian's adherence are separated.
3. **Nicaea/Constantinople:** surviving creed, anathemas, canons, letters, and reception are not inflated into complete minutes.
4. **Priscillian:** surviving tractates are set against later allegations; the Trier execution is identified as civil.
5. **“Semi-Pelagian”:** Orange's propositions control; the later label is not treated as a self-designation.
6. **Ephesus:** Nestorius's writings, Cyril's letters/anathemas, session I, rival session, and 433 reunion remain distinct.
7. **Chalcedon:** Eutyches, Dioscorus, the definition, imperial enforcement, and present Oriental Orthodox faith are not collapsed.
8. **Origenism:** the 543 dossier and disputed fifteen anathemas are not silently made formal session acts of 553.
9. **Honorius:** Constantinople III and Leo II's Greek/Latin confirmation wording govern; later infallibility debates do not replace the sources.
10. **Iconoclasm:** Hieria, Nicaea II, Frankish translation/reception, and later restoration are separate.
11. **Photian and 1054 ruptures:** schism, jurisdiction, creed, and later restoration/reconciliation are not converted into one heresy.
12. **Cathars:** direct ritual/doctrinal texts and regional evidence are distinguished from a single trans-European model.
13. **Waldensians/Humiliati:** initial discipline, later doctrinal development, and reconciled Catholic branches remain visible.
14. **Joachim:** Lateran IV's censure of a Trinitarian proposition and its protection of his monastery/submission both appear.
15. **Paris 1277:** local scope and proposition attribution remain visible; no universal 219-heresy claim.
16. **Beguines/Spirituals:** specified propositions and factions do not condemn entire ways of life.
17. **Constance:** Wyclif/Hus attribution, mixed censures, session authority, and secular executions remain separate.
18. **Exsurge/Unigenitus:** collective “respectively” clauses are not redistributed into invented individual grades.
19. **De auxiliis:** Paul V's permission boundary prevents rival Catholic schools being catalogued as heresies.
20. **Fénelon/Ontologism/Humani generis:** original lower or prudential notes are not upgraded to formal heresy.
21. **Rosmini:** the 2001 CDF clarification governs attribution of the 1887 propositions.
22. **Americanism:** Leo XIII's conditional framing and confidence in the American hierarchy are retained.
23. **Feeney:** the doctrinal correction and later excommunication for disobedience are distinguished.
24. **Postconciliar authors:** text-level notifications do not become judgments of subjective culpability or a new universal heresy list.
25. **Neo-Gnosticism/neo-Pelagianism:** 2018 documents use analogies and expressly do not assert historical identity.

## Translation, quotation, and rights status

- Scripture is cited by book/chapter/verse and paraphrased; no modern translation is reproduced in bulk.
- Ancient and medieval original-language critical editions control where available. New Advent/NPNF, Clerus, Fordham, and comparable English pages are working access texts, not represented as critical editions.
- ACO, CSEL, GCS, *Corpus Christianorum*, *Sources Chrétiennes*, MGH, papal registers, and document-specific editions remain the required controls before any future expansion of direct quotation.
- Holy See and Christian Unity pages are treated as official web witnesses or translations where supplied; official status is not represented as project ownership or blanket permission.
- Denzinger–Hünermann and Tanner are cited as scholarly corpora. Their selection, translation, notes, and arrangement are third-party material; the publication does not reproduce them at scale.
- Inquisitorial depositions are mediated by interrogation, translation, coercive setting, and scribal record. They are never quoted as transparent community catechisms without corroboration.
- Direct quotations are limited to short proposition or definition identifiers whose exact wording matters. The surrounding exposition, classification, comparison, and layout are project-created.

## Rejected or bounded leads

- Lists on apologetics sites, Wikipedia, quotation aggregators, unsourced infographics, and AI-generated catalogues were rejected as evidence of an act or proposition.
- Similarity of ideas was rejected as proof of continuous descent from Gnostics to Manichaeans, Paulicians, Bogomils, Cathars, Reformers, Modernists, or present movements.
- The number in an ancient heresiology was rejected as proof of the number of actual distinct communities.
- The label “anathema” was rejected as automatic proof of a solemn infallible definition or a present canonical crime.
- Every civil execution or confiscation was rejected as though it were the doctrinal content of the preceding ecclesial act.
- Present Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, Assyrian, Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Waldensian, Old Catholic, or other separated Christians were rejected as automatic bearers of founders' culpability.
- The Index of Forbidden Books was rejected as evidence that every listed book or author was formally heretical.
- Action Française, Galileo, Templars, witch sects, Freemasonry, Communism, Nazism, ordinary schisms, and current polemical labels were rejected from the main heresy sequence unless a precise doctrinal object and competent act met the census rule.
- “The Church later reversed the doctrine” was rejected where a later source instead clarified attribution, terminology, civil liberty, ecumenical application, or a disciplinary regime.

## Internal consistency and review state

The dossier headings, corpus keys, timeline events, and reader-facing census are crosschecked before installation. Every full dossier contains the four profile-required fields. Placeholder language, generic `emph{}` markup defects, and uncited direct quotations are publication blockers. The final scan must also confirm that:

- every document dossier has an inventory disposition;
- every timeline row has a matching audit row;
- no main text calls all items in a mixed list heretical;
- no current communion is labeled by a disavowed historic formula;
- the source and reference records identify the same acts and dates; and
- the catalog's maturity and exact-snapshot distribution statement agree with the installed artifact and manifest.

Independent specialist review remains outstanding in all disciplines and languages listed in the research scope.
