\textbf{Formal definition. A sacrament of the New Law is a visible sign of a hidden reality, instituted by Christ to confer grace.}
It is an \emph{efficacious} ecclesial sign: Christ, the principal cause, uses its sensible rite instrumentally to cause the grace it signifies in a capable and duly disposed recipient. Augustine calls the element a ``visible word''; Aquinas orders every sacrament to Christ's Passion, present grace, and future glory (CCC 1131; Trent VII; Augustine, \emph{Tractates on John} 80.3; ST III, q.~60, a.~3).
