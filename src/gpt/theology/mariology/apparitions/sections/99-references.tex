\section{Sources and Status Documentation}

The links below lead to the texts actually used in fixing status, corpus, doctrine, and historical outline. Official acts and competent diocesan or shrine records control; journalistic and devotional sources never upgrade a judgment. All mutable ecclesial-status links and the DDF document index were checked on 13 July 2026. The repository research record identifies the claim-level use and the limitations of each source.

\subsection{Historical context}

The repository source audit maps each work to the exact contextual use and preserves the distinction between history and ecclesial judgment.
