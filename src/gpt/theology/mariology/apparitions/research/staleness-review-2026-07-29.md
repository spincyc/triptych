## Candidate disagreement and verdict

The minimal candidate would add LG 12 as useful conciliar reinforcement. The
rewritten candidate independently arrives at the same authority taxonomy,
corpus method, doctrinal boundaries, and pastoral limits, while showing where
the new Catechism passages could govern a claim if a dossier required them.
Their only disagreement with the old paper is citation density and
organization. Neither candidate adds, removes, strengthens in practical
effect, weakens, or contradicts a consequential conclusion.

**Verdict: no material change.** The present authoring and installed
publication should remain unchanged. This record does not rebaseline the
edition; that operation is deliberately outside this review.
