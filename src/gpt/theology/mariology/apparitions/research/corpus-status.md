## Editorial invariants

- `Nihil obstat` means positive pastoral authorization without certainty about supernatural authenticity.
- “Approved by the Church” is avoided unless immediately unpacked by authority, date, formula, and scope.
- A seer's canonization does not authenticate all extraordinary phenomena in that person's life.
- A shrine, feast, coronation, papal visit, imprimatur, or permission to publish does not by itself establish supernatural origin.
- A message may contain human limitation even within a spiritually fruitful phenomenon; the 1978 and 2024 norms expressly contemplate this.
- No status is transitive: an image modeled on another image does not import the other case's judgment, and thematic resemblance does not extend another decree.

## Source, language, and review controls

- English Holy See translations control the 2024–2025 DDF analyses. Local decrees were checked in Italian, French, Spanish, Croatian, Slovak, or Polish as applicable, with the exact operative formula retained where translation could change status.
- Gietrzwałd is controlled by the Polish decree scan, not a devotional paraphrase; its double negative was independently checked against the original. Akita is controlled by Bishop Narui's 2026 Japanese explanation and its stated practical consequence, not by older English claims of “Vatican approval.”
- French, Italian, Spanish, Croatian, Polish, Slovak, and Japanese sources were machine-assisted for navigation and independently compared at the decisive formula. No complete critical translation of every message corpus is claimed.
- Betania's 1987 act is available here through an interested foundation's document copy rather than a current diocesan archive; San Nicolás through AICA's act reports; Cuapa through a digitized Spanish narrative. Their provenance and resulting limitations remain explicit in the source audit.
- The legacy tier is selected and source-audited, not a universal diocesan registry. The DDF index search is complete for positive Marian determinations from the 2024 Norms through the as-of date; Maccio was reviewed and excluded as non-Marian, while Vailankanni was retained as authoritative reception without a formal result formula.
