# Source Audit — Marian Apparitions in Catholic Ecclesial Judgment

**Checked through:** 2026-07-13
**Governing profile:** universal editorial standard plus `guidance/theology/mariology.md`; this is an authority-qualified Mariological reference work, not a general article-profile document.
**Publication claim:** a selected, source-audited legacy and received-tradition corpus plus every positive Marian determination located in the DDF index from the 2024 Norms through the as-of date; not a universal registry of all historical diocesan judgments.

## Audit method

1. Status was fixed from the competent act itself where publicly available, then from an official Holy See, diocesan, or shrine transcription or report.
2. A shrine, feast, coronation, saint's canonization, papal visit, imprimatur, or permission to publish was not converted into an apparition-authenticity judgment.
3. Each case was recorded across this audit, `corpus-status.md`, and the narrative by authority, act date, formula or best exact paraphrase, object, corpus, geographical reach, later controlling act, source language, and material uncertainty.
4. Historical and message summaries use official diocesan/shrine dossiers where possible and critical historical scholarship for sensitive context. They are summaries, not complete editions of third-party message corpora.
5. Current DDF acts and diocesan status were rechecked on the as-of date, including the DDF's reverse-chronological complete document index: <https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/doc_doc_index.htm>. The absence of a universal registry is a material limit, not evidence that additional cases do not exist.

## Corpus baseline and consequential negative findings

- Vatican News's 2020 survey of fifteen “officially accepted” apparitions was used only as a historical candidate baseline. Its former URL now returns no live article, so it is not relied upon for any status proposition; every retained case has an independent authority source above. Amsterdam is controlled by the DDF's 2024 disclosure, and San Nicolás by its own 2016 decree. The legacy tier is expressly selected rather than universal.
- Searches of DDF documents through the as-of date yielded six positive-pastoral Marian decisions under the 2024 norms: Placanica, Montichiari, Chandavila, Pellevoisin, Medjugorje, and Litmanová. Vailankanni received a warm DDF letter but no formal result formula; it remains in the received-tradition tier.
- The same DDF search located and classified the Marian negative Holy Mountain/Elenita act, Amsterdam, Trevignano, Mount S. Onofrio, and other boundary cases. Maccio was reviewed and excluded from this corpus because the DDF act concerns a non-Marian Trinitarian experience.
- The official Niigata 2026 statement defeats recurring secondary claims that Cardinal Ratzinger or the Vatican approved Akita. No private oral report was allowed to overrule the competent public statement.
- Knock's two commission findings and authoritative reception are strong but are not rewritten as a located *constat* decree.
- No one case's status is transferred through thematic resemblance, copied imagery, a shared recipient, or a relation between devotions.
