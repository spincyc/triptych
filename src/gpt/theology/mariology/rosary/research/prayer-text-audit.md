## Rendered object and textual boundary

[The Form of the Prayer](../sections/10-prayer-form.tex) explains the decade,
the Hail Mary's development, and variable opening and closing customs. The
scope's copyright record expressly states that complete prayers are not
reproduced. This publication therefore has component descriptions and limited
quoted wording, rather than an independently executable received prayer form.
Recitation-text collation is not applicable to this exposition's present
source selection, which selects no complete vocal text.
The current Marian profile's complete-prayer-first requirement is not
established by this index.
