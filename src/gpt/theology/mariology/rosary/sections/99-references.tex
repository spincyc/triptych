\subsection{Scripture, councils, and catechism}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The biblical loci are identified within each mystery study. The principal narrative sources are Luke 1--2; Matthew 3, 17, and 26--28; Mark 1, 8--9, and 14--16; Luke 3, 9, and 22--24; John 2, 6, and 18--21; Acts 1--2; 1 Corinthians 10--11 and 15; and Revelation 12.
  \item Second Vatican Council, \emph{Dei Verbum}, especially 2--4: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651118_dei-verbum_en.html}{Holy See text}.
  \item Second Vatican Council, \emph{Lumen gentium}, especially 52--69: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html}{Holy See text}.
  \item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church}, 65--67, 244--248, 484--511, 522--560, 571--623, 638--667, 731--741, 963--972, 1213--1284, 1322--1419, 1427--1429, 1471--1479, 2617--2619, 2673--2679, and 2705--2708. Principal online divisions: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM}{Holy See index}; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_four/section_one/chapter_two/article_2.html}{Mary in Christian prayer}; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_four/section_one/chapter_three/article_1/ii_meditation.html}{meditation}.
  \item Council of Trent, session XXII, chapter 2, on the sacramental presence of the one sacrifice, and session XIII, chapter 4, on transubstantiation; cited through CCC 1366--1377 and the conciliar decrees.
  \item Third Council of Constantinople, exposition of faith on Christ's two natural wills; cited through CCC 475.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Historical study}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Robert of Ostrevant, \emph{Vita Aiberti}, in \emph{Acta Sanctorum}, April I, 673--680: \sourceurl{https://www.documentacatholicaomnia.eu/04z/z_1643-1925__Societe_des_Bollandistes__Acta_Sanctorum_04_Aprilis_Tomus_01_1675__LT.pdf.html}{digitized Bollandist volume}. Used with Thurston's historical analysis for Aibert's pre-Dominican counted-Ave discipline, not as evidence of the later complete Rosary.
  \item \emph{Ancrene Wisse}, part I, lines 300 and 332--337, ed. Robert Hasenfratz: \sourceurl{https://metseditions.org/read/DP6mDZVUzAY1uNLwU70zrhMQdQ09XGa}{critical Middle English text}. Used for the early-thirteenth-century direction to pray fifty or a hundred Aves and to order a fifty in five tens with distinct postures.
  \item Girolamo Savonarola, \emph{Esposizione sopra l'Ave Maria} (Florence: Bartolommeo di Libri, c.~1495--1496), first printed leaf: \sourceurl{https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Esposizione_sopra_l\%27Ave_Maria_\%28IA_ita-bnc-in2-00000229-001\%29.pdf}{digitized primary text}. The near-modern petition omits \emph{nostrae}; it is not mislabeled the exact final formula.
  \item \emph{Breviarium Romanum: Editio princeps} (1568), anastatic ed. Manlio Sodi and Achille Maria Triacca (Vatican City: Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 1999), facsimile 31: \sourceurl{https://books.google.com/books?id=NTbZAAAAMAAJ\&pg=PA31}{digitized liturgical primary text}. Used for the complete familiar Latin form of the Hail Mary.
  \item Herbert Thurston, ``Hail Mary,'' \emph{Catholic Encyclopedia} 7 (1910): \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07110b.htm}{documented historical synthesis}. Used for the joined biblical greetings, pre-Dominic counted Aves, variable petition stage, and 1568 liturgical stabilization; its dated judgments remain subject to the checked primary witnesses and later scholarship.
  \item Riccardo Barile, O.P., ``The Rosary in History: From the Beginning to the Consolidation of Its Actual Structure,'' \emph{L'Osservatore Romano}, English edition, 22 January 2003, 8--9; accessible in \sourceurl{https://www.catholicculture.org/culture/library/view.cfm?recnum=4728}{English reprint}. Used for the medieval sequence, Dominic of Prussia, Alain de la Roche, confraternities, printed-mystery consolidation, and exact limits of Pius V's description.
  \item Nicola Sinclair, ``En Route to the Ave: Rogier van der Weyden's Miraflores Altarpiece and the Nascent Rosary'' (M.A. thesis, Temple University, 2012), especially 37: \sourceurl{https://scholarshare.temple.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/01abacb7-4dbd-4999-8a33-3641cf0951b0/content}{institutional PDF}. Used for Dominic of Prussia's clause cycle and transmission.
  \item Klara H. Broekhuijsen, ``The Institution of the Rosary: Establishing the Context for a Recently Discovered Copy after a Lost Panel by Geertgen tot Sint Jans in the Pommersfelden Book of Hours, Ms.~343,'' \emph{Oud Holland} 123 (2010), 220--234, especially 223--225: \sourceurl{https://rkddb.rkd.nl/rkddb/digital_book/18750176_123_03-04_s003_text.pdf}{peer-reviewed PDF}. Used for the distinction between Dominic's \emph{vita} and Alain's stories, Dominic of Prussia, Alain, Cologne, confraternal print, and late-medieval visual reception.
  \item Rita George Tvrtkovi\'c, ``Our Lady of Victory or Our Lady of Beauty?: The Virgin Mary in Early Modern Dominican and Jesuit Approaches to Islam,'' \emph{Journal of Jesuit Studies} 7 (2020), 403--416, especially 405: \sourceurl{https://brill.com/view/journals/jjs/7/3/article-p403_403.pdf}{peer-reviewed PDF}. Used to corroborate Alain's fifteenth-century Dominic linkage and Sprenger's 1475 Cologne confraternity.
\end{itemize}
