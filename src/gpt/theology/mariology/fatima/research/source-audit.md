# Fátima Monograph — Claim-Level Source Audit

All online sources were last rechecked on 2026-07-13 unless otherwise noted. Stable official sources control. The Shrine's historical pages are authoritative custodial and pastoral witnesses but do not replace the competent bishop's act or turn a later memoir into a contemporaneous record.

## Theological interpretation

| Consequential claim | Source and exact locus | Use and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interior visions can involve a real transcendent impulse while being received through the visionary's finite capacities; they are not photographs. | CDF 2000, “The anthropological structure of private revelations.” | Governs source discrepancies and symbolic reading without reducing the event either to fantasy or dictation. |
| Prophecy primarily declares God's will for the present; the future is conditional, freedom matters, and fatalism must be rejected. | CDF 2000, “Public Revelation and private revelations” and interpretation, especially discussion of the flaming sword and freedom. | Governs all war, Russia, assassination, and timetable readings. |
| First/second part key: salvation of souls; Immaculate Heart: Mary's grace-formed `fiat`; third part key: threefold penance; mountain/city/cross: history and Way of the Cross; martyr blood: suffering fruitful only in Christ. | CDF 2000, interpretive paragraphs following publication of the secret. | Authoritative theological synthesis; not a claim that every symbol has only one historical referent. |
| Christ alone is Redeemer and Mediator; Marian intercession, reparation, and cooperation are received and subordinate. | Vatican II, *Lumen gentium* 56, 60–62; CCC 970; DDF, *Mater Populi Fidelis* (4 Nov. 2025), especially 17–29, 45–55, 62–70. <https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html> <https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20251104_mater-populi-fidelis_en.html> | Guards the theology of sacrifice, consolation, reparation, consecration, and Mary's Heart. |

| Source | Editorial use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jeffrey S. Bennett, *When the Sun Danced: Myth, Miracles, and Modernity in Early Twentieth-Century Portugal* (University of Virginia Press, 2012). | First Republic, war, modern media, witness plurality, national/international reception. | Historical interpretation, not ecclesial authority or proof/disproof of supernatural origin. |

OCR was used for discovery and checked against printed pages for consequential wording. |
| Shrine narrative and chronology. | Received narrative, documentary dates, places, seers, causes, and later cycle. | Public English translation contains occasional awkwardness and combines source strata; it is not treated as a critical English edition. |

Rejected as proof: anonymous internet secret texts; polemical “consecration failed” apostolates; sensational newspaper summaries; alleged private statements used against the Holy See's 2016 communiqué; astronomical or meteorological certainty unsupported by a cited protocol; crowd numbers presented without ranges; and any claim that canonization, a papal visit, a feast, a shrine, or a devotional promise by itself certifies every apparition or phrase.
