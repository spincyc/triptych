# Fátima Corpus and Ecclesial-Status Control

Checked through 2026-07-13. This inventory controls the publication. Narrative prose may explain these rows but may not broaden their objects or upgrade their authority.

| Corpus element | Date and place | Source / competent act | Current treatment | Boundary |

| Reported cloud-like occurrences involving Lúcia and companions | 1915, near Aljustrel | Lúcia's Fourth Memoir (1941), as identified by the Shrine chronology | Retrospective prehistory in the received Fátima narrative | No contemporaneous record located; not named in the 1930 operative formula |
| Three reported Angel apparitions to Lúcia, Francisco, and Jacinta | Spring, summer, autumn 1916, Cabeço and Poço do Arneiro | Lúcia's Second and Fourth Memoirs (1937, 1941); official Shrine presentation | Strongly received preparatory cycle | Not a contemporaneous 1916 dossier and not named in the 1930 operative formula |
| Six reported Marian apparitions | 13 May–13 October 1917, Cova da Iria / Valinhos | Bishop José Alves Correia da Silva, pastoral letter, 13 Oct. 1930: visions declared `dignas de crédito`; cult officially permitted | Legacy worthy-of-belief judgment and authorized cult | Prudent human assent, not divine and Catholic faith; the act does not make every later wording or explanation inerrant |
| Reported “seventh” apparition to Lúcia | 15 June 1921, Cova da Iria | Later diary account; official Shrine chronology | Received biographical episode | Outside the six 1917 visions named by the 1930 operative clause |
| First-Saturday request and related reported encounters | 10 Dec. 1925 and 15 Feb. 1926, Pontevedra; clarification in later correspondence | Lúcia's 1927 letter and later correspondence; Shrine presentation; broad ecclesial devotional reception | Wider Fátima spiritual family | Not retroactively included in the 1930 event judgment; devotional promise is not automatic salvation or freedom from repentance |
| Reported Trinitarian vision and request concerning Russia | 13 June 1929, Tuy | Lúcia's account to her confessor; Shrine presentation; later papal reception | Wider Fátima spiritual family with strong ecclesial reception | Not retroactively included in the 1930 event judgment; papal acts retain their own objects and wording |
| First and second parts of the secret | Reported 13 July 1917; written 31 Aug. 1941, addition 8 Dec. 1941 | Third and Fourth Memoirs; published in CDF, *The Message of Fatima* (2000) | Authorized, ecclesially received text interpreted under the CDF commentary | Later written witness, not a 1917 stenographic transcript; private revelation remains subordinate to the Gospel |
| Third part of the secret | Reported 13 July 1917; written 3 Jan. 1944 at Tuy | Autograph kept by Leiria, transferred to Holy Office 1957, completely published by papal decision in 2000 | Complete official publication with authoritative theological interpretation | Symbolic vision, not a photographic or fatalistic timetable; no “fourth secret” is admitted by the controlling public dossier |
| John Paul II application to 13 May 1981 assassination attempt | Rome, 1981; interpreted publicly 1982–2000 | John Paul II, Cardinal Sodano, Sister Lúcia interview, CDF 2000 | Privileged, authoritative reception within a wider twentieth-century martyrdom reading | Application is not proof that every image maps only to this event or that the assailant lacked freedom |
| 25 March 1984 act of entrustment / consecration | Rome in spiritual union with bishops | John Paul II act; Lúcia's 8 Nov. 1989 confirmation published by CDF 2000 | CDF states it corresponded to the request; further claim that it remained undone has no basis in that dossier | Later papal acts do not imply that the 1984 act failed |
| Holy See publication and interpretation | 26 June 2000 | Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, by decision of John Paul II | Controlling public documentary and theological interpretation | The commentary is authoritative reception, not a new solemn dogmatic definition |
| Current procedural law | Effective 19 May 2024 | DDF 2024 norms | Governs new discernments; legacy Fátima act retains its historical formula | Do not recast 1930 “worthy of belief” as a 2024 `Nihil obstat` or as a modern `constat` |
