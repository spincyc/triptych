\section{References}

\subsection{Received prayer, liturgical use, and present discipline}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Dom Gaspar Lefebvre, O.S.B., \textit{Daily Missal with Vespers for Sundays and Feasts} (Lophem near Bruges: St.~Andrew's Abbey; St.~Paul, Minnesota: E.~M. Lohmann, 1925), printed pp.~13--14, \sourceurl{https://archive.ccwatershed.org/media/pdfs/21/02/16/05-35-23_0.pdf}{edition scan}. Governing Latin and historical-English prayer witness; its seasonal, posture, and indulgence directions are historical only.
  \item Apostolic Penitentiary, \textit{Enchiridion Indulgentiarum: Normae et Concessiones}, fourth edition (16 July 1999), norms 4, 17, and 22; concession 17 \S2, 2\textsuperscript{o}; and the printed Latin prayer: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/tribunals/apost_penit/documents/rc_trib_appen_doc_20020826_enchiridion-indulgentiarum_lt.html}{Holy See Latin text}. Governs the current universal indulgence claim as checked through 21 July 2026 and identifies the antiphon's Easter-Time Liturgy of the Hours source.
  \item Sacred Congregation for Divine Worship, \textit{General Instruction of the Liturgy of the Hours}, second typical-edition context, no.~92, \sourceurl{https://www.liturgyoffice.org/Resources/Rites/GILH.pdf}{English text published by the Liturgy Office of the Bishops' Conference of England and Wales}. Governs the Compline antiphon claim.
  \item Paul VI, apostolic constitution \textit{Indulgentiarum doctrina} (1 January 1967), norms and concluding dispositions: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/apost_constitutions/documents/hf_p-vi_apc_01011967_indulgentiarum-doctrina.html}{Holy See text}. Governs the break with older quantified partial-indulgence discipline.
\end{itemize}

  \item Benedict XVI, Regina C{\ae}li address for Easter Monday (24 March 2008), \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/angelus/2008/documents/hf_ben-xvi_reg_20080324_easter-monday.html}{Holy See text}, on the prayer as a new Easter announcement of joy to Mary.
  \item United States Conference of Catholic Bishops, \sourceurl{https://www.usccb.org/prayer-worship/liturgical-year/easter}{``Easter,''} presenting the Universal Norms' current Easter-Time boundary. Used for the current calendar statement, not as the reproduced prayer witness.
