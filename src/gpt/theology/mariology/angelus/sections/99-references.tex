\subsection{Received prayer and present discipline}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Dom Gaspar Lefebvre, O.S.B., \textit{Daily Missal with Vespers for Sundays and Feasts} (Lophem near Bruges: St.~Andrew's Abbey; St.~Paul, Minnesota: E.~M.   \item Paul VI, apostolic constitution \textit{Indulgentiarum doctrina} (1 January 1967), norms and concluding dispositions: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/apost_constitutions/documents/hf_p-vi_apc_01011967_indulgentiarum-doctrina.html}{Holy See text}. Governs the break with older quantified partial-indulgence discipline.
\end{itemize}
