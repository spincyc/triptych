# Source Audit — The Angelus

Online and mutable sources were checked through 21 July 2026. Exact vocal text
is controlled separately by `research/prayer-text-audit.md`. Official web pages
are used for doctrine, status, and present discipline; their modern English is
not silently substituted for the selected 1925 prayer witness.

## Scripture, doctrine, and devotional ordering

| Claim | Source and exact locus | Authority, use, and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gabriel announces; Mary conceives by the Holy Spirit; Mary freely answers as the Lord's handmaid; and Elizabeth's blessing contributes to the Hail Mary. | Luke 1:26–45, especially 1:28, 31–35, 38, and 42; Latin checked in the [*Nova Vulgata*, Luke 1](https://www.vatican.va/archive/bible/nova_vulgata/documents/nova-vulgata_nt_evang-lucam_lt.html#1). | Public Revelation controls the exchanges and part of the repeated prayer. The Angelus condenses identified verses; it is not a new revelation or a verbatim transcript of the entire scene. |
| The eternal Word truly became flesh and dwelt among us. | John 1:1–18, especially 1:14; Latin checked in the [*Nova Vulgata*, John 1](https://www.vatican.va/archive/bible/nova_vulgata/documents/nova-vulgata_nt_evang-ioannem_lt.html#1); CCC 456–483. | Christological center of the third exchange. “Flesh” means real humanity without ceasing to be God. |
| Mary's consent is real, free, and grace-enabled creaturely cooperation within God's initiative. | Second Vatican Council, *Lumen gentium* 53 and 56; CCC 484–511. [Holy See conciliar text](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html). | Governs the interpretation of “Behold the handmaid.” It excludes both autonomous salvific causality and a denial of Mary's personal consent. |

## Text comparison, rights, and excluded sources

- The 2005 *Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church*, Appendix A,
  was checked as an official modern comparison witness for the Angelus, not as
  the publication's English prayer source. Its English and its appended Glory
  Be are not imported into the 1925 text. [Official Compendium](https://press.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html).
- The current *Enchiridion* prints a current Latin form whose orthography,
  punctuation, and conclusion are not identical in every detail to Lefebvre.
  It controls the current indulgence claim but does not overwrite the historical
  prayer witness.
- No current proprietary vernacular Missal, Liturgy of the Hours, Bible, or
  devotional translation is reproduced. Official current sources are cited and
  closely paraphrased only.
- The 1925 prayer wording is public domain in the United States by publication
  date and remains excluded from the project's CC BY 4.0 grant. The edition
  says “All rights reserved,” and the audit did not establish who authored or
  translated the exact historical English. Lefebvre's 1966 death therefore
  does not itself clear the translation abroad; non-US rights remain unresolved.
- The scan is cited rather than vendored. The source record retains its exact
  checksum so another reviewer can identify the checked file.

## Consequential negative results and unresolved review

- No checked evidence establishes one inventor or one moment at which the
  entire modern Angelus, its three daily times, bells, posture, and indulgence
  discipline arose together.
- No directly inspected critical edition of every medieval or early-modern
  witness was assembled. Secondary historical reports remain labeled as such.
- The current grant does not print a requirement to hear a bell, kneel or stand,
  pray in a church, pray communally, add the Glory Be, or complete all three
  daily recitations.
- No current proprietary English prayer translation was cleared for
  reproduction, so none is printed.
- Independent historical, liturgical, Latin, Mariological, theological, legal,
  pastoral, rights, and ecclesiastical review remains outstanding.
- Final production completed on 21 July 2026: 8 pages; clean two-pass log and
  PDF/font/text/geometry checks; every final page visually inspected; terminal
  rights colophon verified; and installed/build copies byte-identical at
