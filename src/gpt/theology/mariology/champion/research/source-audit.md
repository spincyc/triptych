## Translation, OCR, and rights

- The 2010 and 2023 decrees are English originals in signed public PDFs. Text extraction and OCR were checked against page images for consequential wording.
- Starr's 1883 scan reproduces the 1871 first-series passage. Searchable OCR was used for discovery; printed pp. 298–301 were visually inspected. The work is public domain.
- Pernin's 1874 English edition is a period translation issued with the French original. Printed pp. 94–102 were visually inspected through Canadiana; OCR errors were not copied. The work is public domain. English wording is cited as that edition's wording, not as a new project translation from French.
- The 2023 Shrine history and other modern shrine materials retain their own rights. Their complete prose is not reproduced.
- Official texts remain excluded from the project's CC BY grant. Quotations are focused and necessary to identify exact legal or source claims.

## Rejected or unresolved leads

Rejected as proof: anonymous apparition lists; devotional sites that call every favor a certified miracle; retellings that attribute the later full dialogue or full refugee-and-rain fire scene to Pernin; claims that all three events occurred on 9 October without qualification; descriptions of the grounds as wholly untouched; claims that the 1859 warning predicted the 1871 fire; claims that the 2023 title change or solemnity was a Vatican reapproval of supernatural origin; an exact national-shrine approval date chosen from conflicting public summaries; the old claim that Pauline contact was chronologically impossible; and claims that opening Adele's cause canonized her or authenticated every narrative detail.

Unresolved and therefore not used as established fact: an exact French original of the 1859 dialogue; the full oral chain behind each sentence in the 1955 history, including whether and what Pauline LaPlante learned directly from Adele; exact dates for the first and second appearances; the identity intended by the anonymized priest in Starr; the Shrine parcel's exact Indigenous-to-settler title chain; the underlying national-shrine approval act and effective date; a scientific mechanism for the site's survival; exact fire casualty and acreage totals; and any healing not covered by a separate competent act.
