An official Sanctuary page is a custodial and pastoral witness; it is not automatically a critical edition, medical journal, or replacement for the competent bishop's act.

## Secondary scholarship, unverified leads, and rejected claims

The following were not used as controlling proof: anonymous apparition chronologies; miracle lists without the competent bishop's act; commercial “Lourdes water” claims; assertions that the water has unique chemistry; claims that the spring was scientifically created from nothing; photographs or art showing a blooming rosebush; wording that has the Lady audibly recite the Hail Mary; alleged publication of Bernadette's private secrets; the candle episode counted as one of the recognized cure miracles; arguments that canonization makes every apparition detail infallible; and claims that failure to be cured proves deficient faith.

René Laurentin's documentary and historical work, Ruth Harris's cultural history, and later medical scholarship are high-value sources for future independent review, but no claim in this revision is attributed to a book that was not directly consulted. The official Sanctuary's own changing summaries require an eventual archival comparison with the complete Laurentin/Cros dossier and the original Tarbes files. That comparison remains outstanding rather than being invented.
