## Doctrinal reading

Water is a biblical sign of creation, life, purification, thirst, Baptism, the Spirit, and the pierced side of Christ (Gen 1:2; Ex 17:1–7; Ezek 47; Jn 4:1–15; 7:37–39; 19:34; Rom 6:3–4). These resonances are theological synthesis. They are not claims that the Lourdes spring is baptismal matter already consecrated, a new sacrament, or the literal referent of every cited verse.

The 25 February gestures are penitential and bodily. Mud becoming clear water can image conversion, but the analogy is editorial. The sick person's body is not “mud” and disability is not sin. Healing in the Gospel includes restoration to community and encounter with Christ; it never licenses blaming a person who is not cured.

## Safety rules

1. Use water only from current authorized outlets and follow Sanctuary notices.
2. Do not drink water known or suspected to be contaminated, share vessels unsafely, or treat devotional water as sterile.
3. Do not discontinue medication, nutrition, rehabilitation, infection control, or emergency treatment because of pilgrimage or prayer.
4. Do not pressure a sick or disabled person to enter baths, perform a water gesture, make a public cure declaration, or disclose medical information.
5. A reported change in health should be assessed by the treating team. A possible Lourdes file requires records from before and after the change.
6. The absence of cure is not evidence of weak faith, secret sin, Marian rejection, or failure to perform the gesture correctly.
