# Lourdes Eighteen-Apparition Chronology Audit

Checked 2026-07-16 against the Sanctuary's detailed apparition page, compressed 2019 official calendar, first-autograph page, and 1862 judgment pages. This is a source-control record, not a substitute event decree or a transcript of heavenly speech.

## Discrepancy ledger

1. **20/23 February prayer:** the detailed page places a personal prayer on 20 February and a secret on 23 February; the compressed calendar calls 19–21 wordless and suggests a secret prayer on 23 February.
2. **Three secrets:** the compressed calendar places three secrets on 1 March; the detailed page does not make that assignment in its 1 March entry. Nothing in the public message depends on their content.
3. **“Fortnight”:** the invitation does not equal fifteen consecutive visions. The authoritative running count is eighteen across the dates above, with express gaps.
4. **Identification:** Bernadette initially spoke of `Aquerò`; the Marian identification and 25 March title belong to the developing sequence. Later headings calling every event a Marian apparition are received retrospective identification, not Bernadette's first label.
5. **Rosary speech:** devotional language sometimes says the Lady prayed the Rosary “with” Bernadette. The first autograph's concrete claim is that Bernadette recited it while the Lady handled beads without moving her lips.
6. **Rosebush:** Peyramale's demanded bloom and the reported title are distinct. The official page expressly denies flowering.
7. **Crowd epistemology:** crowds saw Bernadette, her behavior, and the site; the published corpus does not say they all saw the figure. Crowd growth corroborates publicity, not supernatural origin.

## Source maturity

The Sanctuary pages are sufficient for a bounded reader-facing chronology and for identifying internal custodial differences. They are not a full scholarly apparatus. A future revision should collate each entry against the surviving 1858 civil and ecclesiastical depositions and the complete critical documentary edition, recording exact manuscript dates and translation history.
