# Lourdes: Apparitions, Immaculate Conception, and the Ministry of Healing — Research Scope

## Identity and governing question

This record supports `src/gpt/theology/mariology/lourdes/main.tex`.

- **Provider and collection:** GPT; theology; mariology.
- **Genre and profile:** single-apparition historical, theological, and status-conscious reference monograph. `guidance/theology/mariology.md` governs, especially its apparition-judgment, authority, and currentness rules; `guidance/editorial.md` also controls. This is not a medical paper, canon-law opinion, liturgical proper, or general apparition registry.
- **Rite and jurisdiction:** Latin Church; Diocese of Tarbes in 1858–1862, now the Diocese of Tarbes and Lourdes. The controlling event act is Bishop Bertrand-Sévère Laurence's *Mandement portant jugement sur l'Apparition qui a eu lieu à la Grotte de Lourdes*, 18 January 1862.
- **Question:** What is historically and ecclesially warranted about the eighteen reported apparitions to Bernadette Soubirous; what exactly did Bishop Laurence judge; how should the reported message, the title “I am the Immaculate Conception,” the spring, and the candle episode be understood; and how does the distinct medical and diocesan process for reported cures operate today?
- **Reader:** a serious Catholic reader seeking documentary candor, doctrinal depth, medical restraint, and a spirituality that honors sick and disabled persons whether or not bodily cure occurs.
- **As-of date:** 2026-07-16. The DDF norms and document index, the current Sanctuary and diocesan presentation, the 71st and 72nd cure proclamations, and the Medical Bureau's 2026 change of permanent physician were checked through this date.
- **Primary thesis:** Lourdes is a positively judged legacy private revelation whose durable center is evangelical and ecclesial: Trinitarian prayer, conversion, penance, intercession for sinners, sacramental life, procession, and care for the sick under the sign of Mary's wholly Christ-derived holiness. Its 1862 judgment does not make the reports public Revelation or every harmonized detail a transcript. The spring is ordinary water; medical inability to explain a cure is not itself a miracle decree; and the dignity of a person is never measured by whether bodily healing occurs.

## Included corpus and authority strata

1. **The bounded event corpus:** eighteen reported encounters at Massabielle on 11, 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25, 27, and 28 February; 1, 2, 3, 4, and 25 March; 7 April; and 16 July 1858. The work explicitly records that the “fortnight” was not fifteen consecutive apparitions and that no apparition is counted on 22 or 26 February.
2. **Bernadette's witness:** her oral statements to family, clergy, police, magistrates, and other questioners in 1858; the first autograph account now presented by the Sanctuary; and later recollection. The full archival depositions have not been independently collated in this project, so the official Sanctuary's source-bearing presentations are distinguished from a critical edition.
3. **Received official harmonizations:** the Sanctuary's detailed eighteen-event page and its compressed 2019 official calendar. They establish current custodial presentation. Where they assign the private prayer and secrets differently, this study reports the difference instead of silently combining them.
4. **The event judgment:** the exact French operative sentence transcribed by the Sanctuary from the 18 January 1862 *Mandement*, checked against its official English presentation. The publication supplies a close English paraphrase and preserves the historical grammar. The open official pages do not provide a complete facsimile of every page of the act; no claim is made that this project performed an archival collation of the entire original.
5. **Doctrinal sources:** Pius IX's *Ineffabilis Deus* (8 December 1854); Vatican II, *Dei Verbum* and *Lumen gentium*; the *Catechism of the Catholic Church*; the DDF's 2024 phenomena norms and 2025 *Mater Populi Fidelis*; and papal Lourdes teaching. These sources govern doctrine and authority. The reported 1858 title may be received as a sign after the definition; it is not the formal proof or source of the dogma.
6. **The spring and site:** current Sanctuary statements that the water is ordinary, neither consecrated nor intrinsically miraculous; the geological description of Massabielle as a limestone cavity with a karstic extension; and the history of channeling the spring. These support a natural-hydrological baseline, not a reconstruction of the precise pre-25-February flow path.
7. **Cure discernment:** official accounts of the Medical Bureau, the International Medical Committee of Lourdes (CMIL), and the current three-stage process; the competent diocesan proclamations for John/Jack Traynor and Antonia Raco; and the appointment of Dr Giada Monami in April 2026. Medical and ecclesiastical objects remain distinct.
8. **Ecclesial reception:** Bernadette's beatification and canonization, the growth of the sanctuary, papal teaching and pilgrimage, the liturgical memorial of Our Lady of Lourdes, and the World Day of the Sick. These prove reception within their objects, not a new or enlarged apparition decree.

## Evidentiary vocabulary

- **“Reported”** marks claims transmitted through Bernadette or later custodial narrative.
- **“The 1862 judgment”** refers only to Bishop Laurence's historical act and its stated object. It is not retrofitted into a 2024 DDF category.
- **“Medically unexplained”** means unexplained in the present state of medical knowledge after the relevant review. It does not mean “science proves divine causation.”
- **“Recognized miracle”** denotes a competent bishop's ecclesial proclamation after medical and theological-canonical review. The historical procedures behind the running total were not identical in every era.
- **“Ordinary water”** means that the water itself has no scientifically established special curative property and is not a sacramental merely by origin. It does not prejudge whether God may answer prayer in connection with its use.

## Material discrepancies and safeguards

### Private prayer and secrets

The Sanctuary's detailed chronology assigns a personal prayer to 20 February and a secret “for her alone” to 23 February. Its compressed 2019 calendar calls 19–21 February wordless, assigns an apparent secret prayer to 23 February, and places three secrets on 1 March. The publication does not synthesize these into a newly reconstructed script. It states only that private prayer/secrets belong to the received narrative and were not given as a public message. No internet “Lourdes secret” is admitted.

### The Rosary

Bernadette's first autograph says that she recited the Rosary while the Lady passed beads through her fingers “without moving her lips.” The publication therefore does not say that the figure audibly recited the Hail Mary or dictate a new Rosary formula. Later devotional descriptions such as “praying the Rosary with Mary” are interpreted as shared contemplative presence, not a contradictory audio transcript.

### The rosebush

Father Peyramale reportedly asked for the lady's name and the winter flowering of the rosebush. The Sanctuary explicitly says the rosebush did **not** bloom when the title was reported on 25 March. The publication rejects the familiar embroidered image of a miraculous flowering.

### The candle episode

The Sanctuary calls 7 April the “miracle of the candle” and reports Dr Pierre Romain Dozous's observation that flame surrounded Bernadette's hand without burning it. No separately located diocesan decree was found that adds this physical episode to the canonical running list of Lourdes cure miracles. The work reports the custodial narrative, not an independently replicated thermal experiment or a 73rd cure.

### Water and spring

The Sanctuary describes the water as ordinary and the cave as karstic. The event narrative says Bernadette scraped in muddy ground until water could be drunk. Neither source scientifically demonstrates that water was created *ex nihilo* on 25 February. “Discovery,” “uncovering,” or “bringing the spring to light” is preferred to an unsupported claim that no subsurface flow previously existed.

### Immaculate Conception

The dialect formula `Que soy era immaculada councepciou` is preserved as reported. It followed Pius IX's dogmatic definition by less than four years. Bernadette's limited theological vocabulary is historically relevant, but it is not treated as a laboratory exclusion of every natural channel by which she could have encountered related words. The dogma's authority rests on the Church's solemn definition grounded in public Revelation, not on the apparition.

### Canonization

Bernadette was canonized for holiness, not because canonization is a second event decree or makes every autobiographical recollection inerrant. The DDF's 2024 norms expressly distinguish a holiness cause from judgment of extraordinary phenomena as a general principle.

### Cure count and stale official pages

The current count is 72 through 2026-07-16. A general Sanctuary page still displaying 70 and naming the former permanent physician is retained in the audit as stale. Later specific official sources control the update: John/Jack Traynor became number 71 by Archbishop Malcolm McMahon's proclamation of 8 December 2024, and Antonia Raco became number 72 by Bishop Vincenzo Carmine Orofino's proclamation of 16 April 2025. The current English Sanctuary page is internally inconsistent: its heading and predominant prose use `Antonia`, while one sentence and the URL slug use `Antonietta`. The competent diocesan act uses `Antonia`, which controls the publication's reader-facing form.

## Exclusions

- No full critical edition of every 1858 deposition, police note, medical report, or later recollection is reproduced or claimed to have been collated.
- No private secret is disclosed, reconstructed, or filled from later internet material.
- No claim is made that the 1862 event act is an infallible definition, that Catholic faith is owed to Lourdes, or that the old formula is a post-2024 `Nihil obstat`.
- No mechanism is certified for the candle episode, no rosebush bloom is alleged, and no audible Marian recitation of the Rosary is invented.
- No water sample is called supernatural, curative in itself, newly created from nothing, or a substitute for safe drinking-water, hygiene, medication, diagnosis, vaccination, emergency care, or public-health instructions.
- No medical file is re-diagnosed by the project. The 72 cases are not presented as a uniform clinical dataset, a treatment success rate, or proof that unrecognized cases are fraudulent.
- No sick or disabled person is told that lack of cure reflects deficient faith. Healing language may include bodily cure, spiritual strength, reconciliation, accompaniment, and hope, but those categories are not merged.
- No independent approval by the DDF, Diocese of Tarbes and Lourdes, Medical Bureau, CMIL, physician, historian, canonist, or theologian is claimed.
