\subsection{The 1862 judgment, witness, and event chronology}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes, “La reconnaissance des Apparitions de Lourdes,” presenting an “identical transcription” of the archival \emph{Mandement} and its exact operative French formula: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.com/reconnaissance-apparitions-lourdes/}{official French transcription and historical presentation}. The complete archive original was not independently collated for this study.
  \item Sanctuary, “The Decree,” with English rendering of the operative sentence and summary of Bishop Laurence's commission of 28 July 1858: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/the-decree/}{official English presentation}.
  \item Sanctuary, “The apparitions,” the detailed dated presentation used for the eighteen-event sequence, public message, explicit gaps, non-flowering rosebush, candle episode, and final encounter: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/apparitions/}{official chronology}.
  \item Sanctuary, “The Bernadette year has begun” (2019), the compressed calendar of the eighteen apparitions used to identify its different placement of wordless encounters, private prayer, and three secrets: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/the-bernadette-year-has-begun/}{official compressed calendar}.
  \item Sanctuary, “The account of the First Apparition,” an official translation of an extract identified as Bernadette's first autograph, addressed to Father Gondrand: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.com/en/the-account-of-the-first-apparition/}{primary witness in official presentation}. It postdates earlier oral questioning and says the figure passed the beads without moving her lips.
  \item Sanctuary, “Bernadette Soubirous,” used for her birth and baptism, family poverty, return from Bartrès, First Communion, Nevers vocation, death, beatification, and canonization: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/bernadette-soubirous/}{official biographical chronology}.
  \item Sanctuary, “25th March, the Lady says her name,” for the received Gascon formula and Bernadette's repetition of unfamiliar words: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/25th-march-the-lady-says-her-name-i-am-the-immaculate-conception/}{official event presentation}.
  \item Sanctuary, “Friday 16th July 1858,” for the barred grotto, Bernadette's position across the Gave, and the bounded final encounter: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/expliquez-moi-les-apparitions/}{official event presentation}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Grotto, water, medical process, and cure proclamations}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Sanctuary, “The Grotto of the Apparitions at Massabielle,” for the limestone cavity, roughly ten-metre karstic extension, diocesan acquisition, 1864 statue and procession, and 1974 spring channeling: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/the-grotto-of-the-apparitions-at-massabielle/}{official site history}.
  \item Sanctuary, “The new pilgrim's way,” especially the explicit safeguard that Lourdes water is ordinary like neighboring springs, neither consecrated nor miraculous in itself: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/new-pilgrims-way/}{official pastoral guidance}.
  \item Sanctuary, current schedule and baths/water-gesture pages, used only for the proposition that operational arrangements and safety directions are mutable: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/schedule/}{schedule}; \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/going-baths/}{baths and water gesture}.
  \item Sanctuary, “Le Bureau des Constatations médicales,” for the separate 1862 decree declaring seven cures miraculous, Professor Vergez, Dr Dunot de Saint-Maclou, 1883 establishment, 1886 papal assent, 1905 confirmation, and 1947--1954 committee development: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/bureau-constatations-medicales/}{official institutional history}. Its personnel line was stale on the as-of date and was not used for the current officeholder.
  \item Sanctuary, “Recognition of a miracle,” for the public description of declared, unexpected, confirmed, and medically unexplained cures; summarized Lambertini criteria; CMIL review; transmission; and the competent diocesan bishop's separate act: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/recognition-miracle/}{official process summary}.
  \item Sanctuary, “The miracles of Lourdes,” retained to document an overview page still displaying 70 on 16 July 2026: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/en/the-miracles-of-lourdes/}{official but stale overview}. Later case-specific acts control the current total.
  \item Sanctuary, “John Traynor: 71st miracle at Lourdes”: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.com/en/john-traynor-71st-miracle-at-lourdes/}{official case presentation}; Archbishop Malcolm McMahon, OP, statement of 8 December 2024: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Statement-on-John-Traynor-Case-FINAL.pdf}{signed ecclesial statement}; Archdiocese of Liverpool, “Archdiocese celebrates miracle of Jack Traynor”: \sourceurl{https://www.liverpoolcatholic.org.uk/news/archdiocese-celebrates-miracle-of-jack-traynor}{competent diocesan notice}.
  \item Sanctuary, “Antonia Raco: 72nd Lourdes miracle,” whose English page internally varies the given name while supplying the detailed Bureau and CMIL chronology: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.com/en/antonietta-raco-72nd-lourdes-miracle/}{official case presentation}; Diocese of Tursi-Lagonegro, Bishop Vincenzo Carmine Orofino's 16 April 2025 proclamation concerning Antonia Raco: \sourceurl{https://www.diocesitursi.it/dichiarazione-del-carattere-miracoloso-della-guarigione-di-antonia-raco/}{competent diocesan act}; Vatican News: \sourceurl{https://www.vaticannews.va/fr/eglise/news/2025-04/lourdes-miraculee-italienne-pelerinage-eglise-maladie-guerisson.html}{official corroboration}.
  \item Sanctuary, “Appointment of Doctor Giada Monami in Lourdes” (17 February 2026), effective in mid-April, establishing the current permanent physician and first woman appointed to the post: \sourceurl{https://www.lourdes-france.com/en/nomination-du-docteur-giada-monami-a-lourdes/}{official appointment notice}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Papal and pastoral reception}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Paul VI, apostolic exhortation \emph{Marialis cultus} (2 February 1974), 8, identifying the 11 February commemoration of the Blessed Virgin Mary of Lourdes in the reformed Roman Calendar: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/apost_exhortations/documents/hf_p-vi_exh_19740202_marialis-cultus.html}{official text}; United States Conference of Catholic Bishops, “Optional Memorial of Our Lady of Lourdes,” for the current U.S. rank and Lectionary presentation: \sourceurl{https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/0211-memorial-our-lady-lourdes.cfm}{official U.S. liturgical presentation}. Local calendars and precedence still control actual observance.
  \item Pius XII, encyclical \emph{Fulgens Corona} (8 September 1953), especially 3, receiving Lourdes as a special sign after the 1854 definition: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-xii_enc_08091953_fulgens-corona.html}{official text}.
  \item Pius XII, encyclical \emph{Le Pèlerinage de Lourdes} (2 July 1957), especially 43--59, on conversion, Christian renewal, justice, care for the sick, and suffering united with Christ: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-xii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_p-xii_enc_02071957_le-pelerinage-de-lourdes.html}{official text}.
  \item John Paul II, official itinerary and texts for the 14--15 August 1983 apostolic pilgrimage to Lourdes: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/travels/1983/travels/documents/trav_lourdes.html}{Holy See pilgrimage index}.
  \item John Paul II, letter to Cardinal Fiorenzo Angelini instituting the World Day of the Sick (13 May 1992), 1--2: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/it/letters/1992/documents/hf_jp-ii_let_13051992_world-day-sick.html}{official Italian text}.   \item John Paul II, Lourdes homily for the Assumption (15 August 2004), receiving the shrine through Christ, Mary, illness, women, peace, and hope: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/homilies/2004/documents/hf_jp-ii_hom_20040815_lourdes.html}{official text}.
  \item Benedict XVI, Lourdes torchlight procession meditation (13 September 2008): \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/homilies/2008/documents/hf_ben-xvi_hom_20080913_lourdes-processione.html}{official text}; Mass homily for the apparition anniversary (14 September 2008): \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/homilies/2008/documents/hf_ben-xvi_hom_20080914_lourdes-apparizioni.html}{official text}.
  \item Benedict XVI, homage to the Immaculate (8 December 2008), receiving Lourdes as a “heavenly seal” after the dogmatic definition while keeping Christ's grace primary: \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/speeches/2008/december/documents/hf_ben-xvi_spe_20081208_immacolata.html}{official text}.
\end{enumerate}
