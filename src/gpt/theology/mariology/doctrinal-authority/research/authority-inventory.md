# Marian Authority Inventory

| Claim | Class | Governing object / mode | Required response or liberty | Exclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Divine motherhood | Dogma | Conciliar Christological judgment received universally | Divine and Catholic faith | Mary does not cause the divine nature. |
| Perpetual virginity | Dogma | Ancient universal Tradition and ordinary universal Magisterium, repeatedly articulated | Divine and Catholic faith | No contempt for marriage or speculative anatomy. |
| Immaculate Conception | Dogma | *Ineffabilis Deus* defining revealed first-instant preservation | Divine and Catholic faith | Mary remains redeemed by Christ. |
| Bodily Assumption | Dogma | *Munificentissimus Deus* §44 | Divine and Catholic faith | Mary's death remains undefined. |
| Lifelong personal holiness | Authoritative doctrine | Trent VI.23; LG 56; CCC 493 | Assent to authoritative teaching | Not identical to the 1854 object. |
| Spiritual motherhood | Conciliar/papal doctrine | LG 61–63; CCC 963–970 | Religious submission according to exact proposal | No independent production of grace. |
| Queenship | Papal/liturgical doctrine and title | *Ad caeli Reginam*; LG 59 | Reverent assent and liturgical reception | No equal sovereignty or defined visible ceremony. |
| New Eve | Patristic typology | Justin/Irenaeus and later reception | Strong theological and traditional weight | Christ alone is the saving New Adam. |
| Dormition/death | Weighty tradition and opinion | Eastern liturgy and broad theological reception | Legitimate adherence; not obligatory as the 1950 object | Neither mortality nor non-mortality may be called defined. |
| Co-redemptrix | Historical title judged unsuitable | DDF *Mater Populi Fidelis* 22 | Do not recommend for constructive use | Historical uses remain historical facts. |
| Mediatrix / Advocate | Traditional title under strict qualification | LG 62; DDF *Mater Populi Fidelis* | Receive only within Christ's unique mediation | No independent efficient causality of grace. |
| Reported apparition | Private-revelation claim under administrative judgment | Competent diocesan/DDF act and 2024 norms | No divine faith; respect operative discipline | Judgment does not migrate to every message or accretion. |
