# Dogma, Doctrine, and Devotion in Mariology — Scope

This record supports `src/gpt/theology/mariology/doctrinal-authority/main.tex`.

- **Provider / genre:** GPT; Marian doctrinal reference governed by `guidance/theology/mariology.md`.
- **Question:** How should the Catholic categories of dogma, authoritative doctrine, liturgical and patristic reception, title, devotion, opinion, discipline, and private revelation govern claims about Mary?
- **Reader:** serious Catholic readers, catechists, clergy, students, and ecumenical readers.
- **As-of date:** 2026-07-26 for mutable DDF judgments and norms.
- **Relation to other works:** the universal framework belongs to `articles/faith/catholic-doctrinal-authority`; the full dogma histories belong to `theology/mariology/marian-dogmas`. This leaf applies and cross-checks rather than reproducing them.

The included corpus is exactly the four commonly identified Marian dogmas, selected related doctrines and titles, patristic and liturgical reception, devotion, and private revelation. Apparition-by-apparition status, complete prayer texts, indulgence law, and a full title lexicon are excluded.

Natural goods mean rational clarity, testimony, memory, fair disagreement, and protection of conscience. Supernatural ends mean fidelity to Revelation, Christological confession, holiness, devotion, and communion. They are not two sources of Marian dogma.

The work preserves Christ's unique mediation and the completeness of public Revelation. Marian cooperation is created, graced, maternal, subordinate, and participated. It neither recommends “Co-redemptrix” nor makes “Mediatrix” an independent causal office.

Independent review in Mariology, dogmatics, patristics, ecumenism, history, and canon law remains outstanding. No ecclesiastical approval is claimed.
