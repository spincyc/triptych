# The Four Marian Dogmas in the Ark's Light — Definition and Typology Inventory

This record fixes the exact dogmatic objects used in
`sections/80-four-dogmas.tex` and separately classifies their relations to Ark
typology. It is current to 2026-08-15. It reuses the controlled dogmatic corpus

## Two independent axes

### Dogmatic authority

1. **Ecumenical-conciliar Christological dogma:** divine motherhood as received
   at Ephesus and clarified at Chalcedon.
2. **Universal Tradition and ordinary universal Magisterium, repeatedly
   authoritatively proposed:** perpetual virginity, with ancient conciliar and
   papal-synodal witnesses.
3. **Ex cathedra papal definition of revealed doctrine:** the Immaculate
   Conception and the bodily Assumption.

All four require divine and Catholic faith. Their different historical modes
of proposal do not rank their truth or the assent owed.

### Ark-relation status

1. **Constitutive Christological identity:** the reality because of which Mary
   is the living Ark—she bears the incarnate Lord.
2. **Direct magisterial reception:** an official papal, catechetical, or
   liturgical source expressly applies Ark language.
3. **Received patristic-liturgical typology:** a durable ecclesial image with a
   defined doctrinal horizon but not itself a definition.
4. **Theological fittingness or project synthesis:** a disciplined application
   built from received sources and explicitly labeled as such.
5. **Excluded inference:** a claim the evidence does not support.

`Strength` below ranks only the Ark application. It never ranks the dogma or
the assent owed.

theological horizon.

## Exact-source notes

### Divine motherhood

- Ephesus receives Cyril's teaching that Mary is Mother of God not because the
  Word's divinity originated from her but because the Word personally united
  flesh from her and was born according to the flesh.
- Chalcedon names one and the same Son, born eternally from the Father according
  to divinity and from Mary *Theotokos* according to humanity, in two natures
  without confusion or division.
- Ark typology is at its strongest here because the contents determine the
  identity: Mary is living Ark because she bears the divine Son incarnate.

### Perpetual virginity

- `Before` and virginal conception are directly attested in Matthew 1 and Luke
  1; `in` and `after` belong to the Church's full received confession.
- Lateran 649 gives the explicit threefold formula but must not be mislabeled an
  ecumenical council.
- The closed eastern gate (Ezekiel 44:1–3) has a literal prophetic setting in
  the restored sanctuary. Ambrose and Jerome receive it as Marian typology.
- True marriage is independently governed by Augustine, Aquinas ST III q.29
  a.2, and *Redemptoris Custos* 7, 18–21. Ark imagery cannot overturn them.

### Immaculate Conception

The definition contains five inseparable clauses:

1. first instant of Mary's conception;
2. singular grace and privilege of Almighty God;
3. in view of Jesus Christ's merits;
4. preservation from every stain of original sin;
5. revealed doctrine requiring firm belief.

The Ark application presents this preservation as Christ's prevenient
preparation of his mother. It is fittingness received in papal teaching, not a
claim that Exodus already states the 1854 formula. `Incorruptible wood` is the
Septuagintal expression for the Ark's acacia construction; Pius XII applies it
specifically in an Assumption survey, not as an Immaculate-Conception proof.

### Bodily Assumption

- The definition's subject is identified through the other dogmas: Immaculate
  Mother of God and ever-Virgin.
- `Having completed her earthly course` leaves the fact of death open. Dormition
  remains a weighty ancient tradition, not a clause added to the definition.
- `Was assumed` confesses God's action upon a redeemed creature and differs from
  Christ's Ascension by his own power.
- `Body and soul` excludes a merely spiritual survival or honor paid to an
  absent body.
- Psalm 132:8 and the living Ark's entry into God's rest form a powerful
  received eschatological typology without becoming a canonical narrative of
  the Assumption.

## Fourfold synthesis

**Status: source-grounded project synthesis.**

The old Ark bore the signs of covenant presence; Mary bore the Covenant in
person. Divine motherhood tells us whom she bore. Perpetual virginity names the
Spirit-given form of her maternal vocation. The Immaculate Conception names
Christ's prevenient, preservative preparation of his mother. The Assumption
names the bodily glory to which his Paschal victory brought her. The dogmas
disclose the fullness of one Ark-shaped vocation; the typology gathers and
illuminates them but does not replace their proper grounds.

The Trinitarian and Christological order is controlling: the Father elects and
sends; the Son assumes, redeems, and glorifies; the Spirit overshadows,
sanctifies, and conforms. Mary neither initiates a fourth divine mission nor
supplies anything lacking in Christ. She is created, graced, freely responsive,
and wholly dependent upon the one Mediator.

## Revelation 11–12 control

Revelation 11:19 places the Ark in the heavenly sanctuary and 12:1 presents the
woman clothed with the sun. Their canonical adjacency belongs to the received
Assumption and Marian horizon. The woman is a polyvalent apocalyptic sign whose
referential field includes Israel, the Church, and Mary. The publication may
affirm Mary's personal place within the sign; it may not claim an exclusively
Marian literal referent, a photographic vision, or a stand-alone definition of
the Assumption.

## Exclusion register

- **Excluded:** four mechanical one-to-one biblical proofs. The Ark relations
  are unequal and the dogmas retain their proper grounds.
- **Excluded:** Mary as divine, source of Christ's divinity, fourth hypostasis,
  or inert container.
- **Excluded:** Ark holiness as a reason to despise marriage, conjugal union,
  childbirth, bodies, or women.
- **Excluded:** Uzzah as proof of perpetual virginity, as Joseph's type, or as
  the explanation of Joseph's continence.
- **Excluded:** acacia botany, `incorruptible wood`, or gold plating as proof of
  the Immaculate Conception.
- **Excluded:** Mary's death, non-death, date, place, tomb, or Assumption
  mechanism as defined content.
- **Excluded:** Assumption by Mary's own power or an equivalence with Christ's
  Ascension.
- **Excluded:** an exclusively Marian literal sense of Revelation 12.
- **Excluded:** Ark typology as a fifth dogma or a substitute for conciliar and
  papal definitions.

## Control result

The public section gives each dogma's exact object before applying typology,
names its controlling authority and mode, grades the Ark relation without
grading the dogma, and states its necessary exclusions locally. The final
synthesis is confident and Catholic: Mary is the living Ark because Christ is
the Covenant and divine Presence in person; all four dogmas disclose the
fullness of what his grace accomplished in his mother.
