# Spiritual Pedagogy Plan

## Purpose and reader

This record governs the pedagogical design of *The Ark and the Mother of the
Lord*. The intended reader is a serious Catholic lay reader, catechist,
seminarian, clergy member, or parish study group. The work must be usable as a
continuous narrative-theological study and as six sessions of adult formation.

Its formation objective is faithful Catholic contemplation and conviction:
the reader should know the Ark's scriptural journey, perceive the received
Christological and Marian typology, confess the four dogmas under their exact
objects and authorities, and allow the truth received to mature into worship,
holiness, and apostolic charity. Evidence distinctions protect this movement
from false attribution and brittle overstatement; they do not impose a secular
presumption against miracle, providence, canonical unity, Tradition, or dogma.

The reader-facing apparatus is:

- `sections/90-where-glory-dwells.tex`: spiritual and apostolic synthesis;
- `sections/95-study-guide.tex`: six-session group or self-study path; and
- `sections/96-synopsis.tex`: compact retrieval aid for the journey, dogmas,
  Joseph's vocation, and spiritual application.

These devices are modern project additions. They must never be attributed to
Lewis, Chautard, or Garrigou-Lagrange, and no claim is made that general
learning-science evidence establishes growth in faith, prayer, virtue, or
holiness.

## Reader-facing formation sequence

The recurring sequence is:

1. **Observe:** attend to the literal and canonical text.
2. **Recall:** retrieve consequential details before checking them.
3. **Compare:** identify sequence, diction, theological pattern, and difference.
4. **Distinguish:** classify warrant, received authority, fittingness, and limit.
5. **Contemplate:** remain with the Christological truth and Mary's
   grace-enabled response.
6. **Live:** name one proportionate act of worship, repentance, fidelity, or
   charity.

Application always follows evidence. A spiritual fruit may deepen reception
of a sound interpretation; it cannot establish an interpretation that lacks
textual or received support.

## Prayer and pastoral boundaries

- The project composes no vocal prayer, translation, paraphrase of a received
  prayer, or hybrid devotional text.
- A prompt may direct the reader to silence, to a cited scriptural passage, or
  to an identified received prayer whose wording, witness, and rights are
  handled elsewhere.
- No prompt asks for public disclosure of sin, trauma, marital details, or
  private spiritual experience.
- Study performance is never presented as faith, virtue, mystical attainment,
  or ecclesial status.
- Joseph and Mary's marriage remains true, mutually given, virginal marriage.
  No exercise may imply that marriage, conjugal union, conception, or birth is
  unclean.
- Mary's action remains created, graced, maternal, subordinate, and transparent
  to Christ. The study never makes her an autonomous source of sanctification
  or apostolic fruit.

## Internal versus reader-facing material

Edition history, rights uncertainty, DOI support, detailed transfer limits,
and the attribution firewall remain in this research record and the source
audit.  One concise reader-facing paragraph may identify the three named
Christian pedagogical influences and their strictly formative role; the final
references must then name the checked editions.  The reader otherwise receives
the fruits: clear structure, honest claim boundaries, retrieval aids,
contemplative pauses, and a path into charity.  Learning-science claims, model
history, and internal review status do not enter the exposition.
