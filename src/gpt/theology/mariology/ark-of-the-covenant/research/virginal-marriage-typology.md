# Virginal Marriage and Ark Typology — Source and Claim Control

This record controls the treatment of Joseph and Mary's true virginal marriage
in `sections/75-virginal-marriage.tex`. It is current to 2026-08-15. The
publication's positive thesis is that ordered custody of the old sacred sign
flowers in the New Covenant as obedient, personal, marital, and fatherly
service around the incarnate Son. The Uzzah episode is retained as a bounded
pastoral contrast; it does not explain or prove continence.

## Status legend

- **D — dogma:** truth requiring divine and Catholic faith.
- **O — official or primary online:** checked in Scripture or an
  authority-owned official text.
- **P — primary working text:** exact ancient or medieval work and locus
  checked in a working transcription or translation, not represented as a
  critical edition.
- **C — critical-locus controlled:** critical series or edition locus is
  identified; consequential wording is paraphrased rather than published as a
  long quotation.
- **M — magisterial reception:** an official teaching receives or summarizes
  the earlier source.
- **S — source-grounded project synthesis:** a conclusion drawn from identified
  sources but not attributed to Scripture, a Father, or the Magisterium.
- **X — excluded inference:** not supported or pastorally/theologically unsafe.

## Controlling distinctions

1. Mary's perpetual virginity is dogma. Joseph's lifelong virginity before this
   marriage, age, or prior marital history is not separately defined.
2. Mary and Joseph's marriage was true. Their mutually embraced continence was
   the singular form of genuine marital love, not the absence of marriage.
3. Joseph's legal and affective fatherhood was authentic though not biological.
4. Ephrem's priest-before-Ark image is poetic typology. It does not confer
   Aaronic, Levitical, or sacramental priesthood on Joseph; priestly fulfillment
   belongs uniquely to Christ the High Priest.
5. `Joseph as Davidic husband and guardian of the living Ark` is project
   synthesis built from received predicates. No verified Father or magisterial
   text was found using that exact title.
6. The Uzzah–Joseph comparison is a project-authored pastoral contrast between
   handling contrary to appointed order and God's positive command to receive
   Mary as wife. It is not a biblical or patristic identity and does not assign
   Uzzah a motive of domination.

### Quarantined Ephrem lead

The former claim that *Hymns on the Nativity* 4.149–156 depicts Joseph
caressing and ministering to the infant with joy and awe has not been verified
against the controlled Beck witness. It supplies no publication claim and must
not be cited, paraphrased, or used as support unless that locus and wording are
first verified and bound.

## Bounded Uzzah contrast

### Publication-safe synthesis

The first Davidic procession did not follow the appointed mode of bearing the
Ark. Uzzah's death discloses the seriousness of holy service even when an
action appears helpful. At the New Testament threshold, Joseph son of David is
positively commanded to receive Mary his wife. He obeys, names Jesus, and
repeatedly receives mother and Child through danger. The contrast is therefore
between handling contrary to the appointed order and personal reception by
God's command. It is not an equation of Uzzah with Joseph and not an
explanation of marital continence.

### Excluded claims

- **X:** Uzzah died because ordinary touching as such is evil.
- **X:** Uzzah sought to dominate the Ark; Scripture narrates an attempt to
  steady it but does not disclose that motive.
- **X:** Joseph believed marital contact would kill him.
- **X:** Uzzah proves Mary's perpetual virginity or Joseph's continence.
- **X:** Mary is an untouchable cult object rather than a free person, wife,
  and mother.
- **X:** Conjugal union, bodies, childbirth, or women are contaminating.
- **X:** Joseph is a Levite, Aaronic priest, or sacramentally ordained guardian
  of a physical Ark.
- **X:** Joseph's authority means possession. *Redemptoris Custos* presents it
  as spousal and fatherly self-gift, work, protection, and service.

## Control result

The public section leads with command, true marriage, mutual self-gift, and
authentic fatherhood. Ephrem supplies a strong authenticated positive Ark
image; Augustine, Aquinas, and *Redemptoris Custos* govern its marital meaning.
The Uzzah episode remains visible, canonically interpreted, explicitly labeled
project synthesis, and pastorally subordinate. No published sentence makes
continence the product of fear or makes ordinary marital love impure.
