\newcommand{\arkapparatusappendix}{%
  \section{Scope, Status, and Qualifications}

  \subsection{Catholic standpoint and governing confession}

  This study is a faithful Catholic exposition.  It receives Sacred Scripture
  as the inspired word of God, reads the two Testaments within the unity of
  God's saving economy, and receives the Marian dogmas according to the
  Church's authoritative teaching.  Christ is the New Covenant and definitive
  divine Presence in person.  Mary is called the living Ark because the
  eternal Son took flesh in her and was borne by her; her dignity is created,
  graced, wholly dependent on Christ, and ordered to him.

  \subsection{Kinds of evidence}

  The exposition distinguishes the literal sense of a biblical passage,
  canonical typology, patristic and liturgical reception, magisterial teaching,
  later theological fittingness, project synthesis, and spiritual application.
  These distinctions protect truthful Catholic reasoning and accurate
  attribution; they do not impose a secular presumption against miracle,
  providence, canonical unity, Tradition, or Marian doctrine.  A correspondence
  may be theologically luminous without being a repeated word, an exact road,
  or an independent proof of a dogma.

  \subsection{Material limits}

  The map presents narrative geography and explicitly graded site
  identifications, not a recovered ancient itinerary.  The Ark image relates
  to the four Marian dogmas in unequal ways and gathers their Christological
  coherence without replacing their proper scriptural, traditional, and
  magisterial grounds.  Uzzah is not treated as a canonical figure of Joseph;
  Joseph's vocation is the positive obedience of a true husband who receives,
  names, guards, and serves the incarnate Lord within a freely embraced
  virginal marriage.  No Father, saint, lexical statistic, archaeological
  identification, quotation, or motive is attributed beyond the source state
  recorded in the research apparatus.

  \subsection{Use and review status}

  This publication is intended for study, catechesis, meditation, and adult
  formation.  It supplies neither ecclesiastical approval nor independent
  specialist review, and it should not be used as a substitute for the
  biblical text, the Church's doctrinal documents, or competent pastoral and
  theological judgment.  Source status, attribution controls, translation and
  rights limits, and production review belong to the tracked research record.}
