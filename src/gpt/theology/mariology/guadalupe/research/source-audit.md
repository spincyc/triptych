# Guadalupe Monograph — Claim-Level Source Audit

Online sources were last rechecked on 2026-07-16 unless otherwise noted. This audit separates (a) the date represented in the tradition, (b) the date and genre of the surviving witness, (c) the object of an ecclesial act, and (d) an editor's theological synthesis. A stable official source controls a competent act. A shrine source is a custodial and pastoral witness, not automatically an event-origin decree. A scholarly interpretation is not ecclesial authority. A negative search is dated and reproducible in scope; it is not proof that no undiscovered document exists.

## Rejected leads and non-findings

Rejected as evidence: anonymous apologetic graphics; unattributed “scientists say” lists; NASA claims without an agency record; eye images without raw files and controls; constellation diagrams chosen after the fact; claims that the cloth emits a heartbeat or music; temperature statements without instruments and ambient controls; categorical no-pigment or no-human-intervention bundles; the `coatlaxopeuh` etymology presented as settled fact; any inference that feast, basilica, coronation, canonization, papal visit, crowd size, conversion fruit, or national importance automatically supplies a modern supernatural-origin decree.

The audit also rejects the opposite shortcuts: “nothing existed before 1648”; “Juan Diego is officially fictitious”; “the Church has never received Guadalupe”; “human artistry would make veneration fraudulent”; or “late narrative evidence proves that no event could have occurred.” Each exceeds the evidence or misstates ecclesial acts.
