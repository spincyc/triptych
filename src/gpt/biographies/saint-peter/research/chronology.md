## Synchronization decisions

- No exact birth year or age is supplied.
- The two common ranges for Jesus' ministry are left visible.
- Paul's “three” and “fourteen” years are not converted into false exact years.
- Galatians 2:1–10 is not silently identified with either Acts 11 or Acts 15.
- The Antioch incident is not forced before or after Cornelius or the Acts 15
  deliberation.
- Peter's disappearance from Acts is not treated as death or immediate move to
  Rome.
- Romans' silence is evidence against a simple uninterrupted Roman episcopate
  before the letter, not proof that Peter never came later.
- AD 64–68 is the controlling martyrdom range. AD 64, 67, 29 June, and the
  claim of a same-day death with Paul remain lower-tier traditions.
- A proposed mid-second-century monument and a first-century burial are two
  different chronological claims.

## Review limit

No independent chronological, archaeological, or epigraphic review has been
performed. A future specialist review should focus on the Galatians–Acts visit
mapping, the date and stratigraphy of the Vatican aedicula, the Appian Way
commemoration, and the custody history of the human remains.
