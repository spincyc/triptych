# Saint Paul — source audit

All online sources used below were reviewed on 16 July 2026, including sources
retrieved by the shared research workflow on the preceding evening and
rechecked during drafting. “Used for” names the claim actually carried; it is
not a list of everything the source could support. Evidence codes follow
`guidance/biographies.md` exactly.

checked project paraphrase, not a generated Bible translation. The NABRE text,
notes, and introductions remain outside the project's license. Short quotation

## Consequential negative results and unresolved leads

- No exact birth year or appearance is recoverable.
- No canonical source places Paul on a horse or says Jesus renamed him.
- No exact medical diagnosis of the thorn is supported.
- No wife, widowhood, or never-married status can be established beyond being
  unmarried when First Corinthians was written.
- No Acts verdict, release notice, Spanish letter, or secure post-Acts itinerary
  survives.
- The Muratorian Fragment was not collated and does not support the publication.
- The Paul–Seneca letters and modern critical edition were not collated.
- The *Depositio martyrum* is collated; its cause, intervening calendar
  transmission, and any Appian bodily-transfer history remain unresolved.
- The 25 January and 18 November observances, Pauline Year program, pallium and
  ecumenical practices, patronage catalogues, and art-historical conventions
  were not collated as publication claims.
- Tre Fontane, the three springs, Mamertine custody, exact last words, named
  judge, and a same-day execution with Peter have no source used for the
  historical core.
- No Roman-law authority was inspected to connect citizenship with beheading.
- No reception-history corpus was inspected for later uses of Pauline texts
  concerning Jews, slavery, sexuality, or political authority.
- No complete scientific report or custody record for the sarcophagus test was
  located.
