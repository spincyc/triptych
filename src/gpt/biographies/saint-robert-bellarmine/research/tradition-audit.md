# Saint Robert Bellarmine — tradition audit

This record separates earliest located witness, later reception, historical
status, and responsible significance. “Not established” means not established
within the recorded search; it is not proof that no local or lost tradition
ever existed. Early hagiography can preserve close testimony while remaining
selective and cause-promoting. Official canonization and Doctor acts establish
their ecclesial objects without becoming independent scientific, medical, or
critical-historical verification of every narrative they contain.

Online and current institutional claims were checked through **2026-07-20**.

## Traditions not advanced because no controlled witness was located

- a competent universal patronage act for catechists, catechumens, canonists,
  lawyers, theologians, or another class beyond the exact local Capua act;
- a securely contemporary physical description or authenticated life portrait;
- independent scientific or genetic authentication of the remains now
  identified at Sant'Ignazio;
- an uninterrupted object-by-object custody record from initial burial through
  exhumation, monument changes, and the 1923 transfer;
- independent modern medical adjudication of beatification or canonization
  miracle claims;
- an exact early locus and transmission history for “walls do not catch cold,”
  “an ounce of peace is worth a pound of victory,” or other detached maxims;
- an official lifetime title “hammer of heretics”;
- a promulgated Sixtine Index carrying a final universal condemnation of
  Bellarmine's *Disputationes*;
- a complete transcript of Bruno's lost eight propositions or a record making
  Bellarmine sole judge;
- an act assigning Bellarmine any role in Galileo's 1633 trial;
- a journey to England for personal oath negotiations;
- membership in the dicastery founded as Propaganda Fide after his death; or
- evidence that canonization and Doctor status approve every political,
  coercive, scientific, exegetical, or textual decision.

These negative findings arise from the reasonably accessible autobiography,
works, early lives, modern critical biography, Bruno and Galileo records,
official Catholic acts, shrine pages, institutional exhibits, and current
records actually inspected. They do not claim exhaustion of every Jesuit,
Capuan, Vatican, municipal, medical, artistic, local-calendar, or oral archive.

## Items requiring further tradition-history work

1. direct collation of the 1622 *Adumbrata imago*, Fuligatti's 1624 *Vita*,
   process testimony, and later major lives for every childhood, charity,
   deathbed, and bodily-preservation scene;
2. exact source, date, speaker, and transmission of “hammer of heretics” and
   each detached maxim;
3. Bellarmine's will and initial burial act, the 1622 exhumation record,
   Bernini commission and object custody, and later monument changes;
4. complete 1923 transfer inventory and any current scientific, conservation,
   or custody documentation concerning the Sant'Ignazio remains;
5. item-level miracle-process records and medical testimony, reported without
   independent adjudication beyond the evidence;
6. item-level portrait, print, bust, and attribute chronology before labeling
   any image contemporary, posthumous, or representative;
7. primary act and bounded scope for the Gregorian sodality patronage and any
   other patronage beyond Paul VI's controlled 1967 Capua act;
8. exact promulgating calendar text connecting the 1931 13 May disposition to
   the current 17 September optional memorial;
9. exact primary locus and controlled translation for sayings quoted by
   current official or devotional pages; and
10. reception histories of the Bruno, Galileo, Vulgate, papal-power,
    coercion, and “nearly pope” narratives across apologetic, polemical,
    scientific, and devotional literature.
