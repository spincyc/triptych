# Saint Robert Bellarmine — chronology audit

## Method

Dates are Common Era. **Exact** means that a controlled witness supplies the
day or a securely fixed office or publication act. **Approximate** means that
the sequence is stronger than the modern civil date. **Competing** identifies
a real disagreement in sources or reconstruction. **Traditional** marks later
memory that must not be silently used as a life date.

Evidence codes follow `guidance/biographies.md`: A subject-authored; N
contemporary or near-contemporary external; E early received; L later
tradition; M official ecclesial reception; K modern critical reconstruction;
S source-grounded synthesis. Bellarmine's 1613 autobiography is A
retrospection. A feast or later official anniversary is not silently treated
as a contemporary civil record.

## Chronological questions deliberately left open

1. Exact institutional course, matriculation, degree, and ordination-place
   sequence before and at Louvain.
2. Volume-by-volume publication, revision, and correction history of the
   *Disputationes* and whether a reported Rome 1607 *Recognitio* existed.
3. Procedural stage and exact text of Sixtus V's unpromulgated Index action.
4. Full meeting and edition sequence of the Sixtine and Sixto-Clementine
   Vulgate revision.
5. Exact appointment dates and institutional acts for Index and Holy Office
   service.
6. Exact contents of Bruno's lost eight propositions, full Roman dossier, and
   individual votes and interventions.
7. Document-by-document chronology of *de auxiliis* and Bellarmine's changing
   counsel.
8. The original Capuan appointment, election, consecration, entry, and
   resignation acts; whether the appointment was intended partly as removal
   from Rome; and the dates and acts of each synod, visitation, and council.
9. Exact 1605 conclave ballots and the evidentiary force of “nearly pope.”
10. Complete publication and reciprocal-response chronology for Venice, the
    English oath, James I, Sarpi, and Barclay.
11. Precise legal and documentary relationship among the 25 February papal
    direction, Bellarmine's 26 February warning, the stronger precept record,
    and the 26 May certificate.
12. Exact initial burial, 1622 exhumation, monument, later custody, and the
    physical identity of remains transferred in 1923.
13. Competent acts for patronages other than the controlled 1967 Capua act,
    and the exact promulgating calendar text by which the present 17 September
    observance replaced the historical 13 May observance.
