## Edition, translation, and rights notes

- [CSEL catalog](https://csel.at/en/publikationen/csel/) was used to orient the
  Latin corpus. Modern critical apparatus is copyrighted and not reproduced.
- The [CSEL 33 scan/text](https://bkv.unifr.ch/en/works/cpl-251/versions/aug-conf-csel)
  and Weiskotten's 1919 Possidius are public-domain bases; English prose in the
  biography is newly composed paraphrase. Brief Latin identifiers remain in
  Latin at their loci. No direct Scripture quotation or translated prayer is
  offered in this revision.
- New City Press, CUA/Ancient Christian Writers, Brown, Hermanowicz,
  Fredriksen, Shaw, Ebbeler, Conybeare, and other modern works remain
  copyrighted. Publisher-only records establish bibliography only; their
  arguments are not represented as used or paraphrased.
- Vatican English text remains third-party official material and is paraphrased
  only for the limited reception claim in the papal-audience row.

## Known audit limits

The source architecture is sufficient for a working biography at the reduced
claim level recorded above. A publication-level specialist should still
collate Prosper's exact entry, the 411 *Gesta* and legal dossier, the Rule,
Divjak, and Dolbeau corpora, disputed sermon dates, Pavia translation witnesses,
the earliest securely dated child-and-shell and *Te Deum* recensions, and the
publisher-only modern bibliography before any of those sources carries a
stronger claim.
