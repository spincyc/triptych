# Saint Augustine of Hippo — tradition audit

This record separates earliest located witness, later reception, historical
status, and responsible significance. “Not established” does not mean
spiritually meaningless; “early” does not mean independently authenticated.

## Items requiring further tradition-history work

- earliest securely dated and localized child-and-shell recension;
- earliest witness for the baptismal *Te Deum* scene;
- precise medieval emergence of the flaming-heart and arrow attributes;
- full manuscript history behind the sister-name tradition;
- earliest primary documents for Sardinian custody and Liutprand's translation;
- current official Pavia shrine description and any published examination of
  remains;
- item-level histories for individual patronages before presenting them as
  current official claims.
