# Saint Augustine of Hippo — chronology audit

## Method

Dates are given in the Common Era. “Attested” identifies the event, not
necessarily a modern civil-day conversion. “A” dates depend principally on
Augustine's own retrospective or contemporary writing; “N” adds external
near-contemporary control; “E” records early reception; “K” marks modern
reconstruction. Feast dates are not silently used as death dates.

## Chronological questions deliberately left open

1. Exact day of the 386 garden event.
2. Exact site of Cassiciacum.
3. Exact date of the return to Africa and Adeodatus's death.
4. Whether coadjutor consecration and succession should be separated between
   395 and 396.
5. Fine dating of particular sermons, letters, and books of long works.
6. Start-day conversion for Possidius's “third month” of the siege.
7. Direct text and edition for the received 28 August death date.
8. Date, witnesses, route, and current custody claims for the proposed relic
   translations through Sardinia to Pavia.
