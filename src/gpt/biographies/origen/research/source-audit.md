# Origen — source audit

Online witnesses and institutional records were inspected on **2026-07-19**.
Codes follow `guidance/biographies.md`. “Used” means that the publication relies
on the source at the stated locus and only within the stated transmission limit.
Old public-domain English pages are research aids, not critical editions.

## Consequential negative results and unresolved searches

1. No civil birth, family-register, ancestry, legal-status, or exact birthplace
   record was located. Eusebius's age notice supports c. 184/185 but not a day.
2. No independent contemporary report, self-admission, medical record, or bodily
   evidence for self-castration was located.
3. No charter, register, continuous constitution, campus, or fixed curriculum
   for a modern-style Alexandrian “Catechetical School” was located.
4. No evidence securely identifies Origen's Ammonius with Plotinus's teacher.
5. No source locates Juliana's Symmachian-manuscript transfer specifically in
   Caesarea or assigns each Hexaplar stage to one city.
6. No original Alexandrian act, charge sheet, vote, complete defense, canonical
   judgment, or juridical exile order survives in the controlled corpus.
7. No source reconciles Eusebius's Valentinian Ambrose with Jerome's Marcionite
   Ambrose; the contradiction remains recorded.
8. No initiation or baptismal source was located for Julia Mamaea or Philip the
   Arab through Origen; correspondence or instruction does not supply one.
9. No official Decian prison act, complete post-torture letter collection,
   medical cause of death, last words, or near-contemporary deathbed narrative
   was located.
10. No contemporary burial act, continuous Tyre tomb custody, authenticated
    relic chain, competent modern examination, or current official relic claim
    was controlled.
11. No competent canonization, stable received universal cult, execution
    account, suppressed feast dossier, or formal declaration of Origen as a
    Doctor of the Church was located. This is bounded absence, not proof that no
    local honor ever existed.
12. No complete whole-corpus audit supports a universal characterization of
    every Origenian reference to Jews; ethical conclusions remain restricted to
    *Letter to Africanus* 4–9 and *Against Celsus* 2.4–8.
13. No full neutral archive of Celsus, Heracleon, Demetrius, the first
    Origenist controversy, or the sixth-century Palestinian factions survives.
14. No item-level iconographic, patronage, quotation, local-shrine, or calendar
    audit was completed; attractive sayings and visual attributes remain
    unauthenticated until work/locus or dated object is supplied.
