\item Jerome. Letters 27, 32, 57, and 106. English translation in Fremantle, \emph{Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers}, second series, vol. 6 (1893). \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3001027.htm}{Letter 27}; \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3001032.htm}{Letter 32}; \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3001057.htm}{Letter 57}; \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3001106.htm}{Letter 106}.
\item Jerome. Letter 112 to Augustine, transmitted in Augustine's correspondence as Letter 75. English translation by Cunningham, \emph{Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers}, first series, vol. 1 (1887). \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1102075.htm}{Letter 112/75}.
\item Jerome. ``Helmeted'' Prologue to Kings. Kevin P. Edgecomb, trans. (2006), public domain. \sourceurl{https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/jerome_preface_kings.htm}{Online witness}.
\item Jerome. Prologue to Tobit. Kevin P. Edgecomb, trans. (2006), public domain. \sourceurl{https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/jerome_preface_tobit.htm}{Online witness}.
\item Jerome. Preface to Judith. Andrew S. Jacobs, trans. (2004), public domain. \sourceurl{https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/jerome_preface_judith.htm}{Online witness}.
