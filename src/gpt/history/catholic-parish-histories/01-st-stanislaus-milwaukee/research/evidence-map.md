## Rejected inference map

| Rejected inference | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| “Saint Stanislaus is simply the first Polish parish in the United States.” | National rankings vary with definitions; the checked evidence safely establishes Milwaukee's oldest Polish parish and UWM's “first urban Polish congregation” description. |
| “The current church was finished in 1873.” | It entered use then, but principal building and furnishing campaigns continued through 1894 and major remodeling through 1913. |
| “The school began in one uncontested year.” | Sources may count instruction, organization, sisters' staffing, a schoolhouse, and the present building separately. |
| “The church is purely Romanesque, Baroque, or Gothic.” | Preservation sources describe a Romanesque Revival base, Central-European/Baroque massing, and later Gothic/Romanizing additions. |
| “Daughter parishes prove Saint Stanislaus failed.” | Institutional multiplication was a major measure of immigrant-parish success before later demographic decline. |
| “The 1886 gathering proves the pastor endorsed every labor action.” | The source establishes a gathering point, not a complete institutional position. |
| “One event caused decline.” | Out-migration, highway clearance, school economics, changing urban settlement, and deferred maintenance converged; the record does not allocate percentages. |
| “The parish was suppressed and replaced by ICKSP.” | Current archdiocesan and institutional records still distinguish the parish from the oratory. |
| “The church became the oratory” or “oratory is merely a new parish name.” | The 2018 explanation and current schedules present separate institutions sharing place and clergy. |
| “ICKSP owns the complex.” | Service and administration do not prove ownership; the public property record names St Stanislaus Properties LLC. |
| “The oratory was certainly erected in 2016.” | Stronger contemporary and institutional chronology places care in 2007 and erection in 2008; 2016 records concern anniversary and altar consecration. |
| “The 2016 anniversary marked 150 years of the present building.” | It marked the parish's 1866–2016 history; the 1872 cornerstone anniversary was observed in 2022. |
| “Restoration returned every surface to an uncontested 1873 original.” | The building accumulated campaigns through 1913 and the restoration used conservation, reconstruction, and new historically informed work. |
| “A single total restoration cost is known.” | No audited aggregate ledger across all phases was located. |
| “Growing attendance proves Polish residential repopulation.” | The current constituency is regional and activity reports are not a neighborhood census. |
| “The 2024 expansion is already complete.” | The assessor proves acquisition, and 2025 city records prove two approvals; none is a final inspection, occupancy certificate, or proof of completed rehabilitation. |
| “No public record located means no private record exists.” | Every negative finding is bounded to the checked public record at the cutoff. |
