# Source audit: Saint Stanislaus in Milwaukee

Access dates are 2026-07-19 unless a source is a fixed downloaded record or appears in the pastoral-succession section added and rechecked 2026-07-21. Page references identify printed or PDF pages when available. No source listed as a lead supports published prose unless another checked source is named.

## Original, official, and preservation records

Role: cornerstone and principal campaigns; Schmidtner attribution; initial unheated building; sanctuary, roof, organ, painted interior, porch, tower, buttress, sculpture, marble, altar, and pulpit sequence. The $800 architect story is identified there as tradition and is not published as fact. U.S. federal survey text is cited and paraphrased; no image or plan is reproduced.

## Source reconciliation and negative searches

- **Founding:** 1863 is retained as reported organizing; 1866 as formal congregation. No wording collapses them.
- **First and present church:** the reused former Lutheran building at Fifth/Grove and Mineral is separated from the present Fifth and Mitchell church. The present cornerstone was laid in July 1872, the church entered use in 1873, and substantial campaigns continued through 1894 and 1913.
- **School:** sources variously date instruction, organization, sisters' staffing, or a school to 1866, 1867, 1872, and 1873. Gregory's 1878 schoolhouse and the AHI's 1926 present school are the strongest building dates. No false single origin is selected.
- **Architecture:** Romanesque Revival is the state inventory's broad classification. Central-European/Baroque massing and later Gothic/Romanizing work are source-attributed descriptions, not competing declarations of a single “pure” style.
- **Domes and rectory:** exact early-1960s intervention dates and whether the rectory was replaced or radically remade vary. The narrative uses a centennial-era sequence and describes the extant rectory as the 1963–1964 remaking identified in preservation records.
- **Decline:** no continuous membership series, audited causal allocation, formal suppression decree, or complete sale file was found. School closure and contemplated sale remain attributed to local reporting.
- **Pastoral succession:** no complete parish clergy ledger, appointment-decree series, 1866–1966 centennial booklet, or 2016 anniversary book was inspected. Early names and dates therefore remain retrospective; checked annual directories are bounded publication snapshots, while searchable-text results remain labeled finding-aid leads. The 1997 and 2001–2003 intervals remain unresolved, and target-image uncertainty affects 1915, 1939, 1989, 1999–2000, 2004, and 2006–2012. A retrospective names Joseph Chylewski as pastor, but the checked 1913 directory explicitly names Anthony Lex administrator and the checked 1914 entry lists Chylewski after Bishop Kozlowski, so Chylewski is not entered as a confirmed pastor.
- **Oratory:** the 15 October 2007 announcement and current ICKSP history support care beginning in 2007 and reported oratory erection in 2008. The original decree was not located. One 2018 article says 2016, while contemporaneous 2016 records concern the anniversary and altar. The publication uses 2008 with explicit appendix qualification and assigns no unverified day.
- **Ownership:** current and 2018 sources distinguish parish, oratory, and property. The assessor names St Stanislaus Properties LLC. No source supports saying the Institute itself owns the complex.
- **Mid-century and restored stained glass:** the municipal survey dates the dalle-de-verre installation to 1962, while institutional history gives 1964; the publication retains the 1962/1964 discrepancy. Later source pages place discovery of surviving earlier fragments in 2012, 2014, or roughly 2015, so “early-to-mid-2010s” is used where the exact year is not essential.
- **Restoration cost:** no complete audited total across sanctuary, organ, windows, paint, marble, towers, roof, rectory, bells, and site work was located.
- **Expansion:** the March 2024 announcement is followed by an assessor-recorded October purchase. The official announcement's reference to robust 1980s construction is not used as the age of the whole property because the state inventory and assessor date the principal corner building to 1888. Official July and September 2025 records establish preservation and special-use approvals subject to further requirements; no checked final inspection, occupancy, or completion record was found by the cutoff.
- **Excluded false lead:** the Newberry Foreign Language Press Survey record titled “Parish Schools: The St. Stanislaus Kostka School” was checked and excluded. It concerns the Chicago parish, not Saint Stanislaus in Milwaukee, and supports no published claim here.
- **Current vitality:** news items and attendance figures demonstrate recurring worship and organization at their dates. No audited unique-person count, resident-origin survey, or proof of neighborhood ethnic repopulation was located.
- **Archival gaps:** the Notre Dame centennial booklet is cataloged but not digitized; no parish sacramental books, clergy ledger, appointment-decree series, trustee minutes, school registers, complete contracts, diocesan decree file, or comprehensive financial ledger was retrieved.
