## Rejected inference map

| Rejected inference | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| “Saint Anthony was certainly founded in 1903.” | The 2026 label conflicts with the checked directory sequence and other current official records; no 1903 erection act was found. |
| “Nothing Catholic happened in Willits before 1906.” | A directory's silence is bounded negative evidence, not proof against visits, household practice, or an unlocated record. |
| “The parish was founded in 1923, so no church existed earlier.” | A church photograph is cataloged on 4 April 1921. |
| “Raphael Quinn built the entire present church after 1921.” | The archive credits him with building the parish church, but no contract, campaign ledger, or fabric study reconciles that statement with the 1921 photograph. |
| “Sebastian Brennan founded Saint Anthony in 1903.” | His authority record places his 1903 work elsewhere in Mendocino County and his Willits posting in 1928. |
| “Brenton and Brunton can be silently normalized.” | They are the printed 1925 directory form and the archive-controlled identity respectively; the discrepancy is evidence. |
| “Directory reservation stations prove Indigenous conversion or welcome.” | The directories record Catholic administrative reach and contain no Indigenous voice, attendance, or sacramental evidence. |
| “The Capuchin handoff occurred by a known legal act in 1968.” | Adjacent directories show personnel change, and regional history supplies corroboration, but the Willits act and exact date were not located. |
| “Queen of Peace began in 1969.” | That is the first checked named entry under Willits, not proof of the first building, Mass, title, or community. |
| “The current Covelo relationship is institutionally unchanged from the 1923 or 1941 station network.” | Names, classifications, personnel, and governance can change; no continuous administrative file was located. |
| “English and Spanish Masses prove exact parish demographics.” | A worship schedule establishes offered languages, not ethnicity, residence, citizenship, or unique-person counts. |
| “About 150 families is an audited parish membership.” | The number is a guild officer's reported estimate in a 2024 newspaper article. |
| “No public record located means no private record exists.” | Negative findings are limited to the checked public corpus at the cutoff. |
