## Current-law check

On 2026-07-29 the official Holy See Code pages still state that the 1983 Code
governs the Latin Church (canon 1) and that no one may lawfully pronounce
exorcisms over the possessed without particular and express permission from
the local ordinary, which is granted only to a priest possessing the stated
qualities (canon 1172). The Holy See's published authentic-interpretations
compilation still has no entry for canon 1172. This confirms the current
paper's universal-Latin-law and authorization boundaries as of 2026-07-29; it
does not establish territorial implementation or advice for a particular
