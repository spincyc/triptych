## Verdict

**Pass for the historical-liturgical publication gate within the explicit
published scope.**

The positive historical claims are proportionate to their identified evidence.
The account distinguishes biblical and apologetic witnesses, baptismal
preparation, clerical categories, ritual-book prescriptions, codified
reservation, modern revision, and territorial implementation. It does not
present those layers as one unchanged rite or infer actual frequency and
outcomes from normative books.

The Wilson edition directly controls the bounded Gelasian claim. The 1614
princeps identity, section locus, architecture, and preliminary cautions are
kept distinct from later title numbering, local reception, present
authorization, and efficacy. The 1998 decree, 1999 typical edition and public
presentation, 2004 emended reprint, 2021 translation-norm change, and U.S.
implementation are treated as separate acts and states.

## Binding limits that do not block this scoped pass

The *Pontificale Romano-Germanicum* is only a bibliographic coordinate. No
exact artifact, ordo, page, content, juxtaposition, circulation, or local-use
claim depends on it. No representative medieval ordinal is used to claim
delivery-of-the-book symbolism, officeholder duties, career paths, ordinary
practice, or transfer of extraordinary ministry. Those omissions are explicit
in the publication and research controls.

The absence of those reusable controls therefore does not defeat this bounded
historical-liturgical gate. It would become blocking if any excluded positive
claim were restored. The same rule applies to medieval medicine, magic,
heresy, trials, demonological taxonomy, prevalence, and named cases.

A second pass across the expected critical-edition, official synthesis,
specialist manuscript, medieval ordination, and later-pontifical families found
more exact research leads but no acquired and inspected critical page or
collated manuscript folio. The focused ritual-history audit records the
families, reported loci, transmission cautions, and bounded negative result.
Those leads do not justify restoring an excluded positive claim.

## Remaining bounds

This is not a finding that every reusable source desirable for future
expansion has been registered. Provisional ancient and patristic claims retain
their local qualifications, and the broader reusable-source inventory remains
an editorial scope decision rather than evidence for omitted claims.

This review does not close theological, canonical-currentness, clinical,
safeguarding, pastoral, rights, production, web, catalog, installed-PDF, or
release-binding gates. It authorizes no restoration of excluded material and
does not attach to a later source snapshot without renewed review.
