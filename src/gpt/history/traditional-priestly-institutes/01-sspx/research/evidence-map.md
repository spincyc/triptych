## Rejected inference map

| Rejected inference | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| “Diocesan erection means SSPX always retained approval.” | Erection was bounded and later withdrawn according to competent authorities. |
| “Wright's praise made SSPX pontifical right.” | The displayed letter is not an erection decree. |
| “A formal suppression act proves every SSPX procedural objection baseless.” | The objection is historically documented and not retried here. |
| “A valid episcopal consecration is therefore licit or authorized.” | Sacramental validity and ecclesiastical mandate are distinct. |
| “1988 excommunicated every priest and lay attendee.” | Named penalties and formal-adherence rules are narrower. |
| “2009 remission showed the 1988 penalty never existed.” | The decree remitted it from that date; Benedict defined the boundary. |
| “Specific papal faculties amount to institutional recognition.” | The grants and 2017 instruction expressly remain bounded. |
| “The 2026 note says every sacrament is invalid.” | It says all administration is illicit and specifies penance and assisted marriage as invalid. |
| “The 2026 recourse has already prevailed.” | Filing, claimed effect, admissibility, merits, and scope are different questions. |
