## Rejected inference map

| Rejected inference | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| “ICKSP was founded by or broke away from SSPX/FSSP.” | The checked origin line and 2008 act identify a separate Roman–French–Gabonese–Florentine development. Similar liturgy does not establish descent. |
| “The Vatican named Wach and Mora among the 88 men ordained in 1979.” | Their names come from ICKSP records; the Vatican homily and Angelus contain no roster. |
| “The priestly ordination in 1979 canonically founded the Institute.” | It ordained future founders. Institutional formation developed later, and diocesan erection is dated to 1990. |
| “Opus Sacerdotale and ICKSP are one continuous society.” | Connection and inheritance are documented, but no juridical succession act was located. |
| “The exact French location, opening date, and curriculum are established.” | No original house, agreement, register, or formation plan was located; Moissac remains an unverified later lead. |
| “The Institute was founded only at Gricigliano.” | Institutional records date the first canonical erection to Mouila, Gabon. |
| “A legal transfer from Mouila to Florence is known.” | No transfer instrument was located; each setting’s role is described without inventing one. |
| “The operative 2008 line calls ICKSP a clerical society.” | N. 181/2008 says society of apostolic life of pontifical right; priestly character is established elsewhere in the decree and institutional sources. |
| “Pontifical right makes the prior general an independent diocesan ordinary everywhere.” | Universal law preserves local episcopal authority over houses and public apostolate. |
| “Canonial form means cathedral chapter or canons regular.” | The decree says common life on the model of canons; analogy is not automatic identity. |
| “Every clerical oblate is a canonical cleric.” | Current title is institutional; canon 266 ties clerical state to diaconate. N. 181/2008 calls the early group lay oblates. |
| “Sister Adorers are a pontifical-right society of apostolic life.” | N. 182/2008 erects a public association of faithful for three years; no later status-changing act was located. |
| “The 2014 visitation cleared the Institute.” | No report or dispositive outcome was located. The 2016 approval notice does not state that verdict. |
| “The 2026 figures are exact audited totals.” | The calendar uses “more than” and “about” and is an institutional publication. |
| “*Traditionis custodes* suppressed the ICKSP.” | The act changed liturgical law and competence but did not dissolve the society. |
| “FSSP’s 2022 decree automatically covers ICKSP.” | It is a named special act for FSSP members; no ICKSP extension was located. |
| “The 2024 audience granted an exemption.” | The Press Office records a meeting; the ICKSP reports pastoral content. No juridic act was published. |
| “Chicago proves expulsion, global prohibition, or total institutional suppression.” | The checked page establishes only suspension of public Masses at that location; later dioceses received ICKSP ministry. |
| “Later U.S. invitations prove a universal exemption.” | They are local diocesan acts and appointments. |
| “The 2023 journalism is a canonical judgment.” | It reports allegations and ecclesiastical concern; no public adjudication was located. |
| “Wach’s response independently disproves every allegation.” | It establishes his position and right of reply, not an external finding. |
| “Former-Sister claims are findings against every ICKSP priest.” | The women’s group was separately erected as a public association in 2008 and no later status act was located; alleged links are reported specifically and require competent investigation. |
| “A Vatican investigation of the Sisters was publicly confirmed and underway.” | Former candidates reported being told of a plan; the reporting itself said it was unclear whether one had begun, and no official notice was located. |
| “No public outcome means nothing happened privately.” | Public-source absence is cutoff-bound and does not establish the contents of unpublished files. |
