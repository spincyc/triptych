\section{The Institution at the 2025--2026 Snapshot}

The FSSP statistics dated 1 November 2025 report 579 members, of whom 394 were incardinated. The total comprised 387 priests, 30 deacons, and 162 non-deacon seminarians including postulants. Within the priest total the page lists 365 incardinated, 16 incorporated \emph{ad annum}, three postulants, and three associated. It gives an average member age of thirty-nine and sixteen deceased members.

The same report lists service in 151 dioceses, 251 Mass centers, 48 personal parishes, 139 houses, and 103 erected houses. It records 8,776 members in the Confraternity of Saint Peter. The Confraternity number is not part of the 579 clerical and formation total. Likewise, a Mass center is not necessarily a house or parish, and a house's canonical erection is separately counted.

These are precise self-reported categories, not an independently audited census. They are still historically valuable because the institution defines and dates them. The reported average of fourteen priestly ordinations per year across 2014--2025 suggests sustained reproduction, but annual variation remains substantial. The page's charts should not be reconstructed numerically beyond values supplied in accessible text.

\subsection{Growth and consolidation}

Scale changed government. The North American and French fields developed into provinces; Oceania into a district; two seminaries served major language regions; the general council managed hundreds of members and scores of erected houses. A body created from a small crisis cohort had acquired several generations who entered after \emph{Summorum Pontificum} and even after \emph{Traditionis custodes}.

The dated counts also define an agenda for future independent review; they do not answer it. Assessing safeguarding and formation quality, financial governance, the distribution of priests between seminaries and apostolates, local agreements, or oversight capacity would require evidence outside this public audit. The 2024 visitation belongs in that mature institutional context, but no public report or dispositive outcome was located.
