30 June 1988 & Lefebvre and de Castro Mayer consecrate four SSPX bishops at Écône without pontifical mandate. & Holy See acts; full conflict in TPI-01.\\
2 July 1988 & Future FSSP founders issue a declaration of intention rejecting the illicit consecrations and seeking recognized life under the Roman Pontiff with older worship. John Paul II issues \emph{Ecclesia Dei adflicta}. & Separate founder and papal primary acts; declaration does not erect the society.\\
18 July 1988 & Clerics sign the Act of Foundation at Hauterive and petition for a clerical society of apostolic life under canons 731--746. & FSSP-hosted French-source act; signature categories qualified.\\
22 July 1988 & PCED acknowledges the act, recognizes Bisig's election, and states readiness to erect the society after review. & FSSP-hosted German-source Roman declaration; not final erection.\\
August 1988 & Bishop Josef Stimpfle announces reception of a canonical establishment at Wigratzbad. & Later FSSP seminary history; original diocesan instrument not checked.\\
10 September 1988 & PCED grants use of the four named categories of 1962 liturgical books under location and consent conditions. & FSSP-hosted Latin-source decree; operative scope paraphrased.\\
18 October 1988 & PCED Prot. No. 234/88 erects the FSSP as a clerical society of apostolic life of pontifical right, approves constitutions for three years, and names Bisig for three years. & FSSP-hosted Latin-source decree; formal juridical act.\\
November 1988 & The Wigratzbad history says thirty-one seminarians begin studies in French and German groups. & Later institutional self-report; the anniversary account below uses a different opening date.\\
Autumn 1989 & A separate North American anniversary history says the first seminary opens. & Later institutional self-report; no original act or register located to reconcile its wording with November 1988.\\
1991 & The North American work begins in Dallas, followed by Rapid City and Scranton. & FSSP North American retrospective.\\
1994 & Bishop James Timlin approves Our Lady of Guadalupe Seminary at Elmhurst, Pennsylvania. & FSSP seminary history; original decree not checked.\\
1995--1997 & FSSP administration begins at Ottawa's older-liturgical community in 1995; Archbishop Marcel Gervais erects Saint Clement personal parish in 1997. & Local institutional histories; the community began in 1968.\\
26 October 1998 & John Paul II addresses pilgrims for the tenth anniversary of \emph{Ecclesia Dei}, affirming liturgical reform, pastoral understanding, legitimate diversity, and unity. & Official Vatican address.\\
3 July 1999 & Divine Worship issues Prot. n. 1411/99, generic official responses concerning reformed-Missal use, superiors, and concelebration in institutes with older-book faculty. & Official \emph{Notitiae}; FSSP is not named.\\
February 2000 & A special FSSP assembly meets near Rome amid internal division; a contemporary statement denies suppression and compulsory celebration rumors. & Participant statement preserved by a third-party archive.\\
29 June 2000 & Cardinal Castrillón's letter intervenes in government and the liturgical dispute, limits superior-general service to two successive six-year terms, and appoints Devillers. & Later documentary reproduction and insider scholarly confirmation; no PCED archive original checked.\\
4--14 July 2000 & General Chapter receives the intervention, announces administrative recourse, and elects the general council; Devillers is installed 6 July. & Reproduced chapter declaration and contemporary FSSP district release; no disposition of recourse located.\\
September--December 2000 & The Denton and Wigratzbad seminaries occupy new buildings; Wigratzbad is blessed 2 December. & Institutional seminary histories.\\
29 June 2003 & The Holy See gives definitive approval to constitutions reviewed by Rome in 1999 and the Chapter in 2000. & Public FSSP excerpt; full intervening approval history remains unavailable.\\
2006 & John Berg succeeds Devillers as superior general. & Confirmed retrospectively in FSSP 2024 election notice.\\
7 July 2007 & Benedict XVI issues \emph{Summorum Pontificum}, broadening general access to the 1962 Missal and retaining PCED competence. & Official papal act.\\
17 January 2019 & Francis decommissions PCED and assigns its work to a section of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, noting former institutes' stability. & Official papal act.\\
1 July 2021 & North American district becomes a province; Southern Cross Region becomes the District of Oceania. & FSSP governance communiqué.\\
16 July 2021 & Francis issues \emph{Traditionis custodes}; former PCED societies move under the consecrated-life dicastery. & Official papal act.\\
20 July 2021 & FSSP responds with surprise and appeals to its constitutions, magisterial fidelity, and episcopal collaboration. & Participant communiqué.\\
4 and 11 February 2022 & Two FSSP superiors meet Francis; the pope dates a decree granting every member faculty for the four named 1962 books under stated conditions. & Audience described by FSSP; signed decree published by FSSP.\\
29 February 2024 & Francis receives Andrzej Komorowski; the FSSP later reports discussion of the 2022 decree's application. & Press Office confirms audience; FSSP supplies private content.\\
3--18 July 2024 & Seventh ordinary General Chapter meets at Denton; John Berg is elected superior general for six years on 9 July. & FSSP chapter communiqués.\\
September 2024 & Competent dicastery opens an informational apostolic visitation; FSSP notes an earlier 2014 PCED visit. & Vatican News and participant communiqué; no public outcome by cutoff.\\
1 November 2025 & FSSP statistical snapshot reports 579 members, 387 priests, 251 Mass centers, and 48 personal parishes. & Dated self-report, not independent census.\\
19 January 2026 & Leo XIV receives John Berg; FSSP says Josef Bisig accompanied him and the meeting covered history, charism, and local obstacles. & Press Office confirms audience; FSSP supplies content.\\
29 June 2026 & Announced effective date for the French district to become a province; current chart lists the province and its first provincial. & January announcement plus current chart; no formal erection instrument checked.\\
16 July 2026 & Evidence and mutable-status cutoff. & No public final visitation report located.\\
