## Rejected inference map

| Rejected inference | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| “Every person on the 2 July list was a priest or seminarian.” | The act styles six as priests, Zimmer as a deacon, and leaves eight titles unstated. |
| “The 22 July declaration erected the FSSP.” | It expressed readiness; the formal erection decree is dated 18 October. |
| “Pontifical right lets the FSSP establish any house or public apostolate without a diocesan bishop.” | Canons 733 and 738 preserve defined authority of the local ordinary. |
| “Faithful observance of the 1962 books is the same textual claim as absolute exclusivity.” | The checked public constitutional excerpt does not print that stronger proposition. |
| “The 1999 *Responsa* were issued specifically against the FSSP.” | The official text speaks generically of a member of an institute and does not name the society. |
| “The *Responsa* required every FSSP priest routinely to celebrate or concelebrate the reformed Mass.” | The text distinguishes permission, service in specified circumstances, and free concelebration from universal compulsion. |
| “The announced 2000 recourse is known to have succeeded or failed.” | Filing was announced, but no public disposition was located. |
| “The 1988 three-year approval simply expired and nothing intervened until 2003.” | The checked public record does not disclose the complete sequence of interim approvals. |
| “*Summorum Pontificum* erected or newly approved the FSSP.” | The society had been erected in 1988 and its constitutions definitively approved in 2003. |
| “*Traditionis custodes* suppressed the FSSP.” | The act altered universal liturgical law and curial competence but did not dissolve the society. |
| “The 2022 decree exempts the FSSP from every provision of *Traditionis custodes*.” | It grants specified faculties and expressly asks that the later act be considered as far as possible. |
| “No printed expiry date makes the 2022 faculty legally permanent or irrevocable.” | The instrument's silence about duration supports neither conclusion. |
| “A current chart alone proves every formal step in erecting the French Province.” | The chart and announcement are evidence of public administrative treatment, but the erection instrument was not located. |
| “A papal audience report is legislation or an official transcript.” | The Press Office confirms occurrence; FSSP communiqués report private content. |
