## Consequential negative findings

- No original diocesan decree receiving Wigratzbad, approving the early North American seminary, or erecting Saint Clement personal parish was located in the checked set.
- No single founding source produces an unqualified canonical count of “twelve priest founders”; the lists and moments differ.
- No instrument declaring pontifical-right status before 18 October was located; the earlier wording occurs in an uncollated FSSP-hosted rendering of the 10 September decree.
- No original instrument or register was located to resolve the FSSP retrospectives' November 1988 start and autumn 1989 opening dates for Wigratzbad.
- No complete public FSSP constitutions or full set of internal directories was located.
- No complete public sequence of extensions or interim approvals from the initial 1988 three-year experimental approval to the 2003 definitive approval was located.
- The official 1999 *Responsa* does not name the FSSP.
- No Holy See archive original of the 29 June 2000 intervention, complete recourse file, or public disposition of the announced administrative recourse was located.
- No source says every FSSP priest was forced to celebrate or concelebrate the reformed Mass; the checked 2000 records expressly distinguish permission from compulsion.
- No papal act says *Summorum Pontificum* itself erected or reapproved the FSSP.
- No 2019 or 2021 act suppresses the FSSP as a society of apostolic life.
- No separate Vatican website or *Acta Apostolicae Sedis* publication of the 11 February 2022 decree was located in the checked indexes; the signed instrument is published by the FSSP.
- No formal instrument completing erection of the French Province was located; an announced effective date and current organization chart were located.
- No public final report or dispositive outcome of the apostolic visitation was located through 16 July 2026.
- No independent audit of the 2025 membership, finance, house, or apostolate statistics was located.
