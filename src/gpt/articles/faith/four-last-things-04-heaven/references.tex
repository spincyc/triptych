\section{References}
\begingroup\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.25em,itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt]
\item Sacred Scripture: John 14:1--27 and 17; Matthew 5:1--16 and 22:23--33; Romans 8:18--25; 1 Corinthians 13 and 15:20--58; Philippians 1:20--26 and 3:20--21; Revelation 4--5 and 21. English passages checked in the Douay--Rheims Bible, Challoner revision, Project Gutenberg 1581. Scriptural summaries are paraphrases.
\item Benedict XII, \href{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedictus-xii/la/documents/constitutio-benedictus-deus-29-ian-1336.html}{\emph{Benedictus Deus}} (1336), operative definition, DS 1000--1001. Latin Holy See text: immediate vision before bodily resurrection and its uninterrupted continuance.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church}, 999--1000, \href{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P2M.HTM}{1023--1029}, \href{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P43.HTM}{1402--1405}, and \href{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_one/article_7/ii_the_theological_virtues.index.html}{1817--1821}. Official English version.
\item Second Vatican Council, \href{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html}{\emph{Lumen gentium}} 49--50, 59, 62, 68; \href{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19651207_gaudium-et-spes_en.html}{\emph{Gaudium et spes}} 39. Official English versions.
\item St.~Cyprian, \emph{On Mortality} 26, with 1--3 and 22--25 as context, in \emph{Ante-Nicene Fathers} 5, translated by Robert Ernest Wallis. Registered CCEL whole-volume transcription: homecoming, reunion, and desire for Christ.
\item St.~Irenaeus, \emph{Against Heresies} V.2.2--3, Roberts--Rambaut translation, \emph{Ante-Nicene Fathers} 1, Coxe edition (1887). Registered historical OCR, compared with \href{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103502.htm}{New Advent's revised presentation}: Eucharist, created flesh, and resurrection.
\item St.~Augustine, \emph{City of God} XXII.30, Marcus Dods translation, vol.~II (Edinburgh: T.~\& T.~Clark, 1871), Project Gutenberg 45305 transcription: shared glory, freedom from sin, remembered deliverance, rest, and praise.
\item St.~Thomas Aquinas, \emph{Summa theologiae} I, q.~12, aa.~2--8; I--II, q.~2, a.~8 and q.~3, a.~8. English Dominican translation in Project Gutenberg's complete electronic texts 17611 and 17897 (2006), with their disclosed electronic editorial changes; compared with New Advent's presentation. The electronic texts are not facsimiles of an identified printing.
\item \emph{Summa theologiae}, \href{https://www.newadvent.org/summa/5094.htm\#article3}{Supplement, q.~94, a.~3}, English Dominican translation, Supplement, part III, qq.~87--99 (London: Burns, Oates \& Washbourne, 1922), pp.~109--110, compared with New Advent. The Supplement is a posthumous compilation drawn principally from Aquinas's commentary on the \emph{Sentences}.
\item St.~Bernard of Clairvaux, \emph{On the Love of God}, I and VII--X, translated by Marianne Caroline and Coventry Patmore, second edition (London: Burns \& Oates, 1884), pp.~3--5, 29--46: God as the reason for loving God; the growth of love, its reward, and transformation with human nature preserved.
\item St.~Francis de Sales, \emph{Introduction to the Devout Life: A New Translation} (London, Oxford, and Cambridge: Rivingtons, 1876), I.16, pp.~43--45. The printing names no translator. Meditation on paradise and the movement from desire to resolution.
\item St.~John Henry Newman, ``Holiness Necessary for Future Blessedness,'' \emph{Parochial and Plain Sermons}, vol.~I, sermon 1, new edition (London, Oxford, and Cambridge: Rivingtons, 1869), pp.~1--14. A sermon from his Anglican period, used for its spiritual account of holiness and happiness, rather than as a comprehensive statement of Catholic teaching on justification.
\end{itemize}\endgroup
