\section{References}
\begingroup\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.25em,itemsep=0.2em]
\item Sacred Scripture: John 17; Romans 8; 1 Corinthians 13 and 15; Philippians 3; 1 John 3; 2 Peter 3; Revelation 2, 5, and 21--22.
\item Benedict XII, constitution \emph{Benedictus Deus} (1336), DS 1000--1001; \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 988--1019, 1023--1029, 1042--1053.
\item Second Vatican Council, pastoral constitution \emph{Gaudium et spes} 39.
\item St.~Irenaeus, \emph{Against Heresies} V.6--14, 31--36; St.~Augustine, \emph{City of God} XXII.
\item St.~Thomas Aquinas, \emph{Summa theologiae} I, q.~12; I--II, qq.~2--5; Supplement, qq.~92 and 95. The Supplement is a posthumous compilation principally from Aquinas's \emph{Scriptum super Sententiis}.
\item Roman Rite: the Sanctus, communion of saints in the Eucharistic prayers, and prayers for the dead, cited at source-family level without reproducing a territorial vernacular.
\end{itemize}\endgroup
