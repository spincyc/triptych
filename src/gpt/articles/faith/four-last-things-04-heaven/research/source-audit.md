## Objections and disputed matters

The boredom objection is answered by distinguishing eternity from endless clock time and infinite good from exhausted finite objects. The loss-of-self objection is answered from communion and personal identity. The escape-from-creation objection is answered by bodily resurrection and new creation. Exact modes of memory, recognition, place, sensible activity, and knowledge of earthly events remain disputed and are not settled.

The official Holy See page for *Benedictus Deus* was directly checked. No bulk wording is incorporated. No central-source binding is added because the source is not presently registered as an exact reusable artifact in the repository; this audit records the direct official route and claim-level locus.
