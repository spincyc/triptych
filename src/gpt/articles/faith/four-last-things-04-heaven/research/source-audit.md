# Source Audit — Heaven

## Revision and claim control — 2026-10-12

The revised GPT argument moves from communion with the Father through Christ in the Spirit to vision, ecclesial communion, resurrection, and renewed creation. The following checks support particular claims, not an assertion that every historical theological question has been resolved.

| Claim or inference | Passage actually consulted | Disposition and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eternal life belongs to the Son's communion with the Father, shared with his disciples | John 17:1–5, 24–26, registered Douay–Rheims TSV, complete ranges | Close reading. Trinitarian synthesis is also governed by CCC 1024; the essay does not pretend the selected Johannine verses alone explicitly articulate every clause. |
| Heaven is communion with the Trinity, Christ, Mary, angels, and blessed, preserving identity | Official English CCC 1023–1029 | Direct live reading; the divine essence is not an impersonal object detached from Christ. |
| No created good is perfect happiness; vision exceeds natural power | ST I–II 2.8, 3.8; I 12.4, English Dominican/New Advent delivery | Bodies and relevant replies read. Does not settle controversies about natural desire or pure nature; desire is not made a title to supernatural grace. |
| Purified souls see immediately before resurrection and the vision continues | *Benedictus Deus*, full Holy See Latin act, operative paragraph beginning “hac imperpetuum valitura Constitutione” (DS 1000–1001) | Direct reading, checked for before-resurrection clause, no creature as substitute object, and uninterrupted duration. ## Rights, evidence states, and limits

The article paraphrases its sources and reproduces no substantial modern translation or territorial liturgical text. Challoner, Dods 1871, and the historical ANF volume are public domain in the United States under the central records; their digital representations are identified separately from facsimiles. Modern Vatican English, New Advent editorial delivery, and website wrappers are consulted and linked, not relicensed under project CC BY 4.0. No new third-party payload is retained in the publication leaf.

The source bindings distinguish directly inspected registered artifacts from cataloged work/edition identities whose live URLs were consulted. No live-page check silently upgrades a dated central artifact to acquired or verified. The prior *Gaudium et spes* verified pin retains its original date and scope. No new source graph records are required; exact additional Vatican or New Advent delivery artifacts could be registered later only with separate rights and byte review.

The degrees paragraph deliberately attributes the explanation to Aquinas; it does not claim to enumerate all authoritative witnesses to degrees of vision. No Florence claim has been added without a separate exact-text check.

This is not an exhaustive treatment of nature and grace, all patristic eschatologies, Eastern/Western comparisons, the metaphysics of creaturely eternity, or recognition and memory. The essay gives the governing Catholic teaching and a limited set of identified explanations, with the hard question of another's loss left emotionally serious. No independent specialist or ecclesiastical approval is claimed.
