\section{References}
\begingroup\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.25em,itemsep=0.2em]
\item Sacred Scripture: Matthew 25:31--46; John 3:19--21; 5:22--29; Acts 17:31; Romans 2:6--11; 8:3; 2 Corinthians 5:10; Ephesians 2:8--10; 1 Timothy 2:4; James 2:14--26; Revelation 20:11--15.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church}, official English text: 678--679 (Christ as Judge); 1021--1023, 1030--1041 (particular judgment, purification, and final judgment); 1058 (prayer for universal salvation); 1735, 1750--1756, 1776--1794, 1860 (responsibility, acts, conscience); 2006--2011 (grace and merit).
\item Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, \href{https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_19790517_escatologia_en.html}{\emph{Letter on Certain Questions Concerning Eschatology}} (17 May 1979), nos.~2--5. Official English text.
\item St.~Thomas Aquinas, \emph{Summa theologiae} III, q.~59, a.~5, especially the body and replies to objections 1--3 (final judgment after particular judgment); I, q.~21, a.~4 (mercy at the root of divine justice). Translation of the Fathers of the English Dominican Province, Project Gutenberg eBooks 19950 and 17611 (2006 electronic editions).
\item St.~Thomas Aquinas, \emph{Summa theologiae}, \href{https://www.newadvent.org/summa/5087.htm\#article2}{Supplement, q.~87, a.~2}, body and reply to objection 3: disclosure of forgiven sins as an identified theological opinion. English Dominican translation, second revised edition (1920), through New Advent. The Supplement is a posthumous compilation drawn principally from his commentary on the \emph{Sentences}.
\item Benedict XVI, \href{https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_ben-xvi_enc_20071130_spe-salvi.html}{\emph{Spe salvi}} (2007), nos.~47--48: encounter with Christ, purification, and intercession for the dead. Official English text. The account of purifying fire as encounter is distinguished from the dogmatic teaching on purification.
\item Roman Rite: the Creed and the Eucharistic expectation of Christ's return. These are liturgical allusions; no territorial vernacular text is reproduced.
\end{itemize}\endgroup
