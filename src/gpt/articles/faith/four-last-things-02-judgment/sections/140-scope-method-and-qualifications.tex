\section{Scope, Method, and Qualifications}
This essay is Catholic spiritual reading for annual preparation for death. Scripture and authoritative Catholic teaching govern its treatment of particular and final judgment, grace, works, conscience, Penance, purification, and suffrages. The Fathers and saints are read at the identified loci; their interpretations, Aquinas's theological explanations, and Benedict XVI's proposed account of purifying encounter retain their respective status. Practical applications are source-grounded synthesis. The essay does not determine individual culpability or anyone's eternal state, settle the schools on predestination, or describe a perceptual or chronological mechanism of judgment and purification. English Scripture follows the registered Douay--Rheims/Challoner witness. No text is offered for vocal prayer. Historical translations and official texts retain their separate rights; the study is not an ecclesiastically approved catechism or a substitute for personal pastoral care.
