## Rights, uncertainty and disposition

The registered Dominican electronic editions are public domain in the United States under their central rights records; their electronic-editor qualifications remain applicable. No Latin or printed-image collation was performed. Vatican materials remain restricted third-party text, fetched for research and kept out of tracked assets. The article uses paraphrase and exact paragraph references, without reproducing a territorial liturgical vernacular or bulk source wording.

Not every scriptural cross-reference or Catechism moral paragraph was newly collated. The article does not settle predestination schools, identify the damned, diagnose scrupulosity, determine individual culpability, or describe a chronological mechanism of purgatory. Its historical-effects examples are explanatory synthesis governed by Aquinas's express denial of new merit and demerit. The broad Augustine XX and Supplement 88 references were removed for focus and duplication, not on a finding that they cannot support any relevant claim. Independent theological, pastoral, psychological and ecclesiastical review remains unrecorded.
