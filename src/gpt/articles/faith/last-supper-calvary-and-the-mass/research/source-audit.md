## Witness notes

- Scripture was checked in the registered Douay–Rheims/Challoner corpus and against the loci as cited. The article mostly paraphrases.
- Trent, session XXII, chapters 1–2 and canon 3, controls the identity of sacrifice, priest, victim, and distinct manner of offering.
- Vatican II, *Sacrosanctum Concilium* 47, controls institution at the Supper, perpetuation of the Cross, memorial, banquet, and pledge of glory.
- CCC 1322–1323, 1362–1372, 1382, and 1402–1405 supplies the authoritative catechetical synthesis.
- John Paul II, *Ecclesia de Eucharistia* 11–12, explicitly distinguishes sacramental presence from mere evocation while preserving the Pasch's historical uniqueness.
- Benedict XVI, *Sacramentum Caritatis* 9–11, supports “anticipation” and “sacramental concentration” within the unified Paschal Mystery.
- Justin and Irenaeus were checked in the identified ANF 1 English edition
  against OCR and facsimile passage records; Ambrose was checked in the
  identified PL 16 Latin pages; Augustine X.6 was checked in the Dods English
  and CSEL 40 Latin witnesses.
- Aquinas was checked at ST III, q. 73, a. 4; q. 79, a. 1; and q. 83,
  a. 1. His distinctions among memorial, spiritual nourishment and union,
  representation of the Passion, and containment of Christ control the
  conceptual correction.

## Bounded inspection finding

No bound witness inspected for this article teaches that the apostles literally
moved forward to the civil time of Calvary or that later congregations
literally move backward. This is an inspection finding over the enumerated
loci, not a computational, full-text, author-wide, or universal negative
search.
