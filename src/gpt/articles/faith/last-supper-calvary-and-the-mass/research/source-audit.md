# Source Audit — The Supper, the Cross, and the Mass

Sources were checked through 2026-07-26. Exact loci, not summaries or quotation pages, govern the rendered claims.

## Claim matrix

## Witness notes

- Scripture was checked in the registered Douay–Rheims/Challoner corpus and against the loci as cited. The article mostly paraphrases.
- Trent, session XXII, chapters 1–2 and canon 3, controls the identity of sacrifice, priest, victim, and distinct manner of offering.
- Vatican II, *Sacrosanctum Concilium* 47, controls institution at the Supper, perpetuation of the Cross, memorial, banquet, and pledge of glory.
- CCC 1322–1323, 1362–1372, 1382, and 1402–1405 supplies the authoritative catechetical synthesis.
- John Paul II, *Ecclesia de Eucharistia* 11–12, explicitly distinguishes sacramental presence from mere evocation while preserving the Pasch's historical uniqueness.
- Benedict XVI, *Sacramentum Caritatis* 9–11, supports “anticipation” and “sacramental concentration” within the unified Paschal Mystery.
- Patristic loci were checked in public-domain English working witnesses and against their conventional work divisions. No claim depends on a disputed isolated quotation.
- Aquinas was checked at ST III, q. 73, a. 4 and q. 83, a. 1; his distinction between representation of the Passion and containment of Christ controls the conceptual correction.

## Consequential negative result

No identified scriptural, patristic, scholastic, conciliar, catechetical, or papal witness teaches that the apostles literally moved forward to the civil time of Calvary or that later congregations literally move backward. This is a bounded result over the witnesses listed above, not proof that no later writer has used a similar metaphor.
