This map also corrects a false symmetry. It is not quite right to say that the
Supper points ``forward'' in precisely the same way that Mass points
``backward.'' The Supper occurs within Christ's own movement toward his Passion
and institutes the sacrament before the immolation. Later Masses occur after
the Resurrection and Ascension, in the Church's movement toward his return.
They remember the whole Pasch, offer the living Christ, apply the fruit of the
Cross, and anticipate the heavenly banquet. The Eucharist does not face only
backward.
