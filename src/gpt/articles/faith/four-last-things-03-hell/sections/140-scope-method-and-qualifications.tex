\section{Scope, Method, and Qualifications}
This essay is Catholic spiritual reading for annual preparation for death. Scripture and the Church's teaching establish its account of hell; selected Fathers and saints deepen the contemplation and guide its practice. The Church's doctrine, attributed theological explanations, imaginative meditation, and Teresa's private testimony retain their respective authority. The selection spans martyrdom, patristic catechesis and preaching, scholastic theology, and spiritual masters; it is not an exhaustive survey. It neither estimates the number of the damned nor determines particular persons' final destiny. The companion essays consider death, judgment, and heaven. Practical applications to annual recollection are source-grounded synthesis. Scripture quotations follow the Douay--Rheims, Challoner revision; the citation to Mark 9 follows that witness's numbering. The closing prayer reproduces an identified historical English sentence without adaptation. This spiritual reading does not replace personal sacramental counsel.
