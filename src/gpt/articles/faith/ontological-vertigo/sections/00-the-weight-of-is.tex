\section{When a Fact Changes Its Weight}

A fact can be known for years and then change its weight. You may have long known, in some loose sense, that you did not cause your own birth. Birth, however, can be held safely in the past. The harder question concerns this moment: how are you here to ask it at all? You think, choose, resist, and understand through powers that are genuinely yours; yet you did not first supply the existence in which those powers operate. You are unmistakably real and active. The unexplained remainder is not another event within your life but the act by which there is a life at all.

You can explain a great deal about yourself. Parents and biology help to explain your body; teachers and experience help to explain what you know; reasons, habits, and circumstances help to explain what you choose. All of those answers begin after there is someone with a body, powers, and choices to explain. It is easy to treat existence as already secured and then ask only what has happened within it. The deeper problem is the existence so habitually taken for granted.

That matters because dependence can sound like subtraction. Received being can sound like diminished being; divine causality can sound like less room for creaturely action. Can dependence be total without becoming erasure?

Feeling cannot settle that fear. The inquiry has to begin lower down, with ordinary change and what can bring a possibility into act.
