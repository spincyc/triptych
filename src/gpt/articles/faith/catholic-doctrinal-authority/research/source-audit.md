## Patristic and historical sources

Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* 1.10.1–3 and 3.3.1–4 controls the public rule of faith, legitimate growth in explanation, and apostolic-succession claim. The exact retained ANF1 OCR loci and boundaries were inspected and registered as passages. OCR defects, the disputed Roman clause, and Coxe's editorial polemic are not used; no wording is quoted and no critical-edition status is claimed.

The article makes no claim that the Fathers possessed the 1989/1998 schema. Medieval and manualist notes are described as plural school systems; no label is assigned without author and period in future expansion.

## Negative results and controls

- No single “ladder” captures object, mode, authority, centrality, and assent.
- “Non-infallible” does not mean optional; “binding” does not mean irreformable.
- The hierarchy of truths is not a hierarchy of truthfulness.
- Reception is not a plebiscite that creates dogmatic truth.
- No papal verbal formula mechanically guarantees an ex cathedra act.
- No universal promulgation makes one manualist note table the current law of belief.
- No opposition is attributed uniformly to all Protestants or Orthodox.
- No source is quoted at length; official translations retain their own rights status.
