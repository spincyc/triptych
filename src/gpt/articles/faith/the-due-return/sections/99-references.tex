\subsection*{Patristic witnesses}

{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]
\item St.~Irenaeus of Lyons, \emph{Against Heresies} IV.14.1--2, on divine non-need, service for the creature's good, and fellowship with God; IV.17.1 and 18.1--6, on sacrifice, justice, and offerings that do not supply a divine need. Alexander Roberts and William Rambaut's 1885 ANF working English: \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103414.htm}{IV.14}, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103417.htm}{IV.17}, and \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103418.htm}{IV.18}.
\item St.~Justin Martyr, \emph{First Apology} 67, on the Sunday assembly, Scripture, common prayer, Eucharistic thanksgiving and Communion, and care of those in need; Marcus Dods and George Reith's 1885 ANF working English, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0126.htm}{online witness}.
\item Augustine, \emph{City of God} X.1, 3, 5--6, and 19--20, on \emph{latria}, true religion, outward signs, every work ordered to holy fellowship with God as sacrifice, the redeemed city offered through Christ, and God as needing no offering; public-domain NPNF working English, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/120110.htm}{online witness}.
\item St.~Gregory Nazianzen, \emph{Oration} 14.23--25, on received existence and created goods answered by kindness, love, and just stewardship toward the poor; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/spirit/documents/spirit_20010305_gregorio-nazianzeno_en.html}{Holy See-hosted working excerpt}. See also \emph{Oration} 39.8--10, on prior grace, commandment, purification, illumination, and desire; \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/310239.htm}{NPNF working English}.
\item St.~John Chrysostom, \emph{Homilies on Matthew} 50.3--4, on honoring Christ's Eucharistic body without neglecting Christ in the poor and on almsgiving alongside liturgical splendor; public-domain NPNF working English, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/200150.htm}{online witness}.
\end{itemize}}
