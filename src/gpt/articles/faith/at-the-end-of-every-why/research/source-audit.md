# Source Audit — At the End of Every Why

Sources were checked through 2026-07-23. Public English translations are working study witnesses unless identified as official Holy See English. Consequential claims are governed by the named work and exact locus, not by a claim that an online re-typesetting is critical. No controversial private revelation, extraordinary mystical phenomenon, eroticized report, or disputed locution is used.

## Source classes and exact witnesses

### Scripture and authoritative teaching

The rendered article uses Deut 6:4–5; Job 1–2 and 42:7–9; Ps 27:4; Jer 31:31–34; Ezek 36:26–27; Matt 6:9–13, 21:28–32, and 22:37–40; Mark 14:32–42; Luke 10:25–37; John 6:53–57, 9:1–3, and 15:9–17; Rom 5:5, 8:14–17, and 13:8–10; Gal 4:4–7; Col 1:24; 1 Tim 2:1–4; Jas 5:16; 2 Pet 1:4; and 1 John 4:7–21.

Authoritative witnesses actually used are CCC 1812–1829, 1965–1974, 1992, 1996–2005, 2006–2011, 2634–2636, 2709–2719, 2735–2741, and 1382–1397; CCC 2007–2011, especially 2010, controls the merit-within-grace claim, including the inability to merit initial grace and the possibility of meriting for others graces ordered to sanctification and eternal life; John Paul II's *Salvifici Doloris*; Benedict XVI's audiences of 16 February 2011 and 1 February 2012 and Corpus Christi homily of 23 June 2011; and Francis's *C'est la confiance*. Official Holy See English pages are listed in the rendered References.

## Consequential negative results

- Love is not reduced to feeling, attraction, permissiveness, philanthropy, or benevolence without supernatural friendship with God.
- Justice and sacramental duty are not treated as lower stages to be discarded. No loving intention makes an evil object good.
- “Spiritual laundering” is not a claim that outward acts are useless or hypocritical before affection matures.
- Gethsemane is not used to universalize physical abnegation, tolerate abuse, or suppress lament and natural aversion.
- Dryness is not automatically a dark night, punishment, loss of grace, or proof of divine absence.
- Prayer is not said to revise omniscience, inform God, or secure a promised temporal outcome. Its external effects are not denied.
- The Eucharist is not opposed to interiority, reduced to legal compliance, or replaced by subjective desire.
- Suffering is not called good in itself. Participation in Christ is not passivity before removable evil, self-harm, or an addition to Christ's Redemption. Pain has no merit in itself; merit presupposes grace, charity, and participation in Christ, cannot obtain the first grace, does not control another's freedom, and guarantees no temporal outcome.
- Severe asceticism is not generalized beyond cloistered contemplative or closely analogous vocations under competent direction. The adopted restriction is a conservative prudential boundary, not an invitation, proof of holiness, or exhaustive magisterial classification.
- Mystical union does not imply necessary emanation, emotional transport, erotic phenomenon, annihilation, absorption, identity of essence, or a reproducible experience.
- The exact Johannine confession is not reversed into “love is God.”
