\subsection*{Sacred Scripture}

{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]
\item Deut 6:4--5; Jer 31:31--34; Ezek 36:26--27; Matt 22:37--40; John 10:17--18 and 15:9--17; Rom 5:5, 8:14--17, and 13:8--10; Gal 4:4--7; 2 Pet 1:4; 1 John 4:7--21.
\item The exact confession ``God is love'' occurs at 1 John 4:8 and 4:16, not in the Gospel of John. It is read within the complete unit's account of the Father's initiative, the Son's mission, the gift of the Spirit, the command to love, and the visible neighbor as ethical test; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0839/_P12E.HTM}{working English witness}.
\end{itemize}}

\subsection*{Authoritative Catholic witnesses}

{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]
\item First Vatican Council, dogmatic constitution \emph{Dei Filius} (24 April 1870), ch.~1 and canon 1.5, DS 3002 and 3025: free creation to manifest divine perfection through goods bestowed rather than to acquire or increase divine beatitude; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/i-vatican-council/documents/vat-i_const_18700424_dei-filius_la.html}{official Latin}.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 1812--1829 (theological virtues, charity, commandment, friendship, form of the virtues, and spiritual freedom); \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_one/article_7/ii_the_theological_virtues.html}{official English}.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 1965--1974, especially 1972 (the New Law as law of love, grace, and freedom); \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_three/article_1/iii_the_new_law_or_the_law_of_the_gospel.html}{official English}.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 1987--1995, especially 1992, on justification through the merit of Christ's Passion; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_three/article_2/i_justification.html}{official English}.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 1996--2005 (grace as gratuitous participation in divine life, free response, and the limits of experiential assurance), \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_three/article_2/ii_grace.html}{official English}; 2095--2097 (charity informing religion, justice toward God, and adoration), \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_two/chapter_one/article_1/ii_him_only_shall_you_serve.html}{official English}.
\item \emph{Catechism of the Catholic Church} 2006--2011, especially 2010, on Christ's prior merit, the impossibility of meriting initial grace, and merit within grace; \sourceurl{https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three/section_one/chapter_three/article_2/iii_merit.html}{official English}.
\end{itemize}}

\subsection*{Thomistic framework}

{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]
\item St.~Thomas Aquinas, \emph{Summa theologiae} I--II, q.~10, a.~4 (divine motion according to the voluntary mode); q.~106, a.~1 (the New Law chiefly as grace of the Holy Spirit); q.~109, aa.~3--4 (healing grace and love of God under fallen nature).
\item Aquinas, ST II--II, q.~19, a.~10 (servile and filial fear); q.~23, aa.~1--3, 5, and 8 (friendship, created charity, virtue, love of God and neighbor, and charity as form); q.~24, a.~2 (infusion); q.~44, aa.~1--5, especially a.~1 ad 2 (the command of love and liberty).
\item Aquinas, ST I, q.~19, aa.~2--4 and q.~20, aa.~1--2 (divine non-need, free willing of creatures, and divine love); q.~37, aa.~1--2 (Love as essential and personal name); q.~43, aa.~1--3 (missions and eternal processions).
\item Latin loci checked in the \sourceurl{https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/iopera.html}{Corpus Thomisticum} Leonine-text corpus; the \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/summa/}{New Advent Summa} was used as a working public English index. Work, question, article, and reply control over either site's wording.

\subsection*{Monastic and mystical witnesses}

{\fontsize{8.6}{9.6}\selectfont
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=0.05em]
\item Pseudo-Dionysius, \emph{Divine Names} IV.13--14, on ecstatic love and return toward the Good. John Parker, trans., \emph{The Works of Dionysius the Areopagite}, vol.~1 (London: James Parker, 1897), \sourceurl{https://ccel.org/ccel/dionysius/works/works.i.ii.iv.html}{public-domain working edition}. The author is identified according to modern convention rather than as the convert of Acts 17.
\item St.~Bernard of Clairvaux, \emph{Sermons on the Song of Songs} 83.3--6, on love returning to its divine source, freedom from servility, and the creature's unequal answer. Latin checked in \emph{Sancti Bernardi Opera}, vol.~2, 300--302, with a \sourceurl{https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Sermones_in_Canticum_Canticorum/Sermo_83}{stable online witness}; Samuel J.~Eales's 1895 English used only as a public-domain aid.
\item St.~John of the Cross, \emph{Spiritual Canticle} B, stanza 38.2--6 and 39.4--6, second redaction, on God giving the capacity for participated equality or conformity in love through the Holy Spirit and on union by participation rather than identity of essence. David Lewis, trans., Benedict Zimmerman, rev. (1909), \sourceurl{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/john_cross/canticle.xliii.html}{stanza 38} and \sourceurl{https://www.ccel.org/ccel/john_cross/canticle.xliv.html}{stanza 39}, public-domain working edition.
\end{itemize}}
