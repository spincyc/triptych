## Rights, uncertainty and disposition

Vatican text is treated under the restricted status of the existing central records: no permission to redistribute source bodies is asserted, and downloaded bytes remain untracked scratch. Article prose paraphrases the controlling passages with only brief quotations. The Dods and Dominican texts use the central records' United States public-domain basis; electronic wording is not silently treated as a facsimile. The brief Romans 14:8 wording agrees with the inspected Douay-Rheims text apart from punctuation; “last enemy” is the conventional phrase also printed in CCC 1008.

Not every biblical cross-reference or Catechism paragraph was freshly collated. This revision does not independently settle biological origins, mechanisms of survival, private revelations, medical cases, or a complete account of merit and martyrdom. Removing the former broad patristic survey was a focus decision, not a negative source finding. Independent doctrinal, medical, pastoral and ecclesiastical review remains unrecorded.
