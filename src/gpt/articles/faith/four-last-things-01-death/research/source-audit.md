## Source roles and limits

The Catechism is used as an authoritative synthesis, not as a substitute for Scripture or definitions. *Benedictus Deus* controls the claim that purified souls enjoy the beatific vision before bodily resurrection. Thomistic language concerning the preternatural preservation from death is common theology; the essay does not make it a biological model. Roman funeral and Viaticum allusions identify a liturgical family without reproducing a territorial vernacular.

The publication-local `source-bindings.toml` identifies the official Catechism edition, binds the exact registered *Gaudium et spes* 16–22 passage with its verified fingerprint, and records Irenaeus only at cataloged reception level. The direct conciliar check controls paragraphs 18 and 22 inside their complete registered context. It does not promote the uncollated patristic loci or every Catechism citation to centrally verified status.
