# Death — Research Scope

- **Publication:** `articles/faith/four-last-things-01-death`
- **Profile:** discursive faith/theology article (`guidance/articles.md`).
- **Series:** first of four independent articles in *The Four Last Things*.
- **Reader:** serious general Catholic reader; no prior scholastic training assumed.
- **Question:** What is human death, how does Christ transform its meaning without making it good, and what does truthful Christian preparation require?
- **Governing claim:** death is the last enemy: a rupture of embodied human unity consequent upon sin, entered and defeated by Christ, and destined to be undone in bodily resurrection.
- **Included:** mortality, sin and bodily corruptibility, Christ's death, soul/body distinction, intermediate state, resurrection, preparation, accompaniment, proportionate care, and grief.
- **Excluded:** a medical decision manual, a full moral theology of end-of-life care, funeral rubrics, private revelations, and judgments about particular deaths or deceased persons.
- **Authority:** Scripture and definitive or authoritative Catholic teaching govern. Patristic, Thomistic, and Roman liturgical witnesses illuminate. The rhetorical movement and pastoral applications are project synthesis.

- **Rights:** only brief scriptural phrases appear; third-party translations, official texts, and liturgical wording retain their own status. No bulk source text is reproduced.
- **Review:** internal source, doctrinal-boundary, rhetoric, and production review only. Independent medical, theological, pastoral, or ecclesiastical review remains outstanding.
