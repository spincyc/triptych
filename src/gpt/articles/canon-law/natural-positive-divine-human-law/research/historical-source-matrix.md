## Interpretation controls

1. “Separation” is disaggregated into office distinction, jurisdictional autonomy, civil disestablishment/special recognition, religious immunity, and alleged moral autonomy of civil law.
2. The Church affirms distinct offices and institutional autonomy; Vatican II requires religious immunity; multiple establishment arrangements remain possible under equal liberty; moral independence from divine/natural law is rejected.
3. Pius IX/Leo XIII to Vatican II is not presented as either flat contradiction or unchanged formula. Continuity and genuine juridical development are both stated.
4. Augustine’s coercion development is retained so *Dignitatis humanae* §12’s historical admission is not decorative.
5. Historical sources are never cited as current law without a current reception rule.
