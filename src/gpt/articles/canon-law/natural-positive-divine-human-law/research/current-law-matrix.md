## Authority, translation, and effect cautions

- Official Latin CIC/CCEO text and prescribed promulgation control.
- Vatican City legislation is ordinarily controlled by official Italian unless an authoritative version provides otherwise.
- A legislative document label, doctrinal approval, executive instruction, authentic interpretation, singular act, and judgment do not have interchangeable effects.
- The same facts can yield different moral, canonical, civil, penal, evidentiary, and remedial conclusions.
- A curial institution is not an autonomous universal legislator; a tribunal judgment is not automatically universal law; conscience is not the canonical internal forum.

## Negative-result statement

The official papal-legislation and code archives, authentic-interpretation register, and identified Curial, Roman, and Vatican sources reviewed through the cutoff disclosed no amendment to the central source and divine-law boundary canons relied on in the article. This is an audit result, not proof that every online index is exhaustive or that unpublished particular or proper law is absent. Prescribed promulgation, applicable particular law, and the complete facts remain controlling.
