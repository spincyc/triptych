## Authority and translation cautions

- Official Latin CIC/CCEO text and prescribed promulgation control.
- Holy See English CIC pages are working translations; CLSA CCEO English is a translation aid.
- Vatican City’s controlling text is ordinarily Italian.
- An authentic interpretation in the form of law has legal force and requires promulgation; judicial/administrative interpretation ordinarily binds the case only.
- A doctrinal declaration, executive instruction, papal approval, and enacted law are not interchangeable forms.
- A curial institution does not become a universal legislator by issuing a document.
- The Holy See, Catholic Church, Roman Curia, and Vatican City State are distinct juridical realities.

## Negative-result statement

The official papal-legislation archive and Dicastery for Legislative Texts register reviewed through the cutoff disclosed no post-2022 amendment to CIC cc. 11, 16, 22, 24, 85, 129, 135, 199, 331, 336, 381, 391, 445–446, 455, 1075, 1141, 1254, 1259, 1290 or CCEO cc. 1490, 1498, 1504, 1506, 1536, 1542. This is an audit result, not proof that every online index is exhaustive. The *Acta Apostolicae Sedis*, prescribed promulgation, and applicable particular law remain controlling.
