\subsection{Rights and evidence boundary}

Scripture, official ecclesiastical and state texts, historical sources, translations, and modern scholarship retain their independent status. This work uses only short, claim-focused phrases and project-created synthesis. Links establish only what the source's author, genre, jurisdiction, date, and transmission permit. No third-party code, translation, article, or monograph is reproduced.
