# Twenty-Four Catholic Churches Sui Iuris — Evidence Matrix

Cutoff: 2026-07-15. “Capacity” means legal possibility under CCEO common law and the 2014 Apostolic norm, subject to each Church’s particular law. It never means that ordinations presently occur. “Not established” means no sufficiently current public authoritative Church-specific source was located in this audit.

Universal Eastern baseline: CCEO 180 no. 3, 373–375, 758 §3, 760 §2, 769 §1 no. 2, 804; Congregation for the Eastern Churches, Pontificia Praecepta de Clero Uxorato Orientali, AAS 106 (2014), 496–499.

## Critical qualification

The 2014 decree’s two Indian exceptions are a legal snapshot whose exact scope must not be extended beyond its text. Syro-Malabar evidence proves a post-2014 married permanent deacon even though married candidates to that Church’s presbyterate remained excepted in the decree. The PDF therefore separates presbyterate and stable diaconate and marks the apparent development rather than “correcting” either source.

Unknown rows may conceal unpublished or non-English particular law. No inference from shared ritual tradition was used to fill them.
