# Current-Law Matrix

As-of date: 2026-07-15. Official Latin controls. “Result” is a general educational proposition, not a case opinion.

## Latin Church

| Canon / act | Subject | Result | Case-sensitive qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CIC 1 | Scope | CIC governs the Latin Church. | Eastern Catholics require CCEO and applicable particular law. |
| 266 §1 | Clerical status | Diaconal ordination makes a man a cleric. | Incardination and institute rules follow the canon’s full provisions. |
| 276 | Holiness | All clerics have a special duty to pursue holiness. | Celibacy is one element in a larger spiritual rule. |
| 277 §§1–3 | Continence/celibacy | Clerics owe perfect/perpetual continence and therefore celibacy; bishop may issue specific norms. | Married permanent deacons: see interpretive record below. |
| 281 §3 | Support | Married permanent deacons serving full time deserve remuneration sufficient for themselves and families; other income matters. | Conference/particular law and actual employment govern calculation. |
| 288 | Permanent-deacon exemptions | Lists canons not binding permanent deacons unless particular law says otherwise. | Does not list can. 277, generating a textual issue. |
| 290–291 | Loss of state | Ordination remains; loss of state does not itself dispense celibacy. | Roman rescript must be read for exact effects. |
| 1031 §§2–3 | Age and wife consent | Minimum 25 unmarried; 35 married with wife consent; conference may raise age. | Particular formation and age norms apply. |
| 1036–1037 | Declaration and celibacy assumption | Every ordinand declares free dedication; presbyteral and unmarried permanent-diaconate candidates publicly assume celibacy. | Perpetually professed religious are treated as the canon states. |
| 1042 no. 1 | Married candidate impediment | Married man impeded unless legitimately destined to permanent diaconate. | Other path requires competent dispensation/special norm. |
| 1050 no. 3 | Documents | Married permanent-diaconate candidate supplies marriage certificate and written wife consent. | Suitability requires more than documents. |
| 1087 | Marriage impediment | Person in sacred orders invalidly attempts marriage. | Dispensation reserved under the law; no private permission suffices. |
| 1394 | Attempted marriage | Cleric incurs automatic suspension; further penalties possible after warning. | Imputability, facts, process, and exact canonical status matter. |
| 1395–1398 | Sexual delicts | Specifies concubinage, public/coercive sexual delicts, and offenses with minors/protected persons. | Not every sin is automatically a delict; civil/safeguarding duties are independent. |

## Eastern Catholic Churches

| Canon / act | Subject | Result | Case-sensitive qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CCEO 1 | Scope | CCEO applies to all and only Eastern Catholic Churches unless relations with the Latin Church are expressly concerned. | Each Church’s particular law also matters. |
| 180 no. 3 | Bishop candidate | Candidate may not be bound by marriage. | A widower is not presently bound by a marriage. |
| 373 | Two states | Celibacy for kingdom is esteemed; married clerical state is honored as sanctioned by primitive and Eastern practice. | Neither is an individual dispensation from the other. |
| 374–375 | Chastity/family | Celibate and married clergy owe chastity; married clergy model marriage and child education. | Particular law provides means. |
| 758 §3 | Married candidates | Admission governed by own Church’s particular law or special Apostolic norms. | Common-law recognition is not current implementation. |
| 760 §2 | Stable deacon | Expressly contemplates a deacon not destined for priesthood. | Common-law capacity does not establish restoration, formation, or actual implementation; applicable particular and formation law must be checked. |
| 769 §1 no. 2 | Wife consent | Married candidate supplies marriage certificate and wife’s written consent. | Other documents and suitability still required. |
| 795–796 | Dispensation | Apostolic See ordinarily reserves dispensation from orders impediment, with narrow danger-of-death provision involving deacons. | Exact person, order, and danger-of-death conditions control. |
| 804 | Marriage impediment | Person in sacred orders invalidly attempts marriage. | No general postordination marriage right exists. |
| 2014 Pontificia Praecepta | Diaspora married clergy | Permits competent Eastern authority to ordain/assign married clergy outside traditional territories under stated coordination. | Particular law remains controlling; 2014 Indian exception language requires later stable-diaconate evidence. |
