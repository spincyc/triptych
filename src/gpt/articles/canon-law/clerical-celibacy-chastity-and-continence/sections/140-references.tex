\subsection{Copyright and evidence boundary}

Scripture, official ecclesiastical texts, historical sources, and modern third-party translations retain their own status. Patristic and conciliar wording is paraphrased except for brief, claim-focused phrases; modern scholarship is summarized and attributed. Church-specific pages establish only the identified jurisdiction, program, person, or date, and the research records preserve the appendix audit.
