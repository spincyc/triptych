\section{What the Bound Reception Witnesses Establish}

Augustine treats the countlessness of the stars and allegorizes the sunset and
darkness in the covenant vision, but does not ask the exact daylight question
at the two bound chapters. Josephus, in Whiston I.10.3, retains the
posterity-like-stars comparison and commanded sacrifice while omitting the
command to go outside and look and all the temporal markers that produce the
question.

These are findings about exact loci, not a corpus search. No author-wide or
tradition-wide conclusion about silence or controversy follows from them.
