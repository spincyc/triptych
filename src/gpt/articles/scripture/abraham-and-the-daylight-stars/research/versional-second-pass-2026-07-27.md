# Abraham and the Daylight Stars — versional second pass

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Purpose:** recheck every versional claim narrowed during the evidence repair
before treating absence of a binding as grounds for removal.

## Results

- **Swete:** printed pages 23–24 were directly compared with the registered
  checked transcription. Genesis 15:5, 12, 17–18 agree at every controlling
  locus: outside/look/count; the sun toward setting at verse 12; the sun toward
  setting and a flame at verse 17; and spatial `ἐκεῖ` at verse 18. The
  apparatus records a verse-17 verb-form variant in one witness but no
  verse-18 variant affecting `ἐκεῖ`. The article may describe Swete's
  identified base text, not “the Septuagint” generally. A reusable page-image
  record remains to be normalized.
- **Augustine:** CSEL 40 directly establishes Augustine's Latin wording,
  including *in illo die*. No locus-specific evidence inspected in this pass
  identifies his Greek exemplar. His citation is contrary indirect versional
  evidence and does not prove variation in Greek transmission.
- **Peshitta:** the dated peshitta.eu delivery remains an inspected witness
  with an unidentified textual base. The critical edition expected for a
  tradition-level claim is P. A. H. de Boer, ed., *Vetus Testamentum Syriace*
  I.1 (Leiden: Brill, 1977); it was identified but not collated at these loci.
- **Jubilees:** Charles's 1913 translation, chapter 14, supports the bounded
  statement that rewritten Scripture separates the star promise from the later
  sacrifice and reports sunset later without stating the hour of the star
  sight. It is not versional evidence. Original-language control would require
  an identified critical Ethiopic edition, such as VanderKam's CSCO 510–511.
- **Targum Neofiti:** McNamara, *Targum Neofiti 1: Genesis* (Aramaic Bible 1A,
  1992), was identified as the expected exact edition; the full locus was not
  directly collated.
- **Samaritan Pentateuch:** von Gall's *Der hebräische Pentateuch der
  Samaritaner*, volume I, was identified as the expected edition; the locus was
  not directly collated.
- **Genesis Apocryphon:** the surviving text ends near the beginning of the
  Genesis 15 material and does not preserve the star command or the later
  sunset/covenant clock. It is structurally unavailable for this question, not
  a witness to either chronology.

## Disposition

The Swete base-text comparison, Augustine limit, delivery-specific Syriac
comparison, and rewritten-Scripture treatment of Jubilees may remain. No claim
about Greek transmission, the Peshitta tradition, Neofiti, or the Samaritan
Pentateuch is established by this pass. The Genesis Apocryphon should be
recorded as unavailable rather than as an unresolved witness expected to
answer the question.
