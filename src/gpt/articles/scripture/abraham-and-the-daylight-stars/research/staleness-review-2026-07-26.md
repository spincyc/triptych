## Substantive disagreement

The two candidates agree on every consequential verdict. They differ in
architecture and emphasis: the modified candidate preserves a fuller witness
register and discrete reception subsections; the rewrite makes the distinction
between textual observation, historical reconstruction, and theological
application its organizing principle. Their disagreement is rhetorical, not
doctrinal, historical, linguistic, or pastoral.

Both candidates materially improve the original compact held draft by adding
versional controls, claim-by-claim evidence limits, astronomy, expanded Jewish
reception, canonical non-retrojection, and explicit pastoral safeguards. The
modified candidate is preferable because it retains the fuller audit surface
without contradicting the rewrite.
