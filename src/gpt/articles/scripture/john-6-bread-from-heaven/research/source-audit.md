## Consequential negative results

- No controlled witness in this preliminary set makes the **swaddling cloths
  themselves** Eucharistic linens.
- Jerome and Ambrose establish a Bethlehem/Bread-from-heaven conjunction, but
  neither checked locus proves that the Hebrew place-name was historically
  coined as prophecy.
- Chrysostom establishes continuity of the manger-body and Eucharistic Body,
  but the checked locus does not call the manger an altar.
- The exact popular sentence often attributed online to Cyril of Alexandria
  (“not hay, but His own Body as life-giving bread”) was not found in the
  controlled Cyril witnesses. It remains an unverified attribution and is
  excluded.
- No claim is made that the Greek verb *trōgein* exclusively means “gnaw.”
- No manuscript evidence has yet been controlled for the modern hypothesis
  that John 6:51c–58 is a later redactional insertion; the hypothesis is
  reported only as a compositional proposal.
