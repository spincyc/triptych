## Other deleted references

- The adjective “Origenian” was not attached to a named Origen locus in the
  earlier article. Searches of the local Origen records found no John 6
  passage control. Its removal remains warranted; it was not a material
  argument requiring recovery by conjecture.
- Augustine's *Sermon* 80 appeared only in the bibliography, while the
  published synthesis used *Tractates on John* 25--27. Removing an unused
  reference is a citation correction, not evidentiary narrowing.
- The larger Catechism summary and the expanded Aquinas paragraph were
  replaced by exact, narrower loci. The doctrinal conclusions were retained,
  so these are source-boundary revisions rather than missing-evidence
  removals.
- Claims that no patristic witness makes the swaddling cloths Eucharistic were
  narrowed to the five exact witnesses actually searched. This is the correct
  bounded-negative form. A universal patristic search would be disproportionate
  unless the article again proposes a universal negative.

## Change warranted by this pass

Cyprian 18 and the Jerusalem Mystagogical Catechesis IV are genuine,
material witnesses that were removed only because reusable exact controls were
not yet normalized. They should be eligible for restoration once their exact
artifacts, passage records, rights status, and local bindings are complete.
The Septuagint echo and Hebrew gloss are also recoverable in bounded language,
but require exact edition controls before publication. No main-text change was
made in this pass.
