\weektitle{First Week After Pentecost}{In Festo Ssmae Trinitatis}{1962 TLM: Trinity Sunday; ferias may use the Mass of the First Sunday after Pentecost}

\rubric{Source note.} In the 1962 Missale Romanum the Sunday after Pentecost is the feast of the Most Holy Trinity. The ferial Mass of the First Sunday after Pentecost begins \latin{Domine, in tua misericordia speravi} and gathers the same post-Pentecost movement into mercy, charity, and judgment.

\begin{properstable}
\properrow{Introit}{\latin{Benedicta sit sancta Trinitas} (Tob. 12:6; Ps. 8)}{Praise and mercy}{St. Augustine, \sourceurl{https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/1301.htm}{De Trinitate}; St. Gregory Nazianzen, theological orations}{The Church begins ordinary growth after Pentecost by adoring the source of all mission: one God, three Persons.}
\properrow{Collect}{Confession of true faith: know Trinity, adore unity}{Faith seeking worship}{St. Athanasius on Nicene confession; St. Thomas Aquinas, Summa Theologiae I, qq. 27-43}{Doctrine is not abstract here; right confession becomes protection from adversity.}
\properrow{Epistle}{Rom. 11:33-36}{Incomprehensible wisdom}{St. John Chrysostom on Romans; Augustine on divine simplicity}{The apostle ends in doxology where reason reaches its limit: \latin{ex ipso, per ipsum, in ipso}.}
\properrow{Gospel}{Matt. 28:18-20}{Baptismal name and mission}{St. Hilary of Poitiers on Matthew; St. Leo the Great on Pentecost and mission}{The Trinitarian name is the form of Christian rebirth and the grammar of evangelization.}
\properrow{Offertory}{\latin{Benedictus sit Deus Pater... Filius... Spiritus}}{Mercy revealed}{St. Bonaventure, Itinerarium; St. Elizabeth of the Trinity}{The offering is praise returned to the God whose mercy first descended.}
\properrow{Communion}{\latin{Benedicimus Deum caeli}}{Public confession}{St. Basil, On the Holy Spirit}{Communion sends the worshipper from hidden mystery to public thanksgiving.}
\properrow{Ferial First Sunday}{1 John 4:8-21; Luke 6:36-42}{Charity and mercy}{Augustine on 1 John; St. Benedict, Rule ch. 4}{The ferial Mass unfolds Trinity into moral likeness: mercy because the Father is merciful.}
\end{properstable}

\begin{connectionstable}
Trinity and mission & Pentecost gives the Spirit; Trinity Sunday names the divine life from which that gift proceeds. The Mass moves from confession to mission: the Church baptizes because she has first adored.\\
Mercy and doctrine & The ferial Gospel against judgment is not detached moralism. It is the ethical shape of Trinitarian faith: charity mirrors the God who first loved.\\
Prayer focus & Ask for firm faith that becomes merciful action, especially in speech, judgment, and fraternal correction.
\end{connectionstable}
