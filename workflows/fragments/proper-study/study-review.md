# Review the expansive study

Read the complete canonical study and its exact source records. Audit the
argument and evidence for each interpretation, not merely declarations in its
manifest. Require two to five distinguishable and internally coherent readings,
each engaging the entire appointed formulary and carrying its own four senses.
The moral sense cannot substitute for the literal or anagogical sense. The
interpretations must explain checked witnesses' reasoning rather than collect
names, isolated quotations, or generic spiritual themes. Each interpretation
must develop substantive, compatible contributions from at least two distinct
Fathers or saints; a second name without contributing reasoning does not count.

Check substantial depth, meaningful treatment of every element, accurate
attribution and usable citations, honest source roles, actual agreement and
disagreement, doctrinal precision, and faithful scope. No Father's isolated
exegesis may be represented as his synthesis of the Mass. Complementary
emphases are valid distinct interpretations when the study demonstrates their
different movement or consequence. Historic compositional intent needs evidence.

Judge clarity and reader value as well as correctness. No artificial word or
page quota, gallery, proposal appendix, or global four-senses table can satisfy
this contract. Use `STU-` findings: `research` for missing or false evidence;
`study` for how reviewed evidence is selected, reasoned through or written.
