# Independently inspect every page of selected print exports

This stage applies only to an explicitly requested print export. Ordinary
source/HTML publication has its own source and web reviews and does not claim
print approval. Read the exact print-export receipt supplied by the packet,
open the generated contact sheets, and inspect every page of every selected
PDF, opening full-size rasters wherever needed. A contact
sheet or successful renderer alone is not review. Check legible type and
measure, consistent hierarchy, meaningful page breaks, reasonable density,
table and footnote integrity, clipped or overlapping content, sparse spills,
running furniture, embedded fonts, and the final compact rights colophon.
Verify the shared Latin Modern, 11-point article, 0.75-inch-margin monochrome
baseline, consistent keyed lane headings and four-senses blocks. The homily
has a full-width title, two-column speech and separate full-width terminal
apparatus; neither study has columns. A formatting migration also requires
substantive-text preservation evidence, not a claim based on file counts.

Judge each artifact for its use: navigation and substantial exposition in the
study, visible interleaving in the concise work, comfortable reading and
recoverable paragraphs in the homily. An exported study must occupy 20–50
physical pages and an exported concise work 10–12. Inspect the latter's restored
opening directly:
complete map and exactly four senses rows on page 1; readable, complete
chronology alone on page 2; substantive Themes and Movement on pages 3–4;
developed interleaved commentary beginning on page 5. Compare settled physical
marker evidence with the actual pages. No padding, unreadable reduction or
logical page-counter trick meets this contract. Beauty does not require images.
Ensure the substantive pages actually contain the accepted argument and that
source notes are present without intruding into the spoken homily.
Perform the profile's passage-to-occurrence/place/date lookup at normal reading
size. Record a concrete example and outcome. The table must make the pairing
visible without reading a paragraph down another column; clipping-free fit and
correct physical-page markers do not establish that usability.

Use `VIS-` findings. Regenerating disposable outputs from unchanged approved
sources belongs to `artifacts`. A repair requiring TeX, component or style
changes belongs to the earliest affected source owner: `study`, `synthesis`,
or `homily`. Shared format-only changes require print-template validation under
`guidance/document-schemas.md`, followed by fresh exact-artifact review of the
selected exports. Evidence defects belong to `research`; a source/evidence
change reopens its affected review closure. A formatting change alone does not
restamp or reopen unrelated research acceptance.
Name every reviewed artifact, its receipt hash and page coverage in the result.
Return PASS only after actual full-page inspection. Do not edit the artifacts.
