# Lane: Fixed Pagination

## Your lane

You own criterion **8 (Illegibly compressed fixed-pagination sections)** of
the shared criteria list above, and nothing else. Another lane owns each of
the remaining criteria; do not report on them, and do not judge the artifacts
as a whole.

Follow the shared inspection method: open every full-size page raster of both
the canonical and the synthesis PDF, and answer only:

8. Is the Scriptural Date and Location page (page 2), or the two-page Themes
   and Movement section, compressed to the point of illegibility?

The mechanical gates check that the brief synthesis occupies exactly two
pages. You judge whether the material on the fixed pages remains legible, not
whether the page count is right.

## Result

Return an evaluator result for this lane. `PASS` when the fixed-pagination
sections are legible, `CHANGES_REQUIRED` with blocking findings when they are
not, `BLOCKED` when the defect cannot be resolved by revision.

Finding IDs must use the `VIS-FIX-` prefix and be stable across iterations.
