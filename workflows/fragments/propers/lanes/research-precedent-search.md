# Lane: Precedent Search

## Your lane

You own research into Triptych precedent and comparable prior work, and
nothing else. Another lane owns Scriptural context, patristic reception,
liturgical history, theological synthesis, source coverage, and cultural
afterlife; do not report on theirs. You are read-only, as the shared
fragment above states: your only product is your returned result.

Search and collate:

- neighbouring proper guides, and the treatments they already give to the
  appointed elements, texts, or witnesses in hand
- prior synthesis structures that worked, and the shape that made them work
- analogous treatments elsewhere in the repository, and existing patterns
  worth reusing
- precedent that reveals an omission this proper would otherwise repeat, or
  a useful form it has not considered

You also own the targeted precedent search behind exploratory proposals.
For each distinctive conjunction the appointed elements actually invite,
search the guide's checked corpus and any comparable prior treatment, and
report the nearest located precedent or analogue with the boundary of the
search, classifying the novelty result as `precedent located`, `near
analogue located`, or `not located in the checked corpus`. Never claim that
a connection is universally unknown, unprecedented, or first; the last
formula is bounded and correctable.

Be honest about the ordering. This lane runs before `research-synthesis`
settles which proposals are retained, so you cannot search per retained
proposal. Search instead the precedent field the appointed elements invite,
so that synthesis holds the precedent evidence when it settles them, and
say in `notes` which conjunction each search covered and what it did not
reach.

That makes your coverage the limit on what can be published: synthesis may
retain no proposal whose conjunction you did not reach. It needs four to six,
so cover at least six distinct conjunctions worth proposing. If you cannot,
record the shortfall as a finding naming the conjunctions you could not
reach and why.

This is research into precedent, not authority to copy prior prose. Name in
`evidence` what a prior guide did and where; leave to a later worker what
this proper will do with it.

## Result

Return a research result for this lane, per the shared contract above,
carrying the `lane` and `lane_packet_hash` that `common/result-format.md`
explains. `PASS` when your search completed, `BLOCKED` when something
stopped it.

Finding IDs must use the `PRE-` prefix and be stable across iterations.
