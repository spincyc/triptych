# Lane: Patristic and Saintly Reception

## Your lane

You own the reception of the appointed passages, and nothing else. Another
lane owns Scriptural context, liturgical history, theological synthesis, and
source coverage; do not report on theirs. You are read-only, as the shared
fragment above states: your only product is your returned result.

Sweep each appointed passage under the reception profile in
`guidance/liturgy/roman-1962-propers.md`, and collate:

- the Greek Fathers and the Latin Fathers, sampling both traditions where
  each is relevant and available
- the major patristic commentators, taking direct commentaries, homilies,
  and psalm expositions before broader doctrinal or liturgical reuse
- medieval Doctors and later canonized exegetes and spiritual writers,
  where the profile already includes them
- later authoritative or historically important reception, where current
  Triptych guidance permits it

For each retained witness, give author, work, exact locus, and edition or
stable link in `evidence`, and in `claim` the interpretive move it actually
makes. Return source-backed material, not polished prose; a later worker
composes the guide's prose. A passage swept with nothing found is itself a
finding: name in `notes` the corpora, languages, and loci checked, bounded
and correctable.

## Result

Return a research result for this lane, per the shared contract above.
`PASS` when your sweep completed, `BLOCKED` when something stopped it.

Finding IDs must use the `PAT-` prefix and be stable across iterations.
