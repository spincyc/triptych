# Research / Synthesis Work

## Your task

Conduct the research and synthesis work for the proper guide. This is the
core scholarly work that produces the commentary, themes, and synthesis.

## Steps

1. Read each appointed text in full and read each direct biblical passage in
   its complete literary context.
2. Conduct a broad and deep patristic and saintly reception sweep for each
   appointed passage, following the profile in
   `guidance/liturgy/roman-1962-propers.md`.
3. Maintain a passage-by-passage reception matrix in `research/scope.md`.
4. Keep verified source text, checked quotation or paraphrase, source-grounded
   synthesis, original editorial or AI proposal, and unverified leads
   distinct.
5. Preserve material disagreement, uncertainty, jurisdiction, and
   currentness rather than silently harmonizing sources.
6. Develop the cross-proper synthesis: a redistilled argument whose
   functional units each draw together multiple ritual moments, scriptural
   contexts, and reception witnesses. This is not an abridged procession
   through the propers.
7. Identify 3-6 cross-proper claims for the synthesis commentary.
8. Develop 4-6 exploratory proposals for the Interpretive Possibilities
   section, each joining at least 2 elements.

## Result

Return a worker result with `disposition: "PASS"` and a summary of the
research, including:
- which passages were searched
- which witnesses were retained
- material negative results
- the cross-proper claims identified
- the exploratory proposals developed
