## Archive identities and boundaries

`ARCHIVE-MANIFEST.json` identifies every retained native engine record and
selected auxiliary item by source path, archive path, byte size and SHA-256.
Native bootstrap, manifest, state, events, packets, results, gate logs,
interventions and embedded seals are byte-exact. Their blank-at-EOF gate-log
lines remain untouched. No source payload, facsimile, PDF, page raster,
browser screenshot, renderer cache or full process-log sweep is included.

Two manifest entries are explicit public derivatives rather than copies of
their original operational records:

- `research-review-0001/review-checks.public.json` replaces only a numeric native
  exec-session identifier with an omission marker. The original local hash and
  exact transformation are recorded; the native review result is unchanged.
- `build-artifacts-0000/raster-evidence.sanitized.json` retains document
  identities, PDF hashes and page counts from the original helper receipt,
  omits renderer paths and runtime inventory, and adds full-page raster hashes.
  The original receipt and transformation are identified explicitly.

Other selected auxiliary files are copied byte-exact from their named public
reports. Some reports themselves describe normalized measurements or display
derivatives; their provenance and limits are part of those reports. This does
not convert them into native engine verdicts. The visual identity report binds
its normalized measurements to the original local helper-receipt hashes;
those machine-specific receipts remain operational. The conversion blocker
copies its actual diagnostic and records the native log hash.

Coordinator observations and integrity checks are supplementary evidence,
never substitutes for cold reviewer judgments or engine transitions. The
research checkpoint previously retained here has been extended append-only in
substance: immutable prior records retain their bytes, while native state and
the append-only event stream now reflect the actual terminal outcome.
