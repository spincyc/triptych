# 04-themes.tex: sources and fill

Word count: 1,407 words of prose (1,441 with the section and four subsection
headings; comments and `\runninghead` excluded).

Fill check: `test.tex` (preamble + propers-format, page counter set to 3)
typesets the section on exactly two pages, `test.pdf`. Page 3 is full. Page 4
ends about four body lines short of the bottom; in the test build the
end-of-document rights notice fills that space.

Structure: a two-sentence thesis, then four subsections in the order of the
Mass (Introit–Collect–Epistle; Gradual–Alleluia–Gospel; Offertory–Secret;
Preface–Communion–Postcommunion). Together they cover all eleven texts said on
the day and take up the study's three questions where the Mass reaches them:
what the Lamb receives (Introit, then again at the Offertory), where the
kingdom is (Gospel), and how it ends (Preface, Communion).
