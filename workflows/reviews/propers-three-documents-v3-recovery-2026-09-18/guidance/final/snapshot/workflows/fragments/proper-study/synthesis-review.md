# Review the concise study

Read the concise study independently, then compare it to the accepted
expansive study and exact cited evidence. Does its own reader understand the
alternatives? Do authors and readings meet within each relevant question, or
are they merely recapped in successive blocks? Are all controlling claims
present, material differences preserved, and complementary readings described
without theatrical conflict? Did compression silently upgrade evidence or
remove a qualification? Are its length and apparatus truly concise?

Require approximately 10–12 physical pages with the established first four:
the complete appointed map followed by exactly four senses overview rows on
page 1; the sole Scriptural Date and Location dossier on page 2; two complete,
substantive pages of The Propers: Themes and Movement on pages 3–4; developed
interleaved commentary beginning on page 5. Check the actual material and
settled physical-page evidence, not just labels or declared roles. Every
appointed option remains recognizable. The overview cannot replace the full
study's individual four-sense distillations. Verify canonical chronology and
claim-local qualifications, and reject padding or compressed unreadable tables.

Check that it introduces no new source-dependent claim and that the four-sense
distinctions remain clear where they affect the argument. Judge it as a useful
finished document, never as a teaser that requires the study to be understood.

actual owner. An upstream repair reruns this derivation and its cold review.
