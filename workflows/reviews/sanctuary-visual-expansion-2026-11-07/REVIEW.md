# Sanctuary dictionary and Mass guides — visual expansion review

Accept the exact candidates identified below for integration. No unresolved
visual or diagram-to-prose consistency blocker remains in the inspected scope.
These are worktree candidates on `feature/altar-server`, based on
`589da93da7c9b8c5bd9b84a33429613c45831d2a`, with uncommitted-source revision
stamps. This receipt does not claim a post-commit rebuild or live deployment.

The independent review lane read the applicable pictorial-dictionary and
server-training profiles, new object records, and shared scene/route sources;
inspected the new isolated drawings and both context drawings; and reviewed
actual print rasters and browser captures. It did not edit publication content
or implement the renderer. Source records were checked for agreement with the
depictions, but primary-source passages were not independently recollated in
this review. Source approval and visual approval remain separate facts.

The dictionary now has 87 canonical records. The website presents 69 primary
rows, 71 depicted identities and 77 logical drawings. The five added identities
are altar frontal, altar cards, biretta, cope and humeral veil. The expanded
panels expose stable IDs, alternative names, location, use, handlers,
confusables, related objects, appearance and handling boundaries. The frontal
and humeral veil have additional context drawings so their identifying
relationships are visible. Their captions explicitly limit them to a placement
study and a shoulder-and-hand study.

The guide position diagrams share one sanctuary background and three numbered
levels. The new movement panels make starts, routes, waiting places, facing,
object transfers and finish states visible. Neutral actor figures identify
stations; retained hand-action close-ups show the actions that those station
figures cannot depict. Retained formation and hand studies do not introduce a
competing altar architecture.

Findings raised and resolved during review include:

- Ambiguous isolated frontal and humeral-veil rectangles gained context views
  and explicit scope captions.
- Missal, priest, credence, lane and role labels formerly obscured by actors or
  other labels were moved into readable foreground positions.
- Side-by-side cruet/Lavabo stations gained plan insets; the book/veil crossing
  now names a waiting point and which server passes first, in both prose and
  caption. The arrival frame no longer prematurely places the covered chalice.
- Low-Mass continuation pages gained proper headings and deliberate breaks;
  child/trainer pagination is paired at 33 pages. The Solemn stationary Gospel
  diagram and its facing/role caption now stay together.
- The dictionary MC furnishing row overlap and comprehensive rights-only
  final-page spill were corrected and rechecked by the assigned reviewers.

Every page of the ten selected PDFs was inspected individually at readable
full-page size, 245 pages in total. This coverage is distributed, not a claim
that one reviewer inspected all ten editions:

- Independent review lane: Solemn Mass 35 pages, altar-server dictionary 14,
  and pontifical dictionary 21. Solemn pages 11 and 17–35 were rechecked after
  the caption correction; pages 1–10 and 12–16 matched already inspected PNG
  bytes exactly. Dictionary body pages 1–11 and 1–18 respectively matched the
  prior inspected rasters byte for byte; all six changed apparatus pages were
  inspected at original 2200-pixel resolution. The reviewer independently
  verified those comparisons and final PDF hashes.
- Integration reviewer: Missa Cantata 39 pages and comprehensive dictionary 22.
  The guide owner subsequently verified exact PNG identity for Cantata pages
  1–38 and inspected its revised timestamp-only final page.
- Guide owner: Low Mass 33 pages and trainer manual 33, including the final
  timestamp-only pages after exact raster comparison of the remaining pages.
- Dictionary owner: MC/trainer 15 pages and sacristan 17; unchanged body rasters
  were compared exactly and all changed apparatus pages inspected individually.
- Dictionary-reader owner: general-reader 16 pages; body pages 1–13 matched
  prior inspected PNGs exactly and final pages 14–16 were inspected individually.

The independent lane inspected all five added website entries in expanded
form, both final context panels, the final 1440-pixel table, both sides of the
320-pixel table, and the mobile catalogue facts at 200% text size. The final
reader-owner Chromium report records 58 passing checks covering semantics,
anchors, artwork loading, search, sorting, keyboard/focus behavior, narrow
layouts, disclosures and no-JavaScript fallback, with no HTTP, console or
runtime errors. That automation is attributed evidence, not an independently
executed browser run by this reviewer.

The approval is bounded to these depictions, layouts and internal consistency.
It is not a new liturgical authorization, a complete re-audit of inherited
source claims, a physical print test, or an approval of every local sanctuary
arrangement. Generated drawings are teaching illustrations, not source
evidence. Source, artwork and build dispositions remain in the owning
dictionary and guide research records; release gates remain with integration.
