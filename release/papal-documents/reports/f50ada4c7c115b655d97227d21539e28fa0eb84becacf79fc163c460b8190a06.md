## Scope and method

Every assigned page PNG from the repository `pdf-review` run (1700x2200 rasters) was opened as its own full-size image before its observation was recorded in the lane ledger `observations.jsonl` (one line per page). No contact sheet, thumbnail, rerasterization, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was performed.

Each page was checked against the frozen metadata for title line, responsible issuer, Latin label and omission of the unresolved act date; for headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins, glyphs, clipping and overlap; for natural source closing; for modern digitizer signatures and raw placeholders; and, per the series addendum, for (a) marginal summaries as separate TAB-led blocks, (b) page-foot notes as separate blocks, (c) natural two-column order, (d) running heads, folios and signature marks left inline, (e) fragment presentation and (f) non-word OCR noise. Where a garbled token sat next to a marginal block, the volume's raw OCR (`artifacts/ocr-4ae46d1884f3/ocr.txt`) was read to see whether it was marginal or body text; the loci are listed under each document.
