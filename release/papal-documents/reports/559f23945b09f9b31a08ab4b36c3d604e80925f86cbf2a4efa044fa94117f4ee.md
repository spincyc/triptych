# Pius IX and Benedict XV publication-witness review

Reviewed 2026-10-26.

The bounded sweep covers the 28 Pius IX and 47 Benedict XV Latin-delivery candidate rows selected from the initial Vatican catalog. Every selected row has a disposition: **71 original Latin expressions and two historical Latin translations pass the initial publication-identity and rights admission check; the combined Quanta cura/Syllabus delivery is now resolved into two additional constituent editions, and the duplicate Divina delivery supplies no second payload.** The 73 initial admissions and two additional constituent editions remain distinct from PDF installation and publication.

The source bodies were acquired mechanically in full by the acquisition pipeline. This review independently acquired and hashed 14 complete official ASS/AAS OCR PDFs (43,156,398 bytes total), located each act at its historical locus, and checked the opening and closing passages, dating clause/signature, and reference apparatus against the delivery. All 73 admitted raw HTML and final normalized text hashes were replayed with the current extractor and `validate_approval`. No papal wording was generated, translated, silently corrected, or reconstructed by a model.

The official `-ocr.pdf` files are treated as **OCR reproductions**, not as facsimiles. Inspection of ASS 8 found an ABBYY 2009 reconstruction using digital Times fonts; its page images show OCR errors. Agreement between Vatican HTML and that PDF is therefore not independent full verbal collation. The admitted derivatives retain source spelling, punctuation, page-number intrusions, reference numbering, and other transcription defects, with that limitation in their source records. No full-work image collation is claimed.

Original works, Latin translations, and modern apparatus remain distinct. The exact modern source bibliography, Denzinger 2012 references, wrapper copyright notice, separator lines, and title asterisk markers are excluded only through hash-pinned transformations. Historical internal references and text remain. The governing United States rights position is [the collection rights record](../inventories/papal-documents-rights-v1.toml); no worldwide status or general Vatican license is asserted.

## Material findings

- **Historical translations:** *Ecclesia Dei* (2 March 1871), ASS 6, pp.264–268, and *In luctuosissimis* (16 June 1872), ASS 7, pp.5–24, are expressly headed `VERSIO` in the historical printing’s Latin apparatus beside Italian text. Their approvals use `original_language = false`, `expression_kind = historical-latin-translation`, and an identified historical translation basis.
- **Composite delivery:** the *Quanta cura* page includes the separate *Syllabus errorum*. ASS 3 (1867), pp.160–167, explicitly abbreviates Quanta's terminal Jubilee wording and cannot establish the complete delivery. The full *Pii IX Acta*, pars prima, volume III, printing independently cataloged as 1864, supplies Quanta at printed pp.687–700 and the Syllabus at pp.701–717. The complete modern delivery now has one container owner and one normalized text artifact; Quanta and Syllabus have separate constituent editions and exact physical-line segments. It is not counted as one papal act. See the resolution below.
- **Duplicate delivery:** the apostolic-letter and bull URLs for *Divina disponente* (16 May 1920) contain the same normalized Latin body, SHA-256 `9a1a88f905ec8ee0154ab3bc1ee93147e028b2d71dbb4285aa95a23ae298ece6`, and the same AAS 12, pp.514–529, witness. Only the apostolic-letter representative is admitted; the bull delivery is an identity alias requiring catalog integration.
- **Wrong journal abbreviation:** the first four Pius IX pages and *Quanta cura* cite `AAS` for nineteenth-century ASS volumes. The correct journal is recorded from the actual official historical-volume witness; the modern erroneous bibliography is excluded.
- **Wrong volume/year:** *In hac tanta* matches AAS 11 (1919), pp.209–221, including the 14 May 1919 signature, rather than its web note’s AAS 10 (1918). *In luctuosissimis* belongs to ASS 7 (1872–1873), not the web note’s 1892–1893.
- **Wrong page numbers:** *Graves ac diuturnae* is ASS 8 (1874–1875), pp.369–373. Vatican OCR misreads several page numbers as 569/570/572/575, and its modern bibliography gives 570–575. Independent [BSB facsimile](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb11184954) opening/ending images confirm printed 369 and 373 and the 23 March 1875 signature. The complete IIIF manifest and the two complete page-image objects were acquired and hashed. The manifest exposes individual page images; no whole-volume image download or complete collation is claimed. No BSB images are proposed for redistribution.
- **Act-date discrepancy:** *Supremi apostolatus* has XVII April 1920 in the HTML dating clause and matching historical AAS 12, p.431 (OCR `xvit`), while its catalog index gives 16 April. The preceding act ends XVI on p.430. This supports a documented 17 April correction while preserving the index variant.
- **Preserved source defect:** *Nobis supremum* reads `MCMIX` in both the HTML and historical-volume OCR, despite the 1919 context and pontificate year six. The derivative preserves this wording. It is not silently redated within the text.
