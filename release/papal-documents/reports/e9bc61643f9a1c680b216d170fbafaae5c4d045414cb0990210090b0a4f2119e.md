# Pius IX Acta visual review — pius-0001 lane 06

CHANGES_REQUIRED: 25 assigned PDFs, 67 pages individually opened at full size; 22 PASS, 3 CHANGES_REQUIRED. All are new inspections with zero reused pages. All documents are Latin readers from *Pii IX Pontificis Maximi Acta*, pars prima, volume III.

## Scope and method

Each assigned page PNG (produced by the repository's `pdf-review` tool; not re-rasterized) was opened individually with the Read tool at full size, one image per page. No contact sheet, thumbnail, new raster pass, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was used. Mechanical text extraction (`pdftotext`) was used only as a secondary aid to locate stray lines and repeated passages; every finding was confirmed on the page image.

On each page the displayed title, responsible issuer, act date and Latin label were compared with the frozen metadata, and headings, two-column flow, gutter, margins, glyphs, natural source closing, edition notes, provider signatures, running heads/folios and raw placeholders were inspected under the brief and its standing coordinator rulings.
