# Bullarium Romanum vol. VII reading-order visual review 03

**18 PASS, 5 CHANGES_REQUIRED** of 23 assigned PDFs; 59 pages individually opened at full raster size, zero reused. Every CHANGES_REQUIRED finding is a printed page-foot footnote emitted inside a body paragraph (addendum check (b)); no marginal-summary fusion was found. Scope: lane 03 of review batch `bullarium-ro-0002`, coordinate-reconstructed (whole-volume reading-order derivative `reading-order-c906c8a9a6ff`) readers of the Turin 1862 Bullarium Romanum, volume VII (Pius V).

## Scope and method

Each assigned PNG produced by the repository `pdf-review` run (1700x2200 raster) was opened as its own full-size image with the Read tool before any observation was recorded; one JSON observation per page was then appended to the lane ledger `observations.jsonl`. No contact sheet, thumbnail, new rasterization, source acquisition, word-level collation, text correction or date reconstruction was performed.

Each page was checked for the displayed title, responsible issuer, Latin label and omission of the unresolved act date against the frozen metadata; headings, two-column flow and gutter, margins, glyphs, clipping/overlap; natural source closing; modern digitizer signatures; raw placeholders; and, per the series addendum, (a) marginal summaries as separate TAB-led blocks never fused into a body sentence or hyphen-split word, (b) page-foot footnotes as separate blocks at the page break and never run into a body, synopsis or protocol paragraph, (c) natural two-column order, (d) inline running heads, folios and signature marks and misplaced-but-separate marginal blocks (qualifications), and (e) fragment presentation.

Mechanical support (read-only, lane-local scripts `scan.py`, `notescan.py`, `markscan.py`, `texcheck.py`): each document's manifest line range of the derivative `hybrid.txt` was split into blank-line paragraphs. It contains **281 TAB-led marginal paragraphs**; no paragraph mixes TAB-led and body lines, and each generated body TeX has exactly one `\par` paragraph per derivative paragraph, so every marginal block is its own TeX paragraph. Every note-like line (leading note marker, `(r. t.)`/`(n. t.)` editorial sigla, `Cherub.`, `Regest.`, `tom./pag.` citations) was located and classified by whether it shares a paragraph with body text; the five fused cases below were confirmed in the generated body TeX and on the page images. No `Missing character` entry occurs in any lane PDF log; no `Google`/`Googie`/`Digiti` string and no bare `None` occurs in any lane body TeX. This supports, but does not replace, the visual review.

## Limitations

This is literal, uncorrected, uncollated historical OCR rendered as a mechanical two-column source edition. The review is a layout review only: it makes no word-level collation against the printing or scan, no historical authentication, no date reconstruction, and no publication or installation claim. Wording, spellings, OCR noise and the source's conflicting or damaged dates were not judged or repaired. Mechanical note detection relies on note-marker patterns and may miss a note without a recognizable marker; the visual pass is the controlling evidence.
