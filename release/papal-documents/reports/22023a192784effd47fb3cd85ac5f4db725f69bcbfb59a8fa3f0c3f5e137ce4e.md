# Bullarium reading-order visual review 06 — batch bullarium-ro-0002

PASS: 24 assigned PDFs, 62 pages individually opened at full size; zero blocking findings. All are new inspections with zero reused pages. This report covers only lane 6 of batch `bullarium-ro-0002` (16 complete printed acts from Bullarium Romanum volume 9 and 8 historical fragments from volumes 4, 7 and 9), all coordinate-reconstructed reading-order readers.

## Scope and method

Each page PNG listed in the lane assignment was opened as its own full-size image with the Read tool (1700x2200 rasters, displayed at 1545x2000). No contact sheet, thumbnail or new raster pass was used. Observations were appended to the lane ledger, one line per page, after each few pages viewed.

Checked on every page: displayed title, responsible issuer, Latin/Multilingual label and the omission of the unresolved date against the frozen metadata; headings, two-column flow, gutters, margins and glyphs; natural source closing; modern provider signatures; running heads and furniture; raw placeholders. Per the Bullarium reading-order addendum, every marginal summary was checked to be its own separate block, every printed page-foot note its own block at the page break, two-column order natural, and each fragment to show its fragment title and a clear missing-content notice. Where a page raised a question about a block's role, the volume's tracked reading-order derivative (`hybrid.txt`) was read to confirm TAB-led (marginal) versus body role; no other source was consulted.

Counts: 219 separate marginal-summary blocks and 30 separate note blocks were seen across the 62 pages. **Zero marginal-into-body fusions and zero footnote-into-body fusions were found.** No recognizable modern provider signature ("Digitized by Google" or variants), no raw `None` placeholder, no clipping, no overlap and no tofu/missing-glyph boxes were found. The black square glyph `■` that appears in some OCR noise strings is a literal extracted character (confirmed in `pdftotext` output), not a missing-glyph box.

No document carried a `prior_finding`.
