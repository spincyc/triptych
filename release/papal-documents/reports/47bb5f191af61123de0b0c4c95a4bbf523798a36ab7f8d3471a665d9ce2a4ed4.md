The exact PDFs were independently hashed and their physical page counts
checked against the final typesetting receipt. The repository's
`tools/pdf-review` generated the two editions' page rasters and contact
sheets in a dedicated review directory with two workers. Every one of the
21 pages was opened individually at a readable scale; complete contact
sheets supplemented that inspection. Both opening pages, the transition to
notes in *Divini illius magistri* page 14, and both final pages were also
inspected at original raster size.

## Page findings

| *Miserentissimus Redemptor*, 1–4 | Compact identifying title and date, original document heading, readable continuous two-column text, stable gutter, consistent paragraph spacing, and unclipped folios. No visible replacement glyph, collision, or marginal overflow. |
| *Miserentissimus Redemptor*, 5 | The papal dating clause and signature remain together. The appended prayer's heading and opening are visible in the same column; the prayer continues naturally onto page 6. |
| *Miserentissimus Redemptor*, 6 | The prayer ends before the historical references. Notes 1–55 follow in column reading order. The compact Source notice, revision timestamp, and two-line rights colophon fit below the notes without overlap or a dedicated notice page. |
| *Divini illius magistri*, 1–13 | The original heading and body are readable within the two-column measure. Quotation marks, accented letters, reference numbers, and page numbers render clearly. No clipping, column collision, orphaned heading, or visibly missing glyph. |
| *Divini illius magistri*, 14 | The closing blessing, dating clause, and signature lead directly into numbered notes. Longer notes wrap within their column, including the English quotation in note 28; none crosses the gutter or clips at the margin. |
| *Divini illius magistri*, 15 | Continued note 48 and notes 49–69 precede the compact Source apparatus and legible colophon. The right column is unused after this natural ending, but the page contains substantial historical notes and has not been generated solely for the modern notice. |

No visual changes are required for these exact artifacts. The acceptance
covers page composition, legibility, and the visible body/note/apparatus
boundaries; source fidelity, historical collation, rights, and installation
retain their separate records and checks.
