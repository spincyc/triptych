## Qualifications and nonblocking observations

- The consistorial allocution `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00055108` displays the volume running title `ACTA GREGORII PP. XVI.` as its issuer, matching the frozen metadata. Under the standing coordinator ruling for Gregory XVI Acta allocutions, this is a recorded shared-running-header attribution context and a non-blocking qualification. The allocution has no dating clause, and its date comes from the excluded edition caption, as its source note discloses. Page 2 ends with the genuine allocution closing (the published cardinals, the reservation *in pectore* and the formula "In nomine Patris > et Filii > et Spiritus Sancti. Amen.", where the `>` marks are literal OCR for the printed crosses).
- `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00053086` displays the issuer literally as `GREGORIUS PP. XVI. —`, with a trailing dash exactly as frozen. It still names the pope and is not blocking. Its dating clause prints no pontifical year, as the source note discloses.
- The three bulls `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00047706`, `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00048334` and `gregory-xvi-acta.volume-03.line-00050525.part-02` display the bull-form issuer `GREGORIUS EPISCOPUS`, matching the metadata and their own "servus servorum Dei" protocols. Each ends with a sanction clause and a full incarnation-year Datum clause on page 5.
- The source's own notes are retained after the dating clause and set apart from project apparatus: line-00046259 page 2 ("1S. Cypriani epist. 77 ad Nemesianum et ceteros Martyres,") and line-00051588 page 2 ("1S, Leo epist. 7 al. 8 ad Episcopos per Italiam."). Both match the source-note statement that the act's substantive notes are retained literally.
- Disclosed isolated OCR marks appear exactly where the source notes place them and are not running heads or furniture: line-00046791 (LF46873 "-"), line-00047706 (LF48027 "_extantium"), line-00050525.part-02 (LF51066, LF51419, LF51422 "|"), line-00051426 (LF51474, LF51487 "|"), line-00051954.part-02 (LF52117 "_" and lone LF52120 "|" inside the Datum clause), line-00052634.part-01 ("decernen- / | tes etc,"), line-00052915.part-01 (LF52960 "*" opening a short body line) and line-00055537 (LF55561 "—", LF55605 "©").

- These sparse final pages carry genuine source closing before the project apparatus, so none is apparatus-only: line-00046259 p2 (Datum and the source note), line-00046427 p2, line-00046791 p2, line-00051426 p2, line-00051588 p2 (Datum and the source note), line-00055108 p2 (allocution closing), line-00055294.part-01 p2, line-00055537 p2 (Datum only) and line-00056146 p2 (the last line of the Datum clause).

These are body continuations from the source segmentation, not stranded headings or furniture.
- No recognizable modern provider signature (for example "Digitized by Google"), raw `None` placeholder, clipping, overlapping text, tofu or missing-glyph box, stranded heading, collapsed table, fused marginal summary or running head inside a body sentence was found on any page.
- Literal OCR noise remains throughout, for example "cazlestibus", "m2nse", "p2ramanter", "optabiJius", "at- tentar1", "Maioreny", underscores and stray dashes. These are limitations, not defects.

## Limitations

The PDFs contain literal, uncorrected and uncollated historical OCR rendered as a mechanical two-column source edition. This was a layout and metadata-display review only. It makes no word-level collation, historical authentication, publication, installation or deployment claim. Dates were compared with the metadata and were not inferred or repaired. The Vatican archival OCR is a text reconstruction, so these rasters do not establish the glyphs or pagination of the 1902 printing.
