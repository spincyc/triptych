# Don’t Panic!

Triptych uses AI to assemble carefully sourced studies of Catholic faith,
worship, and law.

**AI is a tool, not an authority.** These studies do not replace Scripture and
Tradition, the Magisterium, the Church's liturgical books, canon law, or
competent advice. Polished prose is not proof: follow the citations and check
important claims against the authorities themselves.

Publications retain separate GPT and Claude editions where available. Each
catalog row links the available editions, so the two can be read against each
other. Read online, print the browser page, or make a selected print edition
using [Printing](docs/printing.md).

If something is wrong, unclear, incomplete, or unbalanced, send a short note.
A useful note can be one sentence. See [How to Contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## How these texts work

Editing this material turned up problems older and stranger than the project:
the psalms are numbered twice over, two printings of the same Vulgate disagree
about where seventeen chapters end, and a citation can resolve successfully and
wrongly with nothing to report it. These are written up at length, and every
figure in them was counted from the sources rather than recalled.

| Study | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| **[The Bibles](docs/bibles.md)** | Why a citation is a three-part address of which only two parts are ever written down. |
| **[The Mass](docs/the-mass.md)** | What a proper is, why two missals answer the same day differently, and where the calendar stops being arithmetic. |
| **[Reading and Commentary](docs/reading-and-commentary.md)** | How the abridged cycle was chosen and what it leaves out; what the commentary harvest can and cannot establish. |

Each begins at the level of someone meeting the subject for the first time and
goes as deep as the evidence allows.

## Source and contact

Everything here is built in the open at
[github.com/spincyc/triptych](https://github.com/spincyc/triptych) — the sources,
the tools that render them, and the checks that gate a release. Canonical
plaintext and structured data stay in the repository; optional PDFs are made
on demand. Exact binary source evidence is kept separately with recorded
hashes and rights, and can be opened offline after the relevant verified pack

## Why “Triptych”?

A triptych joins three distinct images whose meaning deepens through their
relationship. This project joins faith, worship, and law in the same way,
tracing the deep connections among their scriptural, theological, liturgical,
historical, devotional, canonical, and symbolic threads.
