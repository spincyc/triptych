# Sacramental Reference Works

This profile governs the canonical treatise at `src/<provider>/theology/sacraments/`,
its mechanically derived at-a-glance companion, and their shared fragments.

## Governing priorities

1. Keep one authoritative theological source in the treatise.
2. Enter the continuous sacramental treatment or usable retrieval content
   immediately after title and contents.
3. Preserve sacrament-specific distinctions beside the sacrament they govern.
4. Put the work-wide corpus, metaphysical vocabulary, method, jurisdictions, and
   qualifications in terminal apparatus.
5. Rebuild every consumer after a canonical fragment changes.

## Treatise and source ownership

The treatise owns `fragments/`, `sections/`, `summaries/`,
`summary-preamble.tex`, and generation metadata. Companions and ritual-Mass
appendices import them directly and keep no editable theological copies. A
substantive correction begins in the treatise and its research record.

Every sacrament identifies matter or quasi-matter, form, subject, minister,
intermediate reality, proper grace, effect, and the formal, relational, or
substantial change involved. Distinguish primary proper, intrinsic secondary,
contingent, and ultimate ends where applicable. Never confuse Eucharistic
transubstantiation with the determining words or with a change of accidents.

The treatise begins with continuous theology and keeps chapter-specific tables
with their chapters. `References` immediately precedes the terminal appendices.
Those appendices begin with `Scope, Terms, and Qualifications`, including the
corpus, source hierarchy, method, authority and jurisdiction, lexicon, global
limits, rights, and review state; then give the master matrix, one initiation
table covering all twenty-four Catholic Churches *sui iuris*, and seven
structurally consistent one-page summaries in canonical order. Generation
metadata is last. Define an indispensable term briefly at first use, but do not
front-load the complete lexicon.

## At-a-glance companion

The companion contains exactly the imported master matrix, lexicon, seven
summaries, and initiation table, in that retrieval order, with no independent
theological prose. Its local metadata contains its own timestamp followed by
`\AIInheritedGenerationMetadata{theology/sacraments}`; it displays the local
timestamp once and no duplicate model-provenance block. Independent prose ends
this exception and requires an independent source audit and metadata record. The
catalog names the treatise and inherited provenance.

## Profile gate

After a shared change, validate the treatise, at-a-glance companion, and every
ritual guide importing an affected summary. Confirm source identity, contents
and appendix destinations, one-page summary boundaries, readable tables, one
provenance display, and agreement between every summary and its full treatment.
