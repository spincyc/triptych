# Biography Production Plan

This is a maintainer audit ledger, not a catalog or publication commitment.
Rows record persons used as sources or material religious authorities who are
eligible for a source-first life under `guidance/biographies.md`. `pending`
creates no publication leaf, reader link, assignment, clearance, or promise.

## Audit boundary

Audited 2026-07-26 against all canonical work owners beneath
`src/sources/works/`, the complete GPT and Claude publication surfaces recorded
in `src/sources/inventories/publications-v1.toml` and
`claude-publications-v1.toml`, and named-authority searches across their tracked
TeX, Markdown, and TOML surfaces. Canonical source IDs are the reproducible
control; citation-only rows identify representative owning surfaces until the
person receives a canonical source identity.

The name search reviewed saint, blessed, pope, Father, Doctor, and common
patristic or scholastic name forms. It cannot discover an unnamed allusion,
prove that every honorific match denotes a source rather than a subject, or
disambiguate a person without manual reading. Corporate bodies, anonymous
works, Scripture's divine and angelic persons, and persons only narrated rather
than used as authorities are outside this ledger.

## Reviewed exclusions

| Person or class | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modern translators, editors, and reference compilers | excluded | Bibliographic assistance alone is not biographical eligibility. |
| Local clergy named only in parish administration | excluded | Narrative officeholders, not sources or material authorities. |
| Councils, dicasteries, dioceses, conferences, editions, and anonymous corpora | excluded | Corporate or anonymous identities are not persons. |
| Biblical persons other than Peter and Paul | excluded | Mere appearance in Scripture or narrative is not use of that person as a source; add a row when a person is actually invoked as an authority. |
| Secular witnesses and historians used only for incidental facts | excluded | No material religious or ecclesial authority in the repository's use. |
