# Assembling a Mass from the 1962 Roman Missal

This profile governs reference works that combine the Ordinary and variable
propers under the 1962 *Missale Romanum*. It does not govern a weekly proper guide
or exposition of the Ordinary. The controlling system is the 1960 *Codex
rubricarum* as incorporated into the Missal, the general rubrics, *Ritus
servandus*, and the edition's formularies. Present authorization to use the book
is a separate, mutable question unless deliberately researched and dated.

Every calendar arithmetic rule this profile relies on — movable anchors, the
resumed-Sunday mechanism, and the fixed-date windows — is owned by
`guidance/liturgy/calendar-computation.md`. Reference it; never restate it here.

## Governing priorities

1. Determine the liturgical day and calendar before choosing a Mass.
2. Keep precedence, disposition of an impeded item, Mass-category admission, and
   formulary assembly as separate decisions.
3. Trace each result to numbered rubrics and a checked 1962 witness.
4. Teach the workflow in the order a user applies it and test it with solved
   cases.
5. Put whole-work editions, calendars, jurisdictions, methods, and currentness in
   terminal apparatus while retaining every branch condition beside its rule.

## Five-decision workflow

1. **Day:** determine temporal, sanctoral, proper-calendar, vigil, octave, and
   permitted Saturday identities.
2. **Precedence:** apply the 1960 table to the competing days. Day, feast, and
   Mass classes are related but not interchangeable.
3. **Impediment:** determine transfer, reposition, commemoration, or omission from
   its own rule.
4. **Mass category:** test whether the Mass of the Office, festive, votive,
   Requiem, ritual, or external solemnity is admitted.
5. **Assembly:** place Ordinary, proper texts, commemorations, seasonal
   interventions, and ceremonial directions only after the first four decisions.

Occurrence concerns days on one civil date; concurrence concerns adjacent
Offices at Vespers. Do not import concurrence into the morning Mass or infer
Mass-category permission from the day's rank.

## Required records and controlling evidence

Each leaf keeps:

- `research/scope.md`: edition, language, calendars, jurisdiction, Mass
  categories, excluded canonical questions, hierarchy, uncertainties, and review;
- `research/edition-manifest.md`: controlling Missal, rubrical code, general
  rubrics, Order, particular supplements, and non-controlling aids;
- `research/rubric-index.md`: each decision rule and case mapped to exact numbered
  rubrics as direct rule, necessary inference, or editorial workflow;
- `research/worked-cases.md`: conditions, calendar, competitors, precedence row,
  impeded-item result, Mass permission, inserted texts, and answer check; and
- `research/source-audit.md`: facsimiles, official acts, translations, OCR,
  scholarship, rejected leads, and unresolved discrepancies.

The *Acta Apostolicae Sedis* promulgation and a checked 1962 Missal facsimile
control. OCR and calendar tools are finding aids. A diocesan, national,
religious, titular, patronal, dedication, or indult result requires its actual
particular law.

## Rubrical and assembly coverage

Teach the edition's taxonomy exactly: Sundays and ferias have their own class
rules; feasts are class I, II, or III; class IV is not a feast class; Our Lady on
Saturday is a class IV Office; votive and Requiem Mass classes govern separate
admission rules. Name the kind of class whenever ambiguity is possible.

Map the precedence table without suppressing exceptions. Cover the relevant
Sunday and feria classes, privileged seasons and days, universal and sourced
particular feasts, commemoration limits and duplicate prohibitions, accidental
and perpetual occurrence, transfer and reposition, and the distinctions among
external solemnity, transferred feast, and festive or votive Mass. Never reduce
the system to comparing two class numbers.

Give a complete slot map from entrance through last Gospel, identifying material
from the stable Order and Canon; seasonal, sanctoral, Common, votive, ritual, or
Requiem formulary; resumed Sunday or psalter direction; commemoration/additional
collect; conditional Gloria, Alleluia or Tract, Credo, preface, dismissal and
last Gospel; and Low, sung, or Solemn execution where a companion book governs.
Explain how partial formularies use Commons and how color and preface are found;
never silently fill a local, optional, or unresolved slot.

Special devotional weekdays and requested Masses are test cases, not automatic
replacements for the day. For First Friday, First Saturday, other votives,
external solemnities, Requiems, funeral and ritual Masses, state the exact 1962
admission conditions and prohibitions and distinguish Office, Mass, external
observance, and private devotional request. Custom, good reason, attendance, or
an apparition-associated request does not erase the rubrics.

Worked cases must exercise every material branch: close precedence collisions,
privileged ferias and seasons, universal versus particular rank, multiple and
excluded commemorations, admitted and prohibited special Masses, external
solemnity, resumed Sunday use, and a sourced local proper. Each answer shows
calendar facts, competing ranks, controlling row/exception, impeded-item result,
Mass-category admission, full assembly, and residual uncertainty. Use a civil
year only after computing and checking it.

## Reader order and terminal apparatus

After title and contents, begin with the five decisions, then precedence,
impediments, category admission, assembly map, special requests, worked cases,
and checklist as useful. A compact non-authorization warning may point to the
appendix when immediate reliance risk exists.

End with `Scope, Editions, and Qualifications`, any rubrical concordance used
chiefly to document method, references, and generation metadata. The appendix
owns full textual and edition boundary, calendar assumptions, language,
jurisdiction, included and excluded categories, terminology, source hierarchy,
method, date/currentness, global uncertainties, rights, and review state. A
condition that changes a precedence, commemoration, permission, slot, or answer
stays beside that rule or case.

## Profile gate

The rubric index, cases, and publication must agree; every variable slot and
conditional intervention must be accounted for; all calendar layers and all
rubrical concepts above must remain distinct; and no statement may imply present
canonical authorization beyond the dated scope.
