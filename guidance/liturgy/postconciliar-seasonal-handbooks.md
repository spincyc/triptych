# Seasonal handbooks for the postconciliar Roman Rite

The reader enters the season at Mass, in the Liturgy of the Hours and at
home. Identify the Roman Missal, the territorial Lectionary and calendar,
and the Office. Keep one recurring volume per season and edition-locale at
`src/<provider>/liturgy/roman-rite/postconciliar/<edition-locale>/seasons/<season>`.
Use the `seasonal-handbook-postconciliar` kind and the shared
`liturgy-reference-v1` document type. List available and planned volumes in
liturgical order in `library/novus-ordo-liturgy.md`. A season spanning two
periods of the year can have one volume with two points of entry.

## Studying and keeping the season

The historical, spiritual and practical standard of the
[seasonal handbook series](roman-1962-seasonal-handbooks.md) applies. Read
the chapters on historical analysis, Fathers and spiritual writers, prayer,
and editorial review there. Both editions give the reader the full history,
serious study and a daily rule of prayer. A separate volume is warranted
where following the actual books requires different lessons, prayers,
feasts or Hours. Demonstrate that difference from the books. Readers may
move between parishes and books or be encountering either for the first time.

Carry the history through the Council, promulgation and the edition in use.
Read the presidential prayers, Lectionary and Hours together. Explain a
received prayer or chant in its liturgical place. Give the Sunday readings
for each applicable cycle and the weekday lessons, the psalm or canticle,
permitted options and a route through movable and fixed observances. Mark
the calendar, numbering convention and distinction between an appointed
reading and an exercise chosen for home. Annual examples may confirm a
rule in the research record. Use recurring assignments in the reader and
catalogue. The [calendar profile](calendar-computation.md) owns the
computation.

Each week leads from Scripture and a located spiritual reading into prayer
and a work of mercy. A reader opening the book on any day can find a
passage, a meditation and a received prayer. Give first-time readers a way
to begin and give experienced readers sources they can return to. Observe
the [publication policy](../liturgical-text-publication-policy.md) for the
Missal, Lectionary and Hours. Citations and original exposition allow a
reader to use the approved books in the parish and a Bible at home.

Keep the edition scope, source audit, source bindings and fresh-reader
review in the publication. Verify the first and last possible starts, each
distinct dated overlap, the handoff to the next season, and the generated
reader and print edition.
