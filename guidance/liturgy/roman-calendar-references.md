# Roman Calendar References

This profile governs edition-specific histories and complete normative
inventories of Roman-rite calendars. The identified typical book, norms,
territory, language witness, jurisdiction, and cutoff determine the work; never
project one calendar's terms or rules into another. The historical module also
follows `guidance/history/historical-accounts.md`, and the inventory module the
profile for its edition.

## Governing priorities

1. Identify the exact edition and territorial layer before constructing an
   inventory.
2. Reconcile every normative entry to controlling books and amendments.
3. Keep historical development, recurring law, and year-specific computation
   distinct.
4. Begin with construction history or practical calendar structure.
5. Put global edition, period, territory, method, cutoff, and qualifications in
   terminal apparatus; keep rank, transfer, and dating exceptions beside the
   affected entry.

“Complete calendar” means the complete normative movable cycle, fixed universal
inventory, and named territorial overlay as of the cutoff. It is not a computed
Ordo and does not promise that every entry occurs in a particular civil year.

## Layers, terms, and sources

Keep distinct: the temporal cycle; universal fixed calendar; named territorial
calendar; diocesan, religious, titular, dedication, patronal, and other local
propers; and later optional mechanisms or permissions that do not rewrite an
older printed base calendar.

Use the edition's taxonomy. The 1960/1962 system does not have postconciliar
optional memorials; the postconciliar system does not inherit 1962 feast
classes. English labels come from an identified human edition or are marked as
editorial identifiers. Do not reproduce formularies merely to prove an entry.

The source order is promulgating act and typical calendar or Missal, applicable
norms, approved territorial proper, and later competent decrees. Annual official
calendars can test implementation but cannot alone define a recurring inventory.
Search tools and commercial or devotional calendars are finding aids. Record an
exact amendment cutoff, distinguishing later permissions concerning an older
book from changes to its historical calendar and from present authorization to
use that book.

## Required records

Each leaf keeps:

- `research/scope.md`: rite, edition, language witness, territory,
  jurisdiction, historical period, cutoff, reader, inclusion rules,
  terminology, uncertainties, rights, and review state;
- `research/edition-manifest.md`: promulgating acts, controlling calendar or
  Missal, norms, territorial proper, amendments, and non-controlling aids;
- `research/source-audit.md`: exact sources and loci, stable links/access dates,
  checked file identity where material, OCR and rights status, rejected leads,
  and discrepancies;
- `research/evidence-map.md`: principal construction-history and amendment
  claims, witnesses, confidence, and qualifications; and
- `research/calendar-inventory.md`: every temporal identity and fixed entry,
  date rule, rank/status, universal or territorial owner, controlling locus,
  transcription check, and unresolved issue.

## Reader order and terminal apparatus

The title or compact identity line names the edition and territory and may state
the cutoff when detachment from it would be unsafe. After title and contents,
begin with periodized construction history or a guide to the calendar's layers;
then give the complete movable cycle, fixed universal inventory, territorial
overlay, and any material later mechanism.

End with a dated modification timeline and `Scope, Edition, and Qualifications`
appendix, then references grouped by controlling books, amendments, territorial
sources, and history, followed by generation metadata. The terminal block owns
the corpus and thesis boundary, full edition and label witness, territory,
jurisdiction, period, cutoff, terminology, method, source hierarchy, inclusion
and exclusion rules, omitted local layers, global uncertainties, rights, and
review state. The narrative interprets rather than duplicates its tables.

## Profile gate

Inventory counts and rows must agree with the controlling record; every required
month, movable provision, vigil, octave, Ember or Rogation provision, and named
territorial entry must be accounted for as the edition requires. Verify rank,
transfer, territorial status, promulgation, effective date, and every amendment
through the cutoff. Keep all jurisdictional layers distinct and never present
the inventory as a competent Ordo or as present authorization beyond scope.
