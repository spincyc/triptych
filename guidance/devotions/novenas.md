# Novena Pious Exercises

This profile governs numbered novenas beneath `src/gpt/devotions/novenas/` and
their mechanically derived daily-prayer companions. A novena is a sustained
ninefold pious exercise, not automatically liturgy, an approved public text, a
sacrament or sacramental, private revelation, or a mechanism that compels an
outcome.

## Governing priorities

1. Direct the devotion through Christ toward Scripture, liturgy, sacraments,
   conversion, communion, and charity.
2. Print only exact, identified, received vocal prayer and human translation;
   never let project or AI prose become prayer text.
3. Give the common recitation order and nine days before history, method, or
   status apparatus.
4. Keep liturgy, pious exercise, approval, received tradition, apparition claim,
   promise, and indulgence juridically and theologically distinct.
5. Put work-wide history, calendar, status, currentness, translation method, and
   qualifications in terminal apparatus while retaining local warnings beside
   the prayer or claim they govern.

## Identity, ownership, and records

Use insertion-safe internal keys `N00`, `N10`, `N20`, and so forth; `N00` is the
Ascension-to-Pentecost archetype. Keys order sources, audits, and catalogs but
never appear in titles, headings, running matter, PDF metadata, or public prose.
A full guide and its short form share one public catalog row. A shared source has
one textual owner, explicit consumers, and dependencies for every consumer.

Each full guide keeps:

- `research/scope.md`: identity, reader, thesis, inclusion boundary, doctrinal
  and liturgical limits, calendar/jurisdiction variables, currentness,
  uncertainties, rights, and review state;
- `research/source-audit.md`: consequential historical, doctrinal, liturgical,
  hagiographic, private-revelation, indulgence, and pastoral claims with exact
  sources and loci; and
- `research/prayer-inventory.md`: every recited prayer, source-language witness,
  date and status where known, exact English witness and ownership, approval or
  liturgical status, locus, rights basis, and any exact expansion of an explicit
  abbreviation.

An apparition, promise, vision, or locution also requires a controlling status
record or link to the repository's Mariological dossier. Approval of a saint,
title, feast, shrine, scapular, confraternity, or prayer does not authenticate
every associated story or promise.

## Doctrinal, liturgical, and prayer controls

Prayer is offered to God; Mary, angels, and saints are invoked for subordinate
intercession, never as independent sources of grace or powers overriding divine
wisdom. A devotional promise cannot guarantee salvation, healing, a temporal
result, or exemption from repentance and the ordinary means of grace.

Distinguish the saving mystery and its liturgical celebration; a public or
private pious exercise; an approved prayer for an exact use; received
non-liturgical prayer; project-authored non-vocal meditation; and private
revelation with its exact nonbinding judgment. Do not insert devotional material
into Mass or the Hours, call a booklet “the Church's official novena” without a
controlling act, or present private recitation as a liturgical office. State
plainly when competent guidance prefers Vespers, Mass, Scripture, or sacramental
participation and present the novena as subordinate.

Every English vocal prayer appears adjacent to its complete Latin or Greek
witness. Use one exact identified human translation with a lawful rights basis;
do not compose, paraphrase, modernize, conflate, or materially adapt it. Expand
an explicit abbreviation only from the same witness or a governing edition it
incorporates, recording both loci. Prayer instructions, history, Scripture
citations, examinations, and silent meditation are not vocal prayer. Print a
recurring common prayer once and refer to it by name.

## Calendar and historical controls

Explain the devotion's ninefold rationale, development, textual history,
competent commendation or approval, and any late, legendary, disputed, or
unverified origin. Biblical patterns can illuminate persevering prayer but do
not prove an apostolic booklet.

Record a reproducible calendar rule: anchor feast or event, day-one calculation,
one checked dated example, material transfers or jurisdictional differences,
and the distinction between devotional choice, liturgical transfer, and legal
obligation. If prayer at any time is permitted, preserve the principal annual
placement. Give a sober pastoral option after a missed day without superstition
or invented law.

Approval claims name authority, act, date, object, and jurisdiction. An
imprimatur concerns the submitted text, not an origin story or guaranteed
efficacy. A repository-authored unreviewed novena says so in the terminal
appendix. Indulgence claims identify the controlling `Enchiridion` edition,
concession and norms, relevant conditions and approved text, and as-of date;
never carry forward superseded measures or imply automatic fulfillment.

## Full-guide reader order

After title and contents, give:

1. one complete common order;
2. nine distinct days, each with a scriptural center, source-grounded meditation,
   examination or act, intention, and unique received prayer;
3. principal received hymns, antiphons, litanies, or feast prayers used by the
   guide, with controlled source text and translation;
4. material biblical, doctrinal, and historical exposition; and
5. terminal `Scope, Calendar, and Qualifications`, references, and generation
   metadata.

The terminal appendix owns identity and status, historical and textual bounds,
ninefold rationale, calendar rule and example, jurisdiction, approval and
private-revelation limits, indulgence/currentness, translation method, rights,
and review state. A compact immediate notice may distinguish pious exercise from
liturgy or reject a mechanical promise; a prayer-specific status, translation,
promise, or apparition limit stays with that item.

Daily meditations must develop rather than paraphrase one another and connect
doctrine to repentance, virtue, vocation, mercy, unity, or mission. They may
invite prudent fasting, almsgiving, confession, reading, or service, but may not
impose obligations, discourage medical or psychological care, displace
safeguarding or justice, or use suffering to keep a reader in danger.

## Condensed recitation companion

A sibling `<numbered-document>-daily-prayer/` is a prayer-book view of the full
guide, not another edition. The full guide owns every prayer fragment. The
companion contains only its title and relationship, the minimum executable
rubrics, common vocal prayers printed once, nine day headings with canonical
proper prayers and any day-only addition, and a compact terminal `Scope and
Status` appendix pointing back to the full guide. It adds no exposition,
meditation, examination, calendar essay, independent source notes, or prayer
text and never abridges a prayer.

Its metadata gives its own timestamp and one inherited declaration naming the
full guide, with no duplicate model-provenance display. Its sole local audit,
`research/derivation.md`, maps every rendered prayer and rubric to the canonical
owner and records exact text comparison, dependencies, build, and review. The
catalog links it to the full guide's controlling scope, inventory, and audit.

## Profile gate

The full guide must have nine genuinely distinct days and a complete practical
order; every recited text must agree with the prayer inventory; status, calendar,
promise, apparition, healing, and indulgence claims must remain authority-bounded
and current. For a companion, prove one-to-one derivation, absence of independent
claims, exact prayer identity apart from line wrapping, valid inherited
provenance, and dependencies on every canonical fragment.
