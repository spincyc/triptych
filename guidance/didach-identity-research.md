### Linguistic disposition

- `didach.ai` is the strongest exact public rendering because it is both the
  binding future address and the least ambiguous of the renderings considered.
- `Didach AI` is rejected: it destroys the domain reading and advertises a
  technology product.
- `Didach` is rejected as the ordinary shell name: it resembles a missing final
  letter, loses exact navigational identity, and competes with *Didache*.
- `Didachai` is an explanatory etymological note only, not a second brand.
- The corpus must not prescribe a Greek-derived pronunciation as though it were
  linguistically inevitable. For support, spell the address “didach dot A I.”

## Current collision and confusion research

This section is design risk, not trademark clearance or legal advice.

| Source | Current fact | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`didache.ai`](https://didache.ai/) | A live commercial enterprise-AI training/adoption product branded “Didache” advertises role tracks and an AI coach. | One-letter direct-navigation, speech, email, and typo collision in the same teaching-plus-AI semantic field; it embodies the startup reading this project rejects. |
| Identity Digital [RDAP record](https://rdap.identitydigital.services/rdap/domain/didache.ai) | The adjacent domain record reports a 2026-06-13 registration. | It appears recent, so ordinary search may understate direct confusion; recheck immediately before launch. |
| [`didact.ai`](https://www.didact.ai/) | A separate AI analytics product uses another teaching-derived `.ai` name. | Reinforces the market convention “didact/teaching + AI = software product.” |
| [Didact AI education](https://didact-ai.com/) | AI-assisted lesson-planning product. | The first sentence and visual system must establish corpus/library, not education SaaS. |
| Midwest Theological Forum, [The Didache Series](https://theologicalforum.org/the-didache-series/) | Established Catholic curriculum/book series. | Avoid language implying affiliation, a catechetical product line, or a site devoted solely to the Didache. |
| Ignatius Press, [The Didache Bible](https://ignatius.com/books/bibles-bible-resources/specialty-and-gift-bibles/) | Established Catholic Bible product using Didache in its name. | Scripture routes must retain their exact edition identity; didach.ai is the host, not a Bible edition. |

Mitigations supported by the research:

- use exact lowercase dotted spelling everywhere;
- pair the first encounter with “A source-first corpus for Catholic faith,
  worship, and law”;
- make the address copyable rather than requiring manual transcription;
- keep AI in method/provenance instead of making it the headline;
- retain Triptych as truthful transition/provenance while the new identity
  establishes an independent footprint;
- commission real legal/name clearance and recheck adjacent domains before
  launch.

## Repository audit findings at the identity base

The current public identity is centrally owned but still Triptych-specific:

- `release/public-alpha/layout.html` owns the global three-panel mark, Triptych
  wordmark, AI-forward subtitle, Home/About/Feedback navigation, and footer;
- `release/public-alpha/assets/site.css` owns shared masthead, responsive, footer,
  print, and current light-only color behavior;
- `tools/public-alpha` owns the current GitHub Pages origin, `SITE_NAME`, fallback
  description, browser-title grammar, OpenGraph/Twitter output, icon and social
  asset paths, build copy, and verification;
- `release/public-alpha/assets/icon.png` is one 180×180 Triptych-derived PNG used
  for favicon and Apple touch icon;
- `release/public-alpha/assets/social-card.png` is one 1200×630 Triptych-derived
  card used for all eligible routes;
- `release/public-alpha/404.md` says “Triptych home page”;
- 105 web editions reproduce the canonical PDF's Triptych rights colophon and
  must not be hand-edited;
- canonical Liturgy has its own accepted Triptych reader masthead and suppresses
  the global header/footer in its protected composition;
- the repository has no `CNAME`, canonical-link system, sitemap, web manifest,
  favicon family, or structured site-identity record on this base;
- internal `Triptych` JavaScript namespaces, schema IDs, paths, and storage keys
  are contracts, not public branding debt.

The current `ABOUT.md` claim that every work has parallel GPT and Claude
editions is false for the current projection. At the measured base there are
133 works and 178 provider-qualified documents; 45 works have both providers
and 88 have one. The identity candidate therefore uses “some works have
separately produced treatments” and never promises universal parallel output.

## Asset and rights disposition

- Use repository-original live text, simple geometry, and local system-font
  fallbacks. Do not ship an external font, icon set, screenshot, or logo.
- Project-created design can fall within the project's content licence to the
  extent contributors hold rights, while project-identity/trademark rights
  remain separately reserved. The current licence names Triptych and must be
  deliberately reconciled if didach.ai is accepted.
- Third-party material is not relicensed by the repository. External precedent
  screenshots remain links and descriptions, not handoff content.
- The current public generator has an exact file allowlist and release binding.
  New favicon sizes, SVG, ICO, manifest, or card families require explicit
  generator, expected-artifact, verification, tests, and renewed scoped release
  authorization.

## Synthesis

The strongest cross-domain precedent is not a particular visual style. It is a
repeated institutional behavior:

1. a short, consistent parent identity;
2. a plain statement of scope;
3. distinct tools that retain their own names and compositions;
4. objects, editions, rights, citations, and limitations placed before brand
   storytelling;
5. provenance and stable identifiers that make the system cohere;
6. restraint about institutional, legal, editorial, or scholarly authority the
   project does not possess.

That evidence supports one textual `didach.ai` wordmark, a corpus-first Home,
quiet identity inside Readers/Instruments, precise provider and source
qualification, and a transition that retains Triptych only where it remains
true.

## Rejected research-derived directions

- A suffix trick: colored dot, glowing `.ai`, raised AI, animated reveal, or an
  icon whose joke depends on parsing the domain.
- A Greek-letter, cross, seal, manuscript, book-stack, or triptych-panel logo.
- A press/shop mimic with covers, purchase hierarchy, or institutional prestige
  signals the project does not own.
- A museum image wall or manuscript carousel that makes visual artifacts the
  corpus when much of the corpus is textual, legal, liturgical, or rights-
  limited.
- A digital-humanities dashboard exposing internal databases, acronyms, status
  charts, or activity feeds before reader tasks.
- A “trusted/verified/authoritative” brand layer instead of reachable evidence.
- An object-specific social card for a hash-only object.
- A dark identity skin applied before every underlying surface and state is
  ready.
- A blanket replacement of Triptych that breaks attribution, PDFs, citations,
  source paths, or internal contracts.

## Unresolved leads and limitations

- This is not trademark or freedom-to-operate research. The live adjacent
  `didache.ai` collision makes qualified legal/name review necessary.
- Search-result sampling is volatile and cannot establish indexing, reputation,
  consumer confusion, or likely legal outcome.
- No current cross-platform screen-reader session or native mobile device review
  is part of the automated Chromium evidence; those remain pre-release checks.
- The first candidate uses host font fallbacks. Optical results must be reviewed
  on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android before freezing a wordmark asset.
- Platform guidance conflicts on text in social images. Independent review must
  choose the typographic folio or a quieter graphical/text-free family before
  production assets are authorized.
- The exact 16/32/180/512 icon family is not frozen. Review a plain `d` against a
  `d.` only at sizes where punctuation survives; neither becomes an independent
  logo without acceptance.
- The production URL remains the current GitHub Pages origin until an authorized
  cutover. Research references to `https://didach.ai/` describe the governing
  future identity, not a verified live deployment.
