Project the exact Catena voice keys and finish the typed boundary

The V3 independent review (9b1c23680) found the unsupported-voice mechanism
sound but its classifier wrong: `index.held[].languages` is a language
inventory, not a voice authority. Greek stands in this corpus only as
`original:grc` and there are zero Greek translations, so asking whether the
corpus holds `grc` answered yes and the page offered a Greek translation
nobody has. The generator now writes the exact keys the corpus holds, the way
`catena-model.js` composes them, and the route judges the whole key against
them. `translation:grc` fails closed; `translation:en` on Genesis 10 stays a
true statement about a chapter.

The same review left nine typed-presentation sinks open. They are closed as
one boundary rather than nine special cases: four questions — text, list,
record, or a fact that may be a finite number — asked once in the model, so
the page and the model cannot answer them differently. Absence author/work/
reason/partial are typed at the read, so the summary's count and the list's
words are one value. A scalar is no longer widened into a translator list, a
malformed language no longer reaches a control value or the URL, a malformed
extent leaves the book standing rather than printing a locus nobody
established, and an unnamed author no longer collapses into a shared,
unlabelled, corpus-wide filter key. The render tail now sits inside the
funnel that already existed, so a throw there can no longer strand
`aria-busy`, focus, the tally, the announcement or the route.

The type primitives and the voice phrasing live in `catena-model.js` because
it carries no byte ceiling and `catena.js` is at one. No ceiling moved: the
composition falls from 12,995 to 12,981 whole and 8,799 to 8,749 stripped.
