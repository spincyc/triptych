## Qualifications

Every PDF is a working study, not an approved Church publication. “Source-audited” means an internal evidence check, not independent specialist or ecclesiastical review; no current publication records such review.

The release manifest nominally accounts for 115 publications: 80 are marked `release` and 35 are on `hold`. Thirty previously approved publications revised in the corpus-wide corrective-callout cleanup—the Council, Freemasonry, Trustful Surrender, and Two Missals studies; the Jerome, Peter, and Paul biographies; both Mass-ordinary expositions; three 1962 and nine postconciliar proper guides; seven Mariology works; the heresies survey; and the sacramental treatise—await renewed authorization of their exact PDF snapshots. The Aquinas and Bellarmine biographies were also revised and remain on work-specific-rights-review hold. The three parts of *The Creature Before God* remain on renewed-snapshot hold. All held works are excluded from public and private-preview artifacts. The latest 20 July 2026 supplement to the existing user-attested perpetual authority binds 52 earlier Ecclesiastical Latin curriculum snapshots by explicit exception and 22 exact site-input snapshots, subject to no project-initiated promotion. The 52 corrected installed curriculum PDFs no longer match those historical bindings; 28 unchanged release PDFs still match their approvals. Current reader-facing Markdown changes also leave their historical site-input bindings stale, so release checks remain fail-closed pending renewed authorization. The curriculum exception does not complete page-image collation, establish a donor license, supply independent rights or specialist review, or grant broader reuse of received liturgical or donor wording. Exact-byte clearance for a predecessor snapshot is not a judgment that a work is editorially or profile-final and supplies no missing rights analysis, source collation, independent specialist review, imprimatur, nihil obstat, ecclesiastical approval, general license, or broader reuse permission. Changed bytes require renewed authorization. The La Salette study retains its recorded question about the present force of the 1915 Holy Office decree.

Project licenses cover only project-created material. Quotations, public-domain works, and separately licensed material keep their own status. See [Licensing](LICENSE) and [Third-Party Material](THIRD_PARTY.md).
