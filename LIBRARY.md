## Qualifications

Every PDF is a working study, not an approved Church publication. “Source-audited” means an internal evidence check, not independent specialist or ecclesiastical review; no current publication records such review.

The release manifest accounts for 112 publications: 110 are marked `release`, while *Saint Thomas Aquinas: Friar, Teacher, and the Work of Wisdom* and *Saint Robert Bellarmine: Controversy, Office, Pastoral Rule, and Reception* are on `hold` without approval and are excluded from public and private-preview artifacts pending a work-specific rights review. The latest 20 July 2026 supplement to the existing user-attested perpetual authority clears the 52 Ecclesiastical Latin curriculum snapshots by explicit exception and binds 22 exact site-input snapshots, subject to no project-initiated promotion. Beyond the curriculum, 57 installed PDFs still match their earlier approvals; the Ninth Sunday after Pentecost PDF retains its pre-existing exact-hash mismatch, and the two held biographies have no approval. The current `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `LIBRARY.md`, and `library/biographies.md` bytes also no longer match their bound hashes, so release checks fail closed pending renewed authorization. The curriculum exception does not complete the curriculum's page-image collation, establish a donor license, supply independent rights or specialist review, or grant broader reuse of received liturgical or donor wording. This is exact-byte release clearance, not a judgment that every work is editorially or profile-final, and it supplies no missing work-specific rights analysis, source collation, independent specialist review, imprimatur, nihil obstat, ecclesiastical approval, general license, or broader reuse permission. Changed bytes require renewed authorization. The La Salette study retains its recorded question about the present force of the 1915 Holy Office decree.
