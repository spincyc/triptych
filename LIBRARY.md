## Qualifications

Every PDF is a working study, not an approved Church publication. “Source-audited” means an internal evidence check, not independent specialist or ecclesiastical review; no current publication records such review.

The release manifest nominally accounts for 115 publications: 110 are marked `release`; the three parts of *The Creature Before God*—*Ontological Vertigo*, *The Due Return*, and *At the End of Every Why*—are on `hold` pending renewed authorization of their exact PDF snapshots; and *Saint Thomas Aquinas: Friar, Teacher, and the Work of Wisdom* and *Saint Robert Bellarmine: Controversy, Office, Pastoral Rule, and Reception* are on `hold` without approval pending a work-specific rights review. All five held works are excluded from public and private-preview artifacts.
