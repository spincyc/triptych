## Qualifications

Every PDF is a working study, not an approved Church publication. “Source-audited” means an internal evidence check, not independent specialist or ecclesiastical review; no current publication records such review.

The release manifest accounts for 58 publications. All 58 exact current PDF snapshots and all 21 final public-site sources have exact-snapshot distribution clearance under the later 20 July 2026 “approve all” supplement, subject to no project-initiated promotion; no publication remains in `review` or on `hold`. This is exact-byte release clearance only, not a judgment that every work is editorially or profile-final, and it supplies no missing work-specific rights analysis, source collation, independent specialist review, imprimatur, nihil obstat, ecclesiastical approval, general license, or broader reuse permission. Changed bytes require renewed authorization. The La Salette study retains an unresolved question about the present force of the 1915 Holy Office decree discussed in its source audit.

Project licenses cover only project-created material. Quotations, public-domain works, and separately licensed material keep their own status. See [Licensing](LICENSE) and [Third-Party Material](THIRD_PARTY.md).
