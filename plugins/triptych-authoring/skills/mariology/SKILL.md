# Mariology

## What to provide

Treat the user's complete request and thread context as controlling input, whether it follows the suggestions below or is entirely free-form.

- The subject, governing question, proposed title, or existing target path.
- Whether the work concerns the Rosary, Marian dogma, a title or devotion, biblical and patristic theology, historical reception, or another defined corpus.
- Desired depth, intended reader, principal authorities, relevant languages, and any required one-page summaries, tables, timelines, or comparisons.
- Any private-revelation material, promises, indulgences, disputed history, current doctrinal notes, or juridical status that must be bounded.
- Any free-form context, source suggestions, emphases, exclusions, style guidance, limits, staging, or commit instructions.
